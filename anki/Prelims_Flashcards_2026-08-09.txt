Parliament raised the Supreme Court's sanctioned strength in August 2026. From what to what, and under which parent Act?	From 34 to 38 including the CJI (33 to 37 judges other than the CJI). Amends the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956; effective retrospectively from 16 May 2026. Trigger: a letter from CJI Surya Kant to the PM dated 11 May 2026.
Which Article fixes the composition of the Supreme Court and lets Parliament prescribe a larger number of judges?	Article 124(1) - the SC shall consist of a CJI and, until Parliament by law prescribes a larger number, not more than 7 other judges. Strength ladder: 8 (1950), 11 (1956), 14 (1960), 18 (1978), 26 (1986), 31 (2009), 34 (2019), 38 (2026).
The Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Bill, 2026 creates a two-tier route for delayed registration. What is it?	Delay of more than 1 year but within 2 years: approval of the District / Sub-Divisional / authorised Executive Magistrate. Delay beyond 2 years: order of a Judicial Magistrate of the First Class.
Under which List and Entry of the Seventh Schedule does registration of births and deaths fall?	Entry 30 of the Concurrent List (List III) - 'Vital statistics including registration of births and deaths'. Parent Act: RBD Act, 1969, administered by the Registrar General of India under the MHA.
In August 2026 the Supreme Court held judicial officers are NOT government servants. What retirement-age change was at issue, and what are the comparators?	Raising the district judiciary's superannuation from 60 to 62; states cannot block it on cost grounds. Supreme Court judges retire at 65 (Art. 124); High Court judges at 62 (Art. 217). Article 235 vests control over the subordinate judiciary in the High Court.
The Supreme Court extended the domestic-cruelty offence to women in live-in relationships. Which BNS sections replaced Section 498A IPC?	Sections 85 (the offence) and 86 (definition of 'cruelty') of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023. Articles 14, 15(3) and 21 were invoked; precedents include D. Velusamy (2010), Indra Sarma (2013) and Lalita Toppo (2018).
How much additional tax devolution did the Centre release to States on 1 August 2026, and who were the top three recipients?	Rs 1,09,019 crore, over and above the regular instalment due 10 August. Uttar Pradesh Rs 17,908 cr; West Bengal Rs 7,986 cr; Maharashtra Rs 7,022 cr. Devolution is untied money.
Who chairs the 16th Finance Commission, what is its award period, and what vertical devolution share did it recommend?	Arvind Panagariya; award period 2026-31; vertical devolution retained at 41% of the net divisible pool (18 States had demanded 50%).
At its August 2026 meeting, what were the RBI's policy rates and FY27 projections?	Repo 5.25% (unchanged, unanimous), SDF 5.00%, MSF and Bank Rate 5.50%, stance Neutral. FY27 real GDP 6.7% (up from 6.6%); FY27 CPI 5.0% (down from 5.1%), peaking at 5.9% in Q3.
Under which section of which Act is India's inflation target notified, and what is the MPC's composition?	Section 45ZA of the RBI Act, 1934 - 4% CPI with a +/- 2% tolerance band. The MPC has 6 members (3 RBI + 3 government-nominated externals); the Governor chairs it and has a casting vote; it meets at least four times a year.
What is the Bharat Maritime Insurance Pool (BMIP), and what did the 30 July 2026 launch add?	A sovereign-guaranteed marine insurance pool (guarantee Rs 12,980 crore / ~USD 1.5 bn), operational since 12 May 2026 for war risk. The launch extended it to India's first sovereign-backed P&I cover - third-party liability for crew, cargo, pollution and wreck removal, as distinct from Hull & Machinery. First policy: New India Assurance to the Shipping Corporation of India.
What does the MSMED (Amendment) Bill, 2026 make mandatory for Central Public Sector Enterprises?	Settling MSME invoices on TReDS (Trade Receivables Discounting System), the RBI-regulated invoice-discounting platform. The Bill also makes Udyam registration voluntary and moves MSME classification thresholds from the Act to notification.
PM Viksit Bharat Rojgar Yojana: ministry, outlay, window and first-year outcome?	Ministry of Labour & Employment via EPFO; outlay Rs 99,446 crore; 1 August 2025 to 31 July 2027. Year 1: 72 lakh+ first-time formal employees, ~30% women. First-timer incentive = one month's EPF wage capped at Rs 15,000, in two instalments.
Government e Marketplace (GeM) completed ten years on 9 August 2026. What cumulative GMV did it cross, and what share went to MSEs?	Over Rs 20 lakh crore across more than 3.78 crore orders. Micro & Small Enterprises fulfilled Rs 9.07 lakh crore = 45.6% of cumulative GMV; 2.24 lakh women-led MSEs delivered orders worth Rs 99,147 crore.
Under India's Model BIT 2016, how long must an investor exhaust local remedies before ISDS, and what is proposed?	Five years currently; the revised Model BIT heading to Cabinet may cut it to about two years. Model BIT 2016 uses an enterprise-based definition of investment, omits MFN, has no FET clause and excludes taxation measures.
Which lake became India's 101st Ramsar Site in August 2026, and why is it a double first?	Glaw Lake - India's 101st Ramsar Site and Arunachal Pradesh's first. About 2.5 hectares, near Wakro town, Lohit district, inside the Kamlang Tiger Reserve; 150+ tree and 49 orchid species; sacred to the Mishmi tribes. India had 26 Ramsar sites in 2014.
Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana: outlay, target and central assistance?	Rs 5,070 crore for ~5,000 MW of floating solar PV with a minimum 2 hours of storage; CFA of Rs 1 crore per MW post-commissioning plus up to Rs 50 lakh for feasibility studies. Nodal ministry MNRE. NISE puts India's FSPV potential at ~102.18 GWp.
GOBARdhan (National Circular Bioenergy Scheme): outlay, period, administered CBG price and blending trajectory?	Rs 23,731 crore, FY2026-27 to FY2035-36; administered CBG price Rs 2,110 per MMBTU under a 10-year framework. CBG Blending Obligation: 3% in FY2026-27, 4% in FY2027-28, 5% from FY2028-29, on City Gas Distribution entities. Nodal ministry: Petroleum & Natural Gas. Predecessor: SATAT (2018).
India ranked 176th of 177 in the Environmental Performance Index 2026. Who publishes it, what was India's score, and who topped it?	Published biennially by the Yale Center for Environmental Law & Policy with CIESIN, Columbia University. India's score 22.46; Estonia topped with 74.79; Laos was last. India ranked 174th on both air quality and biodiversity; best pillar was Climate Change (130th).
The Clouded Leopard Conservation Action Plan was launched in 2026. What is the species' protection status?	Neofelis nebulosa - IUCN Vulnerable, Schedule I of the Wild Life (Protection) Act 1972, CITES Appendix I; State Animal of Meghalaya. Added to the Species Recovery Programme under IDWH, with 14 priority landscapes in Northeast India.
What kind of body is the National Board for Wildlife, and who chairs it and its Standing Committee?	A statutory body under Section 5A of the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972, chaired by the Prime Minister; its Standing Committee - which transacts most business - is chaired by the Union Environment Minister.
India released captive-bred slender-billed vultures into the wild for the first time in August 2026. From which centre, and how many birds?	10 birds (5 slender-billed + 5 white-rumped) from the Vulture Conservation and Breeding Centre, Rani, near Guwahati - run by the Assam Forest Department with the Bombay Natural History Society. The Central Zoo Authority has approved a wider release of 35 birds into Kaziranga.
Which drug caused India's vulture collapse, when was it banned for veterinary use, and what is the safe alternative?	Diclofenac, banned for veterinary use in 2006; the safe alternative is meloxicam. Between 1992 and 2007 white-rumped vultures declined ~99%, slender-billed and long-billed ~97%. Flagship centre: Jatayu Conservation Breeding Centre, Pinjore.
Which three countries signed the Mecca joint defence pact on 7 August 2026, and what is its core clause?	Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Turkiye - 'an attack on one is an attack on all'. It builds on the bilateral Saudi-Pakistan Strategic Mutual Defence Agreement of September 2025; described as defensive, not aimed at a third country, and open to other regional states.
What size concessional Line of Credit did India sign with Bhutan on 31 July 2026, and to what is it tied?	Rs 4,000 crore / Nu 40 billion, from Exim Bank of India to Bhutan's Ministry of Finance, signed at Thimphu; tied to Bhutan's 13th Five Year Plan (2024-29), with energy the stated focus. India-Bhutan Friendship Treaty: 1949, revised 2007.
Which two INR-denominated Lines of Credit did India extend to Sri Lanka in August 2026, and what was the Cyclone Ditwah package?	USD 250 million for railways and USD 100 million for livestock and priority needs, via EXIM Bank. Cyclone Ditwah reconstruction package: USD 450 million, including a USD 100 million grant.
What is 'Favara', and what went live between it and UPI in 2026?	Favara is the Maldives' national instant payment system, run by the Maldives Monetary Authority. Its linkage with UPI, via NPCI International Payments Ltd, enables real-time transfers initiated in Maldivian Rufiyaa and credited in Indian Rupees; Phase 1 covers family-maintenance and gift remittances only.
When was the Ganga Water Sharing Treaty signed, by whom, and when does it expire?	Signed 12 December 1996 by PM H.D. Deve Gowda and Bangladesh PM Sheikh Hasina; 30-year duration, expiring December 2026. It governs sharing at the Farakka Barrage (Murshidabad, West Bengal, commissioned 1975) in the dry season, 1 January to 31 May, in ten-day cycles.

Hyderabad; nodal department DoPT (Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions). Theme: 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'. Which missile did India user-trial from Chandipur in August 2026, and what are its specifications?	Agni-4 - two-stage, solid-propellant, surface-to-surface MRBM, range up to ~4,000 km, nuclear-capable, road-mobile and canister-launched. Conducted by the Strategic Forces Command (raised 2003) under the Nuclear Command Authority.
What is 'Everest', unveiled by Astrobase in August 2026, and what makes it a first for India?	India's first fully integrated full-flow staged-combustion (FFSC) rocket engine, built by the private sector - 800 kN vacuum thrust, LOX-methane, throttleable between 50% and 110%. Tested at a 21.5-acre facility near Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh, India's first privately owned high-thrust test stand.
What did the DST's R&D Statistics 2025-26 report on India's GERD and the private-sector share?	GERD reached 0.83% of GDP (2021-22) - the first time above 0.8% since 2009-10. Private industry accounted for 51.8% of R&D expenditure in 2023-24, exceeding all government tiers combined. Compiled by NSTMIS (DST, est. 1982). India's GII rank moved from 46th (2021) to 38th (2025).
ICAR released India's first fully annotated Telomere-to-Telomere reference genome in 2026. For which crop and variety?	Pigeonpea (arhar/tur), variety 'Asha', by ICAR-National Institute for Plant Biotechnology. Genome 752.65 million base pairs in 92 contigs, all 11 chromosomes with 11 centromeres and 22 telomeres, 36,557 genes. Assembly NIPB_CcT2T_4, released on NCBI on 20 April 2026. ICAR-NIPB also produced the world's first draft genome of a pulse crop in 2011, on the same variety.
The Ministry of Ayush signed an MoU with the IndiaAI Mission in July 2026. What is 'AIKosh', and what is IndiaAI's outlay?	AIKosh is India's national datasets, models and toolkits repository under the IndiaAI Datasets Platform pillar. IndiaAI Mission: approved 2024 under MeitY, outlay Rs 10,371.92 crore, seven pillars.
Who became the first woman Director General of the Geological Survey of India, and what are GSI's basics?	Varsha Ashok Aglawe, the 54th DG, took charge on 31 July 2026 - the first woman in GSI's 176-year history. GSI was established in 1851, HQ Kolkata, an attached office of the Ministry of Mines; it does not itself grant mineral concessions.
Nauru changed its official name in 2026. To what, and what are its key facts?	Republic of Naoero (country code NRU to NRO; citizens are dei-Naoero). A raised coral atoll in Micronesia, population ~12,000 (3rd-smallest in the world), currency the Australian Dollar, independent 1968, no official capital - government offices at Yaren.
