India's rank in the WEF Energy Transition Index (ETI) 2026, and the top three countries?	70th (up 2 places); a top global improver. Top 3: Sweden, Finland, Denmark. Published by WEF with Accenture.

Per UNCTAD, India's global ship-recycling share in 2025 and its main hub?	35.4% (world's No. 1, up from 30.1% in 2024); hub at Alang, Gujarat. Domestic law: Recycling of Ships Act, 2019, giving effect to the IMO's Hong Kong Convention (ratified 2019).

Why are fibre-optic drones valued in modern warfare?	They are controlled via a physical fibre-optic cable (commands/video by light pulses), emitting almost no RF — so they are immune to jamming and spoofing. Limitation: cable caps range and can break.
Which ministry notified the FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026, and under which parent Act?	Ministry of Home Affairs (22 June 2026); under the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act, 2010 (original FCRA enacted 1976).

Certified via the Green Hydrogen Certification Portal of India (GHCI) under MNRE's National Green Hydrogen Mission (outlay ₹19,744 cr; 5 MMT/yr target by 2030).
