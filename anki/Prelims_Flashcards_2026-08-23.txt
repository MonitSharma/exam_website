India's merchandise exports in July 2026 stood at what record figure, and what was the merchandise trade deficit?	Exports USD 44.24 bn (+19.63%, highest ever for a July); merchandise trade deficit USD 31.98 bn — a six-month high. Overall (goods+services) deficit was USD 15.03 bn.
Which scheme, contained in Chapter IV of the Finance Act 2026, gives small taxpayers a one-time window to disclose foreign assets — and when does it close?	FAST-DS (Foreign Assets of Small Taxpayers – Disclosure Scheme); in force 16 August 2026, window closes 31 December 2026. Category 1: assets up to Rs 1 crore, 30% tax + 30% in lieu of penalty = 60%. Category 2: threshold Rs 5 crore, flat fee Rs 1 lakh.
The DRI busted a Rs 2,500-crore fraud in areca-nut imports. Which trade agreement was misused, and what is the normal BCD on areca nut?	SAFTA (South Asian Free Trade Area) — signed at the 12th SAARC Summit, Islamabad, 2004; in force 1 January 2006. Basic Customs Duty on areca nut is 100%; nuts from Indonesia/Thailand/Malaysia were routed through a Bangladeshi EPZ with fake Certificates of Origin.
Which 2020 rules shift the burden of proving rules-of-origin onto the importer, and under which section of the Customs Act?	CAROTAR, 2020 (Customs — Administration of Rules of Origin under Trade Agreements Rules); rests on Section 28DA of the Customs Act, 1962, inserted by the Finance Act, 2020. In force 21 September 2020.
Index of Core Industries, July 2026: what was the growth rate, the new base year, and which sector led?	5.4% y-o-y (easing from 6.0% in June); base year revised to 2022-23; iron ore led at +29.5%. Fertilisers contracted 8.0%. Cumulative April–July 2026: 4.3%.
Under Hybrid Annuity Mode (HAM), how is a highway project funded and who bears traffic risk?	Government funds 40% of project cost during construction in five milestone-linked instalments; the concessionaire funds the remaining 60%, recovered as annuities plus interest. The authority (NHAI), not the developer, collects toll — so traffic risk stays with the public authority.
Under Section 8 of the RBI Act, 1934, what is the maximum size and composition of the RBI's Central Board?	Maximum 21 members: Governor + up to 4 Deputy Governors, 4 Directors (one from each Local Board), 10 nominated part-time non-official Directors, and 2 Government officials. Local Boards (Section 9) are at Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai and New Delhi.

Reduced from 3 years to 1 year of Bar practice, plus 1 year judicial-academy training and 1 year structured clerkship. Bench: CJI Surya Kant, Justice Augustine George Masih and Justice K. Vinod Chandran; 2:1, with Justice K. Vinod Chandran dissenting.
The Union Cabinet approved a new High Court bench on 20 August 2026 — where, and what is the important distinction?	A bench of the High Court of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh at Ladakh — its third seat after Srinagar and Jammu. It is a bench of the existing common High Court, NOT a separate High Court for Ladakh. A nine-judge Constitution Bench in August 2026 upheld the definition of 'industry' from which case, and what was the crucial carve-out?	Bangalore Water Supply & Sewerage Board v. A. Rajappa (21 February 1978, seven-judge Bench) — the broad test under the Industrial Disputes Act, 1947 wherever an employer–employee relationship exists. Carve-out: future disputes under the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 must be read on the Code's own text, not the 1978 ratio.
Zonal Councils: what is their legal basis, how many are there, and who chairs them?	Statutory, not constitutional — created under Part III of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956. Five Councils (Northern, Central, Eastern, Western, Southern). The Union Home Minister chairs all five; the host State's Chief Minister is Vice-Chairman by annual rotation. Recommendations are advisory. The North Eastern Council is separate, under the NEC Act, 1971.
Under the RTI Act, 2005, where does a second appeal lie and within what time limit?	To the Central/State Information Commission under Section 19(3), within 90 days. The first appeal lies to the First Appellate Authority within the public authority; complaints lie under Section 18. The CIC launched AppCoMS 2.0 on 17 August 2026 (first introduced September 2016).
What did the CAG performance audit find about the National Mission for a Green India (GIM)?	GIM achieved 0.03409 million ha of forest/tree cover against a 1.4 million ha target — a 97.57% shortfall. Only Rs 1,149.14 crore (47.88%) of budgetary support was received over 2015-16 to 2024-25. GIM: launched 2014, a Centrally Sponsored Scheme, one of the eight missions under NAPCC (2008), nodal ministry MoEFCC.
The 2026 Second Amendment Order restratified the Ganga flood plain into which three zones?	Active flood plain = area of the 1-in-5-year flood; Regulatory zone = between the 1-in-5 and 1-in-25-year flood; Warning zone = between the 1-in-25 and 1-in-100-year flood. Based on CWC flood-frequency lines; parent Order of 2016 under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.
Name the three-tier institutional architecture created by the 2016 River Ganga Authorities Order.	National Ganga Council (chaired by the Prime Minister) → Empowered Task Force (chaired by the Jal Shakti Minister) → National Mission for Clean Ganga (NMCG) as implementing authority. It is the legal frame for Namami Gange.

What is India's UNCCD land restoration pledge, and when was it raised?	To restore 26 million hectares of degraded land by 2030 — raised at UNCCD COP14, New Delhi, 2019. It is the operational face of India's Land Degradation Neutrality (LDN) commitment.
Distinguish NTCA from NBWL under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972.	NTCA — statutory, under Section 38L (inserted by the 2006 Amendment), tiger-specific, chaired by the Environment Minister; a Tiger Reserve is notified under Section 38V. NBWL — under Section 5A, advisory, covers all wildlife, chaired by the Prime Minister; its Standing Committee is chaired by the Environment Minister.
What did India's 2022 All India Tiger Estimation report, and what share of world tigers does India hold?	3,682 tigers (mean estimate); India holds roughly 70% of the world's wild tigers. Project Tiger was launched on 1 April 1973.
India's first National Report on Deep-Sea and Distant-Water Fishery Resources was released by which body, and what is India's EEZ area?	CMLRE (Centre for Marine Living Resources & Ecology), an attached office of the Ministry of Earth Sciences, at Kochi during its Silver Jubilee. India's EEZ is about 2.37 million sq km. Flagship vessel: FORV Sagar Sampada. The mesopelagic or 'twilight' zone is roughly 200–1,000 m deep.
ISRO static-tested an improved SS1 booster on 11 August 2026 — which vehicle, where, and what is the gain?	The SSLV's solid first stage, at the static test facility, Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota. Expected to add about 100 kg to LEO payload; over 600 parameters were instrumented. SSLV = three solid stages + a liquid Velocity Trimming Module (VTM).
Where is the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope headed, and what are its two instruments?	A halo orbit around the Sun–Earth L2 point, ~1.5 million km from Earth; launch 30 August 2026 on a SpaceX Falcon Heavy. Wide Field Instrument (300.8-megapixel, FoV ~100x Hubble's) and a Coronagraph Instrument. Named for NASA's first Chief Astronomer.
Which Lagrange points are stable, and who occupies L1 and L2?	L1, L2 and L3 are unstable (need station-keeping); L4 and L5 are stable (Trojan asteroids). L2: JWST, Gaia, Euclid and Roman. L1: SOHO and India's Aditya-L1 (halo orbit insertion 6 January 2024, launched on PSLV-C57, 7 payloads, VELC as lead instrument).
What is SraVaani-1.0 — who built it and what does it cover?	India's multilingual automatic speech recognition (ASR) model from IISc's SPIRE Lab with ARTPARK and Google, released openly on Hugging Face under an MIT licence. Covers 65 Indian languages and dialects (20 scheduled + 45 regional), 10 scripts, with automatic language identification. FastConformer architecture; built on Project Vaani.

Describe the Subansiri river: what is its status, source and confluence?	Largest tributary of the Brahmaputra and a right-bank (north-bank) tributary; trans-Himalayan and antecedent. Rises on the Tibetan Plateau (confluence of Lokong Chu, Chayal Chu and Tsari Chu), enters India near Taksing in Arunachal Pradesh, joins the Brahmaputra at Jamurighat, Lakhimpur district, Assam. Length ~442 km; called the 'Gold River'.
Maharashtra recorded 29 tremors between 30 July and 14 August 2026. What was the largest, and which seismic zone is Nashik in?	M 4.3 at Nashik on 8 August 2026 at ~5 km depth. Nashik and Palghar are in Seismic Zone III (moderate) under IS 1893 (Part 1). India has four zones — II, III, IV and V — with V the most severe; there is no Zone I in the current classification. Peninsular precedents: Koyna (11 December 1967, M 6.5, reservoir-induced) and Latur/Killari (30 September 1993).
Where was the total solar eclipse of 12 August 2026 visible, and why is totality scientifically valuable?	Path over eastern Greenland, western Iceland and northern Spain; not visible from India. Totality reveals the inner solar corona, which exceeds 1,000,000 degrees C against a photosphere of about 5,500 degrees C — the coronal heating problem. Helium was discovered from the 1868 total solar eclipse observed from India.
