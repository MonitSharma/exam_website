What is an e-Zero FIR, who launched it and what is the monetary threshold? What is the IMD's definition of a cloudburst?	More than 100 mm of rainfall in one hour over an area of about 20-30 sq km. A flash flood is flooding that develops typically within 6 hours of the triggering rainfall - not every flash flood is a cloudburst. IMD ruled out a cloudburst behind the July 2026 Upper Assam floods.
How many satellites does NavIC now have operational, and what is the minimum needed for independent positioning?	Only 3 operational; at least 4 are needed. NavIC (operational name of IRNSS) was designed as a 7-satellite constellation - 3 geostationary + 4 inclined geosynchronous - covering India and up to 1,500 km beyond its borders.
What does SHANTI stand for, and where will it be piloted?	Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity - India's proposed norms-based maritime governance framework, to be operationalised first in the Bay of Bengal. Announced alongside India's candidature for a UNSC non-permanent seat for 2028-29.
Distinguish SAGAR, MAHASAGAR and SHANTI.	SAGAR (2015) = Security and Growth for All in the Region (Indian Ocean vision). MAHASAGAR (2025) = Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions (Indo-Pacific/Global South). SHANTI (2026) = the operational, norms-based maritime governance framework.
What did the Supreme Court hold in 2026 about ex post facto environmental clearances?	It quashed the Centre's 2021 Office Memorandum permitting retrospective ECs, holding prior EC mandatory under the EIA Notification, 2006, and that any amnesty must come via a statutory notification under Section 3 of the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 - not an administrative order. Clearances already granted stand unless individually challenged.
