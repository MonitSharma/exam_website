SC ruling (Nov 2026): is the National Book Trust 'State' under Article 12? Which tests did the Court apply?	Yes. Cumulative financial-functional-administrative (deep and pervasive control) test of Ajay Hasia (1981) and Pradeep Kumar Biswas (2002). Society registration/self-earned revenue doesn't exclude it. Consequence: Part III rights enforceable (Arts 32/226); no civil-servant status (Arts 309-311).
Rajatarangini, Arthashastra, Mahabharata manuscripts (Nov 2026): which register were they inscribed on, and where are they held?	UNESCO Memory of the World Asia-Pacific (MOWCAP) regional register (not International). Rajatarangini: Kalhana, 12th c., Srinagar; Arthashastra: Oriental Research Institute, Mysuru; Mahabharata (birch-bark): BORI, Pune. Nominations prepared by IGNCA.
Aditya-L1: launch date, vehicle, orbit and the instrument that found an asymmetric CME?	Launched 2 Sep 2023 on PSLV-C57; halo orbit around Sun-Earth L1 (~1.5 million km) from 6 Jan 2024; 7 payloads. VELC (Visible Emission Line Coronagraph) found the 5 Aug 2024 CME expanded asymmetrically.
MINURSO: when set up, why, and how did it end in 2026?	Set up 1991 to monitor the Western Sahara ceasefire and prepare a referendum. Mandate lapsed (~35 years) after Russia vetoed a US draft extension that made Morocco's autonomy plan the basis for talks. Polisario Front (Algeria-backed) seeks independence.
SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026: effective date, what they replace, who cannot settle?	In force 7 Nov 2026 (under Sec 15JB, SEBI Act 1992); replace the 2018 Settlement Regulations. Wilful defaulters, fraudulent borrowers and fugitive economic offenders cannot settle. Application fee Rs 25,000 (natural persons), Rs 35,000 (others).

J. Rajesh v. M. Kumaran (2026 INSC 1208): what did the SC hold on Hindu succession?	Section 8, HSA 1956 is self-contained: property a father got on partition becomes his separate property; a son's son is not a Class I heir (CWT v. Chander Sen, 1986); Section 6 inapplicable.

Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) Rabi 2026-27: outlay, period, what it covers?	About Rs 62,370 crore; 1 Oct 2026 to 31 Mar 2027. Per-kg subsidy on nutrients for P&K fertilisers (28 grades); urea is outside NBS. In force since 1 April 2010. DAP retail price held at Rs 1,350 per 50-kg bag.
Srinivasa T. v. State of Karnataka (2026): what did the SC hold?	Legislature cannot directly annul a final judgment (separation of powers). Struck down the Karnataka Civil Services (Validation) Act, 2022 protecting the cancelled 2011 KPSC recruitment. It may cure the legal basis of a judgment retrospectively, not overrule it.
