RBI MPC, 7 Oct 2026: what happened to the repo rate, and what are the SDF, MSF and Bank Rate after the move?	Repo raised 25 bps to 5.50% (first hike since Feb 2023), decided unanimously. SDF 5.25%, MSF 5.75%, Bank Rate 5.75%. SDF = repo minus 25 bps; MSF = repo plus 25 bps.
RBI Oct 2026 policy: new stance and FY27 projections?	Stance changed from neutral to 'calibrated tightening'. FY27 real GDP growth projected 7.1% (up from 6.7%); FY27 CPI inflation about 5.2%. Inflation target 4% (+/-2%).
Monetary Policy Committee: composition, casting vote, and legal basis?	6 members: 3 from RBI (Governor and two RBI officials) and 3 external members appointed by the Centre. Governor has a casting vote. Statutory basis: Section 45ZB, RBI Act 1934 (2016 amendment). Target of 4% (+/-2%) is notified by the Centre in consultation with the RBI every five years.
2026 Nobel Prize in Physics: who and for what?	Francis Halzen, for key contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos from astrophysical sources.
IceCube: where is it and how does it detect neutrinos?	A cubic-kilometre detector buried in Antarctic ice at the South Pole. It records faint light (Cherenkov light) from charged particles produced when neutrinos interact in the ice. Neutrinos are electrically neutral, so they point back to their sources.
2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry: who and for what?	Henri B. Kagan (France) and Kenso Soai (Japan), for non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric synthesis, showing how a preferred molecular handedness (chirality) can emerge and be amplified.
What is the Soai reaction?	A 1995 asymmetric autocatalytic reaction (Kenso Soai) in which a tiny excess of one mirror-image molecule is amplified. Related to biological homochirality and single-enantiomer drugs.
2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine: who and for what?	Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann and Georg Nagel, for optogenetics: controlling genetically targeted cells with light using light-gated ion channels such as channelrhodopsin (first found in algae).
2026 Nobel Peace Prize: who won and for what?	Navanethem (Navi) Pillay of South Africa (of Indian Tamil origin), for promoting peace and international law and the prosecution of international crimes. Former UN High Commissioner for Human Rights; judge at the ICTR and the ICC. Awarded by the Norwegian Nobel Committee; ceremony in Oslo on 10 Dec.
India-EFTA TEPA: signing, entry into force, members, and headline target?	Signed 10 Mar 2024; in force 1 Oct 2025; India's first FTA with a European economic bloc. EFTA = Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland (not EU members). Target: US$100 billion investment and 10 lakh direct jobs in India over 15 years.
CAFE-III norms: who notified them, what period, and who implements them?	Notified by the Ministry of Power on 30 Sep 2026. Apply 1 Apr 2027 to 31 Mar 2032 (two blocks: FY28-FY30 and FY31-FY32). Implementing agency: Bureau of Energy Efficiency under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001. Fleet target tightens from 3.996 to 3.3273 litres per 100 km (about 16.7%).
CAFE-III super-credit multipliers and credit rules?	BEV and range-extended EV 3x; plug-in and strong-hybrid flex-fuel 2.5x; strong hybrid 1.6x; flex-fuel 1.1x. Credits can be traded or sold to BEE (buy-back Rs 2,500 per g CO2/km in FY28 rising to Rs 4,500 in FY32); carry forward only within a block. Makers under 1,000 vehicles a year are exempt from targets.
International Whaling Commission: founding convention, moratorium, India's status, IWC-70 venue?	Set up under the International Convention for the Regulation of Whaling, 1946 (Washington); commercial-whaling moratorium since 1986; not a UN agency. India joined in 1981 and backs conservation. IWC-70 was held in Hobart, Australia (28 Sep-2 Oct 2026).
PRIP scheme: ministry, outlay, and the new track announced in Sept-Oct 2026?	Promotion of Research and Innovation in Pharma MedTech; Department of Pharmaceuticals (Ministry of Chemicals and Fertilizers); outlay about Rs 5,000 crore. New Discovery Track: up to Rs 50 crore per project for start-ups/MSMEs on new chemical or biological entities (TRL 1-3), with at least 25% institutional-investor co-funding. First round: 41 projects, about Rs 1,600 crore.
Delhi High Court (1 Oct 2026, Justice Amit Mahajan): what did it hold on visas, and which fundamental rights are for citizens only?	A valid visa is not a right of entry; officers keep discretion (national security, document mismatch). Art 14 and 21 apply to all persons; Art 15, 16, 19, 29 and 30 are for citizens only.
Great Nicobar (SC, 5 Oct 2026): what did the Court do and what is the Shompen's status?	The bench of CJI Surya Kant declined to stay Calcutta HC proceedings on a Forest Rights Act PIL. Justice Bagchi remarked that preserving culture and identity (not forced integration) is a constitutionally protected right. The Shompen are a PVTG; the FRA 2006 requires gram sabha consent.
National Waterway-2 vs National Waterway-1: termini?	NW-2 = Brahmaputra, Sadiya to Dhubri (891 km; declared 1988). NW-1 = Ganga, Prayagraj to Haldia.
Bogibeel (Dibrugarh) cargo to Bangladesh, Oct 2026: what moved and how?	Methanol from Assam Petro-Chemicals Ltd (Namrup) by barge down NW-2 (Brahmaputra) and the Indo-Bangladesh Protocol Route to the Dhaka region; reported as the first international cargo from Upper Assam since Independence. Flagged off 5 Oct 2026 (about 540 MT per vessel).
India-Japan Joint Crediting Mechanism: Paris Agreement article and milestone dates?	Operates under Article 6.2 (bilateral cooperative approaches). MoC 7 Aug 2025; Rules of Implementation 8 Jun 2026; Operational Manual launched 30 Sep 2026.
National Supercomputing Mission: year, steering ministries, implementers, and 2026 status?	Launched 2015 (about Rs 4,500 crore); steered by DST and MeitY; implemented by C-DAC and IISc. About 40 systems, about 68 petaflops deployed, using indigenous Rudra servers and Trinetra interconnects; imported CPUs/GPUs remain the gap.
India's first National Household Travel Survey: who conducted it and a headline finding?	National Statistics Office (MoSPI), July 2025-June 2026, about 4.92 lakh households. Walking is the leading student mode (about 37%); two-wheelers lead worker commutes (about 42.6%).
Integrated Transport and Logistics Authority (ITLA), Cabinet 6 Oct 2026: functions?	Special Purpose Vehicle for integrated planning across 8 transport/logistics domains. Prepares a National Transport Master Plan (10+ years); technically appraises and monitors projects of Rs 500 crore and above; builds a National Transport Data Repository (GSTN, FASTag, Vahan, GPS); advises on National Logistics Policy 2022.
SME Growth Fund (Cabinet, 6 Oct 2026): size and nature?	Rs 10,000 crore government commitment; structured as an Alternative Investment Fund making direct equity investments in growth-stage SMEs (mostly manufacturing; Tier-II/III clusters). Announced in Budget 2026-27.
State UCCs: which states got Presidential assent in 2026, and the constitutional hooks?	Gujarat and Assam (after Uttarakhand, which implemented first). Art 44 is a non-justiciable DPSP; personal law is Concurrent List Entry 5, so a conflicting state law needs Presidential assent under Art 254(2). Gujarat's law excludes Scheduled Tribes.
WHO Pandemic Agreement: adoption date and why it is not yet open for signature?	Adopted by the WHA on 20 May 2025 (124 for, 0 against, 11 abstentions). Under Article 12 it opens for signature only after the Pathogen Access and Benefit-Sharing (PABS) annex is adopted; hence not in force.
Dugong in India: where is the Conservation Reserve and what is its legal/IUCN status?	Dugong Conservation Reserve in Palk Bay, Tamil Nadu (448.34 sq km). WPA 1972 Schedule I; IUCN Vulnerable; herbivorous marine mammal feeding on seagrass. 2026 WII assessment: about 270 dugongs (158 Palk Bay, 112 Gulf of Mannar).
Leprosy in India: year of elimination as a public health problem, 2025-26 caseload and causative organism?	Eliminated as a public health problem in 2005 (prevalence below 1 per 10,000). 91,783 new cases in 2025-26; prevalence 0.56 per 10,000. Caused by Mycobacterium leprae (bacterium); SDR-PEP (single-dose rifampicin) is given to contacts.
RPwD Act 2016: number of recognised disabilities, benchmark disability, and reservation in government posts?	21 disabilities; benchmark disability = at least 40%; 4% reservation in government posts. (SC issued notice on air-travel accessibility, Oct 2026.)
WWF Living Planet Report 2026: headline number, period and compilers?	73% average decline in monitored wildlife populations, 1970-2022 (an average fall in population size, not 73% of animals lost). Based on 35,803 populations of 5,790 species; Living Planet Index compiled with the Zoological Society of London (ZSL). Edition title: The Science of Hope.
MoSPI Environmental-Economic Accounts Strategy 2026-30: framework followed and what it is not?	Follows the UN SEEA Central Framework; successor to the 2022-26 strategy. It supplements GDP with natural-capital accounts; it does not replace GDP with Green GDP.
JOSH teams (launched 8 Oct 2026): full form, ministry and phase-1 coverage?	Joint Squad for Health; Health Ministry with Youth Affairs and Defence (NCC) under TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan. Squads of MY Bharat volunteers and NCC cadets; phase 1 covers 55 districts in 11 States/UTs.
INS Surya: what is it and where was it launched?	India's first indigenously designed and built Fleet Support Ship (FSS-1; at-sea replenishment). Built by Hindustan Shipyard Limited (a Ministry of Defence PSU), Visakhapatnam.
57th GST Council (8 Oct 2026): key recommendations and legal status?	Recommended omitting GST officers' arrest power (Sec 69 CGST Act) and raising the prosecution threshold from Rs 1 crore to Rs 5 crore; no rate changes. Council is a body under Art 279A; its recommendations are persuasive, not binding (Mohit Minerals, 2022). Process reforms to apply from 1 Apr 2027 per the Finance Minister.
World Development Report 2026: title, publisher and core message?	'The Promise of Artificial Intelligence', the World Bank's annual flagship; launched in New Delhi on 8 Oct 2026 (with the IndiaAI Mission). AI gains depend on foundations: electricity, connectivity, compute, data, skills and institutions.
COP31: venue, dates, presidency, and where was Pre-COP31 held?	COP31 at Antalya, Turkiye, 9-20 Nov 2026, with Australia presiding over the negotiations (split arrangement). Pre-COP31 (5-8 Oct) was co-hosted by Australia, Fiji and Tuvalu (Nadi, Fiji). India stresses CBDR-RC and adaptation/means-of-implementation parity.
VB-G RAM G Act, 2025: what did it replace and what did the Oct 2026 schedule add?	Replaced MGNREGA. Rural Development Ministry notified 375 permissible works (41% more than the earlier 266) in four categories: water security (128, largest), core rural infrastructure (111), livelihood infrastructure (98), disaster/extreme-weather preparedness (38, new). Works originate in Gram Sabha-approved Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans.
20th Asian Games (2026): host and India's result?	Aichi-Nagoya, Japan (19 Sep-4 Oct 2026). India finished 4th with 85 medals (21 gold, 27 silver, 37 bronze), below Hangzhou 2023 (106 medals, 28 gold).
National SC-ST Hub: ministry, implementing body, launch year and procurement target?	Ministry of MSME; implemented by NSIC; launched 18 Oct 2016. Supports the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs, 2012: 25% MSE target with a 4% sub-target for SC/ST-owned MSEs.
Reported cap on anti-cancer drug trade margins (Oct 2026): what and which regulator?	Reported decision to cap trade margins at 30% of MRP on about 110 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs (not yet notified as of the briefing). NPPA, under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, handles drug pricing.
DRDO high-altitude systems in Ladakh/Oct 2026: HAPS and who developed it?	High-Altitude Platform (HAPS), a lighter-than-air stratospheric platform by ADRDE, Agra, reached 21 km AMSL. Separately, the Advanced High-Altitude Parachute (AHAP) was jumped at Nyoma (Ladakh); the earlier Military Combat Parachute System (MCPS) is also from ADRDE.
