Indus Waters Treaty: year, place, signatories and which rivers go to India?	Signed 1960 at Karachi by Nehru and Ayub Khan, with the World Bank as a signatory. Eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) go to India; Western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) go to Pakistan, India gets limited non-consumptive use (run-of-river hydro). Dispute ladder: Permanent Indus Commission, Neutral Expert, Court of Arbitration. India defended keeping it in abeyance at UNGA-81 (Jaishankar's statement, 26 Sep 2026).
Which Charter articles are the UN 'enemy state' clauses, and which article governs Charter amendment?	Articles 53, 77 and 107 (Axis states of WWII). UNGA called them obsolete in 1995 (Res 50/52); 2005 World Summit recorded intent to delete, but no formal amendment. Amendment route = Art. 108: two-thirds of GA plus ratification by two-thirds of members including all five P5. Japan sought deletion in Sept 2026; China and Russia refused.

Art. 201: what happens when the President directs the Governor to return a non-Money Bill and the House re-passes it?	The House must reconsider within six months. If re-passed, the President is NOT bound to assent (contrast Art. 111, where assent to a re-passed Bill is compulsory). Art. 200: Governor may assent, withhold, return a non-Money Bill, or reserve it; reservation is mandatory if the Bill endangers the High Court's position.
Preventive detention: what does Art. 22(4) say about detention beyond a certain period?	Detention beyond 3 months needs an Advisory Board report. The 44th Amendment's reduction to 2 months has never been brought into force. Art. 22(1)-(2) rights do not apply to preventive detention.
What did the Supreme Court hold in Mulla Afroz v. Union of India (29 Sep 2026, Sambhal NSA case)?	A confession made in police custody cannot alone ground the detaining authority's satisfaction under the NSA; the State needs cogent material showing a real apprehension of future prejudicial activity. Bench: Justices Dipankar Datta and Sheel Nagu. Detention and Allahabad HC judgment quashed, Rs 10 lakh costs. Confessions to police are inadmissible under s.23 of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023.
