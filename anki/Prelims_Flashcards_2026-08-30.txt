State the 'triple test' for what constitutes an 'industry', and what the 2026 nine-judge Bench held about its continuing application.	Triple test: (i) systematic activity; (ii) cooperation between employer and employees; (iii) production/distribution of goods and services to satisfy human wants. Profit motive is irrelevant; sovereign functions are the main exclusion. Held: the 1978 test continues to govern all PENDING disputes under the repealed ID Act, 1947; Section 2(p) of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 (in force November 2025) must be read on its own terms.
Which two diseases does the NADCP target, and what are their key differences?	Foot and Mouth Disease (FMD) and Brucellosis. FMD: viral, highly contagious, cloven-hoofed animals, vaccination TWICE a year. Brucellosis: bacterial (Brucella abortus), zoonotic, attacks reproduction, vaccinated ONCE in female bovine calves aged 4-8 months. NADCP launched September 2019, 100% central funding for vaccination; 147.16 crore FMD doses; outbreaks fell 132 (2019) to 40 (2025).

How does caste enumeration in Census 2027 differ from SECC 2011?	SECC 2011 was run OUTSIDE the Census Act, 1948 (by MoRD/MoHUA), used open response, and produced over 46 lakh caste names — unusable for policy. Census 2027 puts caste INSIDE the statutory Census, with the confidentiality and legal-penalty architecture of the 1948 Act. The caste column is open-ended for respondents other than SC and ST. The last full caste enumeration in a regular Census was 1931. Census 2027 is India's first DIGITAL Census; sensitive defence establishments along the LAC in Ladakh are recorded on PAPER as 'special charges' to avoid geo-tagging.
Under which Article is the UN Secretary-General appointed, and what vote is needed in the Security Council?	Article 97 of the UN Charter — appointed by the General Assembly upon the RECOMMENDATION of the Security Council. A candidate needs at least 9 of 15 affirmative votes and no veto from any P5 member. Straw polls are informal and anonymous ('Encourage' / 'Discourage' / 'No Opinion'); in later rounds the P5 use differently coloured ballots, so a P5 'Discourage' signals a veto. The Charter fixes NO term — the five-year renewable term and regional rotation are convention. UNGA appointment needs only a simple majority.
India's sugar position 2025-26: production estimate revision, the trigger, and the government's response?	Initial estimate 343 LMT revised down to 306 LMT. Response: duty-free raw sugar import under a Tariff-Rate Quota of up to 10 LMT till 31 October (normal BCD on sugar is 100%); under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955, a 400-tonne dealer stock limit from 1 August to 30 November 2026 and bulk consumers capped at 15 days' consumption from 1 September 2026. Ethanol diversion (~3 MT) was NOT the primary cause. India is the world's second-largest producer (after Brazil) and the largest consumer.

India's three-stage nuclear programme — and which thorium isotope is fertile vs fissile?	Stage I: PHWRs on natural uranium, spent fuel reprocessed for Pu-239. Stage II: Fast Breeder Reactors on Pu-239, breeding more fissile material and converting Th-232 to U-233. Stage III: Th-232/U-233 thermal breeders. Th-232 is FERTILE, not fissile; U-233 is FISSILE. Thorium comes from monazite-bearing coastal beach sands of Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Odisha and West Bengal, plus Jharkhand.

Which 1,800-year-old sculpture reopened the story of the Indian arched harp, and where does that tradition survive today?	A 2nd-century CE (Malava era) Saraswati holding an arched (harp-like) veena, excavated in the 1980s at Manasa, Neemuch district, Madhya Pradesh, housed at the Ujjain Museum. Chronology: rock paintings at Nimbu Bhoj cave near Pachmarhi, references in the Rigveda, treatment in Bharata's Natyashastra, and depiction on the gold coinage of SAMUDRAGUPTA (c. 330-376 CE). The tradition died out in India but survives in MYANMAR as the saung-gauk.
