Global Hunger Index 2026: India's rank and score, and which category?	100th of 122 countries, score 24.1 = 'serious' (serious = 20.0-34.9). Released 14 Oct 2026.
Who publishes the Global Hunger Index?	Concern Worldwide and Welthungerhilfe (with IFHV). Indicators: undernourishment, child stunting, child wasting, child mortality.
GHI 2026: India vs neighbours' ranks?	Pakistan 106, Bangladesh 84, Nepal 72, Sri Lanka 53. India child wasting 19% (stuck high); stunting down from 50% (2000) to 29.3%.
India's Ramsar sites after Oct 2026 additions: total and the two new sites?	103 sites. New: Sukhna Wetland (Chandigarh, Site No. 2602) and Sauj Jheel (Mainpuri, UP, No. 2603). Announced 15 Oct 2026.
Sukhna Wetland: nature, origin and significance?	Man-made reservoir created 1958 by damming the seasonal Sukhna Choe at Shivalik foothills; Chandigarh's first Ramsar site; Central Asian Flyway stopover.
Sauj Jheel (Mainpuri): type and what is notable?	Perennial shallow floodplain lake in Ganga basin, an IBA hosting 30,000+ waterbirds. Mainpuri is the only district with two Ramsar sites (other: Saman Bird Sanctuary, 2019). UP has 14 sites.
IDBI Bank strategic sale (CCEA, 14 Oct 2026): stake, buyer and price?	60.72% stake (GoI 30.48% + LIC 30.24%) to a Fairfax Financial Holdings (Canada) entity for about Rs 52,878 crore. Needs regulatory approvals and an open offer to minority shareholders.
2026 Nobel Prize in Economic Sciences: winners and citation?	Robert E. Hall (Stanford) and Michael Woodford (Columbia), 'for microeconomic foundations of macroeconomic stabilisation'. Announced 12 Oct 2026.
Is the Economics prize an original Nobel Prize?	No. It is the Sveriges Riksbank Prize in Economic Sciences in Memory of Alfred Nobel, established 1968.
RBI's new FX measure of 10 Oct 2026: what must Authorised Dealers hold?	Cash with the RBI equal to 20% of rupee notional on specified rupee FX derivative contracts above US$2 million (Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve).
RBI Oct 2026: limit on FX derivative positions without underlying exposure?	Cut from US$100 million to US$5 million, including on exchanges.
CPI inflation, September 2026: headline and food (CFPI) figures?	Headline 5.54% (23-month high); food (CFPI) 7.74% vs 5.97% in August. Rural 6.08%, urban 4.88%; highest state Tamil Nadu 6.81%.
WPI inflation, September 2026?	11.39% (49-month high) vs 9.92% in August. Fuel and power 25.85%; manufactured products 9.76%; primary articles 8.67%. New 2022-23 base.
PM GatiShakti: launch date, three-tier governance and the seven engines?	Launched 13 Oct 2021. EGoS (chaired by Cabinet Secretary) > NPG (projects above Rs 500 crore) > TSU. Engines: roads, railways, ports, waterways, airports, mass transport, logistics infrastructure.
PM GatiShakti after five years: projects evaluated by the Network Planning Group?	396 projects worth about Rs 18.66 lakh crore (as of Aug 2026); 256 sanctioned, 198 under implementation. Covers 58 Central Ministries/Departments and 36 States/UTs.
PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana: launch, districts, outlay and convergence?	Launched 11 Oct 2025; 100 low-performing agricultural districts; Rs 24,000 crore a year for six years; converges 36 Central schemes of 11 ministries.
PMDDKY year-one district ranking: top three?	Gaya (Bihar) 1st, Nagaur (Rajasthan) 2nd, Umaria (Madhya Pradesh) 3rd. Tracked on 121 indicators with monthly rankings.
Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses: outlay, period, focus crops and procurement?	Rs 11,440 crore, 2025-26 to 2030-31; focus on tur, urad, masoor; 100% MSP procurement by NAFED and NCCF; launched 11 Oct 2025.
First BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting (13 Oct 2026): where, outcome and chair?	New Delhi; 'Delhi Declaration'; chaired by Amit Shah. Seven members: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand.
BIMSTEC basics: founding declaration and Secretariat location?	Bangkok Declaration, 1997; Secretariat in Dhaka; Charter in force 2024.
NSAP extension (Cabinet, 14 Oct 2026): period, outlay and new name?	2026-27 to 2030-31, Rs 53,428 crore; renamed Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana; about 3.20 crore beneficiaries. Linked to Article 41 (DPSP).
NSAP revision: change to disability pension eligibility?	Disability threshold cut from 80% to 40% (aligned with RPwD Act 2016); minimum age condition removed.
NSAP components' new names: IGNOAPS and IGNDPS?	IGNOAPS > PM Vridhavastha Pension Yojana; IGNDPS > PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana (IGNWPS > PM Vidhawa Pension Yojana; NFBS > PM Parivar Labh Yojana).
India-New Zealand FTA: signing date and entry into force?	Signed 27 April 2026; enters into force 20 October 2026. Zero duty on Indian exports to NZ; dairy, sugar, edible oils and most meat excluded.
India-NZ FTA mobility provisions per year?	5,000 Temporary Employment Entry visas and 1,000 Working Holiday visas a year, with post-study work rights.
Gaganyaan: what was validated in Oct 2026 and where?	Crew Module-Service Module separation system, ground-tested at VSSC, Thiruvananthapuram. Uncrewed G1 flight with humanoid Vyommitra: ISRO has said Q4 2026.
DRDO Quantum Key Distribution test (14 Oct 2026): where and why?	At Proof & Experimental Establishment (PXE), Chandipur, Odisha, to secure telemetry from weapon tests. Eavesdropping disturbs quantum states, so it is detectable.
Supreme Court, Oct 2026: key holding on children and the internet?	Bench of Justices J.B. Pardiwala and K.V. Chandran: children have a fundamental right to internet access (Articles 21 and 19(1)(a)); rejected blanket age-based restrictions.
Supreme Court (9 Oct 2026) on Andhra Pradesh's BC quota in local bodies?	Dismissed the State's appeal; HC had quashed 34% reservation as it breached the 50% ceiling without empirical data.
'Triple test' for OBC reservation in local bodies: origin and steps?	K. Krishna Murthy (2010), applied in Vikas Kishanrao Gawali (2021): dedicated commission > local-body-wise proportion > total SC+ST+OBC within 50%.
Indra Sawhney (1992): the 50% reservation ceiling and creamy layer?	50% is a 'rule' with exceptions only in extraordinary situations backed by data; creamy-layer exclusion for OBCs upheld.
Article 243D(6) and 243T(6): what do they enable?	State legislatures may provide reservation of seats for backward classes in Panchayats (243D) and Municipalities (243T).
Jantar Mantar protest site (Oct 2026): what changed and which new venues?	Delhi Police order of 11 Oct 2026 de-notified Jantar Mantar; Ramlila Ground, Burari Ground and CBD Ground (East Delhi) named. Relates to Article 19(1)(b).
J&K and Ladakh HC (Oct 2026): order on Dal Lake?	Banned fuel-powered motorboats and jet skis (incl. motorised shikaras) until further orders; enforcement by LCMA, Tourism Department and Police; next hearing 30 Oct.

CBD COP17: dates and venue?	19-30 October 2026, Yerevan, Armenia (with CP-MOP 12 on biosafety and NP-MOP 6 on ABS); first global review of the Kunming-Montreal Framework.

India approves accession to SIOFA (14 Oct 2026): what is it?	Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement (signed Rome 2006, in force 2012) for non-highly-migratory high-seas stocks; India moves from Cooperating Non-Contracting Party (since 2022) to full participant.
NITI Aayog Data Centre Evaluation Framework (14 Oct 2026): pillars and reporting metrics?	Five pillars: Power & Energy; Water & Cooling; Siting & Environmental Sustainability; Economic & Strategic Value; Policy, Governance & Implementation. Standardised PUE, WUE and CUE reporting.
