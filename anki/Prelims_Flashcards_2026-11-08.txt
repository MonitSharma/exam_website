I-OECM Guidelines 2026 (28 Oct 2026): where were they released, and by whom?	At a side event of CBD COP17, Yerevan (Armenia). By MoEFCC with the National Biodiversity Authority and UNDP.
OECM: what does it stand for and what is it (CBD definition)?	Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures: a geographically defined area OUTSIDE protected areas, governed and managed for positive, long-term biodiversity outcomes.
KMGBF Target 3 (adopted at CBD COP15, Montreal, Dec 2022): what does it say?	'30 by 30': effectively conserve at least 30% of land, inland waters, coastal and marine areas by 2030, via protected areas and OECMs.
India's forex reserves, week ended 23 Oct 2026: level, trend and record high?	$719.75 bn; fell for the 7th straight week. Record: $785.71 bn on 4 Sept 2026. Weekly data released by RBI on Fridays.
Which is the LARGEST component of India's forex reserves, and which valuation effect can change reserves without RBI action?	Foreign currency assets (~$593 bn). Gold/euro/pound/yen price moves change the dollar value (valuation effect). Other components: gold, SDRs, IMF reserve position.
National Social Security Board (notified 28-30 Oct 2026): under which law and who chairs it?	Code on Social Security, 2020; chaired by the Union Labour Minister. Covers unorganised, gig and platform workers; must meet at least 3 times a year.
Aggregator contribution under the Code on Social Security, 2020?	1-2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of the amount paid to gig and platform workers.
DRDO's SSPL (Solid State Physics Laboratory): what wafers has it developed processes for, and why do they matter?	4-inch Silicon Carbide (SiC) wafers, the substrate for GaN HEMTs and X-band MMICs used in radar and electronic warfare.
Band gaps: Si vs SiC vs GaN?	Si ~1.12 eV; SiC ~3.3 eV; GaN ~3.4 eV. Wide band gap = higher breakdown field, temperature and frequency capability.
First BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise (from 30 Oct 2026): who hosts it and where?	Andaman and Nicobar Command (ANC), at Sri Vijaya Puram (renamed Port Blair).
BIMSTEC: number of members and Secretariat location?	7 members (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand). Secretariat: Dhaka.
Next Generation Corvettes contract (2 Nov 2026): value and split between builders?	Rs 53,866.50 crore for 8 ships: Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) 5 + Goa Shipyard Ltd (GSL) 3. Both are defence PSUs; Navy-designed.
Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026: dates, venue, and which ISA session was co-located?	2-5 Nov 2026, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi; co-located with the 9th ISA Assembly and the 4th International Conference on Green Hydrogen.
International Solar Alliance (ISA): where and when launched, and HQ?	Launched by India and France at COP21, Paris (2015). Headquarters: Gurugram.
Census 2027: statutory basis and which Union List entry covers Census?	Census Act, 1948 (run by the Registrar General and Census Commissioner under MHA). 'Census' is Entry 69 of the Union List. India's first digital census; caste recorded for all.
Cabinet (4 Nov 2026) grid-storage VGF scheme: target capacity and its split?	92 GWh: ~30 GWh battery + ~60 GWh pumped storage + ~2 GWh emerging technologies (FY27-FY31). Outlay widely reported as Rs 14,500 crore.
Cabinet (4 Nov 2026) critical-mineral processing scheme: outlay and incentive?	Rs 2,814 crore; 30% capital subsidy for domestic lithium and nickel processing (cobalt as by-product); five-year scheme FY27-FY31.
VISTAR (Cabinet, 4 Nov 2026): full form, ministry and outlay?	Viksit Bharat Initiative for Statistical Transformation, Analytics & Research; MoSPI; Rs 4,371.10 crore, 2026-27 to 2030-31. Includes Economic Census-8.
Ganga Water Treaty (India-Bangladesh): year, signatories, expiry and bilateral mechanism?	Signed 1996 (Deve Gowda and Sheikh Hasina), 30-year term, expires Dec 2026. Covers dry-season flows at Farakka. Mechanism: Joint Rivers Commission (set up 1972). Teesta is NOT covered.
IndiaAI Mission foundation models released 3 Nov 2026: names and developers?	EKA-1 (Soket Labs; ~26.3 bn parameters; English + 22 scheduled languages) and Dhir 2B/5B (ZenteiQ; open source; English + 10 Indian languages). Repository: AIKosh. Ministry: MeitY.
PAP vs ILP: who needs which permit, and under what law?	Protected Area Permit (PAP): foreign nationals; Foreigners (Protected Areas) Order, 1958; Centre/MHA. Inner Line Permit (ILP): Indian citizens from other states; Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation, 1873.
COP31 (UNFCCC, 2026): venue and dates?	Antalya, Turkiye; 9-20 Nov 2026. Turkish presidency proposed a voluntary '35 by 2035' electrification target; India has said it is undecided.
Agnipath scheme: launch date and the Cabinet decision of 4 Nov 2026?	Introduced 14 June 2022 (4-year term, ~25% retained in regular cadre). Cabinet gave a 2-year extension (path to 6 years of service) to Agniveers due to exit.
Fast Track Special Courts (FTSC) scheme: Cabinet decision of 4 Nov 2026?	Continued for 5 years (1 Apr 2026 to 31 Mar 2031), Rs 3,788.04 crore; courts rise from 779 to 850; Centre's share from the Nirbhaya Fund. Began 2 Oct 2019.
Supreme Court, Sujit Das v. Union of India (5 Nov 2026): what did it hold on Article 22(2)?	The 24-hour limit for producing a person before a Magistrate runs from detention (curtailment of liberty), not formal arrest. Only travel time is excludable. Bench: Justices Ujjal Bhuyan and Atul S. Chandurkar.
CERT-In advisory CIAD-2026-0049 (15 Oct 2026): what is its subject and the reporting window?	AI agents as attack vectors. Cyber incidents reportable within 6 hours under CERT-In Directions of 28 Apr 2022 (Section 70B(6), IT Act 2000).
MALABAR 2026: venue, Indian ship and participants?	Yokosuka, Japan; INS Sahyadri; India, Japan, US and Australia (Quad navies). Began in 1992 as an India-US bilateral exercise.
SBM(G) Phase-II: new end date and funding patterns?	Extended to 31 March 2028. Funding 90:10 for NE and Himalayan states, 60:40 for other states, 100:0 for UTs. About 5.70 lakh of 5.86 lakh villages are ODF Plus.
Immigration and Foreigners (Exemption) Order amendment (notified 4 Nov 2026): what changed?	Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis and Christians from Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan who entered by 31 Dec 2024 without valid documents now need an appropriate visa to stay. Made under Section 33 of the Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025.
Pralay missile (tested 3 Nov 2026): type, developer and test site?	Surface-to-surface quasi-ballistic, solid-propellant, road-mobile missile; developed by DRDO's Research Centre Imarat, Hyderabad (BDL and BEL partners); reported range 150-500 km; tested from ITR Chandipur, Odisha.
UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2026: headline finding on 1.5 degrees C?	Chance of holding 1.5 degrees C with no or limited overshoot has been lost; aim is to limit overshoot and return below 1.5 degrees C by 2100. Global GHG emissions 58.4 GtCO2e in 2025.
UPI MDR framework (Finance Ministry notification, 14 Sep 2026): the key rates?	Zero MDR on UPI P2P (any amount) and on P2M up to Rs 2,000; above Rs 2,000, MDR capped at 0.4% (max Rs 300) for eligible P2M. Matter is before the Supreme Court; NPCI argues it is not 'State' under Article 12.
Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026: what does it replace, under which Act and from when?	Replaces the Aircraft Rules, 1937; framed under the Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024 (which replaced the Aircraft Act, 1934); in force 1 Jan 2027. Airlines must disclose the total ticket price.
EPFO wage ceiling (Cabinet, 16 Sep 2026): old and new limit?	Raised from Rs 15,000 to Rs 25,000 per month (unchanged since 2014); over 51 lakh more employees under mandatory EPF/EPS cover.
VB-G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026 (2 Nov): tiers and timelines?	Three tiers: Gram Panchayat, Block, District. Enquiry within 7 working days, resolution within 15 days, then automatic escalation to the next level. Rules under the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 (Rural Development).
India's Biosphere Reserves: how many, first, largest and smallest?	18 notified (13 in UNESCO's World Network). First: Nilgiri (1986). Largest: Great Rann of Kutch. Smallest: Dibru-Saikhowa. UNESCO MAB Programme launched 1971; funding 60:40 (90:10 for NE and Himalayan states).
Vande Mataram: author, first publication and constituent assembly status?	Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay (7 Nov 1875); first in Bangadarshan, later in Anandamath (1882). Constituent Assembly gave it status equal to Jana Gana Mana on 24 Jan 1950 (150-year commemoration ended 7 Nov 2026).
Static: which channel separates the Andaman Islands from the Nicobar Islands?	Ten Degree Channel (10 degrees N).
Static: which Constituent Amendment added the Twelfth Schedule and how many items?	74th Amendment Act, 1992 (Part IXA); Twelfth Schedule has 18 items. The 73rd Amendment added the Eleventh Schedule (29 items).
Static: Indus Waters Treaty, 1960 - which rivers go to India and does it have an expiry?	Eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) to India; western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) to Pakistan, with run-of-river hydro allowed to India. World Bank signed for limited purposes. No fixed expiry.
