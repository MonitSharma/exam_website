What was India's WPI inflation in August 2026, on which base year, and which group led?	9.92% YoY (July 9.78%); all-commodities index 110.8; base year 2022-23. Which body compiles the WPI, and which compiles the CPI?	WPI: Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry. CPI: National Statistical Office (NSO), MoSPI. WPI covers goods only, with no services component; the RBI's statutory inflation target under Section 45ZA of the RBI Act is CPI-Combined at 4% +/- 2%.

How was the sugar stockholding limit for bulk consumers relaxed in September 2026, and with what condition?	Raised from 15 days' to 30 days' consumption, but stock beyond 15 days must come ONLY from sugar imported under the Advance Authorisation Scheme and Tariff Rate Quota; open-market purchases remain capped at 15 days. Parent order: Sugar (Stockholding Limit of Bulk Consumers) Order, 2026, in force 1 September to 30 November 2026; a bulk consumer uses more than 10 MT per month. Statutory basis: Essential Commodities Act, 1955.
What did India and MERCOSUR sign in September 2026, and what are the basic facts of their PTA?	The First Additional Protocol to the 2004 PTA, giving electronic Certificates of Origin legal parity with paper. The PTA was signed at New Delhi on 25 January 2004 and entered into force on 1 June 2009; concessions cover only 450 tariff lines by India and 452 by MERCOSUR. Bilateral trade in 2025-26 was about US$21.8 billion.
Which treaty founded MERCOSUR, which protocol made it a customs union, and what is its membership?	Treaty of Asuncion, 26 March 1991; the Ouro Preto Protocol, 1994 formalised the customs union with a Common External Tariff (which is why members cannot grant tariff concessions individually). Founding four: Argentina, Brazil, Paraguay, Uruguay. Bolivia became a full member in July 2024; Venezuela has been suspended since December 2016. Rotating six-monthly Pro Tempore Presidency.

Under the 1963 Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement, what was ceded, and what does its Article 6 provide?	Pakistan ceded about 5,180 sq km of the Shaksgam Valley / Trans-Karakoram Tract to China (signed 2 March 1963); India has never recognised it. Article 6 makes the settlement PROVISIONAL: after the Kashmir dispute is resolved, the sovereign authority concerned is to reopen negotiations with China for a replacement boundary treaty.
Where is the Shaksgam Valley, and which pass is the operative China-Pakistan road crossing?	Shaksgam lies north of the Siachen Glacier, beyond the Karakoram watershed, drained by the Shaksgam river (a tributary of the Yarkand); China administers it as part of Xinjiang. The Karakoram Highway crosses at the KHUNJERAB Pass (4,693 m), NOT the Karakoram Pass - a distinction UPSC has exploited. Aksai Chin, held by China since 1962, is a separate dispute from the 1963 cession.

What did six Chief Ministers sign on 15 September 2026, on which river, and with what design figures?	The Kishau Multipurpose Project agreement, by the CMs of Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, Delhi and Haryana. Under which constitutional provisions do inter-State river water disputes fall?	Water is Entry 17 of the State List; the Union holds Entry 56 of the Union List over regulation of inter-State rivers. Article 262 lets Parliament provide for adjudication of inter-State water disputes AND bar the jurisdiction of courts - giving the Inter-State River Water Disputes Act, 1956 and the River Boards Act, 1956. Kishau is an example of settlement by negotiated agreement rather than tribunal.
Under which Articles are High Court judges and Additional Judges appointed, and what is the retirement age?	Article 217 - appointment by the President after consultation with the CJI, the Governor, and (for judges other than the Chief Justice) the Chief Justice of that High Court; qualification is ten years' judicial office OR ten years as an advocate of a High Court; no minimum age prescribed; retirement at 62. Article 224 - Additional Judges, for a period not exceeding two years. Article 216 fixes no strength; Article 222 governs transfer; Article 231 allows a common High Court.
Which statutory provision did the Supreme Court invoke in September 2026 to make RBI's loan-recovery directions binding?	Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, which gives RBI directions statutory force. In Hari Dutta Sharma v. State of U.P. (2026 INSC 998), the Court held that arbitrary, unnotified night-time seizure of a vehicle depriving a person of livelihood violates Articles 14 and 21, relying on ICICI Bank v. Prakash Kaur (2007) that banks cannot employ 'goondas' to seize vehicles.

Under which Act was the NGT established, where are its benches, and which major environmental law is NOT in its Schedule I?	The National Green Tribunal Act, 2010; India was among the first countries after Australia and New Zealand to set up a dedicated environmental court. Principal Bench at New Delhi, with zonal benches at Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata and Chennai. Schedule I does NOT include the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972. The NGT is not bound by the CPC or the Evidence Act, applies sustainable development, precautionary and polluter-pays principles, and appeals lie to the Supreme Court.

What is the current status of Project Cheetah, and when was the cheetah declared extinct in India?	The Asiatic cheetah was declared extinct in India in 1952; translocation from Namibia and South Africa began in September 2022 at Kuno National Park, Madhya Pradesh. From 29 founders the population is now 52, with 49 cubs born in India and about 68% cub survival at Kuno. Managed habitat is 3,428 sq km across Kuno and Gandhi Sagar; Banni Grasslands (Gujarat) and Nauradehi WLS (MP) are future release sites.
What is the status of the Great Indian Bustard, and what is the 'jump-start' breeding technique?	Ardeotis nigriceps - IUCN Critically Endangered, Schedule I of the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, State bird of Rajasthan. The 'jump-start' technique swaps infertile wild-laid eggs for viable eggs from the conservation-breeding programme. Gujarat's 2026 action plan targets a minimum viable population of 30+ birds over 200-500 sq km, centred on the Lala-Rawa landscape in Kachchh, with a Soft Release Centre near Naliya. The Wildlife Institute of India, Dehradun, is the technical author.
What did the 2026 Himalayan monal study find, and what is the bird's status?	Monals in noisier areas call at higher frequency, greater amplitude and longer duration - adaptive vocal plasticity escaping ACOUSTIC MASKING, since traffic noise is concentrated at low frequencies (compare the Lombard effect). HNB Garhwal University researchers compared roadless Madmaheshwar Valley with tourist-heavy Chopta Valley inside Kedarnath Wildlife (Musk Deer) Sanctuary, Uttarakhand. Lophophorus impejanus is a pheasant, the State bird of Uttarakhand and the NATIONAL BIRD OF NEPAL (danphe); IUCN Least Concern.
What was India's first soil-carbon payment, and under which methodology were the credits issued?	Over Rs 2.9 crore transferred by DBT to 2,550 smallholder farmers in Punjab and Haryana at Punjab Agricultural University, Ludhiana (16-17 September 2026) - the first instance in India of farmers being paid for carbon stored in their soil. Programme: 'Aadi', run by Grow Indigo with ICAR guidance; first issuance about 30,000 acres and 50,000+ credits.

What did the Health Ministry's September 2026 advisory say about stem cell therapy and autism?	Stem cell therapy may be offered as standard clinical care only for MoHFW-approved indications; for Autism Spectrum Disorder, use of ANY type of stem cell is restricted to duly approved clinical trials. Governing framework: the National Guidelines for Stem Cell Research, 2017, issued jointly by ICMR and DBT. The one established use is haematopoietic stem cell transplantation for defined blood disorders. Enforcement runs through the NMC (professional misconduct), CDSCO under the New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, and the Clinical Establishments Act, 2010.
What did TRAI's Telecom Commercial Communication Customer Preference (Third Amendment) Regulations, 2026 change?	New Regulation 21A makes AI/ML detection of spam-prone sender CLIs binding on telecom service providers, with intelligence shared across operators. The complaint threshold for action falls from 5 to 3 unique complaints in ten days when corroborated by AI/ML flagging. A2P calls - initiated by an application or automated platform without human dialling, including robo-calls - must be pre-declared, or they are treated as UCC; a termination charge of up to Rs 0.05 per minute applies. The 140xx, 1600xx and 1601xx series are protected from spam flagging.

What is Gondhal, and why was it in the news in September 2026?	Gondhal is a ritual devotional folk performance of MAHARASHTRA (and parts of northern Karnataka), performed by hereditary gondhalis, invoking the goddesses Renuka (Mahur) and Tulja Bhavani (Tuljapur), and in places Khandoba, often through the night. Its instruments are the SAMBAL (twin waist drums) and the TUNTUNE (single-string drone). On 18 September 2026 the Film Federation of India chose the Marathi film 'Gondhal' by Santosh Davakhar, from 33 films, as India's entry for Best International Feature Film at the 99th Academy Awards.
