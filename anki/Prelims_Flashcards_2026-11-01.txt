FNTA: which constitutional article gives Nagaland special provisions (link for the Naga settlement)?	Article 371A (Nagaland).
AATAL (inaugurated 24 Oct 2026): full form/purpose, ministry and host institute?	Integrated Atmospheric Chemistry and Aerosol Toxicity Analysis Laboratory; MoES; at IITM's Delhi branch; source apportionment of Delhi-NCR air pollution; under Mission Mausam.
Siang Upper Multipurpose Project: capacity, developer, state, and strategic rationale?	11,200 MW; NHPC; Arunachal Pradesh (Siang = Brahmaputra in Arunachal). Seen as counter to China's Medog project on the Yarlung Tsangpo.
Operation Trident (Oct 2026): agency, region and legal tools?	Narcotics Control Bureau (MHA) with ED and State police; Myanmar-linked narco-financial network in Assam, Mizoram, Manipur; NDPS Act 1985 and PMLA 2002. NCB constituted 1986.
RBI's new Data Centre inaugurated by PM on 25 Oct 2026: where, and which primary data centre does it back up?	Near Bhubaneswar (Khordha district, Info Valley-II), Odisha; backs up RBI's primary data centre at Kharghar, Navi Mumbai.
India's medal count at the 2026 Asian Para Games (Aichi-Nagoya, Japan)?	Record 114 medals (18-24 Oct 2026).
PAIMANA (MoSPI): what does it monitor and above what project cost?	Central Sector infrastructure projects costing Rs 150 crore and above. Sept 2026 flash report: 1,853 projects, revised cost Rs 37.56 lakh crore. Mega projects >= Rs 1,000 crore.
TDB (DST) Rs 116 crore RDI Fund support to Manastu Space: for what technology?	Indigenous green propulsion - hydrogen-peroxide-based propellant for satellites/launch vehicles (alternative to toxic hydrazine). TDB is a statutory body under DST.
CMVR 1989 amendment (Oct 2026) permits which flex-fuel vehicles?	E85 and E100 flex-fuel vehicles (E85 = 85% ethanol + 15% petrol). Only for vehicles designed and certified for it; existing E20 cars cannot run E100.
Cyclone 'Arnab' (landfall 28 Oct 2026): where, and which country proposed the name?	Andhra Pradesh-Yanam coast (near Narsapur); name proposed by Bangladesh (WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones list).
Which institution issues cyclone bulletins for the North Indian Ocean (RSMC)?	IMD, RSMC New Delhi.
Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (Cabinet, 28 Oct 2026): outlay, duration and capacity target?	Rs 10,000 crore; up to FY 2039-40; raise annual container capacity ~10x to 7.5 lakh TEUs. Includes 3% interest subvention.
PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM-VGSY): outlay, period, nature?	Rs 7,395.55 crore (Centre Rs 4,869.48 cr, States Rs 2,526.07 cr); 2026-27 to 2030-31; Centrally Sponsored Scheme for Panchayat capacity building (~2.55 lakh Panchayats).
Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme (Cabinet, Oct 2026): outlay and period?	Rs 1,856.67 crore; 2026-27 to 2033-34.

India at CBD COP-17 (Oct 2026): venue, and name of the fund for digital sequence information (DSI) benefit-sharing?	Yerevan (Armenia); Cali Fund. Framework: Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework.
Which CBD protocol covers biosafety of living modified organisms, and which covers access and benefit-sharing?	Cartagena Protocol = biosafety (LMOs); Nagoya Protocol = access and benefit-sharing (ABS).
I-OECMs: expand OECM and why they matter (India released guidelines at COP-17)	Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures - conservation outside formal protected areas; count towards the 30x30 target of the KMGBF.
Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY): ministry, launch year, helpline?	Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment; umbrella scheme for senior citizens launched 2021 (subsumed NAPSrC); Elderline 14567. Legal frame: Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007.
National Startup Awards 6.0: application window and prize per winning startup?	27 Oct - 20 Nov 2026; Rs 25 lakh per winning startup per category (DPIIT). Startup India launched 16 Jan 2016.

National Social Security Board for gig/platform and unorganised workers: statutory basis and chair?	Constituted under the Code on Social Security; chaired by the Union Labour & Employment Minister.

NCAP: launch year and number of cities covered?	Launched 2019; 130 cities. NAAQS annual limits: PM2.5 40 ug/m3, PM10 60 ug/m3.
