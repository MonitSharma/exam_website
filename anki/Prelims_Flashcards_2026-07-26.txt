Semicon 2.0 (India Semiconductor Mission 2.0), approved by the Union Cabinet on 15 July 2026 — what is its outlay, and what was Semicon 1.0's?	Semicon 2.0: Rs 1,27,500 crore; Semicon 1.0: Rs 76,000 crore. Targets Rs 4 lakh crore of investment and Rs 2 lakh crore of semiconductor production during the scheme period.
What are the six pillars of Semicon 2.0, and which layer does it fund that Semicon 1.0 did not?	Chip design; equipment & materials (machines, chemicals, gases); fabs; ATMP/OSAT; R&D; talent. The new element is the upstream equipment-materials-chemicals-gases layer.
Under which Article is the Election Commission of India constituted, and can it adjudicate citizenship?	Article 324. No — the ECI has no power to adjudicate citizenship; suspected non-citizens must be referred to the competent authority under the Citizenship Act, 1955 (Foreigners Tribunals under the Foreigners Act, 1946, in Assam).
Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls is conducted under which statutes?	Representation of the People Act, 1950 and the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960. The Supreme Court held its outcome relates only to electoral rolls — not to citizenship or welfare entitlements.
Gitchak nakana, in the news in July 2026, is what?	The first subterranean (aquifer-dwelling) fish recorded from Assam — eyeless and lacking a skull roof; collected from a dug well in Goalpara district. (Stygobites = obligate groundwater dwellers; troglobites = obligate cave dwellers.)
Which is India's deepest natural freshwater lake, where does it lie, and is it a Ramsar site?	Mansbal (Manasbal) Lake, about 43 feet deep, in Ganderbal district, central Kashmir. It is NOT a Ramsar site — J&K's Ramsar sites include Wular and Hokersar. Restored by the Wular-Manasbal Development Authority; a node on the Central Asian Flyway.
Cyclospora cayetanensis belongs to which phylum, and how does it spread?	Phylum Apicomplexa (same as Plasmodium). Strictly faeco-oral, with NO person-to-person spread — oocysts must sporulate in the environment first; the oocysts are chlorine-resistant, so washing produce is not reliable control.
After the July 2026 rebasing, what is the base year of the Index of Core Industries and which industry became the 9th core industry?	Base year 2022-23 (replacing 2011-12); iron ore was added as the 9th core industry. Released by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT. Electricity carries the highest weight (30.932).
How many pumped-storage projects (PSPs) are under construction in India as of July 2026, and of what capacity?	11 PSPs totalling 15,870 MW under construction; 6 more (8,140 MW) have CEA concurrence but construction has not begun. The CEA cut the DPR concurrence timeline from 90 days to 50 days.
Which Act protects the National Anthem, and what does the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026 add?	The Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971 (Sec. 2 — Flag and Constitution; Sec. 3 — National Anthem). The 2026 Bill, moved by Amit Shah in the Rajya Sabha, extends the same protection to the National Song 'Vande Mataram' — up to 3 years' imprisonment, fine, or both.
Who composed 'Vande Mataram', in which year and novel was it published, and when was it given equal status with 'Jana Gana Mana'?	Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay, composed 1875, published in Anandamath (1882); first sung at the 1896 Calcutta session of the INC. Constituent Assembly President Dr. Rajendra Prasad's statement of 24 January 1950 gave it equal status — a statement, not a constitutional provision.
What was India's WPI inflation in June 2026, and how did it compare with CPI?	WPI 9.87% (May: 9.68%) against CPI 4.38% — a divergence of about 5.5 percentage points. WPI excludes services and is released by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT; the RBI targets CPI, released by the NSO, MoSPI.
Where is India's second spaceport being built, and which launch vehicle will it primarily serve?	Kulasekarapattinam, Thoothukudi district, Tamil Nadu — about 2,300 acres, roughly Rs 950 crore; dedicated primarily to the SSLV (~500 kg to LEO), designed for about 25 launches a year. Its southern-tip location allows a direct southward launch, avoiding Sriharikota's fuel-costly 'dogleg' around Sri Lanka.
To which two Central Asian countries did India export the Akash SAM in July 2026, and who was Akash's first foreign buyer?	Tajikistan and Turkmenistan. Armenia was the first foreign buyer (Akash-1S). Akash is a DRDO-developed medium-range SAM (~25 km range), produced by BEL and BDL.
The Belwa–Minapur Link Channel inaugurated in Bihar links which two rivers, and at what length and cost?	Diverts surplus Bagmati floodwater into the Burhi Gandak — 68.80 km, Rs 130.88 crore; diverts up to 50,000 cusecs at peak monsoon and adds about 1,35,000 hectares of assured irrigation. Runs from Belwa (Sheohar district) to Minapur (Muzaffarpur district).
The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026 raises the sanctioned strength to what, and under which Article?	From 34 to 38 judges including the CJI (33 to 37 excluding the CJI), under Article 124(1) — an ordinary law passed by simple majority, not a constitutional amendment. The previous hike (31 to 34) was in 2019.
What is QDENGA (TAK-003), approved by the DCGI on 20 July 2026?	India's first approved dengue vaccine, from Takeda — live-attenuated and tetravalent (covers all four serotypes), for ages 4-60, two doses three months apart, with no pre-vaccination screening required.
What is 'Bizum', which India agreed to link with UPI in July 2026?	Spain's domestic instant mobile-payment platform. NPCI International (NIPL) drives such tie-ups; UPI is already live in about 9-10 countries, with Greece the latest.
Which state cleared a Uniform Civil Code Bill in July 2026, who headed the drafting committee, and which Article envisages a UCC?	Madhya Pradesh — the second state after Uttarakhand; committee headed by Justice (Retd.) Ranjana Prakash Desai; Article 44 (Directive Principles). Tribal communities are excluded from the draft.
What are the targets of NITI Aayog's Bioeconomy Roadmap 2035, and the size of the BioEconomy Growth Fund?	Bioeconomy to grow from about USD 195 billion (2025) to USD 691 billion by 2035 (USD 2.6 trillion by 2047); BioEconomy Growth Fund of Rs 50,000 crore (2026-35), embedded in the Rs 1-lakh-crore RDI Scheme. BioE3 = Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment.
Which UNCLOS article governs the 'island vs rock' distinction, and which feature revived the debate in July 2026?	Article 121 — islands generate a 200-nm EEZ and continental shelf; rocks that cannot sustain human habitation or economic life do not. Okinotorishima in the Philippine Sea, where China held a live-fire naval drill inside Japan's claimed EEZ on 20 July 2026.
Five new lichen species of which genus were described from the Western Ghats in July 2026, and by which institute?	Genus Allographa (family Graphidaceae) — by MACS-Agharkar Research Institute (ARI), Pune, an autonomous institute under the Department of Science & Technology. Markers used: mtSSU, nrLSU and RPB2.
What reservation for ex-Agniveers did the Centre notify in the CAPFs and Assam Rifles in July 2026?	50% of Constable (General Duty) posts in the CAPFs and Rifleman posts in the Assam Rifles, with 3 years' age relaxation (5 extra years, total 8, for the first batch) and exemption from the PST, PET and written exam.
Which state topped NITI Aayog's first Investment Friendliness Index (IFI) 2026, and with what score?	Gujarat, 56.6/100 — followed by Maharashtra (53.7), Tamil Nadu (53.3), Goa (53.1) and Odisha (52.4). Built on 84 final indicators across 8 pillars; Infrastructure carries the highest weight (25%).
What is 'AAROH', released by the Ministry of Coal on 22 July 2026?	India's first Annual Report on Mine Closure, documenting the scientific closure of 42 coal mines. Released alongside an India-Germany (GIZ) implementation agreement and the 'Coal NEER' water plants; mine closure is funded through an Escrow account mechanism.
Where were India's first geothermal wells commissioned, by which agency, and why is the site geothermally active?	Puga Valley, Changthang region, south-eastern Ladakh (over 14,000 ft), by the ONGC Energy Centre with Iceland's ISOR as consultant — two wells about 1,000 m deep, 135 degrees C recorded at just 400 m, feeding India's first 1-MW geothermal pilot. The heat comes from the Indus Suture Zone; GSI pegs India's geothermal potential at about 10 GW.
Which agency designed and which firm delivered India's first indigenous 350-kg-thrust expendable turbojet engine (22 July 2026)?	Designed by DRDO's Gas Turbine Research Establishment (GTRE), Bengaluru — which also runs the Kaveri programme; manufactured, assembled and delivered by Azad Engineering, Hyderabad. Intended for India's medium-range anti-ship missile, with spillover to UAVs and cruise missiles.
Per SOFI 2026, what is India's prevalence of undernourishment, and how many Indians cannot afford a healthy diet?	142.5 million people (9.8%) in 2023-25, down from 243.9 million (21.1%) in 2004-06. 520.1 million Indians (35.5%) could not afford a healthy diet in 2025, which costs PPP $4.11 per person per day — up nearly 48% since 2017. Child stunting 32.9%, wasting 18.7%.
Which five UN agencies jointly publish the State of Food Security and Nutrition in the World (SOFI) report?	FAO, IFAD, UNICEF, WFP and WHO.
Chandipura virus belongs to which family and genus, and what is its vector?	Family Rhabdoviridae (same as rabies), genus Vesiculovirus; the vector is the sandfly (Phlebotomus and Sergentomyia spp.), NOT mosquitoes. First isolated in 1965 at Chandipura village, Nagpur district, Maharashtra; causes Acute Encephalitis Syndrome mostly in children under 15, with 57-70% case fatality.
Under which Act did the Puri Jagannath temple secure trademarks over 'Jagannath Dham' and 'Mahaprasad'?	The Trade Marks Act, 1999 (registration valid 10 years, indefinitely renewable) — NOT the Geographical Indications of Goods Act, 1999. Registered by Intellectual Property India under DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry.
What is the outlay and duration of the Regional Connectivity Scheme – Modified UDAN approved in July 2026?	Rs 28,840 crore over 10 years (FY 2026-27 to FY 2035-36), of which Rs 10,043 crore is viability gap funding and Rs 12,159 crore is for developing 100 airports from unserved airstrips; 200 heliports planned; targets 120 new destinations and 4 crore passengers. Scope now extends to heliports and seaplanes.
What is Vikram-1, in the news in July 2026?	A privately built Indian rocket from Skyroot Aerospace that achieved India's first successful private orbital launch.
What is Horizon Europe, and what did the third India–EU TTC agree on it?	The EU's flagship research and innovation programme, about EUR 93.5 billion. The third India-EU Trade and Technology Council (Brussels, 15 July 2026) agreed to open formal negotiations on India's association, targeted to take effect from 2027.
Who was appointed CEO of NITI Aayog in July 2026?	Anurag Jain, a 1989-batch IAS officer of the Madhya Pradesh cadre and former MP Chief Secretary, for a two-year tenure — succeeding B.V.R. Subrahmanyam. NITI Aayog is chaired by the Prime Minister.
What is BHAVYA-Rasayan, approved by the Cabinet on 24 July 2026?	Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojana – Rasayan: Rs 3,030 crore over FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31 for three greenfield chemical parks; central grant of up to Rs 1,000 crore per park against a minimum Rs 500 crore state contribution; each park needs at least 8 sq km (about 2,000 acres) of contiguous land with CETP and TSDF.
Are offences under the Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act, 2024 cognizable, and is the NTA a statutory body?	All offences are cognizable, non-bailable and non-compoundable, and the Act does NOT penalise candidates. Organised crime attracts 5-10 years plus a fine of at least Rs 1 crore. The NTA is a registered society under the Ministry of Education — not a statutory body.
Distinguish the Market Intervention Scheme (MIS) from the Price Support Scheme (PSS).	MIS covers perishable horticultural/agri commodities NOT under MSP, is triggered on a State's request, and losses are shared 50:50 (75:25 for NE states). PSS covers notified MSP crops (oilseeds, pulses, copra). Both are implemented through NAFED/NCCF.
What are the targets of the Nuclear Energy Mission for Viksit Bharat?	100 GW of nuclear power by 2047, announced in Union Budget 2025-26; at least five indigenous Small Modular Reactors (SMRs) operational by 2033, backed by Rs 20,000 crore. The SHANTI Act, 2025 opens the sector to private participation.
What is the status of the India–Israel FTA, and what was bilateral merchandise trade in FY 2025-26?	The second round of negotiations concluded 20-23 July 2026 in New Delhi (Terms of Reference signed November 2025; first round February 2026). India-Israel merchandise trade was USD 3.93 billion in FY 2025-26 — the UAE, not Israel, is India's largest West Asian partner.
