Rs 3,000 crore, five-year (FY2026-27 to FY2030-31) scheme to conserve ~15,000 sacred groves; funded via National CAMPA (statutory under CAF Act, 2016); cleared at the 7th National CAMPA Governing Body meeting, Coimbatore

What is ACITI, launched 9 July 2026, and what are its four focus areas?	Australia-Canada-India Technology and Innovation Partnership — AI, green-energy tech, critical minerals, resilient supply chains. Conceived at the G20 Johannesburg Summit (Nov 2025)
Which commodity did China ban the export of on 10 July 2026, and why does it matter to India?	Helium (temporary ban). India imports nearly all its helium; ISRO uses it to pressurise cryogenic/liquid fuel tanks, and semiconductor fabs need it for cooling/inert atmospheres. China itself imports >80% of its helium and produces only ~1.6% of world supply
Which two countries dominate global helium supply?	USA (Federal Helium Reserve, Texas) and Qatar. Helium is a noble gas, second-lightest element, non-renewable (from radioactive decay in natural-gas fields)

What did IITM Pune's 2026 study find about monsoon raindrop evaporation over the Western Ghats?	On average ~25% of rain mass evaporates before reaching the ground (daily range 4-61%), using a stable-isotope technique (enrichment in 18-O / 2-H). Published in Atmospheric Chemistry and Physics; led by Saikat Sengupta. IITM is under the Ministry of Earth Sciences
