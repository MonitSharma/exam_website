Which ISRO engine was hot-tested at 100% (full 200-tonne thrust) for the first time on 5 September 2026, and where?	The SCE-200 / SE-2000 semi-cryogenic engine's Power Head Test Article (PHTA), at the SIET facility, ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC), Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu. 9th PHTA test; 35 seconds total, 5 seconds at full power.
What propellants and cycle does the SCE-200 use, and which LVM3 stage will the SC120 semi-cryogenic stage replace?	LOX + ISROSENE (refined kerosene, RP-1 class), oxidiser-rich staged combustion, 2,000 kN thrust class, developed by LPSC. SC120 is to replace the L110 core stage (two Vikas engines) of LVM3; SCE-200 is also the NGLV booster engine.
When was India's first National Policy on Geothermal Energy notified, and by which ministry?	15 September 2025, by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE). Single-window clearance, 100% FDI, viability gap funding, and provision for repurposing abandoned oil and gas wells.
Where were India's first two geothermal wells commissioned, and what is India's assessed geothermal potential?	Puga Valley, Changthang region, eastern Ladakh (over 14,000 ft), July 2026 — each drilled to 1,000 m, about 135 degC at 400 m. GSI estimates about 10,600 MW from 381 thermally anomalous areas in 10 geothermal provinces.
From 4 September 2026, what does NPCI's UPI masking mandate require?	Apps may display only the last four digits of a user's registered mobile number to the counterparty; account numbers and VPAs are also masked; for QR payments the number is hidden even after the transaction.
When was NPCI set up, at whose initiative, and under which Act?	2008, at the initiative of the RBI and the Indian Banks' Association, under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007; incorporated as a not-for-profit (Section 8) company.
Which language was recognised as an official and working language of the SCO at the 2026 Bishkek Summit?	English — an India-supported move. The 26th Council of Heads of State met at Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, 31 August-1 September 2026, chaired by President Sadyr Japarov, marking the SCO's 25th anniversary; 28 documents adopted.
Anak Krakatau lies in which strait, and on which subduction system?	Sunda Strait, between Java and Sumatra, Indonesia — on the Sunda Arc, where the Indo-Australian plate subducts beneath the Sunda (Eurasian) plate. September 2026 eruption sent ash to about 50,000 ft and closed 8 airports.
What caused the December 2018 Anak Krakatau tsunami, and why was there no warning?	A lateral (flank) collapse during eruption, not a tectonic earthquake — so there was no seismic precursor for earthquake-keyed warning systems. Run-ups up to 13 m; at least 437 dead.
Which is India's only confirmed active volcano?	Barren Island, in the Andaman Sea (Andaman & Nicobar Islands), on the Sunda-Andaman subduction system. Narcondam is classified dormant.
What was the settlement amount in the SEBI-NSE co-location and dark-fibre case closed by the Supreme Court in September 2026?	About Rs 1,491.21 crore, settled under Section 15JB of the SEBI Act, 1992 (inserted by the Securities Laws (Amendment) Act, 2014) read with the SEBI (Settlement Proceedings) Regulations, 2018 — without admission or denial of guilt.
Under which Article is a High Court judge appointed, and at what age does a High Court judge retire?	Article 217(1) — by the President after consultation with the CJI, the Governor, and (except for the Chief Justice) the Chief Justice of that High Court. Retirement at 62 under the Article 217(1) proviso. India has 25 High Courts.
Which Constitutional Amendment created the NJAC, and what happened to it?	The 99th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2014 (with the NJAC Act, 2014) — struck down 4:1 in 2015 as violating judicial independence, part of the basic structure. Justice Chelameswar dissented.
What were India's net direct tax collections in FY 2024-25, per the CBDT Time Series Data?	Rs 22.26 lakh crore, up from Rs 19.60 lakh crore in FY 2023-24. Corporate tax Rs 9.86 lakh crore; non-corporate Rs 12.35 lakh crore — non-corporate now exceeds corporate. Taxpayer base 12.13 crore.
What were India's direct tax-to-GDP ratio, cost of collection and tax buoyancy in FY 2024-25?	Direct tax-to-GDP 6.73%; cost of collection 0.41% (a historic low); tax buoyancy 1.39 — above 1 for the third consecutive year. Buoyancy = (% change in tax revenue) / (% change in nominal GDP).
From what share of their historically recorded nesting sites have vultures disappeared in India?	About 72%. The overall Indian vulture decline exceeded 99%; Gyps indicus and Gyps tenuirostris populations fell about 97.4%.
Which drug caused India's vulture collapse, when was its veterinary use banned, and what is the safe alternative?	Diclofenac, an NSAID given to cattle, causing renal failure and visceral gout in scavenging vultures. Veterinary use banned in 2006; safe alternative is meloxicam. India's first and largest breeding centre is JCBC, Pinjore, Haryana.
What is the outlay of the Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects, and when was it approved?	Rs 37,500 crore, approved by the Union Cabinet on 13 May 2026, administered by the Ministry of Coal. It replaced the Rs 8,500-crore scheme of January 2024. Target: 75 MT under this scheme, within a national goal of 100 MT gasification by 2030.
What was India's total kharif sown area as on 4 September 2026?	1,086.31 lakh hectares, against 1,104 lakh ha a year earlier. Rice fell to 421.82 lakh ha; pulses rose to 117.13 lakh ha (urad and moong); cotton 109.17 lakh ha; oilseeds 184.46 lakh ha.
Whose birth centenary fell on 8 September 2026, and which award made him the first Assamese recipient?	Dr Bhupen Hazarika, the 'Bard of the Brahmaputra'. First Assamese to receive the Dadasaheb Phalke Award (1992). Centenary to culminate at Bharat Mandapam on 29 November 2026, with a Rs 100 commemorative coin.
What is PM-SETU's outlay and financing split?	Rs 60,000 crore — Centre Rs 30,000 crore + States Rs 20,000 crore + Industry Rs 10,000 crore, with ADB and the World Bank co-financing up to 50% of the central share equally. Upgrades 1,000 government ITIs on a hub-and-spoke model; target 20 lakh youth in five years.
What was the value of Acceptance of Necessity cleared by the Defence Acquisition Council on 7 September 2026?	About Rs 1,10,000 crore, with roughly 98% to be sourced from Indian industry. AoN is the in-principle approval that begins procurement under DAP 2020 — not a contract. Cycle: AoN, RFP, Trials & Evaluation, Contract Negotiation, Signing.
Is the National Commission for Safai Karamcharis constitutional, statutory or non-statutory?	Non-statutory. The NCSK Act, 1993 lapsed on 29 February 2004; tenure is extended by Central Government resolutions (present extension to 31 March 2028). Contrast: NCSC (Art. 338), NCST (338A), NCBC (338B) are constitutional; NHRC, NCW, NCPCR are statutory.
Who heads the Asian-Pacific Postal Union, and where is it headquartered?	Dr. Vinaya Prakash Singh (1988-batch Indian Postal Service), re-elected unopposed at the 14th APPU Congress in Bangkok on 4 September 2026 for January 2027-December 2030. APPU: 32 member states, HQ Bangkok; a Restricted Union under Article 8 of the UPU.
Which country's first UNESCO World Heritage Site is the Boma-Badingilo Migratory Landscape?	South Sudan — inscribed July 2026 through an emergency procedure and placed on the List of World Heritage in Danger the same day. It is the setting of the Great Nile Migration, the largest land mammal migration on Earth.
What new constitutional provision did the MHA propose for Ladakh on 9 September 2026?	Article 371(K), a new 'Chapter K' in Part XXI — a directly elected governing body with legislative powers over land and transfer of property, culture and language, forests, environment and natural resources. Models cited: Articles 371A (Nagaland) and 371G (Mizoram). Statehood and Sixth Schedule inclusion were not conceded.
What dedicated new component is PM-KUSUM 2.0 expected to carry?	An agri-photovoltaics (agrivoltaics) component of about 10 GW — solar generation co-located over cultivated cropland. PM-KUSUM was launched in 2019 under MNRE with an outlay of Rs 34,422 crore and a 34,800 MW target.
Where and when was the 18th BRICS Summit held, and under what theme?	New Delhi (Bharat Mandapam), 12-13 September 2026 — India's fourth Chairship, begun 1 January 2026, theme 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'. Over 350 meetings across 25 cities.
Which country became BRICS' 11th member, and when?	Indonesia, in January 2025 — the first Southeast Asian member. The January 2024 expansion had added Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia and the UAE. A 'Partner Country' category of 10 states was created in 2025.
Under which sections of the Forest Rights Act, 2006 is MoTA the nodal agency, and the Gram Sabha the initiating authority?	Section 11 makes MoTA the nodal agency; Section 6 makes the Gram Sabha the authority that initiates determination of forest rights. Community Forest Resource rights fall under Section 3(1)(i); Individual Forest Rights are capped at 4 hectares.
When do the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) (Amendment) Rules, 2026 come into force, and what is the 'prior price' rule?	In force from 1 January 2027. Any discounted item must display the prior price — the lowest price in the preceding 30 days. Parent statute: Consumer Protection Act, 2019; nodal body: Department of Consumer Affairs. Also mandates annual dark-pattern self-audits.
What was India's fish production in 2024-25, and PMMSY's BE 2026-27 allocation?	A record 197.75 lakh tonnes, up from 141.64 lakh tonnes in 2019-20. PMMSY BE 2026-27: a record Rs 2,500 crore. Launched 2020-21; India is the world's second-largest fish producer and second-largest aquaculture producer.
Where does India rank on research paper volume versus aggregate h-index?	3rd globally by annual volume (1.6 million Scopus-indexed papers, 2020-2025) but 19th by aggregate h-index (925, against the United States' 3,213). University patent grant rate about 3% against a national average of about 24%.
What is the outlay and period of DILRMP 3.0, and what is ULPIN?	Rs 565.50 crore, 2026-2031, a 100% centrally funded Central Sector Scheme under the Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development. ULPIN ('Bhu-Aadhaar') is a 14-digit Unique Land Parcel Identification Number for every geo-referenced parcel.
Under which section of the RP Act, 1951 does the ECI register a political party, and can it de-register one?	Section 29A allows registration; the ECI has no general power to de-register, per Indian National Congress (I) v. Institute of Social Welfare (2002). Only administrative 'delisting' is available, which does not dissolve the entity or end its Section 13A Income Tax exemption.
What is ORV Sagar Manthan, and which vessel will it replace?	India's first advanced ocean research vessel built domestically — about Rs 840 crore, under Vertical-4 (Deep Ocean Survey and Exploration) of the Deep Ocean Mission, Ministry of Earth Sciences. It replaces the 43-year-old ORV Sagar Kanya, acquired from Germany in 1983.
Under PMAY-U 2.0, what share of sanctioned houses goes to women, and what is the income ceiling?	17.19 lakh of 18.77 lakh sanctions (92%) to women in sole or joint ownership; annual household income ceiling Rs 9 lakh. Slabs: EWS up to Rs 3 lakh, LIG Rs 3-6 lakh, MIG Rs 6-9 lakh. Four verticals: BLC, AHP, ARH, ISS.

Badri cattle, Uttarakhand's indigenous breed — via ultrasound-guided ovum pick-up and in-vitro fertilisation, by ICAR-NDRI Karnal with G.B. Pant University, Pantnagar, funded by the Uttarakhand Biotechnology Council.
How many HPV vaccine doses has India administered, and which States report 100% coverage?	Over 80 lakh doses under the National HPV Vaccination Campaign. 100% coverage of identified target populations in Gujarat, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh and Mizoram; Uttar Pradesh has the highest absolute number, over 22 lakh girls. Monitored on the U-WIN platform.
What did NITI Aayog launch at the 5th e-FAST India Summit on 8 September 2026?	PACT (Platform for Aggregating Clean Transport) and the linked Zero Emission Truck (ZET) Marketplace, to pool freight demand for electric medium- and heavy-duty trucks. Heavy trucks are 3-4% of the fleet but over one-third of transport-sector carbon emissions; 70% of India's freight moves by road.
