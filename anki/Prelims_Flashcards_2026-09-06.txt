Which committee was notified on 3 September 2026 to examine the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026, and who chairs it?	A 31-member Joint Parliamentary Committee (21 Lok Sabha + 10 Rajya Sabha), chaired by BJP MP Sanjay Jaiswal; must report by the first week of the Winter Session 2026.
What three key changes did the FCRA (Amendment) Act, 2020 make?	Barred sub-granting of foreign funds; cut the administrative-expenses cap from 50% to 20%; mandated a designated FCRA account at SBI, New Delhi Main Branch.
The Lakhpati Didi target was doubled in September 2026 — from what to what, and with how much credit?	From 3 crore to 6 crore women by 2029-30, backed by Rs 10 lakh crore of bank credit to SHGs plus Rs 1 lakh crore for individual women entrepreneurs.
Define 'Lakhpati Didi' and name the parent mission and its launch year.	An SHG household with sustained annual household income of Rs 1 lakh or more; under DAY-NRLM, launched as NRLM (Aajeevika) in June 2011 (renamed DAY-NRLM in 2015), Ministry of Rural Development, cost-shared 60:40.
Under which section and Act was NFRA constituted, when, and where do appeals against its orders lie?	Section 132(1) of the Companies Act, 2013; constituted October 2018 under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs; appeals lie to the NCLAT. Chairperson plus a maximum of 15 members.
What is the size of India's bioeconomy per India's BioEconomy Report (IBER) 2026, and what is the 2030 target?	US$195.3 billion in 2025 (~18% y-o-y growth, ~4.8% of GDP), up from ~US$10 billion in 2014; target US$300 billion by 2030. Released by BIRAC on 19 March 2026.
Name India's three Green Hydrogen Hub Ports, now also being developed as Green Fuel Bunkering Hubs.	Deendayal Port Authority (Kandla, Gujarat), Paradip Port Authority (Odisha) and V.O. Chidambaranar Port Authority (Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu).
Which mission placed EOS-05 in orbit on 4 September 2026, and why was it a first for India?	GSLV-F17 (19th GSLV flight); EOS-05 (GISAT-1A, ~2,367 kg) is India's first Earth-observation satellite headed for geostationary orbit (~36,000 km); injected into a Sub-GTO for later orbit-raising.
Which agency upgraded India's sovereign rating on 2 September 2026, and to what?	Japan Credit Rating Agency (JCR) raised India's long-term issuer rating from BBB+ to A− with a Stable outlook; country ceiling raised to A.
What is the Index of Services Production (ISP) — publisher, coverage and base year?	MoSPI's services-sector index, in trial phase, covering 19 sub-sectors with base year 2024-25; June 2026 release showed Real estate top at 24.7% and air transport the only contraction at −6%.
Which Kakatiya-era temple at Palampet was declared a Monument of National Importance on 1 September 2026?	Gollalagudi, Palampet, Mulugu district, Telangana — a trikutalaya (three sanctums, one mandapa), 12th–13th century CE. Same village as the Ramappa/Rudreshwara Temple (UNESCO World Heritage Site, 2021).
Which ship carried Vizhinjam's first export consignment, and to where?	MSC Beryl (366 m) departed Vizhinjam on 1 September 2026 for Valencia, Spain, with 8 containers. Vizhinjam is India's first dedicated deep-water container transhipment port, near Thiruvananthapuram.
Who is Belgium's Prime Minister who visited India on 2–4 September 2026, and what is bilateral trade?	Bart De Wever — the first Belgian PM visit to India in about two decades; bilateral trade US$13.01 billion in FY2025-26, with a target to double in five years. Belgium is India's fifth-largest EU trading partner.
What is 'RAKSHA', released on 3 September 2026, and what are its four pillars?	India's first 10-year defence diplomacy roadmap, released by Rajnath Singh. Pillars: strategic partnerships; capacity building; indigenous defence manufacturing and exports; maritime security (net security provider in the IOR).
What peak-warming numbers does UNEP's 'Limiting Overshoot' report (2 September 2026) give?	Full NDC + net-zero implementation → peak warming ~1.8 °C (1.7–2.2 °C); current policies → ~2.6 °C by 2100 (1.9–3.6 °C). It is UNEP's first report dedicated to overshoot.
What did the Supreme Court hold in September 2026 about the Bar Council of India's power over law students?	BCI has no disciplinary power over law students — its regulatory control attaches only after enrolment as an advocate; student conduct is for the university. Trigger: NALSAR, Hyderabad.
Under which section of which Act is CARA a statutory body, and which international convention does it implement?	Section 68 of the Juvenile Justice Act, 2015, under the Ministry of Women and Child Development; Central Authority under the Hague Adoption Convention, 1993, which India ratified in 2003. Runs the CARINGS portal.
Where and when did India commission its first geothermal wells?	Puga Valley, Ladakh, July 2026 — two wells, each 1,000 m deep, at over 14,000 ft, commissioned by the ONGC Energy Centre; paving the way for India's first 1 MW demonstration geothermal project. GSI has mapped 381 hot springs and 10 geothermal provinces; theoretical prospect ~10.6 GW. National Policy on Geothermal Energy notified September 2025 (MNRE nodal).
Which States and UT make up the Western Zonal Council, and under which law were the Zonal Councils created?	Goa, Gujarat, Maharashtra and the UT of Dadra and Nagar Haveli and Daman and Diu; created under Sections 15–22 of the States Reorganisation Act, 1956. The Union Home Minister chairs all five Councils.
What was India's Q1 FY27 (Apr–Jun 2026) real GDP growth, and on which base year?	7.8% (vs 6.9% in Q1 FY26); real GVA grew 8.2%. First full quarter on the new National Accounts series with base year 2022-23 (replacing 2011-12).
Write the GVA–GDP identity from memory.	GVA at basic prices + product taxes − product subsidies = GDP at market prices.
What is the outlay of Semicon 2.0, and who is the nodal ministry and implementing agency?	Rs 1,27,500 crore; nodal ministry MeitY, implemented by the India Semiconductor Mission (ISM). Predecessor Semicon 1.0 was Rs 76,000 crore (2021).
What is India's Electoral Democracy Index score and rank in the V-Dem 2026 release, and who publishes it?	0.38 for 2025 — rank 106 of 179, its lowest since 1975. Published by the V-Dem (Varieties of Democracy) Institute, University of Gothenburg, Sweden. India has been classed an 'electoral autocracy' since 2017.
What does the VB-G RAM G Act guarantee, and what did it replace?	125 days of unskilled manual work per rural household per financial year (MGNREGA guaranteed 100), with unemployment allowance if work is not given within 15 days; MGNREGA, 2005 stands repealed from 1 July 2026.
What are the headline changes in the Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026?	Notified by MoEFCC, in full effect from 1 April 2026 (replacing SWM Rules, 2016): four-stream segregation at source, expanded bulk-waste-generator obligations, and mandatory digital monitoring via centralised portals.
Under which Act and article did the Supreme Court quash all FIRs against the NEET-UG protesters on 1 September 2026?	Article 142 of the Constitution ('complete justice') — a power unique to the Supreme Court; High Courts cannot import it into Article 226. FIRs from 20–25 July 2026 were quashed and future FIRs on the same events barred.
How many global mass coral-bleaching events have there been, and how has the disturbance interval changed?	Four: 1998–99, 2010–11, 2016–17 and 2023–25. The interval between disturbances has shortened from about 10 years to 4–6 years. GCRMN operates under ICRI (1994) with UNEP support.
Where is the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope headed, and how will it hunt exoplanets?	Launched 30 August 2026 on a Falcon Heavy from LC-39A; destination a quasi-halo orbit at Sun-Earth L2 (~1.5 million km); exoplanet census via gravitational microlensing. 2.4 m mirror, 300-megapixel wide-field IR camera, ~100x Hubble's field of view. Formerly WFIRST.
Larak Island, in the news, belongs to which country and lies in which strait?	Iran — in the Strait of Hormuz, off Bandar Abbas, east of Qeshm. The Strait connects the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman, is ~21 nautical miles at its narrowest, and carries roughly a fifth of global seaborne oil.
Who was Rani Avantibai Lodhi, and how did the British seize her estate?	Queen-regent of Ramgarh (present-day Dindori/Mandla, MP); 16 August 1831 – 20 March 1858. The estate was taken over through the Court of Wards with sarbarahkars — NOT the Doctrine of Lapse. Known for the 'bangle appeal' and the Battle of Kheri.
Which countries were elevated to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership on 31 August 2026, and what was the trade target?	India and Uzbekistan — 11 agreements signed, trade target US$5 billion annually by 2030, a framework for long-term uranium supply, and UPI apps enabled to scan Uzbekistan's UZQR. Uzbekistan is one of only two doubly landlocked countries (with Liechtenstein).
Which rules made 'One Nation, One Time' law, and who is the technical custodian of IST?	Legal Metrology (Indian Standard Time) Rules, 2026, notified 27 August 2026 under the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 by the Department of Consumer Affairs; CSIR–National Physical Laboratory, New Delhi, working with ISRO. IST = UTC + 5:30, based on the 82°30'E meridian near Mirzapur, UP.
How many hanging glaciers were mapped in the Alaknanda basin, and why do they matter?	219 hanging glaciers covering 71.7 +/- 3.5 sq km, ice volume 2.39 +/- 0.42 cu km (unstable hanging mass 0.74 cu km). Exposed population projected to rise from ~380 to 8,500 by 2030. A hanging glacier terminates abruptly at a cliff and loses mass by mechanical detachment, not surface melt.

Dalit counter-current: Babu Mangu Ram's Ad Dharm, registered separately in the 1931 Census.

What is Kavach, and how much of the network had Kavach 4.0 as on 31 July 2026?	India's indigenous Automatic Train Protection system, developed by RDSO with industry partners; prevents Signal Passing at Danger (SPAD) and applies brakes automatically. Commissioned on 2,633 Rkm — 1,423 Rkm Delhi–Mumbai and 1,210 Rkm Delhi–Howrah.
Which body signed an MoU with the ADB in September 2026, and how many big cats does it cover?	The International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA) — launched by India in April 2023 and headquartered in India; covers seven big cats: tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar and puma (the last two not found in India).
In Union of India v. Rohith Nathan (11 March 2026), what did the Supreme Court hold on the OBC creamy layer?	That Paragraph 9 of a 2004 DoPT letter could not amend the binding 1993 OM by counting salary income where post equivalence was not established — 'hostile discrimination' under Articles 14 and 16. Creamy-layer doctrine comes from Indra Sawhney (1992); income ceiling Rs 8 lakh since 2017, excluding salary and agricultural income.
