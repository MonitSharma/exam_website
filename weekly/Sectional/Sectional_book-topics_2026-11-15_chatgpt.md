## Revision checklist

- [ ] Clemency: pardon · commutation (lighter form) · remission (shorter period) · respite (special fact) · reprieve (stay). Art 72 covers court martial; Art 161 does not. Review is limited (*Epuru Sudhakar*).
- [ ] Ordinance ladder: *R.C. Cooper* 1970 → *D.C. Wadhwa* 1987 → *Krishna Kumar Singh* 2017 (re-promulgation = fraud on the Constitution).
- [ ] 91st Am 2003: CoM ≤ 15% of Lok Sabha; States ≤ 15%, min 12. Cabinet committees are extra-constitutional; 'Cabinet' appears only in Art 352 (44th Am).
- [ ] Joint sitting (Art 108): Speaker presides; never for money or Art 368 bills; survives dissolution if already notified; held 1961, 1978, 2002.
- [ ] Lok Sabha-only: no-confidence, Money Bill introduction, disapproving an Emergency. Quorum one-tenth; prorogation does not lapse Bills.
- [ ] Grants: supplementary · additional · excess · vote of credit (blank cheque) · exceptional · token. Contingency Fund ₹30,000 cr (2021).
- [ ] FD = TE − (RR + non-debt CR); PD = FD − interest; ERD = RD − grants for capital assets. 2018 FRBM: FD is the operational target, RD target dropped.
- [ ] GST: Art 246A, 269A (imports = inter-State), 279A (¾ weighted vote, Union ⅓); petroleum in scope but unlevied; *Mohit Minerals* = persuasive, not binding; destination-based.
- [ ] Budget order: general discussion → DRSC → vote on demands → Appropriation Bill → Finance Bill.
- [ ] Buxar 1764: Mir Qasim + Shuja + Shah Alam II. Bassein 1802: Baji Rao II. Amritsar 1809: Sutlej line. Punjab annexed 1849 (Dalhousie).
- [ ] Resistance: Santhal (Sidhu-Kanhu) · Kol (Buddho Bhagat) · Paika (Bakshi Jagabandhu) · Faraizi (Shariatullah) · Pagal Panthi (Karam Shah, Mymensingh) · Wahabi (Syed Ahmad, Patna).
