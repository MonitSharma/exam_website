This week's traps: **citizen vs person** (Arts 15/16/19/29/30 vs 14/21), **who legislates** (Art 35 Parliament only; preventive detention split), **Act vs Amendment numbers** (77th/86th/93rd), **neighbour swaps** (Punnapra-Vayalar vs Telangana; Nimbarka vs Vallabha; pir vs murid; vachana vs abhang), **direction reversals** (Awadh Kisan Sabha's split; vote on account comes *before* voting) and **regional swaps** (Bardoli is Surat, not Saurashtra; loo is hot, not cold).
