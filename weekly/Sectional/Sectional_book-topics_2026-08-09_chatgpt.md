## Revision checklist (13 points)

1. **Art 249 vs 250 vs 252 vs 253** — the four routes for Parliament to legislate on a State subject, and the durations attached to each.
2. **Art 254 repugnancy** — Union law prevails on Concurrent List, but a State law reserved for and assented to by the President prevails in that State (Parliament can still override).
3. **Finance Commission (Art 280)** — role, plus the 16th FC (Panagariya, 2026-31, 41% vertical share); remember cesses & surcharges sit outside the divisible pool.
4. **GST Council (Art 279A)** — Centre 1/3, States 2/3, decisions by 3/4 of weighted votes; and the list of what stays outside GST (five petroleum products, alcohol, electricity, stamp duty).
