## Questions

**Q1.** With reference to the India–United Kingdom Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA), consider the following statements:

(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answer Key & Explanations

**Q1. (d)** — India–UK CETA was signed on 24 July 2025 and enters into force on 15 July 2026; it gives duty-free access to ~99% of Indian exports, and the two are the world's 5th and 6th largest economies. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q2. (d)** — India assumed the BRICS chairship in 2026 (its 4th, after 2012/2016/2021), will host the 18th Summit in New Delhi, under the theme 'Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability'. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q3. (b)** — Indonesia became a full BRICS member on 6 January 2025, the first from Southeast Asia; Vietnam, Thailand and Malaysia are BRICS partner countries. *[Current Affairs · Easy]*

**Q4. (c)** — The 2025 Johannesburg Summit (22–23 Nov 2025) was the first G20 Leaders' Summit on African soil, hosted by South Africa under the theme 'Solidarity, Equality, Sustainability'. *[Current Affairs · Easy]*

**Q5. (c)** — The United States took over the G20 Presidency on 1 December 2025 and will host the 2026 Leaders' Summit in Miami, Florida. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q6. (b)** — The India–EU FTA was concluded on 27 January 2026 at an India–EU summit at Hyderabad House in New Delhi. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q7. (d)** — The 25th SCO Summit (31 Aug–1 Sep 2025, Tianjin) adopted the Tianjin Declaration and a Development Strategy 2026–2035; India has been a full SCO member since 2017. *[Current Affairs · Hard]*

**Q8. (a)** — The Quad comprises Australia, India, Japan and the US (statement 1 correct). The 2025 Leaders' Summit India was to host was postponed and rescheduled to 2026 (statement 2 incorrect). *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q9. (b)** — IMEC was launched via an MoU on the margins of the 2023 G20 Summit hosted by India in New Delhi. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q10. (d)** — The 1960 World Bank–brokered treaty gave the eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) to India and the western rivers to Pakistan; India held it in abeyance after the 22 April 2025 Pahalgam attack. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q11. (b)** — MAHASAGAR (Mutual and Holistic Advancement for Security and Growth Across Regions), unveiled in Mauritius in 2025, expands the SAGAR vision into a global maritime engagement doctrine. *[Current Affairs · Moderate]*

**Q12. (d)** — SAGAR (Security and Growth for All in the Region) was articulated in Mauritius in 2015 as India's vision for maritime security and development in the Indian Ocean Region. *[Static · Easy]*

**Q13. (a)** — BIMSTEC's seven Bay-of-Bengal members include India; its secretariat is in Dhaka. Pakistan is not a member, so statement 3 is incorrect. *[Static · Hard]*

**Q14. (c)** — Sri Lanka is a South Asian country, not an ASEAN member; Vietnam, Myanmar and Cambodia are members. *[Static · Easy]*

**Q15. (c)** — SAARC has eight members (Afghanistan, Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Maldives, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka) and its secretariat is in Kathmandu. *[Static · Easy]*

**Q16. (a)** — The G4 — India, Brazil, Germany and Japan — back each other's bids for permanent UNSC seats. *[Static · Moderate]*

**Q17. (c)** — The five permanent members are China, France, Russia, the UK and the US; Germany is not one. *[Static · Easy]*

**Q18. (c)** — The ISA was jointly launched by India and France at COP21 (Paris, 2015); its secretariat is at Gurugram, Haryana. *[Static · Moderate]*

**Q19. (a)** — PM Modi launched the CDRI at the UN Climate Action Summit, New York, September 2019. *[Static · Hard]*

**Q20. (d)** — I2U2 brings together India, Israel, the UAE and the USA. *[Static · Moderate]*

**Q21. (b)** — BBIN stands for Bangladesh, Bhutan, India and Nepal; its Motor Vehicles Agreement (2015) eases vehicle movement among them. *[Static · Moderate]*

**Q22. (c)** — IORA was established in 1997 with India as a founding member; its secretariat is at Ebene (Cyber City), Mauritius. *[Static · Hard]*

**Q23. (a)** — Under the Vienna Convention on Diplomatic Relations, a host state may declare a diplomat 'persona non grata', requiring their recall. *[Static · Moderate]*

**Q24. (a)** — 'Neighbourhood First' focuses on deepening ties with India's immediate South Asian neighbours (Nepal, Bhutan, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Maldives). *[Static · Easy]*

**Q25. (b)** — Malabar began in 1992 as a bilateral India–US naval exercise; Japan and later Australia joined, aligning it with the Quad. *[Static · Moderate]*
