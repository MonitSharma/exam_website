---

## Answer Key & Explanations

1. **(d)** — Repo rose from 5.25% to 5.50%; SDF = repo − 25 bps = 5.25%; MSF = Bank Rate = repo + 25 bps = 5.75%. *(Moderate)*
2. **(c)** — The MPC is chaired by the RBI Governor (ex officio); the Finance Minister has no role in chairing it. Other statements follow the RBI Act, 1934 (Sec 45ZA onwards). *(Easy)*
3. **(b)** — Committee recommended 60% overall: 40% for the Centre and 20% for the States; fiscal deficit target of 3% of GDP. *(Moderate)*
4. **(a)** — Withdrawals are made by the executive and later regularised by Parliament (post-facto approval); prior authorisation is not required. *(Moderate)*
5. **(b)** — WPI covers goods only and excludes services; statements 1 and 2 are correct. *(Easy)*
6. **(b)** — CRR (RBI Act, Sec 42) and SLR (Banking Regulation Act, Sec 24) are separate requirements; CRR balances cannot be counted towards SLR. *(Moderate)*
7. **(d)** — Article 279A: the Centre holds one-third and all States together two-thirds of the votes; decisions need a three-fourths weighted majority. *(Hard)*
8. **(a)** — The 56th GST Council meeting approved a two-rate structure (5% and 18%) plus a 40% rate for sin/luxury items; the 12% and 28% slabs were largely merged. *(Easy)*
9. **(a)** — National MPI uses Health, Education and Standard of Living, with 12 indicators (adding maternal health and bank account over the global MPI's 10). *(Easy)*
10. **(c)** — CACP is an attached office of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, not a statutory body. *(Moderate)*
11. **(b)** — Remittances are 'secondary income' in the current account; FDI, ECB and FPI belong to the financial account. *(Easy)*
12. **(d)** — The SDR basket comprises the US dollar, euro, Chinese renminbi, Japanese yen and pound sterling. *(Easy)*
13. **(b)** — Payments banks accept deposits (up to ₹2 lakh per customer) and offer payments services, but cannot lend or issue credit cards. *(Moderate)*
14. **(a)** — STT is administered by CBDT as a direct tax; stamp duty is an indirect tax. *(Hard)*
15. **(a)** — Fiscal deficit = 150 − (100+10) = 40; primary deficit = fiscal deficit − interest payments = 40 − 20 = 20. *(Moderate)*
16. **(a)** — Core inflation strips out volatile food and fuel items to show underlying price trends. *(Easy)*
17. **(d)** — All three statements are accurate. *(Moderate)*
18. **(b)** — De minimis is 10% for developing countries and 5% for developed countries. *(Hard)*
19. **(b)** — Constituted under Article 280, the 16th FC's award covers 2026-31 (from 1 April 2026). *(Easy)*
20. **(c)** — The CIRP is to be completed in 180 days, extendable by 90 days (outer limit 330 days including litigation). *(Moderate)*
21. **(d)** — All three statements correctly describe M1 (narrow money) and M3 (broad money). *(Hard)*
22. **(b)** — Narasimham Committees dealt with banking and financial sector reforms; direct tax reform was the Kelkar/Chelliah domain. *(Moderate)*
23. **(d)** — The four codes are Wages, Industrial Relations, Social Security, and Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions. Industrial disputes are covered under the Industrial Relations Code. *(Moderate)*
24. **(a)** — NPCI is a company (Section 8-type, not-for-profit) promoted by the RBI and IBA; the PSS Act empowers the RBI, not NPCI. *(Moderate)*
25. **(a)** — GDP at market prices = GVA at basic prices + (taxes on products − subsidies on products). *(Easy)*
