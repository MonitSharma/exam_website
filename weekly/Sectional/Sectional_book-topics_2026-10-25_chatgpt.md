**Q50.** *(Geography · Easy)* Consider the following statements:  
1. The Central Water Commission, first set up in 1945, is an attached office of the Ministry of Jal Shakti.  
2. The Cauvery Water Management Authority was set up after the Supreme Court's February 2018 verdict on the Cauvery dispute.  
3. The Central Ground Water Board is a statutory body created under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.  
Which of the statements given above are correct?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Revision checklist

1. **239A vs 239AA:** Puducherry gets its legislature by ordinary law (not Art 368), and the 2019 Act extends this to J&K. Delhi's comes from the 69th Amendment. Ladakh has none.
2. **Delhi services, 2023:** NCCSA = CM (chair), Chief Secretary and Principal Home Secretary, deciding by majority. The LG's view prevails if they differ.
3. **UT High Courts:** A&N → Calcutta, Lakshadweep → Kerala, DNH&DD → Bombay, Puducherry → Madras, Chandigarh → Punjab & Haryana. There are 8 UTs since 26 Jan 2020.
4. **Fifth Schedule para 5:** the Governor can bar land transfer, regulate money-lending and **amend Acts of Parliament**. This needs the President's assent and TAC consultation.
5. **Sixth Schedule:** ADC of up to 30 (4 nominated). In Assam the Governor (not the ADC) decides which laws apply. Para 21: amended by ordinary law.
6. **Art 371 ladder:** 371 Mah/Guj boards · 371A Nagaland · 371B Assam · 371C Manipur hills · 371D AP/TG · 371E AP university · 371F Sikkim · 371G Mizoram · 371H Arunachal · 371-I Goa · 371J Kalyana-Karnataka.
7. **Tax architecture:** Art 246A / 269A / 279A; 268A was omitted by the 101st Amendment. Cess and surcharge stay out of the divisible pool (Art 270). A surcharge is not earmarked.
8. **2025-26 tax facts:** Income-tax Act 2025 (from 1 Apr 2026, "tax year"); GST 5/18/40 from 22 Sep 2025; corporate regimes 22%/15% are optional and carry no MAT.
9. **Food security:** NFSA covers 75% rural and 50% urban; AAY gets 35 kg/household, priority households 5 kg/person; the eldest woman heads the card; food security allowance. PMGKAY runs 5 years from 1 Jan 2024. Shanta Kumar proposed cutting coverage to about 40%.
10. **Working class:** Lokhande 1890 → Factory Acts 1881/1891 (Lancashire pressure) → Ahmedabad and Madras 1918 → AITUC 1920 (Lajpat Rai) → TU Act 1926 → Girni Kamgar 1928 → Whitley Commission, Trade Disputes Act and Meerut (IPC, not Rowlatt), all 1929 → AITUF 1929, Red TUC 1931 → INTUC 1947.
11. **1945-46 order:** Shimla → INA trials → RIN mutiny → Cabinet Mission. Karachi 1931 = Fundamental Rights; Lahore 1929 = Purna Swaraj.
12. **Orbits:** GEO is about 35,786 km, equatorial, and only one kind of geosynchronous orbit. SSO is LEO and near-polar with the same local time. CME storms affect satellites, HF, GNSS, grids and auroras. Kessler = collision cascade.
13. **Cyber:** sign with your private key, verify with the public key. CCA licenses CAs. CERT-In (70B, MeitY), NCIIPC (70A, NTRO). QKD still needs an authenticated channel. Shreya Singhal: 66A struck down, 69A upheld.
14. **India-USA and Eurasia:** 123 → Hyde 2006 → NSG waiver 2008 (no NPT); MDP 2016, STA-1 2018, no treaty alliance. Caspian = Russia, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Iran, Azerbaijan. Uzbekistan is doubly landlocked. Armenia is landlocked.
15. **Peninsular rivers:** sources (Sihawa, Trimbakeshwar, Mahabaleshwar, Talakaveri, Amarkantak, Multai). Indravati/Wainganga/Manjira → Godavari; Bhima/Tungabhadra → Krishna. Mettur and Sivasamudram are on the Kaveri. Ken-Betwa: Daudhan dam, Panna; NWDA 1982. CGWA (not CGWB) is the EPA body.
