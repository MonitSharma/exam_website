## Questions

**Q1.** As decided in the Reserve Bank of India's Monetary Policy Committee (MPC) meeting of June 2026, the policy repo rate stands at:
(a) 6.50%  (b) 6.00%  (c) 5.25%  (d) 4.00%

**Q2.** Following the 'GST 2.0' reform implemented from 22 September 2025, the two principal (standard) GST rate slabs are:
(a) 5% and 12%  (b) 12% and 18%  (c) 5% and 18%  (d) 18% and 28%

**Q3.** With reference to the 'GST 2.0' reforms, consider the following statements:
1. They replaced the earlier 12% and 28% slabs.
2. A special rate of 40% applies to specified sin and luxury goods such as tobacco and high-end cars.
3. GST is levied in India under the constitutional authority of Article 246A.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q4.** The fiscal deficit target for the Union Budget 2026-27 (Budget Estimates) is approximately what percentage of GDP?
(a) 3.0%  (b) 4.3%  (c) 5.1%  (d) 5.9%

**Q5.** The initiative 'Biopharma SHAKTI', announced in the Union Budget 2026-27, is associated with which of the following sectors?
(a) Renewable energy generation  (b) Biopharmaceutical manufacturing and research  (c) Semiconductor fabrication  (d) Defence shipbuilding

**Q6.** The 'India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0', referenced in the Union Budget 2026-27, is primarily aimed at strengthening which ecosystem?
(a) Space launch vehicles  (b) Semiconductor (chip) manufacturing, design and supply chains  (c) Commercial shipbuilding  (d) Steel and metallurgy

**Q7.** With reference to estimates for the Indian economy in FY 2025-26, consider the following statements:
1. Real GDP is estimated to grow at over 7%.
2. The services sector remained the largest contributor to growth.
3. CPI inflation breached the upper tolerance band of 6% for most of the year.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q8.** The 'repo rate' refers to the rate at which:
(a) the RBI lends short-term funds to commercial banks against government securities  (b) commercial banks lend to one another in the call money market  (c) commercial banks park surplus funds with the RBI  (d) the RBI lends directly to the Central Government

**Q9.** The 'reverse repo rate' is best described as the rate at which:
(a) the RBI lends to commercial banks  (b) the RBI absorbs liquidity by borrowing from commercial banks  (c) banks lend to the government  (d) the government borrows from the public

**Q10.** Which of the following are quantitative (general) instruments of monetary policy of the RBI?
1. Cash Reserve Ratio  2. Statutory Liquidity Ratio  3. Open Market Operations  4. Moral suasion
(a) 1, 2 and 3 only  (b) 1, 2 and 4 only  (c) 3 and 4 only  (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Q11.** With reference to the Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR), consider the following statements:
1. It is the proportion of a bank's Net Demand and Time Liabilities (NDTL) to be kept as reserves with the RBI.
2. Banks earn interest from the RBI on the balances maintained under CRR.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Q12.** In the context of public finance, 'Fiscal Deficit' is correctly defined as:
(a) Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts  (b) Total expenditure minus total receipts excluding borrowings  (c) Total expenditure minus revenue receipts  (d) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments

**Q13.** 'Primary Deficit' is obtained by:
(a) subtracting interest payments from the fiscal deficit  (b) adding interest payments to the fiscal deficit  (c) subtracting interest payments from the revenue deficit  (d) subtracting grants from the fiscal deficit

**Q14.** Narrow Money (M1) in India comprises which of the following?
1. Currency with the public  2. Demand deposits with the banking system  3. 'Other' deposits with the RBI  4. Time deposits with the banking system
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 1, 2 and 3 only  (c) 1, 2 and 4 only  (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Q15.** Which of the following transactions are recorded under the Current Account of India's Balance of Payments?
1. Export and import of goods  2. Trade in services  3. Foreign Direct Investment inflows  4. Private remittances from abroad
(a) 1, 2 and 3 only  (b) 1, 2 and 4 only  (c) 2, 3 and 4 only  (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Q16.** 'Demand-pull inflation' is primarily caused by:
(a) a rise in the cost of inputs such as wages and raw materials  (b) aggregate demand exceeding aggregate supply in the economy  (c) a fall in the velocity of circulation of money  (d) an appreciation of the domestic currency

**Q17.** With reference to the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) in India, consider the following statements:
1. It is compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser in the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
2. It includes the prices of services.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Q18.** Treasury Bills (T-Bills) in India are currently issued for which of the following maturities?
(a) 7, 14 and 28 days  (b) 91, 182 and 364 days  (c) 30, 60 and 90 days  (d) 1, 5 and 10 years

**Q19.** With reference to the Statutory Liquidity Ratio (SLR), consider the following statements:
1. It is maintained by banks with themselves rather than deposited with the RBI.
2. It can be held in the form of cash, gold and approved (government) securities.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Q20.** The Finance Commission of India is constituted by the President under which Article of the Constitution?
(a) Article 263  (b) Article 280  (c) Article 360  (d) Article 324

**Q21.** The Goods and Services Tax (GST) Council is a constitutional body established under:
(a) Article 246A  (b) Article 269A  (c) Article 279A  (d) Article 280

**Q22.** With reference to Special Drawing Rights (SDRs), consider the following statements:
1. They are an international reserve asset created by the International Monetary Fund.
2. Their value is determined on the basis of a basket of major currencies that includes the Chinese renminbi.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Q23.** 'Devaluation' of a currency differs from 'depreciation' in that devaluation:
(a) is a market-driven fall in the currency's value under a floating exchange rate  (b) is a deliberate reduction in the official value of a currency under a fixed exchange rate regime  (c) always leads to an immediate fall in exports  (d) refers to a rise in the external value of the currency

**Q24.** Which one of the following is NOT a category under the RBI's Priority Sector Lending (PSL) norms?
(a) Agriculture  (b) Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises  (c) Education loans  (d) Working-capital loans to large diversified conglomerates

**Q25.** With reference to the Index of Industrial Production (IIP) in India, consider the following statements:
1. It is compiled and released by the National Statistical Office (NSO).
2. Among its three broad sectors — mining, manufacturing and electricity — manufacturing has the highest weight.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2
