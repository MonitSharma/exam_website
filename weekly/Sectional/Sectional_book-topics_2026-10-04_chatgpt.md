## Answer key with explanations

**Q1. (d) 1, 2 and 3** *[Easy - Article 324 - silences of the Constitution on the Election Commission]*

All three are the classic 'defects' Laxmikanth lists under Article 324. Qualifications and term now come from statute (the CEC and Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023), not from the Constitution.

**Q2. (b) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Article 324(2) and 324(5) - strength and removal of Election Commissioners]*

Art 324(2) leaves the number of other ECs to the President; the Commission has been multi-member since 1993. Art 324(5) gives the CEC SC-judge-like protection, and other ECs can be removed only on the CEC's recommendation.

**Q3. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Article 329 - bar to interference by courts; election petitions]*

Statements 1 and 2 are Art 329(a) and 329(b). Under the RPA 1951 election petitions lie to the High Court, with appeal to the Supreme Court.

**Q4. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Moderate - RPA 1950 vs RPA 1951 and the rules framed under them]*

RPA 1950 covers allocation of seats, delimitation and electoral rolls (Registration of Electors Rules, 1960 are under it). RPA 1951 covers the actual conduct of elections, disqualifications, corrupt practices and disputes; the Conduct of Election Rules, 1961 are framed under the 1951 Act.

**Q5. (c) All three** *[Hard - Delimitation Commissions, House scrutiny of orders and the seat freeze]*

Commissions were set up in 1952, 1963, 1973 and 2002. Orders are laid before the Houses but cannot be modified there. The 84th Amendment (2001) extended the 1971-census freeze to the first census after 2026 (the 87th, 2003, allowed intra-State readjustment on the 2001 census). Pattern of the 2024 Prelims question on Delimitation Commissions.

**Q6. (b) 52nd Amendment Act, 1985** *[Easy - Tenth Schedule - amendment that added it]*

The 52nd Amendment (1985) inserted the Tenth Schedule. The 91st Amendment (2003) later modified it.

**Q7. (d) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Tenth Schedule - independent and nominated members; the 91st Amendment]*

The merger exception was always two-thirds; what the 91st Amendment did was delete the 'split' exception (one-third). Statements 2 and 3 are the core rules for independent and nominated members. Pattern of the 2022 Prelims anti-defection question.

**Q8. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Hard - Kihoto Hollohan, deciding authority and Art 361B]*

Under the Tenth Schedule the Chairman/Speaker decides; the President-on-ECI-opinion route (Art 103) is for other disqualifications. Kihoto Hollohan also struck down paragraph 7 for want of State ratification. Statement 3 is Art 361B, inserted by the 91st Amendment along with Arts 75(1B)/164(1B). Pattern of the 2025 Prelims Tenth Schedule question.

**Q9. (d) It holds at least 10% of the seats in the Rajya Sabha** *[Moderate - Election Symbols Order 1968 - national party criteria]*

The three alternative tests are (a), (b) and (c). Rajya Sabha strength plays no part in recognition.

**Q10. (b) Section 29A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951** *[Easy - Registration of political parties - Section 29A RPA 1951]*

Section 29A of RPA 1951 governs registration. Recognition and symbols come from the Symbols Order, 1968.

**Q11. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Electoral reforms - voting age, NOTA, EVMs]*

61st Amendment, 1988. NOTA followed PUCL v. Union of India (2013). EVMs were first used in 1982 in the Paravur Assembly constituency in Kerala, not in a Lok Sabha general election.

**Q12. (b) 2 only** *[Easy - Model Code of Conduct - legal status and timing]*

The MCC has no statutory backing. It grew out of consensus among parties (first observed in Kerala, 1960) and the ECI enforces it from the announcement of the schedule.

**Q13. (b) Only two** *[Hard - Section 8 RPA 1951 - conviction, Lily Thomas, Section 11]*

1 and 2 are correct. Under Section 11 of RPA 1951 the Election Commission itself may remove or reduce the disqualification. The President-on-ECI-opinion route is Art 103, which deals with other disqualifications.

**Q14. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Easy - National income aggregates - GNP, NDP, market vs factor cost]*

Market prices include taxes: GDP(MP) = GDP(FC) + net indirect taxes. NNP at factor cost is national income.

**Q15. (b) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - GDP deflator vs CPI]*

The deflator covers everything produced domestically, including capital goods, and its weights change every year. It excludes imports, which the CPI captures if they are consumed.

**Q16. (c) Only three** *[Moderate - Monetary aggregates M1-M4]*

M1 = currency with public + demand deposits with banks + other deposits with RBI. Time deposits enter only at M3. M1 and M3 are narrow and broad money.

**Q17. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Moderate - CRR vs SLR - legal basis and form]*

CRR is Sec 42 of the RBI Act and SLR is Sec 24 of the BR Act. The RBI pays no interest on CRR balances. SLR is held by the bank itself in liquid assets.

**Q18. (d) 1, 2 and 3** *[Hard - LAF corridor - SDF floor, MSF ceiling]*

The SDF was operationalised in April 2022 and replaced the fixed-rate reverse repo as the floor. It is collateral-free, which removes the RBI's securities constraint. The MSF is the penal ceiling and allows a dip into SLR. Revision angle not tested in earlier sets, which covered the repo rate and MPC.

**Q19. (d) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Differentiated banks - payments banks]*

Payments banks can accept demand deposits (balance cap now Rs 2 lakh per customer) and issue debit cards, but they cannot lend, issue credit cards or accept NRI deposits.

**Q20. (a) 1 and 3 only** *[Easy - Direct vs indirect taxes]*

With a direct tax, the person liable also bears the burden. Customs duty and GST are shifted to the buyer, so they are indirect taxes.

**Q21. (b) Sangama - Saluva - Tuluva - Aravidu** *[Easy - Four dynasties of Vijayanagara]*

Founded in 1336 by Harihara and Bukka (Sangama), then Saluva, Tuluva (Krishnadevaraya's line), and Aravidu after Talikota.

**Q22. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Krishnadevaraya - literature and foreign visitors]*

Krishnadevaraya (r. 1509-29) was a Tuluva ruler. The Ashtadiggajas were the eight Telugu poets at his court.

**Q23. (c) All three** *[Hard - Krishnadevaraya - Raichur, Gajapati campaign, Nagalapuram]*

All three are standard. Raichur (1520) was won from Ismail Adil Shah. The Gajapati Prataparudra's daughter was married to him. Nagalapuram was named after his mother Nagala Devi.

**Q24. (b) 1, 2 and 4 only** *[Moderate - Foreign travellers to Vijayanagara and the Bahmani kingdom]*

Barbosa was a Portuguese writer. Nikitin was in the Bahmani kingdom around 1469-72. The 2021 Prelims question on Nuniz's account of Vijayanagara women follows the same traveller-source pattern.

**Q25. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Amara-nayaka system]*

Amara-nayakas sent tribute every year and appeared at court with gifts. NCERT (Themes II) links the system to the iqta. This is the iqta to amara-nayaka to mansab line the Sweep asks you to keep clear.

**Q26. (a) 1565** *[Easy - Battle of Talikota (Rakshasi-Tangadi)]*

1565. Bijapur, Ahmadnagar and Golconda (with Bidar) combined. Hampi was sacked, and the Aravidus later ruled from Penukonda and Chandragiri.

**Q27. (d) 2 and 3 only** *[Easy - Foundation of the Bahmani kingdom]*

The revolt was against Muhammad bin Tughlaq's Deccan officials. Gulbarga (Ahsanabad) was the capital until Ahmad Shah moved it to Bidar.

**Q28. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Mahmud Gawan]*

Gawan, a Persian from Gilan, was wazir under Muhammad Shah III, who had him executed in 1481. The kingdom broke up soon after.

**Q29. (b) Only two** *[Moderate - Successor states of the Bahmani kingdom]*

Berar was ruled by the Imad Shahis, Bidar by the Barid Shahis and Ahmadnagar by the Nizam Shahis.

**Q30. (d) 2 and 3 only** *[Easy - Project Tiger and NTCA]*

The NTCA is a statutory body chaired by the Union Minister in charge of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, not the PM.

**Q31. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Project Elephant]*

Project Elephant (1992) works through elephant reserves and corridors in elephant-range States, with no link to tiger reserves. The elephant is a Schedule I species and was declared the National Heritage Animal in 2010.

**Q32. (b) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Crocodile conservation and the Chambal sanctuary]*

The gharial is Critically Endangered. The 1975 project had UNDP/FAO support. The tri-state Chambal sanctuary is the gharial's stronghold.

**Q33. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Cheetah reintroduction - Kuno]*

The cheetahs came from Namibia (2022) and South Africa (2023) and are the African subspecies (Acinonyx jubatus jubatus). The Asiatic subspecies (A. j. venaticus) survives only in Iran.

**Q34. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Moderate - CMS / Bonn Convention]*

India has been a party since 1983 and hosted CoP13 at Gandhinagar in February 2020. Appendix I lists endangered migratory species and Appendix II species that need cooperative agreements.

**Q35. (b) Only two** *[Hard - Chemicals and waste conventions]*

Rotterdam deals with Prior Informed Consent (PIC) and Stockholm with POPs, so pairs 2 and 3 are swapped. Basel and Minamata are correct. Basel, Rotterdam and Stockholm share a joint secretariat.

**Q36. (c) Both 1 and 2** *[Moderate - UNCCD]*

The Rio Conventions are UNFCCC, CBD and UNCCD. UNCCD CoP14 was held at Greater Noida in September 2019.

**Q37. (c) TRAFFIC** *[Easy - TRAFFIC - wildlife trade monitoring]*

TRAFFIC was set up in 1976 by WWF and IUCN. IPBES is a science-policy platform. The Global Tiger Forum is an intergovernmental body of tiger-range countries.

**Q38. (c) Both 1 and 2** *[Easy - Look East to Act East]*

Look East began in 1991-92. Act East was announced in November 2014 and adds security, connectivity and the North-East as a land bridge.

**Q39. (d) 3-1-4-2-5** *[Hard - India-ASEAN partnership ladder]*

Sectoral 1992, full 1996, summit 2002, strategic 2012, comprehensive strategic 2022.

**Q40. (a) 1 and 2 only** *[Moderate - Kaladan Multi-Modal Transit Transport Project]*

The route runs Kolkata to Sittwe by sea, Sittwe to Paletwa along the Kaladan river, and Paletwa to Zorinpui (Mizoram) by road. The Thailand link is the separate Trilateral Highway. Pattern of the 2026 Prelims question on India-ASEAN connectivity.

**Q41. (b) Malaysia** *[Easy - Mekong-Ganga Cooperation]*

The MGC (Vientiane, 2000) has six members: India, Cambodia, Laos, Myanmar, Thailand and Vietnam.

**Q42. (d) 1, 2 and 3** *[Hard - AITIGA and RCEP]*

AITIGA was signed in 2009 and came into force in 2010. The services and investment agreements came later (2014-15). India left RCEP in November 2019, citing trade-deficit and safeguard concerns. RCEP has 15 signatories (ASEAN + 5).

**Q43. (a) Moreh** *[Moderate - India-Myanmar-Thailand Trilateral Highway]*

The highway runs from Moreh (Manipur) to Mae Sot (Thailand). Zokhawthar (Mizoram) is a different crossing point, and Dawki is on the Bangladesh border.

**Q44. (c) 1 and 3 only** *[Easy - Features introduced by Indo-Islamic architecture]*

Indo-Islamic building introduced the arcuate style (true arch and true dome) and lime mortar. The trabeate system belongs to the earlier Indian tradition.

**Q45. (d) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Early Sultanate monuments - Qutb Minar, Balban, Alai Darwaza]*

Aibak began the Qutb Minar and Iltutmish completed it; later rulers repaired and added to it. Balban's tomb has the first true arch and the Alai Darwaza (1311) the first true dome.

**Q46. (a) 1 only** *[Moderate - Tughlaq architecture]*

The Tughlaqs built austerely in cheaper grey stone, combining arch and lintel. Red sandstone with marble inlay is the Khalji look (Alai Darwaza).

**Q47. (c) Only three** *[Hard - Provincial styles - Jaunpur, Bengal, Malwa, Bijapur]*

Jahaz Mahal is at Mandu (Malwa style). The other three are correct: Sharqi Jaunpur, the Bengal Sultanate at Pandua, and the Adil Shahi tomb of Muhammad Adil Shah at Bijapur.

**Q48. (c) Both 1 and 2** *[Easy - Vijayanagara temple features - raya gopuram, kalyana mandapa]*

Both are signature Vijayanagara features (NCERT Themes II). Look also for carved pillars with yali and rearing-horse motifs, and the separate Amman shrine.

**Q49. (b) 2 and 3 only** *[Moderate - Hampi - river, Lotus Mahal, Vitthala chariot]*

Hampi lies on the Tungabhadra. The 2019 Prelims question had Hampi paired with the Malaprabha as a trap. The Lotus Mahal's cusped arches show the cross-influence you meet again on Saturday.

**Q50. (d) All three** *[Hard - Hampi - Hazara Rama, Mahanavami Dibba, UNESCO]*

All three are correct. The Hazara Rama was the royal temple, the Mahanavami Dibba was the stage for the king's ritual display, and Hampi was inscribed in 1986.

## Revision checklist

- [ ] Art 324: the Constitution is silent on EC qualifications, term and post-retirement jobs; the 2023 CEC/EC Act now fills them. Number of ECs is fixed by the President; CEC removed like an SC judge; other ECs removed only on the CEC's recommendation.
- [ ] Art 327/328/329: delimitation laws and orders are beyond court challenge; elections are challenged only by election petition (RPA 1951 -> High Court -> SC).
- [ ] RPA 1950 = seats, delimitation, rolls (Electors Rules 1960). RPA 1951 = conduct, disqualification, corrupt practices, disputes, Sec 29A registration (Conduct of Election Rules 1961).
- [ ] Delimitation Commissions: 1952, 1963, 1973, 2002. Orders are laid before the Houses, which cannot modify them. 84th Amdt (2001) freeze till first census after 2026; 87th (2003) intra-State readjustment on 2001 census.
- [ ] Tenth Schedule: 52nd Amdt 1985; 91st (2003) deleted the one-third split exception (merger stays at two-thirds) and added Arts 75(1B), 164(1B), 361B. Kihoto Hollohan (1992): Speaker acts as a tribunal and the decision is reviewable; para 7 struck down. Independent joins a party -> out; nominated member has six months.
- [ ] Sec 8(3) RPA: 2+ years' sentence -> disqualified from conviction + 6 years after release. Lily Thomas (2013) struck down 8(4). Sec 11: the ECI can remove or reduce the disqualification.
- [ ] National party: State party in 4 States, OR 2% of LS seats from 3+ States, OR 6% votes in 4+ States + 4 LS seats. MCC is non-statutory. 61st Amdt = voting at 18; NOTA 2013; first EVM 1982 Paravur.
- [ ] Economy: GDP(MP) = GDP(FC) + net indirect taxes; deflator excludes imports and has no fixed basket; M1 has demand deposits, M3 adds time deposits; CRR (RBI Act s.42, no interest) vs SLR (BR Act s.24); SDF = collateral-free floor (2022), MSF = ceiling with SLR dip; payments banks cannot lend, issue credit cards or take NRI deposits.
- [ ] Vijayanagara: Sangama (1336) -> Saluva -> Tuluva -> Aravidu. Krishnadevaraya: Amuktamalyada, Raichur 1520, Gajapati marriage, Nagalapuram, Paes. Conti - Devaraya I; Razzaq - Devaraya II. Talikota 1565.
- [ ] Bahmani: 1347, Hasan Bahman Shah, revolt against MbT's officials; Gulbarga -> Bidar; Gawan (Gilan, Bidar madrasa, 8 tarafs, executed 1481). Five successors: Bijapur-Adil, Ahmadnagar-Nizam, Golconda-Qutb, Berar-Imad, Bidar-Barid. Nikitin visited the Bahmani kingdom.
- [ ] Amara-nayaka: territory for troops, annual tribute, likely derived from the iqta. Keep the iqta -> amara-nayaka -> mansab line in mind for next week's Mughals.
- [ ] Conservation: Tiger 1973 (NTCA 2006, chaired by the Environment Minister); Elephant 1992; Crocodile 1975 (gharial is CR; Chambal = MP/Raj/UP); cheetah extinct 1952, Kuno Sept 2022 (African subspecies).
- [ ] Conventions: CMS (UNEP, India party since 1983, CoP13 Gandhinagar 2020); Basel = hazardous waste, Rotterdam = PIC, Stockholm = POPs, Minamata = mercury; Rio trio = UNFCCC/CBD/UNCCD (CoP14 India 2019); TRAFFIC = WWF + IUCN.
- [ ] Act East: Look East 1991-92 -> Act East Nov 2014 (Nay Pyi Taw). ASEAN ladder 1992/1996/2002/2012/2022. AITIGA 2009 (goods only); RCEP exit 2019. Kaladan Kolkata-Sittwe-Paletwa-Mizoram; Trilateral Highway Moreh-Mae Sot; MGC 2000 (6 members, no Malaysia).
- [ ] Architecture: arch, dome and lime mortar; Balban's tomb = first true arch, Alai Darwaza = first true dome; Qutb Minar Aibak -> Iltutmish; Tughlaq = battered walls, grey stone. Provincial: Atala (Jaunpur), Adina (Pandua), Jahaz Mahal (Mandu), Gol Gumbaz (Bijapur). Vijayanagara: raya gopuram, kalyana mandapa; Hampi on the Tungabhadra (UNESCO 1986), Lotus Mahal, Vitthala chariot, Hazara Rama, Mahanavami Dibba.
