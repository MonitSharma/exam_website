## Answer Key & Explanations

**Q1. (d)** — NISAR (NASA-ISRO SAR), launched 30 July 2025, is the first dual-frequency radar imaging satellite (NASA L-band + ISRO S-band); radar imaging works through cloud, day or night.
**Q2. (b)** — SpaDeX docked two satellites on 16 January 2025 using the indigenous Bharatiya Docking System, making India the 4th nation (after USA, Russia, China) to demonstrate in-space docking.
**Q3. (b)** — As Axiom-4 pilot (launched 25 June 2025), Shubhanshu Shukla became the first Indian to visit the ISS and only the second Indian in space after Rakesh Sharma.
**Q4. (b)** — The 2025 Physics Nobel (Clarke, Devoret, Martinis) recognised macroscopic quantum tunnelling and energy quantisation in a superconducting electric circuit.
**Q5. (a)** — The 2025 Chemistry Nobel (Kitagawa, Robson, Yaghi) honoured metal-organic frameworks (MOFs), porous materials for gas storage, water harvesting and catalysis.
**Q6. (b)** — The 2025 Medicine Nobel (Brunkow, Ramsdell, Sakaguchi) was for peripheral immune tolerance and the role of regulatory T cells in preventing autoimmunity.
**Q7. (a)** — NavIC is a regional system covering India and ~1,500 km around it (not global), using L5 and S bands; statement 3 is wrong.
**Q8. (b)** — GSLV and LVM3 use a cryogenic upper stage (LH2 + LOX); PSLV does not.
**Q9. (b)** — Aditya-L1 orbits the Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 1 (~1.5 million km), giving an uninterrupted view of the Sun.
**Q10. (d)** — CRISPR-Cas9 edits DNA precisely; a guide RNA directs the Cas9 'scissors'; the system is adapted from a bacterial immune defence.
**Q11. (b)** — mRNA vaccines deliver mRNA coding an antigen and do not alter DNA; they contain no live virus, so statement 2 is wrong.
**Q12. (b)** — BrahMos is a supersonic (~Mach 2.8-3) cruise missile jointly developed by India (DRDO) and Russia.
**Q13. (d)** — IndiaAI Mission (~Rs 10,372 crore) provides subsidised GPU compute and runs AIKosh, a repository of datasets, models and tools.
**Q14. (d)** — NQM (2023, ~Rs 6,004 crore, under DST) targets 50-1000 qubit computers in 8 years; QpiAI-Indus (25 qubits) was unveiled in 2025.
**Q15. (b)** — Green hydrogen is made by electrolysis of water using renewable electricity, emitting no CO2.
**Q16. (c)** — JWST observes primarily in the infrared, ideal for seeing through dust and observing the early, redshifted universe.
**Q17. (d)** — Quantum communication secures data via quantum principles; eavesdropping disturbs the states (detectable); QKD uses single photons or entanglement.
**Q18. (d)** — Gaganyaan is India's first crewed orbital programme using the human-rated LVM3; the crewed flight is targeted for 2026.
**Q19. (b)** — AMR arises when microbes (e.g., bacteria) evolve to survive drugs; it is the microbe, not the human body, that becomes resistant.
**Q20. (a)** — A genome is the complete set of an organism's DNA (coding + non-coding); the full protein set is the proteome.
**Q21. (b)** — The Genome India Project sequenced ~10,000 whole human genomes across India's population for a reference database.
**Q22. (d)** — Embryonic stem cells are pluripotent; iPSCs are reprogrammed adult cells; adult/somatic stem cells are generally multipotent.
**Q23. (c)** — One nanometre = 10^-9 metre (one-billionth of a metre).
**Q24. (d)** — Hypersonic = above Mach 5; a scramjet burns fuel in supersonic airflow and, being air-breathing, needs no onboard oxidiser; DRDO has tested it.
**Q25. (b)** — A fuel cell produces electricity by an electrochemical reaction of hydrogen and oxygen, yielding water and heat (not by combustion).
