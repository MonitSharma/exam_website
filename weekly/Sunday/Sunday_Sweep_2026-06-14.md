## Mains skeletons

**Mains-1 (GS2 — Critically examine; 250w).**
- **Intro:** Article 123 lets the executive legislate when a House is not in session; context — SC sanctioned strength 34→38 by ordinance.
- **The concern:** judicial strength is normally fixed by Parliament amending the *Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act*; the ordinance route bypasses legislative deliberation on the judiciary → separation-of-powers and propriety questions; *D.C. Wadhwa* and *Krishna Kumar Singh v. State of Bihar (2017)* (re-promulgation = "fraud on the Constitution").
- **The other side:** ordinances are constitutional, **temporary**, and need ratification within 6 weeks of reassembly; massive pendency (crores of cases) creates genuine urgency; expanding the bench aids access to justice.
- **Balance:** legitimacy depends on *urgency + early parliamentary ratification + no re-promulgation*; durable changes to judicial architecture deserve the legislative route.
- **Conclusion:** efficiency and constitutional propriety are not opposed — use the ordinance as a bridge, convert promptly into law, and resist normalising executive law-making over the judiciary.

**Mains-2 (GS2/GS3 — Discuss; 250w).**
- **Intro:** JJM ~82% rural tap coverage + UPI ~2,264 cr monthly transactions — welfare delivered as **Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)**.
- **Technology:** JAM trinity + DBT cut leakages; UPI = interoperable, low-cost rails now exporting to 8 countries; geo-tagging/dashboards enable real-time monitoring.
- **Cooperative federalism:** JJM is centre-state **co-funded**; states execute; **Pani Samitis / Gram Panchayats** own O&M — a model of 3-tier collaboration; GST Council as the federal-bargaining template.
- **Challenges:** O&M and water-quality sustainability, the digital divide, fraud/data privacy (DPDP Act 2023), and target-driven over-reporting.
- **Way forward:** JJM 2.0 (source sustainability + quality testing), grievance-redress, capacity-building of local bodies, DPI as a global public good.
- **Conclusion:** technology widened reach; cooperative federalism made it stick — together they mark a shift from scheme-delivery to **rights-like universal access**.

## Ethics case — model approach

- **Stakeholders:** the un-served households (right to safe water, dignity); you as SDM (public duty, oath); the junior engineer (livelihood, one lapse vs clean record); the contractor (accountability); senior officials & political leadership (reputation, pressure); the wider public and integrity of official data.
- **Values in conflict:** **integrity & truthfulness vs. loyalty/obedience to superiors**; **public interest (real water) vs. optics (the award)**; **compassion for the engineer vs. accountability**; courage vs. self-preservation.
- **Options:** (i) certify as complete — gains credit now, but is **falsification**, betrays beneficiaries, and is legally indefensible if audited later; (ii) silently delay without reporting — avoids confrontation but conceals wrongdoing and lets it recur; (iii) **document the gap factually, report it through proper channel, fix the connections, and seek a short extension** — costs short-term embarrassment but is honest, lawful and durable.
- **Recommended course:** Option (iii). Put the discrepancy in writing with evidence; refuse to certify non-functional connections; order rectification with a time-bound plan; brief seniors candidly, framing honesty as *protecting* them from a future audit/CAG indictment; resist the "don't rock the boat" pressure politely but firmly.
- **On the engineer:** **developmental, not merely punitive** — proportionate accountability (he enabled it) but weigh the clean record and coercion by the contractor; a documented warning + corrective role, with the **contractor** bearing primary penalty. Upholds justice without destroying a salvageable officer.
- **Anchor:** a civil servant's first loyalty is to the Constitution and the citizen, not to the optics of a review. *Real taps over reported taps.*

## Essay — model approach

**Thesis:** A Constitution endures not as frozen text but as a living instrument — its words are interpreted, stretched and renewed by each generation, so its true meaning is written as much by society and the courts as by its framers.

**Multidimensional points:**
- **Political:** "living document" vs "originalism"; the **Basic Structure doctrine** (Kesavananda, 1973) lets the Constitution evolve while protecting its core.
- **Historical:** framers deliberately built in **amendability**; the Constituent Assembly saw it as a starting point, not a finished monument (Nehru's Objective Resolution).
- **Social:** rights expand with the age — privacy (*Puttaswamy*), dignity, decriminalisation — the "spirit of the age" reshaping the same text.
- **Economic:** Directive Principles shifting from a planned-economy idiom to a welfare-plus-market reading; property's journey from a fundamental right to a legal one.
- **Ethical:** a Constitution as a **moral compact** — Ambedkar's warning that its working depends on the character of those who run it.
- **International:** comparison — the adaptive US "living constitution" vs rigid charters; constitutions as vehicles of national life everywhere.
- **Individual:** the citizen as the Constitution's ultimate custodian — constitutional morality lived daily, not just litigated.

**Sample opening:** *"A document signed in 1950 still settles disputes no one in 1950 could have imagined — proof that a Constitution lives only if each generation reads it anew."*

**Sample closing:** *"The framers gave us the vessel; whether it carries justice or merely floats is, in every age, our choice to make."*

**Fodder (quotes / data):**
- *B.R. Ambedkar:* "However good a Constitution may be, it is sure to turn out bad if those called to work it happen to be a bad lot."
- *Granville Austin:* the Indian Constitution is "first and foremost a **social document**."
- *Nani Palkhivala:* "The Constitution is not a mere lawyers' document, it is the vehicle of a nation's progress."
- **Data peg:** **106+** constitutional amendments since 1950 — adaptation in action, balanced against an unamendable basic structure.
