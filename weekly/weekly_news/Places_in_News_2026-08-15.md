## Indian places

**1. Mandi & Kullu (Beas valley) — Himachal Pradesh.** Central HP astride the **Beas river**, on the **Mandi–Kullu (Chandigarh–Manali) NH**; Mandi sits below the **Pandoh dam**, Kullu upstream toward Manali and the **Pir Panjal**. *Why in news:* the state's **monsoon toll crossed ~157 dead** (Jun 30–Aug 9) with **259 roads blocked** — **Mandi worst-hit (120 roads)**, the Mandi–Kullu highway repeatedly severed by landslides.

**2. Jammu division (Tawi & Chenab basin) — Jammu & Kashmir (UT).** The southern, sub-Himalayan half of the UT; **Jammu city** sits on the **Tawi**, with the **Chenab** (and its feeders **Neru, Kalnai**) draining the Pir Panjal to its north. *Why in news:* **relentless rain triggered a flood emergency across Jammu division** in mid-August — at least **10 killed** as the Tawi and Chenab surged past danger levels.

**3. Brahmaputra floodplain — Assam.** The **Brahmaputra** valley threading Assam west-to-east between the **Eastern Himalaya** (north) and the **Karbi–Meghalaya plateau** (south). *Why in news:* the **Assam flood death toll reached ~100**, with the Brahmaputra and tributaries overflowing and **nearly 300,000 people moved to relief camps**.

**4. Beas–Sutlej belt / Harike — Punjab.** Southwest Punjab where the **Beas meets the Sutlej at Harike** (Harike wetland, a Ramsar site), upstream of the Indira Gandhi Canal head. *Why in news:* Punjab pushed **~₹32 crore of flood-protection works along the Beas and Sutlej** ahead of peak monsoon, as upstream HP/J&K rain raised downstream flood risk.

## World places

**5. Chocó / San José del Palmar — Colombia.** NW Colombia on the **Pacific coast**, west of the **Western Cordillera** of the Andes; the epicentre lay east of San José del Palmar toward the **Coffee Axis (Eje Cafetero — Risaralda, Caldas, Quindío)**. *Why in news:* a **magnitude-7.4 earthquake on Aug 10** killed 180+ and was felt in **Panama, Ecuador, Venezuela and Peru**.

**6. Puracé volcano — Cauca, Colombia.** A stratovolcano in the **Central Cordillera** near **Popayán**, within the **Colombian Massif (Macizo Colombiano)** where the **Cauca and Magdalena rivers rise**. *Why in news:* Puracé **stirred to life after the Aug 10 quake**, emitting ash/gas and forcing flight suspensions at Popayán.

**7. Volcán de Fuego — Guatemala.** One of Central America's most active volcanoes, ~16 km from **Antigua Guatemala** (Sacatepéquez), beside **Acatenango** on the **Pacific volcanic arc**. *Why in news:* an **intensified eruption (Aug 3–5)** sent **pyroclastic flows up to 7 km** and forced **~1,700 evacuations**, banning hikes near Antigua.

**8. Karangetang (Api Siau) — Siau Island, Indonesia.** A stratovolcano at the north end of **Siau**, in the **Sangihe island arc** between **Sulawesi (Celebes)** and **Mindanao**, on the edge of the **Celebes/Molucca Sea**. *Why in news:* **eruptive activity continued Aug 6–12** (Alert Level 2) with daily plumes and avalanche/tremor events.

**9. Klyuchevskoy — Kamchatka, Russia.** The highest active volcano in **Eurasia**, on the **Kamchatka Peninsula** (Russian Far East) along the **Pacific Ring of Fire**, between the **Sea of Okhotsk** and the **Bering Sea**. *Why in news:* a **new eruption began around Aug 13–15**, with Strombolian activity and an ash-laced plume to ~5.5 km, prompting aviation advisories.

**10. Path of totality — Greenland · Iceland · northern Spain.** The **Aug 12 total solar eclipse** swept from **Arctic Siberia** across **eastern Greenland**, the **west coast of Iceland**, the **North Atlantic**, and **northern Spain** (with a sliver of NW **Portugal**). *Why in news:* it was the **first total-eclipse path across continental Europe since Aug 1999**; not visible from India.

**11. Durban — KwaZulu-Natal, South Africa.** South Africa's main east-coast port on the **Indian Ocean**, backed by the **Drakensberg**. *Why in news:* Durban hosts the **46th SADC (Southern African Development Community) Summit on Aug 17**, with ~12 heads of state on industrialisation, critical-minerals processing and regional security.

**12. Scarborough Shoal & Second Thomas Shoal — South China Sea.** Contested reefs west of **Luzon (Philippines)**; **Scarborough Shoal** north near the **Luzon Strait**, **Second Thomas Shoal** in the **Spratlys**. *Why in news:* repeated **China–Philippines coast-guard clashes** through late July–August triggered **joint US–Philippines–Japan maritime drills** as Code-of-Conduct talks stalled.

## Map / location MCQs

**Q1.** The magnitude-7.4 quake of Aug 10 struck **Chocó**, which lies on Colombia's coast facing which ocean, west of which Andean range?
a) Caribbean; Eastern Cordillera b) Pacific; Western Cordillera c) Atlantic; Central Cordillera d) Pacific; Eastern Cordillera

**Q2.** **Puracé volcano** sits in the Colombian Massif, the highland source of which two major rivers?
a) Orinoco & Amazon b) Magdalena & Cauca c) Atrato & San Juan d) Caquetá & Putumayo

**Q3.** **Karangetang**, erupting through mid-August, stands on Siau Island in the Sangihe arc — between which two large islands?
a) Java & Bali b) Sumatra & Borneo c) Sulawesi (Celebes) & Mindanao d) New Guinea & Halmahera

**Q4.** The **Aug 12 total solar eclipse** path of totality crossed which sequence of lands?
a) Canada → USA → Mexico b) Siberia → Greenland → Iceland → northern Spain c) Chile → Argentina d) Egypt → Saudi Arabia → India

**Q5.** **Durban**, host of the Aug 17 SADC Summit, is a port on which ocean, in which South African province?
a) Atlantic; Western Cape b) Indian Ocean; KwaZulu-Natal c) Atlantic; Eastern Cape d) Indian Ocean; Gauteng

**Q6.** In India, **Mandi and Kullu**, hardest hit by this monsoon's landslides, lie along which river?
a) Ravi b) Sutlej c) Beas d) Chenab

### Answer key

- **Q1 — b** (Chocó fronts the Pacific, west of the Western Cordillera; felt across Panama/Ecuador/Peru)
- **Q2 — b** (Magdalena & Cauca rise in the Colombian Massif around Puracé)
- **Q3 — c** (Siau/Sangihe arc lies between Sulawesi and Mindanao)
- **Q4 — b** (Arctic Siberia → E Greenland → W Iceland → N Spain; first European totality since 1999)
- **Q5 — b** (Durban, KwaZulu-Natal, on the Indian Ocean)
- **Q6 — c** (the Beas; Mandi sits below Pandoh dam, Kullu upstream toward Manali)
