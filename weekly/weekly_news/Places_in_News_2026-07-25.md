## Indian places

**1. Boh Valley, Kangra district — Himachal Pradesh.**
Western Himalaya, in the **Beas** river basin below the **Dhauladhar range**; near **Dharamshala / McLeodganj** (Shahpur–Shahpur subdivision). *Why in news:* a **cloudburst and landslides on July 21** swept away several houses (Spedu, Gharghoon villages), damaging ~18 homes; families moved to relief camps.

**2. Vizhinjam (Thiruvananthapuram) — Kerala.**
On the **Arabian Sea**, near the **southern tip of India** just south of Thiruvananthapuram city (not far from Kanyakumari). India's first deep-water, semi-automated **transshipment** port. *Why in news:* crossed the **2-million TEU** mark in ~18 months (fastest in India) and is set to begin **full EXIM (gateway) operations from August 18, 2026**.

**3. Changthang & Karakoram (Nubra–Shyok) Cold Desert Wildlife Sanctuaries — Ladakh (UT).**
**Changthang** is the high cold-desert plateau of **eastern Ladakh** bordering Tibet (holds **Pangong Tso** and **Tso Moriri**); the **Karakoram/Nubra–Shyok** sanctuary lies in the north toward the **Siachen–Karakoram** belt. *Why in news:* the **National Board for Wildlife** cleared 30+ **defence projects** inside these sanctuaries (forward aviation base at **Shyok**, artillery/field hospital at **Bogdang**, ammunition storage at **Gapshan**, troop housing at **Nidder**).

**4. Dibang Wildlife Sanctuary — Arunachal Pradesh.**
Upper **Dibang valley** in the **Mishmi Hills** near the China (LAC) border; the **Dibang** is a major left-bank tributary of the **Brahmaputra**. *Why in news:* NBWL approved two **Border Roads Organisation** roads through the sanctuary — the 56-km **Malinye–Balua–Kapuda** and 20.88-km **Kapuda–Phuphu** roads.

**5. Kaziranga Elevated Corridor (Kaliabor) — Assam.**
Along **NH-715** on the southern edge of **Kaziranga National Park & Tiger Reserve**, between the **Brahmaputra** floodplain and the **Karbi Anglong hills**. *Why in news:* foundation stone laid for an **~86-km elevated animal corridor** (₹6,950+ crore) to let wildlife move freely and cut roadkill during floods.

## World places

**6. Emerald Triangle — Preah Vihear & Ta Muen Thom (Thailand–Cambodia border).**
Where **Thailand, Cambodia and Laos** meet, along the **Dângrêk mountain range**; the disputed **Preah Vihear** and **Ta Muen Thom** temples sit on the escarpment. *Why in news:* **renewed border clashes in late July** spread to ~12 sites before an **ASEAN-brokered ceasefire (July 28, Putrajaya, Malaysia)**.

**7. Great Sitkin Volcano — Aleutian Islands, Alaska (USA).**
On **Great Sitkin Island** in the **Andreanof Islands** group of the central **Aleutian chain**, ~26 miles east of **Adak**, between the **Bering Sea** and the **North Pacific**. *Why in news:* its slow **lava eruption passed five years** (July 12); held at Alert **WATCH / Aviation ORANGE** with fresh notices around **July 20–22** — a hazard for **North Pacific flight paths**.

**8. As-Suwayda (Sweida) — southern Syria.**
On the **Jabal al-Druze (Hauran) plateau**, near the **Jordan** border and south-east of **Daraa** and the **Golan Heights**; Druze-majority province. *Why in news:* **renewed clashes in July 2026** (Druze fighters vs. Syrian government/Bedouin groups) revived fears after 2025's deadly violence.

**9. Typhoon Bavi ("Inday") — Zhejiang, China / Taiwan / Philippines.**
Tracked from the **Northern Mariana Islands & Guam** across the **Philippine Sea**, brushing **northern Taiwan**, to landfall at **Taizhou, Zhejiang** (eastern China) before curving into the **Yellow Sea**. *Why in news:* **~1.7 million evacuated** in China; landfall **July 11**, dissipated **July 16**.

**10. Southern Europe wildfires — Guadalajara (Spain), southern France, Greece, Türkiye.**
Across the **Iberian Peninsula** and the northern **Mediterranean** rim: the **La Mierla** blaze in **Guadalajara** (near **Madrid**), fires in **southern France** (60,000+ evacuated), around **Thessaloniki** in Greece, and western **Türkiye**. *Why in news:* a record **July 2026 heatwave** drove mass evacuations; EU area burned crossed ~254,000 ha by July 23.

**11. Bab-el-Mandeb Strait — between Yemen and Djibouti/Eritrea.**
The chokepoint linking the **Red Sea** to the **Gulf of Aden** (and onward to the Arabian Sea/Indian Ocean); the **Perim/Mayyun Island** splits its channels; the **Horn of Africa** lies to the west. *Why in news:* Houthi threats of a **naval blockade** in **July 2026** made ships **turn back in the Red Sea**, reviving "next Strait of Hormuz" fears for global oil/trade. *(Back in news for a new reason.)*

## Quick map MCQs

**Q1.** The Changthang Cold Desert, in the news over defence-project clearances, is best associated with which two lakes?
a) Wular and Dal  b) Pangong Tso and Tso Moriri  c) Chilika and Pulicat  d) Loktak and Umiam

**Q2.** Vizhinjam port, which crossed 2 million TEU, opens onto which sea and lies nearest to which coast?
a) Bay of Bengal, Coromandel coast  b) Arabian Sea, southern Kerala coast  c) Arabian Sea, Konkan coast  d) Laccadive Sea, Andaman coast

**Q3.** The Preah Vihear and Ta Muen Thom temples, at the centre of July's clashes, sit along which range at which international boundary?
a) Arakan Yoma, Myanmar–India  b) Dângrêk range, Thailand–Cambodia  c) Annamite range, Vietnam–Laos  d) Barisan, Indonesia–Malaysia

**Q4.** The Bab-el-Mandeb Strait connects which two water bodies?
a) Mediterranean Sea and Red Sea  b) Red Sea and Gulf of Aden  c) Persian Gulf and Gulf of Oman  d) Black Sea and Sea of Marmara

**Q5.** Great Sitkin volcano belongs to which island arc?
a) Kuril Islands  b) Aleutian Islands (Andreanof group)  c) Ryukyu Islands  d) Mariana Islands

### Answer key
**Q1 — b** (Pangong Tso & Tso Moriri lie in Changthang, eastern Ladakh)
**Q2 — b** (Arabian Sea; near the southern tip, just below Thiruvananthapuram)
**Q3 — b** (Dângrêk range, along the Thailand–Cambodia border)
**Q4 — b** (Red Sea ↔ Gulf of Aden, via the Perim/Mayyun chokepoint)
**Q5 — b** (Andreanof Islands, central Aleutian chain, Alaska)
