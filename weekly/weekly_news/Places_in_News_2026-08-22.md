## Indian places

**1. Hirakud Dam & the Mahanadi — Sambalpur, Odisha.** The world's longest earthen dam, straddling the **Mahanadi** in western Odisha just above the coastal plains; reservoir near **Sambalpur**, Chhattisgarh border to the west, the deltaic mouth at **Cuttack/Paradip** on the **Bay of Bengal** to the east. *Why:* heavy inflow from the Mahanadi's **Chhattisgarh catchment** forced authorities to open ~20+ gates in mid-August, releasing about **3.5 lakh cusecs**; the reservoir climbed toward its 630-ft full level and downstream districts were put on flood alert.

**2. Bhadrachalam & the Godavari — Bhadradri Kothagudem, Telangana.** A temple town on the **Godavari** in eastern Telangana; downstream the river reaches the **Dowleswaram (Sir Arthur Cotton) Barrage** at **Rajahmundry, Andhra Pradesh**, with the **Polavaram** project between them, before fanning into the Bay of Bengal delta. *Why:* the **Godavari flood** this monsoon brought second-warning levels at Bhadrachalam and heavy discharge at Dowleswaram, briefly cutting the **Vijayawada–Jagdalpur NH** at Bhadrachalam.

**3. Yamunotri (Uttarkashi) & Kalpeshwar (Chamoli) — Uttarakhand.** **Yamunotri**, source of the **Yamuna**, sits in **Uttarkashi** district of the Garhwal Himalaya; **Kalpeshwar**, one of the **Panch Kedar**, lies in the **Urgam valley of Chamoli** in the **Alaknanda** basin. *Why:* mid-August monsoon fury blocked the **Yamunotri National Highway** near Durbil/Junglechatti (Uttarkashi) and pushed debris into the Kalpeshwar temple premises (Chamoli); Char Dham routes disrupted.

**4. Puri — coastal Odisha.** On the **Bay of Bengal** south of the Mahanadi delta, near **Chilika Lake** to its southwest; seat of the **Jagannath Temple**. *Why:* the Odisha government pressed the Centre to back a **UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage** nomination for the **Jagannath Rath Yatra**, keeping Puri in the news through mid-August.

**5. Kochi (Cochin) — Kerala.** On the **Arabian Sea/Malabar coast**, headquarters of the **Southern Naval Command**; **Willingdon Island**, the **Vembanad** backwaters and Fort Kochi cluster here. *Why:* the **8th India–US Navy Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) Exercise 2026** concluded on **Aug 14** at Southern Naval Command, Kochi.

## World places

**6. Flores Island & Ende — East Nusa Tenggara, Indonesia.** In the **Lesser Sunda Islands**, between the **Flores Sea** (north) and the **Savu Sea** (south), east of **Sumbawa** and west of **Timor**; epicentre ~68 km NNW of **Ende**. *Why:* a **M7.7 offshore earthquake on Aug 15** (local) triggered a **tsunami warning** for eastern Indonesia; 47+ dead and 230+ aftershocks.

**7. Kuril Islands / Iturup (Etorofu) — Russia (claimed by Japan).** A volcanic **island arc between the Sea of Okhotsk and the Pacific**, running from **Kamchatka** to **Hokkaido**; Iturup is the largest of Japan's disputed "Northern Territories." *Why:* on **Aug 13**, **Putin made his first-ever visit** to the Kurils (Iturup), amid rising tensions with Japan.

**8. Strait of Hormuz — between Iran and Oman/UAE.** The world's key oil chokepoint, linking the **Persian Gulf** to the **Gulf of Oman/Arabian Sea**, hemmed by **Iran** (north) and Oman's **Musandam** exclave (south). *Why:* the **2026 Iran-war blockade crisis** deepened in mid-August — Iran alternately closing/reopening the strait, a US naval blockade in force, transits down sharply and vessels damaged.

**9. Pag-asa (Thitu) Island — Spratly Islands, South China Sea.** Administered by the **Philippines** (Kalayaan, off **Palawan**), near **Subi Reef** and **Sandy Cay**, deep inside the contested Spratlys. *Why:* around **Aug 11**, Chinese coast-guard and PLA-Navy ships plus drones massed near Pag-asa; Manila called the moves "escalatory," as its runway-extension works continued.

**10. Sakurajima (Aira Caldera) — Kagoshima, Kyushu, Japan.** A stratovolcano in **Kagoshima Bay**, southern **Kyushu**, on the **Pacific Ring of Fire**, overlooking Kagoshima city. *Why:* explosive eruptions around **Aug 8–10** into mid-August sent ash plumes to ~4 km, kept the volcano at **Alert Level 3**, and dusted Kagoshima with ash.

**11. Northwest Spain wildfires — Galicia and Castile & León.** The **NW Iberian Peninsula** — **Ourense**, **A Coruña** (Galicia) and **León** (Castile & León), inland of the Bay of Biscay/Atlantic. *Why:* record mid-August wildfires amid a severe **heatwave** forced tens of thousands to evacuate; over **400,000 ha** have burned in Spain this year, with the northwest worst hit.
