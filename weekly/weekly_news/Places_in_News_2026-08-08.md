# Places in News — week of 8 August 2026

Saturday map drill for UPSC CSE 2027. Fresh locations this week (no repeats from the last four lists). For each place: where it is + neighbouring features to fix it on a blank outline, and a one-line "why in news". Verify positions as you plot them.

## Indian places

**1. Pathanamthitta district — Kerala.** South-central Kerala in the Western Ghats foothills, drained by the **Pamba river** (of Sabarimala fame), between Kottayam and the Achankovil valley; **Laha** is a hill hamlet here. *Why:* the **first week of August brought devastating floods** to Kerala — Laha recorded ~250 mm in 24 hours (ending Aug 1), an offshore trough from Gujarat to Kerala drove extremely heavy rain, and 11+ died with thousands moved to relief camps.

**2. Dakshina Kannada district — Karnataka.** Coastal Karnataka on the Arabian Sea around **Mangaluru**, backed by the **Western Ghats** ghat-passes (**Charmadi, Shiradi, Sampaje**); laced by the **Netravathi** and **Kumaradhara** rivers. *Why:* torrential monsoon rain on **Aug 2** triggered landslides on NH ghat sections and pushed the Netravathi–Kumaradhara–Phalguni (Gurupura) rivers to danger levels; a landslip also hit the Yedakumeri–Shiribagilu railway stretch.

**3. Glaw Lake & Kamlang Tiger Reserve — Arunachal Pradesh.** A pristine freshwater lake in the **Eastern Himalayas** of eastern Arunachal, inside **Kamlang Tiger Reserve** (Lohit basin), near the Mishmi Hills and not far from the Myanmar frontier. *Why:* on **Aug 3** Glaw Lake was designated **India's 101st Ramsar site** — and **Arunachal's first** — a habitat for all four Indian big cats and the critically endangered white-bellied heron.

**4. Asan Wetland Conservation Reserve — Uttarakhand.** In the **Doon valley** near Dehradun, at the **confluence of the Asan and Yamuna rivers** on the Himachal border; India's first conservation-reserve Ramsar site. *Why:* the **Supreme Court extended its Asan-Wetland order nationwide** (clarified early August), barring mining within 10 km of any wetland conservation reserve without NBWL/MoEFCC clearance.

**5. Hyderabad — Telangana.** On the **Deccan plateau** astride the **Musi river**, capital of Telangana; near **Osman Sagar/Himayat Sagar** reservoirs. *Why:* India (under its **BRICS Presidency 2026**) hosted the **2nd BRICS Anti-Corruption Working Group meeting** here on **Aug 4–5**, with a side event on tech-driven ethical governance.

## World places

**6. Off Davao / eastern Mindanao — Philippines.** The southern Philippine island facing the **Philippine Sea** and the deep **Philippine Trench** to its east. *Why:* a **magnitude-6.3 offshore earthquake struck on Aug 5** at shallow depth, shaking the southern coast (no major damage reported).

**7. Kanlaon Volcano — Negros Island, Philippines.** A stratovolcano in the central Philippines (Visayas), ~36 km SE of **Bacolod City**, overlooking the country's prime **sugarcane** belt. *Why:* Kanlaon stayed at **Alert Level 2 with continuing eruptive activity into early August** — one of 2026's most active Philippine volcanoes after a run of ash-plume eruptions.

**8. Gulf of Suez — Egypt.** The northwestern arm of the **Red Sea**, between mainland Egypt and the **Sinai Peninsula**, leading up to the **Suez Canal**. *Why:* a **magnitude-5.5 earthquake on Aug 3** near the gulf was felt across several Middle Eastern countries.

**9. Mount Etna — Sicily, Italy.** Europe's most active volcano, on the east coast of **Sicily** above **Catania**, near the **Ionian Sea** and the Strait of Messina. *Why:* renewed **Strombolian activity at the Voragine summit crater (around Jul 30–31)** sent an ash cloud to roughly **7 km**, briefly disrupting the area.

**10. Western Tigray (Shererina & Humera) — Ethiopia.** Ethiopia's northernmost region, wedged between **Eritrea** and **Sudan**; **Humera** sits at the tri-border, **Shererina** to its south. *Why:* **fighting resumed on Aug 1–3** between the federal army and TPLF — the gravest breach yet of the 2022 Pretoria peace deal — pushing refugees across into Sudan.

**11. Goma & North Kivu — DR Congo.** Eastern DRC city on the north shore of **Lake Kivu**, at the **Rwanda border** beneath the **Virunga** volcanoes (Nyiragongo). *Why:* in early August the **M23–government process wobbled** — a prisoner exchange proceeded even as M23 expelled a government member from the joint ceasefire-verification mechanism, keeping the eastern Congo war live.

## Map / location MCQs

**Q1.** The Pamba river, in the news as Kerala floods hit its district, is best associated with which pilgrimage centre?
a) Guruvayur  b) Sabarimala  c) Thiruvananthapuram  d) Malayattoor

**Q2.** Glaw Lake, India's 101st Ramsar site, lies inside which tiger reserve and in which state?
a) Namdapha, Assam  b) Kamlang, Arunachal Pradesh  c) Pakke, Nagaland  d) Dampa, Mizoram

**Q3.** The Asan Wetland Conservation Reserve sits at the confluence of the Asan river with which major river?
a) Ganga  b) Ramganga  c) Yamuna  d) Kali

**Q4.** The Gulf of Suez, jolted by an August earthquake, separates mainland Egypt from which landmass?
a) Arabian Peninsula  b) Sinai Peninsula  c) Cyprus  d) Anatolia

**Q5.** Goma, a flashpoint in the DRC's M23 conflict, lies on the shore of which lake, beneath the Virunga volcanoes?
a) Lake Tanganyika  b) Lake Edward  c) Lake Kivu  d) Lake Albert

**Q6.** Mangaluru and the Dakshina Kannada landslides this week lie along which mountain belt, crossed by the Charmadi and Shiradi ghats?
a) Eastern Ghats  b) Western Ghats  c) Satpura range  d) Nilgiris only

### Answer key

- **Q1 — b** (Sabarimala, on the Pamba in Pathanamthitta)
- **Q2 — b** (Kamlang Tiger Reserve, Arunachal Pradesh — the state's first Ramsar site)
- **Q3 — c** (Yamuna; the Asan meets it near Dehradun in the Doon valley)
- **Q4 — b** (Sinai Peninsula; the Gulf of Suez is the Red Sea's NW arm)
- **Q5 — c** (Lake Kivu, on the Rwanda border in eastern DRC)
- **Q6 — b** (Western Ghats; the ghat passes link coastal Karnataka to the Deccan)
