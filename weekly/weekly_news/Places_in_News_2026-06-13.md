## Indian places

**1. Surha Tal / Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary — Uttar Pradesh.** Ballia district, far-eastern UP near the Bihar border, on the Ganga plain close to the Ganga–Ghaghara confluence. *Why:* declared **India's 100th Ramsar site** on World Environment Day (June 5). India now has the most Ramsar sites in Asia and ranks 3rd globally (after UK and Mexico).

**2. Kaziranga National Park & Tiger Reserve — Assam.** South bank of the **Brahmaputra**, Golaghat–Nagaon districts; Karbi Anglong hills to the south. *Why:* a rapid raptor-and-stork survey released June 5 recorded 30 raptor and 6 stork species — the park holds ~50 of India's 112 raptor species.

**3. Western Ghats / Kerala.** The Sahyadri ranges along the SW coast; districts from Idukki and Wayanad to the coastal lowlands. *Why:* IMD **Red Alert** as the southwest monsoon intensified — extremely heavy rain, landslides and waterlogging across several districts.

**4. Konkan coast / Maharashtra.** West-coast strip between the Arabian Sea and the Western Ghats. *Why:* **monsoon onset** over Maharashtra with heavy rain and strong winds; the monsoon was advancing north toward Delhi (expected June 25–30).

**5. Mundra & Nhava Sheva ports — Gujarat & Maharashtra (Arabian Sea).** Mundra on the Gulf of Kutch; Nhava Sheva (JNPT) across the harbour from Mumbai. *Why:* a third India-bound vessel was attacked in the **Arabian Sea** in June, spotlighting the Gulf of Aden–Arabian Sea trade corridor and India's seaborne crude imports (~85% by sea).

**6. Eastern Ladakh / Line of Actual Control.** Aksai Chin frontier; Depsang Plains and Demchok sectors. *Why:* India's new envoy to China discussed border-area developments with China's boundary-affairs DG, reviewing the post-disengagement situation along the LAC.

## World places

**7. Évian-les-Bains — France.** On the southern shore of **Lake Geneva (Lac Léman)**, Haute-Savoie, just across the lake from Switzerland. *Why:* host of the **G7 Summit (June 15–17, 2026)**; India invited as a guest along with Brazil, Kenya, South Korea and Syria.

**8. Strait of Hormuz.** Chokepoint between **Iran** (north) and **Oman/UAE** (south), linking the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman and Arabian Sea. *Why:* focus of a 2026 crisis over keeping it open — roughly 20–25% of the world's seaborne oil and LNG passes through it; disruption caused fuel shortages in parts of Asia.

**9. Gulf of Aden & Bab-el-Mandeb.** Between the **Horn of Africa** (Somalia/Djibouti) and the **Arabian Peninsula** (Yemen), the gateway from the Red Sea to the Arabian Sea. *Why:* the corridor for attacks on India-bound shipping; pairs with the Suez–Red Sea route for the exam.

**10. Mindanao / General Santos — Philippines.** Southernmost large island of the Philippines, facing the Celebes Sea. *Why:* an **offshore M7.8 earthquake** (June 8) killed dozens, triggered a ~1 m tsunami and landslides, and displaced tens of thousands.

**11. Kīlauea volcano — Hawaii (Big Island), USA.** Shield volcano within Hawaiʻi Volcanoes National Park, in the central Pacific. *Why:* its **48th eruptive episode** (from June 1) set a record for most episodes documented in a single eruption; lava fountains topped 200 m.

**12. Kanlaon volcano — Negros Island, Philippines.** Central Visayas, between Panay and Cebu. *Why:* part of the ongoing 2024–26 Kanlaon eruptions — a reminder of the Philippines' place on the **Pacific Ring of Fire**.

a) Red Sea  b) Gulf of Oman  c) Gulf of Aden  d) Caspian Sea

**Q2.** India's 100th Ramsar site, Surha Tal, lies in which state?
a) Bihar  b) Madhya Pradesh  c) Uttar Pradesh  d) West Bengal

**Q3.** The Bab-el-Mandeb strait separates the Arabian Peninsula from which landmass?
a) Indian subcontinent  b) Horn of Africa  c) Anatolia  d) Balkans

a) Lake Constance  b) Lake Geneva  c) Lake Como  d) Lake Annecy

a) Barak  b) Teesta  c) Brahmaputra  d) Subansiri

2 — **c** (Uttar Pradesh, Ballia district)
3 — **b** (Horn of Africa, across from Yemen)
4 — **b** (Lake Geneva / Lac Léman)
5 — **c** (Brahmaputra, south bank)
