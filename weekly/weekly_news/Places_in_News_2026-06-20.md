## Indian places

**1. Lipulekh Pass — Uttarakhand.** Pithoragarh district, at the **India–Nepal–China tri-junction** near Kalapani; sits at the head of the Kali (Sharda) river, with Dharchula as the base town. *Why:* India–China **border trade resumes here in June 2026** after a ~6-year freeze (suspended since the 2020 standoff / COVID); Lipulekh is also one of the two official routes for the Kailash Manasarovar Yatra.

**2. Nathu La & Shipki La — Sikkim and Himachal Pradesh.** Nathu La is on the Sikkim–Tibet border above the **Chumbi Valley** (east of Kanchenjunga); Shipki La lies in Kinnaur, where the **Sutlej enters India** from Tibet. *Why:* the other two designated India–China trade passes cleared to **reopen alongside Lipulekh** in the 2026 season.

**3. Great Nicobar Island — Andaman & Nicobar Islands.** Holds **Indira Point**, India's southernmost tip, at the mouth of the Bay of Bengal / Andaman Sea; the **Six Degree Channel** separates it from Indonesia's Aceh, and it guards the western approach to the **Strait of Malacca**. *Why:* the mega **Galathea Bay International Transshipment Port** and island-development project stayed in the news (DPR milestone; debate over its "Malacca chokepoint" strategic value vs. ecological and tribal-rights costs).

**4. Vadhavan Port — Maharashtra.** Off **Dahanu in Palghar district**, on the Arabian Sea ~150 km north of Mumbai/JNPT. *Why:* India's upcoming **deep-draft greenfield mega-port** (designed for 20M+ TEUs) advanced this month — set to rank among the world's largest container ports.

**5. Aravalli Range — Delhi–Haryana–Rajasthan–Gujarat.** India's **oldest fold mountains**, running NE–SW from Delhi to near Ahmedabad; they shield the Indo-Gangetic plain from the **Thar Desert**. *Why:* the **Aravalli Green Wall** afforestation drive (a Delhi-to-Gujarat green belt across ~29 districts to fight desertification) is being scaled up, amid concern over dust storms linked to Aravalli degradation.

**6. Eastern Arunachal Pradesh — Project UDAYAK.** The easternmost districts — **Anjaw, Dibang Valley, Lohit, Changlang, Tirap, Longding** — straddling the LAC (McMahon Line) and the Indo–Myanmar border; Doomdooma (Assam) is the base. *Why:* the **BRO's Project UDAYAK** (1,457 km of strategic border roads) marked its 37th Raising Day and its border-connectivity push featured in news.

## World places

**7. Mount Kailash & Lake Manasarovar — Tibet, China.** In the **Kailash (Gangdise) range** of the Trans-Himalaya; the source region of four great rivers — the **Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra (Yarlung Tsangpo) and Karnali (Ghaghara)**. *Why:* the **Kailash Manasarovar Yatra resumes in 2026** — 20 batches of pilgrims via the Lipulekh and Nathu La passes — after a multi-year pause.

**8. Padma River & Padma Barrage — Bangladesh.** **Padma** is the name of the Ganga after it crosses into Bangladesh (downstream of India's **Farakka Barrage**); it joins the Jamuna (Brahmaputra) and Meghna before reaching the Bay of Bengal. *Why:* Bangladesh's **ECNEC approved a ~2.1 km Padma Barrage** (~Tk 345 bn; build 2026–33) to curb salinity and store water — reviving **transboundary river-sharing questions** with upstream India.

**9. Mayon Volcano — Philippines.** The near-perfect cone in **Albay province, Bicol region, southern Luzon**, overlooking Legazpi City. *Why:* its **2026 eruption continued** through mid-June with lava, pyroclastic flows and ashfall over Albay — a textbook Pacific **Ring of Fire** stratovolcano.

**10. Shiveluch (Sheveluch) Volcano — Russia.** Among the most active volcanoes of the **Kamchatka Peninsula**, on the **Bering Sea** side of the Russian Far East, across from Alaska's Aleutians. *Why:* powerful **June 2026 eruptions** threw ash ~10 km high, triggering the highest ("red") aviation alert.

**11. El Fasher — Sudan.** Capital of **North Darfur** in western Sudan, on the Sahel–Sahara fringe, far west of Khartoum and the Nile. *Why:* the besieged city stayed an epicentre of Sudan's civil war, with **famine (IPC Phase 5) and mass-atrocity warnings** flagged by the UN in mid-June 2026.

**12. Ankara — Türkiye.** Turkey's capital, sited on the **Anatolian plateau**; the country bridges Europe and Asia across the **Bosphorus and Dardanelles**. *Why:* confirmed host of the **2026 NATO Summit (July 7–8)**, putting it in diplomatic news as preparations ramped up.

## Quick map MCQs

**Q1.** Shipki La, in the news for reopening India–China trade, is the point where which river enters India?
a) Beas  b) Sutlej  c) Ravi  d) Chenab

**Q2.** Indira Point, India's southernmost tip, lies on which island?
a) Car Nicobar  b) Little Andaman  c) Great Nicobar  d) Minicoy

**Q3.** Mount Kailash and Lake Manasarovar form the source region of all of the following rivers EXCEPT:
a) Indus  b) Sutlej  c) Brahmaputra  d) Ganga

**Q4.** El Fasher, repeatedly in the news for famine and conflict, is a city in which country?
a) Chad  b) Sudan  c) South Sudan  d) Ethiopia

**Q5.** "Padma" is the name given in Bangladesh to which river after it crosses the border?
a) Brahmaputra  b) Teesta  c) Ganga  d) Meghna

### Answer key
1 — **b** (Sutlej enters India near Shipki La, Kinnaur)
2 — **c** (Great Nicobar; Indira Point is the southernmost point of India)
3 — **d** (the Ganga rises at Gangotri; Kailash feeds the Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra and Karnali/Ghaghara)
4 — **b** (Sudan — capital of North Darfur)
5 — **c** (the Ganga becomes the Padma below the Farakka Barrage)
