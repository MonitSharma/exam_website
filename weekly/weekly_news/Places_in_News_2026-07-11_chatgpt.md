## Indian places

**1. Guwahati — Assam.** On the south bank of the **Brahmaputra**, at the gateway to Northeast India; the Nilachal Hills and Kamakhya temple sit on its western side. *Why:* India hosted the **BRICS Heads of Anti-Drug Agencies meeting (July 6-7)** here, where members adopted the **Guwahati Declaration** against illicit drug trafficking and transnational organised crime.

**2. Wayanad district — Kerala.** Northern Kerala in the **Western Ghats**, between the Nilgiri plateau, Kozhikode lowlands and the Karnataka border. *Why:* rescue teams searched for missing workers after a **monsoon-triggered landslide near a tunnel construction site**, with deaths and injuries reported.

**3. Dehradun & Nainital — Uttarakhand.** Dehradun lies in the **Doon Valley** between the Shivaliks and Lesser Himalaya; Nainital sits in the Kumaon Himalaya around Naini Lake. *Why:* heavy rain alerts, overflowing rivers and flash-flood risk put Uttarakhand back in the news during the active monsoon spell.

**4. Meerut & Muzaffarnagar — Uttar Pradesh.** Upper **Ganga-Yamuna Doab**, west UP, between the Yamuna-side Delhi region and the Ganga-side Haridwar-Bijnor belt. *Why:* west UP saw exceptional monsoon rain; Meerut recorded its **second-highest July rainfall in 156 years**.

**5. Kamarajar Port / Ennore — Tamil Nadu.** On the **Coromandel Coast**, about 24 km north of Chennai, near Ennore Creek and the Kosasthalaiyar-Pulicat coastal wetland system. *Why:* the port completed capital dredging works and became **India's second major port with 18 m draft capability**, enabling Capesize vessel handling.

**6. Ken-Betwa link / Panna-Chhatarpur — Madhya Pradesh.** Bundelkhand plateau; the **Ken** flows past Panna Tiger Reserve and the **Betwa** joins the Yamuna downstream in UP. *Why:* fresh protests around India's first river-linking project followed tribal complaints over disrupted water supply, compensation and displacement.

**7. Buxa Tiger Reserve — West Bengal.** Alipurduar district in the eastern Himalayan foothills; bounded by the **Bhutan hills** to the north and connected to the Assam-Bhutan north Bengal wildlife corridor. *Why:* the planned tiger release was pushed to **October 2, 2026** because of monsoon conditions and delays in relocating villages from the core area.

## World places

**8. Cox's Bazar Rohingya camps — Bangladesh.** Southeast Bangladesh on the **Bay of Bengal**, near Teknaf and the Naf River border with Myanmar's Rakhine State. *Why:* repeated monsoon landslides in the crowded refugee camps killed Rohingya refugees, including children, and forced relocations from high-risk slopes.

**9. Hengzhou / Guangxi — China.** Southern China, inland from the Gulf of Tonkin, in the **Xi River-Pearl River basin**. *Why:* Tropical Storm **Maysak** triggered deadly floods, reservoir breaches and mass evacuations; flooded snake-breeding farms created an additional public-safety crisis.

**10. Taiwan Strait / Fujian-Zhejiang coast — China-Taiwan.** The **Taiwan Strait** separates Taiwan from mainland China's Fujian coast; Zhejiang lies just north, south of Shanghai. *Why:* Typhoon **Bavi** tracked north of Taiwan toward a forecast landfall near the Fujian-Zhejiang border, closing schools, ports and transport links.

**11. Oamaru & Waitaki District — New Zealand.** On the east coast of the **South Island**, south of Canterbury and north of Otago; near the Waitaki River mouth. *Why:* a local state of emergency was declared after significant flooding in Oamaru, while red rain warnings covered parts of Marlborough and Canterbury.

**12. Prambanan / Yogyakarta — Indonesia.** Central **Java**, northeast of Yogyakarta, near the Merapi volcanic landscape and the Solo-Yogyakarta cultural corridor. *Why:* India and Indonesia launched a restoration and conservation project for the **Prambanan temple compound**, a UNESCO World Heritage Hindu temple complex.

**13. Los Gallardos / Bedar — Spain.** Almeria province, **Andalusia**, in arid southeast Spain between the Sierra de los Filabres and the Mediterranean coast. *Why:* a fast-moving wildfire during extreme heat became one of Spain's deadliest recent fires, with fatalities, missing persons and evacuations reported.

**14. Iron & Reynolds counties — Missouri, USA.** Ozark Plateau of southeast Missouri; includes the **Black River** and the Taum Sauk mountain area. *Why:* a flash-flood emergency followed 6-12 inches of rain, with camp evacuations, swift-water rescues and a state emergency declaration.

## Quick map MCQs

**Q1.** Guwahati, host of the BRICS anti-drug agencies meeting, lies on which river?
a) Barak  b) Brahmaputra  c) Teesta  d) Subansiri

**Q2.** The Ken-Betwa river-linking project transfers water from the Ken basin toward which river basin?
a) Narmada  b) Godavari  c) Betwa-Yamuna  d) Mahanadi

**Q3.** Buxa Tiger Reserve lies close to India's border with which country?
a) Nepal  b) Bhutan  c) Myanmar  d) Bangladesh

**Q4.** Prambanan, in the news for India-backed restoration, is located on which Indonesian island?
a) Sumatra  b) Java  c) Bali  d) Sulawesi

**Q5.** Cox's Bazar, where Rohingya camps faced deadly landslides, is in which part of Bangladesh?
a) Northwest near Rangpur  b) Southeast on the Bay of Bengal  c) Central Padma delta  d) Northeast Sylhet hills

### Answer key
1 — **b** (Brahmaputra)
2 — **c** (toward the Betwa-Yamuna system)
3 — **b** (Bhutan foothills, north Bengal)
4 — **b** (Java, near Yogyakarta)
5 — **b** (southeast Bangladesh on the Bay of Bengal, near Myanmar)
