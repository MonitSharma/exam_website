## Indian places

**1. Valmikinagar Barrage & the Gandak plain — West Champaran, Bihar.**
On the **Gandak (Narayani)** where it leaves Nepal and enters India at the **Indo-Nepal border** in **West Champaran** district, at the western edge of the north-Bihar plains; **Valmiki Tiger Reserve** and the Someshwar (Shivalik) hills lie immediately north, the Ganga confluence at **Sonepur/Hajipur** far downstream. *Why in news:* after the **Aug 26 Himalayan glacier-collapse flood** in Nepal, all **36 gates of the Gandak barrage** were opened and **8 Bihar districts** — Gopalganj, Saran (Chapra), West Champaran (Bettiah), East Champaran (Motihari), Muzaffarpur, Vaishali, Sitamarhi, Sheohar — were put on alert with NDRF deployed and low-lying areas evacuated. UP and Uttarakhand also evacuated riverside pockets.

**2. Kaziranga National Park & the Karbi Anglong hills — Assam.**
In the **Brahmaputra floodplain** of central Assam, spanning **Golaghat and Nagaon** districts; the **Karbi Anglong plateau** forms its southern boundary (elephants cross between the two in flood season), Jorhat to the east, the Eastern Himalaya beyond the river to the north. A **UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985)** and **Tiger Reserve (2006)**, holding the world's largest one-horned rhino population. *Why in news:* on **Aug 24** the **Central Empowered Committee** censured Assam for failing to enforce the Supreme Court's **2019 ban on mining** in the catchments of streams rising in Karbi Anglong; the same SC bench ordered a **fresh nationwide survey of elephant corridors** and barred fireballs/spikes used to divert elephants.

**3. Khalingduar Reserve Forest, Udalguri — Assam (Bodoland Territorial Region).**
Northern Assam in the **Bodoland Territorial Region**, on the **Bhutan border** at the foot of the **eastern Himalayan duars**, in the Dhansiri forest division north of the Brahmaputra. *Why in news:* an Aaranyak project reported this week converts three **invasive alien plants** — *Lantana camara*, *Chromolaena odorata* (Siam weed) and *Mikania micrantha* — into **biochar** to restore native elephant forage.

**4. Bhitardari & the Singhbhum Craton — Jharkhand / Odisha.**
The **Singhbhum craton** is the ancient stable block of the **Chota Nagpur–north Odisha** shield, roughly between the **Subarnarekha** and the Baitarani, taking in Jharkhand's Singhbhum copper belt and northern Odisha (Mayurbhanj, Keonjhar). *Why in news:* a **PNAS** paper (reported Aug 24) dated a banded **chert** at **Bhitardari** to **~3.5 billion years** using zircon U-Pb, making it the **oldest directly dated rock carrying a confirmed biosignature** of microbial life.

**5. Domiasiat & the West Khasi Hills — Meghalaya.**
On the **Meghalaya (Shillong) plateau** west of Shillong, in **West Khasi Hills / South West Khasi Hills** districts, on the rain-drenched southern slope facing the Bangladesh plains (Wahkyn and Lostoin lie nearby). *Why in news:* on **Aug 26** the **Meghalaya Assembly unanimously passed a resolution against all uranium mining and processing** in the state, asking the Centre to shut the **Kylleng-Pyndengsohiong Mawthabah (KPM)** project — Domiasiat holds one of India's richest uranium deposits.

**6. Paradip Port — Jagatsinghpur district, Odisha.**
On the **Bay of Bengal** at the mouth of the **Mahanadi delta** (near the Jatadhari and Mahanadi estuaries), north of the **Devi** river mouth and south of **Dhamra**; a major deep-draught eastern port. *Why in news:* seven projects were commissioned here on **Aug 21** (including deepening to **18.5 m**), and on **Aug 28** a **30-year concession** was awarded to mechanise the CQ-III berth.

## World places

**7. Rasuwa district & the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli system — Nepal.**
Central Nepal directly **north of Kathmandu**, on the **Tibet (China) border**; **Timure** and **Syapru Besi** sit in the **Lhende Khola / Bhote Koshi** valley, which becomes the **Trishuli**, flows through **Nuwakot and Dhading**, joins the **Narayani**, and crosses into Bihar as the **Gandak**. Langtang National Park frames the valley. *Why in news:* on **Aug 26** an **ice-rock avalanche** off a glacier at ~5,200 m dammed the Lhende; the burst sent a surge that raised the Trishuli **9 m in 30 minutes** — **389+ dead in Nepal**, 1,500+ missing across Nepal and Tibet, 19 bridges and ~40 km of road gone. The landslide itself registered as a **magnitude-5.2** seismic event (not an earthquake trigger, per USGS).

**8. Gyirong (Kyirong) County & Gyirong Port — Shigatse, Tibet, China.**
On the **southern rim of the Tibetan Plateau** in **Shigatse prefecture**, the main **China–Nepal land trade gateway** west of the older Kodari/Zhangmu crossing, on the road toward **Mount Kailash and Lake Mansarovar**. *Why in news:* mud and debris from the same Aug 26 flood struck **Gyirong Port**; **558 missing** (260 foreign nationals, many Kailash-Mansarovar pilgrims). **Premier Li Qiang** visited, and **Xi Jinping** ordered an "all-out" search.

**9. Tashkent — Uzbekistan.**
Capital of Uzbekistan in the **Chirchik valley** at the western end of the **Fergana/Tian Shan** foothills, in the **Amu Darya–Syr Darya (Turan) basin** of doubly-landlocked Central Asia; Kazakhstan lies north, the Kyzylkum desert west. *Why in news:* **PM Modi's state visit on Aug 29–30, 2026** (his fourth), with delegation-level talks with President Mirziyoyev.

**10. Bishkek — Kyrgyzstan.**
Capital in the **Chuy valley** at the northern foot of the **Kyrgyz Ala-Too (Tian Shan)**, near the Kazakh border; **Lake Issyk-Kul** lies east, the **Fergana valley** south-west. *Why in news:* hosts the **26th SCO Summit, Aug 31–Sep 1, 2026**, marking the bloc's **25th anniversary** — agenda includes an SCO development bank and the flagship **China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan (CKU) railway**.

**11. Torugart Pass & the CKU railway corridor — China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan.**
A **Tian Shan pass** on the China–Kyrgyzstan border; the ~530 km line runs **Kashgar → Torugart → Makmal → Jalal-Abad → Andijan**, threading the Tian Shan (Naryn, Kosh-Dobo and Fergana tunnels) into the **Fergana valley**, and feeds the **Middle Corridor** toward the Caspian and Türkiye. *Why in news:* the flagship connectivity project of the Bishkek SCO summit; note the **gauge break at Makmal** (1,435 mm Chinese standard → 1,520 mm Russian broad gauge).

**12. Kaub on the Rhine — with the Danube at Paks, the Po at Cremona, the Loire at Saumur.**
**Kaub** is the Rhine's shallowest reference gauge, in the **Rhine gorge** between Bingen and Koblenz, Germany; **Paks** is on the middle **Danube** in Hungary; **Cremona** on the **Po** in the north-Italian plain; **Saumur** on the **Loire**, France's longest river. *Why in news:* Europe's drought-hit summer pushed all four to record lows — **Kaub fell to 21 cm on Aug 3–4, the lowest since records began in 1880** — stalling Rhine barge traffic into late August and straining cooling water at the **Paks nuclear plant**.

**13. Kīlauea & Halemaʻumaʻu crater — Hawaiʻi Island, USA.**
A shield volcano on the **southeast flank of Hawaiʻi (Big Island)**, beside Mauna Loa, on the **Hawaiian hotspot** in the mid-Pacific — a classic **intra-plate / hotspot** volcano, *not* on the Ring of Fire. *Why in news:* **Episode 54** of the episodic Halemaʻumaʻu eruption ran through **Aug 25**, with lava fountains to ~**150 m (500 ft)** over nine hours before pausing.

**14. Surat Thani — Thailand (with Subang, Malaysia).**
**Surat Thani** lies on the **Gulf of Thailand** coast of the **Malay Peninsula**, near the **Isthmus of Kra** and opposite Ko Samui; **Subang** air base sits near **Kuala Lumpur** on the peninsula's western, **Strait of Malacca** side. *Why in news:* **Exercise Maitree 2026** (India–Thailand jungle/counter-insurgency) ran **Aug 18–31** at Vibhavadi Rangsit Camp, Surat Thani; **Exercise Udara Shakti 2026** (India–Malaysia air) was held at Subang from Aug 13.

## Map / location MCQs

**Q1.** The Aug 26 Himalayan flood surge travelled down which sequence before threatening Bihar?
a) Lhende → Bhote Koshi → Trishuli → Narayani → Gandak
b) Arun → Sun Koshi → Kosi
c) Karnali → Ghaghara
d) Kali Gandaki → Rapti

**Q2.** **Gyirong Port**, hit by the same flood, is a China–Nepal trade gateway in which prefecture of Tibet?
a) Ngari  b) Shigatse  c) Lhasa  d) Nyingchi

**Q3.** **Kaziranga National Park** lies in which two Assam districts, with which hills forming its southern boundary?
a) Sonitpur & Biswanath; Bhutan hills
b) Golaghat & Nagaon; Karbi Anglong hills
c) Dhubri & Kokrajhar; Garo hills
d) Tinsukia & Dibrugarh; Patkai hills

**Q4.** **Kaub**, which fell to a record 21 cm this summer, is the shallowest reference gauge on which river?
a) Danube  b) Elbe  c) Rhine  d) Rhône

**Q5.** The **China–Kyrgyzstan–Uzbekistan railway** crosses the Tian Shan at which pass, running from Kashgar toward the Fergana valley?
a) Khunjerab  b) Torugart  c) Karakoram  d) Zoji La

**Q6.** **Kīlauea**, erupting again on Aug 25, is best described as which type of volcano?
a) A Ring-of-Fire subduction stratovolcano
b) A mid-ocean-ridge fissure volcano
c) An intra-plate hotspot shield volcano
d) A continental rift volcano

**Q7.** The **Singhbhum Craton**, source of the 3.5-billion-year biosignature, spans which two states?
a) Chhattisgarh & Telangana  b) Jharkhand & Odisha  c) Bihar & West Bengal  d) Odisha & Andhra Pradesh

### Answer key

- **Q1 — a** (Lhende Khola → Bhote Koshi → Trishuli → Narayani; it is the **Gandak** in India)
- **Q2 — b** (Gyirong County, Shigatse prefecture, southern Tibet)
- **Q3 — b** (Golaghat & Nagaon; the Karbi Anglong hills, where the mining violations occurred)
- **Q4 — c** (the Rhine, in the gorge between Bingen and Koblenz; record low since 1880)
- **Q5 — b** (Torugart Pass; note the Makmal gauge break, 1,435 mm → 1,520 mm)
- **Q6 — c** (Hawaiian hotspot shield volcano — *not* on the Ring of Fire)
- **Q7 — b** (Jharkhand & north Odisha, the Chota Nagpur–Singhbhum shield)
