## Indian places

**1. Gollala Gudi, Palampet — Mulugu district, Telangana.** Palampet lies in northeastern Telangana on the **Deccan plateau**, in the **Godavari** basin north-east of **Warangal** (Mulugu was carved out of Warangal in 2019); it already hosts the **Ramappa (Rudreshwara) Temple**, a UNESCO World Heritage Site inscribed in 2021. *Why in news:* on **Aug 31** the Centre/ASI notified the Kakatiya-era **Gollala Gudi** — a *trikutalaya* (three-shrine) temple beside Ramappa — as a **Monument of National Importance** under the **AMASR Act, 1958**.

**2. Sriharikota & the Satish Dhawan Space Centre (SDSC-SHAR) — Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh.** A **barrier island** on the **Bay of Bengal** that separates **Pulicat Lake** (India's second-largest brackish lagoon, shared with Tamil Nadu) from the sea, roughly 80 km north of **Chennai**; the spindle-shaped island gives an eastward over-sea launch corridor and sits close to the equator. *Why in news:* ISRO flew **GSLV-F17 with EOS-05** at **02:55 IST on Sep 4**, placing the 2,367-kg satellite — the GSLV's heaviest payload yet — into a sub-geosynchronous transfer orbit.

**3. Prayagraj (Sangam of Ganga & Yamuna) — Uttar Pradesh.** At the **confluence (Sangam)** of the **Ganga** and the **Yamuna** in the southern Doab; upstream the Yamuna passes **Delhi–Agra–Etawah**, the Ganga passes **Kanpur**; downstream the combined river runs to **Varanasi** and the **Ghaghara/Son** junctions. *Why in news:* around **Sep 1** both rivers rose sharply after torrential rain over Uttarakhand and western UP, pushing floodwater into riverbank settlements at Prayagraj and putting Ganga–Yamuna danger marks under watch.

**4. Pakal Dul dam & the Marusudar river — Kishtwar district, Jammu & Kashmir.** On the **Marusudar**, the largest tributary of the **Chenab**, in the **Chenab valley** between the **Pir Panjal** and the **Great Himalaya**; Kishtwar lies east of Doda, with the Kiru, Kwar and Ratle projects on the same stretch. *Why in news:* the **1,000 MW Pakal Dul** project (CVPPL/NHPC) — which will carry **India's tallest dam on the Indus system (167 m)** — is being pushed to commissioning through **September–December 2026**, part of the accelerated Chenab build-out after the Indus Waters Treaty was put in abeyance.

**5. Vizhinjam (Thiruvananthapuram) — Kerala.** On the **Arabian Sea/Malabar coast** about 16 km south of Thiruvananthapuram, next to **Kovalam** and close to **Cape Comorin (Kanniyakumari)**; it sits ~10 nautical miles off the **east–west international shipping lane**, with a natural depth of ~20 m. *Why in news:* India's first deepwater transshipment port began **full EXIM (export–import) operations** from **Aug 18**, reviewed again in late August; Kerala simultaneously launched **"Mission Samudra"**, a port-led plan for a Vizhinjam Maritime Economic Region along the state's 600-km coast — a direct challenge to **Colombo, Singapore and Jebel Ali**.

**6. Bharat Mandapam, Pragati Maidan — New Delhi.** On the **Yamuna's** right bank in the National Capital Territory, the venue that hosted the 2023 G20 summit. *Why in news:* India, chairing **BRICS in 2026** under the theme "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability," will host the **18th BRICS Summit here on Sep 12–13** — announcements and sherpa-level preparation ran through the first week of September.

**7. Kargil → Leh, Ladakh (UT).** **Kargil** sits on the **Suru river** (an Indus tributary) in the trans-Himalayan **Zanskar range**, linked to the Kashmir valley by the **Zoji La** pass; **Leh** lies downstream on the **Indus** itself, below the **Ladakh range** and the **Khardung La**. *Why in news:* a fortnight-long **"Ladakh Peace and Justice March"** from Kargil to Leh was announced in late August for **Sep 10–24**, timed to the first anniversary of the 2025 Leh firing, reviving the demand for **statehood and Sixth Schedule** status for Ladakh.

## World places

**8. Zhangpu (Zhangzhou), Fujian — with Yuhuan, Zhejiang, China.** **Zhangpu** is on Fujian's south coast facing the **Taiwan Strait**, near **Xiamen**; **Putian** lies up the same coast; **Yuhuan** is an island-county of **Taizhou, Zhejiang**, on the **East China Sea**. *Why in news:* **Typhoon Saudel**, the 18th storm of the 2026 Pacific season, made a **rare third landfall** at Zhangpu on **Sep 3** after two landfalls at Yuhuan on **Aug 28**; 83,000+ were evacuated in Fujian and more than 100 houses collapsed at Huating, Putian.

**9. Johnson Bayou (Cameron Parish), Louisiana — USA.** On the **Gulf of Mexico** coast right at the **Texas–Louisiana line**, beside **Sabine Pass** and **Sabine Lake**, west of the Atchafalaya basin and east of **Galveston Bay**. *Why in news:* **Tropical Storm Edouard** came ashore here on **Sep 1** with 60 mph winds, then stalled over east Texas and dumped **10–21 inches of rain**, knocking out power to 150,000+ customers.

**10. Anak Krakatau & the Sunda Strait — Indonesia.** The "child of Krakatoa" cone in the **Sunda Strait**, the channel separating **Sumatra** from **Java** and linking the **Java Sea** to the **Indian Ocean**; the 1883 caldera collapse and the 1927 rebirth of the cone happened here. *Why in news:* a major eruption at **00:50 WIB on Sep 5** threw ash to about **15 km (50,000 ft)**, tracked west by the **Darwin VAAC** — a live aviation and tsunami-risk concern on one of the world's busiest sea lanes.

**11. Mount Sinabung — Karo, North Sumatra, Indonesia.** A stratovolcano in the **Barisan range** of northern Sumatra, close to **Lake Toba** (the world's largest volcanic lake) and the **Toba caldera**, inland of **Medan**. *Why in news:* Indonesia's Geological Agency raised Sinabung to **Level III (Siaga)** after an **Aug 31** eruption sent ash ~3,500 m above the summit — its first serious reactivation in several years.

**12. Kuh-e Kolang Gaz La ("Pickaxe Mountain") & Natanz — Isfahan province, Iran.** On Iran's arid **Central Plateau**, ~225 km south of **Tehran**, immediately south of the **Natanz** enrichment complex and north of **Isfahan**, in the shadow of the **Zagros**. *Why in news:* on **Sep 5** the US president said Washington might "hit" the site "very soon" — the deeply buried (100 m+) hall that the **IAEA has never been allowed to inspect** — as US–Iran strikes continued through early September.

**13. Niamey & Base 101 — Niger (with the Tillabéri, Téra and Dosso regions).** **Niamey** stands on the **Niger river** in south-western Niger; **Base 101** adjoins Diori Hamani airport. **Tillabéri** covers the **Liptako-Gourma** tri-border with **Mali and Burkina Faso** (Téra and Ouallam within it); **Dosso** lies south toward the **Benin/Nigeria** frontier. *Why in news:* on the night of **Aug 28–29** mutinous junior officers — reportedly drawn from the jihadist-hit Dosso, Téra and Tillabéri regions — seized the airport, state radio and TV in a **coup attempt**; the Presidential Guard, with Russian backing, retook Base 101, and the junta declared the plot foiled.

**14. Kyiv, Odesa and Dnipro — Ukraine.** **Kyiv** on the **Dnieper (Dnipro)** in the north; **Dnipro** city downstream in the industrial east; **Odesa** the Black Sea port near the **Dniester estuary** and the **Danube delta**. *Why in news:* **Sep 1–2** brought a **sixth consecutive night** of mass Russian missile-and-drone strikes, killing at least a dozen in Kyiv (seven of them at a railway depot) and hitting Odesa and Dnipro as the school year opened.

### Answer key

- **Q1 — c** (Pulicat Lake; Sriharikota is the barrier island in Tirupati district, AP)
- **Q2 — b** (Chenab; the Marusudar rises in the Warwan valley and joins the Chenab in Kishtwar)
- **Q3 — d** (Sunda Strait, between Sumatra and Java)
- **Q4 — a** (Lake Toba; Sinabung sits in Karo regency, Barisan range)
- **Q5 — c** (Natanz; the buried hall lies immediately south of the enrichment complex, Isfahan province)
- **Q6 — b** (Liptako-Gourma; Téra and Ouallam lie within Tillabéri)
- **Q7 — b** (Ramappa/Rudreshwara Temple, inscribed 2021 — Gollala Gudi is a Kakatiya *trikutalaya* alongside it)
