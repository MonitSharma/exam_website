## Indian places

**1. Amarnath Cave — Jammu & Kashmir.** The ice-lingam cave (~3,888 m) sits in the upper **Lidder valley** of the Himalayas. Two routes: the longer traditional **Pahalgam route** (Anantnag district → Chandanwari → **Sheshnag Lake** → **Mahagunas Pass**, ~4,276 m → Panchtarni) and the short, steep **Baltal route** (Ganderbal district). *Why:* the **Amarnath Yatra 2026** is set to begin — *Pratham Puja* on **June 29**, the 57-day yatra running **July 3–Aug 28**; J&K Police launched **'Project Hawk Eye'** surveillance in Anantnag ahead of it.

**2. Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary — Madhya Pradesh.** Spread across **Mandsaur and Neemuch** districts on the **Chambal river**, right up against the **MP–Rajasthan border** (the Gandhi Sagar dam is on the Chambal). *Why:* the **Cheetah Project Steering Committee approved shifting some cheetahs from Kuno** here, to build a **Kuno–Gandhi Sagar metapopulation** of 60–70 cats across the MP–Rajasthan landscape.

**3. Tungabhadra Dam — Karnataka.** At **Munirabad, Koppal district**, near **Hosapete/Hampi**; the **Tungabhadra** is the largest right-bank tributary of the **Krishna**, formed by the Tunga + Bhadra and shared by **Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana**. *Why:* CM inaugurated **33 newly installed spillway crest gates on June 25**, replacing the gate lost when a chain snapped in the August 2024 failure that dumped huge volumes of water.

**4. Kuchai & Bhagaiya (Jharkhand).** **Kuchai** is in **Seraikela-Kharsawan** district and **Bhagaiya** in **Godda** district, on the **Chhota Nagpur plateau**. *Why:* Jharkhand bagged **four new GI tags** — **Kuchai tasar (tussar) silk, Bhagaiya silk, Munda tribal jewellery and the state's bamboo craft** — its biggest single GI haul.

**5. Kozhikode (Calicut) — Kerala.** On the **Malabar Coast** of northern Kerala, between the Arabian Sea and the Wayanad spur of the Western Ghats. *Why:* a **lab-confirmed Nipah virus (NiV) case** (confirmed June 11) with 100+ contacts traced — Kerala's recurring Nipah hotspot (outbreaks since 2018).

**6. Sitapur — Uttar Pradesh.** Central UP (Awadh), just north of Lucknow in the **Ganga–Gomti plain**. *Why:* approval for a **250 MW solar park with battery storage on ~850 acres of vacant defence land** — flagged as a model for repurposing idle land for renewables.

## World places

**7. San Felipe / Caracas / La Guaira — Venezuela.** On the **Caribbean coast of northern South America**; Caracas sits just inland of the coastal range, with **La Guaira** (its port and main airport) on the shore below. *Why:* a **Mw 7.5 earthquake on June 24** (preceded 39 seconds earlier by a Mw 7.2 foreshock), epicentre near **San Felipe, Yaracuy** — the strongest to hit Venezuela since 1900, with worst damage in **La Guaira and Caracas**.

**8. Mount Etna — Sicily, Italy.** **Europe's largest and highest active volcano**, on the east coast of **Sicily** near **Catania**, overlooking the **Ionian Sea**. *Why:* a **June 2 pyroclastic flow** (from a caldera-wall collapse) sent an ~6.5 km ash-and-SO₂ plume up and scattered tourists, with further summit activity through mid-June.

**9. Goma & Lake Kivu — DR Congo.** Capital of **North Kivu**, on the north shore of **Lake Kivu** (an African Great Lake in the **Albertine/Western Rift**), at the foot of **Nyiragongo** volcano and on the **Rwanda border** (Gisenyi across). *Why:* fighting persisted as **M23 held Goma**; the Congolese army carried out a **drone strike at Kibati** just north of the city, with peace talks expected in Switzerland.

**10. Strait of Hormuz — Iran / Oman.** Chokepoint between **Iran** (north) and Oman's **Musandam**/UAE (south), linking the **Persian Gulf** to the **Gulf of Oman**; ~20–25% of seaborne oil and LNG transits here. *Why (new development):* a UN-backed evacuation corridor was **paused after a vessel was hit by a projectile off Oman (June 24–25)**, even as the US said tanker flows were nearing normal — a fresh escalation in the ongoing 2026 Gulf crisis.

**11. Busan — South Korea.** Korea's second city, a major port on the **southeast tip of the peninsula**, on the **Korea Strait** facing Japan (Tsushima). *Why:* confirmed host of **UNESCO's 48th World Heritage Committee (July 20–29, 2026)**, which will weigh ~30 new World Heritage nominations — in the news as preparations ramped up.

**12. Ibu Volcano — Indonesia.** On **Halmahera island, North Maluku**, in eastern Indonesia's **Pacific Ring of Fire**. *Why:* one of Indonesia's most restless volcanoes in 2026 kept erupting through June — frequent ash plumes (to ~2 km) triggering repeated aviation ash advisories.

## Quick map MCQs

**Q1.** Pahalgam, the base of the longer Amarnath Yatra route, lies in which district of Jammu & Kashmir?
a) Ganderbal  b) Anantnag  c) Baramulla  d) Pulwama

**Q2.** The Tungabhadra, whose dam's new spillway gates were just inaugurated, is the largest tributary of which river?
a) Godavari  b) Kaveri  c) Krishna  d) Penna

**Q3.** Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary, India's second cheetah home, lies on which river?
a) Betwa  b) Ken  c) Chambal  d) Narmada

**Q4.** Goma, in the news for the M23 conflict, sits on the shore of which lake?
a) Lake Tanganyika  b) Lake Kivu  c) Lake Edward  d) Lake Albert

**Q5.** Mount Etna, which erupted again in June, is located on which island?
a) Sardinia  b) Crete  c) Sicily  d) Cyprus

### Answer key
1 — **b** (Anantnag; the Baltal route starts in Ganderbal)
2 — **c** (Krishna — Tungabhadra is its largest right-bank tributary)
3 — **c** (Chambal; the Gandhi Sagar dam is on the Chambal, MP–Rajasthan)
4 — **b** (Lake Kivu, in the Albertine Rift, on the DRC–Rwanda border)
5 — **c** (Sicily — Europe's largest active volcano, near Catania)
