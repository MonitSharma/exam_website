## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**
1. **(a)** — *Puttaswamy* (privacy under Art. 21) and *Anuradha Bhasin* (internet speech under 19(1)(a); no indefinite shutdowns) are right; Art. 19(2) lists specific grounds (sovereignty, security, public order, decency or morality, etc.) and "child protection" is not one, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(c)** — 11.39% is above August's 9.92%, and the WPI is compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT; the WPI covers goods only, not services, so 2 is wrong.
3. **(c)** — *Vyommitra* flies on the uncrewed G1, and the crew is to be placed in about 400 km LEO; the Service Module is jettisoned before re-entry and is not recovered with the Crew Module, so 2 is wrong.
4. **(d)** — ASPEX is in-situ (as are PAPA and the magnetometers); VELC, SUIT, SoLEXS and HEL1OS are the four remote-sensing payloads.
5. **(b)** — The first Public Service Commission began on 1 October 1926 under the Government of India Act, 1919, so 1 is wrong; the Federal PSC (1937) under the 1935 Act preceded the UPSC, and Art. 315 provides for Union and State Commissions.
6. **(a)** — The report is a yearly UNEP publication and its 2026 verdict is that 1.5°C without overshoot is gone; NDCs are communicated every five years, not ten, so 3 is wrong.
7. **(b)** — SIOFA excludes highly migratory species (tuna fall under the IOTC, which India joined in 1996), so 1 is wrong; India was a Cooperating Non-Contracting Party from 2022.
8. **(c)** — Barren Island is India's only active volcano and a Tentative List entry must precede nomination; Sirpur is in Chhattisgarh, not Odisha.
9. **(a)** — Revised ICI: base 2022-23, nine industries (Iron Ore added), Electricity 30.93% then Refinery Products 22.57%; it is compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, not the NSO, so 3 is wrong.
10. **(b)** — Ladakh has been a UT without a legislature since August 2019, so 1 is wrong; the Sixth Schedule under Art. 244(2) and the Art. 371–371J special provisions are right.
11. **(d)** — All three hold: Strategic Partnership in August 2024, borders with Ukraine and Belarus, and EU membership from 2004.
12. **(c)** — OIC founded 1969 (Rabat), HQ Jeddah, and India was Guest of Honour at Abu Dhabi in 2019; India is neither a member nor an observer, so 2 is wrong.

**Part B — Modern History**
13. **(c)** — The first three are correct; Bareilly's leader was Khan Bahadur Khan (Tantia Tope is linked with Kanpur and Gwalior).
14. **(a)** — The Nizam accepted the Subsidiary Alliance first (1798) and Satara fell under the Doctrine of Lapse (1848); Awadh was annexed in 1856 on the charge of misgovernance, not Lapse.
15. **(b)** — Permanent Settlement fixed the demand in perpetuity, so 1 is wrong; Ryotwari was with the cultivator and Mahalwari with the village community.
16. **(d)** — All three are correct: Partition effective 16 October 1905 under Curzon, boycott proclaimed 7 August 1905, annulment announced at the 1911 Delhi Durbar.
17. **(b)** — The resolution was first adopted at the Calcutta special session (September 1920) and ratified at Nagpur, so 1 is wrong; Chauri Chaura and the Swaraj Party (1923) are right.
18. **(a)** — The Simon Commission was all-white and the Nehru Report rejected separate electorates; the Lahore session was presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru, not Gandhi.
19. **(b)** — Provincial Dyarchy ended and Dyarchy came to the Centre; the Federation never came into being, and separate electorates were retained and extended (to depressed classes, women and labour).
20. **(d)** — The 8 August 1942 resolution and "Do or Die" call, the 9 August flag hoisting by Aruna Asaf Ali, and Usha Mehta's underground Congress Radio are all correct.
