**RP1.** Name the two Bills passed by Parliament this week that concern national symbols and State identity respectively — and the article/act each engages.

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** The Bill repeals the 2021 Act and creates the permanent NTC; the chairperson is a former SC Judge / HC Chief Justice appointed after consultation with the CJI. Statement 3 is wrong — the **jurisdiction of tribunals is not altered.**

**Q2 — (a).** Article 3 requires the President's prior recommendation to introduce the Bill and referral to the State Legislature — but the Legislature's views are **advisory, not binding** (so 2 is wrong). First Schedule changes under Article 4 are not Article 368 amendments (3 is correct). "Malayalam" is unchanged, so no constitutional amendment was needed.

**Q3 — (a).** The Bill removed the captive-mine sale cap and renamed NMET → **NMEDT** (exploration *and development*). It did **not** abolish auction (3 is wrong).

**Q4 — (b).** Non-fossil capacity (300.50 GW of ~552 GW) is over 54% of installed base and **includes large hydro and nuclear**. The 500 GW figure comes from the **Panchamrit / COP26** pledge, not the formal NDC text (c wrong); capacity share ≠ generation share (d wrong).

**Q5 — (a).** Ladakh is enumerated early due to winter inaccessibility; Census 2027 is the first caste enumeration beyond SC/ST since 1931. **"Census" is a Union List subject** (Entry 69), not Concurrent — so 3 is wrong.

**Q6 — (b).** The Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Act, 2026 inserts Vande Mataram into **Section 3** of the 1971 Act. It remains **absent from Article 51A(a)** (which names only the National Flag and Anthem) and is not a Fundamental Right.

**Q7 — (b).** Section 22 = sole right to issue; Section 26 = legal tender guaranteed by the Centre. Statement 2 is wrong — **form, material and design fall under Section 25** (Section 24 governs denominations, up to ₹10,000).

**Q8 — (b).** It was a **user trial by the SFC** of the already-inducted Agni-4 — a canisterised, road-mobile, two-stage **solid-propellant** MRBM. Not an ICBM, SLBM, or liquid-fuelled system.

**Q9 — (b).** CHPV is a **sandfly-borne Rhabdovirus** (Vesiculovirus genus) causing acute febrile illness and encephalitis, chiefly in children; historically reported from central/western India.

**Q10 — (b).** The Mecca joint defence pact was signed by **Saudi Arabia, Pakistan and Türkiye.**

**Q11 — (c).** The Asiatic lion population rose to **891 (2025)** from **523 (2015)** — a ~70% rise, per the latest Gir-region estimation.

**Q12 — (d).** All three are correctly matched: Param Pragya (IIT Delhi–Sonipat AI supercomputer), Deepak Dhar (2026 ICTP Dirac Medal), and X(2370) (BESIII — lightest pseudoscalar **glueball**).

**Q13 — (a).** FDs were added by the **42nd Amendment (1976)** per the Swaran Singh Committee; the education duty (6–14 yrs) was added by the **86th Amendment (2002)**. They are **non-justiciable** — so 3 is wrong.

**Q14 — (a).** Articles 323A and 323B were inserted by the **42nd Amendment, 1976**. Article 323A (administrative tribunals) empowers **only Parliament** (b wrong); Article 323B tribunals can be created by **Parliament or State Legislatures** within their competence (c wrong); the provisions were added in 1976, not present from 1950 (d wrong).

**Q15 — (b).** The **Chairman/Speaker** of the House decides disqualification under the Tenth Schedule; the decision is **subject to judicial review** (*Kihoto Hollohan*, 1992).

**Q16 — (d).** All correct: Article 280 constitutional body, constituted every fifth year or earlier by the President, and its recommendations, while advisory in law, are treated as **binding in practice on tax devolution** and have never been rejected. *(UPSC accepts "binding" for the tax-sharing recommendations as the intended answer.)*

**Q17 — (a).** **Adjournment sine die** ends a sitting/session with **no date fixed** for reassembly and is done by the **presiding officer**. It is *not* prorogation (a President's act), does not dissolve the House, and pending bills do **not** lapse merely on adjournment.

**Q18 — (d).** Under Article 111 the President may assent, withhold assent, or **return a non-Money Bill** for reconsideration — but if the Houses pass it again (with or without changes), the President **must** assent. All three are correct.

**Q19 — (c).** DPSPs (Part IV) are **fundamental in governance but non-justiciable** (Article 37). They are borrowed from **Ireland**, not the US (b wrong).

**Q20 — (c).** The **CAG** (Article 148) is a constitutional body. NITI Aayog is an executive body; NHRC and CIC are statutory bodies.

**RP1.** (i) **Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Act, 2026** — inserts *Vande Mataram* into **Section 3** of the 1971 Act (national symbol). (ii) **Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026** — renames Kerala to "Keralam" under **Article 3** (First Schedule, no Art. 368 amendment needed).

**RP2.** Unemployment rate (15+) **rose to 5.4%** (from 5.0% in Jan–Mar 2026) and **LFPR slipped to 54.6%** — PLFS Quarterly Bulletin, April–June 2026 (NSO/MoSPI).
