## Part C — Active Recall Prompts (one-line answers)

**R1.** In one line, explain *why* expanding the Supreme Court's strength through an **ordinance** (rather than an Act of Parliament) raised concerns about judicial independence.

**R2.** In one line, state what makes a wetland eligible for **Ramsar** designation and name India's 100th such site.

## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**

1. **B** — The strength was raised via the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026, promulgated under Article 123 (President's ordinance power).
2. **C** — Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal) is in Ballia district, Uttar Pradesh.
3. **B** — The MPC kept the repo rate at 5.25% and retained a 'Neutral' stance (decision announced 5 June 2026).
4. **B** — Surha Tal is a natural oxbow lake formed by the meandering course of the Ganga.
5. **B** — The CCEA approved the ₹2,533 crore CGGPOA/GPRA campus at Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh's capital.
6. **C** — 22 applicants were cleared under Round-3 of the PLI Scheme for Textiles.
7. **C** — The 52nd G7 Summit (June 2026) is hosted at Evian, France; India attends as a partner country (Modi's 7th consecutive participation).
8. **B** — India now has the most Ramsar Sites in Asia and ranks 3rd globally (after the UK and Mexico).
9. **A** — The 2026 Ordinance amends Section 2 of the Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Act, 1956.
10. **B** — GISAT-2, a geo-imaging satellite for rapid disaster imagery, was slated for a June 2026 launch.
11. **A** — The Court held the ECI may conduct Special Intensive Revision and examine citizenship for electoral purposes, but cannot make a final declaration of citizenship.
12. **B** — The FCRA Amendment Bill, 2026 deals with regulation/centralised control of foreign contributions and NGO assets.

**Part B — Polity**

13. **B** — Article 123 empowers the President to promulgate ordinances during the recess of Parliament (Article 213 is the Governor's equivalent for states).
14. **C** — Article 124(1) fixes a minimum strength but lets Parliament increase the number of SC judges by law.
15. **B** — The basic structure doctrine was propounded in Kesavananda Bharati v. State of Kerala (1973).
16. **C** — The Collegium is not in the original Constitution; it evolved through the Second and Third Judges Cases. The NJAC (99th Amendment) was struck down in 2015.
17. **B** — *Habeas Corpus* means "you may have the body"; it is issued to produce a detained person before the court.
18. **A** — A Money Bill is defined in Article 110, and the Speaker of the Lok Sabha certifies it.
19. **B** — The Tenth Schedule (added by the 52nd Amendment, 1985) contains the anti-defection provisions.
20. **C** — The 44th Amendment removed the Right to Property as a Fundamental Right and made it a constitutional/legal right under Article 300A.

**Part C — Recall Prompts**

**R1.** An ordinance is an executive act with no prior parliamentary debate, so creating new judicial posts (and appointments to them) this way concentrates timing/control in the executive and can be seen as undermining the judiciary's institutional independence and security of tenure until Parliament ratifies it.

**R2.** A wetland qualifies for Ramsar status if it meets internationally agreed criteria of ecological significance (e.g., supporting rare/vulnerable species or large waterbird populations); India's 100th Ramsar Site is the Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal), Ballia, Uttar Pradesh.
