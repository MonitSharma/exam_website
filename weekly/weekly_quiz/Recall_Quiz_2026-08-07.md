## Part C — Recall Prompts (one-line answers)

**RP1.** Name the two Supreme Court-related developments Parliament/the Court delivered this week — one on the Court's *composition*, one on the reach of the *domestic-cruelty* offence.

**RP2.** This week saw four big Cabinet-approved schemes with large outlays — PM-KISAN (₹3.15 lakh cr), Samudra Manthan (₹84,084 cr), PM-SSY (₹5,070 cr) and one ₹23,731-crore circular-bioenergy scheme. Name the last one and what it scales up.

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** PM-KISAN is a **Central Sector Scheme** (100% Union-funded, no State share); benefit stays ₹6,000/year in three ₹2,000 instalments via DBT; nodal ministry is Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare.

**Q2 — (b).** India's first Telecom Manufacturing Zone is a 350-acre plug-and-play cluster at **Gwalior, Madhya Pradesh** (DoT–MP MoU, Phase-I 100% central funding of ₹493 crore).

**Q3 — (a).** The ₹36,441-cr Khelo India expansion added **Khelo India Feeder Schools (KIFS)** and **Khelo India Utkrishta Vidyalayas (KIUV)**; funding is anchored to hosting **CWG 2030** and the **Olympics 2036 bid**. Nodal ministry: Youth Affairs & Sports.

**Q4 — (a).** NavIC/IRNSS is a **7-satellite (3 GEO + 4 GSO)** *regional* system covering India + ~1,500 km beyond — not global (statement 3 wrong). Four satellites are needed to solve x, y, z and receiver clock bias.

**Q5 — (a).** **SAGAR (2015) → MAHASAGAR (2025) → SHANTI (2026)**, the last being the operational norms-based maritime governance framework piloted in the Bay of Bengal.

**Q6 — (b).** The Court held **prior EC under the EIA Notification 2006 is mandatory**; any retrospective amnesty needs a statutory notification under **Section 3 of the EP Act, 1986**, not an OM. It allowed only a narrow, time-bound, supervised window — not an absolute bar (so (a) is wrong).

**Q7 — (b).** 'Samudra Manthan' is a **Central Sector Scheme of the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas** (₹84,084 cr to FY2030-31) for deepwater/ultra-deepwater oil & gas exploration; targets >600 MMTOE reserve accretion.

**Q8 — (a).** C-193 is the **first binding standard for the platform economy**, and the ILO is the **only tripartite UN agency**. India **abstained** (it did not vote against) — so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q9 — (b).** The **16th FC (Arvind Panagariya)**, award period **2026–31**, kept the vertical share at **41%**; devolution is **untied** money, so (c) is wrong.

**Q10 — (b).** PM-SSY (₹5,070 cr, MNRE) builds **Floating Solar PV with mandatory energy storage** (≥2 hours) — a land-neutral, grid-firming design, not plain capacity.

**Q11 — (a).** Glaw Lake (Lohit district) is India's **101st Ramsar Site** and **Arunachal Pradesh's first**, lying inside the **Kamlang Tiger Reserve**.

**Q12 — (d).** All three occurred this week: the two INR LoCs to Sri Lanka (USD 250 mn railways + USD 100 mn livestock), EPI 2026 ranking India 176/177, and Nauru becoming the **Republic of Naoero**.

**Q13 — (b).** There are **75 PVTGs** (of 705 Scheduled Tribes), and the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** runs the Development of PVTGs scheme. First identified in 1975 (52) + 1993 (23).

**Q14 — (c).** **M.N. Srinivas** coined *Sanskritisation* (and also the "dominant caste" and "Westernisation" concepts).

**Q15 — (b).** NFHS-5 recorded an all-India sex ratio of **1020 females per 1000 males** — the first time it crossed 1000 (up from 991 in NFHS-4). Note: this is the *total-population* ratio, not sex ratio at birth.

**Q16 — (b).** The **"dominant caste"** concept is **M.N. Srinivas's**, from his study of Rampura village, Karnataka.

**Q17 — (b).** The **demographic dividend** is the growth potential from a rising share of the **working-age population (15–64)** relative to dependents.

**Q18 — (a).** Article 342 lets the **President specify STs in consultation with the Governor** (1 correct); the Constitution does **not define "tribe"** (3 correct). Lists are **State-specific**, not a single uniform national list (2 wrong).

**Q19 — (b).** Replacement-level fertility is a TFR of **2.1**. (India's TFR is now below this, ~2.0.)

**Q20 — (b).** India follows **"principled distance"** (Rajeev Bhargava's term) — the state may engage with or distance itself from religion to advance equality, liberty and reform — not the strict "wall of separation" (a).

**RP1.** (i) Parliament passed the **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026**, raising sanctioned strength from **34 to 38** (amending the 1956 Act, not the Constitution). (ii) The SC held that **domestic cruelty** (Section 498A IPC ≡ Sections 85–86 BNS 2023) extends to women in **live-in relationships "in the nature of marriage."**

**RP2.** **GOBARdhan — the National Circular Bioenergy Scheme** (₹23,731 crore, 10 years). It scales up **Compressed Biogas (CBG)** production (administered price ₹2,110/MMBTU + CBG Blending Obligation), fixing the offtake/price failures that limited the earlier SATAT initiative.
