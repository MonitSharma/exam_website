## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs (27 June – 3 July 2026)**

1. **B — Only two.** Statements 1 and 2 are correct (Seychelles' 50th Independence Day; MAHASAGAR articulated in Mauritius, March 2025, expanding SAGAR). Statement 3 is wrong — only ~5% of Seychelles' citizens are of Indian origin, not a majority.
2. **B** — The Court held Article 329(b) bars writ interference once an election process has begun, with no exception even for "glaring" illegality; the only remedy is a post-poll election petition.
3. **B** — Bangladesh joined the International Big Cat Alliance (HQ New Delhi) as its 27th member on 24 June 2026.
4. **A** — SE-2000 (LOX + refined kerosene) will power the new SC120 stage replacing the LVM3's L110 liquid stage; it is semi-cryogenic, not hydrogen-burning.
5. **B** — Both were launched under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) umbrella, alongside the "Ayushman Sarathi" WhatsApp chatbot.
6. **B — Only two.** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — rollout was deferred to 1 January 2027, not immediate.
7. **B** — It needed a special majority under Article 368 (majority of total membership + two-thirds present and voting); it secured only 298 of the 352 votes required.
8. **D — 1, 2 and 3.** GST 2.0 (effective 22 September 2025) created the 5%/18% structure with a 40% rate for luxury/sin goods.
9. **A** — e-OCI is a QR-code digital replacement for the physical OCI booklet; FCRA 2.0 is an API-based, AI-chatbot-enabled compliance portal for foreign-funded NGOs.
10. **A** — The portal (1,04,561 species across 121 checklists, 36 phyla) made India the first country with a complete taxonomic checklist of its own fauna.
11. **B** — The 1994 UYRB agreement covers Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan and NCT of Delhi.
12. **B** — MANAS is run by the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB) under the Ministry of Home Affairs; helpline 1933, web portal, UMANG app.

**Part B — Ancient & Medieval History & Art/Culture**

13. **B** — The Great Bath is at Mohenjo-daro (Sindh), a large watertight tank thought to be used for ritual bathing.
14. **B — Only two.** Statements 1 and 2 are correct (Prakrit/Brahmi is the norm; Kharosthi used in the north-west). Statement 3 is wrong — the Shahbazgarhi and Mansehra edicts are in Kharosthi, not Greek/Aramaic (the Greek-Aramaic bilingual edict is the separate Kandahar inscription).
15. **B** — Nalanda's major monastic and academic development is chiefly credited to Gupta-era patronage (with later expansion under Harsha and the Palas).
16. **B** — Ramanuja propounded Vishishtadvaita ("qualified non-dualism"), distinct from Shankaracharya's Advaita and Madhvacharya's Dvaita.
17. **A** — Chronological order: Mamluk/Slave (1206) → Khilji (1290) → Tughlaq (1320) → Lodi (1451, after the brief Sayyid dynasty).
18. **B** — Khwaja Moinuddin Chishti founded the Chishti order in India, with his dargah at Ajmer; Nizamuddin Auliya and Baba Farid were later Chishti saints.
19. **C** — The Buland Darwaza ("Door of Victory") is the grand gateway of the Jama Masjid at Fatehpur Sikri, Akbar's capital.
20. **B** — Mohiniyattam originates in Kerala. (Kathakali is also Kerala, not Odisha; Kuchipudi is Andhra Pradesh, not Tamil Nadu; Sattriya is Assam, not Manipur.)

**Part C — Recall Prompts**

**R1.** The Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 needed the Article 368 special majority (total membership + two-thirds present and voting) but secured only 298 of the 352 votes required, falling 54 votes short.

**R2.** GST 2.0 (from 22 September 2025) collapsed most slabs into a 5%/18% structure with a 40% rate for luxury/sin goods, and gross annual GST collections have grown from ~₹7.4 lakh crore (2017-18) to ~₹22.27 lakh crore (2025-26).
