## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**

**Q1 — (b).** India's reserves hit a record ~$785.7 billion, overtaking **Russia** to become the world's **4th-largest** holder, behind **China, Japan and Switzerland**.

**Q2 — (c).** WPI inflation was **9.92%** in August 2026; **Fuel & Power** led at **22.93%** (Primary Articles ~7.76%, Manufactured Products ~8.37%).

**Q3 — (b).** The wage ceiling rose from **₹15,000 to ₹25,000/month** — the **first revision in twelve years** — effective 17 September 2026, extending coverage to ~51 lakh more workers.

**Q4 — (d).** All three are correct: **0.4% MDR** on P2M above **₹2,000**, **capped at ₹300**, with P2P and sub-₹2,000 P2M staying zero-MDR; the framework takes effect **15 October 2026**.

**Q5 — (b).** WCEF 2026 was held in **Gandhinagar** — its **first edition in South Asia** (10th edition overall).

**Q6 — (b).** '**Operation Deep Manifest**' was a **DRI** action seizing **362+ MT of Pakistan-origin dry dates** transshipped through a third country (UAE/Jebel Ali) to evade origin rules.

**Q7 — (b).** *Schizopygopsis waddellii* is a **Tibetan snowtrout** (fish), recorded in India for the first time in **Tawang, Arunachal Pradesh**.

**Q8 — (b).** The BRICS Traditional Medicine Expert Working Group sits primarily under the **Ministry of Ayush**.

**Q9 — (b).** India–MERCOSUR signed the **First Additional Protocol** to their 2004 **PTA**, giving **electronic Certificates of Origin** legal parity with paper — not a full FTA.

**Q10 — (d).** All three: theme "**Silicon to Systems**", venue **Yashobhoomi, New Delhi**, with an **ECMS** backgrounder (106 projects, ~₹69,548 crore approved investment).

**Q11 — (a).** India's first soil-carbon payments: **₹2.9 crore via DBT to ~2,550 smallholder farmers in Punjab and Haryana**.

**Q12 — (b).** August 2026 PLFS: unemployment ~**5.0%**, rural joblessness at a multi-month low (~4.1%), and overall **LFPR rising to ~55.6%**.

**Part B — Environment**

**Q13 — (a).** 1 and 2 are correct. The Ramsar Convention protects wetlands, and the **Montreux Record** flags sites with adverse ecological change. Statement 3 is wrong — the **UK** has the most Ramsar sites; India leads in Asia but not the world.

**Q14 — (c).** The **core zone** is legally protected, undisturbed, and reserved for non-manipulative research; the buffer allows limited activity and the transition zone permits sustainable human use.

**Q15 — (b).** The **Montreal Protocol** phases out **ozone-depleting substances**; the **Kigali Amendment (2016)** adds the phase-down of **HFCs**, which are potent greenhouse gases.

**Q16 — (d).** All three are correct. CITES regulates wildlife trade, **Appendix I** species get the strictest protection (commercial trade generally banned), and it operates under **UNEP**.

**Q17 — (b).** A **keystone species** has an **outsized effect on its ecosystem relative to its abundance** (e.g., a top predator or a fig tree); removing it can collapse community structure.

**Q18 — (a).** India has parts of **four** hotspots: the **Himalaya, Western Ghats–Sri Lanka, Indo-Burma, and Sundaland** (Nicobar Islands).

**Q19 — (c).** **Schedule I** of the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 confers the **highest protection** with the harshest penalties. (After the 2022 amendment the Act was restructured to fewer schedules, but Schedule I retains top protection.)

**Q20 — (b).** **Mangroves** store large amounts of "**blue carbon**" in biomass and waterlogged soils and **protect coasts from storm surges and erosion**.
