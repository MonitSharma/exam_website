## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**

**Q1 — (b).** Pakistan purported to cede **~5,180 sq km** of the Shaksgam Valley to China under the **Sino-Pakistan Boundary Agreement of 2 March 1963**; India has never recognised it, and Article 6 of that pact itself makes the settlement provisional pending resolution of the Kashmir dispute.

**Q2 — (d).** All three. SBR (effective 1 October 2022) has four layers — **Base, Middle, Upper, Top**; Upper-Layer NBFCs must **list within three years** of identification; the **Top Layer** stays empty unless the RBI perceives substantial systemic risk. (Tata Sons was classified NBFC-UL on 30 Sep 2022; its bid to surrender CIC registration was rejected on 11 Sep 2026.)

**Q3 — (a).** Use in **Autism Spectrum Disorder** is confined to approved clinical trials; the one accepted standard-of-care use is **haematopoietic stem cell transplantation** for defined blood disorders. Everything else remains investigational.

**Q4 — (b).** The bird shifts its call **upward** to escape low-frequency traffic/crowd noise — **acoustic masking avoidance / vocal plasticity**, linked to the **Lombard effect** (raising vocal effort in noise). Studied in Kedarnath WLS (Chopta vs Madmaheshwar valleys).

**Q5 — (b).** The **Graham Act** (signed 18 Sep 2026) **authorises** — it does not automatically impose — tariffs of up to **100%** on the five largest buyers of Russian oil/gas, and preserves a **presidential waiver**. It sits on top of the pre-existing 50% US tariff on India.

**Q6 — (a).** The MPC has **six members (3 RBI + 3 Centre-nominated)**, with the Governor holding the **casting vote**. It was created by the **Finance Act, 2016** (amending the RBI Act, 1934), targets **4% CPI ±2%**, and meets at least **four** times a year — so (b), (c) and (d) are wrong.

**Q7 — (d).** All three. Non-permanent members serve **two-year terms**, elected by **two-thirds present and voting**; India faces **Tajikistan** for the single Asia-Pacific seat (2028–29); and adding permanent members needs a **Charter amendment** (Articles 108–109). These are India's two distinct tracks — reform vs election.

**Q8 — (b).** GOBARdhan (₹23,731 crore; MoP&NG) assures **up to 100% offtake** and a **decade-long administered price (₹2,110/MMBTU to 31 March 2036)** for **Compressed Biogas** producers, with a purchase obligation on City Gas Distribution entities.

**Q9 — (b).** A **one-way attack drone / loitering munition** is expendable — **the airframe is the weapon** — unlike a reusable armed UAV that releases a munition and returns, or a cruise missile that flies a fast pre-planned profile without loitering. KAL: 1,000 km range, 50 kg payload, built by the private firm IG Defence.

**Q10 — (c).** Both. Within the alternative-fuel set, **CNG/LPG (25.28%)** dwarfed EVs (7.63%) and hybrids (9.04%) — a *gas* transition with an electric tail. **FADA** reports **retail registrations** (VAHAN), distinct from **SIAM's wholesale despatches**.

**Q11 — (b).** **Veer Guardian 2026** was the **second edition** and the **first held in India** (AFS Jodhpur) — Japanese **F-2** fighters flew from Indian soil for the first time. (The maiden 2023 edition was at Hyakuri, Japan.) It is an **air** exercise, not naval.

**Q12 — (b).** Only **stratospheric ozone depletion** and **atmospheric aerosol loading** remain within the safe operating space; ocean acidification was the most recent boundary declared breached (2025). The 2026 edition newly names the **food system** as the dominant driver across all nine.

**Part B — Science & Technology**

**Q13 — (d).** All three. **Vikram** (lander) and **Pragyan** (rover) soft-landed on **23 August 2023**; the site is **Statio Shiv Shakti**; India was the **first** country to land near the lunar **south pole** (and 4th overall to soft-land on the Moon).

**Q14 — (b).** **Stage 1** = PHWRs on **natural uranium**, breeding **plutonium-239**. Stage 2 = Fast Breeder Reactors (Pu-239 + U-238, with a thorium blanket breeding **U-233**); Stage 3 = **thorium–U-233** reactors — the whole point being to exploit India's large **thorium** reserves.

**Q15 — (d).** All three. NavIC is a **regional** system (India + ~1,500 km), uses **geostationary + inclined geosynchronous** satellites, and provides a **Standard Positioning Service** and a **Restricted Service**.

**Q16 — (b).** **iPSCs** are adult somatic cells reprogrammed to a pluripotent state. Totipotent cells can form a whole organism *plus* extra-embryonic tissue (placenta); multipotent cells are limited to related lineages; **autologous = from the patient** (allogeneic = from a donor).

**Q17 — (b).** A **cruise missile** sustains powered, guided, low-altitude flight using aerodynamic lift. A ballistic missile is powered only in its **boost phase** then follows a ballistic (gravity-driven) trajectory; a loitering munition is **expendable**, not reusable; and ballistic missiles can carry conventional warheads.

**Q18 — (b).** **CRISPR-Cas9** is a **genome-editing** tool: a **guide RNA** directs the **Cas9** nuclease to a specific DNA sequence, where it makes a cut for editing (2020 Nobel Prize in Chemistry, Doudna & Charpentier).

**Q19 — (b).** The **Kessler syndrome** is a runaway **cascade of orbital collisions** in a debris-saturated orbit, each collision generating more fragments — the core rationale for space situational awareness programmes like **NETRA**.

**Q20 — (b).** A **geostationary** satellite sits over the equator at **~35,786 km** with a **~24-hour** period, appearing fixed. Polar/sun-synchronous orbits pass over the poles (remote sensing); the **ISS** is in low-Earth orbit (~400 km).

**Part C — Recall Prompts**

**RP-1.** The **Graham Act** (signed 18 Sep 2026); it **does not** impose tariffs automatically — it grants the President authority (up to 100% on the largest Russian-oil buyers) with a **waiver retained**; India's rationale: securing **energy for 1.4 billion people** via diversified sourcing on **evolving market dynamics**.

**RP-2.** (i) **Structural reform** — expanding UNSC permanent + non-permanent membership, which needs a **Charter amendment** (stuck in the IGN process); (ii) an **ordinary election** — India's bid for a **non-permanent seat (2028–29)**, decided by a simple two-thirds vote in June 2027 (vs Tajikistan).
