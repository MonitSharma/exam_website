## Part D — Answers & Explanations

**Part A — Current Affairs (3–9 July 2026)**

1. **B** — Published by the UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (SDSN); India scored 68.3/100, up from 112th in 2015 (Finland tops the index).
2. **B** — The Melbourne summit's flagship was a civil-use uranium supply pact, alongside a defence-partnership upgrade.
3. **D** — All three correct: Greece is the 10th UPI country and 2nd in the EU (after France), enabled via NIPL–Eurobank.
4. **B** — SOLVE's solid motor is part of ISRO's new Gaganyaan crew-recovery / escape test-vehicle development.
5. **B** — Ocean acidification is the newest of seven breached planetary boundaries (surface-ocean acidity up 30–40% since the industrial era), per CSE/*Down To Earth*.
6. **A** — The SC set "zero tolerance" for AI-hallucinated (fabricated) precedents cited in proceedings, setting aside the affected NCLT/NCLAT orders.
7. **B** — INS Mahendragiri (F38) is the 6th of 7 Project 17A (Nilgiri-class) stealth frigates; built by MDL, >75% indigenous, commissioned at Visakhapatnam.
8. **C** — Modified UDAN runs FY2026-27 to FY2035-36 with a ₹28,840 crore outlay (₹12,159 cr for 100 new aerodromes, VGF, 200 helipads, O&M).
9. **C** — India has 58 notified tiger reserves; the roadmap flags 25 for priority recovery.
10. **B** — Designated Repositories are notified under Section 39 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 (on the NBA's recommendation).
11. **D** — All three correct: Haryana's first GI-tagged fruit, a mandarin hybrid, quality credited to the Indo-Israel CoE, Mangiana.
12. **B** — The Pinaka LRGR was fired from the existing in-service launcher (no new launcher) for a user-defined minimum range of 60 km at ITR Chandipur.

**Part B — Geography**

13. **B** — The Strait of Hormuz links the Persian Gulf to the Gulf of Oman (Iran to the north, Oman's Musandam Peninsula to the south).
14. **C** — The Tropic of Cancer crosses 8 states but NOT Odisha; it passes Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura, Mizoram.
15. **B** — Indira Point (formerly Pygmalion/Parsons Point) is on Great Nicobar Island, the southernmost tip of Indian territory.
16. **D** — The Narmada flows westward through a rift valley into the Arabian Sea; Godavari, Krishna and Mahanadi drain east into the Bay of Bengal.
17. **B** — El Niño (warm ENSO phase) is typically linked with weak/deficient Indian southwest-monsoon rainfall, though not one-to-one.
18. **B** — Regur (black cotton soil) forms from the weathering of basaltic Deccan Trap lava; it is clayey, moisture-retentive and self-ploughing.
19. **B** — 82°30′E passes through Mirzapur (Uttar Pradesh), the basis for IST (UTC+5:30).
20. **B** — The Somali/Findlater Jet is a cross-equatorial low-level jet over the western Indian Ocean that funnels moisture and drives SW-monsoon onset.

**Part C — Recall Prompts**

RP1. **Indonesia, Australia and New Zealand** — the Australia leg's flagship deliverable was a civil-use uranium supply pact (plus a defence-partnership upgrade).
RP2. **Seven of nine** planetary boundaries are now breached; the newest to cross is **ocean acidification** (*State of India's Environment 2026*, CSE / *Down To Earth*).
