## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**
1. **(b)** — Pillay was UN High Commissioner for Human Rights and a judge at the ICTR and the ICC; India is not a party to the Rome Statute, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(c)** — Threshold ₹1 crore → ₹5 crore and arrest powers removed; Art. 279A body. GST Council recommendations are persuasive, not binding (*Mohit Minerals*, 2022), so 2 is wrong.
3. **(a)** — The 50% ceiling is judge-made (*Indra Sawhney*) and the triple test is correct; Arts. 243D/243T enable backward-class reservation but do not state the 50% limit, so 3 is wrong.
4. **(d)** — FERR: 20% cash with the RBI of rupee notional on qualifying contracts above US$2 million; the limit on positions without underlying exposure falls from US$100 million to US$5 million.
5. **(b)** — The Delhi Declaration and the Dhaka Secretariat are correct; BIMSTEC's seven members are Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka and Thailand — not the Maldives or Pakistan.
6. **(c)** — FRA recognises individual and community (CFR) rights and PVTG habitat rights; claims go Gram Sabha → Sub-Divisional Level Committee → District Level Committee (final), so 3 is wrong.
7. **(d)** — El Niño = warm central/eastern Pacific and often a weak monsoon; it weakens (not strengthens) the Walker circulation and trade winds.
8. **(c)** — COP17 is in Yerevan (19–30 Oct) and Nagoya Protocol operationalises ABS; the 2002 Act pre-dates the 2010 Nagoya Protocol and implements the CBD (1992), so 3 is wrong.
9. **(b)** — India is 100th of 122 with 24.1 (serious); the four indicators are right; wasting is low weight for height (stunting is low height for age), so 3 is wrong.
10. **(c)** — The Government and LIC are both sellers (about 30.48% and 30.24%), so 1 is wrong; RBI fit-and-proper approval is pending; the Government retains about 15% and LIC about 19%.
11. **(d)** — Measuring a quantum state disturbs it, so interception creates detectable errors; the resulting keys then feed classical encryption.
12. **(d)** — Renamed PM Samajik Sahayata Yojana and run by the Ministry of Rural Development; the disability benchmark was lowered from 80% to 40% (not raised), so 2 is wrong.

**Part B — Society**
13. **(a)** — 74.04% for population aged 7+; Kerala highest, Bihar lowest; the effective literacy rate uses the 7+ population as denominator (the crude rate uses total population).
14. **(c)** — Census 2011: sex ratio 943, child sex ratio 919; Kerala highest (1084), Haryana lowest (879) among the States.
15. **(b)** — The PVTG category arose from the Dhebar Commission's identification of less-developed tribal groups, not the Mandal Commission; Odisha has the most PVTGs (13 of 75).
16. **(d)** — Art. 17 abolishes untouchability and is enforceable against private individuals too; the 1989 Atrocities Act supplements, and did not replace, the Protection of Civil Rights Act, 1955.
17. **(b)** — Stage 2 (early expanding): the death rate falls rapidly from better health and food while the birth rate stays high, producing the population surge.
18. **(c)** — One-third of Lok Sabha and Assembly seats are reserved, and Rajya Sabha and Councils are excluded; implementation follows delimitation after the relevant census, not immediately.
19. **(a)** — "Secular" and "socialist" were added by the 42nd Amendment (1976); *S.R. Bommai* (1994) held secularism part of the basic structure; Art. 25 covers all persons.
20. **(d)** — Sanskritisation (M.N. Srinivas) is the emulation of upper-caste customs, rituals and lifestyle by lower-caste groups seeking upward mobility.

**Part C — Recall Prompts (model answers)**
- **R1.** Rank 100 of 122, score 24.1, "serious" (20.0–34.9); child wasting (about 19%, up from about 15% in 2012) worsened even as stunting fell to about 29%.
- **R2.** Buyer: a Fairfax Financial Holdings entity (Canada); 60.72% stake for ₹52,878 crore; Government sells about 30.48% and LIC about 30.24%; still pending: RBI fit-and-proper clearance.
