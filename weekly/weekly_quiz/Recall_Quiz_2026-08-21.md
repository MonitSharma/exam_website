## Answers

**Q1 — (c).** The seven *dharas* are Manufacturing; Agriculture & Food Processing; Technology & Innovation; Gati-Shakti (logistics); Raksha Shakti (defence); Green & Blue Economy; and Soft Power. "Jan Dhan Shakti" is not one of them.

**Q2 — (b).** Exports of USD 44.24 bn were a record for any July; a net services surplus (~USD 17 bn) cut the overall deficit to ~USD 15 bn — *smaller*, not larger, than the goods deficit of ~USD 32 bn. So statement 2 is wrong.

**Q3 — (a).** FAST-DS sits in Chapter IV of the Finance Act, 2026, closes on 31 December 2026, targets *small* undisclosed foreign assets (Category 1 ≤ ₹1 crore), and grants valid declarants immunity from penalty/prosecution under the Black Money Act, 2015.

**Q4 — (a).** The core sector carries ~40% weight in the IIP and now uses base year 2022-23. Growth *eased* to 5.4% in July from 6.0% in June — so statement 3 is wrong. *(The eight core industries are coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilisers, steel, cement and electricity.)*

**Q5 — (b).** The RBI's Central Board is constituted under the **Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934**. Somanath and Mahindra were appointed part-time non-official directors.

**Q6 — (a).** CMAS is a ₹10,000-crore, five-year scheme of the **Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways** to build a domestic shipping-container manufacturing ecosystem (~7.5 lakh TEUs near-term), reducing import dependence on China.

**Q7 — (b).** SACU (est. 1910; HQ Windhoek) is the world's oldest existing customs union, comprising South Africa, Botswana, Namibia, Lesotho and Eswatini (B-L-E-N-S). Not all are landlocked — South Africa and Namibia have coasts — so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q8 — (a).** The UNCCD is one of the three Rio Conventions (with UNFCCC and CBD) and India hosted COP14 (New Delhi, 2019). COP17 was at **Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia** (Riyadh hosted COP16 in 2024) — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q9 — (a).** GIM is one of the eight NAPCC missions, and the CAG conducted a performance audit finding a 97.57% shortfall. GIM is a **Centrally Sponsored Scheme** (shared funding), not a Central Sector Scheme — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q10 — (b).** The NDB is headquartered in Shanghai, and its first President was K. V. Kamath (India). NDB accession is *separate* from BRICS membership — Iran joined BRICS in 2024 but must still complete NDB accession — so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q11 — (b).** The 1978 *Bangalore Water Supply* ruling was by a seven-judge Bench, and the broad "industry" test applies wherever an employer–employee relationship exists under the ID Act, 1947. The nine-judge Bench expressly ruled the Industrial Relations Code, 2020 must be read on its own terms — so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q12 — (a).** SraVaani-1.0 is a multilingual ASR model (20 scheduled + 45 regional = 65 languages/dialects) from IISc's SPIRE Lab with ARTPARK and Google, released openly on Hugging Face (built on Project Vaani).

**Q13 — (a).** Primary Deficit = Fiscal Deficit − Interest payments (it strips out past interest liabilities to show the *current year's* fresh borrowing need).

**Q14 — (c).** Private remittances (secondary income / transfers) are a **current account** item. FDI, FPI and ECBs are capital-account flows.

**Q15 — (c).** Moral suasion is a qualitative/selective tool. CRR, SLR and OMO are quantitative (general) instruments.

**Q16 — (a).** M1 is narrow money (most liquid) and M3 (broad money) adds net time deposits. M1 is the *most* liquid, not the least — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q17 — (a).** The RBI targets headline CPI (Combined) at 4% ±2% (retained for 2026–2031; new CPI base year 2024). The CPI is compiled by the NSO/MoSPI, not the RBI — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q18 — (a).** The GST Council is a constitutional body under Article 279A, and alcohol for human consumption is constitutionally excluded from GST. GST is a **destination-based consumption tax**, not an origin/production tax — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q19 — (a).** GNP = GDP + Net factor income from abroad. (NNP = GNP − Depreciation; NNP at factor cost = National Income.)

**Q20 — (a).** The FRBM Act was enacted in 2003, and the N. K. Singh Committee recommended a debt-to-GDP anchor (60% general govt). The Act did *not* mandate RBI deficit financing via ad hoc T-bills — those were phased out in 1997 — so statement 3 is wrong.

**RP1.** Because a large **net services surplus** (services exports ≫ services imports, plus remittances/invisibles) offset most of the goods trade gap — the classic reason India's current account stays manageable despite a big merchandise deficit.

**RP2.** **GDP and IIP → 2022-23**; **CPI → 2024** (base 2024=100); **Index of Core Industries → 2022-23** (WPI is also being moved to a 2022-23 base).
