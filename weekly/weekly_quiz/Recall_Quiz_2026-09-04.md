## Answers

**Q1 — (d).** All three are correct: elevation to Comprehensive Strategic Partnership plus a uranium supply framework; Uzbekistan is doubly landlocked (with Liechtenstein); and UPI–UZQR interoperability was agreed.

**Q2 — (d).** All correct — enabling statute is the Legal Metrology Act, 2009; CSIR-NPL is custodian with ISRO; IST = UTC+5:30 on the 82°30′ E meridian near Mirzapur.

**Q3 — (a).** 1 and 2 are correct (7.8% vs 6.9% a year earlier; real GVA 8.2% > real GDP 7.8%). Statement 3 is wrong — the new series uses base year **2022-23**, not 2011-12.

**Q4 — (b).** Semicon 2.0 (₹1,27,500 crore, six pillars, ten categories) shifts from a fabs-only capex subsidy to whole-of-stack ecosystem support.

**Q5 — (c).** The Electoral Democracy Index is a V-Dem (Varieties of Democracy) product, hosted at the University of Gothenburg, Sweden — distinct from EIU's Democracy Index and Freedom House.

**Q6 — (b).** Article 142 lets only the Supreme Court pass orders "necessary for doing complete justice"; High Courts cannot import it into Article 226, and per *Prem Chand Garg* it cannot override fundamental rights or substantive law.

**Q7 — (d).** All correct — bleaching = loss of zooxanthellae; disturbance interval down to ~4–6 years; DHW (4 °C-weeks = significant bleaching, 8 °C-weeks = widespread mortality) is the stress metric.

**Q8 — (b).** Roman heads for a halo orbit at Sun-Earth **L2** (~1.5 million km) and will census exoplanets chiefly via **gravitational microlensing**.

**Q9 — (b).** Larak is an Iranian island in the Strait of Hormuz, off Bandar Abbas, east of Qeshm — a chokepoint carrying roughly a fifth of global seaborne oil.

**Q10 — (b).** Javelin is a third-generation, fire-and-forget, top-attack ATGM (tandem HEAT warhead) acquired via the US **Foreign Military Sales** route through the DSCA.

**Q11 — (a).** 1 and 3 correct. Statement 2 is wrong — JCR is Japanese and **not** one of the Big Three (S&P, Moody's, Fitch).

**Q12 — (b).** Gollalagudi is a Kakatiya-era trikutalaya (three-shrine) temple at Palampet — the same village as the UNESCO-listed Ramappa (Rudreshwara) Temple.

**Q13 — (b).** The Durga temple's apsidal *gajaprishta* plan resembles a Buddhist chaitya; Aihole was the Chalukyas' architectural laboratory.

**Q14 — (d).** All correct — Kanishka's council in Kashmir, Sarvastivada school, compiled in Sanskrit (the *Mahavibhasha*), with Vasumitra presiding and Ashvaghosha as deputy.

**Q15 — (d).** All correct — Rajaraja I, c. 1010 CE; Dravida vimana; part of the UNESCO Great Living Chola Temples.

**Q16 — (c).** The **Lyrist type** shows Samudragupta playing the veena, projecting him as a cultured ruler; the Ashvamedha type commemorates his horse sacrifice.

**Q17 — (c).** Nalanda Mahavihara is traditionally credited to **Kumaragupta I** (identified with "Shakraditya"), 5th century CE.

**Q18 — (d).** All pairings are correct: Mohenjodaro–Great Bath, Lothal–dockyard, Dholavira–water reservoirs.

**Q19 — (a).** 1 and 2 correct. Statement 3 is wrong — Sangam literature is in **old Tamil**, associated with Pandya (Madurai) patronage, not Sanskrit/Chola.

**Q20 — (b).** Indo-Islamic building introduced **arcuate** construction (true arches and domes) in place of the indigenous **trabeate** (post-and-lintel) system; early Delhi Sultanate work used corbelling before true arches matured.

**R1.** Real GDP grew **7.8%** in Q1 FY27, estimated on the new **2022-23** base-year National Accounts series.

**R2.** The **Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope**, bound for **Sun-Earth L2**.
