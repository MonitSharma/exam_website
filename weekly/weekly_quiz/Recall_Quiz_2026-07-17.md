## Answers

**Q1 — (c).** West Bengal became the **36th** State/UT under PM-JAY (via an NHA–WB MoU), covering ~1.24 crore families and completing 100% national rollout. Health being a State subject, states opt in voluntarily.

**Q2 — (d).** All three correct. Astra Mk-1 is India's first indigenous BVRAAM (DRDO-developed, BDL-produced, ~Mach 4.5, ~100+ km, on the Su-30MKI); Indonesia became its first foreign buyer in July 2026.

**Q3 — (b).** AVSY is a ₹3,000-crore, five-year scheme to conserve ~15,000 sacred groves ("Aastha Vans"), funded through National CAMPA (statutory under the CAF Act, 2016).

**Q4 — (d).** All three correct: Strategic Partnership upgrade, first Indian PM visit to NZ in 40 years, a Roadmap to 2030 plus concluded FTA negotiations (trade-doubling target ~NZ$7 bn by 2030).

**Q5 — (b).** In *Maniyar Iliyaz v. P. Ayyappan* (2026 INSC 647), the Court grounded the right to walk in **Article 21 read with Article 19(1)(d)** (freedom of movement), with a correlative duty on urban local bodies.

**Q6 — (c).** China banned **helium** exports (effective 10 July 2026). India imports almost all its helium; ISRO uses it to pressurise cryogenic/liquid fuel tanks and fabs need it for cooling and inert atmospheres.

**Q7 — (b).** ISP is a **monthly volume index of services output** released by **MoSPI/NSO**, base year **2024-25** — the services counterpart to the IIP (health and education excluded for now).

**Q8 — (b).** 1 and 3 correct. The UK zeroed duties on ~99% of Indian tariff lines on day one, and the DCC exemption is five years. Statement 2 is wrong — India cut duties on 90% of tariff lines (64.1% immediately), not all goods.

**Q9 — (b).** Vikram-1 is India's first **privately developed orbital-class** launch vehicle (Skyroot Aerospace, ~350 kg to LEO). The first private Indian rocket to fly was **Vikram-S** (sub-orbital, Nov 2022), so (a) is wrong.

**Q10 — (c).** The **Maharashtra Women Farmers Empowerment Act, 2026** issues a 'Women Farmer Certificate' delinking farmer status from land ownership — a first in India.

**Q11 — (a).** SHANTI = *Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity* — the theme of India's UNSC 2028–29 non-permanent-seat campaign, launched at UN HQ (EAM Jaishankar). India contests the Asia-Pacific seat against Tajikistan.

**Q12 — (b).** NIPU-2026 (Dept. of Fertilizers) targets ~10 MT of new **gas-based** urea capacity via 8–9 plants to cut import dependence. India is a urea *importer* (~30 MT vs ~40 MT demand); urea is *outside* NBS with a statutorily fixed MRP.

**Q13 — (d).** All correct — sacred groves are in-situ conservation, can be notified as Community Reserves under the WLPA (added by the 2002 amendment), and Orans/Kavu/Sarna are regional names (Rajasthan/Kerala/Jharkhand).

**Q14 — (b).** CAMPA funds come chiefly from **Net Present Value (NPV)** and compensatory-afforestation charges levied when forest land is diverted, under the CAF Act, 2016 (90% to States, 10% Centre).

**Q15 — (c).** The **Kigali Amendment (2016)** brought **HFCs** — potent greenhouse gases (though not ozone-depleters) — under the Montreal Protocol for phase-down.

**Q16 — (d).** All correct — the Gangetic dolphin is India's National Aquatic Animal (2009), is in Schedule I of the WPA 1972, and is functionally blind, navigating by echolocation.

**Q17 — (b).** Ex-situ = conservation *outside* the natural habitat: botanical gardens and seed/gene banks. Biosphere reserves and wildlife sanctuaries are in-situ (within the habitat).

**Q18 — (d).** All correct — Ramsar is the wetlands treaty; the Montreux Record lists Ramsar sites whose ecological character is threatened; India regulates wetlands under the Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules, 2017.

**Q19 — (d).** "Urban heat-island intensity" is **not** a planetary boundary. The nine are: climate change, biosphere integrity, land-system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical (N & P) flows, ocean acidification, atmospheric aerosol loading, stratospheric ozone depletion, and novel entities.

**Q20 — (b).** Only two. Asiatic Lion–Gir and Rhino–Kaziranga are correct; the **Snow Leopard** inhabits the high Himalaya/trans-Himalaya, **not** the Sundarbans (which is home to the Royal Bengal Tiger).

**RP1 —** (i) **China's temporary helium export ban** (10 July 2026) — India imports nearly all its helium, so it squeezes ISRO's launch programme, semiconductor fabs and MRI cryogenics, reinforcing the critical-resources/strategic-autonomy theme. (ii) **US Senate bill authorising up to 500% "secondary tariffs" on Russian-oil buyers** — India (a top buyer of discounted Russian crude) faces an energy-security vs strategic-autonomy squeeze, sharpened by the lapse of the US Treasury general licence.

**RP2 —** (i) **PRAGATI** — "Promoting Regenerative Agriculture Through Innovative Growth Initiatives," launched by the **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare** to build ~20,000 agri-entrepreneurs for ~20 lakh smallholders. (ii) **Gramodyam** — launched by **NABARD (45th Foundation Day) with NSDC** to develop 4,000 rural entrepreneurs over three years. *(Do not confuse the two — PRAGATI is regenerative-agriculture-specific; Gramodyam is enterprise-generic.)*
