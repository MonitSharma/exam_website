## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**
1. **(a)** — India welcomed the peace agreement, and the INSTC runs India–Iran–Azerbaijan–Russia; but the 2000 agreement was signed by India, Iran and Russia only (Azerbaijan acceded later), so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — The issue was entirely a fresh issue (up to 27 crore shares) with no OFS, so 1 is wrong; ₹27,500 crore goes to prepay Reliance Jio Infocomm's borrowings, and it overtook Hyundai Motor India's ₹27,870 crore (2024).
3. **(c)** — Sector-based commands (incl. Mumbai and Chennai port sectors) start 1 Nov 2026 and the CISF is the ISPS-Code Recognised Security Organisation for seaports; the CISF is under the Ministry of Home Affairs, not Defence, so 2 is wrong.
4. **(b)** — Councils are now possible in all eight districts and the Ladakh MP is an ex-officio member; this is a statutory route, not a Sixth Schedule extension (which remains the core local demand), so 2 is wrong.
5. **(d)** — A Council has no power to reject a Money Bill; it can only return it with recommendations within 14 days, and the Assembly may accept or reject them. Statements (a) to (c) are correct.
6. **(d)** — All three are correct: FRLD agreed at COP27 and operationalised at COP28, US$ 21.6 m ($20 m for Nepal plus $1.6 m oversight fees) set aside on 22 Oct, and the World Bank is the interim trustee.
7. **(b)** — The Speaker chaired the Forum, and the listed members include Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran and the UAE; the Forum is a parliamentary-diplomacy platform that supports summit decisions and its resolutions are not binding, so 2 is wrong.
8. **(d)** — The Siang is the Arunachal stretch of the Yarlung Tsangpo/Brahmaputra and the 11,200 MW project is NHPC's; it is on the Siang, not the Subansiri, so 3 is wrong. It is also seen as a counter to China's proposed Medog project.
9. **(c)** — ₹10,000 crore up to FY 2039–40, target of about 7.5 lakh TEUs a year (nearly tenfold), with 3% interest subvention, opex assistance and testing/certification support; the other options misstate outlay, capacity or purpose.
10. **(a)** — A Centrally Sponsored Scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 of ₹7,395.55 crore (Centre ₹4,869.48 crore, States ₹2,526.07 crore); being centrally sponsored, it is cost-shared, so 3 is wrong.
11. **(c)** — The cheetah was declared extinct in India in 1952 and Project Cheetah began at Kuno on 17 Sept 2022 with Namibian cheetahs; Kuno is in Madhya Pradesh (Sheopur), not Rajasthan, so 3 is wrong.
12. **(b)** — A PTA covers a negotiated list of tariff lines and is narrower than an FTA/CECA, and Mexico is in the USMCA; the Terms of Reference only launch PTA negotiations and do not conclude any agreement, so 3 is wrong.

**Part B — International Relations**
13. **(b)** — Panchsheel (Agreement on Trade and Intercourse with Tibet, 29 April 1954) comprises mutual respect for sovereignty and territorial integrity, non-aggression, non-interference, equality and mutual benefit, and peaceful coexistence; non-alignment is a separate doctrine.
14. **(a)** — Bandung (1955) and Belgrade (1961) are correct; the NAM's founding leaders were Nehru, Nasser, Tito, Sukarno and Nkrumah, and China was never a NAM member, so 3 is wrong.
15. **(c)** — I.K. Gujral's doctrine (1996–97) rests on non-reciprocity towards smaller neighbours; it is among its five principles, which also stress non-interference and peaceful settlement of disputes.
16. **(d)** — INSTC was agreed by India, Iran and Russia in 2000 (St Petersburg) and Chabahar is on the Gulf of Oman; the corridor runs through Iran (Bandar Abbas/Chabahar), so 3 is wrong.
17. **(b)** — G4 is Brazil, Germany, India, Japan and Uniting for Consensus (led by Italy, Pakistan and others) opposes new permanent seats; the Ezulwini Consensus is the African Union's position, which seeks two permanent seats with veto for Africa.
18. **(a)** — Look East dates to 1991–92 (P.V. Narasimha Rao); Act East was announced by Modi in November 2014 at the East Asia Summit in Nay Pyi Taw, Myanmar.
19. **(c)** — The first SAARC summit was in Dhaka (1985) and the Secretariat is in Kathmandu; Afghanistan joined in 2007 as the eighth member, and Myanmar is only an observer, so 3 is wrong.
20. **(b)** — Both agreements are described correctly; under Shimla the 17 Dec 1971 ceasefire line became the Line of Control, which is not an international boundary, so 3 is wrong.

**Part C — Recall Prompts**
- **R1.** Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (₹10,000 crore, to FY 2039–40), PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (₹7,395.55 crore, 2026-27 to 2030-31), Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme (about ₹1,857 crore, to 2033-34); DA/DR raised by 3 percentage points from 1 July 2026.
- **R2.** NVS-03 is the third second-generation NVS satellite, meant to rebuild NavIC after ageing rubidium clocks and the NVS-02 failure (Jan 2025); per the 25 Oct briefing, of 11 satellites launched 8 are functional but only 3 serve navigation, against 4 needed for a 3D fix.
