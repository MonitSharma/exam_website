## Answers

**Q1 — (c).** All three are correct: Bar practice cut from three years to one, replaced by one year of judicial-academy training plus one year of clerkship, arising from review petitions in the *All India Judges Association* cases.

**Q2 — (b).** India and **Japan** signed the MoA on Maritime Security Cooperation on 20 August 2026; Rajnath Singh and Japan's Defence Minister **Shinjirō Koizumi** signed it in New Delhi.

**Q3 — (c).** CMLRE is an attached office of the **Ministry of Earth Sciences** (not the Fisheries Ministry) — a common trap.

**Q4 — (a).** MPMS is a **₹62,500 crore, five-year (FY 2026–27 to 2030–31)** successor to the PLI for large-scale electronics, notified by **MeitY**; it shifts emphasis toward value addition and design.

**Q5 — (c).** All three: the **5:4** verdict held the 1978 *Bangalore Water Supply* "triple test" governs pending Industrial Disputes Act cases, while Section 2(p) of the **Industrial Relations Code, 2020** is to be read independently.

**Q6 — (d).** All three: UPI completed **ten years**, is run by **NPCI**, and was recognised as the world's largest real-time payments system.

**Q7 — (b).** The **Chennai Declaration** was a **BRICS** declaration on **science, technology and innovation**, adopted under India's 2026 BRICS chairship.

**Q8 — (c).** All three: **Adi Vaani** is the Ministry of Tribal Affairs' AI tribal-language translator; **Santali** is the only tribal language in the Eighth Schedule, added by the **92nd Amendment Act, 2003** (alongside Bodo, Dogri and Maithili).

**Q9 — (c).** All three: the summit is at **Bishkek**, **Kyrgyzstan** chairs the SCO in 2026, and it marks the grouping's **25th anniversary**.

**Q10 — (c).** All three: **Ladakh** enumerates first, Census 2027 is the first statutory Census in independent India with a **caste column**, and it is run by the **Registrar General & Census Commissioner** under the MHA.

**Q11 — (b).** The roundtable operated within the framework of the **International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA)** — an India-headquartered body — its first major bilateral outing with Russia.

**Q12 — (c).** Both correct: **INS Nipun** is the **second Nistar-class Diving Support Vessel**, built indigenously by **Hindustan Shipyard Limited, Visakhapatnam**.

**Q13 — (c).** All three: the Vernacular Press Act (1878) was enacted under **Lytton**, discriminated against the vernacular press, and was repealed by **Ripon in 1882**.

**Q14 — (c).** **Rash Behari Ghosh** was the Moderate-backed President at Surat (1907); Tilak, from the host province, could not preside, and the split followed.

**Q15 — (c).** All three: INC founded **1885**, first session at **Bombay** under **W.C. Bonnerjee**, with **72 delegates**; A.O. Hume was the key organiser.

**Q16 — (c).** Both correctly matched: **Tilak's Indian Home Rule League** (April 1916) and **Annie Besant's Home Rule League** (September 1916).

**Q17 — (c).** All three correctly matched: **Champaran** (indigo/Tinkathia), **Kheda** (revenue remission after crop failure), **Ahmedabad** (mill wage dispute).

**Q18 — (c).** Both correct: the **Lucknow Pact (1916)** was a Congress–Muslim League agreement in which the Congress **accepted separate electorates** for Muslims.

**Q19 — (c).** All three: the **Poona Pact (1932)** between **Gandhi and Ambedkar** replaced the Communal Award's **separate electorates** for the Depressed Classes with **reserved seats within the general electorate**.

**Q20 — (c).** All three: the **Cabinet Mission (1946)** rejected a sovereign Pakistan, proposed a **three-tier structure with grouping of provinces**, and provided for a **Constituent Assembly**.

**R1.** (i) The Supreme Court cut the minimum Bar practice for Civil Judge (Junior Division) from **three years to one year** (plus academy training + clerkship); (ii) the nine-judge Bench in *Jai Bir Singh* held (5:4) that the 1978 *Bangalore Water Supply* "**triple test**" for "**industry**" survives for pending Industrial Disputes Act cases, while the IRC 2020's definition is read afresh.

**R2.** SCO Heads of State summit at **Bishkek** (Kyrgyzstan); the coming **18th BRICS Summit at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi** (12–13 September); and the **25th round of India–China Special Representatives talks** in Beijing, between NSA **Ajit Doval** and Chinese Foreign Minister **Wang Yi**.
