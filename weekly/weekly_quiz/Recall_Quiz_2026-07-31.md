## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** The ETJE is in the **350-kg thrust class**, designed by DRDO's **GTRE** (Bengaluru) and built by **Azad Engineering** (Hyderabad); it's an *expendable* (single-use) engine to power a medium-range anti-ship missile — India's first indigenous jet-engine win, not a reusable aero-engine.

**Q2 — (c).** **Puga Valley** is in **Ladakh**; India's first geothermal wells were commissioned there, tapping one of the country's most promising geothermal fields.

**Q3 — (b).** The Cabinet cleared a harsher amendment to the **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Act**; the NTA simultaneously sacked 47 officials amid the exam-integrity clean-up.

**Q4 — (c).** The second round of India's FTA talks was with **Israel** (20–23 July, New Delhi).

**Q5 — (b).** Chandipura virus (a Rhabdovirus) is spread mainly by **sandflies (Phlebotomus)**; it causes acute encephalitis chiefly in children.

**Q6 — (b).** The Puri temple secured trademarks over **'Jagannath Dham', 'Mahaprasad' and 'Neelachakra'** to protect the identity and prevent commercial misuse.

**Q7 — (b).** **Kusha** is India's first indigenous **long-range surface-to-air missile (SAM)**, developed by DRDO for layered air defence.

**Q8 — (b).** Sarnath (near Varanasi, UP) is where the Buddha delivered his **first sermon — the Dharmachakra Pravartana** ("Turning of the Wheel of Dharma"); it became India's **45th** UNESCO World Heritage property. *(Bodh Gaya = enlightenment; Kushinagar = parinirvana; Lumbini = birth.)*

**Q9 — (b).** Press Note 3 (2026 Series) allowed FDI in **inventory-based e-commerce only for exports**, keeping the domestic restriction intact.

**Q10 — (b).** Trade resumes via **Nathu La (Sikkim)** and **Lipulekh (Pithoragarh, Uttarakhand)** from **1 August 2026**, after a ~six-year closure.

**Q11 — (b).** Per the BIMCO–ICS Seafarer Workforce Report 2026, India is now the world's **2nd-largest supplier of seafarers**.

**Q12 — (b).** The Delhi Lakshmi Yojana pays ₹2,500/month, partly disbursed in **programmable CBDC (e-Rupee)** — an early use of programmable digital currency for a welfare scheme.

**Q13 — (a).** The UNSC has **15 members**; the **5 permanent members** (US, UK, France, Russia, China) hold veto power, and 10 non-permanent members serve two-year terms. India is not a permanent member.

**Q14 — (b).** The **New Development Bank** is headquartered in **Shanghai** (first regional office in Johannesburg).

**Q15 — (d).** The **Quad** comprises **India, USA, Japan and Australia** — all four.

**Q16 — (b).** The **WTO** came into being on **1 January 1995** as the successor to **GATT (1947)** and is headquartered in **Geneva**.

**Q17 — (a).** India became a **full SCO member in 2017** (with Pakistan); the SCO **Secretariat is in Beijing** and its RATS (counter-terror) body is in Tashkent. The SCO was founded in 2001, but India was not a founding member.

**Q18 — (b).** The **BIMSTEC** permanent Secretariat is in **Dhaka, Bangladesh**.

**Q19 — (b).** India is **not a signatory** to the NPT, which recognises only **five nuclear-weapon states** (those that tested before 1 Jan 1967). *(The CTBT — not NPT — deals with a comprehensive test ban.)*

**Q20 — (b).** **I2U2** = **India, Israel, USA and UAE** (the "West Asian Quad").

**R1.** It reopens a formal, sanctioned trade channel after ~six years, signalling a cautious thaw in India–China ties and reviving border-village economies — now aided by the motorable road to Lipulekh (completed 2020).

**R2.** The **ETJE** (350-kg-class expendable turbojet — indigenous jet propulsion for missiles/UAVs) and **Kusha** (India's first indigenous long-range SAM — layered air defence).
