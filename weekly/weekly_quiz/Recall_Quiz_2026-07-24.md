## Part C — Recall Prompts (one-line answers)

**RP1.** Why did China's live-fire naval drill near **Okinotorishima** this week revive the UNCLOS "island vs rock" debate — and what turns on that distinction?

**RP2.** In one line, how does **Semicon 2.0** differ in ambition from the original India Semiconductor Mission (2021)?

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** The Jakkur (RRSL) network uses CERN's open-source **White Rabbit** technology over Ethernet/optical fibre (IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol) to disseminate IST with sub-nanosecond accuracy — part of the "One Nation, One Time" push led by the Legal Metrology Division with CSIR-NPL, ISRO, BSNL and SEBI.

**Q2 — (b).** It was the **first-ever Indian presidential visit to Moldova and North Macedonia**; the Romania leg was the first by an Indian President in over three decades. (Moldova and N. Macedonia are landlocked EU candidates; Romania is an EU member fronting the Black Sea.)

**Q3 — (a).** The SC gave conditional clearance (subject to CEC + Central Zoo Authority + MoEFCC safeguards) to the ~₹1,500-crore **Kukrail Reserve Forest** night safari and zoo in Lucknow, UP — India's first urban night safari, confined to 855.07 ha of the reserve.

**Q4 — (b).** **₹1,27,500 crore** — a big jump from the original India Semiconductor Mission's ₹76,000 crore. Semicon 2.0 covers the full value chain (design, fab, packaging, equipment, materials, R&D, talent).

**Q5 — (b).** QDENGA (TAK-003), from Takeda, is a **live-attenuated tetravalent** vaccine covering all four serotypes and needs **no pre-vaccination screening** — approved by CDSCO for ages 4–60, two doses three months apart.

**Q6 — (c).** The Bill raises SC strength to **38** judges. Parliament fixes the number "by law" under Article 124(1) — a simple-majority statute, not a constitutional amendment.

**Q7 — (c).** **Gujarat** topped NITI Aayog's inaugural Investment Friendliness Index (IFI) 2026 — a cooperative/competitive-federalism tool tied to Viksit Bharat @2047.

**Q8 — (b).** **Vikram-1** achieved India's first successful **private orbital rocket launch** (Skyroot Aerospace) — a milestone for India's private space sector.

**Q9 — (c).** **Ladakh.** Puga Valley (a known geothermal hotspot) saw India's first-ever geothermal wells commissioned this week.

**Q10 — (b).** The **Akash** SAM was exported to **Tajikistan and Turkmenistan**, extending India's defence footprint into Central Asia.

**Q11 — (b).** UDAN (Ude Desh ka Aam Naagrik) makes **regional air travel affordable** and revives under-served/unserved airports; the modified scheme adds ₹28,840 crore over 10 years.

**Q12 — (b).** **Iron ore** was added as the 9th core industry in the rebased Index of Core Industries (base year **2022-23**).

**Q13 — (c).** **LVM3 (GSLV Mk III)** — India's heaviest operational launcher — carries heavy communication satellites to GTO and human-rated for Gaganyaan. PSLV is the reliable workhorse for polar/LEO payloads.

**Q14 — (a).** A cryogenic engine burns **liquid hydrogen (fuel) + liquid oxygen (oxidiser)** at very low temperatures, giving high specific impulse.

**Q15 — (b).** CRISPR-Cas9 is a **genome-editing** tool that makes precise cuts in DNA, enabling gene correction/insertion/deletion.

**Q16 — (c).** A PHWR uses **heavy water (D₂O)** as moderator/coolant and **natural (unenriched) uranium** as fuel — India's first-stage reactor design.

**Q17 — (c).** Stage three uses **thorium-232**, bred into fissile **uranium-233**, exploiting India's large thorium (monazite) reserves. (Stage 1: PHWRs on natural U; Stage 2: fast breeder reactors using Pu-239.)

**Q18 — (a).** **Superposition** lets a qubit hold 0 and 1 states simultaneously; combined with entanglement it underpins quantum computing's parallelism.

**Q19 — (b).** mRNA vaccines deliver **genetic instructions** so the body's own cells produce a viral antigen (e.g., the spike protein), triggering an immune response — no live virus involved.

**Q20 — (b).** Monoclonal antibodies are **identical antibodies from a single cell clone**, engineered to bind one specific antigen — used in therapeutics and diagnostics.

**RP1.** Under **UNCLOS Article 121**, a naturally formed **island** that sustains human habitation/economic life generates a 200-nm EEZ, whereas a **rock** that cannot does not — Japan treats tiny Okinotorishima as an island (claiming an EEZ around it), a status China contests; the classification decides whether the surrounding waters are Japan's EEZ or high seas, and thus the legality of foreign military drills there.

**RP2.** **Semicon 2.0** (₹1,27,500 cr) scales up from the original ₹76,000-cr mission by funding the **entire semiconductor value chain** — design, fabrication, advanced packaging, equipment, materials, R&D and talent — rather than mainly attracting fabs, aiming for a self-reliant domestic ecosystem.
