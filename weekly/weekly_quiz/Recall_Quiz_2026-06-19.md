## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs (13–19 June 2026)**

1. **C** — All three correct. Modi's visit was the first-ever by an Indian PM to Slovakia; ties were raised to a "Comprehensive Partnership," and he received the Order of the White Double Cross (1st Class), Slovakia's highest civilian honour.
2. **C** — All three correct. AD-1/AD-2 are Phase-II BMD interceptors; NASM-MR is a naval anti-ship (medium-range) missile; all the tests were from ITR Chandipur, Odisha (10–11 June 2026).
3. **A** — India hit 20% average ethanol blending (E20) in 2025, roughly five years ahead of the original 2030 target (advanced under the National Policy on Biofuels, 2018, as amended in 2022).
4. **C** — 7 of 9. CSE's report flagged 7 of 9 planetary boundaries as breached (safe: stratospheric ozone and atmospheric aerosol loading); the framework originates from the Stockholm Resilience Centre.
5. **D** — 1, 2 and 3. The Court created the "Loss of Domestic Care" head, fixed ₹30,000/month notional income, and relied on the NSO Time Use Survey, 2019 (unpaid domestic work ≈ 15–17% of GDP).
6. **A** — 1 and 2 only. GPI 2026 is published by the IEP; Iceland is the most peaceful country; India is ranked ~127th of 163 — well outside the top 50, so statement 3 is wrong.
7. **C** — Normal onset over Kerala is ~1 June; the 2026 onset on 4 June was about 3 days later than normal (a minor delay, not a weak-season signal).
8. **B** — Section 69A of the IT Act, 2000 empowers the Central Government to block public access to online content (grounds: sovereignty/integrity, security, public order, etc.); the order was issued by MeitY on NTA's recommendation.
9. **A** — 1 and 2 only. The new WPI series uses base 2022-23 and the new PPI covers services (which WPI excludes). Statement 3 is wrong: WPI is compiled by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT — not the NSO (which compiles the CPI).
10. **B** — UNICEF's Children's Climate Risk Report 2026 found ~392 million Indian children (~92%) exposed to extreme heat, placing India among the most climate-vulnerable countries in the region.
11. **D** — 1, 2 and 3. SVAMITVA uses drone surveys of *abadi* land, is run by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj (technical agency: Survey of India), and its property cards help unlock institutional credit.
12. **C** — All four GI-tagged products (Bhagaiya silk, Kuchai silk, Munda jewellery, bamboo craft) are from Jharkhand; the tags were NABARD-facilitated.

**Part B — Economy**

13. **A** — 1 and 2 only. CRR is held as reserves with the RBI; SLR can be held by the bank itself as cash, gold or approved securities. Statement 3 is wrong: a higher CRR *reduces* banks' lending capacity.
14. **B** — The SDF rate is the floor (below the repo), and the MSF rate is the ceiling (above the repo); the corridor is bounded by SDF (floor) and MSF (ceiling), with the repo rate in between.
15. **A** — Primary deficit = Fiscal deficit − Interest payments (it strips out interest on past borrowings to show the current year's "fresh" borrowing need).
16. **A** — GNP = GDP + Net Factor Income from Abroad; the only conceptual difference is the inclusion of net income earned by residents abroad (minus that earned by foreigners domestically).
17. **A** — 1 and 2 only. M1 (narrow money) = currency with the public + demand deposits + 'other' deposits with the RBI. Time/term deposits fall under M3 (broad money), so statement 3 is wrong.
18. **C** — Corporation tax is a direct tax (levied on income/profits). GST, customs and excise are indirect taxes levied on goods/services.
19. **A** — 1 and 2 only. The current account records merchandise trade, services, primary income (interest/dividends) and transfers. FDI is a financial/capital-account flow, so statement 3 is wrong.
20. **C** — The FRBM Act was enacted in 2003 to institutionalise fiscal discipline and set deficit-reduction targets for the Union government.

**Part C — Recall Prompts**

**R1.** India practises issue-based "multi-alignment": it engages Western-led platforms (G7 invitee, QUAD) for technology, capital and trade *while simultaneously* leading non-Western ones (chairing BRICS 2026, plus SCO) as a "voice of the Global South" — gaining influence and bargaining leverage across blocs without binding itself to any, the essence of strategic autonomy.

**R2.** The Supreme Court's "Loss of Domestic Care" head recognised unpaid domestic work as having independent economic value (notional income ₹30,000/month, a separate head in motor-accident compensation), relying on the NSO's Time Use Survey, 2019, which valued women's unpaid domestic work at roughly 15–17% of GDP.
