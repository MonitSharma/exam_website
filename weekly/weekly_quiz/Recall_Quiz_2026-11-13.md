## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**
1. **(d)** — The clock runs from detention, only travel time to the Magistrate is excludable, and the Magistrate must record the actual detention time and refuse remand if the limit was breached.
2. **(c)** — The NBT was held to be "State" through deep and pervasive control under the *Ajay Hasia*/*Pradeep Kumar Biswas* tests; registration as a society does not take a body out of Art. 12, so 2 is wrong.
3. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; registered Tibetans (entered 1959–30 May 2003) stay exempt but must get a registration certificate, so 3 is wrong.
4. **(c)** — Association gives funding access and consortium-leading rights under a ~€93.5 bn programme; it does not confer EU single-market membership.
5. **(b)** — e-3W was about 63% of the three-wheeler segment in October 2026 (e-2W ~9.7% share; overall penetration ~11.5%).
6. **(c)** — The Great Rann of Kutch is the largest reserve but is not in the World Network (others not in it: Manas, Dibru-Saikhowa, Dehang-Dibang, Seshachalam); the other three are.
7. **(d)** — The *Natyashastra* and *Bhagavad Gita* were inscribed on the International register in April 2025, not in November 2026; the other statements are correct.
8. **(a)** — MINURSO (1991) lapsed after the Russian veto of the US draft; the Polisario Front is Algeria-backed, not Morocco-backed, so 3 is wrong.
9. **(c)** — Wilful defaulters, fraudulent borrowers and fugitive economic offenders cannot settle; pending 2018-regime matters could be refiled within 90 days.
10. **(b)** — Halo orbit around L1 (~1.5 million km) and VELC as a coronagraph are correct; Aditya-L1 launched on PSLV-C57 on 2 Sep 2023, so 3 is wrong.
11. **(d)** — India is an EAS participant but not an ASEAN member; the Philippines, Vietnam and Indonesia are ASEAN members.
12. **(a)** — NBS fixes per-kg nutrient subsidies for P&K fertilisers for 1 Oct 2026–31 Mar 2027; urea is outside NBS, so 2 is wrong.

**Part B — Geography**
13. **(a)** — Humboldt is cold and Kuroshio is warm; the Benguela is a cold current.
14. **(a)** — The Himadri holds the highest peaks and the Duns lie between the Lesser Himalaya and Shiwaliks; the Shiwaliks are the southernmost, outermost range.
15. **(d)** — The 82°30′E meridian passes through Uttar Pradesh (near Mirzapur), and also through MP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha and Andhra Pradesh.
16. **(b)** — The Ten Degree Channel separates Little Andaman from the Car Nicobar group; the Eight Degree Channel separates Minicoy from the Maldives, the Nine Degree Channel separates Minicoy from the rest of Lakshadweep, and the Palk Strait lies between India and Sri Lanka.
17. **(d)** — Pulicat is a lagoon on the Andhra Pradesh–Tamil Nadu coast, not Odisha (Chilika is Odisha's lagoon).
18. **(c)** — Warm SST and weak Coriolis near the equator are correct; strong vertical wind shear disrupts and weakens cyclones.
19. **(d)** — The Son is a right-bank (south) tributary; Ghaghara, Gandak and Kosi join from the left, with Himalayan/Nepal sources.
20. **(b)** — Talcher is in Odisha; Korba is in Chhattisgarh, Singrauli is in MP/Uttar Pradesh and Raniganj is in West Bengal.

**Part C — Recall Prompts**
- **R1.** From the moment of detention (not formal arrest); only the travel time to the Magistrate is excluded.
- **R2.** The chance of holding 1.5°C with no or limited overshoot has been lost (aim is to limit overshoot and return below 1.5°C by 2100); India is one of six G20 members likely to meet its unconditional 2030 targets (with China, Indonesia, Russia, South Africa and Türkiye).
