## Answers

**Q1 — (b) Only two.** Statements 1 and 2 are correct (Sunda Arc, Indo-Australian Plate subducting beneath the Sunda Plate; the 2018 tsunami came from a flank collapse, not an earthquake). Statement 3 is wrong — Anak Krakatau lies in the **Sunda Strait** (between Java and Sumatra), not the Strait of Malacca.

**Q2 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** The SCE-200 burns ISROSENE (refined kerosene) with liquid oxygen, uses oxidiser-rich staged combustion, and is meant to replace the LVM3's L110 core and power the NGLV.

**Q3 — (b) Puga Valley, eastern Ladakh.** India's first two geothermal wells were commissioned there in July 2026; the National Policy on Geothermal Energy (MNRE) was notified 15 September 2025.

**Q4 — (b).** UNEP says 1.5 °C will likely be crossed within a few years but a return is possible under an "overshoot, peak and decline" pathway; peak warming (not end-of-century) drives the damage, and CDR can return the world below 1.5 °C only if peak stays well below 2 °C.

**Q5 — (b).** Only the last four digits of the mobile number may be shown; VPAs and account numbers are also masked. It was issued as an **NPCI network circular**, not a statute.

**Q6 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct: a directly elected legislative body for UT Ladakh, a new special provision in Part XXI, and the current position that the President legislates for Ladakh under Article 240.

**Q7 — (b).** EOS-05 is India's first imaging satellite in a geosynchronous orbit (~36,000 km), giving continuous "staring" coverage of the subcontinent, unlike the usual polar/sun-synchronous remote-sensing satellites.

**Q8 — (c).** An AoN is the DAC's in-principle approval that *begins* procurement — not the contract. The DAC is chaired by the **Defence Minister** (not the CDS), and "Buy (Indian-IDDM)" is the topmost priority category under DAP 2020.

**Q9 — (b).** Boma–Badingilo (South Sudan) — its first UNESCO World Heritage Site, inscribed via emergency procedure and simultaneously placed on the List of World Heritage in Danger; it hosts the Great Nile Migration.

**Q10 — (b).** ORV *Sagar Manthan* — India's first domestically built advanced ocean research vessel, built by GRSE (Kolkata) for NCPOR under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

**Q11 — (b).** The Bishkek Declaration adopted **English** as a working language of the SCO, alongside Russian and Chinese.

**Q12 — (c).** PM-KUSUM 2.0 is expected to carry a dedicated **agri-photovoltaics** component of about 10 GW — solar generation over cultivated cropland.

**Q13 — (a) 1 and 3 only.** The Ring of Fire is a horseshoe belt of intense seismic/volcanic activity round the Pacific, along **convergent (subduction)** boundaries — not divergent — so statement 2 is wrong. The Andes and Cascades lie along it.

**Q14 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three pairings are correct.

**Q15 — (c) Odisha.** The Tropic of Cancer passes through eight states — Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, West Bengal, Tripura and Mizoram — but **not Odisha**.

**Q16 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct: Kerala onset ~1 June, the "burst," and the retreating (northeast) monsoon feeding the Coromandel Coast in Oct–Dec.

**Q17 — (c) Narmada.** The Narmada (like the Tapi) flows west into the Arabian Sea through a rift valley; Mahanadi, Krishna and Godavari are east-flowing to the Bay of Bengal.

**Q18 — (c) All three.** Nathu La (Sikkim), Zoji La (J&K/Ladakh) and Bomdila (Arunachal Pradesh) are all correctly matched.

**Q19 — (a).** El Niño is the abnormal warming of the central/eastern equatorial Pacific; it is generally associated with weaker/deficient Indian monsoon rainfall.

**Q20 — (b).** The Deccan Trap is an extensive basaltic-lava plateau formed by fissure (flood) volcanism, weathering into the black regur soil of the region.

**RP1.** UNEP shifts the framing from *"avoid crossing 1.5 °C"* to *"manage the overshoot"* — how high the peak goes, how long it lasts, and what becomes irreversible before warming is brought back down.

**RP2.** It offers Ladakh an elected legislature through a bespoke Part XXI special provision (modelled on Articles 371A/371G) — giving legislative autonomy without granting full statehood and without invoking the Sixth Schedule's autonomous councils.
