## Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs**
1. **(a)** — NVS-03 adds L1 to L5 and S-band, and the original constellation is 3 GEO + 4 inclined geosynchronous; NavIC is regional, not global, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — Import-linked GST (+42.2%) outgrew domestic (+15.8%) and the GST Council is under Art 279A; its decisions need three-fourths of weighted votes, not a simple majority, so 3 is wrong.
3. **(c)** — The notification under Para 19 of DPCO 2013 caps trade margin at 30% of MRP on non-scheduled drugs (outside NLEM ceilings); it does not cap ex-factory prices and needs no parliamentary approval.
4. **(d)** — Nashik is on the Godavari, Ujjain's Kumbh is on the Kshipra, and the Kumbh Mela was inscribed on UNESCO's ICH Representative List in 2017.
5. **(c)** — KMGBF Target 3 is 30x30 via protected areas and OECMs, and the guidelines were launched at CBD COP17; OECMs are by definition outside protected areas, so 2 is wrong.
6. **(b)** — Reserves are FCA + gold + SDRs + IMF reserve position and data come on Fridays; a gold price fall lowers reserves through the valuation effect without any RBI sale, so 1 is wrong.
7. **(d)** — The Board is statutory, created under the Code on Social Security, 2020, and chaired by the Labour Minister; it is not a constitutional body.
8. **(a)** — BIMSTEC has seven members including Bhutan and Nepal, and Sri Vijaya Puram is Port Blair (ANC headquarters); the Secretariat is in Dhaka, so 2 is wrong.
9. **(c)** — ISA was launched by India and France at COP21 and India is undecided on "35 by 2035"; ISA's headquarters is in Gurugram, so 1 is wrong.
10. **(d)** — The 1996 treaty (Deve Gowda–Hasina, 30 years) expires in December 2026 and the Joint Rivers Commission is the mechanism; Teesta sharing is a separate unsigned issue, so 2 is wrong.
11. **(b)** — Census is Entry 69 of the Union List and Aadhaar is optional and not proof of citizenship; the Census is run by the Registrar General under the MHA, not the ECI, so 3 is wrong.
12. **(c)** — PAP is for foreigners in notified border areas and Art 371A covers Nagaland; the Inner Line Permit is for Indian citizens (Bengal Eastern Frontier Regulation, 1873), so 2 is wrong.

**Part B — Ancient & Medieval History & Art/Culture**
13. **(c)** — Megasthenes' *Indica* and the saptanga theory are correct; the *Arthashastra* is in Sanskrit, so 3 is wrong.
14. **(c)** — Gandhara shows Greco-Roman influence and both schools flourished under the Kushanas; Mathura used spotted red sandstone, not grey-blue schist, so 1 is wrong.
15. **(c)** — The Shore Temple was built under the Pallava king Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha); the monolithic rathas belong to the earlier Mamalla phase.
16. **(c)** — Uttaramerur records lot-based elections under Parantaka I and the Sabha/Ur distinction is correct; the Nagaram was the assembly of merchants, so 3 is wrong.
17. **(b)** — Kandariya Mahadeva at Khajuraho was built by the Chandellas, not the Paramaras; the other three pairs are correct.
18. **(a)** — Alauddin's market control under the Shahna-i-Mandi and Muhammad bin Tughlaq's token currency are correct; the iqta system was organised earlier, by Iltutmish, so 3 is wrong.
19. **(d)** — Zat and sawar ranks and the Zabt/Todar Mal revenue system are correct; mansabs were not hereditary, so 3 is wrong.
20. **(b)** — Bahauddin Zakariya of Multan belonged to the Suhrawardi order; Chishti saints include Moinuddin and Nizamuddin Auliya, while Qadiri and Naqshbandi are different orders.

**Part C — Recall Prompts**
- **R1.** Grid-scale energy-storage VGF (92 GWh, ₹14,500 crore); critical-mineral processing capex scheme (₹2,814 crore, 24 minerals); VISTAR statistical-system revamp (₹4,371.10 crore).
- **R2.** India says it has not decided whether to back the voluntary "35 by 2035" electrification target, invoking common but differentiated responsibilities (CBDR-RC) and national circumstances while pressing for predictable climate finance and technology transfer.
