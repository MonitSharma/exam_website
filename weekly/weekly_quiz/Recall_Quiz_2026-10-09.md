## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** Repo went up 25 bps to 5.50% (first hike since Feb 2023) with SDF 5.25% and MSF 5.75%. The vote was unanimous, not 4–2.
**Q2 — (c).** IceCube sits in the South Pole ice and detects light from charged secondary particles. Neutrinos are electrically neutral, which is why they point back to their sources.
**Q3 — (d).** The Soai reaction (1995) is an asymmetric autocatalytic reaction that amplifies a tiny imbalance between mirror-image molecules; Kagan's non-linear effects (1980s) were the related earlier work.
**Q4 — (a).** BEE implements CAFE under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, and the period is FY28–FY32. BEVs get 3x; strong hybrids get 1.6x.
**Q5 — (c).** TEPA has been in force since 1 Oct 2025 and Liechtenstein is an EFTA member (with Iceland, Norway, Switzerland). Austria is in the EU, not EFTA.
**Q6 — (b).** Two tagged captive-bred males were released near Jaisalmer on 4 Oct 2026 by the WII with the Rajasthan Forest Department. The NTCA deals with tigers.
**Q7 — (c).** Central India was normal at about 97%; the deficits were in the East/North-East (74%) and the South Peninsula (76%).
**Q8 — (d).** Art 44 is a non-justiciable DPSP, personal law is Concurrent List Entry 5 (hence Art 254(2) assent), and Gujarat's law excludes Scheduled Tribes.
**Q9 — (b).** ITLA has an eight-domain remit and a ₹500 crore appraisal threshold. The SME Growth Fund is a ₹10,000 crore equity fund for growth-stage SMEs, not a guarantee scheme.
**Q10 — (a).** Adopted 20 May 2025; under Article 12 it opens for signature only after the PABS annex is adopted, so it is not in force.
**Q11 — (c).** NW-2 is the Brahmaputra, Sadiya–Dhubri (NW-1 is the Ganga, Prayagraj–Haldia).
**Q12 — (d).** Disaster preparedness (38 works) is a new category, not a dropped one.
**Q13 — (c).** The MPC has six members: three from the RBI (Governor, a Deputy Governor, another officer) and three external members appointed by the Centre. The Governor has a casting vote, and the target (4% ±2%) is notified every five years.
**Q14 — (a).** 5.50 − 0.25 = 5.25% (SDF) and 5.50 + 0.25 = 5.75% (MSF), which is also the Bank Rate.
**Q15 — (d).** CRR is held in cash with the RBI without interest; SLR is held by banks themselves in liquid assets (cash, gold, approved securities), not with the RBI.
**Q16 — (b).** Contingency Fund advances are made by the executive and approved by Parliament afterwards, not before.
**Q17 — (b).** GDP at market prices = GVA at basic prices + taxes on products − subsidies on products.
**Q18 — (a).** WPI covers goods only. CPI-IW comes from the Labour Bureau; CPI (Combined) from the NSO.
**Q19 — (c).** Remittances (secondary income) and investment income (primary income) are current account items. FDI and ECBs are financial account items.
**Q20 — (d).** Alcohol for human consumption is outside GST (states tax it). The other three statements are correct.

**R1 (model answer).** It hiked the repo rate 25 bps to 5.50% and moved to a "calibrated tightening" stance; drivers: food and fuel inflation with second-round risks, the deficient monsoon (87% of LPA), crude near US$100/bbl, and elevated global yields.
**R2 (model answer).** Battery electric and range-extended EVs get 3x; surplus credits can be sold to other manufacturers or to the BEE (buy-back price ₹2,500 per g CO₂/km in FY28 rising to ₹4,500 in FY32), carried forward only within a compliance block.
