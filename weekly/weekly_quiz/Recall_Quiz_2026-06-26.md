## Part D — Answers

**Part A — Current Affairs (19–25 June 2026)**

1. **A** — ₹2.87 lakh crore is the highest-ever transfer, decided via the Economic Capital Framework (Bimal Jalan Committee). It is **non-tax revenue outside the divisible pool**, so States get no automatic share (statement 3 wrong).
2. **C** — The NSC is a **non-statutory, non-constitutional advisory body** set up by an executive resolution (1999) — a frequent Prelims trap.
3. **B** — RELOS is an India–Russia logistics-sharing pact, of the LEMOA/ACSA family; it is **non-basing** (no permanent foreign bases), so statement 2 is wrong.
4. **D** — All three correct: NOAA's El Niño Advisory (June 2026), monsoon suppression over India, and the Southern Oscillation as the east–west tropical-Pacific pressure see-saw.
5. **A** — India–UK CETA enters into force **15 July 2026**, alongside the Double Contribution Convention (DCC); the UK eliminates duties on ~99% of Indian tariff lines.
6. **D** — All three correct: GI Act, 1999; 10-year renewable validity; India's first GI was **Darjeeling Tea (2004)**.
7. **A** — MRM and GRM are run by the **Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)** under the MHA; MRM lets victims reclaim up to ₹50,000.
8. **D** — All three correct: UNCTAD ranks India No.1 (35.4% share, 2025); hub at **Alang, Gujarat**; **Recycling of Ships Act, 2019** gives effect to the Hong Kong Convention.
9. **D** — All three correct: Gadgil (2011) = entire Ghats as ESA (~1,29,037 sq km); Kasturirangan (2013) = ~37% (~60,000 sq km); ESAs notified under the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
10. **D** — All three correct: India won the FATF Vice-Presidency for the **first time** (Vivek Aggarwal, 2026–27 term); FATF is **Paris**-based; India joined in **2010**.
11. **A** — A physical **fibre-optic cable** carries commands/video by light pulses, emitting almost no RF → immune to jamming/spoofing (limitation: cable caps range and can break).
12. **A** — Rakhigarhi (Haryana) is among the **largest Harappan (Indus–Saraswati)** sites; remains went to AnSI, Kolkata, for ancient-DNA study.

**Part B — Modern History**

13. **A** — The major outbreak began at **Meerut** on 10 May 1857. (Mangal Pandey's earlier individual revolt was at Barrackpore, 29 March 1857.)
14. **D** — All correct: Partitioned in 1905 under **Curzon**, sparking the **Swadeshi** movement; **annulled in 1911**.
15. **A** — First INC session: **Bombay, 1885**, presided over by **W.C. Bonnerjee** (founded with A.O. Hume's help).
16. **A** — Champaran (1917) → Kheda (1918) → Dandi (1930) → Quit India (1942) = **2 – 1 – 3 – 4**.
17. **A** — **Separate electorates** for Muslims were first introduced by the **Indian Councils Act, 1909 (Morley–Minto Reforms)**.
18. **A** — The **Arya Samaj** was founded in 1875 by **Swami Dayananda Saraswati** ("Back to the Vedas"; *Satyarth Prakash*).
19. **A** — The **Purna Swaraj** resolution was adopted at the **Lahore Session, 1929** (presided over by **Jawaharlal Nehru**); 26 Jan 1930 was observed as Independence Day.
20. **A** — The **Ghadar Party** was founded in 1913 at **San Francisco** (Sohan Singh Bhakna, president; Lala Har Dayal, secretary).

**Part C — Recall Prompts**

**R1.** **Gadgil Committee (WGEEP, 2011)** — entire Western Ghats (~100%) as ESA; **Kasturirangan Committee (HLWG, 2013)** — about **37%** (~60,000 sq km) as ESA.
**R2.** India–UK CETA enters into force **15 July 2026**; the India–US BTA talks are racing the **24 July 2026** expiry of the US's temporary 10% tariff window.
