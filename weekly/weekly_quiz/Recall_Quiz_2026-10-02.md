## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct (s.123(3); trial in the High Court, appeal under s.116A). Statement 3 is wrong: *Abhiram Singh* read "his religion" to cover the candidate, the agent and the voter.

**Q2 — (c).** Signed 1960 at Karachi with the World Bank as signatory, and the dispute ladder is as stated. The Western rivers go to Pakistan, with India limited to non-consumptive uses such as run-of-river hydro.

**Q3 — (a).** Enters into force 20 October 2026, and India excluded dairy. The US$20 bn/15-year pledge is New Zealand's; the US$100 bn/15-year pledge belongs to the EFTA TEPA. It was signed on 27 April 2026.

**Q4 — (c).** MEE is run by NTCA with WII on the IUCN-WCPA framework. Tiger numbers come from the All-India Tiger Estimation (every four years), which is a different exercise.

**Q5 — (b).** TDB is a statutory body under DST, and the funding was structured via optionally convertible debentures. Semi-cryogenic engines burn refined kerosene with liquid oxygen, not liquid hydrogen.

**Q6 — (d).** The cut came mainly from the ₹1,14,286 crore switch of maturing G-secs into longer-dated papers. It is a debt-management move; the debt stock does not fall.

**Q7 — (a).** PRAMAAN is the Programme for Recognition and Accreditation of sustainable Management practices for Agroforestry And Natural forestry resources, under the voluntary IFWCS (launched December 2023), operated by IIFM Bhopal.

**Q8 — (d).** All three are correct: strength raised to 38 by the 2026 Amendment Act, the Collegium is the CJI plus four senior-most judges, and the three names are sitting High Court Chief Justices.

**Q9 — (b).** Custodial confession alone cannot ground the subjective satisfaction for preventive detention; cogent material on a real apprehension is needed. The Court quashed the order and imposed ₹10 lakh costs.

**Q10 — (c).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. GEC-III funds intra-state transmission (InSTS), not inter-state lines.

**Q11 — (a).** Pakistan took the chair on 1 September 2026, and India's delegation was led by MEA Additional Secretary Alok Dimri. The Council of National Coordinators is not a ministerial body.

**Q12 — (d).** The Court refused both a total ban and unrestricted use. It balanced Article 25 against Article 21 and deferred the barium decision to 15 October 2026.

**Q13 — (c).** A Governor can reserve a Bill, and must do so if it derogates from the High Court's powers. The Governor can return only a non-Money Bill.

**Q14 — (a).** Under Article 201 the President is not bound to assent even if the Bill is re-passed. This differs from Article 111, where assent to a re-passed Bill is compulsory.

**Q15 — (d).** Only statement 1 is correct (Article 22(4)). The 44th Amendment change has never been brought into force. Article 22(1)–(2) does not apply to preventive detention.

**Q16 — (d).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Other Election Commissioners can be removed only on the CEC's recommendation (Article 324(5) proviso).

**Q17 — (b).** Article 249 needs a Rajya Sabha resolution backed by two-thirds of members present and voting. It is valid for one year, renewable.

**Q18 — (c).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. An independent member who joins a party after election is disqualified.

**Q19 — (d).** *Kesavananda Bharati* (1973), by 7:6, laid down the basic-structure doctrine. Parliament can amend any provision but cannot alter the basic structure.

**Q20 — (b).** Article 143 opinions are advisory, not binding. The Court may decline to answer, as it did in the 1993 Ayodhya reference. Questions of both law and fact can be referred.

**R1 (model answer).** India defended keeping the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance until Pakistan credibly ends cross-border terrorism, and co-founded, with Liberia and others, a Group of Friends on the Safety and Security of Shipping and Seafarers.

**R2 (model answer).** A confession made in police custody cannot alone ground a detention order's satisfaction; the State must show cogent material of a real apprehension of future prejudicial activity (quashed with ₹10 lakh costs).
