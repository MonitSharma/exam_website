---

## General Instructions

1. There are EIGHT questions divided in TWO sections.
2. Candidate has to attempt FIVE questions in all.
3. Questions No. 1 and 5 are COMPULSORY and out of the remaining, THREE are to be attempted choosing at least ONE from each Section.
4. The number of marks carried by a question/part is indicated against it.
5. Answers must be written in the medium authorised in the Admission Certificate. No marks will be given for the answers written in a medium other than the authorised one.
6. Word limit in questions, wherever specified, should be adhered to.
7. Neat diagrams are to be drawn wherever necessary. Credit will be given for well-labelled diagrams.
8. Unless stated otherwise, all symbols carry their usual meanings.
