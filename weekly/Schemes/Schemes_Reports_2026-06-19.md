## SCHEMES & PROGRAMMES

### 1. PM-KISAN — 23rd Installment Released
**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Agriculture, income support, DBT; GS2 — Government welfare schemes

- **Ministry / nodal agency:** **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare** (Dept. of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare).
- **Launched:** February 2019 (income support effective Dec 2018). **23rd installment scheduled for release 20 June 2026.**
- **Type:** **Central Sector Scheme** — 100% Union funding.
- **Objective:** Assured income support to land-holding farmer families to supplement input costs and household needs.
- **Key features:**
  - **₹6,000 / year** in **three equal ₹2,000 installments** via **Aadhaar-linked DBT**.
  - 23rd installment: **₹18,880 crore** to be transferred to **~9.44 crore farmers** (₹2,000 each).
  - **e-KYC, land-seeding and Aadhaar-bank linkage** mandatory to receive the payout.
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** Land-holding farmer families nationwide (subject to exclusion criteria — institutional landholders, income-tax payers, etc.).
- **Significance:** World's largest DBT income-support programme; recurring fiscal anchor of farmer-welfare messaging.
- **Criticism / issues:** Excludes **tenant farmers and landless labourers**; flat ₹6,000 unindexed to inflation since 2019; periodic exclusion errors from e-KYC/land-record mismatches.

### 2. Prime Minister Research Chair (PMRC) Scheme 2026
**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Science & Technology, R&D, innovation ecosystem; GS2 — Education / institutions

- **Ministry / nodal agency:** **Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education** (PMRC Portal — pmrc.education.gov.in).
- **Launched:** 2026; **applications open 1 June – 15 July 2026** (notably in the news this fortnight).
- **Type:** Central Sector Scheme.
- **Objective:** Attract accomplished **Indian-origin researchers/scientists/technologists** from globally reputed institutions back to premier Indian HEIs, national labs and research centres.
- **Key features:**
  - Supports work in **13 national-priority thematic areas** — AI, quantum computing, semiconductors, cybersecurity, healthcare, biotechnology, climate change, advanced materials, defence, communications, manufacturing, agriculture, blue economy and atomic energy.
  - Three pillars: **Lead Institutions, Host Institutions, PMRC Fellows.** **Seven Lead Centres** — IIT Delhi, IIT Bombay, IIT Madras, IIT Kanpur, IIT Hyderabad, IIT (ISM) Dhanbad and **IISc Bengaluru.**
  - Three fellowship tiers — **Young Research Fellow** (<5 yrs post-PhD), **Senior Fellow** (5–10 yrs), **Research Chair** (10+ yrs) — with estimated benefits up to **₹4 cr / ₹6.5 cr / ₹14 cr** respectively.
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** Indian-origin researchers abroad + the host premier institutions; aligned to **Viksit Bharat 2047** research goals.
- **Significance:** A targeted "reverse brain-drain" instrument linking diaspora talent to national-security and competitiveness priorities.
- **Criticism / issues:** Concentrating chairs in a handful of IITs/IISc may widen the institutional divide; success depends on lab infrastructure and retention beyond the fellowship term.

### 3. PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana — crosses 40 lakh solarised homes
**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Energy security, renewables, climate commitments

- **Ministry / nodal agency:** **Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE).**
- **Launched:** **13 February 2024.** Milestone of **~40 lakh households solarised** reached by 2026; rooftop-solar tenders (e.g., large Bihar projects) in the news mid-June 2026.
- **Type:** Central Sector Scheme.
- **Objective:** Provide **free electricity to 1 crore residential households** via subsidised rooftop solar.
- **Key features:**
  - Outlay **₹75,021 crore**; target **1 crore homes by FY 2026-27.**
  - **Central Financial Assistance / subsidy** on rooftop solar capacity; free power up to a defined slab for participating households.
  - Progress trajectory: 10 lakh (Mar 2025) → 20 lakh (Oct 2025) → **40 lakh (2026).**
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** Residential rooftop-solar adopters nationwide.
- **Significance:** India's largest residential-solar push; supports the **500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030** goal and household energy bills.
- **Criticism / issues:** Uptake still well short of the 1-crore target with limited runway; upfront cost, financing access, DISCOM net-metering delays and rooftop suitability remain bottlenecks.

### 4. India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0
**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Science & Technology, manufacturing, economy; GS2 — Industrial policy / supply-chain resilience

- **Ministry / nodal agency:** **Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY)** — India Semiconductor Mission.
- **Launched:** Announced in **Union Budget 2026-27**; expansion being finalised. In the news mid-June 2026 as the **first "Made in India" chips began hitting the market this month.**
- **Type:** Central Sector (incentive/PLI-style support to fabrication, ATMP/OSAT, design).
- **Objective:** Move India from a chip *consumer* to a manufacturing/design *anchor* — deepening fabrication, packaging, equipment, materials and IP.
- **Key features:**
  - Builds on ISM 1.0; **scope widened** beyond fabs to **semiconductor equipment, raw materials and supply-chain resilience.**
  - Outlay being scaled up — **initial allocation ~₹1,000 cr**, with proposals to raise it substantially (reports cite figures up to **₹40,000 cr and a longer ~12-year horizon**; *final figures not yet notified — treat as provisional*).
  - Ambition toward **advanced nodes (2nm) and AI hardware**; first commercial Made-in-India chips rolling out in 2026.
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** Domestic fabs, OSAT/ATMP units, design startups, electronics manufacturers.
- **Significance:** Strategic-autonomy play in a geopolitically contested sector; jobs and import-substitution upside.
- **Criticism / issues:** High capital intensity and long gestation; dependence on foreign technology partners, talent and water/power; exact ISM 2.0 design and outlay still under wraps.

### 5. Panchayati Raj at 12 Years — Decentralisation Review (RGSA · SVAMITVA · e-GramSwaraj)
**Syllabus tag:** GS2 — Devolution to local governments, 73rd Amendment, Finance Commissions, e-governance

- **Ministry / nodal agency:** **Ministry of Panchayati Raj** (12-year review, **15 June 2026**, "Building Viksit Bharat from the Ground Up").
- **Constitutional frame:** **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992** — constitutional status to PRIs, three-tier system, **Eleventh Schedule (29 subjects)**, Article 243G (devolution of *funds, functions, functionaries*), Article 243-I (State Finance Commission every 5 yrs), **PESA Act, 1996** for Scheduled areas.
- **Type:** Bundle of Centrally Sponsored / Central Sector programmes for local self-government.
- **Objective:** Strengthen PRIs as effective units of self-government through capacity, digital governance, fiscal devolution and inclusion.
- **Key features / headline data:**
  - **RGSA (Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan):** **4.10 crore** elected reps & functionaries trained; **33.55 lakh women** reps trained (FY23–FY26).
  - **SVAMITVA (2020):** drone survey of inhabited land — **3.18 crore property cards** across 1.92 lakh villages (with Survey of India).
  - **e-GramSwaraj–PFMS:** **₹3.16 lakh crore** in online Panchayat transactions; **Meri Panchayat App** 1 cr+ downloads.
  - **Fiscal devolution:** 15th FC released **₹2.82 lakh cr (94.98%)** to Rural Local Bodies; **16th FC recommends ₹4.35 lakh cr (~84% jump).**
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** ~2.59 lakh Gram Panchayats and their populations.
- **Significance:** SVAMITVA converts informal occupancy into bankable titles; 16th FC jump signals genuine fiscal deepening of the third tier.
- **Criticism / issues:** Devolution of the "3 Fs" remains **uneven across States**; grants largely tied/conditional with weak Own Source Revenue; functionary vacancies and irregular State Finance Commissions blur accountability.

## REPORTS & INDICES

### A. New WPI & Producer Price Index (PPI) Series — Base Year 2022-23
**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Indian economy: inflation, price indices, government statistics

- **Publishing body:** **Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry** — released **15 June 2026.**
- **What it measures:** Wholesale price change (WPI) plus, for the first time, **Producer Price Indices (PPI)** capturing producer-side and **services** prices — beginning India's transition from WPI to PPI (per IMF guidance and advanced-economy practice).
- **Key findings / features:**
  - **Base year revised 2011-12 → 2022-23**; basket items expanded **697 → 957**; weights now use **Gross Value of Output (GVO)** instead of net traded value.
  - **Three PPIs** introduced — Output PPI, a **trial Input PPI** (manufacturing only), and a **Service PPI for 7 services** (Banking, Securities, Insurance, Pension Funds, Railways, Air-Passenger, Telecom).
  - **WPI inflation rose to 9.68% (May 2026)** from 8.26% (April), led by **Fuel & Power (30.33%).** WPI to run **5 more years** alongside PPI, then be discontinued.
- **Caveat:** Service PPI still covers only 7 services with no overall weights; Input PPI experimental; 2022-23 base may carry pandemic-recovery distortions.

### B. Energy Transition Index (ETI) 2026 — World Economic Forum
**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Energy security, renewables, climate; GS2 — Global indices

- **Publishing body:** **World Economic Forum (WEF)** (with Accenture) — released **c. 18 June 2026.**
- **What it measures:** Countries' **energy-system performance** (security, equity, sustainability) and **transition readiness** (infrastructure, regulation, investment, human capital).
- **India's standing / key findings:**
  - India **rose two places to 70th**, and was among the **strongest improvers globally** — driven by gains in **infrastructure, equity, sustainability and financial investment.**
  - **Top 3:** Sweden, Finland, Denmark (retained).
  - Global energy-transition **readiness dipped overall** even as several large emerging economies (incl. India) gained — underscoring an uneven global transition.
