## SCHEMES & PROGRAMMES

### 1. GOBARdhan — National Circular Bioenergy Scheme ⭐ *(scheme of the week)*

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Full form** | **G**alvanizing **O**rganic **B**io-**A**gro **R**esources **Dhan** |
| **Ministry / nodal agency** | **Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas (MoPNG)** |
| **Approved** | Union Cabinet, **6 August 2026** |
| **Type** | **Central Sector Scheme** (100% Union funding) |
| **Outlay & period** | **₹23,731 crore**, FY 2026-27 to FY 2035-36 (10 years) |

**Objective:** Convert India's agricultural residue, cattle dung, press mud, municipal organic waste and other biomass into **Compressed Biogas (CBG)**, organic manure and rural income — building a national circular bioeconomy and cutting import dependence on fossil gas.

**Key features — the "Six Growth Engines":**

1. **Assured CBG offtake** — procurement by City Gas Distribution (CGD) entities against a notified **CBG Obligation trajectory: 3% (FY27) → 4% (FY28) → 5% (from FY29)** in CNG (transport) and PNG (domestic) segments.
2. **Stable administered price of ₹2,110 per MMBTU**, government-backed, with a **minimum 10-year horizon** for revenue visibility.
3. **Capital assistance up to ₹2 crore per TPD** of installed CBG capacity (greenfield; brownfield expansion also eligible), extending to feedstock aggregation and manure-processing assets.
4. **Pipeline infrastructure support** (cluster-based and standalone) + **Credit Guarantee mechanism** for MSME CBG projects + a **CBG Ecosystem Challenge Fund** for district-level feedstock mapping and capacity building.

**Consolidation angle (very Prelims-friendly):** GOBARdhan merges the earlier fragmented architecture — **SATAT**, **Market Development Assistance (MDA)** for organic manure, **Biomass Aggregation Machinery (BAM)**, **Development of Pipeline Infrastructure (DPI)**, and **CFA for CBG plants under the National Bioenergy Programme** — into one platform.

**Beneficiaries / coverage:** Farmers and feedstock aggregators, rural entrepreneurs, cooperatives, MSMEs, women entrepreneurs, CBG developers. Builds on **200+ CBG plants** already commissioned.

**Significance:** Targets a **near ten-fold rise in domestic CBG output**; CBG is chemically equivalent to natural gas and drops straight into existing gas infrastructure; supports waste-to-wealth, GHG reduction and the organic-manure (FOM/LFOM) economy.

**Criticism / watch-points:** An administered price plus a blending obligation transfers cost to CGD entities and eventually consumers; past CBG targets under SATAT badly undershot (feedstock aggregation and land are the binding constraints, not price alone); manure offtake economics remain weak; heavy reliance on a single ministry to coordinate an inherently agriculture-and-urban-waste value chain.

**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Energy / Environment / Infrastructure; GS3 — Agriculture (crop residue management); GS2 — Government policies. *(Prelims: outlay, price, obligation %, ministry.)*

### 2. Nasha Mukt Yuva for Viksit Bharat Sankalp Abhiyan

- **Ministry / nodal agency:** **Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (MYAS)**, through **Mera Yuva Bharat (MY Bharat)**; convergence with Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment's Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan ecosystem.
- **Launched:** **2 August 2026** by the Prime Minister via video conferencing — over **1 crore young citizens from 28,000+ locations** joined.
- **Type:** Central Sector campaign / *Jan Bhagidari* movement (not a DBT scheme).
- **Objective:** Make substance-abuse prevention a youth-led social movement; convert young people into ambassadors of a drug-free India.

**Key features:**

1. A **100-week nationwide campaign** — activities every **Sunday** ("100 Sundays, 100 steps to a drug-free India").
2. Weekly formats: sports events, walkathons, meditation sessions, cultural programmes, *nukkad natak*, discussion forums, art competitions.
3. District → State → National level competitions under MY Bharat: **"Words for Change"** (slogan), **"Colours of Change"** (painting), **"Youth Dialogue for Change"** (debate), and a music/rap contest — district rounds began **13 July 2026**.
4. Delivery through the MY Bharat volunteer platform, linking youth clubs, NYKS and educational institutions.

**Significance:** Shifts anti-drug policy from enforcement-led (NDPS) to demand-reduction and peer-influence models; leverages India's demographic dividend framing.

**Criticism:** Campaign-mode initiatives risk event-count metrics over measurable reduction in prevalence; no dedicated treatment/rehabilitation capacity expansion announced alongside; de-addiction infrastructure (IRCAs, DDACs) remains thin in high-burden districts (Punjab, Manipur, Mizoram, North-East border belt).

**Syllabus tag:** GS2 — Social Justice / Health-related welfare; GS3 — Internal security (drug trafficking linkage); Essay — demographic dividend.

### 3. Mukhya Mantrir Nijut Moina Aasoni 2.0 + Mukhya Mantrir Nijut Babu Aasoni *(State: Assam)*

- **Nodal agency:** Government of Assam, Department of Higher Education.
- **Launched:** **Nijut Moina** — announced in Assam Budget 2024-25, rolled out **August 2024**; **Phase 2.0** from 2025. **Nijut Babu Aasoni** — **1 January 2026**. Application-form distribution for both formally launched by CM Himanta Biswa Sarma on **6 August 2026** in Guwahati.
- **Type:** State scheme, Aadhaar-based **DBT**.
- **Objective:** Raise girls' (and now boys') enrolment in higher education, lift the State's **Gross Enrolment Ratio (GER)**, cut dropouts, and — for Nijut Moina — attack **child marriage**.

**Key features:**

1. **Nijut Moina:** monthly assistance to girl students — **₹1,000 (HS/UG level) rising to ₹2,500 (PG level)**, for a maximum of **10 months** per academic year.
2. **Nijut Babu:** monthly scholarship for male students — **₹1,000/month (UG 1st year)** and **₹2,000/month (PG 1st year)**, also for 10 months.
3. Eligibility: domicile of Assam; regular enrolment in Government / Government-aided / Central University / approved venture institutions / Government polytechnics. Applications routed through the institution.
4. **Disbursal from 10 October 2026** via Aadhaar-based DBT.

**Beneficiaries / coverage:** Nijut Moina targets **10 lakh girls** over its life; **6 lakh+** already benefited. Nijut Babu extends the model to boys.

**Significance:** One of the few state conditional-cash-transfer models explicitly linked to child-marriage reduction — the State reports a **~80% decline in child marriage** since the crackdown-plus-incentive package began. The addition of Nijut Babu answers the "boys left behind in higher education" critique of girl-only transfers.

**Criticism:** Attribution of the child-marriage decline is contested (coercive FIR-driven enforcement ran in parallel); transfer amounts are small relative to the real cost of higher education; the exclusion of private unaided institutions limits reach.

**Syllabus tag:** GS2 — Education / Welfare schemes for vulnerable sections; GS1 — Indian Society (women, social empowerment).

### 4. 'Vetri Vaanmagal' Drone Training Scheme *(State: Tamil Nadu)*

- **Nodal agency:** Government of Tamil Nadu, Department of Agriculture; training delivered through the **Remote Pilot Training Organisation (RPTO) of Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU)**.
- **Announced:** **6 August 2026**, in the **Tamil Nadu Agriculture Budget 2026-27**.
- **Type:** State scheme (training + capital subsidy).
- **Objective:** Train women farmers and women-led SHG members as **licensed agricultural drone pilots**, and seed a women-run drone-services economy in agriculture.

**Key features:**

1. **500 persons to be trained**, of whom **100 will be women**; support for obtaining **DGCA remote-pilot licences**.
2. **₹1.50 crore** allocated for drone training.
3. **₹2.50 crore** from state funds to supply drones at **50% subsidy to 50 trained women**.
4. Positioned as a livelihood/service-provider model (drone spraying as a paid service), not just a skilling exercise.

**Significance:** Lands in the year the **UN has declared 2026 the International Year of the Woman Farmer**. Complements the Centre's **Namo Drone Didi** (Central Sector, drones to 15,000 women SHGs) — a useful Mains comparison of central vs state design.

**Criticism:** Scale is modest (100 women); drone-spraying economics depend on assured acreage and pesticide-label approvals for aerial application; maintenance, insurance and battery-replacement costs are typically not covered by one-time capital subsidy.

**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Agriculture, technology in agriculture; GS1/GS2 — Women empowerment.

### 5. 'Meri Beti – Mera Abhiman' Campaign *(State: Chhattisgarh)*

- **Nodal agency:** Government of Chhattisgarh, Panchayat & Rural Development Department; implemented **under the Centre's VB-G RAM G** (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)).
- **Launched:** by CM Vishnu Deo Sai; campaign runs **15 August 2026 → 26 January 2027**.
- **Type:** State campaign layered on a Centrally Sponsored employment-guarantee framework.
- **Objective:** Dignity and safety of girls through school sanitation, plus rural social infrastructure and groundwater recharge.

**Key features:**

1. **6,671 girls' toilets** in rural government schools — **₹286.85 crore** (~₹4.30 lakh per unit).
2. Crematoriums with waiting sheds in **6,524 villages (₹391.44 crore)** + **549 more (₹18.22 crore)**.
3. Groundwater recharge: sand filters and recharge structures in **10,027 defunct borewells** — **₹52.14 crore**.
4. Executed as **VB-G RAM G works**, so it doubles as rural wage employment.

**Significance:** A clean illustration of **convergence** — one campaign delivering WASH (girls' school sanitation → retention), dignity infrastructure, water security and wage employment. Links directly to the retention argument in Swachh Bharat's school-toilet literature.

**Criticism:** Toilet *construction* has historically outrun **operation, water supply and maintenance**; no announced O&M or female-attendant funding line. Crematorium construction, while socially valuable, is a low-multiplier use of employment-guarantee funds.

**Syllabus tag:** GS2 — Welfare schemes, education; GS3 — Water conservation, rural employment.

### 6. Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY) — completes one year *(new development on a previously noted scheme)*

- **Ministry:** **Ministry of Labour & Employment**, operated through **EPFO**.
- **Launched / period:** **1 August 2025**; implementation window **1 Aug 2025 – 31 July 2027**.
- **Type:** **Central Sector Scheme**. **Outlay ₹99,446 crore.**
- **Milestone (1 August 2026):** **72 lakh+ first-time employees** have entered the formal workforce since launch. On **19 June 2026** the PM disbursed **~₹2,400 crore** in incentives, supporting **15 lakh** employment opportunities.

**Design recap:** Part A — incentive of **up to ₹15,000** to **first-time employees** (EPFO-registered), paid in instalments; Part B — incentive to **employers** for additional sustained hiring. Overall target: employment generation for **3.5 crore+ persons**, including **1.92 crore first-time employees**.

**Significance:** India's principal **Employment-Linked Incentive (ELI)** instrument — the labour-side counterpart to the PLI schemes on the output side. Doubles as a formalisation drive (EPFO enrolment) and social-security expansion.

**Criticism:** EPFO-anchored design captures only the formal, establishment-based segment — it structurally excludes the ~90% informal workforce, gig/platform workers and own-account enterprises; risk of **churning** (re-labelling existing workers as "first-time") and of subsidising hiring that would have happened anyway (deadweight).

**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Employment, growth & development; GS2 — Government policies and interventions.

## POLICY / LEGISLATIVE CHANGE OF THE WEEK

### 7. The Bankers' Books Evidence Bill, 2026

- **Ministry:** **Ministry of Finance** (introduced in **Lok Sabha on 3 August 2026** by FM Nirmala Sitharaman, Monsoon Session Phase 2).
- **What it does:** **Repeals and replaces the Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 1891** — a 135-year-old colonial statute — with a **technology-neutral** framework for the proof and admissibility of bank records.
- **Key changes:** (i) expands "bankers' books" to cover **physical, electronic, digital, virtual and cloud-based** formats; (ii) formally recognises **electronic banking records as admissible evidence**; (iii) aligns banking evidence law with the **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023** and modern core-banking/cloud architecture.
- **Significance:** Reduces friction in banking litigation, recovery proceedings and cyber-fraud prosecution, where certified electronic statements were previously challenged on admissibility grounds. Part of the broader colonial-law-repeal drive.
- **Watch-point:** Custody, integrity and chain-of-custody standards for cloud-hosted records — and data-localisation implications — will decide whether the reform actually holds up in court.

**Syllabus tag:** GS2 — Polity/Governance, laws enacted by Parliament; GS3 — Banking, economic reforms.

*Also in Parliament this session:* **The Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Bill, 2026** (MHA) — introduced in Lok Sabha on 25 March 2026, back in focus this week; it creates a framework for **supervision, management and disposal of foreign contributions and assets of organisations that cease to hold an FCRA certificate**. Contested by parts of civil society over NGO space. *(GS2 — Role of NGOs / Statutory bodies.)*

## REPORTS & INDICES

### A. "Reimagining Care: Strategies for Empowering Caregivers in Viksit Bharat@2047" — **NITI Aayog**

- **Publishing body:** **NITI Aayog**. Released **6 August 2026** by Member **Dr. R. Balasubramaniam** and CEO **Nidhi Chhibber**, with WHO, UNICEF, World Bank, UN Women and ILO representatives present. Vice Chairperson **Dr. Ashok Kumar Lahiri** spoke at the release.
- **What it covers:** India's **care economy** and caregiving workforce — deliberately framed around **care, not medical treatment** — as a pillar of inclusive growth and as an export opportunity given global caregiver shortages.

**Key recommendations (3 to remember):**

1. A **National Policy on Caregiving** and a **National Caregiver Council** for coordination.
2. A **National Caregiving Qualification Framework**, a **National Caregiver Registry** and a digital platform for workforce planning and service delivery.
3. Recognise **caregiving as an allied profession** — defined roles, fair wages, job security, career progression; plus overseas placement mechanisms to make India a **global hub for skilled caregivers**.

**Why it matters:** Directly relevant to India's **ageing transition**, the invisibility of **unpaid female care work** in GDP, and the ILO/UN Women "5R" care framework (Recognise, Reduce, Redistribute, Reward, Represent). A very likely GS1/GS2 Mains hook.

**Syllabus tag:** GS1 — Ageing, role of women; GS2 — Health & social sector; GS3 — Employment / human resource development.

### B. Environmental Performance Index (EPI) 2026 — **India ranks 176 of 177**

- **Publishing body:** **Yale Center for Environmental Law & Policy** with **Columbia Climate School's CIESIN** (Center for International Earth Science Information Network). **Biennial.** Released **July 2026**; dominated Indian environmental commentary in the first week of August.
- **What it measures:** Country environmental performance across indicators grouped under **environmental health, ecosystem vitality and climate change**.

**India's standing and key findings:**

1. **Rank 176 of 177**, score **22.46/100**, against a regional average of **31.81** — India remains among the worst performers, with only one country below it.
2. **But the 10-year change score is +7.47** — i.e. India is improving over time even though its level remains very low.
3. Relative gains recorded in **climate-change mitigation indicators, waste management and sanitation**; the drag comes from **air quality, biodiversity and habitat protection, ecosystem services and urban environmental management**.

**Standard Indian counter-argument (worth writing in Mains):** India (and other developing countries) has repeatedly criticised EPI methodology for weighting indicators that favour rich, low-population-density economies, using projected 2050 GHG trajectories rather than historical/cumulative emissions, and ignoring per-capita emissions and equity — MoEFCC rejected the 2022 edition on similar grounds.

**Syllabus tag:** GS3 — Environment & conservation, pollution; GS2 — Important international indices/reports.

## PRELIMS POINTERS

- **GOBARdhan expands to:** Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan. Administered by **MoPNG** (not Jal Shakti — the older 2018 GOBAR-Dhan sanitation initiative sat with the **Department of Drinking Water & Sanitation**; this 2026 Cabinet-approved scheme is the CBG-focused national scheme).
- **Schemes subsumed into GOBARdhan:** SATAT, MDA (organic manure), BAM, DPI, CFA under the National Bioenergy Programme.
- **CBG obligation trajectory:** 3% → 4% → 5% (FY27, FY28, FY29 onwards) in CNG-Transport and PNG-Domestic.
- **Ramsar:** India = **101 sites**; Glaw Lake is **Arunachal Pradesh's first**, located in **Kamlang Tiger Reserve**; flagship species = **white-bellied heron** (Critically Endangered).
- **EPI** is **biennial**, published by **Yale + Columbia (CIESIN)** — do not confuse with the **Environmental Vulnerability Index** or the **Climate Change Performance Index (Germanwatch/NewClimate/CAN)**.
- **Hexavalent vaccine = 6 antigens:** Diphtheria, Pertussis, Tetanus, Hepatitis B, Polio (IPV), Hib.
- **PM-VBRY is an ELI (Employment-Linked Incentive) scheme**, routed through **EPFO**, window **Aug 2025 – Jul 2027**.
- **2026 is the UN International Year of the Woman Farmer.**
