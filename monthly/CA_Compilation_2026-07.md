## 1. International Relations

**India–Japan 16th Annual Summit (1–3 July, New Delhi).** Modi hosted new Japanese PM **Sanae Takaichi**; joint declarations on **economic security & AI**. *Remember:* **$12.5 billion** Japanese investment pledge.

**PM Modi's 3-nation Indo-Pacific tour (6–11 July): Indonesia, Australia, New Zealand.** Indonesia = first bilateral since the **2018 Comprehensive Strategic Partnership**; Australia (Melbourne summit) delivered a **civil-uranium supply pact** + critical minerals; **New Zealand elevated to a Strategic Partnership** with a "Roadmap to 2030." Linked outcome: India–Australia–Canada launched the **"ACITI"** trilateral technology/critical-minerals partnership. *Remember:* NZ ties upgraded to Strategic Partnership; ACITI trilateral.

**India's UNSC 2028–29 campaign launched at UN HQ (13–14 July).** Vision — **"SHANTI"** (Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity). *Remember:* would be India's **9th term**; contests **Tajikistan** for the Asia-Pacific seat; election **June 2027**; UNSC has 10 non-permanent members (2-yr terms, Art. 23 UN Charter).

**India–UK CETA + Double Contribution Convention entered into force (15 July).** *Remember:* the DCC prevents double social-security payments for Indian professionals in the UK.

**US–Iran hostilities resume; Strait of Hormuz back in play.** *Remember:* Brent spiked **~15% to ~$85/barrel**; ~**90% of India's LPG imports** transit Hormuz; India voted for **UNSC Resolution 2817** (a break from its usual abstention).

**China imposes a temporary helium export ban** — critical-gas squeeze touching ISRO and India's chip push.

**President Murmu's 3-nation Europe visit: Moldova, North Macedonia, Romania (19–25 July)** — historic firsts in Moldova & North Macedonia.

**Other IR:** India–Israel conclude **2nd round of FTA talks** (20–23 July); India hosts the **13th AITIGA Joint Committee** (ASEAN–India trade-pact review) and the **3rd BRICS Ministerial on Disaster Risk Reduction** (India's 2026 BRICS Chairship); India–Kyrgyzstan open the **"Manas and Mahabharata" Centre** for Civilizational Studies, Bishkek; **Akash SAM exported to Tajikistan & Turkmenistan**; India–Spain to fast-track **UPI–Bizum** interoperability; **India–China border trade resumes via Nathu La & Lipulekh from 1 August 2026**; China holds a live-fire drill near **Okinotorishima** (Japan-claimed EEZ); a US Senate bill threatens **up to 500% "secondary tariffs"** on Russian-oil buyers (India, China named).

## 2. Science & Technology

**Semicon 2.0 + Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme — ₹2.19 lakh crore package.** Semicon 2.0 = **₹1,27,500 crore** for the full chip value chain; **MPMS = ₹62,500 crore** (successor to PLI-LSEM). *Remember:* Semicon 2.0 headline figure **₹1,27,500 crore**.

**Skyroot's Vikram-1 — India's first *private* orbital rocket — launched successfully** from Sriharikota. Alongside: ISRO ground-tested the **SOLVE** solid motor (Gaganyaan crew-recovery test vehicle) and cleared the **CE20/C32 cryogenic stage for LVM3-M7**.

**Bharat 6G Mission — DoT–TSDSI MoU** to push indigenous telecom standards. *Remember:* target **≥10% of global 6G patents**; TSDSI is India's national telecom SDO (got the LMLC waveform into ITU 5G).

**Defence firsts:** **INS Mahendragiri commissioned** (6th of 7 Project 17A stealth frigates); **Astra Mk-1** becomes India's **first BVR air-to-air missile export** (Indonesia = first buyer); DRDO flight-tests **'Kusha'**, India's first indigenous long-range SAM; DRDO-GTRE builds India's **first 350-kg-thrust-class expendable turbojet engine**.

**Health-tech:** India approves its **first dengue vaccine — QDENGA (TAK-003)**. *Also:* **Chandipura virus** outbreak — nine paediatric deaths across Gujarat & Rajasthan.

**UN's first scientific assessment of AI** — Independent International Scientific Panel; co-chairs **Yoshua Bengio & Maria Ressa**; warns AI capability outpaces governance; compute concentration **USA ~75%, China ~15%**.

**Other S&T:** "One Nation, One Time" — **White Rabbit-based IST distribution network**, Bengaluru; **Made-in-Bharat chips headed into Indian launch vehicles**; IIT Delhi–DRDO tactical aerostat (payload to ~20 km); SC declares **"zero tolerance" for AI-hallucinated precedents**; India wins **all four golds at the 58th International Chemistry Olympiad**, Tashkent.

## 3. Environment

**CSE "State of India's Environment 2026": 7 of 9 planetary boundaries now breached**, ocean acidification the newest to cross. *Remember:* ocean acidification = 7th boundary breached.

**NTCA/MoEFCC "Tiger Conservation Roadmap"** — **25 of India's 58 tiger reserves** flagged for priority recovery. Related: **Kaimur WLS expanded** — on track to be **Bihar's second tiger reserve**.

**7th National CAMPA** clears four new species projects + the **₹3,000-crore "Aastha Van Sanrakshan Yojana" (AVSY)** to conserve **~15,000 sacred groves**.

**Biodiversity milestones:** Mizoram University's **Natural History Museum = India's 21st Designated Repository** under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002; **ZSI logs 709 new faunal records (2025)** + launches India's first **Fauna Checklist Portal**; **Indian Grey Hornbill re-established in Gir** after 60+ years; India's **first night safari** cleared at **Kukrail, Lucknow**.

**New species (Places-in-News fodder):** two solitary bees (**Siang Valley, Arunachal**); five *Allographa* lichens (**Western Ghats**); *Gitchak nakana*, India's first **subterranean fish** (Assam); *Caridina sharmai*, a freshwater shrimp (**Gomti, Lucknow**).

**Clean energy & climate finance:** **PM Surya Ghar: Muft Bijli Yojana** — World Bank **$890 mn** + ADB **$850 mn** (Subprogram 2); India has **11 pumped-storage projects under construction, 15,870 MW**; Coal Ministry's **AAROH** — first national report on scientific mine closure.

## 4. Economy

**Growth & prices:** **IIP +5.1% (May 2026)** — five-month high; **retail (CPI) inflation 4.38% in June** — highest since Dec 2024; **WPI inflation 9.87% in June** (wide CPI–WPI gap); **IMF July WEO** trims India FY27 growth to **6.4%**, still the fastest-growing major economy.

**Banking / RBI:** revised **bank-board governance framework** (effective **1 Oct 2026**; PSB norms extended to private banks); revised **Capital Market Exposure norms** (from 1 July); July Bulletin flags FDI recovery.

**GST at 9 years** — collections cross **₹22 lakh crore**; **"GST 2.0" two-slab** structure beds in.

**Big-ticket approvals:** **Modified UDAN** — ₹28,840 crore, extended to FY2035-36; **NIIF** gets an extra ₹30,000 crore (total commitment doubles to **₹60,000 crore**); **NIPU-2026** — nine gas-based urea plants, 10 MT; **BHAVYA-Rasayan** — ₹3,030 crore for three Chemical Parks.

**New statistical tools:** **Index of Services Production (ISP)** launched (services counterpart to IIP); **Core Industries index rebased to 2022-23**, **iron ore added as the 9th core industry**; MoSPI to shift the **GDP deflator from WPI to output PPI**.

**Bioeconomy Roadmap 2035** — target **USD 691 billion** bioeconomy + a **₹50,000-crore BioEconomy Growth Fund**.

**Other:** FDI now allowed in **inventory-based e-commerce — but only for exports** (Press Note 3, 2026 Series); **NSE** launches natural-gas futures on a **domestic benchmark**; customs duty waived on **Li-ion cell inputs**; **Telecom Manufacturing Zone at Gwalior (MP)**; **Delhi Lakshmi Yojana** — ₹2,500/month, part-paid in programmable **CBDC**; **Q1 FY27** exports **$232.73 bn (+11.37%)**, deficit widens to **$37.42 bn**.

## 5. Polity & Governance

**Constitution (131st Amendment) Bill, 2026 fails in Lok Sabha**; Delimitation Bill and UT Laws Bill withdrawn.

**Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Bill, 2026** raises sanctioned strength to **38 judges**.

**Judiciary in action:** SC sets up **four dedicated "oldest cases" benches** (from 15 July, ~200 cases each); rules the **Governor's Article 161 remission power can't be overridden by a later statutory CrPC policy**; holds the **"Right to Walk" on safe footpaths a Fundamental Right**; bars **SIR data** from being used to strip welfare/decide citizenship; stays coercive action in **anti-NEET protest FIRs**; issues notice on the **Transgender Persons (Protection of Rights) Amendment Act, 2026** and on a **bank–ARC–borrower "nexus."**

**Bills / legislation:** **Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026** — statutory protection for **Vande Mataram**; **Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026** (Nilekani task force on exam reforms; NTA sacks 47 officials); **Draft Advocates (Amendment) Bill, 2026** out for consultation.

**Delivery & governance:** **West Bengal becomes the 36th State/UT under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY** — nationwide rollout complete; **PM-SETU** rolled out nationwide (₹1,237.58 crore for 200 ITI clusters); **DISHA 2.0** — ₹255 crore to take legal aid to **3 crore people by 2031**; Maharashtra legislature goes **paperless (NeVA)**.

**Digital state / rights friction:** **e-OCI digital card + FCRA 2.0 portal** launched; **UMANG data-exposure flaws** (DPI trust test); India orders **GitHub to pull BitChat repos** (Sec 79(3)(b)); film *Satluj* pulled off ZEE5 under **Section 69A**.
