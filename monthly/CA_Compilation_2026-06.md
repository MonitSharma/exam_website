## International Relations

Links to India's National Critical Mineral Mission, MMDR Act 2023 amendment, KABIL, and Minerals Security Partnership (MSP) membership.
- **India–Oman CEPA in force** — India's 2nd FTA with a GCC nation (after UAE CEPA, 2022) entered into force **1 June 2026**; duty-free access for **99.38%** of India's export value to Oman; key Oman ports outside Hormuz — **Duqm, Salalah**.
- **India's BRICS Chairship 2026** — Theme *"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"*; now an **11-member bloc** (added Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Indonesia). June events under India's chairship: BRICS NSAs' Meeting (22–23 June, Delhi, NSA Doval), Agriculture Ministers' "Indore Declaration" (13 June), and the **BRICS Heads of Space Agencies (HOSA)** meeting in Bengaluru (23–24 June) advancing a BRICS Remote Sensing Satellite Constellation. NDB: HQ **Shanghai**, launched Fortaleza 2014.

- **G7 Summit 2026, Évian (France)** — India attended its **13th** time as an outreach/partner (not a member); G7 = US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Canada, Japan + EU (non-counted). France held the 2026 presidency. Sidelines: **India–France Innovation Roadmap 2030**, India–Slovakia and other bilaterals advanced.

- **India–US Bilateral Trade Agreement (BTA)** — Ministerial talks (Goyal–Greer, ~20–21 June) raced to finalise **Phase-1** before the **10% temporary US tariff** (imposed 24 Feb 2026) expires **24 July 2026**.
- **India–UK CETA to enter force 15 July 2026** — India's most consequential FTA with a developed economy; **>99%** of India's tariff lines go zero-duty for UK goods; aims to double bilateral trade by 2030.
- **India's first-ever FATF Vice-President** — **Vivek Aggarwal** elected at the Paris Plenary (17–19 June); term July 2026–June 2027; FATF Secretariat sits at OECD, Paris (est. 1989 G7 Summit).
- **Bimal N. Patel elected ITLOS judge** — Term 2026–2035 (115/168 votes); ITLOS (under UNCLOS 1982) is HQ'd in **Hamburg**, not The Hague; 21 judges, 9-year terms.
- **India–Russia logistics pact (RELOS) operationalised** — Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement, signed Moscow (18 Feb 2025), in force Jan 2026, valid 5 years; strict **non-basing clause**, ~3,000-personnel administrative ceiling.
- **Bangladesh revives Padma Barrage as 1996 Ganga Waters Treaty nears expiry (Dec 2026)** — 30-year treaty shares dry-season Ganga flows at Farakka; Dhaka's new demand: account for upstream withdrawals before the Farakka gauge.
- **PM Modi's State Visit to Seychelles (27–29 June)** — Guest of Honour at Seychelles' 50th National Day (independence 29 June 1976 = also 50 years of bilateral ties); reaffirmed **SAGAR (2015) → MAHASAGAR (2025)** doctrine; deliverables included ICAR–Seychelles MoU, ambulance handover, UPI deployment.

- **ASI–National Museum of Denmark MoU** — ASI's first-ever international collaboration, to locate/document the Danish ship *Oresund* (1619), which sank near Karaikal, Puducherry.

## Science & Technology

- **ISRO's GISAT-1A (EOS-05)** — Geostationary (not polar-SSO) Earth-observation satellite for near-continuous disaster monitoring; launcher **GSLV Mk II**, Sriharikota.
- **DRDO's RudraM-II** — Indigenous anti-radiation missile flight-tested from a **Su-30 MKI** (2 June); range ~300 km, ~Mach 5.5; gives IAF a SEAD capability.
- **DRDO validates Phase-II Ballistic Missile Defence (AD-1, AD-2) + maiden NASM-MR test** — Tests at ITR Chandipur/Dhamra, Odisha (10–11 June); places India among a small club (US, Russia, China, Israel) with layered BMD covering up to **~5,000 km**-range threats.
- **DRDO's Netra Mk-1 AEW&C gets Final Operational Clearance** — 25 June, at CABS Bengaluru; platform = Embraer ERJ-145; IAF operates 3 aircraft from Bhisiana AFS, Punjab; distinguish from the bigger AWACS (on IL-76).
- **Aqueous zinc-ion battery breakthrough (BDIM additive)** — INST Mohali (under DST) developed an electrolyte additive suppressing dendrite growth, hydrogen evolution and corrosion — a safer, cheaper lithium-ion alternative.
- **ISRO semi-cryogenic engine (SE-2000) hot-tested at 175-tonne thrust** (~88% of design) — At Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu; will power the **SC120 stage** replacing LVM3's current L110 core stage.
- **India's 12-year tech-ecosystem review** — National Supercomputing Mission (₹4,500 cr, PARAM Rudra), Semicon India (₹76,000 cr), **National Quantum Mission** (₹6,003.65 cr, 4 pillars, Quantum Valley at Amaravati), IndiaAI Mission (₹10,300+ cr, 38,000+ GPUs); Global Innovation Index: India **38th (2025)**, up from 81st (2015).
- **World's first nuclear-heat hydrogen plant (Cu–Cl cycle)** — Commissioned at IGCAR, Kalpakkam (26 June) using process heat from the Fast Breeder Test Reactor; developed by BARC + IGCAR — links Stage-II of India's three-stage nuclear programme to clean hydrogen.
- **India's commercial space sector** — NSIL revenue up ~10x (₹321.77 cr FY22 → ₹3,246.09 cr FY25); **400+** registered space startups (Feb 2026, from 1 in 2014); enablers — IN-SPACe, Indian Space Policy 2023.
- **ISRO–DAE lunar-night survival tech** — Developing Radioisotope Heater Units to extend lander life to **100–200 days** (vs Chandrayaan-3 Vikram's ~14 days); feeds Chandrayaan-4/LUPEX (Chandrayaan-5, ISRO–JAXA).
- **BGP hijacking** in the news (post-Telegram block) — Border Gateway Protocol misrouting via false IP-prefix advertisement; can cause DoS or Man-in-the-Middle interception.
- **Iran's uranium "downblending"** back on the table — IAEA-reported 60%-enriched stockpile (~440.9 kg) proposed to be downblended to ~20% or the JCPOA's 3.67% cap.
- **Millisecond pulsar PSR J0125−5854 discovered** — First MSP found by the Murchison Widefield Array's SMART survey; India is a full SKAO member (uGMRT, InPTA are key pathfinders).
- **AIR SUVIDHA 2.0 relaunched** amid the Ebola PHEIC — WHO declared a PHEIC (16 May 2026) for the DRC/Uganda Ebola outbreak (Bundibugyo ebolavirus); no case reported in India; portal shares data with Airport Health Officer, IDSP.
- **Fibre-optic drones** — Cable-tethered UAVs immune to RF jamming, reshaping asymmetric warfare (Russia–Ukraine, Israel–Hezbollah); limited by cable range/fragility.

## Environment

- **India crosses 100 Ramsar sites** — **Jai Prakash Narayan Bird Sanctuary (Surha Tal), Ballia, UP** declared the 100th site on World Environment Day (5 June); India now **3rd globally** (after UK–176, Mexico–144) and **1st in Asia**.
- **World Environment Day 2026** — Theme *"Inspired by Nature. For Climate. For Our Future"*; host **Azerbaijan (Baku)**; India's tally: 58 tiger reserves, 100 Ramsar sites, ~75% of world's wild tigers.
- **State of India's Environment (SoE) 2026 report (CSE/Down To Earth)** — **7 of 9 planetary boundaries breached**, including the newly-breached **ocean acidification**; **Lantana camara** (invasive shrub, ~50% of forest/scrubland) linked to rising human–tiger conflict (≥43 killed near reserves, Jan–Jun 2025); India's tiger estimate **~3,682** (2022 cycle).
- **NOAA confirms a new El Niño** — **63%** chance of a "super" event (SST anomaly >+2°C) by winter 2026–27; only 4 such super-events since 1950; risk of monsoon suppression.
- **India becomes world's No.1 ship-recycling nation (2025)** — UNCTAD: India's global share rose to **35.4%** (from 30.1% in 2024), meeting the Maritime India Vision 2030 target 5 years early; **Alang, Gujarat** is the world's largest ship-breaking yard; enabling law — Recycling of Ships Act, 2019 (Hong Kong Convention).
- **Green Hydrogen Certification Portal of India (GHCI)** launched (18 June, MNRE) — "Green" only if **<2 kg CO₂e/kg H₂**; under the National Green Hydrogen Mission (₹19,744 cr; 5 MMT/yr by 2030 target).
- **Small Hydro Power Development Scheme** guidelines operationalised — ₹2,584.60 cr outlay (FY27–FY31); SECI as implementing agency; target ~1,500 MW, mostly Himalayan/NE regions.
- **WT-MARUT** — India's first Wind Turbine Supply Chain Management Portal, launched at Global Wind Day 2026, Goa; localisation target proposed to rise to 85% by 2030.
- **World Day to Combat Desertification & Drought 2026** (17 June) — Theme *"Rangelands: Recognize. Respect. Restore"*; host Kenya; delivered via **WDC–PMKSY 2.0** across 813 project areas in India.
- **UNICEF Children's Climate Risk Report 2026** — **~392 million (92%)** Indian children exposed to extreme heat; India's Heat Action Plans flagged as insufficiently child-sensitive.
- **Aravalli protection row** — Experts urged the CJI to restructure the SC-appointed **Kanchan Devi Committee** (constituted 25 May, under MoEFCC) reviewing where the Aravalli range legally "begins and ends"; report due 31 Aug 2026.
- **Western Ghats Eco-Sensitive Area — phased, state-wise notification** — Centre moving to notify states in consensus (Gujarat, Goa, Maharashtra) rather than waiting for all six; recall **Gadgil (2011): ~1,29,037 sq km** vs **Kasturirangan (2013): ~37% / ~60,000 sq km**; current 6th draft ≈ 56,825.7 sq km.
- **Chhattisgarh's "5% Model" groundwater recharge (Korea district)** — Farmers dedicate 5% of farmland to recharge pits; scaled from ~2,000 (2025) to ~30,000 farmers (2026); praised in *Mann Ki Baat* as a national template; women lead as "Neer Nayikas."
- **Bangladesh joins the International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA) as 27th member** — IBCA (treaty-based, HQ New Delhi, launched April 2023) covers 7 big cats; significant given the shared Sundarbans tiger habitat.
- **IITM–ARIES MoU for a Devasthal (Uttarakhand) climate-observation station** — Under the Bharat Climate Observation Network and Mission Mausam (₹2,000-cr MoES programme).

## Polity & Governance

- **Supreme Court's sanctioned strength raised 34 → 38** — Via the *Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026*, promulgated **16 May** under **Article 123** (Art. 124(1) makes SC strength a matter of ordinary law, hence amendable by ordinance).
- **SC: "Right to Walk" on safe, demarcated footpaths is a Fundamental Right** — Landmark 19 June ruling (*Re: Fundamental Right to Walk and Footpath*; also *Maniyar Iliyaz v. P. Ayyappan*), Justices Narasimha & Chandurkar; rooted in **Article 19(1)(d) read with Article 21**; pedestrians given priority over motorised vehicles; pedestrian deaths rose 163% (2015→2024).
- **SC: Homemakers are "Nation Builders"** — New **"Loss of Domestic Care"** compensation head (*Shishu Pal v. Surjeet*, 11 June); notional income fixed at **₹30,000/month**, based on the NSO's 2019 Time Use Survey (unpaid care work ≈15–17% of GDP).
- **SC: Article 329(b) bars a writ against nomination-rejection mid-election** — *Meenakshi Natarajan v. ECI*; reaffirms *N.P. Ponnuswami* — remedy lies only in a post-poll election petition under the RPA, 1951, not Article 32/226.
- **FCRA (Amendment) Bill/Rules, 2026** — Bill proposes a "Designated Authority" (new Section 16A) to provisionally vest assets of NGOs losing FCRA registration; **Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Rules, 2026** notified by MHA (22 June) — organises permitted NGO activities into 5 categories (social, economic, educational, cultural, religious); bars proselytisation.
- **National Statistical Commission reconstituted** — **Dr Saibal Chattopadhyay** (ex-IIM Calcutta Director) named Chairperson; NSC is a **non-statutory, non-constitutional** advisory body under MoSPI (created by executive resolution, 2005).
- **University of Liverpool gets UGC nod for a Bengaluru campus** — Under the UGC Foreign HEI Regulations, 2023 (regulator = UGC, not AICTE); part of NEP 2020's internationalisation push.
- **India's social-protection coverage** — Rose from 19% (2015) to **64.3% (2025)** — ~94 crore people; India ranks **2nd globally** by beneficiary count (per ILO, showcased at the 114th ILC, Geneva).
- **MeitY temporarily blocked Telegram** (16–22 June) under **Section 69A, IT Act** — to protect the NEET-UG 2026 re-exam from message-editing-based fake "leak" scams; ~150 million Indian users affected.
- **Census 2027** — India's first fully digital census; Maharashtra completed Phase-I Houselisting (16 May–14 June); reference dates: **1 Oct 2026** (snow-bound areas), **1 March 2027** (rest of country).
- **Digital India completes 11 years** — UPI now ~81% of India's digital payments and ~49% of global real-time payment volume; JAM-enabled DBT crossed ₹51 lakh crore; Aadhaar 144+ crore enrolments.
- **Samvidhan Hatya Diwas** (25 June) — Marks the 1975 Emergency (Article 352, "internal disturbance"); the 44th Amendment (1978) replaced this ground with "armed rebellion" and barred suspension of Articles 20 & 21 during Emergency.
- **Draft National Food Security (Amendment) Bill, 2026** — Proposes shifting Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) entitlement from a flat **35 kg/household** to **7 kg/person (capped at 35 kg/household)** — a per-capita redesign with equity implications for small households.
- **19th National Statistics Day** (29 June) — "75 Years of the National Sample Survey"; honours Prof. P.C. Mahalanobis, founder of ISI Kolkata.
- **NCRB launches four digital-policing apps** — NCRB-Abhigyan (fingerprint match via smartphone, ~1.3 crore database), CrPI (facial/iris/DNA, operationalising the Criminal Procedure Identification Act 2022), e-Forensics 2.0, e-Prosecution 2.0 — building a fingerprint-to-conviction ICJS chain.
- **Haryana–Rajasthan MoU + Kishau Multipurpose Project consensus** — Operationalises the 1994 Upper Yamuna River Board Agreement; six states (HP, Uttarakhand, Delhi, UP, Haryana, Rajasthan) reached consensus on the **₹15,000-crore, 236-m dam / 660 MW** Kishau project on the Tons River, ending an 8-year deadlock.
