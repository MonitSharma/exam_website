*Ancient & Medieval History · Purusha Sukta and varna*

**4. (d)** — Rudradaman's Sanskrit inscription at Junagadh (Girnar) credits Pushyagupta, governor of Chandragupta Maurya, with the lake, making it a key source for Mauryan provincial rule in western India.  
*Ancient & Medieval History · Junagadh inscription and Sudarshana lake*
