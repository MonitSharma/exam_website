## 2. Science & Technology

**India's nuclear pathway laid out — the month's biggest S&T thread.** Current fleet **24 reactors / 8.78 GW**; 9 units (7.5 GW) under construction. **PFBR** (500 MWe, MOX, IGCAR Kalpakkam, ~90% indigenous) achieved **first criticality April 2026**. Target **100 GW nuclear by 2047** (restated from the Red Fort). **SHANTI Act, 2025**, in force **21 Dec 2025**, opens the sector to Indian private companies — but "company" **excludes foreign-incorporated entities** (no FDI route). SMRs: **₹20,000 crore**, **BSMR-200**, **SMR-55**, an HTGR for hydrogen, ≥5 indigenous SMRs by 2033. *Three-stage recall:* PHWR/natural U → **FBR on Pu-239, breeding Th-232 → U-233** → Th-232/U-233 thermal breeders. **Th-232 is fertile; U-233 is fissile.**

**NavIC can no longer provide standalone navigation — only 3 operational satellites left** (≥4 needed for independent PNT). NavIC = operational name of **IRNSS**; designed as **3 GEO + 4 GSO**; coverage **India + 1,500 km**; SPS (civil) and RS (encrypted); NVS-03 launch-ready.

## 3. Environment

**India crosses 300 GW of non-fossil installed capacity (31 July 2026) — 60% of the 2030 goal.** **300.50 GW** of ~552 GW total → **>54%** of installed capacity. Break-up: **Solar 164.59 · Wind 58.14 · Hydro 57.24 · Bio 11.75 · Nuclear 8.78 GW** (note: wind is *second*, narrowly ahead of hydro). Record FY26 addition **55.29 GW**. *Panchamrit (COP26):* 500 GW non-fossil by 2030, 50% energy from RE, 1 bn t emission cut, 45% emissions-intensity cut, **net zero 2070**.

**Environmental Performance Index 2026 — India 176th of 177, score 22.46.** Yale **YCELP** + Columbia **CIESIN**, biennial. Ahead only of **Laos (177th)**. Weakest: **air quality and biodiversity, 174th on both** (air quality actually *declined*). Strongest: **Climate Change, 130th, +13.98 points**. Rank history: 141 (2016) → 177 (2018) → 168 (2020) → 180 (2022) → 176 (2024) → **176 (2026)**.

**Two Cabinet schemes.** **GOBARdhan — National Circular Bioenergy Scheme, ₹23,731 crore** (FY27–FY36, MoPNG): administered CBG price **₹2,110/MMBTU** on a 10-year framework; **CBG Blending Obligation 3% → 4% → 5% (from FY29)** on CGD entities; capital assistance up to ₹2 crore/TPD. *Why it exists:* SATAT (2018) targeted 5,000 CBG plants by 2023; only ~132 by June 2026. **Pradhan Mantri Surya Sarovar Yojana — ₹5,070 crore, ~5,000 MW floating solar** with **≥2 hours storage (~10,000 MWh)**; CFA **₹1 crore/MW post-commissioning**; NISE puts India's FSPV potential at **~102.18 GWp**.

**Two big Supreme Court environment rulings.** (i) **The 2021 Office Memorandum on ex post facto environmental clearances is quashed** — prior EC is mandatory under the **EIA Notification, 2006**; ECs already granted survive unless individually challenged; pegs Articles 14 and 21. (ii) ***Amravati Municipal Corporation v. Ganesh Dadarao Anasane*, 2026 INSC 796** — environmental compensation cannot be left to discretion; MoEF&CC to frame guidelines under **Rule 17(2), Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026**; compensation is **restitutionary**, in addition to penalty, and must be proportional with a rational nexus.

**Species & biodiversity.** **ZSI completes India's first national Catalogue of Lepidoptera — 13,703 species** (1,417 butterflies + 12,286 moths) = ~8.25% of world total, 22 volumes over 12+ years, final volume in *Zootaxa*. **Asiatic lion population 523 (2015) → 674 (2020) → 891 (2025)**; 2025 census 10–13 May over ~35,000 sq km, 58 talukas, 11 Gujarat districts; **a majority now live outside protected areas**. **Clouded Leopard Conservation Action Plan** — species enters the **Species Recovery Programme** under IDWH; cleared at the **91st SC-NBWL**; State Animal of Meghalaya. **First release of captive-bred slender-billed vultures** (5 slender-billed + 5 white-rumped) from the VCBC, Rani, Assam — diclofenac banned for veterinary use **2006**; white-rumped declined ~99% between 1992 and 2007. **Snow leopard roundtable, Moscow** — IBCA's first bilateral outing; *SPAI 2024: **718** snow leopards — Ladakh 477, Uttarakhand 124, HP 51, Arunachal 36, Sikkim 21, J&K 9.* **216 Star Tortoises** released at **Tadoba–Andhari** under Project STRIPES (*Geochelone elegans*, IUCN Vulnerable, **CITES Appendix I since CoP18, 2019**). **Black-necked crane** (*Grus nigricollis*, only alpine crane, State Bird of Ladakh, **<100 in India**) — 2,950+ feral dogs sterilised in Ladakh.

**New species from the month:** *Apristurus drona* (**Arabian Slender Catshark**, off Kollam, 400–650 m); *Gangaichthys indonepalicus* — **first subterranean fish from the Gangetic Basin**, from Bihar borewells, first stygobitic Chaudhuriidae anywhere; *Doronicum sanctum* — Leopard's bane, first record of the genus from **Northeast India**, Tawang, ~4,086 m, CSIR–IHBT Palampur.

Beat the RBI's ~7% projection. *Three methodology points that matter more than the number:* **base year 2022-23** (new series released 27 Feb 2026); quarterly GDP uses the **benchmark-indicator method**, not independent measurement; manufacturing now uses **double deflation**.

**RBI holds repo at 5.25% (MPC, 3–5 Aug, Governor Sanjay Malhotra).** SDF **5.00%**, MSF & Bank Rate **5.50%**, stance **Neutral**, decision unanimous. **FY27 GDP raised to 6.7%**; **FY27 CPI cut to 5.0%** (Q3 peak 5.9%). Target **4% ± 2%** under **Sec. 45ZA, RBI Act, 1934**; MPC = 6 members, Governor has the casting vote.

**Prices and output.** **July CPI 4.45%** (June 4.38%); **CFPI 5.52%**; rural 4.84% / urban 3.96%; series base **2024 = 100** (weights from **HCES 2022-23**). **IIP steady at 124.8 in July** — manufacturing weight **76.062**, base 2022-23; mining fell sharply (metallic minerals 141.9 → 102.3). **Index of Core Industries July: +5.4%** — iron ore **+29.5%**, cement +13.1%, electricity +9.0%; fertilisers **−8.0%**, crude −5.3%. **The Monsoon Session's economic legislation.** **MMDR (Amendment) Bill, 2026** — the **50% cap on sale from captive mines is removed**; **NMET → NMEDT** (exploration *and development*); no extra payment to add **critical/strategic minerals** (lithium, graphite, nickel, cobalt, gold, silver) to a lease; deep-seated minerals (**below 200 m**) get lease-area expansion of **30% (composite licence) / 10% (mining lease)**. *Anchors:* **30 critical minerals** identified; **MSP joined 2023**; **NCMM — ₹34,300 crore, seven years**. **MSMED (Amendment) Bill, 2026** — **TReDS becomes mandatory for CPSEs**; Udyam registration voluntary; mediation in 90 days; decriminalisation with graded penalties. **Bankers' Books Evidence Bill, 2026** repeals the **1891** Act — electronic/cloud records admissible. **Taxation and Other Laws (Amendment) Bill, 2026** amends the Income-tax Act 2025, Finance Act 2026 and **Payment and Settlement Systems Act 2007** — opens the legal door to **MDR on UPI**; electronics tax holiday extended to 2040-41; **FIIs and BIS exempted** on G-sec interest and capital gains.

**Manufacturing and infrastructure schemes.** **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme — ₹62,500 crore, FY27–FY31** (MeitY): 2.25–5% incentive; **5% + 3% R&D** for Indian brands; aims to lift domestic value addition from **~23% to 35–40%**. **'Samudra Manthan' National Offshore Exploration Scheme — ₹84,084 crore** to FY31, targeting **>600 MMTOE** reserve accretion. **Container Manufacturing Assistance Scheme — ₹10,000 crore** over five years, ~**7.5 lakh TEUs** of domestic capacity. **REPM Scheme** — **20 bids** received; ₹7,280 crore; **6,000 MTPA** across five beneficiaries. **CCEA clears ₹13,041 crore** of rail and highway projects (₹9,450 cr rail across 410 km + ₹3,590 cr NH).

## 5. Polity & Governance

**Monsoon Session 2026 adjourned *sine die* on 13 August — 12 Bills passed by both Houses.** 19 sittings over 25 days; productivity **Lok Sabha ~19%, Rajya Sabha ~39%**; **two Bills replaced Ordinances** (SC Number of Judges; Income-Tax Amendment); the **FCRA (Amendment) Bill, 2026 was referred to a Joint Committee**, not passed.

**Supreme Court strength rises from 34 to 38.** The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act, 2026 takes judges *other than the CJI* from **33 to 37**; replaces the **Ordinance of 16 May 2026**, with retrospective effect from that date. Trigger: a letter from **CJI Surya Kant** dated 11 May 2026. *Constitutional hook:* **Article 124(1)** — strength is fixed by ordinary law, not the Constitution. Progression: **8 (1950) → 11 → 14 → 18 → 26 → 31 → 34 (2019) → 38 (2026)**.

**Kerala becomes "Keralam."** The Kerala (Alteration of Name) Bill, 2026 under **Article 3** — needs (i) prior **Presidential recommendation** to introduce, and (ii) reference to the State Legislature, **whose views are recommendatory, not binding**. **Article 4** — First and Fourth Schedule changes are **not** Article 368 amendments. *Precedents:* Madras→Tamil Nadu (1969), Mysore→Karnataka (1973), Uttaranchal→Uttarakhand and Pondicherry→Puducherry (2006), Orissa→Odisha (2011).

**Tribunals Reforms Act, 2026 — a National Tribunals Commission at last.** Repeals the 2021 Act; jurisdiction of tribunals unchanged. **NTC = 5 members** (chairperson who has been an SC Judge or HC Chief Justice + 2 judicial + 2 technical members with ≥25 years' experience); tenure **5 years or age 70**; chairperson and judicial members appointed **after consultation with the CJI**; NTC runs selection, performance review, complaints and a **National Tribunals Data Grid**. Tribunal member tenure: 5 years or **70 (chairperson) / 67 (member)**.

**Census 2027 — Ladakh becomes the first place in independent India to enumerate caste in a statutory Census.** Ladakh sequence: **self-enumeration 17–31 Aug 2026 → house-to-house PE 1–30 Sept 2026 → revisional round 1–5 Oct 2026**; reference date **00:00 hrs, 1 October 2026** for snow-bound areas (Ladakh, J&K, HP, Uttarakhand) versus **1 March 2027** for the rest of India. **Caste column is open-ended for non-SC/ST respondents.** First **digital** Census; PE schedule has ~40 questions; ₹4,102 crore released for Phase 2. *Contrast:* **SECC 2011 was outside the Census Act, 1948** and produced 46 lakh+ unusable caste names; the last full caste enumeration in a regular Census was **1931**. Constitutional hook: **Entry 69, Union List**.

**Nine-judge Bench on "industry" — *State of U.P. v. Jai Bir Singh*, 2026 INSC 897 (20 Aug, 5:4, CJI Surya Kant presiding).** Revisits *Bangalore Water Supply v. A. Rajappa* (1978, seven judges, Sec. 2(j) of the ID Act). The **triple test** — systematic activity; employer–employee cooperation; production/distribution of goods or services — is **left undisturbed for pending ID Act disputes**; the reformulated test is **prospective** and to be read with **Sec. 2(p) of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020** (in force November 2025). Nagarathna J. dissented with three colleagues.

**Other judicial developments.** Bar-practice requirement for civil judges cut from **three years to one** (2:1, CJI Surya Kant; hybrid 1 year Bar + 1 year academy + 1 year clerkship; transitional window for exams notified 25 May 2025 – 31 March 2027). **Judicial officers are not government servants** — states cannot block a 60→62 retirement age on cost grounds (*All India Judges Association*); recall **Art. 233/234/235**. **Ladakh bench of the J&K and Ladakh High Court** approved by Cabinet — the third seat after Srinagar and Jammu; a *bench* is an executive/Presidential-notification route, a new *High Court* needs Parliament. **SC directs Karnataka to comply with CWMA on Cauvery releases** — Art. 262, ISRWD Act 1956, CWMA + CWRC under the **Cauvery Water Management Scheme, 2018**; measuring point **Biligundlu**. **SC refuses to move the SIR base year for Sikkim** (Art. 324; RPA 1950; **Art. 371F**). **SC moves toward a clarification Bench on the OBC creamy layer** (*Union of India v. Rohith Nathan*, 11 Mar 2026) — 1993 DoPT OM, income ceiling **₹1 lakh (1993) → ₹8 lakh (2017)**, **excluding salary and agricultural income**.

**Also:** **Vande Mataram gets statutory protection** — the Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Act, 2026 extends **Section 3** of the **1971 Act** (which had covered only the National Anthem) to the National Song; punishment up to **3 years**. **Registration of Births and Deaths (Amendment) Bill, 2026** — new two-tier delay regime: **1–2 years → DM/SDM approval; beyond 2 years → magistrate's order**. *Entry 30, Concurrent List.* **'Shakti ki Sapt Dhara'** — the Red Fort's seven-stream Viksit Bharat framework: manufacturing, agriculture & food processing, technology & innovation, Gati-Shakti, Raksha Shakti, green & blue economy, soft power. **Nilekani task force on public examination reform** — consultation to 13 September 2026; distinguish from the **2024 K. Radhakrishnan committee** on NTA. **PM CARES Fund** publishes FY24/FY25 accounts — corpus **₹8,452.07 crore**, FY25 spend **₹87.85 lakh**; audited by an independent CA, **not the CAG**. **CPGRAMS** — 15.2 lakh grievances (Jan–15 Jul 2026); average disposal **13 days**, from **157 days in 2014**; redressal timeline cut 30 → 21 days.

## 6. Geography (Places in News)

**Nauru is now the Republic of Naoero.** Constitution (Amendment) (No. 2) Bill 2026, passed May 2026, **no referendum**; country code **NRU → NRO**; citizens **dei-Naoero**. **Micronesia**, Central Pacific, **raised coral atoll** just south of the Equator; population **~12,000** (3rd-smallest); currency **Australian Dollar**; independence **1968**; **no official capital** — offices at **Yaren**; the textbook **phosphate/resource-curse** case.

**219 hanging glaciers mapped in the Alaknanda basin — the hazard India was not counting.** **71.7 ± 3.5 km²**; ice volume **2.39 ± 0.42 km³**, of which unstable hanging mass **0.74 ± 0.14 km³**; mean slope **~33°**; typology **117 ramp-slab, 99 terrace-slab, 3 terrace-wedge**; simulated **Badrinath–Mana** avalanche flows could exceed **50 m** in height; exposed population projected to rise from ~**380 to 8,500 by 2030**. *Definition:* a hanging glacier terminates abruptly at a cliff and loses mass by **mechanical detachment**, not surface melt. *Static:* Alaknanda rises from **Satopanth and Bhagirath Kharak** glaciers; **Panch Prayag** downstream — **Vishnuprayag (Dhauliganga), Nandprayag (Nandakini), Karnaprayag (Pindar), Rudraprayag (Mandakini), Devprayag (Bhagirathi)**.

**Ladakh's glaciers are slowing down, not just shrinking.** *The Cryosphere* (2026) on 12 Zanskar glaciers: **deceleration of 2.43 m yr⁻¹ per decade, 1992–2023**; thinning accelerated from ~0.22 to ~0.57 m yr⁻¹. Mechanism: mass loss → reduced driving stress → deceleration. *Contrast with the **Karakoram Anomaly**.* Zanskar is the largest Indus tributary in Ladakh, joining at **Nimmu**.

**A barrier lake in Tibet breaches; the Bhote Koshi–Trishuli valley evacuated (28 Aug).** *Trap:* the **Rasuwa Bhote Koshi is in the Gandaki basin, not the Koshi basin** — it becomes the **Trishuli**, joins the Kali Gandaki at **Devghat** to form the **Narayani**, and enters Bihar as the **Gandak**. Districts: Rasuwa, Nuwakot, Dhading; **Rasuwagadhi–Kerung (Gyirong)** trade point. **India has no water treaty with China** — only two hydrological-data MoUs (Brahmaputra and Sutlej).

**India standardises 27 place names in Arunachal Pradesh** on the Survey of India map (MHA, 7 Aug) — land areas, passes, a lake and a monument, including **Long Ju, Thag La ridge, Jaswant Garh, Sambho Sarovar**. China's renaming batches: 2017 (6), 2021 (15), 2023 (11), 2024 (30), 2025 (27). **Survey of India, est. 1767**, under DST, is the sole authority on India's external boundaries.

**El Niño and the monsoon.** **INCOIS puts El Niño persistence at 70–90% to February 2027**; **IMD's second-stage LRF cuts the monsoon to 90% of LPA (±4%)** from 92% (±5%). *Perspective:* **16 El Niño years since 1950, only 7 below-normal monsoons**; a positive **IOD** can offset it. *Map the institutions:* **IMD — New Delhi; INCOIS — Hyderabad; IITM — Pune; NCMRWF — Noida.**

**Also:** **The Subansiri rises without rain** — largest, **right-bank (north-bank)** tributary of the Brahmaputra; **trans-Himalayan and antecedent**; ~442 km; the "Gold River"; **Lower Subansiri HEP (2,000 MW, Gerukamukh, NHPC)** will be India's largest hydro plant. **Maharashtra earthquake swarm** — 29 tremors in 16 days, largest **M 4.3 at Nashik (8 Aug)**, depth ~5 km; Nashik and Palghar are **Seismic Zone III**; India has zones **II–V only (no Zone I)**. **Total solar eclipse of 12 August 2026** — Greenland, Iceland, northern Spain; **not visible in India**; corona **>1,000,000 °C** vs photosphere ~5,500 °C; **helium was discovered during the 1868 eclipse seen from India**. **Varsha Ashok Aglawe becomes the first woman Director General of the GSI** (GSI est. **1851**, HQ **Kolkata**, Ministry of Mines).

## 8. Art & Culture

**Vande Mataram completes 150 years and is sung from the Red Fort ramparts for the first time.** Written by **Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay, 7 November 1875**, in Sanskritised Bengali; included in ***Anandamath*** (serialised in *Bangadarshan*, 1881-82; book 1882; set against the **Sannyasi Rebellion**). First sung at the **1896 Calcutta Congress session by Rabindranath Tagore**; became the cry of the **1905 Swadeshi/anti-Partition agitation**. **1937** — after Tagore's objection, the CWC resolved that only the **first two stanzas** be sung. **24 January 1950** — Rajendra Prasad declared it would be **honoured equally with Jana Gana Mana**. (Now also statutorily protected — see Polity.)

**An 1,800-year-old Saraswati holding an arched harp reopens a lost chapter of Indian music.** Excavated in the 1980s at **Manasa, Neemuch district, MP**; Ujjain Museum; **2nd century CE, Malava era**. The **arched harp** is distinct from the later stick-zither veena. Chronology: **Pachmarhi (Nimbu Bhoj) rock paintings → Rigveda → Bharata's Natyashastra → Samudragupta's gold coinage**. The tradition survives in Myanmar as the **saung-gauk**.

**A 12th-century Veeragallu at Neeragunda may fix the death date of Hoysala king Vishnuvardhana.** Inscription date = **11 December 1141 CE**. Vishnuvardhana (originally **Bitti Deva**) defeated the Cholas at **Talakad (1116 CE)** and consecrated the **Chennakeshava Temple at Belur (1117 CE)**, originally *Vijaya Narayana*; influenced by **Ramanujacharya**. Hoysala capitals **Belur → Halebidu (Dwarasamudra)**; **Vesara** style with stellate plans, raised **jagati**, soapstone carving. **Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas (Belur, Halebidu, Somanathapura) — UNESCO World Heritage Site, 2023.** *Veeragallu/hero stones:* three panels — battle, ascent to heaven, worship before a deity.

**Srikalahasti seeks ₹114 crore under PRASHAD — the Vayu sthalam of the Pancha Bhoota five.** Tirupati district, on the **Swarnamukhi**; "Dakshina Kailasam"; Dravidian; inner temple 5th c., main structure 11th c. under **Rajendra Chola I**, **~120 m Rajagopuram** by the Vijayanagara empire in the 16th c. Cross-link: **Srikalahasti Kalamkari = pen/freehand**, versus **Machilipatnam = block-printed**. **PRASHAD** — Ministry of Tourism, Central Sector, launched 2014-15 as PRASAD, renamed **October 2017**.

**The Golconda "secret tunnel" is a granite chamber** — ~1.2 m × 1.8 m × 2.2 m deep, medieval red potsherds, verified by ASI Hyderabad Circle. **Golconda** from Telugu *Golla Konda*; Kakatiya mud fort rebuilt in granite by the **Qutb Shahis (1518–1687)**; **Hyderabad founded 1591** by Muhammad Quli Qutb Shah (Charminar); **annexed by Aurangzeb in 1687** (Bijapur fell 1686); famed for the **Kollur diamond mines**. UNESCO status: **Tentative List only**. ASI operates under the **AMASR Act, 1958**.

**Also:** **GI & Beyond 2.0 Summit** and the **Digital Handloom Atlas** launch; **12th National Handloom Day (7 August)**, commemorating the **Swadeshi Movement of 7 August 1905**; Sant Kabir + National Handloom Awards 2025 (22 awards). India's GI framework: **GI Act, 1999**, GI Registry **Chennai**, under **DPIIT**; first GI **Darjeeling Tea (2004-05)**; a GI is a **community-held right**, not an individual's.

## Answer Key

**Q1 — (d).** All three are correct: biennial Yale (YCELP) + Columbia (CIESIN) publication; India 176/177 with 22.46; worst on air quality and biodiversity (174th each), best decadal gain on Climate Change (+13.98 points).

**Q2 — (c) 38 including the CJI.** The Act raised judges *other than* the CJI from 33 to 37, i.e. 34 → 38 in total. Option (d) misstates the exclusion.

**Q3 — (b).** Glaw Lake, ~2.5 ha in Lohit district inside the Kamlang Tiger Reserve, is India's 101st Ramsar Site and Arunachal Pradesh's first. India's network of 101 is Asia's largest.

**Q4 — (d).** 300.50 GW of ~552 GW is >54%; wind (58.14 GW) is narrowly ahead of hydro (57.24 GW); nuclear contributes 8.78 GW.

**Q5 — (b) Article 3; recommendatory only.** Article 3 requires prior Presidential recommendation to introduce and reference to the State Legislature, but the Legislature's views do not bind Parliament. Article 4 makes the consequential First/Fourth Schedule changes non-Article 368 amendments.

**Q6 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Statement 3 is reversed: **no additional payment** is required to add critical and strategic minerals (lithium, graphite, nickel, cobalt, gold, silver) to an existing lease.

**Q7 — (c) Micronesia.** A raised coral atoll in the Central Pacific just south of the Equator; the world's third-smallest country by population; no official capital, with government offices at Yaren.

**Q8 — (a) 2 only.** Statement 1 is wrong — **Th-232 is fertile, not fissile**; it is converted to fissile U-233 in Stage II. Statement 3 is wrong — the SHANTI Act's definition of "company" **excludes** foreign-incorporated companies. The PFBR did attain first criticality in April 2026.

**Q9 — (a).** Adopted unanimously at the 14th BRICS Science, Technology and Innovation Ministerial Meeting at Chennai on 22 August 2026, under India's BRICS Chairship.

**Q10 — (a).** PAI 2.0 covered 2,59,867 Gram Panchayats across 33 States *and* UTs; the Ministry of Panchayati Raj released it; the largest group was grade C (1,23,719), and **no GP reached A+**.

**Q12 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** The judgment was **5:4**, with Justice Nagarathna and three colleagues dissenting — so statement 3 is wrong. The reformulated test operates prospectively and is to be read with Section 2(p) of the Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

**Q13 — (b).** ZSI (est. 1916, Kolkata, under MoEFCC) completed the 22-volume catalogue — 1,417 butterflies and 12,286 moths, about 8.25% of world Lepidoptera.

**Q14 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** *Doronicum sanctum* is a **Leopard's bane of the family Asteraceae** described from **Tawang, Arunachal Pradesh** at ~4,086 m — the genus's first record from Northeast India, not an orchid from the Western Ghats.

**Q15 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** Statement 2 is wrong: PM-VBRY has **Part A (first-time employees) and Part B (employer incentives for additional employment)**. The first-timer incentive equals one month's EPF wage capped at ₹15,000, in two instalments, the first after six months.

**Q16 — (b) Gandaki basin; the Gandak.** The classic trap: this Bhote Koshi becomes the **Trishuli**, joins the Kali Gandaki at **Devghat** to form the **Narayani**, and enters Bihar as the **Gandak**. A *different* Bhote Koshi, in Sindhupalchok, feeds the Sun Koshi.

**Q17 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Statement 3 is wrong: **SECC 2011 was conducted outside the Census Act, 1948** (by MoRD/MoHUA), which is precisely why its open-response caste data — 46 lakh+ names — proved unusable.

**Q18 — (b) Section 3, to the National Song.** Section 3 of the 1971 Act had covered only the National Anthem; Section 2 separately covers insult to the National Flag and the Constitution. Punishment: up to 3 years, or fine, or both.

**Q19 — (b) Belur, Halebidu and Somanathapura.** Hoysala Vesara architecture — stellate plans, raised *jagati* platform, soapstone carving. Belur's Chennakeshava Temple was consecrated by Vishnuvardhana in 1117 CE.
