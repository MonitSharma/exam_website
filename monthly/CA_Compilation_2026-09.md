# Monthly CA Compilation — September 2026

*Consolidated, de-duplicated revision doc for UPSC CSE 2027 · Built from Monit's own September daily files (30 CA editions + 30 PIB editions), organised by canonical subject. A topic that recurred across days appears once, at its latest status. One-sitting revision. Facts are drawn from the daily briefings, each web-checked when it was generated. Three gaps were filled by web search this run: August CPI, Rabi MSP 2027-28 and the final monsoon figure; these are marked [web]. GGGR and TTDI ranks, GEC-III, the 72nd National Film Awards, the Fed hike and the US sanctions Act were re-verified.*

**The five things September will be remembered for:** India's 18th BRICS Summit and the New Delhi Declaration; a US law threatening 100% tariffs on buyers of Russian oil; Semicon 2.0 alongside SEMICON India; the EPFO wage ceiling rising to ₹25,000 and the first Merchant Discount Rate (MDR) on UPI; and a deficient monsoon (87% of LPA) while inflation kept rising (CPI 4.82%, WPI 9.92%).

## 1. International Relations

**18th BRICS Summit, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi (12–13 Sep): the New Delhi Declaration.** The 140-point Declaration was adopted **unanimously on day one**. India's theme was *"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"*. This was India's **4th** chairship (after 2012, 2016 and 2021), with 350+ meetings. India proposed two mechanisms meant to outlast its chairship: a **BRICS Continuity and Implementation Mechanism** (run through the Troika) and a **Seafarers Emergency Support Network**. Other outcomes: an Expert Working Group on **Traditional, Complementary and Integrative Medicine (TCIM)**, endorsement of the **IBCA**, and a statement on AI governance. There was **no endorsement of a common BRICS currency**. **China chairs in 2027.** *Static:* BRICS has 11 members (Indonesia became the 11th in Jan 2025) and 10 partner countries. The first BRIC summit was at Yekaterinburg in 2009, not Rio. The NDB was agreed at Fortaleza in 2014 and is headquartered in Shanghai. The CRA is a $100 bn swap framework, not a pooled fund. *Follow-on:* the BRICS Theatre Festival is in New Delhi on 12–14 Oct, with the NSD as nodal agency.

**SCO: three tracks this month.**
1. **Bishkek Declaration** (26th Heads of State Council, 31 Aug–1 Sep). **English was recognised as an official and working language**, which India had backed, and 28 documents were approved. The 2026-27 chair passed to **Pakistan** under the theme "Turning Vision into Action: Connectivity, Innovation and Shared Prosperity". **Lahore** is the SCO Tourism & Cultural Capital for 2026-27.
2. India attended Pakistan's first SCO event as chair, the **Council of National Coordinators in Islamabad (28–30 Sep)**. This was not the Council of Foreign Ministers (CFM). The Heads of State summit is due in **Islamabad in Aug 2027**.
3. **25th SCO trade ministers' meeting, Dushanbe (17 Sep):** adopted the Action Plan 2026–30.

*Static:* Secretariat in Beijing, RATS in Tashkent; 10 members, the latest being Belarus (2024).

**US "Lindsey O. Graham Sanctioning Russia and Iran Act of 2026" (signed 18 Sep).** The Act authorises tariffs of **up to 100%** on the **five largest buyers of Russian oil or gas**. It gives the President authority to impose them but does not impose them automatically, and he keeps a waiver. It also extends Iran's energy and weapons sanctions by 5 years and targets Russia's "shadow fleet" of tankers. *Baseline:* the US already levies **50% tariffs on India**, 25 percentage points of them for Russian energy. *MEA reply:* India will protect energy security for **1.4 bn people** and keep diversified sourcing based on "evolving market dynamics". *Concepts:* primary vs **secondary sanctions**; extraterritoriality; CAATSA (2017); the G7 price cap.

**Trade agreements.**
- **India–New Zealand FTA enters into force on 20 Oct 2026** (signed 27 Apr 2026). India's exports get zero duty on 100% of NZ tariff lines, and India liberalises about 70% of its own lines. **Dairy is excluded.** Apples, kiwifruit and Manuka honey enter only through tariff-rate quotas (TRQ) with a minimum import price (MIP). NZ will facilitate **$20 bn of investment over 15 years**; this is India's 2nd FTA with an FDI pledge, after EFTA-TEPA ($100 bn). NZ also gives **5,000** temporary employment visas and 1,000 working-holiday visas a year.
- **India–EU FTA** (concluded 27 Jan 2026). On 11 Sep the Commission sent its signing proposals to the **Council of the EU**. The chain from here: Commission → Council authorises signature → European Parliament consents → entry into force. About 96% of EU goods exports to India get duty cuts. CBAM stays outside the FTA (definitive phase from 1 Jan 2026). *Trap:* European Council ≠ Council of the EU ≠ Council of Europe.
- **India–MERCOSUR (14 Sep).** Talks launched to expand the 2004 PTA (in force 2009, covering only **450/452 tariff lines**). A protocol gives **electronic Certificates of Origin** the same legal standing as paper ones. MERCOSUR is a customs union. Bolivia became a full member in 2024; Venezuela has been suspended since 2016.
- **ASEAN.** The 9th ASEAN–India agriculture ministerial (AIMMAF) in New Delhi (9 Sep) set a Medium-Term Plan of Action for 2026–30. At the 23rd ASEAN–India Economic Ministers' consultation in Manila, the AITIGA review was still pending; India–ASEAN trade was $128.38 bn in 2025-26.

**UNGA-81** (General Debate 22–28 Sep; President of the General Assembly: Khalilur Rahman of Bangladesh).
- **Jaishankar's statement (26 Sep):** the **Indus Waters Treaty stays in abeyance** until Pakistan "credibly and irrevocably" ends cross-border terrorism. India and **Liberia** co-launched a *Group of Friends on the Safety and Security of Shipping and Seafarers*. He described a "4F crisis" in the Global South: food, fuel, fertiliser and finance.
- **India's UNSC bid:** India is running for a non-permanent seat for **2028–29** against **Tajikistan**. The election is in June 2027 and needs two-thirds of members present and voting. India has served 8 previous terms.
- **First V4+India Foreign Ministers' meeting (22 Sep).** The V4 are Czechia, Hungary, Poland and Slovakia (formed 1991). Slovakia announced V4 support for India's bid for a *permanent* seat.
- **"Enemy state" clauses.** Japan's PM Sanae Takaichi asked for the Charter's **"enemy state" clauses (Arts 53, 77, 107)** to be deleted. The UNGA declared them obsolete in 1995, and the 2005 World Summit recorded the intent to delete them, but they are still in the text. Deleting them needs Art 108: a two-thirds vote plus ratification by all P5. China and Russia objected.
- *IWT static:* signed 1960 at Karachi by Nehru and Ayub Khan, with the World Bank as a signatory. The Eastern rivers (Ravi, Beas, Sutlej) go to India; the Western rivers (Indus, Jhelum, Chenab) go to Pakistan.

**Neighbourhood.**
- **China–Pakistan:** the MEA (16 Sep) rejected the new **Pakistan–China Boundary Joint Commission**. Under the 1963 Sino-Pak agreement, Pakistan ceded about **5,180 sq km of the Shaksgam Valley** to China; Art 6 of that agreement calls the settlement provisional. Don't confuse this with Aksai Chin.
- **Bhutan:** a **₹4,000 cr concessional LoC for the energy sector** (Jaishankar's visit, 16–18 Sep). Punatsangchhu-II (1,020 MW) has been inaugurated, and work on Punatsangchhu-I (1,200 MW) has resumed.
- **Nepal:** India approved export of up to **654 MW** of power until 31 Dec, after floods damaged Nepal's hydro plants. That is 600 MW over the Muzaffarpur–Dhalkebar 400 kV line plus 54 MW over the Tanakpur–Mahendranagar 132 kV line.
- **Belgium:** PM Bart De Wever's visit (2–4 Sep) was the first by a Belgian PM in about 20 years. Trade of $13.01 bn is to double in 5 years.
- **Vietnam:** defence ties move from exchanges to co-production; 2027 is the Year of Friendship (55 years of relations).

**Defence diplomacy and exercises.**
- **RAKSHA (3 Sep):** India's first 10-year defence-diplomacy roadmap. Its four pillars are strategic partnerships, capacity building, indigenous exports and maritime security (India as "net security provider").
- **Colombo Security Conclave table-top exercise (TTX)**, Visakhapatnam, 9–11 Sep. CSC members: India, Sri Lanka, Maldives, Mauritius, Bangladesh and Seychelles; Secretariat in Colombo.

## 2. Science & Technology

**Semiconductors: Semicon 2.0 notified, SEMICON India 2026 held.**
- **Semicon 2.0:** **₹1,27,500 crore**, approved by Cabinet in July 2026. Six pillars: fabs; ATMP/OSAT; equipment and materials; chip design and IP; R&D; talent.
- **Capex support:** **40%** for silicon fabs; 35% for compound-semiconductor, photonics, MEMS and display fabs and for advanced packaging; 25% for conventional packaging.
- **Semicon 1.0** (2021) had **₹76,000 cr**. It approved **12 projects worth ₹1.64 lakh cr across 6 States, and 3 are in commercial production**.
- **SEMICON India 2026:** 5th edition, Yashobhoomi, 17–19 Sep, theme "Silicon to Systems".
- **Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS):** 106 projects with ₹69,548 cr of approved investment, above the ₹59,350 cr target; 38 plants are already producing.
- India joined **Pax Silica**, a coalition to secure the full silicon supply chain, at the AI Impact Summit. The India Photonics Association was launched with 65 founding members.

**Space.**
- **GSLV-F17 / EOS-05 (GISAT-1A)**, launched 4 Sep: India's **first imaging satellite for geosynchronous orbit**. It was placed in a sub-GTO and will raise its own orbit. This was the 19th GSLV flight.
- **SCE-200 semi-cryogenic engine** (LOX + ISROSENE, a refined kerosene) reached **100% thrust (200 t) for the first time** on 5 Sep, at IPRC Mahendragiri, in the 9th power-head test (PHTA). It is meant to replace LVM3's L110 core stage and to power the Next Generation Launch Vehicle (NGLV).
- **ISRO's Advanced Radar Research Facility** at Chandrapur, Assam, will support **Project NETRA** for tracking space objects.
- **Private space funding:** the Technology Development Board (TDB) gave **₹200 cr to Agnikul** for the fully reusable **Agnibaan**, as optionally convertible debentures from the RDI fund, to take it from TRL-4 to TRL-8. It also gave **₹63.84 cr to GalaxEye**, whose *Drishti* is the world's first commercial satellite carrying both SAR and optical sensors ("OptoSAR").
- **Global:** Mercury has shrunk by up to about 23 km in diameter, about 30% more than MESSENGER-era estimates. ESA–JAXA's **BepiColombo** is due to enter Mercury orbit in Nov 2026.

**Defence technology.**
- **DAC approved ₹1.10 lakh cr of proposals (AoN, 7 Sep)**, about 98% from Indian industry. Items include CBRN reconnaissance vehicles, Arudhra radars, marine gas turbines and the DEFSAC smart-card system.
- **SAT-SAAW:** ₹810.79 cr contract with BDL for 160 units. The weapon was developed by DRDO's RCI; it is a 125-kg glide bomb with a range of about 100 km.
- **KAL:** a 1,000-km one-way attack drone built by a private firm, IG Defence, and tested at Pokhran.
- **SHIELD:** a DRDO request for proposals, under the Technology Development Fund (TDF), for an S-band (2–4 GHz) high-power microwave test rig.
- The TDF's first high-value deep-tech contract is for a **20 mK dilution refrigerator** (Zero mK India), a key tool for quantum computing.
- **VIMARSH:** DRDO will share software source code with licensee companies; 9 technology-transfer licences (LAToTs) were handed over.
- Steel was cut for the 2nd Next Generation Missile Vessel (NGMV) at Cochin Shipyard.

**Biotechnology and health technology.**
- **BIO-NIVESH:** a DBT/BIRAC platform connecting biotech start-ups with investors. **IBER 2026** puts India's **bioeconomy at $195.3 bn (2025), about 4.8% of GDP**, with a target of $300 bn by 2030.
- India's first **PLA bioplastic** facility: 100 TPA at Kumbhi, UP, with a ₹75 cr BIRAC grant, under BioE3.
- **SPROUT** at NIPGR, New Delhi: its SpeedSeed protocols cut chickpea's generation time to about 40 days.
- **First Badri calf born by OPU-IVF** (11 Sep, Pantnagar), carried by a Sahiwal surrogate cow. Badri is Uttarakhand's first registered cattle breed and its State Animal. OPU collects immature eggs without surgery; don't confuse it with MOET.
- **Stem-cell advisory** (MoHFW, 16 Sep): stem-cell therapy as standard care only for approved indications; **for autism, only within approved clinical trials**. The NMC treats violations as professional misconduct. The governing guidelines are the ICMR–DBT guidelines of 2017.
- The **Biovigilance Programme of India** was approved, covering adverse events in organ and tissue transplantation; it is run by the Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC). It sits alongside PvPI (drugs) and MvPI (medical devices). India is now **8th** in reports to WHO's VigiBase, up from 123rd.
- **HPV vaccination:** more than 80 lakh doses given (a single dose for girls aged 14, tracked on U-WIN).

**Digital and telecom.**
- **UPI number masking (4 Sep):** apps now show only the last 4 digits of a phone number.
- **DoT's Financial Fraud Risk Indicator (FRI)** has prevented ₹5,043.73 cr of suspected fraud since May 2025.
- **TRAI's spam rules (TCCCPR Third Amendment):** application-to-person (A2P) calls are now defined, AI/ML spam detection is mandatory, and action against a sender is triggered at **3 complaints instead of 5**.
- **TRAI's Digital Connectivity Rating platform** (25 Sep) rates buildings for indoor connectivity.
- India endorsed the **Call to Action for 6G Leadership and Security**. Under the Bharat 6G Vision, India aims for 10% of global 6G patents.
- **Data centres:** capacity grew from 375 MW (2020) to **1.57 GW (Aug 2026)** and is projected at about 8 GW by 2030.
- APNIC 62 was held in Mumbai, hosted by NIXI. **BHASHINI** is extending to Nepal, covering Nepali, Newari, Tamang and Gurung.

## 3. Environment

**Global climate and planetary reports.**
- **UNEP, "Limiting Overshoot" (2 Sep):** warming will cross 1.5 °C. If all NDCs and net-zero pledges are met, warming peaks at about **1.8 °C**; under current policies it reaches **2.6 °C** by 2100. The recommended path is to overshoot, peak and then bring temperatures back down.
- **Planetary Health Check 2026** (Potsdam Institute, 21 Sep): **7 of 9 boundaries are breached**. Only **stratospheric ozone** and **aerosol loading** are still safe. The report also sets out the first "food-system boundaries".
- **GCRMN, *Status of Coral Reefs of the World* (31 Aug):** data from 36,886 sites in 124 countries. Hard-coral cover is 9.5% below the 1980–2009 baseline, at **25.8% in 2024**. It fell 8.9% in 2023–24 alone. Mass bleaching now recurs every **4–6 years** instead of about every 10. The 4th global bleaching event ran from 2023 to 2025.
- **IEA Special Report on Electrification** (released at a New Delhi pre-COP meeting, 23 Sep): India's share of electricity in final energy use rises from **19% to 32% by 2035**. Türkiye, which holds the COP31 Presidency, proposes a voluntary benchmark of 35% by 2035. **COP31 will be held at Antalya.**
- **FAO, *Forests for Food* (15 Sep):** more than 50,000 wild species are used for food and medicine, and at least 10,000 are eaten directly. Forested watersheds supply water to more than 85% of major cities.

**India's climate and energy measures.**
- **Green Energy Corridor-III** (Cabinet, 30 Sep): **₹1,86,405 cr**, made up of ₹1,36,378 cr for intra-state transmission (InSTS) and **₹50,000 cr for 50 GWh of battery storage (BESS)**. It is the first phase of the scheme to include storage. Central support is ₹54,082 cr. It will evacuate **135 GW** of renewable power by FY33, with greenfield lines awarded by tariff-based bidding (TBCB).
- **Non-fossil share of installed capacity: 54.18%.** The NDC target of 50% has been met about 9 years early.
- **Net Zero Portal and NAPCC Dashboard** (World Ozone Day, 16 Sep): companies can voluntarily register net-zero targets and disclose emissions; Scope 3 reporting is voluntary.
- **Geothermal:** the first two wells were drilled at **Puga Valley, Ladakh** (ONGC Energy Centre, July 2026, 1,000 m deep) and will support a 1 MW demonstration plant. GSI estimates about 10.6 GW of potential from 381 hot springs in 10 geothermal provinces. MNRE notified the national policy in Sep 2025.
- **PM-KUSUM 2.0** will add about **10 GW of agrivoltaics** (solar panels raised above crops).
- **Small hydro:** 21 GW of potential, only 5.2 GW harnessed. A ₹2,584.60 cr scheme for 1,500 MW has been proposed.
- **Swachh Vayu Diwas (7 Sep):** of the 130 NCAP cities, 108 improved PM10 levels against 2017-18, 26 cut them by more than 40%, and **14 met the national air-quality standards (NAAQS)**. The cities have received ₹16,425 cr in performance-linked grants.
- **India's first soil-carbon payments:** ₹2.9 cr paid by DBT to **2,550** farmers in Punjab and Haryana (PAU Ludhiana, 17 Sep). The credits came from Grow Indigo's 'Aadi' programme under Verra's **VM0042** method. Agriculture is not an obligated sector under India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS), so these credits have no domestic compliance buyer.

**Wildlife.**
- **Project Rhino** was announced on World Rhino Day (22 Sep). The greater one-horned rhino is IUCN Vulnerable, in Schedule I and in CITES Appendix I. Its range states are Assam, West Bengal, UP and Bihar. India has 4,000+ rhinos, and Assam alone holds about 80% of the world population.
- **Project Cheetah: 52 cheetahs** (29 founders and 49 cubs born in India). They live in Kuno and Gandhi Sagar; Banni and Nauradehi are the next sites.
- **Great Indian Bustard:** a Gujarat Action Plan was released (Bhuj, 15 Sep) and a soft-release centre opened near **Naliya**. A "jump-start" method swaps infertile wild eggs for viable captive-bred ones.
- **Vultures** have vanished from about **72%** of their recorded nesting sites. The Pinjore breeding centre holds 356 birds; India has 9 vulture species. Veterinary use of diclofenac was banned in 2006; meloxicam is the safe alternative.
- **Management Effectiveness Evaluation of Tiger Reserves (MEE-TR) 2026:** the 6th cycle covers all **58 tiger reserves** and grades solid-waste management for the first time. Periyar topped the 2022 cycle.
- Pygmy hogs number 190+ (Mann Ki Baat). An IBCA–ADB MoU was signed on 2 Sep.
- **New finds:** *Buellia maharajensis*, a new rock-dwelling (saxicolous) lichen from Mathikettan Shola NP, Kerala. *Schizopygopsis waddellii*, a Tibetan snowtrout, was recorded in India for the **first time** (Pangkang Teng Tso lake, Tawang); it is a new record, not a new species.
- The **Himalayan monal** calls at a higher pitch in the noisy Chopta valley, to be heard over traffic (acoustic masking). The Noise Rules of 2000 do not cover protected areas.

**Land, waste and the circular economy.**
- **World Circular Economy Forum (WCEF) 2026, Gandhinagar (15–18 Sep):** the 10th edition and the first in South Asia, co-organised by Finland's Sitra and the CPCB. India now co-chairs the board of the UN's 10-Year Framework of Programmes on sustainable consumption and production (10YFP).
- **Legacy dumpsites:** 3.66 crore MT of old waste was remediated in 2025, and 459 urban local bodies fully cleared their dumpsites (SBM-U 2.0; DRAP launched Nov 2025). The SWM Rules 2026 have been in force since 1 Apr.
- **EIA amendment (22 Sep):** environmental clearances for ports, harbours and dredging now last **20 years instead of 10, extendable to 30**.
- **Eco-Sensitive Zones (ESZs)** are notified, but most Zonal Management Plans have never been written. Their legal basis is the Environment (Protection) Act 1986, not the Wildlife Act.
- **Western Ghats ESA:** the Karnataka Assembly rejected the 7th draft notification. Recall that Gadgil (2011) proposed the whole Ghats as sensitive, while Kasturirangan (2013) proposed about 37%.
- **PRAMAAN:** the first certificates were issued under India's own Forest and Wood Certification Scheme. It is run by IIFM Bhopal, audited by NABCB-accredited bodies, voluntary, and designed to help exporters meet the EU deforestation rules (EUDR).
- **Biodiversity:** at the 16th national meeting of State Biodiversity Boards, the target was set to make 2.76 lakh Biodiversity Management Committees functional. The NBA has shared ₹200+ cr in access-and-benefit-sharing funds, and India has filed its 7th National Report to the CBD.

**Ports and green shipping.**
- The three Green Hydrogen Hub ports — **Deendayal (Kandla), Paradip and V.O. Chidambaranar (Tuticorin)** — are building green-fuel bunkering hubs.
- **India's first port-based e-methanol plant** is coming up at Kandla: 150 tonnes per day, ₹2,300 cr, owned 76:24 by Deendayal Port Authority and Assam Petro-Chemicals.
- Four **mega ports** were notified under Sec 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025: Deendayal, JNPA, Paradip and **Mundra, the only private one**.

## 4. Economy

**Prices and growth.**
- **CPI inflation for Aug was 4.82%**, the 10th straight monthly rise (July: 4.45%); food inflation was about 5.95% [web].
- **WPI inflation for Aug was 9.92%**, driven by fuel and power at **22.93%** (base year 2022-23).
- The **Index of Core Industries (ICI)** grew 4.8% in Aug.
- **Index of Services Production (ISP)** (trial series, base 2024-25): 8 of 19 sub-sectors grew at double digits in June.
- **GST 2.0** completed one year of the two-slab (5%/18%) structure. Aug GST collections were ₹1.99 lakh cr (+14.8%).
- *(The OECD growth upgrade, the JCR rating and CBDT tax data are in the reports table in §9.)*

**External sector.**
- **Forex reserves hit a record $785.7 bn** (week ended 4 Sep), after a jump of $44.9 bn in a single week. India is now **4th** in the world, behind China, Japan and Switzerland, having passed Russia. Most of the inflow came through the FCNR(B) deposit swap window, so it is borrowed money: an asset for the RBI but a liability in the balance of payments.
- **The US Fed raised rates** (16 Sep) by 25 bp to **3.75–4.00%**, its first hike since 2023, under Chair Kevin Warsh. A US hike can pull capital out of India, weaken the rupee and make foreign borrowing (ECBs) costlier.
- **Edible-oil import duties cut** (24 Sep): crude sunflower to **0%**; crude palm and soybean from 10% to 5%; refined palm and soybean to 27.5%; refined sunflower to 22.5%.
- **Export paperwork:** shipments worth up to ₹3 lakh (FOB) no longer need a Registration-cum-Membership Certificate (RCMC).

**Money, banking and markets.**
- **UPI's first Merchant Discount Rate (MDR)** (notified 15 Sep): **0.4%, capped at ₹300**, on person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000. All person-to-person transfers, merchant payments up to ₹2,000, and small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh a month stay free, so about 96% of merchant payments are unaffected. UPI had been zero-MDR since 1 Jan 2020 (Sec 10A, Payment and Settlement Systems Act).
- **NSE co-location case closed:** the SC disposed of SEBI's appeals after a **₹1,491.21 cr** settlement (Sec 15JB, SEBI Act).
- **Tata Sons:** the **RBI refused to let it surrender its registration** as a Core Investment Company. As an "Upper Layer" NBFC under scale-based regulation (base, middle, upper and top layers), it must now list on the stock exchanges.
- **Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 2026** in force from **1 Oct**, replacing the 1891 Act.
- **ECLGS 5.0** (Emergency Credit Line Guarantee Scheme): up to ₹2.55 lakh cr of extra credit, with a 100% guarantee for MSMEs and cover for airlines, valid until 31 Mar 2027.
- **Government borrowing:** FY27 gross borrowing cut to **₹15.99 lakh cr**. H2 borrowing is ₹7.86 lakh cr, including ₹15,000 cr of sovereign green bonds; the RBI's ways-and-means advance (WMA) limit for H2 is ₹50,000 cr.
- **Also:** NFRA set up a 9-member committee on audit quality and technology. The Sahara refund portal reopened (₹9,267 cr paid to 41.56 lakh depositors). Sagarmala Finance Corp **withdrew** India's first blue bond (₹600 cr) after bids came in above its target rate.

**Labour and employment.**
- **EPFO wage ceiling raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000** (Cabinet 16 Sep, effective 17 Sep). Another 51 lakh+ workers come under EPF, EPS and EDLI, at a cost of about ₹11,339 cr a year.
- **PLFS, Aug:** unemployment **5.0%**; rural **4.1%** (a 7-month low); urban 6.8%; female LFPR 34.8%.
- **VB-G RAM G** (the law that replaced MGNREGA from 1 Jul; it guarantees 125 days of work): rural person-days fell **49.94%** year-on-year in July.
- **Shram Samvad:** a 90-day outreach on the four Labour Codes across 316 districts.
- **First district-level data:** estimates for unincorporated enterprises (ASUSE) now cover 757 districts, and district labour snapshots show Surat with the highest LFPR.

**Agriculture.**
- **Monsoon:** ended at **87% of LPA (about a 13% deficit)**, which IMD classes as "deficient" [web]. Kharif sowing stood at 1,086.31 lakh ha against 1,104 lakh ha last year; only pulses and jute were up. The crop-health index (NDVI) fell more than 30% in Jaisalmer, Bikaner, Barmer and Kutch.
- **Rabi MSP 2027-28** [web]: wheat **₹2,610** (+₹25); mustard ₹6,613 (+₹413); lentil (masur) ₹7,390 (+₹390); safflower ₹7,215 (+₹675, the largest rise).
- **Rabi Abhiyan:** 100% MSP procurement of tur, urad and masoor; ₹1,082 cr of RKVY sanctions.
- **PMMSY turns 6:** fish output **197.75 lakh t** (2024-25); exports ₹73,890 cr; ₹2,500 cr budget for FY27.
- **Dryland Congress:** produced the **Delhi Declaration on Drylands**; the ICAR–ICRISAT partnership turned 50.
- **Coal gasification scheme** (₹37,500 cr): Round 1 drew **7 bids**; the national target is 100 MT of gasification by 2030.
- **Sugar:** dealer stock limit 2,000 quintals (15 Sep–30 Nov). Bulk users may hold 30 days' stock instead of 15, but only if the extra is imported sugar.
- **Tobacco:** FCV tobacco growers in Andhra Pradesh get ₹50,000 per curing barn as interest-free working capital.
- **Jute:** MSP ₹5,925 per quintal (FY27). The Jute Packaging Materials Act requires jute bags for 100% of foodgrains and 20% of sugar.

**Industry and infrastructure.**
- **Make in India turns 12:** FDI of about $843 bn (2014–26); electronics output up from ₹1.9 lakh cr to **₹13.11 lakh cr**; defence production ₹1.78 lakh cr.
- **The Dedicated Freight Corridor is complete: 2,800 km** (8 Sep).
- **Modified UDAN:** MoUs signed with 22 States. The scheme has ₹28,840 cr for 100 airports and 200 helipads over FY27–FY36.
- **Cars:** for the first time, alternative-fuel cars outsold petrol cars (Aug: 41.95% vs 40.85%, FADA retail data).
- **DILRMP 3.0:** ₹565.50 cr for 2026–31; every land parcel gets a 14-digit ULPIN ("Bhu-Aadhaar").

## 5. Polity & Governance

**Supreme Court: the month's rulings.**
- **Article 142** was used to quash every FIR filed anywhere in India between 20 and 25 July against NEET-UG protesters, and to bar new ones. The Centre must frame a compensation policy for families of students who died by suicide within 3 months. *Only the SC has Art 142 powers;* High Courts cannot claim them under Art 226.
- **The Bar Council of India has no disciplinary power over law students** (arising from a NALSAR dispute). Its control begins only once a person enrols as an advocate under the Advocates Act, 1961.
- ***Hari Dutta Sharma v. State of UP*** (2026 INSC 998): a lender's night-time seizure of a truck without notice violated **Arts 14 and 21**. The Court awarded ₹10 lakh compensation and told the RBI to enforce its loan-recovery rules.
- ***Balaji Formalin*** (2026 INSC 1009): struck down Rules 18A and 18B of the Maharashtra Poisons Rules on methanol under **Arts 14 and 19(1)(g)**.
- **Malegaon Central election case:** saying a *dua* (prayer) is not an appeal to religion for votes under s.123(3) of the Representation of the People Act. Recall *Abhiram Singh* (2017, 4:3).
- ***Mulla Afroz v. UoI*** (29 Sep): an NSA detention cannot rest on a **confession made in police custody** alone (see s.23, Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam 2023). The Court quashed the order and imposed ₹10 lakh costs on UP.
- **CBSE three-language rule for Class 6:** the SC urged the Centre to defer it to 2027. Education is on the Concurrent List (Entry 25, moved there by the 42nd Amendment).
- **Vande Mataram** (T.M. Krishna's petition): the SC said orally that it expects no criminal consequences for someone who declines to sing it on religious grounds, and that *Bijoe Emmanuel* (1986) governs. It issued notice to the Union; this is not a final judgment.

**Judicial appointments.**
- The **Collegium (28 Sep)** recommended High Court Chief Justices **Sunita Agarwal, D.K. Upadhyaya and Aparesh Kumar Singh** for the SC. The Court's working strength is **34 of a sanctioned 38**, and would become 37 if all three are appointed. Justice Agarwal's appointment would put three women on the Court.
- **8 High Court Chief Justices were notified on 5 Sep**, so every High Court now has a regular (not acting) Chief Justice.
- 14 judicial officers were appointed to the Delhi, J&K-Ladakh, Jharkhand and Karnataka High Courts; 5 are women.

**Legislature, federalism and elections.**
- **FCRA (Amendment) Bill 2026:** its 31-member Joint Parliamentary Committee (21 Lok Sabha + 10 Rajya Sabha), chaired by Sanjay Jaiswal, has begun work. The main dispute is over a new "designated authority" that would take custody of an NGO's assets when its registration is cancelled. The report is due in the first week of the Winter Session.
- **Ladakh:** the MHA proposed a draft **Art 371(K)**, giving Ladakh an elected body with law-making powers over land, culture and forests. It would sit in Part XXI, modelled on Arts 371A (Nagaland) and 371G (Mizoram). Statehood and the Sixth Schedule were not conceded.
- **Kishau dam:** six States signed the agreement (15 Sep). The dam is on the **Tons**, 232.6 m high, and is a national project funded 90:10 by the Centre and States. Himachal's water share goes to Delhi and Rajasthan, which in turn pay for Himachal's share of the power component.
- **West Bengal's "anti-goonda" Bill:** PIB's Fact Check unit said reports that the President had returned it were wrong; it is still with the MHA. Revise Arts 200, 201 and 254(2).
- **Election Commission:** for "unmapped" voters in the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of rolls, BLOs will now collect documents at home. The ECINet system will be audited. SIR is complete in 20 States/UTs.
- **Registered unrecognised parties (RUPPs):** 2,800+ are registered but only about 750 contested in 2024. The ECI can de-register a party only for fraud, for renouncing allegiance to the Constitution, or if the party is declared unlawful.
- **V-Dem 2025:** India's Electoral Democracy Index is **0.38**, ranked 106/179, its lowest since 1975.

**Governance and internal security.**
- **National Commission for Safai Karamcharis (NCSK):** the 10th Commission is chaired by Bhagwat Prasad Makwana. It has had no statutory basis since 2004 and has been extended to 31 Mar 2028.
- **Forest rights:** a Tribal Affairs Ministry memo (31 Aug) says the Forest Rights Act does not require Gram Sabha consent at the Stage-II forest clearance. This is contested.
- **E-commerce Amendment Rules 2026** (in force 1 Jan 2027): a discount must show the "prior price", meaning the lowest price in the previous 30 days. Platforms must self-audit every year for dark patterns.
- **Jhiram Valley attack (2013):** an NIA court sentenced all 10 convicts to death (16 Sep).
- **Narcotics:** the 3rd conference of Anti-Narcotics Task Force heads adopted the Vision Document 2026–29, with a target of ending the drug trade by **31 Dec 2029**; Telangana's ANTF was rated best.
- **Counter-terrorism:** a conference on **PRAHAAR**, India's national counter-terrorism policy, launched an **Extradition Portal**. 288 fugitives have been returned from 36 countries (2019–26).
- **Operation Deep Manifest:** the DRI seized 362+ MT of Pakistan-origin dates shipped via Jebel Ali in the UAE, in breach of the DGFT ban of 2 May 2025.
- **Crypto:** FIU-IND issued notices to 15 offshore virtual-asset exchanges under s.13 of the PMLA.
- **LPG:** biometric Aadhaar authentication became mandatory for subsidised refills from **1 Oct** (89.9% of consumers done). Those who opt out can still buy unsubsidised 5 kg or 10 kg cylinders.
- **Vibrant Villages Programme:** VVP-I has ₹4,800 cr for 662 villages; VVP-II has ₹6,839 cr for 1,954 villages.
- **Also:** the 28th Western Zonal Council met in Panaji. CPGRAMS grievances now take 13 days on average to resolve. Special Campaign 6.0 runs 2–31 Oct, with e-waste as its focus. DISHA 2.0 covers Tele-Law and Nyaya Bandhu.

## 6. Geography (Places in News)

- **Larak Island and the Strait of Hormuz.** The US struck IRGC rocket launchers on Larak (30 Aug). Larak is an Iranian salt-dome island off Bandar Abbas, east of Qeshm. Hormuz is about 21 nautical miles wide at its narrowest. Qatar has no pipeline to bypass it; the bypasses are Saudi Arabia's Petroline to Yanbu and the UAE's Habshan–Fujairah line.
- **Perim Island and the Bab el-Mandeb.** The Houthis took Perim (11 Sep) and now hold Yemen's whole Red Sea coast. The strait is about 30 km wide and has no land bypass; the alternative is the Cape of Good Hope. It is the world's 3rd-busiest oil chokepoint, after Hormuz and Malacca.
- **Anak Krakatau** (Sunda Strait, Indonesia) erupted on 6 Sep, sending ash to about 50,000 ft and shutting 8 airports; it is on alert Level III of 4. In 2018 its flank collapsed and caused a tsunami with no earthquake warning. India's only active volcano is **Barren Island**.
- **Tigray, Ethiopia.** From 23 Sep the TPLF seized the Mekelle, Axum and Shire airports, and the 2022 Pretoria peace deal is fraying. Ethiopia has been landlocked since 1993 and has 6 neighbours. Lake Tana is the source of the Blue Nile, on which the GERD dam stands.
- **Boma–Badingilo Migratory Landscape** became South Sudan's first World Heritage Site (July 2026) and went straight onto the "in Danger" list. About 6 mn antelope migrate there, mostly white-eared kob — more than twice the Serengeti migration.
- **Monsoon 2026** ended at 87% of LPA. Withdrawal began around 19 Sep from west Rajasthan (normal date: 17 Sep) and reached Delhi on 23 Sep. The South Peninsula was hit hardest. A deep depression crossed the coast near **Kalingapatnam** (24 Sep). It stayed below 62 km/h, so the next name, "Arnab" (contributed by Bangladesh), was not used.
- **Vasundhara Tal (Chamoli)** is getting Uttarakhand's first glacial-lake early-warning system. The State has 13 high-risk glacial lakes: 6 in Pithoragarh and 4 in Chamoli.
- **Bakhira Lake** (Sant Kabir Nagar, UP; a Ramsar site since 2022): its sediments gave a 25,000-year monsoon record (BSIP).
- **Kalpasar (Gulf of Khambhat):** India and the Netherlands (Deltares, TU Delft) signed MoUs. The planned dam would run from Ghogha to Hansot, across a gulf with a tidal range above 10 m.
- **Bairabi–Sairang (Mizoram)** completed one year. The 51.38 km line is Aizawl's only rail link.
- **Gwalior** hosts India's first integrated Telecom Manufacturing Zone.
- **Vizhinjam (Kerala)** sent its first export cargo; its capacity is to rise to 5.7 mn TEU.
- **Ocean research:** the research vessel **ORV *Sagar Manthan*** was launched (built by GRSE for NCPOR, ₹840 cr). It will replace *Sagar Kanya*, which India bought in 1983. India holds an ISA exploration contract over 75,000 sq km in the Central Indian Ocean Basin and a second sulphide-exploration contract on the **Carlsberg Ridge**.
- *Also mapped elsewhere:* Puga Valley (§3), Shaksgam Valley (§1), the Tons and Kishau (§5).

## 8. Art & Culture

- **Gollala Gudi** (Palampet, Mulugu district, Telangana) was declared a **Monument of National Importance** (1 Sep). It is a Kakatiya-era temple with three shrines sharing one hall (*trikutalaya*). Telangana now has 10 ASI-protected monuments. Ramappa temple, a World Heritage Site since 2021, is in the same village.

  1. the Colonial Penal Settlement of the Andamans (Cellular Jail, Viper, Chatham and Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose islands);

  4. the Painted Haveli Towns of Shekhawati.

- **Gondhal** is India's Oscar entry. Gondhal is a Maharashtrian ritual folk performance honouring Renuka and Tulja Bhavani, played on the *sambal* drum and *tuntune* string instrument. It is the 4th Marathi film chosen; India has never won this category.
- **Bhupen Hazarika's centenary** (8 Sep): born at Sadiya in 1926; Bharat Ratna 2019; Dadasaheb Phalke Award 1992. The year-long celebrations end on 29 Nov with a ₹100 coin.
- **Rani Avantibai Lodhi** got a statue in Lucknow. She was queen-regent of Ramgarh (now Mandla/Dindori, MP) and fought in 1857. The British took over her estate through the Court of Wards, not the Doctrine of Lapse. She won the Battle of Kheri and was martyred on 20 Mar 1858.
- **Vinoba Bhave** (born 11 Sep 1895): Gandhi chose him as the first individual satyagrahi (1940). He began the Bhoodan movement at Pochampally on 18 Apr 1951 and won the first Magsaysay award (1958); Bharat Ratna 1983.
- **72nd National Film Awards** (22 Sep, Ekta Nagar, the first ceremony held outside Delhi): Best Feature Film *Article 370*; Dadasaheb Phalke Award **Anant Nag**; Best Actor shared by Mammootty and Kartik Aaryan; Best Actress Yami Gautam.
- **Language:** Karnataka made **Tulu** a second additional official language in Dakshina Kannada and Udupi; Tulu is not in the Eighth Schedule (22 languages). **Hindi Diwas** marks 14 Sep 1949, when Hindi was adopted under Art 343.
- **Grass Mache:** weed cleared from Dal Lake is being used in Kashmiri papier-mâché (a GI craft). Dal Lake is not a Ramsar site.
- **Also:** a stamp on Sant Malukdas (Bhakti poet-saint); the 17th Kathakali Festival at Kalakshetra (founded 1936); a working group on Sowa-Rigpa medicine (National Institute of Sowa-Rigpa, Leh). At the Central Silk Board's 77th foundation day, silk output was put at 42,000 MT, with a goal of becoming No. 1 in the world by 2028-29; Karnataka leads in production.

**Q5 — (b) Articles 53, 77 and 107.** The UNGA declared them obsolete in 1995 and the 2005 World Summit recorded the intent to delete them, but no formal amendment has happened. That needs Art 108: a two-thirds vote plus ratification by two-thirds of members, including all five permanent members.

**Q10 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** The greater one-horned rhino is **Vulnerable** on the IUCN Red List. It is also in Schedule I of the Wildlife Act and CITES Appendix I.
