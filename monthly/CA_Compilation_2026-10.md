## 2. Science & Technology

**Space.**
- **NVS-03 on GSLV-F18 (30 Oct, SDSC Sriharikota)** — 3rd 2nd-gen NavIC satellite, **20th GSLV flight**, ~2,232 kg; restores NavIC to the **4-satellite minimum** needed for a 3D fix. **NVS-02 (Jan 2025)** failed to reach orbit. NavIC: 7-satellite design (3 GEO + 4 IGSO), L1/L5/S-band, coverage ~1,500 km beyond India; SPS (civil) and RS (restricted).
- **ISRO plan:** 7 launches by Mar 2027 incl. first uncrewed **Gaganyaan (G1, carries Vyommitra)**; **Kulasekarapattinam** spaceport (SSLV) due 2026-27; SSLV tech transferred to **HAL**.
- **Gaganyaan:** **Crew Module–Service Module separation** validated (Service Module jettisoned before re-entry).
- **Aditya-L1 / VELC:** first **Doppler velocity images of the corona** (IIA Bengaluru). VELC is the largest of 7 payloads; halo orbit at **L1** since 6 Jan 2024.
- **TRISHNA** (ISRO–CNES thermal-infrared mission) in advanced realisation; CNES gives TIR payload; PSLV launch.
- **MOI-1A:** Indian startup's orbital AI-computing CubeSat on SpaceX Transporter-18.
- **Space economy:** ~$9 bn → **$40–45 bn in 7–8 years** (Jitendra Singh). **IN-SPACe "AI INSPIRED"** seed fund — up to ₹6 cr, ≤₹1 cr per grant (ss).
- **Martian analogues (DST):** **Neyveli** (melanterite), **Tiruchirappalli Badlands** and **Atacama** (gypsum).

**Quantum and cyber.**
- **QKD:** 5.56 km **free-space** link (QNu Labs + **BISAG-N**); **DRDO QKD on a live network at PXE Chandipur**. **TDB backs QNu Labs with ₹150 cr** (RDI Fund, optionally convertible debt) for quantum-secure and sensing tech.
- **RDI Fund deployments (TDB-DST):** QNu Labs ₹150 cr; **Manastu Space ₹116 cr** (hydrogen-peroxide **green propulsion**); **Peptris ₹86 cr** (AI drug discovery).
- **CERT-In advisory "AI Agents as Attack Vectors"** (15 Oct): autonomous agents reached live Indian systems; 180-day log retention advised. CERT-In = **Sec 70B, IT Act**, under MeitY.
- **eMudhra CA keys cracked** — browsers distrusted the certificates; CAs licensed by the **CCA** under the IT Act.
- **Cloud:** MeghRaj adopted by 2,330 departments; data-centre capacity 1.57 GW → ~8 GW by 2030 (ss); **NITI Data Centre Evaluation Framework** for States — 5 pillars, "not a regulatory layer".
