## 3. Revisit Plan (Pause & Consolidate)

Go back over these before the next fortnight's content stacks on top. This is a consolidation pass, not a test.

**Polity**
- Revise the timeline: Regulating Act 1773 → Pitt's India Act 1784 → Charter Acts → Government of India Acts 1919 & 1935 → Cabinet Mission 1946 → Constituent Assembly. Laxmikanth Ch1 hangs entirely on this sequence.
- Re-read the Objectives Resolution and how it maps onto the final Preamble — this is a classic cross-link (Ch2 to Ch3).
- Cross-link: which features were borrowed from which country (British, American, Irish, Canadian, etc.) — build or refresh a one-page borrowing table since this recurs constantly in PYQs.
- Note the salient features chapter (Ch3) side by side with Preamble amendments (42nd, 44th) — they get tested together.

**Economy**
- Consolidate the picture of the pre-1947 economy: stagnant agriculture, deindustrialization of handicrafts, one-way trade drain. Keep this as a contrast baseline — the post-1991 reforms (coming later in the roadmap) will only make sense against this.
- Note key thinkers associated with colonial-drain critique (Naoroji, Dutt) for quick recall.

**History**
- Revisit the sequence of Bengal's fall (Plassey → Buxar → Diwani of 1765) as one continuous narrative rather than three separate facts.
- Cross-link with Polity: the Regulating Act 1773 was a direct administrative response to the Company's post-Diwani expansion — tie the two subjects together here.
- Keep a running map of the early Anglo-Mysore and Anglo-Maratha engagements; these get elaborated in later fortnights (Spectrum Ch7–9), so a light skeleton now saves rework later.

**Geography**
- Redraw the earth's interior cross-section (crust/SIAL-SIMA, mantle, outer/inner core) from memory — this diagram recurs across NCERT and PYQs.
- Revisit continental drift evidence (jig-saw fit, fossil distribution, tillite deposits) alongside plate tectonics theory (Ch4) so the two theories don't blur together.
- PYQ angle: examiners like testing "evidence for" theories (drift, tectonics) as assertion-reason or statement-based questions — flag this pattern.

**Physics (optional)**
- Re-derive the Euler-Lagrange equation from Hamilton's principle at least once by hand, not just read it.
- Revisit the Legendre transformation connecting L to H, and re-derive Hamilton's canonical equations.
- Rework 2–3 standard problems: simple pendulum via Lagrangian, particle in a central potential in generalized coordinates, and a system with cyclic coordinates (conservation of conjugate momentum).
- Touch a couple of PYQs on generalized coordinates/degrees of freedom to keep the formalism concrete rather than purely symbolic.

### Answers

1. (b) — Dr. B.R. Ambedkar chaired the Drafting Committee.
2. (a) — Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution in December 1946.
3. (c) — The Cabinet Mission rejected Pakistan and proposed grouping provinces into sections A, B, C.
4. (c) — DPSP was borrowed from the Irish Constitution, not the USA.
5. (b) — 284 members are generally cited as having signed the final copy.
6. (b) — The 42nd Amendment (1976) added 'Socialist', 'Secular', and 'Integrity'.
7. (a) — Article 1 states "India, that is Bharat, shall be a Union of States."
8. (b) — M.N. Roy is credited with first proposing the idea in 1934.
9. (b) — Industrial growth was stagnant/very low, not high, on the eve of independence.
10. (d) — Both Naoroji and R.C. Dutt are associated with the drain of wealth critique.
11. (a) — Agriculture (primary sector) contributed the largest share.
12. (d) — The ISP uses 2024-25 as its base year.
13. (b) — The Big Bang Theory explains the origin of the universe.
14. (b) — SIMA (Silica + Magnesium) is the lower, basaltic layer of the crust.
15. (c) — Wegener cited all three as supporting evidence.
16. (b) — Oceanic-continental convergence produces a subduction zone/ocean trench.
17. (b) — Plassey (1757) was fought against Siraj-ud-Daulah.
18. (a) — Mughal emperor Shah Alam II granted the Diwani rights.
19. (d) — Tipu Sultan died in the Fourth Anglo-Mysore War (1799).
20. (b) — Vasco da Gama landed at Calicut in 1498.
21. (c) — Trade reached approximately $1.84 trillion in Jan-Mar 2026.
22. (b) — LWE-affected districts fell to 2 by 2026.
23. (b) — Bengaluru hosted the BRICS Heads of Space Agencies Meeting 2026, organised by ISRO/Dept of Space.
24. (b) — ULPIN/Bhu-Aadhaar is a unique 14-digit land parcel ID.
25. (b) — The roadmap centred on making the Indian Ocean Region an 'Ocean of Opportunity'.
26. (b) — Reform calls focus on representation for the Global South and emerging powers.
27. (b) — Demographic dividend refers to a large working-age share relative to dependents.
28. (a) — Green Revolution relied on HYV seeds, assured irrigation, and chemical fertilizers.
29. (b) — Tillite is a glacial deposit, cited as evidence of past glaciation/continental positioning.
30. (b) — The 2024-25 base year better reflects the current structure of the services economy.
