## 1. DRDO conducts three successful flight tests — multi-layered Ballistic Missile Defence + maiden NASM-MR

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Defence / DRDO

DRDO carried out three consecutive flight tests on 10–11 June 2026 (widely reported 13 June): two interceptions validating the **Phase-II multi-layered Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD)** architecture, plus the **maiden flight test of the Naval Anti-Ship Missile – Medium Range (NASM-MR)**. The target missile, mimicking an adversary ballistic missile, was launched from **LC-IV, Dhamra (Odisha)** and tracked by land-based weapon-system radars before being engaged. Why it matters: it places India among the small group of nations with operational-level BMD capability and advances indigenous naval strike weapons under Aatmanirbhar Bharat.

**Key facts to remember:** Phase-II BMD (exo- and endo-atmospheric interception); NASM-MR is an indigenous anti-ship missile for the Navy; test range LC-IV Dhamra, Odisha; congratulated by Defence Minister Rajnath Singh.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Tech / Defence & Internal Security.

## 2. 15th BRICS Agriculture Ministers' Meeting adopts the 'Indore Declaration'

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (under India's BRICS Presidency 2026)

On 13 June 2026, the **15th BRICS Agriculture Ministers' Meeting** at **Indore (Madhya Pradesh)**, chaired by Union Minister **Shivraj Singh Chouhan**, unanimously adopted the **farmer-centric 'Indore Declaration'** on food security, farmer welfare, climate-resilient agriculture and digital farming. Members agreed to strengthen the **BRICS Agricultural Research Platform**, transforming it into a "Knowledge-to-Action Hub", and to set up new cooperation networks (including on agro-ecology and digital agriculture). Why it matters: showcases India's agenda-setting role in BRICS and links to GS3 agriculture themes.

**Key facts to remember:** Venue Indore; India holds the 2026 BRICS chairmanship; declaration named after host city ("Indore Declaration"); BRICS accounts for roughly **42% of global agricultural land** and a similar share of world foodgrain production, and nearly half the world's population.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (BRICS); cross-link GS3 — Agriculture.

## 3. Union Cabinet / CCEA approvals — Ahmedabad Metro Phase-2(A) and Amaravati central office complex

**Issuing body:** Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) / Union Cabinet (10 June 2026)

The Cabinet approved **Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase-2(A)** — a **6.032 km** corridor from **Koteshwar Road to Ahmedabad Airport** (five stations: four elevated, one underground) at an estimated **₹2,169.04 crore** (including IDC). On operationalisation, the Ahmedabad–Gandhinagar network reaches **77.63 km**. Separately, the CCEA cleared construction of **Central Government office (CGGPOA) and residential (GPRA) complexes at Amaravati, Andhra Pradesh** at a combined **₹2,533 crore** (CGGPOA ₹1,299 cr + GPRA ₹1,235 cr), to house ~8,000 officials, executed by **MoHUA through CPWD**.

**Key facts to remember:** Ahmedabad Metro 2(A) = ₹2,169.04 cr, Koteshwar Rd–Airport; Amaravati complex = ₹2,533 cr via MoHUA/CPWD.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Infrastructure / Economy (urban transport, capital works).

## 4. BSNL–IIT Kanpur MoU for next-generation telecom

**Issuing body:** Department of Telecommunications / BSNL × IIT Kanpur

BSNL and **IIT Kanpur** signed an MoU to jointly develop, test and deploy indigenous telecom technologies, focused on **Direct-to-Mobile (D2M) broadcasting, indigenous 4G development, and advanced spectrum research**. Why it matters: feeds the "digital self-reliance" / Atmanirbhar Bharat narrative and is a likely prelims/MoU pairing question.

**Key facts to remember:** Partners — BSNL + IIT Kanpur; focus areas — D2M broadcasting, indigenous 4G, spectrum research.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (telecom, indigenisation).

## 5. Sangeet Natak Akademi — 7 Fellows and 108 Akademi Awards (2024 & 2025)

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Culture (Sangeet Natak Akademi)

The General Council of the **Sangeet Natak Akademi** — the National Academy of Music, Dance and Drama — elected **7 eminent personalities** as **Akademi Fellows (Akademi Ratna)** and selected **108 artists** for the **Sangeet Natak Akademi Awards (Akademi Puraskar)** for the years **2024 and 2025**, plus **106 young artists** (under 40) for the **Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar**. The Fellowships and Awards will be conferred by **President Droupadi Murmu**.

**Key facts to remember:** Sangeet Natak Akademi is under the **Ministry of Culture**; Fellowship = "Akademi Ratna" (most prestigious honour, limited numbers); Yuva Puraskar named after **Ustad Bismillah Khan**; Awards conferred by the President.

**Notebook:** GS1 — Art & Culture (institutions, honours).

## 6. Scheme update: Credit Guarantee Scheme for Micro Finance Institutions (CGSMFI-2.0) extended

This is the day's clearest **updated-scheme** item — exam template below.

- **Name:** Credit Guarantee Scheme for Microfinance Institutions – 2.0 (CGSMFI-2.0)
- **Ministry:** Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)
- **Objective:** Provide credit-guarantee cover that channels affordable funding to NBFC-MFIs/MFIs, sustaining last-mile microcredit to small borrowers.
- **Key features (update of June 2026):** (i) validity **extended to 31 August 2026** (or until guarantees worth **₹20,000 crore** are issued); (ii) maximum loan limit for **large NBFC-MFIs raised from ₹300 cr to ₹1,000 crore** (subject to 20% of AUM); (iii) guarantee fee **0.50% p.a.**; (iv) default cover tiered — **80% (small), 75% (medium), 70% (large)** institutions.
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** NBFC-MFIs and MFIs, and through them low-income microfinance borrowers.
- **Significance:** Eases liquidity stress in the microfinance sector and supports financial inclusion.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (financial inclusion, microfinance).

**Q1.** With reference to the recent (June 2026) DRDO flight tests, consider the following statements:
1. They validated the Phase-II multi-layered Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) system of India.
2. The tests included the maiden flight test of the Naval Anti-Ship Missile – Medium Range (NASM-MR).

(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

(b) 15th BRICS Agriculture Ministers' Meeting
(c) SCO Council of Heads of Government
(d) Quad Food Security Dialogue

1. It functions under the Union Ministry of Culture.
2. Its highest honour, the Fellowship, is designated as 'Akademi Ratna'.
3. The Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar is given by it to young artists.

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct: the June 2026 tests validated India's Phase-II multi-layered BMD and included the maiden NASM-MR flight test. Statement 3 is wrong — the target missile was launched from **LC-IV, Dhamra (Odisha)**, not Sriharikota.

**Q2 — (b).** The 'Indore Declaration' was adopted at the **15th BRICS Agriculture Ministers' Meeting** at Indore (Madhya Pradesh) under India's 2026 BRICS Presidency, chaired by Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan.

**Q3 — (d).** All three are correct. The Sangeet Natak Akademi (National Academy of Music, Dance and Drama) is under the Ministry of Culture; its Fellowship is the 'Akademi Ratna' (most prestigious honour); and it confers the Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar on young performing artists.
