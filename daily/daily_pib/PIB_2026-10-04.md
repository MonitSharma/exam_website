## 1. Ministry of Education (Department of Higher Education): **Five Centres of Excellence for Studies in Classical Languages approved** (3 Oct)
- **What:** The Department of Higher Education approved five Centres of Excellence (CoEs), one each for the languages given Classical status in October 2024. Press reports of the Office Memorandum (dated 1 Oct 2026) list: **Assamese – Tezpur University; Bengali – Visva-Bharati; Marathi – Deccan College PG & Research Institute (Pune); Pali – Central University of Odisha; Prakrit – Central University of Gujarat.** Mandate: advanced research, digitisation of linguistic/literary heritage, wider dissemination, and strengthening regional academic ecosystems.
- **Why it matters:** It operationalises the 2024 Classical-language recognition (India now has **11** classical languages) and complements existing CoEs for Classical Kannada, Telugu, Malayalam and Odia and the Central Institute of Classical Tamil. It is also an NEP 2020 / "Gyan Bharatam"-style heritage-preservation push.
- **Key facts:** Classical status granted by Union Cabinet in **Oct 2024** to Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali. Earlier classical languages: Tamil (2004), Sanskrit (2005), Telugu & Kannada (2008), Malayalam (2013), Odia (2014). Funding amounts not stated in sources available.
- **Notebook:** GS1 Art & Culture / Society (language heritage); GS2 Governance (education policy).

## 2. Ministry of Electronics & IT: **India's first 5.56 km free-space Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) link demonstrated** (3 Oct)
- **What:** PIB reports a 5.56 km free-space QKD link demonstrated by **QNu Labs** (Bengaluru quantum-security start-up) in collaboration with **BISAG-N** (Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geo-informatics). Further technical detail (location, key rate) was not retrievable.
- **Why it matters:** Free-space QKD sends quantum-secured keys through air (no optical fibre), enabling secure links to remote sites, between buildings, and — eventually — satellites. Eavesdropping disturbs the quantum state and is detected. It adds to India's quantum-communication milestones under the **National Quantum Mission (NQM)** (approved 2023, ₹6,003 crore, 2023-24 to 2030-31).
- **Key facts:** Free-space (not fibre) QKD; distance 5.56 km; industry–government institute partnership. Static link: QKD security rests on physics (no-cloning theorem), unlike classical encryption that rests on computational difficulty — hence "quantum-safe".
- **Notebook:** GS3 Science & Tech (quantum communication, cyber security).

## 3. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **Two male Great Indian Bustards released into the wild at Desert National Park, Jaisalmer** (4 Oct) *(UPDATE — the 3 Oct briefing covered the process being initiated; what is new is the actual first release of two captive-bred males.)*
- **What:** PIB: the Union Environment Minister released **two male** Great Indian Bustards (GIBs) into the wild at Desert National Park — the first actual release in the recovery programme, culminating about ten years of effort (Project GIB, 2016).
- **Why it matters:** Moves from ex-situ breeding to in-situ rewilding; post-release survival (power lines, predators, habitat loss) will decide if the programme works. See the 3 Oct note for the programme background (captive breeding since 2019, "jump-start" egg-swap technique, rewilding tunnel).
- **Static link:** GIB — IUCN *Critically Endangered*, Schedule I WPA 1972, CMS Appendix I, Rajasthan's State bird.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment (species conservation).

## 4. Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship: **PM-SETU completes one year — PIB Backgrounder "Transforming India's ITIs"** (3 Oct) *(UPDATE — scheme covered 8 Sep; the one-year stocktake figures could not be retrieved, so only verified scheme facts are given.)*
### Scheme-in-focus: **PM-SETU (Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs)**
- **Ministry:** MSDE. **Launched:** 4 Oct 2025 (Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi). **Outlay:** ₹60,000 crore.
- **Objective:** Modernise **1,000 government ITIs** into industry-aligned institutions.
- **Key features:** (i) **Hub-and-spoke** — 200 hub ITIs + 800 spoke ITIs (about 4 spokes per hub); (ii) hubs have innovation centres, trainer development, production units, placement support; (iii) **government-owned, industry-managed** with Anchor Industry Partners running clusters; (iv) co-financing by the **World Bank and Asian Development Bank**.
- **Significance:** Addresses skill-mismatch and youth employability; a model of industry-led public-institution governance. **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (skilling) / GS3 Economy (employment).

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following pairs of Classical language and the host of its newly approved Centre of Excellence (October 2026):
1. Pali — Central University of Odisha
2. Prakrit — Central University of Gujarat
3. Assamese — Visva-Bharati University

Which of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to Quantum Key Distribution (QKD), consider the following statements:
1. Free-space QKD transmits quantum-secured keys without requiring optical fibre.
2. Any eavesdropping attempt on a QKD channel disturbs the quantum state and can be detected.
3. QKD security depends on the computational difficulty of factoring large numbers.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** Under PM-SETU, which of the following is NOT a feature?
(a) Hub-and-spoke model with 200 hub and 800 spoke ITIs
(b) Government-owned, industry-managed clusters led by Anchor Industry Partners
(c) Co-financing by the World Bank and Asian Development Bank
(d) Establishment of 1,000 new private ITIs by industry

## Answers

1. **(a)** — Pali–Central University of Odisha and Prakrit–Central University of Gujarat are correct; the Assamese CoE is at Tezpur University (Visva-Bharati hosts Bengali).
2. **(a)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; QKD relies on quantum physics, not computational difficulty of factoring (that underpins RSA).
3. **(d)** — PM-SETU upgrades 1,000 existing *government* ITIs; it does not create private ITIs.
