## 1. Ministry of Home Affairs: **Conference on 'PRAHAAR – National CT Policy and Strategy' inaugurated; new Extradition Portal launched** (28 Sep)

**What it is:** Union Home Minister Amit Shah inaugurated a conference in New Delhi on India's counter-terrorism (CT) policy and strategy, built around the PRAHAAR framework. He called for an "automated" counter-terror response institutionalised down to police-station level and a technology-driven CT mechanism for the next decade, and launched an **Extradition Portal** to synergise the work of states and central agencies in bringing back fugitives from abroad. Attendees: Union Home Secretary, Foreign Secretary, CAPF chiefs, DGPs of 15 states and ATS chiefs.

**Why it matters:** It turns the "zero-tolerance" doctrine into a stated, multi-pillar strategy with state-level ownership — a direct GS3 Internal Security / GS2 federalism (police is a State subject) link.

**Key facts:**
- Seven pillars of PRAHAAR: **P**revention, **R**esponse, **A**ggregating internal capacities, **H**uman rights, **A**ttenuating conditions for terrorism, **A**ligning international efforts, **R**ecovery & resilience.
- Extradition figures cited: **288 fugitives returned from 36 countries (2019–2026)**.
- Also cited: 40+ modules of the Shahzad Bhatti network dismantled; 100+ FIRs and ~350 arrests across 15 states (cross-border weapons/narcotics smuggling).

**Notebook:** GS3 – Internal Security (terrorism, centre–state coordination); GS2 – IR (extradition).

## 2. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare: **National Agriculture Conference — Rabi Abhiyan 2026 begins at Pusa, New Delhi (28–29 Sep)**

**What it is:** The annual pre-season Centre–State conference to finalise the Rabi crop plan. Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan stressed **data-based planning and Centre–State partnership**, and named the **"water budget"** as the most critical parameter — states should assess irrigation availability and prefer **pulses and oilseeds** where water is limited. He flagged drought stress across roughly two-thirds of Maharashtra and parts of Karnataka; central assessment teams are to visit by **3 October**.

**Why it matters:** Sets input planning (seed, fertiliser, credit) for the Rabi season and pushes crop diversification away from water-guzzling cereals; also shows the drought-assessment machinery in action.

**Key facts:**
- Themes: self-reliance and diversification; sustainability and climate-friendly farming; digital agriculture, infrastructure, finance and risk management.
- Day 2 agenda: rainfall forecast, fertiliser availability, digital farming and **farmer registries**, pulses and oilseeds missions, institutional marketing.

**Notebook:** GS3 – Agriculture (cropping patterns, water use, food security, drought).

## 3. Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying (Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying): **Livestock Insurance Portal launched** (28 Sep)

**What it is:** Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh (Lalan Singh) launched a single digital platform for livestock insurance — policy issuance, claim processing, monitoring and reporting — a web-based workflow under the **National Livestock Mission (NLM)**. Available in **11 regional languages plus Hindi and English**.

**Why it matters:** Livestock is a key income buffer for small and marginal farmers; a digital, transparent claims process addresses low insurance penetration and delayed claim settlement.

**Key facts (as reported):** 60.8 lakh animals insured over the latest five-year period; ₹161.07 crore released in claims; RFID tagging being explored for animal identification and claim settlement.

### Scheme-in-focus: **Livestock Insurance Scheme (component of the National Livestock Mission)**
- **Ministry:** Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying (DAHD).
- **Objective:** Protect farmers from loss of animals to death (disease, accident, natural calamity) and demonstrate insurance benefits.
- **Key features:** Premium subsidy shared by Centre and States, with small/marginal farmers paying a reduced share (reported as ~15% of the premium; verify exact norms on the DAHD site); cover per household is capped by animal units; the new portal digitises issuance and claims; RFID/ear-tag identification being explored.
- **Beneficiaries:** Livestock farmers, especially small and marginal, across States/UTs.
- **Significance:** Risk management for the allied sector; supports doubling farm incomes and formalising rural credit and insurance.

**Notebook:** GS3 – Agriculture (allied sectors, farm risk management, technology in agriculture).

## 4. PMO / Prime Minister: **Mann Ki Baat, 138th episode (27 Sep) — Census progress, 10 years of the 2016 surgical strikes, pygmy hog, Modhera, nutrition**

**Key points:**
- **Census 2027 (first phase — houselisting):** data on **32 crore+ families** recorded; **2.6 crore+** used the self-enumeration portal (15–20 min, **16 languages**); PM framed participation as a civic responsibility.
- **10 years of the surgical strikes (28–29 Sep 2016)** after the Uri attack; linked to Balakot (2019) and Operation Sindoor (2025) under the "zero-tolerance" doctrine.
- **Pygmy hog conservation (Assam):** population reported at **190+**, alongside grassland habitat restoration.
- **Modhera (Gujarat):** highlighted as India's first 24×7 solar-powered village, alongside its Sun Temple heritage.
- **Nutrition (Poshan Maah):** ~9 crore beneficiaries on the **Poshan Tracker**; Chhattisgarh's "Har Ghar Munga"; Mizoram nutrition gardens; millet dishes competition in Gujarat.
- Others: Nasha Mukt Yuva Abhiyan; Pratapgarh (UP) women's SHG amla processing (~100 units); Sundarbans women planting mangroves; Ashok Singhal birth centenary; Ganesh worship in Thailand.

**Static link — Pygmy hog (*Porcula salvania*):** world's smallest wild pig; IUCN Critically Endangered; Schedule I, Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972; native to tall, wet grasslands of the Himalayan foothills in Assam (Manas NP is the natural stronghold); Pygmy Hog Conservation Programme (captive breeding since 1996, Assam Forest Department with partners) has reintroduced hogs into Assam grasslands such as Sonai Rupai and Orang.

**Notebook:** GS3 – Environment (species conservation), Internal Security; GS1/GS2 – Census, Society, Nutrition; GS3 – Energy (solar villages).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the 'PRAHAAR' counter-terrorism policy framework discussed at the conference chaired by the Union Home Minister, consider the following statements:
1. It is structured around seven pillars, including Prevention, Response and Recovery & Resilience.
2. Human Rights is one of its stated pillars.
3. It is a legally binding statute enacted by Parliament that overrides State police laws.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The Livestock Insurance Portal recently launched in New Delhi is associated with which of the following?
1. Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying.
2. A web-based workflow under the National Livestock Mission.
3. Service availability in 11 regional languages in addition to Hindi and English.

Select the correct answer using the code below:
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** With reference to the pygmy hog, recently highlighted in Mann Ki Baat, consider the following statements:
1. It is the smallest wild pig species in the world.
2. It is categorised as Critically Endangered on the IUCN Red List.
3. Its natural habitat is mangrove forests of the Sundarbans.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

1. **(a)** — PRAHAAR is a policy/strategy framework with seven pillars (Prevention, Response, Aggregating internal capacities, Human rights, Attenuating conditions for terrorism, Aligning international efforts, Recovery & resilience); it is not a statute, and police remains a State subject.
2. **(d)** — All three are correct: DAHD-led portal, a web workflow under NLM, 11 regional languages plus Hindi and English.
3. **(a)** — Pygmy hog is the world's smallest wild pig and is Critically Endangered; it lives in tall wet grasslands of the Assam Himalayan foothills, not Sundarbans mangroves.
