## 1. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: **JOSH Teams launched under TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan** (8 Oct, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi)
Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda launched youth-led "JOSH" teams (reported as *Joint Squad for Health*) to push TB prevention, early diagnosis, treatment completion and stigma reduction. Volunteers identify vulnerable people, encourage testing and support patients through treatment. The initiative was built with the Ministries of Youth Affairs & Sports and Defence.
- Phase 1: 55 districts in 11 States/UTs; remaining districts to follow (all 804 districts joined the launch virtually).
- 6,347 JOSH squads of 31,429 MY Bharat volunteers and 26,794 NCC cadets.
- Cited WHO-based figures: India's TB incidence down 21% (global 12%); treatment coverage 92% (global 78%). 67,933 Panchayats in 697 districts declared TB Mukt in 2026.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (Health) / GS2 Social Sector.

### Scheme-in-focus: **JOSH Teams (under TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan)**
- **Ministry:** Health & Family Welfare, with Youth Affairs & Sports and Defence (NCC).
- **Objective:** Community, youth-led support to end TB.
- **Features:** (1) Squads of MY Bharat volunteers and NCC cadets; (2) screening and testing drives for vulnerable groups; (3) treatment-adherence and nutrition support; (4) stigma reduction and healthy-habit promotion.
- **Coverage:** 55 districts / 11 States-UTs in phase 1, scaling nationwide.
- **Significance:** Uses youth and community capital to close the last-mile gap toward the TB elimination goal.

## 2. Ministry of Ayush: **Ayush Services Sector Report 2026 (study by RIS) released around Ayurveda Day** (reported 8 Oct)
First sector-level economic mapping of Ayush services.
- Sector size US$36.92 billion in FY2024-25; 16.91% CAGR since FY2022-23; projected US$216–318 billion by 2047.
- About 1.76 million livelihoods, over a million in local and standalone clinics.
- Women are 43% of the workforce; above 50% in several organised segments (spas/wellness resorts over 60%, educational institutes 58%, day-care centres 57%, hospitals 53%).
- **Why it matters:** Evidence base for Ayush as an employment and services-export driver and for women's labour participation.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy / GS1 Society (women's workforce).

## 3. Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation: **Strategy for Environmental-Economic Accounts 2026–2030** (7 Oct)
A non-statutory, "self-evolving" five-year roadmap issued by the NSO, successor to the 2022–26 strategy. Based on the UN SEEA Central Framework and the 2013 Green National Accounts report.
- Accounts: forests, minerals, soil, land, water, carbon stock, environment-related activities, biodiversity and environment statistics.
- EnviStats India shifts to biennial publication; 18 States/UTs have notified Inter-Departmental Groups (per a secondary summary); aligned with the Kunming–Montreal GBF.
- **Why it matters:** Natural capital accounting lets GDP reflect resource depletion, supporting "green GDP" style policy.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy + Environment.

## 4. Ministry of Electronics & IT (IndiaAI Mission): **World Bank's World Development Report 2026 — "The Promise of Artificial Intelligence" launched in India** (8 Oct, New Delhi)
Launched by Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw with the World Bank Group.
- Advice to developing countries: adopt, adapt, then advance, instead of building frontier models from scratch.
- Benefits depend on foundations: electricity, connectivity, compute, data, skills, institutions.
- Only 1 of 25 low-income countries has a national AI strategy.
- Secondary-summary figures (not checked against the report): about 4% of Indian jobs at immediate automation risk; AI talent pool ~50,460.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech / Economy; GS2 International Organisations (Reports).

## 5. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **India at the informal pre-COP31 ministerial in Fiji** (7 Oct) *(single secondary source)*
Co-hosted by Australia, Fiji and Tuvalu. India's line: judge COP31 (Antalya, Türkiye, November 2026) by implementation, not new targets; honour existing commitments "in true letter and spirit"; expand capacity-building and means of implementation.
- NDC (2022 update): 45% emissions-intensity cut vs 2005 by 2030; ~50% non-fossil installed capacity; extra 2.5–3 Gt CO₂e sink; net zero 2070.
- Under the COP30 arrangement Türkiye hosts and Australia leads negotiations; India has offered to host COP33 (2028).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment / GS2 IR.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the JOSH Teams launched in October 2026, consider:
1. They operate under the TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan.
2. Volunteers are drawn from MY Bharat and the NCC.
3. In phase 1 they cover all 804 districts.

Which are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The Strategy for Environmental-Economic Accounts 2026–2030 is issued by:
(a) NITI Aayog (b) Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change (c) National Statistics Office, MoSPI (d) Reserve Bank of India

**Q3.** The World Development Report 2026 on Artificial Intelligence is published by:
(a) IMF (b) World Bank Group (c) OECD (d) World Economic Forum

## Answers
1. **(b)** — JOSH is under TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan with MY Bharat volunteers and NCC cadets, but phase 1 covers only 55 districts in 11 States/UTs.
2. **(c)** — It is issued by the NSO under MoSPI, on the SEEA framework.
3. **(b)** — The WDR is the World Bank Group's flagship report; the 2026 edition is "The Promise of Artificial Intelligence".
