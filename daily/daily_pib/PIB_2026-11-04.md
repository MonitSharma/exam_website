## 1. Cabinet: Viability Gap Funding (VGF) for grid-scale Energy Storage — ₹14,500 crore, 92 GWh (4 Nov 2026) — GS3 Economy/Energy
**Ministry:** Power (implied; reports do not name it).
**What it is:** A VGF scheme for FY2026-27 to FY2030-31 to create 92 GWh of new grid-scale storage: ~30 GWh battery storage, ~60 GWh pumped storage and ~2 GWh emerging technologies. 
**Why it matters:** Storage firms up variable solar/wind, lets existing transmission lines be used more fully (less new transmission investment) and eases peak-demand management — key to the 500 GW non-fossil target. Links to the PSP backgrounder covered on 21 Oct (new: the funding approval).

## 2. Cabinet: Capex support scheme for Critical Mineral Processing — ₹2,814 crore (4 Nov 2026) — GS3 Economy/Sci-Tech/Mining
**Ministry:** Mines (implied; not named in reports).
**What it is:** Five-year scheme (FY27–FY31) giving a **30% capital subsidy** on eligible investment for domestic lithium and nickel processing.
**Key facts:** two lithium plants (min. 30,000 t each) and two nickel plants (min. 50,000 t each); cap of ₹500 crore per lithium and ₹900 crore per nickel beneficiary; cobalt recovered as by-product of nickel; two-stage selection (technical, then least-cost financial bid); incentive in three milestone-linked tranches. Vaishnaw described it as covering processing of 24 critical minerals (mined and recycled) — one report; verify.
**Why it matters:** Cuts import dependence in EV batteries, storage, electronics, defence and specialty steel; complements the National Critical Mineral Mission (processing/midstream gap).

## 3. Cabinet: VISTAR — Viksit Bharat Initiative for Statistical Transformation, Analytics & Research — ₹4,371.10 crore (4 Nov 2026) — GS3 Economy / GS2 Governance
**Ministry:** MoSPI (per Business Today).
**What it is:** Modernisation of India's official statistical system, FY2026-27 to FY2030-31.
**Key facts:** 59 surveys planned in the 16th Finance Commission cycle (vs 43 earlier); new indicators such as a Headline Index of Services Production and an Index of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises; 8th Economic Census covering non-farm establishments down to Gram Panchayat/ward level; a National Statistical Business Register; stronger state and district-level estimates.
**Why it matters:** Better data for policy, GDP/IIP revisions and cooperative federalism. (Distinct from Census 2027's ₹11,718.24 crore outlay.)

### Scheme-in-focus: VISTAR
- **Ministry:** MoSPI (National Statistical Office). **Period:** 2026-27 to 2030-31. **Outlay:** ₹4,371.10 crore.
- **Objective:** Reliable, timely, granular statistics aligned with Viksit Bharat 2047.
- **Features:** more surveys (59), new services/informal-sector indices, Economic Census-8, business register, validation/ground-truthing and state capacity building.
- **Beneficiaries:** policymakers, states/districts, researchers, businesses.
- **Significance:** Responds to data-quality concerns; supports evidence-based Finance Commission transfers.

## 4. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: India's Biosphere Reserves (International Day for Biosphere Reserves, 3 Nov) — GS3 Environment
**Facts (PIB summary):** 18 notified biosphere reserves, 13 in UNESCO's World Network; UNESCO MAB Programme launched 1971; first Indian reserve Nilgiri (1986); largest Great Rann of Kutch, smallest Dibru-Saikhowa; central scheme funded 60:40 Centre–State (90:10 for NE and Himalayan states); integrated five-year management plans piloted at Nanda Devi, Pachmarhi, Dehang-Dibang and Agasthyamalai.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Cabinet-approved capex support scheme for critical mineral processing (Nov 2026), consider:
1. It provides a 30% capital subsidy on eligible investment.
2. It covers lithium and nickel processing, with cobalt as a by-product.
3. Beneficiaries are selected through a nomination by the State governments.

Which are correct? (a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** VISTAR, approved by the Union Cabinet in November 2026, is associated with:
(a) Expansion of rural broadband (b) Modernisation of the official statistical system (c) Digital land records (d) Strengthening of cooperative banks

**Q3.** Regarding Biosphere Reserves in India, which is correct?
(a) India has 18 notified reserves, all in UNESCO's World Network (b) The Man and the Biosphere Programme was launched in 1971 (c) Dibru-Saikhowa is the largest by area (d) Central funding is 50:50 for all States

## Answers
1. **(a)** — Subsidy is 30%; lithium/nickel with cobalt by-product; selection is by technical qualification and least-cost bidding, not State nomination.
2. **(b)** — VISTAR is MoSPI's ₹4,371.10 crore statistical modernisation scheme (2026-27 to 2030-31).
3. **(b)** — MAB launched 1971; only 13 of 18 are in UNESCO's network, Great Rann of Kutch is largest, funding is 60:40 (90:10 for NE/Himalayan States).
