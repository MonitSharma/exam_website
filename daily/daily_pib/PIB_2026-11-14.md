## 1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: **Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada (1–15 Nov 2026) culminating in Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, 15 Nov** — GS1 Society / GS2 Governance

- **What:** Fortnight-long nationwide observance ending on Birsa Munda's birth anniversary (15 Nov). The day was designated Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas in 2021 to honour tribal freedom fighters.
- **Why it matters:** Showcases delivery to Scheduled Tribe communities and two digital tools: the **Jan-HIT portal** (tribal health data) and the **National FRA Digital Platform** (supports claims under the Forest Rights Act, 2006).
- **Key facts:** 300+ projects (228 inaugurations, 74 foundation stones); 160+ state-level activities; National Tribal Literary Festival, New Delhi (12–13 Nov); ~200 EMRS students met the President (12 Nov); Rani Durgavati Adivasi Mahila Sammelan with 500 tribal women entrepreneurs from 30 States/UTs (14 Nov).
- **Notebook:** GS1 Society (tribal heroes), GS2 Governance (FRA, ST welfare).

## 2. Ministry of Defence / Indian Navy: **Sea Vigil-26 — 5th Pan-India Coastal Defence Exercise (11–19 Nov 2026)** — GS3 Internal Security / Maritime Security

- **What:** Biennial apex-level exercise coordinated by the Indian Navy to test coastal defence and maritime security preparedness across the entire coastline and EEZ.
- **Why it matters:** Conceived in 2018 to validate measures strengthened after the 26/11 Mumbai attacks; involves all coastal states/UTs plus fishing and coastal communities — a whole-of-nation coastal security test.
- **Key facts:** Phase 1 — Coastal Defence and Security Readiness Evaluation (11–17 Nov); Phase 2 — Sea Phase (18–19 Nov).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (coastal security architecture).

## 3. Department of Pharmaceuticals (Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers): **PLI scheme for Medical Devices extended to 2035-36 with ~₹2,700 crore; fresh applications invited** (reported 10–11 Nov 2026) — GS3 Economy / Industry / Health

- **What:** The PLI scheme for medical devices (launched 2020-21) is extended by eight years with a revised outlay of about ₹2,700 crore, eligibility norms eased, and a new online application portal.
- **Why it matters:** Reduces import dependence in medtech and deepens domestic manufacturing of critical components and advanced devices.
- **Key facts:** Four segments — (i) consumables, disposables and implants; (ii) critical components; (iii) advanced devices with limited domestic manufacturing; (iv) high-end equipment currently only assembled in India. Average incentive ≈5% / 9% / 6% by segment (reported); minimum sales threshold for claiming incentives dropped; minimum investment lowered (earlier ₹180 crore; new figure not confirmed); three years to set up production. Original scheme reportedly supported 60+ devices/components and >₹16,000 crore output.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (industrial policy, PLI), Health.

## 4. Ministry of External Affairs: **Inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum, New Delhi (20–22 Nov 2026)** — invitations confirmed by MEA spokesperson, 13 Nov *(single secondary source; verify)* — GS2 International Relations

- **What:** India-proposed forum on cultural exchange and people-to-people ties across Eurasia; all 10 SCO members (including Pakistan) invited, plus observers and dialogue partners.
- **Why it matters:** Soft-power / civilisational diplomacy within the SCO; India's first such forum while Pakistan holds the SCO Council of Heads of State chair (2026–27).
- **Key facts:** SCO members: Belarus, China, India, Iran, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Pakistan, Russia, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan (India full member since 2017). Observers include Afghanistan and Mongolia; 15 dialogue partners.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (regional groupings).

### Scheme-in-focus: PLI Scheme for Medical Devices (PLI-MD 2.0)

- **Name / Ministry:** PLI Scheme for Promoting Domestic Manufacturing of Medical Devices — Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers.
- **Objective:** Build domestic medtech manufacturing capacity and cut import dependence.
- **Key features:** (1) Extended to FY 2035-36, outlay ≈₹2,700 crore; (2) four target segments from consumables to high-end equipment; (3) incentive on incremental sales, with minimum sales threshold removed and investment threshold lowered; (4) three-year gestation to set up plants; online portal for fresh applications.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Domestic and foreign medical-device manufacturers setting up in India.
- **Significance:** Part of the broader PLI architecture (pharma, bulk drugs, medical devices) under Atmanirbhar Bharat.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to Exercise Sea Vigil-26, consider the statements:
1. It is the fifth edition of the Pan-India Coastal Defence Exercise coordinated by the Indian Navy.
2. It covers the entire coastline and Exclusive Economic Zone, with all coastal states and UTs participating.
3. It is conducted exclusively by the Indian Coast Guard.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) scheme for medical devices, consider the statements:
1. It is administered by the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
2. It has been extended up to 2035-36 with an outlay of about ₹2,700 crore.
3. The revised scheme retains a minimum sales threshold for claiming incentives.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** With reference to Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, consider the statements:
1. It is observed on 15 November, the birth anniversary of Birsa Munda, and was designated in 2021.
2. Birsa Munda is associated with the Ulgulan (Munda rebellion).
3. The National FRA Digital Platform is run by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

1. **(b)** — Sea Vigil-26 is the 5th edition, Navy-coordinated, spanning the whole coastline/EEZ with all coastal states/UTs; it is not Coast Guard-exclusive.
2. **(a)** — Administered by DoP and extended to 2035-36 (~₹2,700 crore); the minimum sales threshold has been dropped.
3. **(b)** — Designated in 2021 for 15 Nov; Birsa Munda led the Ulgulan; the FRA platform is supported by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs, the FRA nodal ministry, not MoEFCC.
