## 1. Department of Space / ISRO (PMO congratulatory release, 31 Oct): **GSLV-F18 launches NVS-03 — third satellite of the next-generation NavIC series** — GS3 Sci-Tech

**What / why it matters:** The PMO congratulated ISRO scientists on the successful launch of GSLV-F18 carrying NVS-03 (PIB headline, 31 Oct; launch was scheduled for the night of 30 Oct from Sriharikota). NVS satellites replace ageing IRNSS satellites to keep India's regional navigation system (NavIC) alive, which matters for strategic autonomy in position-navigation-timing (PNT) and defence/civil dependence on GPS.

**Key facts:**
- GSLV-F18 is the 20th flight of the GSLV; launched from the Second Launch Pad, Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota; places the satellite in Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit (ISRO mission page).
- NVS-03 carries an indigenous atomic clock plus procured atomic clocks, and adds an **L1 signal** to the existing L5 and S-band signals; coverage is India plus ~1,500 km beyond its boundary (ISRO).
- Context (secondary report, WION): NVS-02 (Jan 2025) failed to reach its operational orbit and only about 3 of 11 NavIC satellites are performing core PNT; at least four are needed for core services.
- Mass ~2,250 kg (pre-launch report).

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (Space), GS3 Internal Security (strategic autonomy).

## 2. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare / PMO: **24th PM-KISAN instalment released from Bhildi, Banaskantha (30 Oct)** *(UPDATE — PM-KISAN covered earlier; new is the 24th instalment and the Gujarat package)* — GS3 Agriculture

**What / why it matters:** At the national "PM Kisan Utsav Diwas" event, the PM released ₹18,680+ crore to 9.34 crore farmer families (pre-event reports; Gujarat share ₹1,051 crore for 52.33 lakh families). DBT-based income support remains the Centre's flagship farm-income transfer.

**Key facts:**
- Across 23 earlier instalments, over 11 crore families received more than ₹4.47 lakh crore (report).
- Same visit (PMO programme note): Bharat Bovine Breeding & Research Centre (127 acres; first facility for Kankrej breed) at Bhildi; two Banas Dairy Bio-CNG plants (75 and 100 tonnes dung/day); **521 completed Agriculture Infrastructure Fund projects (~₹4,950 crore)**; 12 horticulture clusters (~1.46 lakh ha); 4,030 new child-friendly Anganwadi centres (₹800 crore programme); Ahmedabad Metro Phase-2A/2B; SH-41 widening.

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture; GS2 Governance (DBT, cooperatives/dairy).

### Scheme-in-focus: **PM-KISAN (Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi)**
- **Ministry:** Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare. **Objective:** supplementary income support to farmer families.
- **Features:** Central Sector scheme (100% Central funding); ₹6,000 a year in three equal instalments of ₹2,000; transferred by DBT to bank accounts; launched Feb 2019 (details from standing knowledge, not from today's releases).
- **Coverage:** ~9.34 crore families in the 24th instalment.
- **Significance:** income stability, Agri-Stack/Farmer ID linkage (11 crore Farmer IDs reported 24 Oct).

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to ISRO's GSLV-F18/NVS-03 mission, consider the following statements:
1. NVS-03 adds an L1 signal to the existing L5 and S-band signals of the navigation system.
2. The satellite carries an indigenous atomic clock along with procured atomic clocks.
3. The mission was launched from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the PM-KISAN scheme, which one of the following is correct?
(a) It is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme funded in a 60:40 ratio between the Centre and States.
(b) It is a Central Sector scheme providing ₹6,000 a year to eligible farmer families in three equal instalments through DBT.
(c) It provides interest-free crop loans up to ₹3 lakh through cooperative banks.
(d) It provides ₹6,000 per month as pension to farmers above 60 years.

**Q3.** Consider the following statements:
1. Rashtriya Ekta Diwas is observed on 31 October to mark the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel.
2. The Delhi Declaration adopted at the ARISE Cities Forum 2026 is intended to be carried to COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye.
3. The International Tourism Mart held at Tawang is organised by the Ministry of Culture.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

1. **(d)** — All three are correct per ISRO's mission page: L1 added to L5/S, indigenous plus procured atomic clocks, launched from Sriharikota.
2. **(b)** — PM-KISAN is 100% Centre-funded (Central Sector), ₹6,000/year in three ₹2,000 instalments via DBT; (a), (c), (d) describe other mechanisms (PM Kisan Maandhan is the pension scheme).
3. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; the International Tourism Mart is organised by the Ministry of Tourism, not Culture.
