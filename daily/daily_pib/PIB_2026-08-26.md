## 1. PM chairs the 53rd PRAGATI Meeting — six infrastructure projects worth over ₹30,000 crore reviewed

**Issuing body:** Prime Minister's Office / Cabinet Secretariat (PRAGATI platform) — 25 August 2026

**What it is:** PRAGATI (**Pro-Active Governance And Timely Implementation**) is the ICT-enabled, multi-modal platform through which the Prime Minister directly reviews stalled or delayed projects and public grievances with Union Secretaries and State Chief Secretaries on a single video-conference dashboard. The 53rd edition was held at Seva Teerth, New Delhi.

**Why it matters:** PRAGATI is the standard UPSC example of "cooperative federalism in execution" and of technology used for real-time monitoring — it fuses three functions in one meeting: grievance redressal, programme review, and project monitoring.

**Key facts:**

- **Six infrastructure projects** across the **Railway, Road and Power** sectors were reviewed, spanning **nine states**, with a cumulative cost of **more than ₹30,000 crore**.
- The PM stressed that delays do not merely escalate cost — they **postpone the benefits** intended for citizens, businesses and the economy.
- Direction to treat projects as an **integrated whole** rather than as isolated packages/stretches; ministries and states to adopt an **end-to-end approach** to commissioning.
- Call to explore **common utility corridors** for infrastructure services at the *planning stage* itself, subject to technical and economic feasibility.
- Two thematic reviews were also taken up:
  - **AgriStack under the Digital Agriculture Mission** — use AI and modern digital tools across the agricultural value chain, from input planning to processing, for better targeting and data-driven decisions.
  - **Cyber fraud, including "digital arrest" cases** — prevention through sustained training, awareness and education campaigns targeted at **senior citizens, youth and other vulnerable sections**, plus regular capacity building of enforcement personnel.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (e-governance, transparency and accountability, Centre–State coordination); overlaps GS3 (Infrastructure; Internal Security — cyber crime).

## 2. Scheme-in-focus: **e-Shram Portal completes five years** (26 August 2021 → 26 August 2026)

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Labour & Employment (PIB Backgrounder/Explainer, posted 25 August 2026)

**What it is:** e-Shram is India's **first Aadhaar-authenticated National Database of Unorganised Workers (NDUW)**, developed by the **National Informatics Centre (NIC)** and launched on 26 August 2021. Every registered worker gets a unique **12-digit Universal Account Number (UAN)**.

**Objective:** Enable convergence of welfare schemes across ministries, allow secure API-based data sharing for evidence-based policymaking, deliver **portability** of social security for migrant and construction workers, and create a national database usable for planning and crisis response.

**Key features (post-2024 "One-Stop Solution"):**

1. On **21 October 2024** e-Shram was converted from a registry into a **One-Stop Solution** integrating **15 social security and welfare schemes** — including **PM-SYM, PMSBY, PMJJBY, NSAP, ONORC and PMAY-G** — in line with the Union Budget 2024–25 vision of single-window labour services.
2. Direct enrolment into **PMSBY** (₹2 lakh for accidental death/permanent disability; ₹1 lakh for partial disability) and **PMJJBY** (₹2 lakh life cover, any cause) from the portal; **PM-SYM** offers a minimum assured pension of **₹3,000 per month** from age 60.
3. Linkage to the **National Career Service (NCS)** for jobs, **Skill India Digital Hub** for skilling/apprenticeship, and **myScheme** for eligibility discovery; construction workers' data is shared with State **BOCW Boards**.
4. **Multilingual functionality in 22 languages** introduced in 2025, powered by **Bhashini**.

**Beneficiaries / coverage (as on 18–20 August 2026):**

- **31.89 crore** total registrations.
- **Women 54.28%**, men 45.72% — a rare welfare database where women are the majority.
- Age: **55.21% aged 18–40**, 24.18% aged 40–50, 20.53% above 50, 0.08% aged 16–18.
- Top occupations: **agriculture, domestic & household work, construction, apparel**. Top states: **Uttar Pradesh, Bihar, West Bengal, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra**.
- **~11.5 lakh gig and platform workers** registered — the base for extending social security to platform work.
- **Eligibility:** any unorganised-sector worker aged **16+**, who is **not an income-tax payer and not a member of EPFO or ESIC**. Registration is **free**, one-time, via the portal, a **Common Service Centre (CSC)** or State Seva Kendra, using Aadhaar eKYC + mobile number.

**Significance:** The classic answer-writing example of **digital public infrastructure applied to informality** — it converts an invisible workforce into an addressable one, enabling targeted DBT, portability under ONORC-type schemes, and, prospectively, statutory social security for gig workers under the Code on Social Security, 2020.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance/Welfare schemes for vulnerable sections; GS3 — Economy (informal sector, employment, gig economy).

## 3. 36th Annual Conference of Vice-Chancellors of Agricultural Universities begins in New Delhi

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare / DARE–ICAR — 25 August 2026 (two-day conference, 25–26 August)

**What it is:** The annual conclave of Vice-Chancellors of State Agricultural Universities together with Directors of ICAR institutes and senior DARE/ICAR officials, inaugurated by Union Minister for Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare and Rural Development **Shri Shivraj Singh Chouhan**.

**Why it matters:** India's agricultural research and education runs on the **National Agricultural Research and Education System (NARES)** — ICAR (under the **Department of Agricultural Research and Education, DARE**) plus a network of State Agricultural Universities, deemed universities and Krishi Vigyan Kendras. Reform of this "lab-to-land" chain is a standing GS3 theme.

**Key facts:**

- **Theme:** *"Reimagining Agricultural Higher Education for Viksit Bharat @2047."*
- Structured around **five sessions covering 14 agenda items** — student-centric reforms, faculty development, institutional strengthening, quality enhancement, academic reform, research and innovation.
- The Minister's central message: **research must move from laboratories to fields** and deliver usable solutions to farmers; agricultural universities were asked to focus on **student employability and maintain placement/job data**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Agriculture (agricultural research and extension, NARES/ICAR); GS2 — Education/Governance (higher education reform).

## 4. India–Seychelles high-level bilateral meeting on fisheries, aquaculture and the blue economy

**Issuing body:** Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying — meeting held 25 August 2026, released 26 August

**What it is:** A high-level bilateral in New Delhi with a delegation from the **Republic of Seychelles** led by **H.E. Mr. Wallace Cosgrow, Minister of Fisheries, Agriculture and Blue Economy**; the Indian side was led by **Dr. Abhilaksh Likhi, Secretary, Department of Fisheries**.

**Why it matters:** Seychelles is a western Indian Ocean archipelago and a core partner in India's **MAHASAGAR / SAGAR** Indian Ocean outreach. Fisheries cooperation is the economic face of maritime diplomacy — it sits alongside hydrography, maritime domain awareness and capacity building in India's IOR toolkit.

**Key facts:**

- India shared that **national fish production has reached 19.7 million tonnes**, with **seafood exports valued at USD 8.46 billion**.
- Technical agenda: **mariculture (marine aquaculture)**, modern hatcheries, **tuna fisheries optimisation**, stock assessment, post-harvest engineering, and marine conservation.
- Digital/innovation agenda: fisheries **data systems**, **Monitoring, Control and Surveillance (MCS)** infrastructure, and use of **Artificial Intelligence in harvest management**.
- Seychelles expressed interest in a **bilateral MoU specifically on fisheries and aquaculture**, backed by an **advanced annual work plan**, institutionalised engagement, regulatory alignment, reciprocal laboratory access and joint pilot projects.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (India and its neighbourhood/IOR, bilateral agreements); GS3 — Economy (blue economy, fisheries).

## 5. IICT and Netflix partner to fund the next generation of Indian storytellers

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Information & Broadcasting — Indian Institute of Creative Technologies (IICT) — 26 August 2026

**What it is:** IICT, the Government's institute for the **AVGC-XR** (Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics and Extended Reality) sector, has tied up with Netflix to offer **scholarships to 100 students** with **up to 80% financial support** across **six professional programmes**.

**Why it matters:** This is the operational follow-through on the **creative economy** push — the AVGC sector is one of the Government's identified sunrise sectors, and the model here is public institution + private platform co-funding a skills pipeline rather than a pure subsidy.

**Key facts:**

- **Two one-year diplomas:** Interactive Comics & Sequential Arts; Visual Effects Mastery.
- **Four six-month certificates:** Virtual Art Department Content Creation; Media Tech Workflow; Art of Character Animation; E-Sports & Gaming Management.
- Open to students **and working professionals** across India.
- Flows from the **Netflix India Storytelling Initiative**, announced after the PM's meeting with Netflix Co-CEO **Ted Sarandos on 5 August 2026**.
- **Shri Chanchal Kumar**, Secretary, Ministry of I&B, is Chairman of the IICT Board.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (creative/orange economy, skill development, sunrise sectors); GS1 — Indian Society/Culture (soft power, storytelling).

## 6. ICAT issues India's first compliance certificate for a 15-metre multi-axle sleeper bus

**Issuing body:** International Centre for Automotive Technology (ICAT), under the Ministry of Heavy Industries — 26 August 2026

**What it is:** ICAT — one of India's designated vehicle testing and homologation agencies set up under the **NATRiP** programme — has issued the **first-ever compliance certificate** for a **15-metre multi-axle sleeper bus**, to **Synaty Automotive Pvt. Ltd.** The certification was granted under **AIS-119 and AIS-153**, read with the applicable provisions of the **Central Motor Vehicles Rules (CMVR)**.

**Why it matters:** 15 metres is among the **longest permissible bus-body configurations** in the passenger transport category in India. Certifying it domestically signals maturing indigenous homologation capability and supports higher-capacity, longer-haul inter-city road transport — relevant to both the *Make in India* automotive story and road-safety standard-setting.

**Key facts:**

- **AIS** = **Automotive Industry Standards**, framed by the Automotive Industry Standards Committee and notified under the CMVR, 1989.
- ICAT (Manesar) is one of the CMVR-notified test agencies alongside ARAI, CIRT, VRDE and others.
- The certificate was handed over by **Shri Saurabh Dalela**, Director, ICAT, to the manufacturer.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (manufacturing, standards and certification, infrastructure/transport).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the **e-Shram Portal**, consider the following statements:

1. It was launched in 2021 by the Ministry of Labour & Employment and created India's first Aadhaar-authenticated National Database of Unorganised Workers.
2. Every worker registered on the portal is issued a unique 12-digit Universal Account Number.
3. Membership of the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO) or the Employees' State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) is a pre-condition for registration on the portal.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about **India–Seychelles cooperation in fisheries and the blue economy**, as discussed at the recent high-level bilateral meeting:

1. Seychelles is an archipelagic state located in the western Indian Ocean.
2. The Indian side is led by the Department of Fisheries under the Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry and Dairying.
3. The agenda included Monitoring, Control and Surveillance (MCS) infrastructure and the use of Artificial Intelligence in harvest management.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** With reference to India's agricultural research and education system, consider the following statements:

1. The Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR) functions under the Department of Agricultural Research and Education (DARE) of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare.
2. State Agricultural Universities and Krishi Vigyan Kendras form part of the National Agricultural Research and Education System.
3. ICAR is a statutory body created by an Act of Parliament.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** e-Shram was launched on 26 August 2021 by the Ministry of Labour & Employment and issues a 12-digit UAN. Statement 3 is the inverse of the rule: a registrant must **not** be an EPFO/ESIC member or an income-tax payer.

**Q2 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** Seychelles is a western Indian Ocean archipelago; the Indian delegation was led by the Secretary, Department of Fisheries (MoFAH&D); and MCS infrastructure plus AI in harvest management were both on the agenda.

**Q3 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** ICAR is an **autonomous body registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860** — a registered society, not a statutory body created by an Act of Parliament — so statement 3 is incorrect. It functions under DARE, and SAUs and KVKs are part of NARES.
