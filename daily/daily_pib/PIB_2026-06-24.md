## 1. DISHA 2.0 — restructured "Access to Justice" scheme approved (₹255 crore, 2026–31)
**Issuing body:** Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice (approved by MoS I/C Arjun Ram Meghwal) | **Date:** 23–24 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2 — Polity & Governance (Access to Justice; DPSP)

**What & why it matters:** The government approved the continuation of the Central Sector Scheme **DISHA — "Designing Innovative Solutions for Holistic Access to Justice"** in a restructured form as **DISHA 2.0**, for five years (**1 April 2026 – 31 March 2031**, co-terminus with the XVI Finance Commission cycle). It is the umbrella vehicle for free legal aid, legal literacy and tele-law, and it operationalises the constitutional promise of access to justice. A live example for prelims (scheme + ministry + constitutional articles) and mains (welfare delivery, justice for the poor).

**New-scheme snapshot:**
- **Name / Ministry:** DISHA 2.0 — Department of Justice, Ministry of Law & Justice.
- **Objective:** Realise the constitutional vision of **"Access to Justice"** under the **Preamble and Articles 14, 21 and 39A** of the Constitution; aligns with **UN SDG-16** (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions) and the Viksit Bharat @2047 vision.
- **Key features (4):** (i) Funded **100% by Gross Budgetary Support** of the Centre, outlay **₹255 crore**; (ii) implemented **pan-India across all States/UTs** through **four components**; (iii) a new flagship add-on — **VIDHI (Vision for Integrated Delivery of Harmonized Legal Initiatives) Sanjeevani**, a centralised digital platform with an **AI-powered "Nyaya Setu" chatbot**; (iv) builds on legacy verticals like **Tele-Law, Nyaya Bandhu (pro bono), Pro Bono Legal Services and legal literacy/awareness**.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Targets a cumulative outreach of **3 crore beneficiaries**; focus on the poor, marginalised and vulnerable.
- **Significance:** Article 39A (DPSP) directs the State to provide free legal aid; DISHA 2.0 is the scheme that gives this teeth, now layered with AI/DPI for last-mile delivery.

## 2. FAO's SOFIA 2026 — India is the world's 2nd-largest producer of aquatic animals
**Issuing body:** Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO) — *State of World Fisheries and Aquaculture (SOFIA) 2026* | **Date:** released June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Blue economy / Agriculture & allied) · Reports & Indices

**What & why it matters:** FAO's flagship biennial **SOFIA 2026** (theme *"Blue Transformation: Turning Vision into Impact"*) ranks **India the world's second-largest producer of aquatic animals** (after China), contributing **9% of global output in 2024**. India is **No. 1 in the world for inland water catches** (2.2 MT from rivers/lakes/freshwater, ahead of Bangladesh's 1.4 MT) and **2nd in aquaculture** (≈12% of farmed output). Reinforces the policy push behind PMMSY, the blue economy and fisheries exports.

**Key facts to remember:**
- **Issuing body:** **FAO** (HQ Rome); SOFIA is its flagship **biennial** fisheries report.
- **Global numbers (2024):** Total fisheries + aquaculture production hit a record **235 MT** (195 MT aquatic animals + 40 MT algae); **aquaculture alone a record 142 MT** — now the main driver of growth.
- **Top-5 bloc:** China, Indonesia, India, Viet Nam, Bangladesh together produce **≈82–84%** of all farmed aquatic animals.
- **Sustainability flag:** Share of **marine stocks fished within biologically sustainable limits fell to 62.4% (2023)** from 64.5% (2021).
- **India link:** Nodal ministry is the **Department of Fisheries, Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying**; flagship scheme **PMMSY**.

## 3. BHASHINI Rajyam — DIBD–AISF MoU to scale multilingual AI in Assam
**Issuing body:** Digital India BHASHINI Division (DIBD), Digital India Corporation, MeitY, with the Assam Innovation & Startup Foundation (AISF) | **Date:** ~22 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (Digital Public Infrastructure) / GS3 — Science & Tech (Language AI)

**What & why it matters:** The **DIBD** (which runs **BHASHINI**, India's national language-translation **Digital Public Infrastructure**) signed an MoU with **AISF** (a Section-8 company under Assam's Department of Innovation, Incubation & Start-ups) under the new **"BHASHINI Rajyam"** initiative to embed Indian-language access into government platforms and energise the North-East startup ecosystem. A clean example of **DPI + AI + federal cooperation** and inclusive digital governance in a non-Hindi, multilingual state.

**Key facts to remember:**
- **BHASHINI** = *Bhasha Interface for India*; an initiative under the **National Language Translation Mission**, run by **DIBD** under **Digital India Corporation, MeitY**.
- **Scale:** powers **800+ government websites**, processes **~15 million AI inferences daily**, supports **36 Indian text languages, 23 Indian voice languages and 35 international languages**.
- **MoU focus:** integrate BHASHINI **translation APIs** into official platforms; strengthen **Assamese** and regional-language services; voice-enabled apps; crowdsourced data via **Bhashadaan**.
- **Why North-East matters:** advances linguistic inclusion and Digital India's "tech for all" goal in a linguistically diverse region.

## 4. World Bank approves USD 1.5 billion to back India's jobs & business reforms
**Issuing body:** World Bank Group (Board of Executive Directors) | **Date:** approved 18 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Employment, reforms, external finance)

**What & why it matters:** The World Bank approved **USD 1.5 billion** under the **"Boosting Job Creation in the Private Sector" Development Policy Financing (DPF)** operation, supporting reforms to accelerate **private-sector-led job creation**. The Bank frames it around the **~11 million youth** expected to enter the labour market **every year over the next two decades** — directly relevant to India's demographic-dividend and formalisation debates.

**Key facts to remember:**
- **Instrument:** A **Development Policy Financing (DPF)** loan — budget support tied to policy reforms (distinct from project/investment loans).
- **Three reform pillars:** (i) **business-enabling environment** (easier entrepreneurship, lighter regulation); (ii) **trade & investment openness**; (iii) **mobilising private capital** for firm expansion.
- **Inclusion angle:** includes **labour-law updates to ease women's participation in formal employment**.
- **UPSC hook:** know the World Bank Group arms — **IBRD, IDA, IFC, MIGA, ICSID** — and that DPF is policy-based budget support.

## 5. PFRDA launches AI-enabled "Pension Sahayak" grievance portal
**Issuing body:** Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority (PFRDA) | **Date:** June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2/GS3 — Governance & Economy (financial regulation, e-governance, social security)

**What & why it matters:** **PFRDA** launched **"PFRDA Pension Sahayak"**, an **AI-enabled single-window grievance-redressal platform** for subscribers of pension schemes it regulates (NPS, APY). It **replaces the earlier Central Grievance Management System (CGMS)** and unifies **web, mobile and WhatsApp** channels — strengthening the social-security architecture's citizen interface.

**Key facts to remember:**
- **PFRDA** is a **statutory regulator** under the **PFRDA Act, 2013**; it regulates the **National Pension System (NPS)** and **Atal Pension Yojana (APY)**.
- **What's new:** AI grievance bot; **login via mobile number + OTP** (no need to recall PRAN/password); all PRANs on one mobile shown under a single login.
- **Replaces:** the older **CGMS**.
- **Static link:** **PRAN** = Permanent Retirement Account Number, the unique ID for every NPS subscriber.

## Answers

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** DISHA 2.0 is a **Central Sector Scheme** of the **Department of Justice** (statement 1 correct) and advances "Access to Justice" under the **Preamble and Articles 14, 21 and 39A** (statement 2 correct). Statement 3 is wrong — it is funded **100% by Central Gross Budgetary Support**, not a 60:40 Centre–State split (Central Sector Schemes are fully central).

**Q2 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** SOFIA is published by the **FAO** (statement 1 correct), and India ranks **first in inland water catches** (2.2 MT) (statement 3 correct). Statement 2 is wrong — India is the **second-largest** producer of aquatic animals (9% in 2024), behind **China**.

**Q3 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** BHASHINI is run by the **Digital India BHASHINI Division under MeitY** (statement 1 correct) and is part of the **National Language Translation Mission** (statement 2 correct). Statement 3 is wrong — it supports **36 Indian text languages, 23 Indian voice languages and 35 international languages**, far beyond Hindi–English.
