# Daily PIB Briefing — July 7, 2026

## 1. Indiahandmade crosses 64 lakh artisans — digital marketplace for India's handloom & handicraft heritage

**Ministry:** Textiles (platform built by Digital India Corporation, under MeitY)

Indiahandmade.com, launched in 2023, has onboarded over 64 lakh artisans, weavers, SHGs and rural producer companies as of July 2026 — already past the original 60-lakh target set under the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision. It matters for GS3 as a case study in digital-commerce-led rural livelihoods and for GI-tagged product promotion.

**Key facts:** Zero-commission model — full sale proceeds credited directly to artisans' bank accounts, no intermediary cut; special prominence given to Geographical Indication (GI)-tagged products; women form a large share of the onboarded artisan base.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (MSME/rural livelihoods) / Digital India.

---

## 2. India–UK CETA: Customs Tariff Rules notified, trade pact set to enter force on 15 July 2026

**Ministry:** Finance (CBIC) / Commerce & Industry

The Finance Ministry has notified Customs Tariff Rules determining "origin of goods" under the India–UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA), clearing the way for the pact — concluded May 2025, signed July 2025 — to take effect on 15 July 2026 alongside a companion Social Security pact (Double Contribution Convention, DCC). High exam relevance as India's biggest bilateral FTA in years.

**Key facts:** India gets immediate duty-free access on 99% of tariff lines; UK tariffs cut to zero on processed foods (up to 70%), marine products (up to 21.5%), engineering goods/auto components (up to 18%), leather/footwear (up to 16%), textiles (up to 12%); DCC raises the social-security exemption period for Indian workers in the UK from 3 to 5 years.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations / GS3 — Economy (trade policy).

## 3. BRICS Women's Working Group meets in Kochi ahead of Ministerial (8–9 July)

**Ministry:** Women & Child Development / Ministry of External Affairs (India's BRICS Chairship 2026)

*What's new since the 4 July item ("India prepares for the BRICS Women's Ministerial"):* the preparatory Working Group Meeting was actually held in Kochi on 6–7 July, finalising the outcome documents that ministers will adopt at the Ministerial on 8–9 July.

**Key facts:** Theme — "Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability"; four priority tracks — women in governance/leadership, digital & financial inclusion, entrepreneurship & skilling, and women's role in climate action/food security; outcomes include a Digital Repository of Best Practices and BRICS Women's Capacity-Building Guidelines.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations / Society (women's empowerment).

## 4. Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways holds industry consultation on the Maritime Investment Fund

**Ministry:** Ports, Shipping & Waterways (with Sagarmala Finance Corporation & SBI Ventures)

A new ministry entering the briefing rotation. The Maritime Investment Fund (MIF), under the Maritime Development Fund initiative, aims to mobilise long-term institutional capital — patient equity — for commercially viable maritime infrastructure and shipping businesses, complementing Sagarmala, Maritime India Vision 2030 and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047.

**Key facts:** Consultation chaired by Secretary (Shipping) Vijay Kumar; participants included port operators, shipyards, banks, NBFCs and PE/infra funds; fund is meant to plug India's long-term maritime capital gap.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy / Infrastructure.

## 5. Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) crosses 104 crore linked health records, 93 crore ABHA accounts

**Ministry:** Health & Family Welfare (National Health Authority)

ABDM has become one of the world's largest digital health ecosystems, now linking patients, hospitals, doctors and insurers on one interoperable network via the ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) ID, reducing paperwork and wait times. Builds on the recently rolled-out Aarogya Setu 2.0 (27 June item) with AI-governance layers SAHI and BODH.

**Key facts:** 104+ crore health records linked; 93+ crore ABHA accounts created; ABHA functions as an Aadhaar-like unique health identifier.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (digital public infrastructure) / GS3 — Science & Technology.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about "Indiahandmade":
1. It is a digital marketplace developed by the Digital India Corporation under the Ministry of Textiles.
2. It operates on a zero-commission model, crediting full sale proceeds to artisans' bank accounts.
3. It gives special prominence to Geographical Indication (GI)-tagged products.

How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q2.** With reference to the India–UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA), which of the following statements is/are correct?
1. It is set to enter into force on 15 July 2026, alongside a Double Contribution Convention (DCC) on social security.
2. Under CETA, India gets immediate duty-free access on 99% of tariff lines.
3. The DCC increases the social-security contribution exemption period for Indian temporary workers in the UK from 3 to 5 years.

Select the correct answer using the code below:
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The "ABHA" (Ayushman Bharat Health Account) number, frequently in the news, is best described as:
(a) A subsidised health insurance cover under Ayushman Bharat–PMJAY for economically weaker families
(b) A unique, Aadhaar-like digital health identifier under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) linking an individual's health records across providers
(c) A grievance-redressal ticket number issued to patients under CGHS
(d) A certification number issued to hospitals empanelled under Ayushman Bharat

---

## Answers

**Q1. (c) All three.** All three statements about Indiahandmade — DIC/Textiles ownership, zero-commission model, and GI-product promotion — are correct per the July 2026 PIB update.

**Q2. (d) 1, 2 and 3.** CETA and the DCC both take effect 15 July 2026; India secures duty-free access on 99% of tariff lines; and the DCC extends the exemption period from 3 to 5 years for Indian workers in the UK.

**Q3. (b).** ABHA is a voluntary, unique digital health ID under ABDM (not an insurance scheme, grievance number, or hospital certificate) that links an individual's medical records across the health ecosystem.
