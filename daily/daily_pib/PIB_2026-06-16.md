## 1. India–Slovakia relations elevated to a 'Comprehensive Partnership'; PM Modi conferred Slovakia's highest civilian honour

**Issuing body:** Prime Minister's Office / Ministry of External Affairs

During PM Narendra Modi's State Visit to **Slovakia (14–16 June 2026)** — the **first-ever** by an Indian head of government since Slovakia's independence in 1993 — the two sides elevated bilateral ties to a **"Comprehensive Partnership."** Slovak President **Peter Pellegrini** conferred on PM Modi the **Order of the White Double Cross (First Class)**, Slovakia's **highest civilian honour**, rarely given to foreign dignitaries. The visit yielded **14 key outcomes**, including a **Joint Working Group on Counter-Terrorism**, a **Consular Dialogue** mechanism, an **MoU on Labour Migration**, and a **Letter of Intent on defence-industry cooperation** (joint development and production). Why it matters: deepens India's Central European footprint and adds counter-terror, defence and mobility tracks to an EU member state.

**Key facts to remember:** Slovakia capital — **Bratislava**; PM **Robert Fico**, President **Peter Pellegrini**; honour — **Order of the White Double Cross (1st Class)**; Slovakia became independent on **1 Jan 1993** (split of Czechoslovakia); other outcome areas — cybersecurity, post-quantum tech, audio-visual co-production, nuclear energy, science cooperation.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (India–Europe; bilateral agreements).

## 2. 'Panchayats at the Grassroots': 12 Years of Decentralisation Reforms

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Panchayati Raj (review note, 15 June 2026)

A government review marked **12 years of strengthening Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs)** across capacity-building, digital governance, fiscal devolution and inclusion. Headline markers: **~3.18 crore SVAMITVA property cards** issued across 1.92 lakh villages; **4.10 crore** representatives/functionaries trained under **RGSA**; and the **16th Finance Commission's ₹4.35 lakh crore grant** to Rural Local Bodies (an **~84% jump** over the 15th FC). Why it matters: directly tied to the Constitution's local-government architecture — a recurring GS2 polity theme.

**Key facts to remember:** **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1992** gave PRIs constitutional status; **Eleventh Schedule** lists **29 subjects**; **Article 243G** enables devolution of the "3 Fs" (funds, functions, functionaries); **Article 243-I** mandates a **State Finance Commission** every 5 years; **Article 280** tasks the Union FC with local-body grants; **PESA Act, 1996** extends Panchayat provisions to Fifth Schedule areas. Tools: **e-GramSwaraj** (planning/accounting portal), **Meri Panchayat App**, **Panchayat Advancement Index** (9 Localised SDG themes).

**Notebook:** GS2 — Polity & Governance (local self-government, devolution, Finance Commission).

### Scheme-in-focus (today's Panchayati Raj review): SVAMITVA

- **Name:** SVAMITVA (Survey of Villages and Mapping with Improvised Technology in Village Areas)
- **Ministry:** Ministry of Panchayati Raj (technical agency: **Survey of India**)
- **Objective:** Provide rural households in inhabited (*abadi*) areas a record of property rights ("**Record of Rights**") to reduce land disputes and unlock the economic value of property.
- **Key features:** (i) **drone-based survey** of inhabited rural land; (ii) issue of **property cards** to owners; (iii) helps access **institutional credit** against titled property; (iv) strengthens **Gram Panchayat revenue** (property tax base).
- **Beneficiaries / coverage:** Rural property owners — **~3.18 crore property cards** issued so far across **1.92 lakh villages**.
- **Significance:** Converts informal occupancy into bankable, formal titles; launched **2020**, a structural link between property formalisation and rural credit.

## 4. FSSAI tightens norms on 'healthy' and 'organic' food-labelling claims

**Issuing body:** Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) / Ministry of Health & Family Welfare

The **FSSAI** issued tightened regulations on the use of **"healthy" and "organic" claims** on food-product labels and brand names, and served notices to companies violating labelling norms. Why it matters: a regulatory/consumer-protection theme that ties statutory-body facts to current action — strong prelims fodder.

**Key facts to remember:** FSSAI is a **statutory body** established in **2008** under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**; it functions under the **Ministry of Health & Family Welfare**; HQ — **New Delhi**; the move targets misleading health claims and consumer deception in packaged food.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (statutory/regulatory bodies, consumer protection); cross-link GS3 — Economy (food processing).

## 5. PLFS data: urban unemployment eases to 6.4% in May 2026

**Issuing body:** National Statistical Office (NSO), Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)

The latest **Periodic Labour Force Survey (PLFS)** data put **urban unemployment at 6.4% in May 2026**, indicating improving urban labour-market conditions. Why it matters: PLFS is the primary official source for India's employment indicators — useful for GS3 economy and for prelims on which agency releases what.

**Key facts to remember:** **PLFS** is conducted by the **NSO** under **MoSPI**; it provides India's official **unemployment, LFPR and worker-population-ratio** estimates; uses **Usual Status** and **Current Weekly Status (CWS)** approaches; first launched in **2017–18**.

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. The **Order of the White Double Cross (First Class)** is Slovakia's highest civilian honour, conferred on PM Modi during the June 2026 State Visit that elevated India–Slovakia ties to a 'Comprehensive Partnership'. Statement 3 is wrong — Slovakia became independent in **1993 after the dissolution of Czechoslovakia** (not Yugoslavia).

**Q2 — (b).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. SVAMITVA uses **drone-based surveys** of inhabited rural land and its property cards help unlock **institutional credit**. Statement 2 is wrong — it is implemented by the **Ministry of Panchayati Raj** (with the Survey of India as the technical agency), not the Ministry of Rural Development.

**Q3 — (a).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. FSSAI is a **statutory body** under the **Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006**, and regulates food manufacture, storage, distribution, sale and labelling. Statement 2 is wrong — it functions under the **Ministry of Health & Family Welfare**, not Consumer Affairs.
