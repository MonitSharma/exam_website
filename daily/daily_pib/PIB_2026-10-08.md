## 1. Ministry of Rural Development: **375 permissible works notified under the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 — 41% more than the earlier list of 266** (schedule issued 5 Oct; reported 6 Oct)

**What / why it matters:** VB-G RAM G (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)) has replaced MGNREGA. The new schedule widens the menu of works a Gram Panchayat can take up, shifting the focus from generic wage work to durable, climate-resilient assets and measurable outcomes. Strong GS2 (rural governance, welfare) and GS3 (employment, climate adaptation) material.

**Key facts:**
- Categories: water security 128 (largest), core rural infrastructure 111, livelihood-related infrastructure 98, extreme-weather and disaster preparedness 38 (a new category, e.g. cyclone/flood shelters, embankments, firebreaks).
- Every work originates in the Viksit Gram Panchayat Plan, approved by the Gram Sabha (bottom-up planning).
- Works are tracked through the Viksit Bharat National Rural Infrastructure Stack.
- Ministry's stated aim: align wage employment with durable rural assets and measurable development outcomes.
- Not verified from sources read: wage-days guarantee and Centre-State funding ratio — do not quote.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (rural development) / GS3 Economy (employment)

### Scheme-in-focus: **VB-G RAM G (replacing MGNREGA)**
- **Ministry:** Rural Development. **Objective:** rural wage employment linked to durable assets and livelihoods.
- **Features (verified):** 375-work schedule in four categories; Gram Sabha-approved Viksit Gram Panchayat Plans; National Rural Infrastructure Stack for tracking; new disaster-preparedness window.
- **Beneficiaries:** rural households seeking work. **Significance:** moves a demand-driven job scheme toward asset and climate-resilience planning.

## 2. Ministry of Defence (DRDO): **Indigenous High-Altitude Platform (HAPS) reaches 21 km; Advanced High-Altitude Parachute (AHAP) makes maiden live jump in Ladakh** (6 Oct)
*(UPDATE-adjacent: the 7 Oct briefing covered the MCPS parachute; HAPS and AHAP are distinct systems.)*

**What / why it matters:** A lighter-than-air stratospheric platform for long-endurance surveillance/communication, developed by ADRDE, Agra. Complements earlier stratospheric airship trials (May 2025). Useful for Sci-Tech and Internal Security.

**Key facts:**
- HAPS: reached 21 km AMSL, held 20 km for over 30 minutes; payload had IMU, GPS, cameras and altitude-control mechanism; real-time video and parameters reached the Ground Control Station; platform recovered. Coordinated with IAF, CEMILAC, DGCA, AAI.
- AHAP: static-line parachute for mass paratrooper drops, rated up to 18,000 ft; jump from 16,000 ft AMSL at Mudh Drop Zone, Nyoma (Ladakh), landing at 13,700 ft. Made by Ordnance Parachute Factory, Kanpur; certified by CEMILAC/RCMA Kanpur.

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech / Internal Security

## 5. Ministry of Defence: **Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit appointed 29th Chief of the Air Staff** (announced 6 Oct)

**What / why it matters:** Currently Vice Chief of the Air Staff; will take over on 31 October 2026 from Air Chief Marshal Amar Preet Singh (superannuating) and hold the rank of Air Chief Marshal. ## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the schedule of permissible works notified under the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025, consider: 1. It lists 375 works, a 41% increase over the earlier 266. 2. Water security is the largest category. 3. Disaster-preparedness works are included for the first time. Which are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** In the recent DRDO trials of the High-Altitude Platform (HAPS), which is correct?
(a) A heavier-than-air UAV by DRDO's ADE Bengaluru reached 12 km
(b) A lighter-than-air platform developed by ADRDE, Agra reached 21 km AMSL
(c) A satellite launched by ISRO for stratospheric imaging
(d) A balloon built by HAL tested at Nyoma, Ladakh

**Q3.** Which statement about the new Chief of the Air Staff appointment is correct?
(a) Air Marshal Ashutosh Dixit is the 29th Chief and takes charge on 31 October 2026
(b) He is currently Chief of Integrated Defence Staff
(c) He succeeds ACM Amar Preet Singh, who moved to CDS
(d) He will hold the rank of Air Marshal

## Answers
1. **(d)** — All three statements match the ministry's notification: 375 works, water security 128, new disaster category (38).
2. **(b)** — HAPS is a lighter-than-air stratospheric platform by ADRDE, Agra: 21 km AMSL, 20 km for 30+ minutes.
3. **(a)** — He is currently Vice Chief; he succeeds ACM Amar Preet Singh on retirement and takes the rank of Air Chief Marshal.
