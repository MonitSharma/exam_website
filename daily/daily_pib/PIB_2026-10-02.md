## 1. Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas: **GOBARdhan Scheme launched to scale up Compressed Biogas (CBG)** (1 Oct)
- **What:** Union Minister Hardeep Singh Puri launched the GOBARdhan Scheme (Cabinet-approved 6 Aug 2026) to build India's CBG sector through an integrated support framework. It targets a nearly ten-fold rise in CBG production.
- **Why it matters:** CBG links energy security, waste management and farm incomes, and feeds the CBG blending obligation (CBO) that supports import substitution of natural gas/LPG.
- **Key facts:** outlay ₹23,731 crore over 10 years (FY 2026-27 to 2035-36); CBG blending obligation of 3% (FY27), 4% (FY28), 5% (FY29 onwards); feedstock = cattle dung, agri-residue, sugar-industry press mud, municipal organic waste.
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Economy / Energy / Environment.

### Scheme-in-focus: **GOBARdhan Scheme (CBG)**
- **Ministry:** Petroleum & Natural Gas, with oil marketing companies and city gas distribution entities.
- **Objective:** accelerate a CBG ecosystem and cut dependence on imported fuels while treating organic waste.
- **Key features:** assured offtake agreements; stable pricing mechanism; capital assistance for new plants; pipeline/infrastructure support; credit guarantees; technology support.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** CBG plant developers, farmers and dairy/sugar/municipal feedstock suppliers, gas distribution entities.
- **Significance:** moves the earlier Galvanizing Organic Bio-Agro Resources Dhan (GOBARdhan) idea from rural sanitation to a large-scale energy-sector programme; links to circular economy and SATAT-style CBG offtake.

## 2. Ministry of Panchayati Raj: **Special Gram Sabhas on 2 October launch the "People's Plan Campaign 2026-27 — Sabki Yojana, Sabka Vikas"** (1–2 Oct)
- **What:** Special Gram Sabhas across India on Gandhi Jayanti (2 Oct) review ongoing schemes, identify local needs and launch the campaign under which Gram Panchayats prepare their development plans (for 2027-28) through a "Whole-of-Government" approach.
- **Why it matters:** operationalises Art. 243G / Eleventh Schedule bottom-up planning (Gram Panchayat Development Plan – GPDP) and the Gram Sabha as the base of Panchayati Raj.
- **Key facts:** agenda includes rural employment, Jal Jeevan Mission, sanitation (SBM-G), digital services, women's safety, Solid Waste Management Rules 2026, e-GramSwaraj, SVAMITVA, ONORC, DILRMP; more than 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayats prepare and upload plans on e-GramSwaraj.
- **Notebook:** GS2 – Polity & Governance (Local Government).

## 3. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (ICAR) with FCI: **MoU for safe and sustainable foodgrain storage — alternatives to chemical fumigants** (1 Oct)
- **What:** ICAR and the Food Corporation of India signed an MoU to trial alternative storage-pest management in wheat and rice, reducing reliance on aluminium phosphide (ALP) and malathion.
- **Why it matters:** repeated use has caused pest resistance (reported 72–100% in key states), raised costs and worker-safety risks; storage losses affect food security and the PDS.
- **Key facts:** Phase I (2 years) trials — nitrogen fumigation of wheat in metal silos; CO2 + phosphine combination for bagged wheat/rice in warehouses; CO2 fumigation for bagged grain in hermetic covers. Phase II (1 year) – multi-location expansion across agro-climatic zones to frame region-specific SOPs.
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Agriculture / Food security & storage.

## 4. Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (NSO): **CAPEX 2026 — third Forward-Looking Survey on Private Corporate Sector Investment Intentions launched** (1 Oct)
- **What:** NSO's survey of large private corporate enterprises on past, current (2026-27 provisional) and next-year capex intentions; data collection October–December 2026 via a secure web portal.
- **Why it matters:** gives early signals on private investment, a key gap in India's growth story; frame drawn from Ministry of Corporate Affairs registrations.
- **Key facts:** conducted under the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008; follows CAPEX 2024 (Nov 2024–Jan 2025) and CAPEX 2025 (Oct–Dec 2025); previous round covered 3,819 enterprises, with manufacturing at 50.17% of provisional capex.
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Economy (Investment, data).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the GOBARdhan Scheme launched in October 2026, consider the following statements:
1. It has an outlay of ₹23,731 crore spread over ten years from FY 2026-27.
2. It envisages CBG blending obligations of 3% in FY 2026-27 rising to 5% from FY 2028-29.
3. It is implemented by the Ministry of Jal Shakti through the Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen).

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 2 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The "People's Plan Campaign 2026-27 — Sabki Yojana, Sabka Vikas" was launched through special Gram Sabhas. Consider the following statements:
1. It is led by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.
2. Gram Panchayats prepare their development plans and upload them on the e-GramSwaraj portal.
3. The plans are uploaded on the PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan portal.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The ICAR–FCI MoU of October 2026 on foodgrain storage aims to trial alternatives to chemical fumigation. Consider the following statements:
1. Phase I includes nitrogen fumigation of wheat stored in metal silos.
2. The trials seek to reduce reliance on aluminium phosphide and malathion, to which storage pests have developed resistance.
3. A combination of carbon dioxide and phosphine is among the methods to be trialled for bagged grain.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

1. **(c)** — Outlay ₹23,731 crore over FY27–FY36 and the 3%→4%→5% CBO are correct; the scheme is run by the Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas, not Jal Shakti.
2. **(b)** — Panchayati Raj leads it and plans go on e-GramSwaraj; PM Gati Shakti portal is not involved.
3. **(d)** — All three are part of the ICAR–FCI Phase I trial design / objective.
