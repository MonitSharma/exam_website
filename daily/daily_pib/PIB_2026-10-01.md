## 1. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (CCEA): **MSP for Rabi crops, Marketing Season 2027-28 approved — wheat only +₹25, oilseeds and pulses get bigger hikes** (30 Sep)

The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs raised MSP for all six mandated rabi crops, keeping MSP at a minimum of 1.5 times the all-India weighted average cost of production. The tiny wheat hike and big oilseed/pulse hikes signal a push for crop diversification and import substitution (edible oils, pulses).

| Crop | MSP 2026-27 (₹/qtl) | MSP 2027-28 (₹/qtl) | Rise | Margin over cost |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Wheat | 2,585 | **2,610** | +25 | 106% |
| Barley | 2,150 | 2,286 | +136 | 58% |
| Gram | 5,875 | 5,958 | +83 | 59% |
| Lentil (Masur) | 7,000 | 7,390 | +390 | 92% |
| Rapeseed & Mustard | 6,200 | 6,613 | +413 | 96% |
| Safflower | 6,540 | **7,215** | **+675 (highest)** | 50% |

*Note:* The margin over cost is calculated on A2+FL cost; MSP is recommended by CACP. **Notebook: GS3 Agriculture / Economy.**

## 2. Ministry of Power (Bureau of Energy Efficiency): **CAFE-III — new Corporate Average Fuel Consumption norms for passenger cars for 2027-32 notified** (30 Sep)

Fleet-average fuel-consumption standards for car makers for 1 Apr 2027 – 31 Mar 2032 (CAFE-III), under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, administered by BEE. Why it matters: cuts oil imports and CO₂, and nudges manufacturers towards EVs, hybrids and ethanol/CBG-compatible vehicles.

- Target: ~16.7% improvement in fleet fuel efficiency; benchmark tightens from **3.996 to 3.3273 litres/100 km** (2027-28 → 2031-32).
- Reference curb weight raised from 1,082 kg to 1,229 kg (eases burden on lighter cars).
- Super-credits for BEVs, plug-in hybrids, strong hybrids, flex-fuel; **Carbon Neutrality Factor** rewards ethanol blends and compressed biogas (CBG).
- Eligible fuel-saving technologies expanded from 4 to 12 (credit capped at 9.0 g CO₂/km); credit pooling/trading among manufacturers; buy-out mechanism; exemption for makers producing under 1,000 units/yr; dual reporting under MIDC and WLTP.

**Notebook: GS3 Environment / Energy / Economy.**

## 3. Ministry of Mines: **Auction of two offshore mineral blocks in the Andaman Sea launched** (1 Oct)

India's first offshore-mineral auction attempt in 2024 (13 blocks) was cancelled; this is a fresh round of two blocks under composite licences (exploration + mining), under the Offshore Areas Mineral (Development & Regulation) Act, 2002 and Offshore Areas Mineral Auction Rules, 2024 (amended 2026).

- Blocks (per reports): **West Sewell Ridge-01** (~1,000 sq km, off Great Nicobar) and **Sewell Rise-01** (~632 sq km); polymetallic nodules/crusts — cobalt, copper, manganese, nickel.
- Rule change (24 Sep 2026): minimum technically-qualified bidders for a first-attempt auction cut from **three to two**, to reduce cancellation risk.
- Significance: critical-mineral security (EV batteries, renewables), Blue Economy, EEZ resource use alongside ISA/UNCLOS frameworks.

**Notebook: GS3 Economy (Mining, Critical Minerals) / GS1 Geography (Oceanic resources).**

## 4. UIDAI (MeitY): **Five new initiatives unveiled at 'Aadhaar Samvaad', Kolkata** (30 Sep)

1. **Face authentication for ePDS** — with Dept. of Food & Public Distribution and NIC; alternative where fingerprints fail (worn prints, etc.).
2. **SWIK Portal 2.0** — single window for NGOs/entities to seek Aadhaar Authentication for Good Governance approvals from ministries.
3. **Sub-AUA / Sub-KUA LITE framework** — cheaper, faster onboarding for low-volume entities using already-certified infrastructure.
4. **Token Management System** at UIDAI offices — queue/appointment management.
5. **Revamped website and careers portal** — BHASHINI-based multilingual support expanded from 13 to 23 languages.

**Notebook: GS2 Governance / e-Governance.**

### Scheme-in-focus: Rabi MSP / Price Support (Agriculture)
- **Ministry:** Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare; MSP recommended by CACP, approved by CCEA.
- **Objective:** assured remunerative prices; cropping-pattern correction.
- **Features:** 1.5× cost-of-production formula; covers 22 mandated crops (+FRP for sugarcane); procurement under PSS/PM-AASHA, with state agencies/FCI/NAFED.
- **Beneficiaries:** farmers across India; effective mostly for wheat/paddy in practice.
- **Significance:** low wheat hike reflects ample buffer stocks; oilseed/pulse tilt supports National Mission on Edible Oils and pulses self-reliance.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Minimum Support Prices (MSP) for Rabi crops for Marketing Season 2027-28, consider the following statements:
1. The Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs approved the hike on the recommendation of the CACP.
2. Wheat MSP was raised by the largest absolute amount among all rabi crops.
3. Safflower recorded the highest absolute increase among the rabi crops.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the new CAFE-III norms notified in September 2026, consider the following statements:
1. They apply to passenger cars for the period 1 April 2027 to 31 March 2032.
2. They are administered by the Bureau of Energy Efficiency under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001.
3. They provide a Carbon Neutrality Factor that rewards ethanol blends and compressed biogas.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The Ministry of Mines recently launched an auction of offshore mineral blocks in the Andaman Sea. Which one of the following statements is correct?
(a) The blocks are being offered under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957 only.
(b) Composite licences confer both exploration and mining rights.
(c) The first-attempt rule now requires at least three technically qualified bidders.
(d) The blocks mainly contain hydrocarbons and gas hydrates.

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** CCEA approves MSP on CACP recommendation (S1 correct). Wheat got the *smallest* rise (+₹25); safflower the highest (+₹675) (S2 wrong, S3 correct).

**Q2 — (d).** All three are correct: period 2027-32, BEE administers CAFE under the Energy Conservation Act, and the Carbon Neutrality Factor rewards ethanol/CBG.

**Q3 — (b).** Offshore blocks fall under the Offshore Areas Mineral (Development & Regulation) Act, 2002; composite licence = exploration + mining. The minimum bidders was lowered from three to two; the blocks hold polymetallic nodules/crusts, not hydrocarbons.
