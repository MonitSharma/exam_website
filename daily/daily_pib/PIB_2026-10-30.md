## 1. Ministry of Labour & Employment: **National Social Security Board for Unorganised Workers and Gig & Platform Workers constituted** (PIB, 30 Oct 2026; via a news report citing PIB)

**What / why it matters:** The Centre has set up the statutory National Social Security Board under the Code on Social Security to advise on welfare schemes for unorganised, gig and platform workers. It is the institutional piece that turns the Code's gig-worker provisions into actual schemes, so it is relevant to labour-law reform and social security debates.

**Key facts:**
- Chair: Union Minister for Labour & Employment.
- Members: representatives of Central and State Governments, aggregators, employers, civil society, and workers' unions/associations of gig and platform workers.
- Functions (as reported): recommend suitable welfare schemes to the Centre; advise on administering the Code when referred; monitor Central welfare schemes; review State-level record-keeping; examine expenditure from the relevant fund and account.
- Meets at least three times a year.
- *Flag: the report says "six functions" but lists five; check the PIB text.*

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance / Social Justice (labour welfare); GS3 Economy (gig economy, labour codes).

## 2. Ministry of Jal Shakti: **Punah Bharan mobile app and web portal for groundwater recharge planning** (launched 28 Oct 2026 by Union Minister C.R. Patil)

**What / why it matters:** A geospatial decision-support tool for village-level artificial groundwater recharge planning. It helps move recharge work from ad hoc to evidence-based siting, in line with the Jal Shakti Abhiyan: Catch the Rain and Atal Bhujal Yojana approach.

**Key facts:**
- Developed by BISAG-N (Bhaskaracharya National Institute for Space Applications and Geo-informatics) with the National Water Mission, Ministry of Jal Shakti.
- App identifies potential artificial recharge zones within a radius of up to 3 km and suggests suitable structures (check dams, percolation ponds, etc.).
- Companion web portal: village-level Artificial Recharge Structure zonation maps, GIS tools and thematic layers.
- Inputs used: rainfall, groundwater level, groundwater yield, slope and drainage.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment / Geography (water resources, groundwater); GS3 Sci-Tech (geospatial governance).

## 3. Department of Space (IN-SPACe): **AI INSPIRED seed-fund programme — six private space-tech entities selected** (reported 30 Oct 2026; *single secondary source for the selection; programme launch in Feb 2026 is widely reported*)

**What / why it matters:** IN-SPACe, the single-window regulator and promoter of private space activity, is funding AI-for-space ideas with small seed grants. It shows the NSIL/IN-SPACe ecosystem moving beyond launch vehicles into AI-enabled space applications.

**Key facts:**
- Total allocation up to ₹6 crore; grants of up to ₹1 crore each.
- Six private entities selected in this round.
- IN-SPACe functions under the Department of Space and was created in 2020 as part of the space-sector reforms.

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (space, AI, startups).

## 4. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **India releases four publications at CBD COP-17, Yerevan, including Guidelines for I-OECMs** (28 Oct 2026) *(UPDATE — India's national statement covered on 29 Oct; the publications are new; single secondary source)*

**What / why it matters:** The guidelines set out how India will recognise "Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures" (OECMs), conservation outside formal protected areas, which count towards the 30×30 target of the Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework. Formal protected areas cover only about 5% of India's land area.

**Key facts:**
- Four publications: Guidelines for Designation of India's OECMs (I-OECMs) 2026; 5th National Report on the Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety; CBD Handbook: Reference Guide and Indian Perspective; India's ABS Success Story.
- Partners: MoEFCC, National Biodiversity Authority and UNDP.
- OECM recognition needs verified outcomes and respect for local community rights.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (biodiversity, international conventions).

### Scheme-in-focus: Artificial recharge planning under the National Water Mission (Punah Bharan)
- **Ministry:** Jal Shakti (National Water Mission, DoWR, RD & GR).
- **Objective:** scientific siting of artificial groundwater recharge structures.
- **Features:** 3 km search radius; structure suggestions; village-level zonation maps; GIS layers.
- **Beneficiaries:** State/district water agencies, panchayats, planners in water-stressed areas.
- **Significance:** data-driven governance for groundwater, India being the world's largest groundwater user.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the National Social Security Board constituted under the Code on Social Security, consider the following statements:
1. It is chaired by the Union Minister for Labour & Employment.
2. Its membership includes representatives of aggregator companies and gig-worker associations.
3. It is a body of the Ministry of Finance that sets contribution rates for the EPFO.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The "Punah Bharan" mobile application, recently launched, is meant for

(a) real-time monitoring of river water quality by CPCB
(b) planning artificial groundwater recharge using geospatial data
(c) tracking irrigation water use by farmers under PMKSY
(d) mapping flood-prone areas for early warning

**Q3.** With reference to "Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures" (OECMs), consider the following statements:
1. They are conservation areas outside formally designated protected areas that deliver long-term biodiversity outcomes.
2. They count towards the 30×30 target of the Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework.
3. They are recognised under the Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

1. **(b)** — The Board is chaired by the Labour Minister and includes aggregator and worker representatives; it is under the Labour Ministry, not Finance, and does not set EPFO rates.
2. **(b)** — Punah Bharan, developed by BISAG-N with the National Water Mission, identifies artificial recharge zones within 3 km and suggests structures.
3. **(b)** — OECMs are conservation outside protected areas and count towards 30×30; the Cartagena Protocol deals with biosafety of living modified organisms, not OECMs.
