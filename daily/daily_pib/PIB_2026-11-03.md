## 1. Ministry of Communications (TRAI): **Telecom Consumers Complaint Redressal (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2026** (issued 2 Nov 2026; PIB PRID 2331704) — GS2 Governance / GS3 Economy
- **What/why:** TRAI tightens the grievance-redressal framework for telecom and internet-service consumers — a consumer-protection and regulatory-governance item.
- **Key facts (per secondary summary of the release):** complaint centres to be available 24/7, with human assistance for at least 16 hours a day; window to file an appeal cut from 30 to 15 days after complaint disposal; appellate authority must decide within 15 days; ISP provisions apply to providers with more than 10,000 subscribers at the end of the previous financial year; feedback surveys, quarterly reporting, oversight committees and financial disincentives for non-compliance.
- **Note:** TRAI is a statutory regulator under the TRAI Act, 1997.

## 2. Ministry of Road Transport & Highways with Government of Bihar: **MoU on the "Nadi Sanjeevani" initiative — desilting rivers and using silt in National Highway construction** (signed New Delhi, 3 Nov 2026) — GS3 Environment / Infrastructure
- **What/why:** MoRTH and Bihar signed an MoU (Union Minister Nitin Gadkari and Bihar CM) to desilt rivers and use the excavated silt as fill material in NH projects — links river health/flood management with reduced mining of earth/sand for highways.
- **Key facts:** single secondary (photo-report) source; funding, rivers and timelines not stated — verify on PIB.
- **Link:** earlier coverage of Green Highways Policy (20 Oct) was on plantation; this is a different resource-efficiency angle.

## 3. Ministry of Finance (Dept. of Financial Services): **High-Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat** — GS3 Economy
- **What/why:** six-month committee chaired by Suresh N. Patel (former Central Vigilance Commissioner) to review banking regulation, including Regional Rural Banks and cooperative banks, and recommend reforms for Viksit Bharat 2047. Announced in the Union Budget 2026-27 (Feb 2026); constituted October 2026.
- **Key facts:** members include RBI Deputy Governor Shirish Chandra Murmu, Padmaja Chunduru and N.S. Vishwanathan (ex-RBI Deputy Governor); Joint Secretary (Banking), DFS, is Member Secretary. *(secondary source)*

## 4. Ministry of Finance (CBIC): **GST changes under the Finance Act, 2026 effective 1 Nov 2026** — GS3 Economy
- **Key facts (secondary summary):** post-supply discounts excluded from value of supply if supplier issues a credit note and recipient reverses the matching ITC (no prior agreement/invoice-wise linkage needed); provisional refund for inverted duty structure — 90% of eligible refund within 7 days (Sec 54(6)); ₹1,000 minimum refund limit removed for tax paid on exported goods (Sec 54(14)).
- **Why it matters:** eases working capital for exporters and manufacturers facing inverted duty structure.

## 5. Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship with Government of Assam: **Skill India International Centre (SIIC), Assam; PM-SETU cluster** (3 Nov 2026, Guwahati) — GS2 Governance / GS3 Economy *(UPDATE — PM-SETU covered on 21 Oct; new is the Assam SIIC MoU and cluster)*
- **What/why:** MoU formalised at the Northeast Tourism & Hospitality Skill Conclave 2026; SIIC aims at industry-aligned training, international certification and overseas-job pathways. A PM-SETU cluster for Assam involves NSDC, Numaligarh Refinery Ltd and Oil India Ltd. No financial figures reported. *(single secondary source)*

### Scheme-in-focus: Skill India International Centres (SIIC) — MSDE
- **Objective:** prepare Indians for overseas employment through global-standard skills.
- **Features:** international certification and language/cultural training; industry-aligned curricula; pathways to overseas placements; state partnerships via MoUs.
- **Beneficiaries:** youth seeking overseas jobs; **Significance:** harnesses demographic dividend and remittances. (Details beyond the Assam MoU not verified this run.)

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the Telecom Consumers Complaint Redressal (Fourth Amendment) Regulations, 2026:
1. They were issued by the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India.
2. The time to file an appeal after complaint disposal is reduced from 30 to 15 days.
3. They apply to the Ministry of Electronics and IT as the regulator.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The "Nadi Sanjeevani" MoU signed between the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways and the Bihar Government aims at:
(a) Interlinking Bihar rivers with the Ganga
(b) Desilting rivers and using the silt in National Highway construction
(c) Building riverfront highways along the Kosi
(d) Promoting inland waterways cargo on the Ganga
 
**Q3.** Consider the following regarding the High-Level Committee on Banking for Viksit Bharat:
1. It is chaired by a former Central Vigilance Commissioner.
2. Its mandate includes reviewing regulation of Regional Rural Banks and cooperative banks.
3. Its tenure is six months.
Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers
1. **(b)** TRAI is the regulator, and the appeal window is cut from 30 to 15 days; MeitY is not the regulator here.
2. **(b)** The initiative links river desilting with NH construction material needs.
3. **(d)** Chaired by Suresh N. Patel (ex-CVC); covers RRBs and cooperative banks; six-month tenure.
