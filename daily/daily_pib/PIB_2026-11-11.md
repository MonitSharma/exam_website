## 1. Ministry of Coal: **Coal gasification incentive scheme — Round 2 applications reviewed, Round 3 open** (10 Nov 2026) — GS3 Economy / Energy
**What & why:** The Centre's ₹37,500 crore incentive scheme for coal/lignite gasification is drawing proposals in successive rounds; Round 2 reportedly drew two NTPC proposals (urea; methanol and DME), and Round 3 is open (reported deadline 11 Jan 2027). Gasification turns coal into syngas for urea, methanol, DME and ammonia, cutting imports of gas and chemicals.
**Key facts:** Outlay ₹37,500 crore (Cabinet-approved); national target of 100 million tonnes of gasification capacity by 2030; NTPC and Adani Enterprises were among earlier applicants. *(Round-3 deadline and round-2 detail: single digest source.)*

## 2. Ministry of Minority Affairs / MEA: **Bilateral Haj Agreement for Haj 2027 signed in Jeddah** (9 Nov 2026) — GS2 International Relations
**What & why:** Union Minority Affairs Minister Kiren Rijiju and Saudi Hajj & Umrah Minister Tawfiq Al-Rabiah signed the annual bilateral agreement fixing India's quota and arrangements for Haj 2027.
**Key facts:** India's quota 1,75,025 pilgrims (same figure as 2026); signed in Jeddah; PTI-reported. Haj is governed by the Haj Committee of India Act, 2022 and Saudi quota rules (about 1,000 pilgrims per million Muslim population).

## 3. Ministry of Home Affairs (Registrar General & Census Commissioner): **Census 2027 Phase II — master trainer training begins (Keralam, 9 Nov)** — GS2 Polity & Governance
**What & why:** Population Enumeration (Phase II) of India's first digital, caste-inclusive census is being prepared; in Keralam 50 master trainers (36 State, 14 Directorate of Census Operations) began training 1,200 field trainers.
**Key facts (as reported for Keralam):** self-enumeration 10–24 Jan 2027; house-to-house enumeration 25 Jan–28 Feb 2027; reference date 1 March 2027; ~40 questions. Phase I (Houselisting & Housing Census) preceded it. Census is a Union subject (Entry 69, Seventh Schedule) conducted under the Census Act, 1948. *(Dates: single digest source.)*

## 4. Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying: **Island fisheries — Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar under PMMSY** (10 Nov 2026) — GS3 Economy / Agriculture
**What & why:** PIB highlighted that the two island UTs account for nearly 40% of India's EEZ and hold large untapped tuna and deep-sea potential.
**Key facts:** PMMSY investment — A&N ₹218 crore, Lakshadweep ₹175 crore; Lakshadweep tuna target 50,000 tonnes; India's seafood exports ~₹73,890 crore (2025-26). *(Single digest source.)*

## 5. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (Coconut Development Board): **Kera Suraksha Insurance Scheme — enrolment till 20 Nov 2026** (PIB 10 Nov) — GS3 Agriculture

### Scheme-in-focus: Coal Gasification Incentive Scheme
- **Ministry:** Coal; **Objective:** commercial-scale coal/lignite gasification; reduce import dependence for gas, urea, methanol.
- **Features:** ₹37,500 crore outlay; competitive rounds for projects; targets 100 MT gasification by 2030; supports PSUs and private firms.
- **Beneficiaries:** gasification project developers (NTPC, Adani among applicants); downstream fertiliser/chemical users.
- **Significance:** energy security, Atmanirbhar Bharat in chemicals; concerns: cost, water, emissions.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to coal gasification in India, consider:
1. Syngas from gasification can be used to produce methanol and urea.
2. The Centre's incentive scheme has an outlay of ₹37,500 crore.
3. The Ministry of Coal targets 100 million tonnes of gasification capacity by 2030.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The bilateral Haj Agreement for Haj 2027 was signed in November 2026 at:
(a) Riyadh (b) Jeddah (c) Mecca (d) New Delhi

**Q3.** Which of the following is correct about the Census in India?
(a) It is a State subject under the Seventh Schedule
(b) It is conducted under the Census Act, 1948
(c) It is conducted by the Election Commission
(d) It has been conducted every five years since 1881

---

## Answers
1. **(d)** — All three are correct: gasification produces syngas for methanol/urea/DME; scheme outlay ₹37,500 crore; target 100 MT by 2030.
2. **(b)** — Signed in Jeddah by Kiren Rijiju and Saudi Minister Tawfiq Al-Rabiah; quota 1,75,025.
3. **(b)** — Census is a Union subject (Entry 69), conducted under the Census Act, 1948 by the Registrar General; it is decennial since 1881.
