## 1. Ministry of Finance: **57th GST Council meeting (8 Oct, Bharat Mandapam) — arrest powers scrapped, prosecution threshold raised to ₹5 crore, faster refunds**
**Notebook: GS3 Economy (Taxation, Federal Finance) / GS2 Governance**

The Council, chaired by the Union Finance Minister, focused on process reform and decriminalisation, not rate changes. It recommends removing arrest powers of GST officers (omitting Section 69 CGST Act). Several outlets note that prosecution is not abolished, only its threshold is raised.

- Prosecution threshold up from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore (limited to fraudulent ITC).
- No show-cause notice for demands below ₹10,000; notices already issued for such demands to be withdrawn (per press reports).
- Refund acknowledgement cut from 15 to 10 days; about 90% of export and inverted-duty refund claims to be sanctioned provisionally by the system.
- Inverted-duty refunds extended to input services from 1 Nov 2026; capital goods credit refundable from 1 Apr 2027.
- E-way bill interception only on specific intelligence, by an officer not below Joint Commissioner.
- Optional quarterly payment scheme for B2C businesses up to ₹5 crore turnover (in principle); 2% TDS on B2B scrap supplies.
- No rate changes.

*Why it matters:* ease of doing business, decriminalisation of tax law, and the Council's role in cooperative federalism (Art. 279A). Note that these are Council recommendations and need legislative or notification follow-up.

## 2. Ministry of MSME: **National SC-ST Hub completes a decade (launched 18 Oct 2016, implemented by NSIC)**
**Notebook: GS2 Governance (Welfare of SC/ST) / GS3 Economy (MSMEs)**

The Hub helps SC/ST entrepreneurs meet the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs, 2012 (25% MSE target, with a 4% sub-target for SC/ST-owned MSEs).

- Procurement from SC/ST-owned MSEs rose from ₹99.37 crore (2015-16) to ₹4,013.42 crore (2025-26), from 12,524 MSEs.
- As of 31 July 2026: 1,90,414 beneficiaries assisted, 54,499 trained.
- Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy: 25% subsidy, capped at ₹25 lakh; 3,160 MSEs supported with ₹356.10 crore (to March 2026).
- 2025-26 training: 10,717 candidates, 55% women.
- Leading states: UP, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Assam.

### Scheme-in-focus: **National SC-ST Hub**
- **Ministry:** MSME (via NSIC).
- **Objective:** raise SC/ST entrepreneurs' share in central government procurement and build capacity.
- **Key features:** vendor development programmes with CPSEs; capital subsidy up to ₹25 lakh; reimbursement of loan processing, bank guarantee and testing fees (80%, up to ₹1 lakh); entrepreneurship training and exhibitions.
- **Coverage:** SC/ST-owned micro and small enterprises.
- **Significance:** inclusive growth and use of public procurement as a social-justice tool. The 4% sub-target remains a target, so compliance by CPSEs is the real test.

## 3. Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways: **Sagarmanthan 2026 — 3rd edition of "The Great Oceans Dialogue" (New Delhi, 7–8 Oct)**
**Notebook: GS2 International Relations / GS3 Economy (Infrastructure, Blue Economy)**

Organised by MoPSW with the Observer Research Foundation. The PM's message called for a "free, open, secure and rules-based" maritime order. The first edition was in Nov 2024.

- Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 presented as a partnership offer (government estimate: about ₹80 lakh crore investment; top-five shipbuilding goal).
- Agenda tracks: India–EU Green Shipping Corridor; review of the India–Korea shipbuilding and logistics framework; IMEC governance; OECD Blue Economy Scoreboards; African corridors such as Lobito.
- Context figures cited: coastline 11,098.81 km, 12 major ports, about 95% of trade by volume by sea.
- Critical point: India's share of global shipbuilding is below 1%.

## 4. Ministry of Women & Child Development: **Departmental Summit on Women and Child Development (8–9 Oct, Sushma Swaraj Bhawan) — second in the PM's series of departmental summits**
**Notebook: GS2 Governance (Centre–State, Welfare) / GS1 Society**

Aimed at cooperative federalism, with the vision "Suposhit, Surakshit and Sashakt Bharat". Participants were Centre and state leaders and officials. The PM was scheduled to address the valedictory session on 9 Oct; its outcomes could not be verified.

- Four themes: reimagining Anganwadi Centres (nutrition and early education); child protection systems; women's safety frameworks; effectiveness of women's empowerment schemes.
- Discussion topics: take-home ration and hot cooked meals quality, Poshan Tracker data quality, ECCE, impact of social media on children.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the recent GST Council recommendations, consider:
1. Arrest powers of GST officers are to be removed.
2. The prosecution threshold is raised from ₹1 crore to ₹5 crore.
3. GST rates on all goods were reduced.

Which are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The National SC-ST Hub is implemented by which body under the Ministry of MSME?
(a) SIDBI (b) NSIC (c) KVIC (d) Mudra Ltd

**Q3.** Sagarmanthan: The Great Oceans Dialogue is organised by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways with which partner?
(a) Observer Research Foundation (b) NITI Aayog (c) IMO (d) Indian Navy

## Answers
1. **(b)** — Arrest powers are to be omitted and the prosecution threshold raised to ₹5 crore; no rate changes were made.
2. **(b)** — Launched 18 Oct 2016 and run by NSIC; the Public Procurement Policy for MSEs, 2012 carries a 4% SC/ST sub-target.
3. **(a)** — Organised by MoPSW with ORF; the 3rd edition was held in New Delhi in Oct 2026.
