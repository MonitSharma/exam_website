## 1. Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI): **PAIMANA Flash Report (September 2026) — 1,853 Central Sector infrastructure projects tracked** (26 Oct 2026)

**What it is / why it matters:** MoSPI's monthly Flash Report is built on the PAIMANA platform that monitors Central Sector infrastructure projects costing ₹150 crore and above. It is a key data source for questions on project delays, cost escalation and "data-driven infrastructure governance".

**Key facts:**
- Projects monitored: **1,853** (up from 1,731 in August), across **17** ministries/departments.
- Revised cost: **₹37.56 lakh crore** (August: ₹33.60 lakh crore). Reports caution that the rise reflects more projects being added, not necessarily fresh investment.
- Cumulative expenditure: **₹17.71 lakh crore (~47%)**.
- Transport/logistics and energy account for over 82% of projects; MoRTH has the most projects, followed by Railways.
- Background (July 2026 report): Mega projects are ≥ ₹1,000 crore; Major projects are > ₹150 crore and < ₹1,000 crore. PAIMANA-PROJ is the tracking platform; PAIMANA-CRIP (launched July 2026) is the data repository, with nearly 80% of data updated through APIs.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (infrastructure, project monitoring); GS2 Governance (data-driven governance).

## 2. Department of Science & Technology — Technology Development Board (TDB): **₹116 crore RDI Fund support to Manastu Space for indigenous green propulsion** (26 Oct 2026)

**What it is / why it matters:** TDB, a statutory body under DST, signed an agreement with Navi Mumbai-based Manastu Space Technologies to support a hydrogen-peroxide-based "green" propulsion system for satellites and launch vehicles. It is among the early deployments of the **Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Fund** for private deep-tech.

**Key facts:**
- Support: **₹116 crore** from the RDI Fund as **optionally convertible debt**; total project cost **₹277.63 crore**.
- Aim: mature the technology from **TRL-6 to TRL-9**; thrust range from **5 mN to 300 N**.
- Tech: hydrogen-peroxide-based propellant with additives, integrated engine and catalyst system; deliverables include the propulsion engine, propellant feed system and high-temperature ceramic catalysts. Claimed to be safer and cheaper than conventional (toxic hydrazine-type) propellants.
- Context: the RDI Fund is described by Minister Jitendra Singh as a ₹1 lakh crore initiative alongside the National Research Foundation.

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Tech (space, R&D financing, startups).

## 3. Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH): **Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 amended to permit E85/E100 flex-fuel vehicles** (G.S.R. 911(E) dated 23 Oct; published 26 Oct 2026) *(single trade-press source for the final notification; draft of 27 Apr 2026 verified separately)*

**What it is / why it matters:** The amendment extends emission/testing provisions (Rule 115) that referred to E85 so they cover **E85 or E100**. It is a regulatory step toward higher ethanol use, linked to crude-import dependence.

**Key facts:**
- E20 = 20% ethanol; E85 = 85% ethanol + 15% petrol; E100 = nearly pure ethanol.
- Applies only to vehicles **designed and certified** for high-ethanol fuels; existing petrol/E20 cars cannot use E100 and owners need not switch.
- Weight limit in the relevant provisions raised from **3,000 kg to 3,500 kg**.
- Does not itself create nationwide retail availability of E100. E100 has lower energy content per litre, so more litres are needed per km.
- The April draft also covered B100 biodiesel and hydrogen-CNG.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy/Energy, Environment (biofuels; National Policy on Biofuels).

**Key facts:**
- Organised by CSIR under guidance of the Office of the PSA (CSIR-NIIST is the organising partner); **13 ministries** involved.
- Theme: "Creative Minds, Catalyzing Science, Empowering Technologies"; **12 thematic areas** (semiconductors, quantum communication, AI, robotics, advanced manufacturing, synthetic biology, healthcare, energy, agri-tech, ocean technology, space).

- Minister Jitendra Singh cited growth from ~350–400 startups (2014) to over 2.3 lakh, and said 50–60% come from smaller towns (ministerial claims, not independently verified).

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Tech (S&T ecosystem, innovation policy).

## 5. India Meteorological Department / Ministry of Earth Sciences: **Cyclonic Storm "Arnab" over the Bay of Bengal — landfall expected 28 Oct** *(secondary sources; check IMD bulletins for latest)*

**What it is / why it matters:** The system intensified from a deep depression into a cyclonic storm on 26 Oct, forecast to cross the Andhra Pradesh–Yanam coast (between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam) by the forenoon of 28 Oct, with winds of 115–125 kmph (gusts to 140 kmph) and very heavy rain over Odisha.

**Key facts:**
- Name **"Arnab"** (Bengali for "sea/ocean") was proposed by **Bangladesh**, from the cyclone-name list adopted in April 2020 for the WMO/ESCAP Panel on Tropical Cyclones region.
- Good case study for the IMD (RSMC New Delhi) bulletin system, cyclone naming and disaster preparedness.

**Notebook:** GS1 Geography (cyclones); GS3 Disaster Management.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to MoSPI's PAIMANA-based Flash Report on Central Sector infrastructure projects (September 2026 edition), consider the following statements:
1. It monitors projects costing ₹150 crore and above across 17 Central Ministries/Departments.
2. The number of monitored projects rose to 1,853, with a revised cost of about ₹37.56 lakh crore.
3. Cumulative expenditure was about 47% of the revised cost.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1, 2 and 3
(d) 1 and 3 only

**Q2.** The Technology Development Board (TDB) recently signed an agreement to provide ₹116 crore from the Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Fund to Manastu Space Technologies. Which of the following best describes the project?

(a) A grant-based project to build a reusable launch vehicle
(b) Optionally convertible debt support for a hydrogen-peroxide-based green propulsion system, moving from TRL-6 to TRL-9
(c) Equity funding for a satellite-based Earth observation constellation
(d) A subsidy for cryogenic engine manufacturing under ISRO

**Q3.** Regarding the recent amendment to the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989 by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways concerning high-ethanol fuels, which of the following statements is correct?

(a) All existing petrol vehicles must be converted to run on E100 within a year
(b) E100 has a higher energy content per litre than petrol, so mileage improves
(c) E100 is permitted only for vehicles designed and certified for high-ethanol blends, and existing owners need not switch
(d) The amendment guarantees nationwide retail availability of E100

## Answers

1. **(c)** — All three statements match the September 2026 Flash Report: 1,853 projects (₹150 crore+, 17 ministries), ₹37.56 lakh crore revised cost, ~47% spent.
2. **(b)** — TDB (under DST) provides ₹116 crore as optionally convertible debt from the RDI Fund for a ₹277.63 crore hydrogen-peroxide-based green propulsion project (TRL-6 to TRL-9).
3. **(c)** — The amendment is confined to certified flex-fuel vehicles; existing vehicles need not switch; it does not ensure retail supply; E100 has lower energy content per litre.
