## 1. MeitY temporarily blocks Telegram ahead of NEET-UG re-exam

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY), on recommendation of the National Testing Agency (NTA) | 16 June 2026

**What & why it matters:** MeitY issued a calibrated, time-bound order under **Section 69A of the IT Act, 2000** blocking public access to Telegram in India (16–22 June) to protect the **NEET-UG 2026 re-examination on 21 June** from organised cheating rings. Channels were exploiting Telegram's *message-editing* feature — posting placeholder messages before the exam and editing them afterward (keeping the old timestamp) to fabricate "pre-exam paper leak" evidence. Telegram was separately directed to **disable message-editing in India until 30 June**. This is the first time a large messaging app has been blocked in India for exam-integrity reasons — a live example of the State's IT-blocking power and the surveillance-vs-rights debate.

**Key facts to remember:** Section 69A → Central Govt power to **block public access** to online content for sovereignty/integrity, defence, security, public order, friendly relations, or preventing incitement of a cognizable offence; procedure under the IT (Blocking) Rules, 2009. App removed from Play Store/App Store; ~150 million Indian users affected; block is temporary/timebound.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (IT regulation, rights) / GS3 Internal Security (Sci-Tech).

## 2. New WPI series (base 2022-23) and India's shift to a Producer Price Index

**Issuing body:** Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry | 15 June 2026

**What & why it matters:** DPIIT released a revised **Wholesale Price Index with base year 2022-23** (replacing 2011-12) and, for the first time, **Producer Price Indices (PPI)** — beginning India's planned transition from WPI to PPI in line with **IMF guidance** and advanced-economy practice. WPI's big blind spot was that it **excludes services**; PPI covers them. WPI will run alongside PPI for **five more years**, then be discontinued.

**Key facts to remember:** New WPI basket expanded **697 → 957 items**; weights now use **Gross Value of Output (GVO)** instead of Net Traded Value; Solar/Wind/Nuclear electricity added; Crude & Natural Gas moved from "Primary Articles" to "Fuel & Power." **Service PPI covers 7 services:** Banking, Securities Transaction, Insurance, Pension Fund Management, Railways, Air (Passenger), Telecom. **WPI vs CPI:** WPI = wholesale, by Office of Economic Adviser (DPIIT), excludes services; **CPI = retail, by NSO/MoSPI, is RBI's headline policy anchor.** WPI inflation rose to **9.68% (May 2026)** from 8.26% (April), led by Fuel & Power (30.33%).

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (inflation, price indices, government statistics).

## 3. ASI–National Museum of Denmark MoU to find the Danish ship 'Oresund' (1619)

**Issuing body:** Archaeological Survey of India (Underwater Archaeology Wing), Ministry of Culture | 15 June 2026

**What & why it matters:** ASI's **Underwater Archaeology Wing (UAW)** signed an MoU with the **National Museum of Denmark (Njord Centre, Copenhagen)** to locate and document the **Oresund**, the first Danish ship to reach India (1619 CE), which sank near **Karaikal, Puducherry**. It is the **UAW's first-ever international collaboration since its inception in 2001**. The project uses a **non-invasive survey** with advanced scientific methods, deepening India–Denmark maritime/cultural ties and India's Indian-Ocean maritime-heritage diplomacy.

**Key facts to remember:** ASI functions under the **Ministry of Culture**; established 1861 (founder **Alexander Cunningham**); statutory mandate under the **Ancient Monuments and Archaeological Sites and Remains (AMASR) Act, 1958.** Oresund → first Danish ship to India, wrecked 1619 near Karaikal; Danish India trading post was **Tranquebar (Tharangambadi)**, Tamil Nadu.

**Notebook:** GS1 Culture & History (maritime heritage) / GS2 IR (India–Denmark).

## 4. Jharkhand's four traditional products get GI tags (NABARD-facilitated)

**Issuing body:** Geographical Indications Registry (CGPDTM, DPIIT); facilitated by NABARD | reported 14 June 2026

**What & why it matters:** Four Jharkhand products received **Geographical Indication (GI) tags** — **Bhagaiya silk, Kuchai silk, Munda jewellery, and Jharkhand bamboo craft** — boosting tribal artisans' incomes, protecting authenticity, and enabling premium pricing. Reinforces the link between GI, tribal livelihoods, and cultural preservation.

**Key facts to remember:** GI governed by the **Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999** (in force 2003); administered by the **Controller General of Patents, Designs & Trade Marks (CGPDTM)** under **DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry**; **GI Registry is at Chennai**. Validity **10 years, renewable**. India's first GI: **Darjeeling Tea**. Kuchai is a noted **tussar (tropical) silk** cluster.

**Notebook:** GS1 Society/Culture / GS3 Economy (IPR, tribal economy).

## 5. QCI–FDDI MoU to strengthen the leather & footwear quality and skilling ecosystem

**Issuing body:** Quality Council of India (QCI) & Footwear Design and Development Institute (FDDI) | PIB, 12 June 2026 (PRID 2272310)

**What & why it matters:** QCI and FDDI signed an MoU to build a structured framework for **capacity building, certification, testing infrastructure and quality/skilling** across India's leather & footwear sector — a labour-intensive, export-oriented MSME industry. Includes a **multi-level worker assessment and personal-certification framework with Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL)** pathways for semi-skilled workers lacking formal qualifications.

**Key facts to remember:** Clusters covered — **Agra, Kanpur (UP); Bahadurgarh (Haryana); Ranipet, Chennai (TN); Calicut (Kerala).** QCI is a non-profit autonomous body (set up 1997 by Govt + industry) — the national accreditation body. FDDI is a "Centre of National Importance" under FDDI Act, 2017 (Ministry of Commerce & Industry).

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (skilling, MSMEs, quality ecosystem).

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) and the newly introduced Producer Price Index (PPI), consider the following statements:
1. The WPI in India is compiled by the National Statistical Office (NSO).
2. The new WPI series has shifted its base year to 2022-23.
3. Unlike the WPI, the Producer Price Index covers services.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Section 69A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 — invoked in June 2026 to temporarily block a messaging application — was in the news. Consider the following statements:
1. It empowers the Central Government to issue directions for blocking public access to online content.
2. Grounds for its exercise include sovereignty and integrity of India, security of the State, and public order.
3. The order in June 2026 was issued by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** With reference to the Geographical Indication (GI) tags recently accorded to certain Jharkhand products, consider the following statements:
1. GI tags in India are governed by the Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999.
2. Bhagaiya silk and Munda jewellery, which received GI tags in June 2026, are associated with Jharkhand.
3. The GI Registry functions under the Ministry of Culture.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** WPI is compiled by the **Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT**, not the NSO (which compiles the CPI) — statement 1 is wrong. The new series uses base year **2022-23** and the new **PPI covers services** (which WPI excludes) — statements 2 and 3 correct.

**Q2 — (d).** All correct. **Section 69A** lets the Central Government direct blocking of public access to online content on grounds including sovereignty/integrity, defence, security of the State, friendly relations with foreign states, public order, or preventing incitement of a cognizable offence; the June 2026 Telegram block was ordered by **MeitY** (on NTA's recommendation) ahead of the NEET-UG re-exam.

**Q3 — (a).** GI is governed by the **GI of Goods (Registration and Protection) Act, 1999**, and Bhagaiya silk and Munda jewellery are from **Jharkhand** — statements 1 and 2 correct. Statement 3 is wrong: the **GI Registry** works under the **CGPDTM, DPIIT (Ministry of Commerce & Industry)**, not the Ministry of Culture.
