## 1. Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports + Ministry of Education + UNESCO: **MoU to take the "Sports Values in Every Classroom" toolkit to schools** (exchanged 28 Oct 2026, NADA India Clean Sport Conclave, Delhi) — GS2 Governance/Education
- **What:** The two ministries and UNESCO exchanged an MoU to introduce the Sports Values in Every Classroom Toolkit, which uses classroom activities to teach respect, integrity and fairness (clean-sport values) to schoolchildren. Video modules are in 13 Indian languages.
- **Why it matters:** It links education and sport, a gap highlighted in NEP-era discussions, and builds anti-doping awareness early. It also puts NADA India's outreach on a school footing.
- **Key facts:** NADA India did 7,000+ tests in 2025–26 and ran 350+ awareness sessions reaching 27,000 participants. Supplement-testing facilities are at NFSU Gandhinagar and NIPER Hyderabad. UNESCO is the lead UN agency for the Sport Values toolkit (a 2017+ UNESCO resource).
- *Sources: secondary (DD India, Hans India); signatories not named.*

## 2. Ministry of Commerce & Industry (with DGFT): **Guwahati airport approved as an export point for agarwood chips and oil** (31 Oct 2026) — GS3 Economy/Environment
- **What:** Lokapriya Gopinath Bordoloi International Airport, Guwahati, has been approved as an export point for agarwood chips and oil. Assam holds about 81% of agarwood resources, per the report.
- **Why it matters:** Agarwood (Aquilaria spp.) is a CITES-listed (Appendix II) species, so exports need designated ports, permits and traceable supply chains. A designated North-East point cuts transit to other ports.
- **Background (verified):** The Centre released a *Roadmap for Agarwood Export Promotion from the North Eastern Region* on 11 Aug 2026 (MDoNER with CHEMEXCIL). India has about 139.89 million agarwood trees; the North-East has 96%+ (Assam ~114.3 million, Tripura ~15.15 million). An Agarwood Cluster Development Project (Assam, Tripura; PM-DevINE) and an Agarwood International Trade and Research Centre in Agartala (NEC schemes) are in the pipeline; an Export Promotion Cell was formed in May 2026.
- *The airport approval is from a single secondary source; the Aug roadmap is from a news report on the summit.*

## 3. Ministry of Defence: **Inaugural BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise (30 Oct – 3 Nov 2026), India-convened, Andaman & Nicobar Command** — GS2 IR / GS3 Internal Security
- **What:** The first-ever joint maritime security exercise of BIMSTEC's seven members (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand). The BIMSTEC Secretary-General had said in April 2026 that India would convene it in November 2026.
- **Why it matters:** It gives BIMSTEC a security-cooperation dimension in the Bay of Bengal (maritime domain awareness, anti-piracy, trafficking), and positions India as the net security provider, in line with its Neighbourhood First and MAHASAGAR approach.
- **Key facts:** Seven members; Pakistan, Maldives and Afghanistan are not members. BIMSTEC Secretariat is in Dhaka.
- *The exact dates and Andaman & Nicobar Command role come from one secondary source; the "first exercise, India-convened, Nov 2026" part is from a WION report of 12 Apr 2026.*

## 5. Ministry of Panchayati Raj: **People's Plan Campaign 2026-27 — national workshop; MoU with CESL for EV charging and battery swapping on panchayat land** (30 Oct 2026) — GS2 Governance

Per a secondary roundup, the 2026-27 campaign began on 2 Oct 2026; at the workshop the ministry signed an MoU with CESL (EESL's subsidiary) for EV charging/battery swapping on panchayat land. - *Campaign basics verified on AIR; the 2026-27 specifics (MoU, FC grant) are from a single secondary source.*

### Scheme-in-focus: **People's Plan Campaign (Sabki Yojana Sabka Vikas)**
- **Ministry:** Panchayati Raj. **Objective:** bottom-up, convergent GPDPs through a campaign-mode Gram Sabha process.
- **Features:** annual campaign (starts around 2 Oct, Gandhi Jayanti); capacity-building of elected representatives and officials; a planning guideline and booklet released each year; Annual Action Plan under Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan (RGSA); plans uploaded on the e-GramSwaraj portal.
- **Beneficiaries:** all Gram Panchayats and their gram sabhas. **Significance:** operationalises "Local Self-Government" planning under Art. 243G and prepares panchayats for the 16th Finance Commission tied grants.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the BIMSTEC, consider the following statements:
1. Its first joint maritime security exercise was announced for November 2026 with India as convener.
2. The Maldives is a member of BIMSTEC.
3. Thailand and Myanmar are members of BIMSTEC.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Agarwood, in the news for a new export point at Guwahati, is obtained from
(a) Santalum album
(b) Aquilaria species
(c) Pterocarpus santalinus
(d) Dalbergia latifolia

**Q3.** With reference to the People's Plan Campaign (Sabki Yojana Sabka Vikas), consider the following statements:
1. It supports the preparation of Gram Panchayat Development Plans.
2. It is run by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.
3. It is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development under the MGNREGA Act.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers
1. **(b)** — BIMSTEC has seven members (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand); the Maldives is not one.
2. **(b)** — Agarwood (oud) is the resinous heartwood of Aquilaria trees, a CITES Appendix II species; Santalum album is sandalwood, Pterocarpus santalinus is red sanders, Dalbergia latifolia is Indian rosewood.
3. **(b)** — The campaign is run by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj to support GPDPs; it is not an MGNREGA/Rural Development programme.
