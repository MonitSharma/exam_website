## 1. Ministry of Education (Parliamentary Standing Committee on Education, Women, Children, Youth & Sports): **382nd Report on "Reforms and Strengthening of the National Testing Agency (NTA)" presented** (adopted 25 Aug; presented in Rajya Sabha 9 Oct 2026)

- **What it is:** The Committee (chair: Mukul Wasnik) examined the NTA after the NEET-UG 2024 irregularities and the 2026 NEET-UG cancellation (original exam 3 May; re-exam 21 June). It recommends making the NTA accountable to Parliament, building a permanent in-house cadre, and moving NEET-UG to computer-based testing (CBT) in stages.
- **Why it matters:** Exam integrity and institutional design of a key national body. The Committee's press-reported view is that procedural fixes alone are not enough. Recommendations are not binding on the government.
- **Key facts (single secondary source, not checked against the PIB text):** NTA handles about 1.2 crore candidates a year; NEET-UG alone is above 22 lakh. 23 of 39 sanctioned posts are filled, all on deputation. Revenue surplus of ₹590 crore (FY19–FY25), proposed for funding about 1,000 government CBT centres. A public registry of blacklisted exam vendors, NTA-owned printing presses and an annual equity audit are suggested. Members also raised statutory status for NTA (it is currently an autonomous body registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860) and concerns that CBT may disadvantage rural and marginalised candidates. The Dr K. Radhakrishnan High-Level Committee made 101 recommendations in 2024, being implemented in phases.
- **Notebook:** GS2 – Governance / Education; Polity (Parliamentary Committees).

## 2. Ministry of Communications (DoT): **India Mobile Congress 2026 (10th edition, inaugurated 8 Oct) — Bharat 6G Symposium, 6G patent and standards push**

- **What it is:** At IMC 2026 and the 3rd International Bharat 6G Symposium (New Delhi), Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia said India lagged in 4G, kept pace in 5G and aims to lead in 6G. MoS Chandra Sekhar Pemmasani said India aims for at least 10% of global 6G patents and standards, and urged operators to set measurable targets for standards contributions.
- **Why it matters:** Technology sovereignty and standard-setting (ITU, 3GPP). Links to the Bharat 6G Vision (2023), Bharat 6G Alliance and indigenous 4G/5G stack.
- **Key facts (press reports; figures from one source, verify before citing):** Over 400 million 5G subscribers, covering every district. Bharat 6G Alliance: about 90 institutions; the 6G vision involves 1,800+ organisations. Revised TDIP scheme outlay ₹203 crore for 2026–31; about 136 R&D projects (about ₹500 crore) funded under TTDF. Other DoT items: India Post–Blue Dart parcel agreement, ITU joint trials against cross-border telecom fraud, TRAI network-quality assessments.
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Science & Technology (Telecom).

### Scheme-in-focus: **TDIP (Telecom Technology Development and Innovation scheme, revised) — reported**
- **Ministry:** Communications (DoT). **Objective:** support Indian participation in global standards bodies and pilot projects (name expansion unverified; confirm on the PIB release). **Features:** ₹203 crore outlay for 2026–31; supports standards participation, SEP/patent creation and pilots. **Coverage:** Indian R&D institutions, start-ups and industry. **Significance:** a funding arm for the 10% 6G patent target. *(Single source — flagged.)*

## 3. Ministry of Commerce & Industry: **15th ASEAN–India Trade in Goods Agreement (AITIGA) Joint Committee meeting, Jakarta (6–9 Oct 2026)**

- **What it is:** Review of AITIGA, signed in 2009 and in force since 2010. The Joint Committee told sub-committees to speed up pending chapters with time-bound deliverables. India will host the next round, in hybrid mode, in January 2027.
- **Why it matters:** The review is meant to make the pact more trade-facilitative; ASEAN is about 11% of India's global trade (press-reported figure). India's concerns include a large trade deficit with ASEAN and rules-of-origin misuse (background knowledge, not in the reports).
- **Notebook:** GS2 – International Relations (ASEAN, Act East); GS3 – Economy (trade).

## 4. Ministry of Defence (Indian Navy): **INS Mangrol — third Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW-SWC) — to be commissioned at Naval Dockyard, Mumbai on 12 Oct 2026**

- **What it is:** Built by Cochin Shipyard Limited, Kochi. Over 80% indigenous content (press-reported). Sensors and weapons include the Abhay hull-mounted sonar, lightweight torpedoes, an indigenous anti-submarine rocket launcher and the Mareech torpedo decoy system. Commissioning to be presided over by VAdm Sanjay Vatsayan, FOC-in-C Western Naval Command.
- **Also on 9 Oct (Defence):** Raksha Mantri presented the SIDM Champion Awards 2026; Project SEABIRD infrastructure inaugurated (Karwar).
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Internal Security / Defence (Atmanirbharta in defence).

## 5. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (ICAR): **New facilities inaugurated at ICAR–Indian Institute of Agricultural Biotechnology (IIAB), Ranchi** (8 Oct 2026)

- **What it is:** Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan inaugurated an admin-cum-school building, the Birsa Munda Multipurpose Hall, the Jaipal Singh Munda Sports Complex and a NEXTGEN breeding facility, and laid the foundation of a ₹119 crore guest house. IIAB is at Garhkhatanga, Ranchi.
- **Why it matters:** Agri-biotechnology research capacity in the eastern plateau; he stressed improved seeds, integrated farming and balanced fertiliser use for Jharkhand.
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Agriculture / Science & Tech.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the National Testing Agency (NTA), consider:
1. It is an autonomous body registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860.
2. A Parliamentary Standing Committee report on its reforms was presented in Rajya Sabha in October 2026.
3. The report is binding on the Union government.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about India's 6G efforts reported at India Mobile Congress 2026:
1. The Bharat 6G Alliance is a part of the Department of Telecommunications' ecosystem.
2. India targets at least 10% of global 6G patents and standards.
3. 6G standards are developed mainly by ITU and 3GPP.
Which are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** INS Mangrol, recently in the news, is:
(a) a Scorpene-class submarine (b) an Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft built by Cochin Shipyard Limited (c) a fleet tanker built by Hindustan Shipyard (d) a Next-Generation Corvette built by GRSE

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — NTA is an autonomous society (Societies Registration Act, 1860); the 382nd Report was presented on 9 Oct 2026; committee recommendations are not binding.
2. **(d)** — Bharat 6G Alliance functions under DoT; the target of 10% of global 6G patents/standards was stated at IMC 2026; ITU and 3GPP lead standardisation.
3. **(b)** — INS Mangrol is the third ASW-SWC, built by CSL, Kochi, with over 80% indigenous content.
