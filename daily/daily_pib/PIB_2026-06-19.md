## 1. PMAY-U 2.0 — 7th CSMC sanctions 2.13 lakh houses; total crosses 16 lakh
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA)
**Notebook:** GS2 (Governance — welfare schemes) · GS3 (Economy — housing & urbanisation)

At the **7th Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (CSMC)** meeting on **17 June 2026**, chaired by MoHUA Secretary Srinivas Katikithala, the government approved over **2.13 lakh additional houses** for eligible urban poor families under **PMAY-Urban 2.0**. This pushes cumulative sanctions under the 2.0 phase past **16.13 lakh houses**. Strong "scheme + data + women empowerment" item for both Prelims and Mains.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **~2.05 lakh** of the new houses allotted to **women**; **~25,000** sanctioned for **senior citizens** — underscoring the scheme's gender focus.
- Vertical split of the new sanctions: **~1.89 lakh under Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC)**; **23,602 under Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP)**.
- CSMC also reviewed grounding of sanctioned houses and prioritised projects near **mass transit corridors** (transit-oriented affordable housing).

> **Scheme template**
> - **Name:** Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana–Urban 2.0 (PMAY-U 2.0)
> - **Ministry:** Housing & Urban Affairs
> - **Objective:** "Housing for All" in urban areas — pucca houses for eligible urban poor & middle-income families over 2024–2029
> - **Key features:** (i) Four verticals — **BLC, AHP, Affordable Rental Housing (ARH), Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS)**; (ii) houses sanctioned via the **CSMC**; (iii) preference to women, with title in the woman's name; (iv) target of **1 crore additional urban houses**
> - **Beneficiaries/coverage:** EWS/LIG/MIG urban families, with focus on women, senior citizens, Divyangjan, SC/ST, minorities
> - **Significance:** Asset creation in women's names, formal urban housing, and construction-led jobs

## 2. Smart Warehousing System launched for foodgrain storage
**Issuing body:** Department of Food & Public Distribution (DFPD), Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution
**Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — food security, PDS, supply-chain modernisation; Sci-Tech application)

Union Minister **Pralhad Joshi** launched a **Smart Warehousing System** for foodgrain storage on **18 June 2026** at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. It digitises and automates godown operations across the **Central Warehousing Corporation (CWC)** and **Food Corporation of India (FCI)** networks, reducing storage losses and improving transparency in the grain supply chain — a good example of tech-enabled governance in the PDS.

**Key facts/figures:**
- Rolled out across **215 bag-based foodgrain warehouses of CWC**; FCI deploying it in **150 additional warehouses**.
- Tech stack: **FASTag/ANPR-based gate automation**, geo-tagged **smart locks**, **AI-enabled bag counting**, face recognition & object detection, **IoT** environmental monitoring, and AI surveillance for **fire, smoke & rodent** detection.
- Provides real-time dashboards for inventory visibility and weighbridge/gate operations.
- Best-performing CWC & FCI warehouses were felicitated under the **Depot Darpan** assessment framework.

## 3. "Virasat Bhi, Vikas Bhi" — 12 years of heritage conservation reviewed
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Culture
**Notebook:** GS1 (Indian Heritage & Culture; Art & Culture) · GS2 (cultural diplomacy)

Marking **12 years (2014–2026)** of the **"Virasat Bhi, Vikas Bhi"** approach, the government consolidated achievements across monument conservation, pilgrimage development, antiquities repatriation, museums, UNESCO recognition and digitisation. PM Modi (18 June) hailed the campaign for preserving and carrying forward India's cultural heritage. High prelims value — bundles schemes, institutions and numbers that recur in the exam.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **653 antiquities repatriated since 2014** (with the bulk — ~600+ — returned in the last five years); marquee return: the **Piprahwa Relics** of Lord Buddha in 2025, after 127 years.
- India has **44 UNESCO World Heritage Sites** (36 cultural, 7 natural, 1 mixed) and **15 Intangible Cultural Heritage** elements; hosted the **46th World Heritage Committee Session** (New Delhi, 2024).
- **Gyan Bharatam Mission (2025)** — manuscript digitisation via a National Digital Repository; complemented by the **Vedic Heritage Portal** (2023, IGNCA) and **National Film Heritage Mission** (2015).
- Schemes recalled: **PRASHAD** (pilgrimage), **Swadesh Darshan 2.0** (destination tourism), **Adopt a Heritage 2.0** (CSR-based monument amenities); legal backbone — **AMASR Act, 1958** and **Antiquities & Art Treasures Act, 1972**; **ASI** under the Ministry of Culture.

## 4. Indigenous breakthrough in aqueous zinc-ion batteries (BDIM additive)
**Issuing body:** Institute of Nano Science and Technology (INST), Mohali — autonomous institute under the Department of Science & Technology (DST)
**Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Tech — energy storage; renewable-energy integration)

Scientists at **INST Mohali** developed a new electrolyte additive, **BDIM**, to improve the stability and lifespan of **aqueous zinc-ion batteries (AZIBs)** — a cheaper, safer, water-based alternative to lithium-ion cells. The work tackles three long-standing barriers to commercialising AZIBs and showcases India's indigenous energy-storage R&D push. Published in *ACS Electrochemistry*.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **AZIBs** use **water-based (aqueous) electrolytes** instead of flammable organic solvents → inherently **safer and lower-cost**, but historically less stable over cycles.
- BDIM (an imidazolium-based additive) **adsorbs onto the zinc anode**, displacing water at the **Inner Helmholtz Plane**, thereby suppressing **three failure modes: dendrite growth, Hydrogen Evolution Reaction (HER), and corrosion**.
- Approach is **interface engineering** (not costly material redesign) → scalable and inexpensive.
- Applications: grid-scale storage, renewable-energy backup — relevant to India's clean-energy integration goals.

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** PMAY-U 2.0 is implemented by the **Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs**, not Rural Development (Statement 1 wrong). Houses are sanctioned through the **CSMC** — its 7th meeting (17 June 2026) cleared ~2.13 lakh houses, taking the total past 16.13 lakh (Statement 2 correct). **BLC** is indeed a vertical, alongside AHP, ARH and ISS (Statement 3 correct).

**Q2 — (a).** Since 2014, India has repatriated **653 antiquities** — well over 600 (Statement 1 correct). The **Piprahwa relics** of Lord Buddha were returned in 2025 after 127 years (Statement 2 correct). India has **44** UNESCO World Heritage Sites — a high number but **not the most** in the world (Italy and China lead), so Statement 3 is wrong.

**Q3 — (a).** AZIBs use **aqueous (water-based) electrolytes**, making them safer and cheaper than lithium-ion cells (Statement 1 correct). **INST Mohali** is an autonomous institute under **DST** (Statement 2 correct). The **BDIM** additive stabilises the **zinc anode in AZIBs** (suppressing dendrites, HER and corrosion) — it is not about lithium-ion energy density (Statement 3 wrong).
