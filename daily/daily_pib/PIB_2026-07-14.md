## 1. MoSPI launches India's first Index of Services Production (ISP) — trial series released

**Issuing body:** National Statistics Office, **Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)** · **Date:** trial indices released 14 July 2026 · **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (statistical systems, services sector) · Reports & Indices

**What it is & why it matters:** MoSPI released, on a **trial/experimental basis**, India's first-ever **Index of Services Production (ISP)** — a high-frequency, monthly volume indicator for the services sector, meant to be the services-side counterpart of the long-standing **Index of Industrial Production (IIP)**. This closes a major data gap: services have contributed **over 50% of India's Gross Value Added (GVA)** since 2013-14, yet there was no monthly real-terms measure of how the sector is actually growing. High Prelims value (new index, base year, methodology) and Mains relevance (evidence-based policy, data infrastructure).

**Key facts:**
- Trial indices published for **2025-26 and April 2026** first, to test stability before regular monthly release (with ~60-day lag).
- **Base year 2024-25**; compiled as a **fixed-weight Laspeyres volume index** with **GVA-based weights**; aligned with the rebased CPI series.
- Framework designed by a **Technical Advisory Committee (TAC)** chaired by **Debjani Ghosh**.
- Sectors covered: wholesale & retail trade, transport, banking, insurance, telecom, hotels, real estate, professional services, arts & entertainment; **health and education to be added later**.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 (Economy — national statistics, services-led growth) · Reports & Indices.

## 2. MeitY releases the 2nd edition of the "Digital Threat Report 2025-26" for the BFSI sector

**Issuing body:** **Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY)**, with **CERT-In**, **CSIRT-Fin** and cybersecurity firm **SISA** · **Date:** 13 July 2026 · **Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (cybersecurity) · Internal Security · Reports & Indices

**What it is & why it matters:** The government released the second edition of the **Digital Threat Report 2025-26** for India's **Banking, Financial Services and Insurance (BFSI)** and digital-payments ecosystem. Its headline warning — **"AI asymmetry"** — is that offensive AI cyber-capabilities are advancing faster than defensive ones, letting low-resource attackers run operations that once needed specialist teams and weeks of effort. A clean, current GS3 anchor on cybersecurity, digital public infrastructure risk, and AI governance.

**Key facts:**
- Central finding: a shift from direct system compromise to **"trust-chain manipulation"** — attacking biometric onboarding, partner apps, AI-driven decisioning, real-time payments and APIs.
- **BFSI cyberattacks in India run at ~1.6× the global average**; incidents more than doubled between 2021 and 2025.
- Introduces an **"Anatomy of Cyber Failure"** — a four-layer framework showing breaches build up through a chain of weaknesses, not a single lapse.
- **CERT-In** is India's national nodal cyber-incident response agency (under MeitY, statutory backing via the IT Act, 2000); **CSIRT-Fin** is the sectoral CSIRT for finance.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 (Sci-Tech — cybersecurity, AI; Internal Security — critical information infrastructure) · Reports & Indices.

## 3. Indian Navy commissions INS Mahendragiri — 6th Project 17A stealth frigate; Warship Design Bureau's design milestone

**Issuing body:** **Ministry of Defence / Indian Navy** (designed by the **Warship Design Bureau**, built by **Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Ltd, Mumbai**) · **Date:** commissioned 11 July 2026, Visakhapatnam (Eastern Naval Command) · **Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security / Defence (indigenisation) · Science & Technology

**What it is & why it matters:** **INS Mahendragiri** — the **sixth and final Project 17A (Nilgiri-class) stealth frigate** — was commissioned into the Eastern Fleet, capping the induction of all six P-17A frigates in about 1.5 years. It is a marquee **Aatmanirbhar Bharat** example: the ship carries **over 75% indigenous content**, and the class showcases the Indian Navy's in-house **Warship Design Bureau (WDB)**, the country's apex body for indigenous warship design.

**Key facts:**
- Displacement **~6,670 tonnes**; speeds up to **28 knots**; full-spectrum role — fleet air defence, anti-surface, anti-submarine, maritime interdiction, surveillance and HADR.
- Project 17A is a follow-on to the earlier Shivalik-class (Project 17) frigates.
- Ships built across **MDL (Mumbai)** and **GRSE (Kolkata)** under a build-two-yards approach.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 (Internal Security/Defence — defence indigenisation, security forces) · Science & Technology.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the **Index of Services Production (ISP)**, recently launched on a trial basis, consider the following statements:
1. It is compiled and released by the Reserve Bank of India.
2. It uses 2024-25 as its base year and is designed as a monthly counterpart to the Index of Industrial Production.
3. Health and education services are the two largest components covered in its first trial release.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The **"Digital Threat Report 2025-26"**, seen in the news, was released by the Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) in collaboration with which of the following?
(a) The Reserve Bank of India and SEBI
(b) CERT-In, CSIRT-Fin and a cybersecurity firm (SISA)
(c) NITI Aayog and the Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)
(d) TRAI and the National Payments Corporation of India

**Q3.** With reference to **INS Mahendragiri**, recently commissioned, consider the following statements:
1. It is a Project 17A (Nilgiri-class) stealth frigate designed by the Indian Navy's Warship Design Bureau.
2. It was built by Mazagon Dock Shipbuilders Limited, Mumbai.
3. It was commissioned into the Western Naval Command at Mumbai.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**Q1. (b) 2 only.** The ISP is released by **MoSPI's National Statistics Office**, not the RBI (statement 1 wrong). It uses **base year 2024-25** and is the services-sector counterpart of the IIP (statement 2 correct). **Health and education are explicitly NOT yet included** — they are to be added later — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q2. (b).** MeitY released the second edition of the Digital Threat Report 2025-26 for the BFSI ecosystem jointly with **CERT-In, CSIRT-Fin and SISA**; its central theme is "AI asymmetry" in cyber threats.

**Q3. (a) 1 and 2 only.** INS Mahendragiri is the sixth **Project 17A stealth frigate**, designed by the **Warship Design Bureau** and built by **MDL, Mumbai** (statements 1 and 2 correct). However, it was commissioned into the **Eastern Fleet at Visakhapatnam (Eastern Naval Command)**, not the Western Naval Command — statement 3 is wrong.
