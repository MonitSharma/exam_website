## 1. Centre reviews below-normal rainfall & El Niño impact — Amit Shah and Shivraj Singh Chouhan direct drought preparedness

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Home Affairs jointly with Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare · **Date:** 3 July 2026

**What it is & why it matters:** Union Home Minister Amit Shah and Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan reviewed the emerging below-normal rainfall situation linked to an El Niño effect, which has produced a significant kharif-sowing shortfall. This is a fresh, high-value GS3 item bridging agriculture, water-resource management and disaster preparedness — exactly the kind of "current event with a static-syllabus hook" (El Niño, drought contingency planning, crop diversification) that Prelims favours.

**Key facts:**
- India recorded roughly a **40% rainfall deficit in June 2026**; Central India was worst-hit at **50.4%** shortfall.
- Kharif sowing had declined **23%** (to 182.72 lakh hectares as of 25 June) versus the normal pace.
- Shah directed the Agriculture Ministry and states to coordinate on **alternative, less-water-intensive crops** — fodder, millets and pulses — and asked the Department of Water Resources to monitor reservoir levels nationwide, plus ensure adequate power supply for farmers.
- An **Inter-Ministerial Central Team (IMCT)** has been deputed to **Assam and Arunachal Pradesh** to assess damage from recent floods/landslides — showing the same review straddling both drought (west/central India) and flood (northeast) extremes of this monsoon.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 (Agriculture — monsoon dependence, crop diversification, drought/flood management) · GS2 (Governance — Centre-state coordination, IMCT mechanism).

## 2. Ministry of Steel grants Miniratna Category-I status to MECON Limited

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Steel (Department of Public Enterprises norms) · **Date:** 1 July 2026

**What it is & why it matters:** MECON Limited, the Ranchi-headquartered engineering-consultancy PSU, has been upgraded to **Miniratna Category-I**, after clearing DPE's financial eligibility criteria. This is a clean, fact-based Prelims item on the CPSE classification ladder (Maharatna → Navratna → Miniratna-I/II) that rarely gets fresh, ministry-specific news pegs — useful to anchor the concept to a real 2026 example.

**Key facts:**
- MECON recorded a **positive net worth of ₹535.42 crore** as of 31 March 2026, and Profit Before Tax of ₹104.53 crore in FY 2025–26 (up from ₹32.08 crore in FY 2024–25).
- Founded **1959**, headquartered in **Ranchi, Jharkhand**; originally a steel-sector consultant, now diversified into mining, infrastructure, power and oil & gas engineering.
- Miniratna-I status gives the board enhanced autonomy — it can now clear capital investments, technology upgrades and joint ventures without routine ministry-level approval, up to prescribed limits.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 (Economy — PSU/CPSE categorisation, disinvestment & autonomy framework).

## 3. SHE-LEAPS — new digital platform to scale the Lakhpati Didi mission (Ministry of Rural Development)

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Rural Development, built by Digital India Corporation on the LokOS platform · **Launched:** 29 June 2026, at the *Rashtriya Gramin Vikas Sammelan*

**Note on freshness:** The same Sammelan's "AI-enabled Rural Internal Audit Portal" was already covered in the 30 June PIB briefing — SHE-LEAPS is a *separate, previously uncovered* initiative from that conference.

**What it is & why it matters:** SHE-LEAPS (**S**elf-Help Entrepreneur–**L**ivelihoods and **E**nterprise **A**pplication for **P**rosperity and **S**ustainability) is a unified digital platform to track enterprise growth and income of women in Self-Help Groups (SHGs), directly supporting the Lakhpati Didi mission's steep target escalation.

**Key facts:**
- Built on the **LokOS** platform by the **Digital India Corporation**.
- Will assist **State Rural Livelihood Missions** in tracking SHG enterprise performance and income growth across **34 States/UTs**.
- Supports the **Lakhpati Didi mission**, whose target has been raised from **3 crore to 6 crore** women; government plans to channel **₹10 lakh crore** to Lakhpati Didis over the next five years.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 (Governance — welfare-scheme digitisation, women's SHG ecosystem) · GS3 (Economy — rural livelihoods).

## 4. Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) & APAAR cross 26.3 crore verified student IDs — "One Nation, One Student ID"

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Education, under NEP 2020 / National Credit Framework · **Milestone reported:** end-June/early-July 2026

**What it is & why it matters:** APAAR (Automated Permanent Academic Account Registry), the 12-digit student ID linked to the Academic Bank of Credits, has now generated **over 26.3 crore verified IDs**, operationalising NEP 2020's Multiple Entry and Exit (MEE) and credit-transfer vision. This is a recurring Prelims favourite (ABC vs. APAAR vs. National Credit Framework) with a fresh statistic to anchor it.

**Key facts:**
- **153 universities** now offer multiple-entry options; **31,156 UG** and **5,583 PG** students have benefited from MEE so far.
- **1,262 autonomous colleges** are ABC-registered (33 lakh APAAR IDs); **100+ Institutes of National Importance** (AIIMS, IITs, IIMs, NITs) are onboarded.
- Schools are the largest contributor: **16.62 crore institutions linked**, **71.46 crore academic records** uploaded.
- APAAR operationalises the **National Credit Framework (NCrF)**, letting credits earned across institutions/modes be banked and transferred.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 (Governance/Education — NEP 2020 implementation, digital public infrastructure in education).

## 5. Corporate Mitra Scheme — MCA, ICAI, ICSI, ICMAI and IIT Madras operationalise MSME compliance-support cadre

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Corporate Affairs, with professional institutes and IIT Madras · **MoU date:** 25 June 2026

**What it is & why it matters:** Announced in Union Budget 2026–27, the Corporate Mitra scheme creates a certified cadre of "para-professionals" to help MSMEs navigate regulatory and compliance burdens (accounting, taxation, secretarial practice) — a governance-cum-MSME item linking Budget announcements to on-ground implementation.

**Key facts:**
- MoU signed between **IIT Madras/IIT Pravartak/SWAYAM Plus** and **ICAI, ICSI, ICMAI** on 25 June 2026.
- Training: **~150 hours** of online learning plus **six months** of on-the-job training with professional firms.
- Open to Indian nationals **up to 30 years**, graduates or final-year students; course fee **₹3,000 + GST**, with a **50% concession** for women and candidates from the North-East, Himalayan region, Andaman & Nicobar, and Lakshadweep.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 (Economy — MSME ecosystem, ease of compliance) · GS2 (Governance — skill-linked regulatory support).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**A1. (b) Only two** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is incorrect: the IMCT was deputed to Assam and Arunachal Pradesh to assess flood/landslide damage, even as the broader review focused on drought preparedness elsewhere — the same exercise covered both extremes of the 2026 monsoon.

**A2. (a)** — SHE-LEAPS (Self-Help Entrepreneur–Livelihoods and Enterprise Application for Prosperity and Sustainability) is the Ministry of Rural Development's digital platform, launched 29 June 2026, to track SHG women's enterprise growth and support the Lakhpati Didi mission (target raised from 3 crore to 6 crore).

**A3. (a) 1 and 2 only** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong — schools are in fact the largest contributor to APAAR, with 16.62 crore institutions linked and 71.46 crore records uploaded, alongside universities and autonomous colleges.
