## 1. Ministry of Home Affairs: **Martyrs' Memorial ("Azadi ki Pehli Ladai ka Shaheed Smarak") of the 1857 Revolt inaugurated, Ambala Cantonment** (5 Nov 2026) — GS1 Modern History / Art & Culture
- Inaugurated by the Union Home Minister on 5 Nov 2026; public opening the next day. About 22 acres, ~22–23 galleries using VR, holography and interactive projection.
- **Cost is reported inconsistently (₹594 cr / ~₹600 cr / ~₹800 cr)** — do not memorise the figure.
- Haryana narrative: the 60th Native Infantry revolted at Ambala on 10 May 1857, hours before Meerut. Mainstream accounts start with Barrackpore (29 Mar 1857) and Meerut (10 May 1857).
- Static link: Enfield cartridges (trigger); Doctrine of Lapse, annexation of Awadh, General Service Enlistment Act 1856 (causes). Aftermath: Government of India Act 1858 (power to the Crown), Queen's Proclamation 1 Nov 1858.

## 2. Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution (BIS): **BIS Hallmarking Scheme — stocktake** (PIB, early Nov 2026) — GS2 Governance / GS3 Economy
- Hallmarking certifies purity of gold/silver articles; BIS is the sole agency. Over **65 crore** gold articles given an HUID; BIS-registered jewellers up from 43,153 to **2,36,624** (as of 15 Oct 2026).
- Mandatory gold hallmarking began 23 Jun 2021 in 256 districts; now **392 districts**. Silver HUID mandatory for BIS-hallmarked silver from 1 Sep 2025 (silver hallmarking itself remains voluntary).
- Standards: gold IS 1417 (9K–24K, seven caratages); silver IS 2112:2025 (eight grades, 800–999). HUID = six-digit alphanumeric code. BIS CARE app (12 languages).
- Exemptions: jewellers with turnover up to ₹40 lakh; watches, fountain pens, Kundan/Polki/Jadau. Offences under BIS Act, 2016.

## 3. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (ICAR / DARE): **1st International Maize Conference 2026, Hyderabad** (2–4 Nov) — GS3 Agriculture
- Theme "Maize Revolution for Food, Feed, Fodder, Fuel and Sustainability" (**M4FS**); JD Convention Centre, Hyderabad. DG ICAR / Secretary DARE Dr M.L. Jat addressed it.
- Output ~**55 million tonnes**; target **75 MT in a decade**. 329 maize hybrids developed in five years. Proposal to widen the "4F" vision (Food, Feed, Fodder, Fuel) to **5F** by putting Farmers at the centre.
- Why it matters: maize demand from poultry feed and ethanol blending; ICAR-Indian Institute of Maize Research (IIMR) is the nodal institute.

## 5. Ministry of Culture (with Railways): **Culmination of 150 years of Vande Mataram commemoration** (ends 7 Nov 2026) — GS1 Art & Culture / Modern History
- Written by Bankim Chandra Chattopadhyay on 7 Nov 1875 (Akshaya Navami), first published in *Bangadarshan*, later in *Anandamath* (1882). Tagore sang it at the 1896 INC Calcutta session; a rallying cry in Swadeshi after the 1905 Partition of Bengal.
- Constituent Assembly gave it status equal to Jana Gana Mana on 24 Jan 1950. Year-long commemoration launched 7 Nov 2025; Railways closed its four-phase programme at Rail Bhawan on 5 Nov 2026.

### Scheme-in-focus: BIS Hallmarking Scheme (HUID-based)
- **Ministry:** Consumer Affairs; implementer BIS. **Objective:** consumer protection against impure gold/silver. **Features:** mandatory HUID; three-mark system only (since 2023); district-wise phased rollout (256 → 392); BIS CARE verification app. **Coverage:** 2.36 lakh registered jewellers, 65+ crore articles. **Significance:** traceability, formalisation of the jewellery sector, consumer trust.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to hallmarking of gold and silver in India, consider:
1. The Bureau of Indian Standards is the sole agency for the hallmarking scheme.
2. Hallmark Unique Identification (HUID) is a six-digit alphanumeric code.
3. Silver hallmarking has been made mandatory across all silver articles.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The "M4FS" theme of the 1st International Maize Conference 2026 refers to maize for:
(a) Food, Feed, Fodder, Fuel and Sustainability
(b) Food, Fibre, Fertiliser, Finance and Security
(c) Farmers, Feed, Fodder, Fuel and Storage
(d) Food, Feed, Fisheries, Forestry and Sustainability

**Q3.** Consider the following about "Vande Mataram":
1. It was first published in the journal *Bangadarshan* and later included in *Anandamath*.
2. It was sung by Rabindranath Tagore at the 1896 Calcutta session of the Indian National Congress.
3. The Constituent Assembly adopted it as the National Anthem on 24 January 1950.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers
1. **(b)** — BIS is the sole agency and HUID is a six-digit alphanumeric code; silver hallmarking remains voluntary (only BIS-hallmarked silver must carry HUID).
2. **(a)** — M4FS = Maize for Food, Feed, Fodder, Fuel and Sustainability.
3. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; on 24 Jan 1950 it was given status equal to Jana Gana Mana (the National Anthem), not adopted as the National Anthem.
