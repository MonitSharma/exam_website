## 1. UPSC (constitutional body): **Commission completes 100 years (1 Oct 1926 – 1 Oct 2026)** — *PIB backgrounder, via PIB-summary sites, 19 Oct*
- **What / why it matters:** The Public Service Commission began on 1 Oct 1926 as an advisory body (Chairman and Members appointed in London). The Government of India Act, 1935 made consultation mandatory. On 26 Jan 1950 the Federal PSC became UPSC, an independent constitutional body under **Part XIV, Arts. 315–323**. Directly relevant to the merit-based civil service and the "steel frame" theme.
- **Key facts:** ICS exam introduced in London 1855; first held in India (Allahabad) 1922; Lee Commission (1924) recommended a statutory PSC; Kothari Committee (1979) gave the three-stage exam; Mains restructured to four GS papers in 2013; Universal Registration Number introduced May 2025; AI-based facial authentication used for CS (Prelims) on 24 May 2026 (about 5.5 lakh candidates). Women's share among recommended candidates rose from 6% (1952) to 31.2% (CSE 2025). Current Chairman: Dr Ajay Kumar (since 15 May 2025).
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance / Polity (constitutional bodies).

## 2. Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (NHAI): **Greening of National Highways — Green Highways Policy, 2015; NH Green Cover Index; plantation norms** — *PIB-based summaries, Sept–Oct 2026*
- **What / why it matters:** The Green Highways (Plantation, Transplantation, Beautification & Maintenance) Policy, 2015 guides tree-planting along National Highways to cut air pollution and dust, stop soil erosion on embankments, and create local employment (work done via Self Help Groups, State Forest Departments, private agencies and contractors; monitored by a Plantation Cell via NHAI regional offices).
- **Key facts:** Over 3.61 crore saplings planted across about 1.32 lakh km of NH in the last five years (as of July 2026). NH network grew from 91,287 km (2014) to 1,46,572 km (Mar 2026). NHAI guidelines (Sept 2026): plantation is "compliant" at provisional completion only if at least 80% of the earmarked Right of Way is covered; minimum 90% sapling survival at inspection and in the O&M period. First **NH Green Cover Index (NH-GCI) 2025–26** report prepared with ISRO's NRSC covers about 30,000 km in 24 states. **Bio-bitumen** (CSIR-CRRI + CSIR-IIP; announced Jan 2026) trial of 100 m on the Jorabat–Shillong Expressway (NH-40); India imports about 50% of its bitumen. "Trees for Bees" pollinator corridors on the Delhi–Amritsar–Katra Expressway.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment / Infrastructure.

## Scheme-in-focus: Green Highways Policy, 2015 (MoRTH/NHAI)
- **Ministry:** Road Transport & Highways (implemented by NHAI).
- **Objective:** Create green corridors along National Highways to reduce pollution and erosion and generate local employment.
- **Key features:** (1) Plantation, transplantation, beautification and maintenance of roadsides; (2) execution through SHGs, State Forest Departments, private agencies; (3) Plantation Cell monitoring via NHAI regional offices; (4) 2026 norms: 80% ROW coverage, 90% survival; NH-GCI monitoring with ISRO-NRSC.
- **Coverage:** National Highway network (about 1.47 lakh km).
- **Significance:** Links infrastructure growth with climate mitigation and carbon sinks.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Union Public Service Commission (UPSC), consider the following statements:
1. It is an independent constitutional body provided for under Part XIV of the Constitution.
2. The Federal Public Service Commission was renamed UPSC on 26 January 1950.
3. The Public Service Commission was first set up in 1926 following the recommendation of the Lee Commission.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the Green Highways (Plantation, Transplantation, Beautification and Maintenance) Policy, 2015, consider the following statements:
1. It is administered by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change.
2. It envisages involvement of Self Help Groups and State Forest Departments in plantation along National Highways.
3. The National Highways Green Cover Index was prepared with ISRO's National Remote Sensing Centre.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** Consider the following statements regarding the Inter-Parliamentary Union (IPU):
1. It is the global organisation of national parliaments.
2. The 153rd Assembly was held in Arusha, Tanzania.
3. The Indian delegation to the 153rd Assembly was led by the Lok Sabha Speaker.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers
1. **(d)** — All three are correct: UPSC is a constitutional body (Part XIV, Arts. 315–323); the Federal PSC became UPSC on 26 Jan 1950; the Commission was set up in 1926 after the Lee Commission (1924) recommended a statutory PSC.
2. **(b)** — Policy is under MoRTH/NHAI, not MoEFCC (statement 1 wrong); statements 2 and 3 correct.
3. **(b)** — Delegation was led by Harivansh, Deputy Chairman of Rajya Sabha (statement 3 wrong).
