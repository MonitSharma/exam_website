## 1. Aarogya Setu 2.0 — Health Ministry to launch a single-window digital health stack

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW) | **Date:** announced 27 June 2026 (launch 29 June 2026) | **Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (digital service delivery, health) · GS3 — Science & Technology (digital public infrastructure)

**What & why it matters:** Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda will launch **Aarogya Setu 2.0** along with a suite of digital-health initiatives on **29 June 2026**. The COVID-era contact-tracing app is being re-launched as a **Personal Health Record (PHR) app** — a single gateway to create/manage an **ABHA (Ayushman Bharat Health Account)**, store and consent-share digital health records, get AI-powered health insights, integrate wearables, and do OPD "Scan & Register" and hospital "Scan & Pay". It marks the maturation of the **Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM)** from infrastructure-building into citizen-facing services — a strong governance + DPI hook.

**Key facts:**
- Companion launches: **Ayushman Sarathi** (a WhatsApp chatbot for **PM-JAY** beneficiaries) and the **National Health Claims Exchange (NHCX)** — digital public infrastructure for standardised, paperless health-insurance claims between hospitals (providers) and insurers (payers).
- Built around the **ABHA** number — the unique health ID under ABDM.
- Theme: interoperability, registries and data standards for "inclusive" healthcare; consent-based health information exchange.

## 2. NAFED goes digital — Amit Shah launches NAFEX.in and three farmer-centric initiatives

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Cooperation (via NAFED) | **Date:** 23 June 2026, New Delhi | **Notebook:** GS3 — Agriculture (marketing, cooperatives) · Economy

**What & why it matters:** Union Cooperation Minister Amit Shah launched **four initiatives of NAFED** (National Agricultural Cooperative Marketing Federation of India), led by the in-house digital auction portal **NAFEX.in**. The push is significant because NAFED is the central agency for **price-support (PSS)** and **price-stabilisation (PSF)** procurement of pulses, oilseeds and onions; digitising its trade aims at **transparent price discovery, direct farmer procurement and full control over its own transaction data** (previously dependent on third-party platforms). It dovetails with the government's drive for **self-sufficiency (Aatmanirbharta) in pulses and oilseeds**.

**Key facts:**
- **NAFEX.in** — NAFED's own digital auction portal for agricultural commodities (developed with Deloitte); may later be opened to state cooperative agencies as a shared ecosystem.
- **DRISHTI** — an integrated **inventory-management system** for pulses and oilseeds stocks.
- **Saksham ERP** — enterprise resource planning to streamline NAFED's finance, supply-chain, HR and inventory operations.
- **NAFED-KALYAN** — a scholarship scheme earmarking **1% of NAFED's profits** for education/career development of children of farming families.
- NAFED works under the **Ministry of Cooperation** (created 2021); it is a multi-state cooperative society registered in 1958.

### Scheme-in-focus: NAFED-KALYAN Scholarship
- **Ministry/agency:** Ministry of Cooperation / NAFED.
- **Objective:** support higher education and career aspirations of children of farming families.
- **Key features:** funded by **1% of NAFED's annual profits**; ties a cooperative marketing body's surplus directly to farmer-household welfare; complements NAFED's commercial digitisation (NAFEX.in/DRISHTI).
- **Significance:** an example of using cooperative-sector profits for human-capital investment in rural India.

## 3. Samvidhan Hatya Diwas — Culture Ministry marks the 1975 Emergency

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Culture / Centre for Cultural Resources and Training (CCRT) | **Date:** 25 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2 — Polity (Emergency provisions) · GS1 — Post-Independence history

**What & why it matters:** The Ministry of Culture, through **CCRT**, held a commemorative programme for **"Samvidhan Hatya Diwas"** ("Constitution Murder Day"), the annual observance on **25 June** marking the proclamation of the 1975 National Emergency. The day was notified by the government via a **gazette notification dated 12 July 2024**. The observance is framed around constitutional awareness — fundamental rights, rule of law and the post-Emergency safeguards — making it a clean revision anchor for **Article 352 and the 44th Amendment**.

**Key facts:**
- The National Emergency was proclaimed on **25 June 1975 under Article 352** (then on the ground of **"internal disturbance"**); it lasted ~21 months, revoked **21 March 1977**.
- Article 352 has been invoked **three times** — **1962** (China war), **1971** (Indo-Pak war), **1975** — none since 1977.
- **44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978** safeguards: Emergency now needs the **written advice of the Union Cabinet** (not the PM alone); **"internal disturbance" replaced by "armed rebellion"**; parliamentary approval window cut from two months to **one month**; **Articles 20 and 21 cannot be suspended** during an Emergency.
- During a National Emergency, India's federal structure functions in a **unitary** manner — without any formal amendment.

## 4. "India on the Move" — Ministry of Tourism's decade-of-growth review (2014–2026)

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Tourism | **Date:** 26 June 2026 (decadal review) | **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (infrastructure, employment, inclusive growth) · GS1 — Society (heritage)

**What & why it matters:** A Ministry of Tourism review claims a shift from **fragmented, circuit-based schemes to integrated, infrastructure-led, sustainable destination development**, positioning tourism within **Viksit Bharat@2047**. Tourism is a high-employment, foreign-exchange-earning, soft-power sector — useful for both prelims (scheme facts) and an essay/GS3 angle on tourism-led growth versus overtourism and outcome-data gaps.

**Key facts (schemes to revise):**
- **Swadesh Darshan 1.0** (2014–15): theme-based tourist **circuits**; 76 projects, ~₹5,293 cr; **Swadesh Darshan 2.0** (2022) shifted to a **destination- and sustainability-centric** model.
- **PRASHAD** (Pilgrimage Rejuvenation and Spiritual, Heritage Augmentation Drive, 2014–15): pilgrimage/heritage sites (e.g., Somnath, Srisailam, Govardhan).
- **SASCI** (Special Assistance to States for Capital Investment): **interest-free loans to states** to develop iconic tourist centres to global scale.
- **CBDD** (Challenge-Based Destination Development): a sub-scheme of SD 2.0.
- India ranked **20th** in international arrivals in 2024 (up from 25th in 2016); **Mamallapuram** became the first South Asian UNESCO site to win Green Destinations **Silver** certification; **Travel for LiFE** promotes responsible tourism.

## 5. NHAI–NCAER set up India's first Centre for Economics of Transportation, Mobility & Logistics

**Issuing body:** National Highways Authority of India (NHAI), Ministry of Road Transport & Highways | **Date:** MoU signed 23 June 2026, New Delhi | **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy / Infrastructure (evidence-based policy)

**What & why it matters:** NHAI signed an MoU with the **National Council of Applied Economic Research (NCAER)** to establish the **NHAI Centre for Economics of Transportation, Mobility and Logistics** — described as India's **first permanent, independent research centre** dedicated to transport economics. It is meant to move highway/logistics decisions toward **evidence-based policymaking** — toll policy, asset monetisation, freight logistics, modal integration, road safety and the regional economic impact of highway investment.

**Key facts:**
- Hosted at **NCAER** (an autonomous economic think-tank, est. 1956), New Delhi; **NHAI will support it for 10 years**.
- Governed by an **Advisory Committee** (economists, transport specialists, academics) plus an NHAI **Steering Committee**.
- Links to **PM GatiShakti** / **National Logistics Policy** goals of cutting logistics costs and improving multimodal integration.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the proclamation of a National Emergency under Article 352 of the Constitution:
1. In 1975 it was proclaimed on the ground of "internal disturbance".
2. The 44th Constitutional Amendment Act, 1978 replaced "internal disturbance" with "armed rebellion".
3. A proclamation of National Emergency now requires the written advice of the Union Cabinet.

How many of the statements given above are correct?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q2.** With reference to the recently launched NAFED initiatives, consider the following pairs:
1. NAFEX.in — digital auction portal for agricultural commodities
2. DRISHTI — inventory-management system for pulses and oilseeds
3. NAFED-KALYAN — scholarship scheme for children of farming families

How many of the pairs given above are correctly matched?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q3.** Consider the following statements:
1. The Swadesh Darshan scheme develops theme-based tourist circuits.
2. The PRASHAD scheme focuses on the development of pilgrimage and heritage destinations.
3. Under SASCI, the Centre provides interest-free loans to States for developing iconic tourist centres.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**Q1 — (c) All three.** The 1975 Emergency was proclaimed on the ground of "internal disturbance" (statement 1). The 44th Amendment Act, 1978 substituted "armed rebellion" for "internal disturbance" (statement 2) and made the **written advice of the Union Cabinet** a precondition for any Emergency proclamation (statement 3).

**Q2 — (c) All three.** All three pairs are correctly matched. NAFED — under the **Ministry of Cooperation** — launched NAFEX.in (auction portal), DRISHTI (inventory management for pulses/oilseeds), Saksham ERP (internal ERP) and the NAFED-KALYAN scholarship (1% of profits for farmers' children).

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All correct. Swadesh Darshan develops theme-based circuits; PRASHAD targets pilgrimage/heritage sites; **SASCI** gives **interest-free 50-year loans** to States for iconic, globally benchmarked tourist destinations.
