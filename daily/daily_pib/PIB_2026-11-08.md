## 1. Department of Pharmaceuticals (NPPA): **Trade-margin rationalisation on 137 non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs — new MRPs effective 9 Nov 2026** — GS2 Governance / Health, GS3 Economy
*(UPDATE — announced 17 Oct and briefed earlier; new is the compliance data and the go-live date, 9 Nov.)*
- The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA, under the Department of Pharmaceuticals) has capped trade margins on 137 identified **non-scheduled** anti-cancer drugs (drugs outside the NLEM price-control list). Secondary reports put the cap at 30% of MRP; official aim: MRPs fall by **up to ~70%** depending on existing margins.
- Reported from the 7 Nov release (single secondary source): 76 companies filed revised MRPs for 1,225 formulations (121 drugs); 1,031 show reductions; projected consumer saving about **₹3,502 crore a year** (a projection, not realised savings).
- Precedent: the 2019 pilot capped margins on 42 anti-cancer drugs at 30%.
- Why it matters: out-of-pocket expenditure on cancer care; use of Para 19 (extraordinary powers) of DPCO 2013 to regulate non-scheduled drugs "in public interest" — a standard Prelims hook.

## 2. Ministry of Defence: **Maiden BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise (30 Oct–3 Nov) and Exercise "Rahat Mitra" (India–Myanmar HADR, Chennai, 5–6 Nov)** (PIB 6 Nov 2026) — GS2 International Relations / GS3 Internal Security
- BIMSTEC exercise: first time warships exercise under the grouping's own banner; held at Sri Vijaya Puram (Andaman & Nicobar Command); harbour phase then sea phase 2–3 Nov. Ships: INS Taragiri (Project 17A stealth frigate), INS Karmuk, INS Saryu (India); UMS King Aung Zeya (Myanmar); SLNS Gajabahu (Sri Lanka); a Bangladesh Navy ship attended. Follows the 4th BIMSTEC Experts Group Meeting on Maritime Security Cooperation (Nov 2025).
- Rahat Mitra: inaugural bilateral **tri-services HADR** exercise with Myanmar, 300+ personnel, theme "Together for Humanity, Stronger through Partnership"; linked by MoD to **Neighbourhood First and MAHASAGAR**.
- Static: BIMSTEC founded 1997 (Bangkok Declaration), seven members, Charter in force May 2024; India leads the security pillar.

### Scheme-in-focus: NPPA trade-margin rationalisation (price regulation of non-scheduled drugs)
- **Ministry/body:** Department of Pharmaceuticals (Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers) / NPPA.
- **Objective:** make cancer medicines affordable by curbing excessive retail trade margins.
- **Key features:** margin cap on 137 identified anti-cancer drugs (reported 30%); companies must publish revised MRPs; effective 9 Nov 2026; legal route is DPCO 2013 powers over non-scheduled drugs; price-monitoring continues.
- **Beneficiaries:** cancer patients, especially out-of-pocket payers.
- **Significance:** extends the 2019 pilot; complements Jan Aushadhi, AMRIT pharmacies and the Ayushman Bharat cover.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the NPPA's recent trade-margin rationalisation for anti-cancer drugs, consider:
1. NPPA functions under the Department of Pharmaceuticals.
2. The 137 drugs covered are non-scheduled drugs, i.e., outside the NLEM price-control list.
3. The exercise fixes statutory ceiling prices for scheduled drugs.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The maiden BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise (Oct–Nov 2026) was hosted at Sri Vijaya Puram under which Indian command?
(a) Andaman & Nicobar Command (b) Eastern Naval Command (c) Southern Naval Command (d) Strategic Forces Command

**Q3.** The Piprahwa relics of the Buddha, recently exposed at Tawang Monastery, were excavated in 1898 from a stupa in present-day which State?
(a) Bihar (b) Uttar Pradesh (c) Madhya Pradesh (d) Odisha

## Answers
1. **(b)** — NPPA is under the Department of Pharmaceuticals; the 137 drugs are non-scheduled, so statement 3 (ceiling prices for scheduled drugs) is wrong — this is margin rationalisation under public-interest powers.
2. **(a)** — Held at Sri Vijaya Puram (Port Blair) under the Andaman & Nicobar Command, India's only tri-service theatre command.
3. **(b)** — Piprahwa, in Siddharthnagar district, Uttar Pradesh, excavated by W.C. Peppé in 1898; associated with Kapilavastu and the Shakya clan.
