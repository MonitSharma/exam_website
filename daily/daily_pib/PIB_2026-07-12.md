## 1. India–New Zealand elevate ties to a Strategic Partnership; unveil "Roadmap 2030"

**Issuing body:** Ministry of External Affairs / PMO (outcome of PM's visit to Wellington, 10–11 July 2026)

During the concluding leg of PM Modi's three-nation tour, India and New Zealand **elevated their relationship to a "Strategic Partnership"** and released the **India–New Zealand Strategic Partnership Roadmap 2030**. This was the first visit by an Indian PM to New Zealand in 40 years, and converts the general visit (flagged in the 6 July briefing) into concrete deliverables.

**Why it matters:** Deepens India's Indo-Pacific and Global South footprint, adds a maritime-security and critical-minerals partner in the South Pacific, and advances India's Free Trade Agreement (FTA) network.

**Key facts:**
- **10 agreements** signed and **8 strategic announcements** made, spanning defence, maritime security, trade, agriculture, disaster management, science, education and culture.
- A **Memorandum of Arrangement on Maritime Cooperation** (information-sharing and joint activity in the Indo-Pacific) and a **Joint Working Group on Counter-Terrorism** were established.
- FTA negotiations **concluded**; the two sides set a target to **double bilateral trade** (aiming ~NZ$7 billion) by 2030.
- New Zealand announced intent to invest in India over the coming years.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 — International Relations.

## 2. Foundation stone laid for ICMR's Centre for High Altitude Medicine & Public Health Research, Keylong

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare / Indian Council of Medical Research (Department of Health Research) — Union Minister J.P. Nadda, 11 July 2026

The foundation stone was laid at **Keylong, Lahaul & Spiti district (Himachal Pradesh)** for India's first dedicated centre on high-altitude medicine, upgrading ICMR's existing Keylong field station into a full multidisciplinary research hub.

**Why it matters:** High-altitude and cold-desert populations (and troops) face distinct physiological and disease burdens; the centre institutionalises indigenous research on "mountain medicine," a strategically and clinically under-served area.

**Key facts:**
- Research focus: high-altitude physiology, mountain medicine, climate-sensitive & emerging diseases, maternal-child health, nutrition, mental health, and disaster medicine.
- Set up under the **Department of Health Research (DHR)**, MoHFW, via ICMR.
- Ceremony included Bhumi Pujan, native Himalayan sapling plantation under a **"Green ICMR Campus" initiative**, and release of a commemorative postal special cover.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology / GS2 — Health Governance.

## 3. PARIVARTAN Scheme: two more EV makers join the Delhi-NCR truck & bus replacement drive

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH); scheme routed through NCRPB

**Olectra Greentech** and **Montra Electric (TI Clean Mobility)** signed MoUs to join the Cabinet-approved **PARIVARTAN Scheme** for replacing ageing commercial vehicles in Delhi-NCR with BS-VI or electric vehicles.

**Why it matters:** Direct lever on NCR air pollution and a template linking **vehicle scrappage + fleet electrification + demand incentives**; recurring GS3 theme (environment × economy).

**Scheme-in-focus — PARIVARTAN (Delhi-NCR fleet replacement):**
- **Ministry:** MoRTH, implemented via the National Capital Region Planning Board (NCRPB).
- **Objective:** Retire old, polluting BS-III/BS-IV trucks and buses in NCR and shift to BS-VI/EV fleets.
- **Key features:** ~₹9,585 crore two-year outlay (₹5,041 crore central share + state tax concessions); mandatory scrapping of BS-III/older at Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities; participating OEMs give an **8% discount** on ex-showroom price; 5% interest subvention on loans (5 years); monthly fuel vouchers; state waivers on registration fee and motor-vehicle tax.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Commercial truck and bus operators in the NCR airshed.
- **Significance:** Operationalises the "scrap-and-replace" model as a pollution-control instrument.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Environment / Economy.

## 4. Scheme-in-focus: PRAGATI — 20,000 rural youth as agri-entrepreneurs

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and Rural Development — Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan (launched 7 July 2026)

**PRAGATI** is a national initiative to build a nationwide cadre of agri-entrepreneurs to raise the incomes and productivity of small and marginal farmers.

- **Objective:** Create a network of **20,000 new agri-entrepreneurs** (adding to an existing ~26,000) serving **~20 lakh small & marginal farmers**.
- **Key features:** Village-level advisory, soil testing, machinery/custom-hiring services, financial linkages and market access; promotes **regenerative agriculture** and diversified incomes.
- **Targets:** ≥30% increase in farmer incomes; 15–20% higher yields in paddy, maize and potato; inclusive participation.
- **Coverage:** Key farm states incl. Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Assam, West Bengal, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, Rajasthan.
- **Significance:** "Last-mile" delivery model that treats rural youth as service-providers, linking farmer-income goals to entrepreneurship.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Agriculture.

## 5. Prime Minister Research Chair (PMRC) Scheme 2026 — reverse brain-drain window closes 15 July

**Issuing body:** Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education

The **PMRC Scheme** is running its 2026 application cycle to attract Indian-origin researchers from abroad into premier Indian institutions; the applications window closes **15 July 2026**.

**Scheme-in-focus — PMRC:**
- **Ministry:** Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education.
- **Objective:** Attract and place accomplished **Indian nationals / OCIs / PIOs** working abroad in Indian government higher-education institutions and national labs for cutting-edge research ("reverse brain drain").
- **Key features:** Three tiers by post-PhD experience — **Young Research Fellows** (up to 5 yrs), **Senior Fellows** (5–<10 yrs) and **Research Chairs** (10+ yrs); support scales up to roughly ₹14 crore for Research Chairs (fellowship + research grant + institutional support); operates through strategic thematic areas.
- **Significance:** A talent-repatriation instrument aligned with the research-ecosystem push (ANRF, NEP 2020).

**Destination notebook:** GS2 — Governance / Education Policy.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the *India–New Zealand Strategic Partnership Roadmap 2030*, consider the following statements:
1. It was announced during the first visit by an Indian Prime Minister to New Zealand in four decades.
2. Both countries concluded negotiations for a Free Trade Agreement during the visit.
3. A Memorandum of Arrangement on Maritime Cooperation in the Indo-Pacific was signed.

How many of the statements given above are correct?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q2.** The *Centre for High Altitude Medicine and Public Health Research* at Keylong, whose foundation stone was recently laid, is being established by:
(a) The Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO)
(b) The Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) under the Department of Health Research
(c) The Department of Science and Technology
(d) The Indian Institute of Science, Bengaluru

**Q3.** Consider the following statements about the *PARIVARTAN Scheme* recently in the news:
1. It aims to replace old trucks and buses in the Delhi-NCR region with BS-VI or electric vehicles.
2. It is implemented by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy.
3. Scrapping of eligible old vehicles at Registered Vehicle Scrapping Facilities is a condition under the scheme.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (c) All three.** The visit was the first by an Indian PM to New Zealand in 40 years; FTA negotiations were concluded with a goal to double trade by 2030; and a Memorandum of Arrangement on Maritime Cooperation plus a Joint Working Group on Counter-Terrorism were established.

**Q2 — (b) ICMR under the Department of Health Research (MoHFW).** The centre upgrades ICMR's Keylong field station into a dedicated high-altitude medicine hub; it is not a DRDO or DST facility.

**Q3 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** PARIVARTAN targets replacement of old NCR trucks/buses with BS-VI/EVs and mandates scrapping of BS-III/older vehicles; it is implemented by the **Ministry of Road Transport & Highways** (via NCRPB), **not** the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy, so statement 2 is incorrect.
