## 1. Agriculture Ministry launches "PRAGATI" — 20,000 rural agri-entrepreneurs to support 20 lakh farmers

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan) · **Launched:** 8 July 2026, New Delhi
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Agriculture (farmer incomes, regenerative agriculture, agri-entrepreneurship) · GS2 Governance (public–private–philanthropic partnership)

**What it is / why it matters:** PRAGATI — **Promoting Regenerative Agriculture Through Innovative Growth Initiatives** — is a landmark public–private–philanthropic programme to build a cadre of *agri-entrepreneurs* who deliver last-mile advisory, inputs and market linkage to smallholders. It is a strong GS3 case study on converting the "agri-entrepreneur / hub-and-spoke" model into measurable income and yield gains, and on blended finance (government + corporates + global foundations) in agriculture.

**Key facts & figures:**
- Aims to empower **20,000 rural youth as agri-entrepreneurs** and directly support **~20 lakh (2 million) smallholder farmers**.
- Targets: at least **30% increase in farmer incomes**, a **15–20% boost in yields** of key crops (paddy, maize, potato), and adoption of regenerative/diversified income practices by at least 20% of participating farmers.
- Roll-out states: Madhya Pradesh, Bihar, Uttar Pradesh, Assam, West Bengal, Jharkhand, Maharashtra, Rajasthan.
- Partners: PepsiCo Foundation, SBI Foundation, Gates Foundation, IDH, Heifer International, Environmental Defense Fund, Transform Rural India Foundation (TRIF), Agri Entrepreneur Growth Foundation, and others.

## 2. Ministry of Textiles releases "Mapping of Textile Waste Value Chain in India" report

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Textiles (Union Minister Giriraj Singh) · **Released:** 13 July 2026, New Delhi · PIB PRID 2237779
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Environment (circular economy, waste management) · GS3 Economy (textiles, sustainable manufacturing)

**What it is / why it matters:** The report is the first official mapping of India's textile-waste ecosystem, meant to guide policy, investment and industry collaboration to make India a global hub for **circular and sustainable textiles**. It dovetails with the **PM MITRA** parks push (5F vision: Farm → Fibre → Factory → Fashion → Foreign), where sustainability is embedded via common effluent treatment plants (CETPs), wastewater recycling and waste management.

**Key facts & figures:**
- India manages **~7.8 million tonnes of textile waste annually**; more than **70%** is already recovered through reuse, recycling or upcycling.
- **~95% of pre-consumer (factory) textile waste** is collected and reprocessed through existing value-chain networks.
- **PM MITRA (Pradhan Mantri Mega Integrated Textile Region and Apparel):** 7 parks, outlay **₹4,445 crore** (up to 2027-28), at Virudhunagar (TN), Warangal (Telangana), Navsari (Gujarat), Kalaburagi (Karnataka), Dhar (MP), Lucknow (UP) and Amravati (Maharashtra).

## 3. EPFO rolls out a 6-month "Amnesty Scheme, 2026" to regularise exempted PF trusts

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Labour & Employment / EPFO · **Notified:** 29 June 2026; applications invited **12 July 2026**
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Economy (labour codes, social security, formalisation) · GS2 Governance (ease of compliance)

**What it is / why it matters:** A one-time, six-month window for establishments running **exempted Provident Fund trusts** — recognised under the Income Tax Act but lacking a formal exemption notification — to **regularise their status** under the EPF & MP Act, 1952. It is distinct from the *EPF Scheme, 2026* covered on 3 July (which replaced the 1952 Scheme); this is a compliance-amnesty measure aligned with the new labour codes.

**Key facts & figures:**
- Provides **retrospective exemption** under **Section 17 of the EPF Act, 1952** and **Section 143 of the Code on Social Security, 2020**.
- Pending assessments (dues, damages, interest) are **withdrawn and abated**, provided member accounts received contributions and interest **at par with or better than** statutory rates; past finalised orders are treated as *void ab initio*.
- Window: **6 months** from notification (29 June 2026). Applications routed to the concerned EPFO Regional Office (rc.exemption@epfindia.gov.in).

## 4. POWERGRID secures ¥80 billion JBIC green loan for the Khavda–Nagpur HVDC line

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Power (Power Grid Corporation of India Ltd.) with Japan Bank for International Cooperation (JBIC) · **Loan signed:** 17 June 2026; reported early–mid July 2026
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Economy/Infrastructure (power transmission, green finance) · GS3 Environment (renewable integration) · GS2 IR (India–Japan economic cooperation)

**What it is / why it matters:** One of the largest green-finance arrangements for India's power-transmission sector. The **JPY 80 billion (≈₹4,600 crore) green loan** funds a **High Voltage Direct Current (HVDC)** line to *evacuate* renewable power from the **Khavda Renewable Energy Park (Gujarat)** — among the world's largest RE parks — to demand centres near Nagpur. It links three UPSC threads: renewable-energy integration, transmission as the grid bottleneck, and India–Japan development finance.

**Key facts & figures:**
- Lender: **JBIC** — Japan's wholly government-owned policy financial institution, headquartered in Tokyo (established 1999).
- Project: **Khavda–Nagpur HVDC** transmission link; HVDC is preferred for **long-distance, low-loss bulk power** transfer.
- Khavda RE Park (Kutch, Gujarat) is a flagship in India's target of **500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030**.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the **PRAGATI** initiative launched in July 2026, consider the following statements:
1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare to develop a cadre of rural agri-entrepreneurs.
2. The acronym stands for "Promoting Regenerative Agriculture Through Innovative Growth Initiatives."
3. It is funded exclusively by the Union Government with no corporate or philanthropic participation.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Consider the following statements regarding the **Khavda–Nagpur HVDC** project in the news:
1. It is being built to evacuate renewable power from a large renewable energy park in Gujarat.
2. It received a green loan from the Japan Bank for International Cooperation (JBIC).
3. HVDC technology is generally preferred for long-distance, bulk transmission of power with lower losses.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The **Comprehensive Dam Safety Evaluation (CDSE)**, discussed at the July 2026 All-India Water Resources Secretaries' Conference, is mandated under which of the following?
(a) The Interstate River Water Disputes Act, 1956
(b) The Dam Safety Act, 2021
(c) The Environment (Protection) Act, 1986
(d) The National Water Framework Bill

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct: PRAGATI ("Promoting Regenerative Agriculture Through Innovative Growth Initiatives") is a Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare initiative to empower 20,000 agri-entrepreneurs supporting ~20 lakh farmers. Statement 3 is wrong — it is a public–private–philanthropic partnership (PepsiCo Foundation, SBI Foundation, Gates Foundation, TRIF, etc.).

**Q2 — (d).** All three are correct. The HVDC line evacuates renewable power from the Khavda RE Park (Gujarat) to Nagpur, is backed by a ¥80 billion JBIC green loan, and HVDC is used for low-loss, long-distance bulk power transfer.

**Q3 — (b).** The CDSE is carried out under the **Dam Safety Act, 2021**, which set up the National Dam Safety Authority (NDSA) and mandates periodic safety evaluation of specified dams; States were reviewed for completing CDSE by December 2026.
