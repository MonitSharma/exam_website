## 1. Ministry of Defence: **Contracts worth ₹53,866.50 crore signed for 8 Next Generation Corvettes (NGC) for the Indian Navy** (2 Nov 2026) — GS3 Internal Security / Defence Manufacturing
- **What:** MoD signed contracts in New Delhi (in the presence of the Defence Secretary) with **Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) – 5 ships** and **Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL) – 3 ships**. The Navy designs the ships; the DPSU shipyards were selected through a competitive process.
- **Roles:** protection of maritime trade routes, anti-surface and anti-submarine warfare, coastal defence/littoral combat, surveillance and presence; missiles, weapons and sensors for air, surface and sub-surface domains.
- **Why it matters:** Aatmanirbhar Bharat in naval shipbuilding — MoD estimates ~72 lakh person-days of shipyard employment and ~460 lakh person-days in ancillary industry/MSMEs over ten years; involves private shipyards and MSMEs as sub-contractors. Strengthens Indian Ocean Region security posture, linking to the BIMSTEC maritime exercise covered on 1 Nov.
- **Key facts:** total ₹53,866.50 cr; 8 ships (5 GRSE + 3 GSL); Navy in-house design; delivery timeline not stated in the source.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (defence indigenisation); GS2 IR (IOR).

## 2. Ministry of Rural Development: **VB–G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026 notified** (2 Nov 2026) — GS2 Governance
- **What:** Rules under the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 set up a **three-tier grievance mechanism — Gram Panchayat, Block, District** — with designated Grievance Redressal Officers; the District Programme Coordinator oversees the district level.
- **Key provisions:** a complaint cannot be refused for format, language, mode or procedural defect; filing in person, by speed post, via the central digital (DPI) platform, or phone helplines; physical complaints digitised by next working day with a registration/tracking number; **enquiry within 7 working days, resolution within 15 days**; if unresolved, **automatic escalation to the next higher level**, electronically monitored; officer can call for records and hear the complainant; appeals as per Schedule I of the Act. Source does not mention penalties.
- **Why it matters:** statutory, time-bound accountability for a rural employment guarantee — links to social audit, citizen charters and the Right-to-Services idea.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (grievance redressal, rural employment).

## 3. Ministry of Labour & Employment (PIB Backgrounder, 31 Oct/1 Nov): **EPFO wage ceiling raised ₹15,000 → ₹25,000 per month** — GS3 Economy / GS2 Social Security
- **What:** Union Cabinet (16 Sep 2026) raised the mandatory EPF/EPS coverage wage ceiling, unchanged since Sept 2014; the PIB backgrounder places it within the wider social-security reform story.
- **Key facts:** >51 lakh more employees to come under mandatory cover; employees in the ₹15,000–25,000 band gain PF, EPS pension and EDLI protection; estimated annual government outgo ~₹11,339 crore (5-year ~₹56,696 crore); contribution rates not stated as changed. Backgrounder context: social protection coverage 68.4% (2026) vs 19% (2015); ~7.98 crore EPFO contributing members, ~82 lakh pensioners; Code on Social Security, 2020 in force from 21 Nov 2025; e-Shram 31.89 crore registrations (Aug 2026). *(Backgrounder figures via a secondary PIB summary; wage-ceiling facts verified across multiple outlets.)*
- **Update vs earlier coverage:** the 30 Oct briefing covered the National Social Security Board for gig/unorganised workers; this is the EPFO wage-ceiling piece.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (labour reforms); GS2 Social Justice.

### Scheme-in-focus: **VB–G RAM G (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin)) — governed by the VB–G RAM G Act, 2025**
- **Ministry:** Rural Development. **Objective:** statutory rural employment and livelihood guarantee (the Centre's successor framework to the earlier rural job guarantee law, as widely reported; confirm entitlement details on pib.gov.in).
- **Features (verified this run):** statute-backed; Grievance Redressal Rules 2026 with 3-tier officers, DPI digital platform, 7-day enquiry / 15-day resolution / auto-escalation; appeals per Schedule I; states have been rolling it out (e.g., Punjab from 1 July 2026).
- **Beneficiaries:** rural households seeking work. **Significance:** pairs entitlement with enforceable accountability.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about the contracts for Next Generation Corvettes signed by the Ministry of Defence on 2 November 2026:
1. Contracts worth ₹53,866.50 crore cover eight ships.
2. Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers will build five and Goa Shipyard Limited three.
3. The ships are designed by a foreign shipyard and built in India under licence.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Under the VB–G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026, which of the following is correct?
(a) Only Gram Panchayat level officers handle complaints
(b) Enquiry within 7 working days and resolution within 15 days, with unresolved complaints escalated to the next higher level
(c) Complaints can be accepted only through the online platform
(d) Complaints unresolved in 15 days are automatically closed

**Q3.** The Union Cabinet's September 2026 decision on the EPFO wage ceiling:
(a) raised it from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month, bringing over 51 lakh more employees under mandatory coverage
(b) raised it from ₹6,500 to ₹15,000 per month
(c) abolished the ceiling for mandatory EPF coverage
(d) raised it from ₹15,000 to ₹21,000 per month for only EPS

## Answers
1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; the Navy designs the ships (not a foreign yard), so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — Three-tier mechanism (GP, Block, District); multiple filing modes incl. in person, speed post, DPI platform, helplines; unresolved complaints auto-escalate.
3. **(a)** — Ceiling raised from ₹15,000 (set 2014) to ₹25,000; >51 lakh more employees covered; ₹6,500→₹15,000 was the 2014 change.
