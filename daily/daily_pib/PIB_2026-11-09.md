## 1. Ministry of Science & Technology / MEA (with European Commission): **India–EU conclude negotiations on India's association with Horizon Europe** (announced 6–7 Nov 2026) — GS3 Sci-Tech / GS2 IR
- **What:** Negotiations for India to become an *Associated Country* of Horizon Europe, the EU's research & innovation framework programme (2021–2027, budget ~€93.5 billion), have concluded. Exploratory talks began Feb 2026; formal talks July 2026. Formal agreement is expected to be signed around Jan 2027, subject to procedures on both sides.
- **Why it matters:** Indian researchers, institutions and industry can bid for funding and lead collaborative projects (notably under Pillar II, "Global Challenges and European Industrial Competitiveness": health, digital, industry, space, climate, energy, bioeconomy) on equal terms with EU counterparts, in return for a financial contribution to the programme. Complements the India–EU Trade & Technology Council and FTA push.
- **Key facts:** Horizon Europe 2021–27; €93.5 bn; association agreement ~Jan 2027; reciprocal access to research infrastructure.

## 2. Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs: **10th Swachh Survekshan (2025-26) awards — President to confer** (PIB listing, 9 Nov 2026) — GS2 Governance / Urban
- **What:** The President is to present the awards of the 10th edition of the annual urban cleanliness survey under Swachh Bharat Mission–Urban. Results/winners were not retrievable at the time of this run — check PIB for the winners list.
- **Why it matters / design of this edition (launched 20 Dec 2025, Bhopal):** Citizen-feedback weightage raised from ~32% to ~35%; "Ganga towns" category renamed "River towns"; separate matrix for coastal cities; new indicators on open defecation/urination, tourist-heritage-religious areas, school behaviour-change programmes. Five population categories (Very Small, Small, Medium, Big, Million Plus). **Super Swachh League** expanded to Indore, Navi Mumbai, Surat, Vijayawada plus Ahmedabad, Bhopal, Lucknow.
- **Scheme link — Swachh Shehar Jodi** (Sept 2025, SBM-U): pairs 72 mentor cities with 200 mentee cities; a new award recognises best pairs.
- *(Previous edition for context: Indore ranked cleanest for the 8th consecutive time in 2024-25, with Surat second.)*

## 3. Department of Chemicals & Petrochemicals: **PIB Backgrounder — India's chemicals sector and the three operational PCPIRs** (7 Nov 2026)* — GS3 Economy / Industry
- **What:** The backgrounder says the three operational Petroleum, Chemicals & Petrochemicals Investment Regions (PCPIRs) — **Dahej (Gujarat), Visakhapatnam–Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh), Paradeep (Odisha)** — have drawn cumulative investment of about **₹3.4–3.5 lakh crore (₹3,49,192 crore per the Dept's Aug 2026 figures)**, ~2,200+ functional chemical units and ~3.7 lakh jobs.
- **Sector facts:** India is the 6th-largest chemicals producer globally and 4th in Asia; ~USD 220 bn today, projected ~USD 300 bn by 2030. Most chemical sub-sectors permit 100% FDI via the automatic route; the RDI scheme is open to chemicals; Budget 2026 announced a ₹600 crore chemical-parks scheme with three dedicated clusters. Major Accident Hazard (MAH) workforce-training programme under Viksit Bharat@2047 plan targets all 2,393 MAH units over 5 years.
- **Significance:** Cluster-based, infrastructure-sharing model to cut logistics cost and import dependence in a strategically important input industry.

### Scheme-in-focus: Swachh Bharat Mission–Urban (Swachh Survekshan & Swachh Shehar Jodi)
- **Ministry:** MoHUA. **Objective:** Garbage-free, open-defecation-free, sustainably sanitised cities; Swachh Survekshan is the annual competitive, citizen-weighted ranking tool.
- **Features:** (1) Population-tiered rankings and star/ODF++/Water+ style certifications; (2) year-round citizen feedback via apps/QR codes; (3) Super Swachh League for repeat top performers; (4) Swachh Shehar Jodi mentor–mentee city pairing.
- **Coverage:** All urban local bodies formed on/before 31 Dec 2024. **Significance:** Competitive federalism and behaviour change in urban governance.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to India's association with Horizon Europe (reported Nov 2026), consider:
1. Horizon Europe is the EU's research and innovation framework programme for 2021–2027.
2. Under association, Indian entities can lead collaborative projects and receive direct EU funding.
3. Associated countries are not required to make any financial contribution to the programme.

Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Swachh Survekshan 2025-26 (10th edition) has introduced which of the following?
(a) Abolition of citizen-feedback weightage (b) Renaming of "Ganga towns" as "River towns" and a separate matrix for coastal cities (c) Ranking only of cities above 10 lakh population (d) Conduct by the Ministry of Jal Shakti

**Q3.** Which one of the following sets lists the operational PCPIRs in India?
(a) Dahej, Haldia, Paradeep (b) Kochi, Visakhapatnam–Kakinada, Paradeep (c) Dahej, Visakhapatnam–Kakinada, Paradeep (d) Dahej, Visakhapatnam–Kakinada, Ennore

## Answers
1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; associated countries contribute financially to the programme, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — Citizen-feedback weightage was *raised* (~32%→~35%); the survey is run by MoHUA for all size categories; "Ganga towns" became "River towns" and coastal cities got a separate matrix.
3. **(c)** — The three operational PCPIRs per the Department are Dahej (Gujarat), Visakhapatnam–Kakinada (Andhra Pradesh) and Paradeep (Odisha).
