## 1. Ministry of Defence: ₹4,106 crore contracts for the GSAT-7C satellite (IAF) and Varunastra heavyweight torpedoes (Navy) (signed 15 Oct, New Delhi)
- **What:** The Ministry of Defence signed two indigenous-procurement contracts worth about ₹4,106 crore. Both were signed in the presence of the DG (Acquisition), Department of Defence.
  - **GSAT-7C (₹2,563 crore):** A communication satellite for the Indian Air Force, supplied by **NewSpace India Limited (NSIL), Bengaluru**, a Government of India company under the Department of Space. It is described as indigenously designed, developed and manufactured. It gives the IAF a dedicated communication capability and complements the existing GSAT-7A, with secure links over greater distances.
  - **Varunastra (₹1,543 crore):** A ship-launched heavyweight anti-submarine torpedo for the Indian Navy, supplied by **Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL), Hyderabad**. It was developed by the **Naval Science and Technological Laboratory (NSTL), a DRDO lab**.
- **Key facts:** Varunastra is 533.4 mm in diameter and 7.78 m long. It runs at 27–40 knots and operates at depths of 8–600 m. It uses acoustic homing with counter-countermeasure features. Both deals are tied to Aatmanirbhar Bharat and Make in India.
- **Why it matters:** The deals show defence demand being routed through the Space PSU (NSIL) and the Defence PSU (BDL), using DRDO designs. This is the demand-led indigenisation model.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security / Science & Tech (defence indigenisation).

## 2. Department of Space: India's space economy could reach USD 40–45 billion in 7–8 years (17 Oct, New Delhi) *(UPDATE: only a one-line mention on 18 Oct; details below)*
- **What:** MoS (Independent Charge) Dr Jitendra Singh spoke at a Bloomberg New Economy Forum roundtable, "Space, Sovereignty and New Economy". He said the sector could grow from about **USD 9 billion** now to **USD 40–45 billion in 7–8 years**, nearly five times its present size.
- **Drivers he named:**
  - More domestic and foreign investment.
  - Predictable demand, with government institutions acting as **anchor customers** and backing proof-of-concept projects.
  - Faster regulatory approvals, including installation and export licences.
  - Lower business costs, stronger supply-chain integration, and GST reforms that give practical benefits for space products.
- **Why it matters:** He credited the 2019–20 opening of the sector to private players for the progress so far. The stated next priority is turning technical capability into sustainable businesses, in satellite communication, Earth observation and geospatial applications.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech / Economy.

## 3. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare: Mahila Kisan Diwas 2026 (15 Oct, New Delhi)
- **What:** The Department of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare observed Mahila Kisan Diwas, held at the Dr. B. R. Ambedkar International Centre. Its Secretary chaired the event.
- **Details:** The theme was "Role of Women Farmers in Building Developed Agriculture and a Developed India". Outstanding women farmers were felicitated, and the book *Women Farmers of India: Journey from Challenges to Success* was released.
- **Why it matters:** The day follows the International Day of Rural Women (15 Oct). It spotlights women's role in agriculture and feeds into themes such as the feminisation of agriculture, land and credit access, SHGs and agri-extension.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture / GS1 Society (women's role).

## 4. Ministry of Culture: Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar for 2024 and 2025 (to be conferred 19 Oct, New Delhi)
- **What:** Culture and Tourism Minister Gajendra Singh Shekhawat is to confer the Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar for 2024 and 2025 on 19 October 2026. The PIB text I could access did not list the awardees.
- **Background (general knowledge, not from the release):** The award is given by the Sangeet Natak Akademi to young artists in music, dance and theatre. It is named after the shehnai maestro Bharat Ratna Ustad Bismillah Khan. Pairing two years in one ceremony suggests earlier delays.
- **Notebook:** GS1 Art & Culture.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the recent defence contracts signed by the Ministry of Defence, consider the following statements:
1. The GSAT-7C communication satellite is being procured from NewSpace India Limited for the Indian Air Force.
2. The Varunastra torpedo was developed by the Naval Science and Technological Laboratory, a DRDO laboratory.
3. Varunastra is an air-launched anti-ship missile.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Recently, a Union Minister said India's space economy could grow from about USD 9 billion to USD 40–45 billion in 7–8 years. Which of the following was highlighted as a growth enabler?
1. Government institutions acting as anchor customers
2. Faster installation and export licence approvals
3. Prohibiting private participation in launch services

Select the correct answer using the code given below:
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The Ustad Bismillah Khan Yuva Puraskar, in the news recently, is administered by which of the following?
(a) Lalit Kala Akademi
(b) Sahitya Akademi
(c) Sangeet Natak Akademi
(d) National School of Drama

## Answers

1. **(a)** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Varunastra is a ship-launched anti-submarine torpedo, not an air-launched missile, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** Anchor government customers and faster approvals were named. Private participation was welcomed, not prohibited, so 3 is wrong.
3. **(c)** The award is a Sangeet Natak Akademi honour for young performing artists, under the Ministry of Culture. *This rests on general knowledge; the PIB release text did not state the administering body.*
