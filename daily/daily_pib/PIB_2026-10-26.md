## 1. Ministry of Home Affairs (Narcotics Control Bureau, with ED and State police): **"Operation Trident" — simultaneous strike on a Myanmar-linked narco-financial network** (operation 23 Oct; announced 25 Oct 2026)

**What / why it matters:** NCB launched a multi-agency operation with 38 teams hitting 38 locations at once across Assam, Mizoram and Manipur (e.g. Aizawl, Champhai, Zokhawthar, Churachandpur, Cachar), targeting a cartel moving methamphetamine and heroin from Myanmar towards India and Bangladesh. The emphasis was on the *money trail* (hawala), not only on drug seizures. Union Home Minister Amit Shah reiterated the goal of dismantling drug networks by 31 Dec 2029.

**Key facts**
- ~₹100 crore of hawala transactions traced; 1,424 bank accounts frozen; 18 arrests.
- Property worth ₹17.82 crore attached; 500 detonators and ₹12 lakh cash seized (figures as stated by the Home Minister).
- Route described as the "Death Triangle Corridor" (Myanmar–Mizoram–Manipur–Assam, extending to Bangladesh); a Myanmar-based kingpin is alleged to lead the network.
- Legal tools: NDPS Act, 1985; PMLA, 2002 (attachment/freezing). NCB was constituted in 1986.

**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (drug trafficking–terror/organised-crime nexus, border management, Golden Triangle/Crescent) ; GS2 (Centre–State/agency coordination).

## 2. Prime Minister's Office / RBI (Ministry of Finance sphere): **PM inaugurates RBI's new Data Centre and Enterprise Computing & Cybersecurity Training Institute, Bhubaneswar** (25 Oct 2026)

**What / why it matters:** A greenfield disaster-recovery-grade data centre for the central bank, supporting currency management, payment and settlement systems and regulatory data, plus a training institute for cybersecurity and emerging technologies. It strengthens resilience of critical financial infrastructure (a recurring Mains theme) and spreads such infrastructure beyond Mumbai.

**Key facts** (project details from pre-event reports; PIB listing confirms the inauguration)
- Location: Info Valley-II, Khordha district, near Bhubaneswar, Odisha; ~18.55-acre campus; reported cost ~₹169 crore.
- Acts as a backup to RBI's primary data centre at Kharghar, Navi Mumbai; foundation stone laid 22 Mar 2023.
- Chosen partly for seismic stability and to diversify cyber/concentration risk.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (financial-sector infrastructure, cyber-resilience); GS3 Sci-Tech/Security (critical information infrastructure).

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to "Operation Trident" in the news, consider the following statements:
1. It was conducted by the Narcotics Control Bureau along with the Enforcement Directorate and State police.
2. It targeted a network trafficking drugs from Myanmar through Mizoram, Manipur and Assam.
3. It is carried out under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The Narcotics Control Bureau functions under the:
(a) Ministry of Finance (b) Ministry of Home Affairs (c) Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (d) Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment

**Q3.** RBI's new data centre inaugurated in October 2026 is located near:
(a) Chennai, Tamil Nadu (b) Hyderabad, Telangana (c) Bhubaneswar, Odisha (d) Nagpur, Maharashtra

## Answers
1. **(b)** — NCB with ED and State police ran it, targeting the Myanmar-linked route; it is a Home Ministry matter, not Social Justice (that ministry runs Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan).
2. **(b)** — NCB is the apex drug law-enforcement agency under the Ministry of Home Affairs, constituted in 1986.
3. **(c)** — The RBI data centre and cybersecurity training institute is at Info Valley-II, Khordha, near Bhubaneswar; it backs up the Kharghar (Navi Mumbai) centre.
