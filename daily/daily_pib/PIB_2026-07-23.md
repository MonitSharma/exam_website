## 1. India hosts the 16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting in Chandigarh (22–24 July); traditional-medicine vertical created
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (BRICS Chairship 2026)

- **What it is:** Under its **BRICS Chairship 2026**, India is hosting the **16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting** in **Chandigarh from 22–24 July 2026** (Senior Officials' Meeting on 22 July; Ministerial on 23 July, chaired by Union Health Minister **J.P. Nadda**). Guiding theme: **"Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability."**
- **Why it matters:** BRICS now has **11 members** (Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa + newer entrants Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Indonesia) representing a large share of the global disease burden and generic-medicine supply. A key outcome: BRICS agreed to establish a **dedicated institutional vertical and expert working group for Traditional, Complementary and Integrative Medicine (TCIM)** under India's chairmanship — a diplomatic win aligning with India's AYUSH push.
- **Key facts:** Focus areas — health security, pandemic preparedness, digital health, affordable medicines, TB, mental wellness, healthy lifestyles and resilient health systems.
- **Destination notebook:** GS2 (International Relations / Health governance).

## 2. ICMR-NIN-led "Let's Fix Our Food" (LFOF) Consortium releases policy priorities for healthier diets of children & adolescents
**Issuing body:** ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition / Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (22 July 2026, New Delhi)

- **What it is:** The **ICMR-NIN-led "Let's Fix Our Food" (LFOF) Consortium** released a policy document — **"Priority Policy Actions to Promote Healthier Diets and Food Environments for Children and Adolescents in India."** It was launched by **Dr. M. Srinivas, Member (Health), NITI Aayog** at the LFOF Dissemination & Consultation Workshop, India Habitat Centre.
- **Why it matters:** Targets India's rising **childhood obesity and diet-related non-communicable diseases (NCDs)** via evidence-based action on **school food environments, unhealthy-food marketing, nutrition education, dietary behaviour and fiscal policy** (e.g., taxing HFSS — high fat/sugar/salt — foods).
- **Key facts:** LFOF is a **multi-institutional platform led by ICMR-NIN** with partners including **UNICEF India, WHO, FSSAI, PHFI, WFP, IFPRI, Lady Irwin College** and others.
- **Destination notebook:** GS2 (Health/Governance) & GS1 (Society).

## 3. Rural Development spotlights RSETIs under DAY-NRLM — a rural entrepreneurship success story
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Rural Development (PIB feature, 22 July 2026)

- **What it is:** A Ministry feature — *"Journeys of grit, pathways of growth"* — on **Rural Self Employment Training Institutes (RSETIs)** operating under the **Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)**, with human-interest case studies from Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra and Karnataka.
- **Why it matters:** RSETIs are a bank-led rural skilling-plus-credit model — a template for **"train → finance → self-employment"** that UPSC frames under livelihood security and financial inclusion.
- **Key facts:** As of **March 2026, 632 RSETIs** operate in **33 States/UTs, 619 districts**, backed by **25 sponsor banks**. Since inception (2009) to Feb 2026 they trained **60.63 lakh** candidates (99.7% of target); **43.89 lakh** secured sustainable livelihoods and **22.52 lakh** received bank finance. **2024-25** saw a record **6.21 lakh** trained in a year.

### Scheme-in-focus: DAY-NRLM
- **Ministry:** Rural Development.
- **Objective:** Reduce rural poverty by organising the poor — especially women — into **Self-Help Groups (SHGs)** and federations, and building sustainable livelihoods with financial linkage.
- **Key features:** Universal social mobilisation into SHGs; revolving fund & community investment support; **interest subvention / bank credit** linkage; skilling arms (RSETIs, DDU-GKY).
- **Beneficiaries:** Rural poor households, predominantly women SHG members.
- **Significance:** Backbone of India's rural livelihoods and women's economic empowerment architecture.
- **Destination notebook:** GS2 (Governance/Welfare) & GS3 (Inclusive growth, employment).

## 4. Centre approves Totapuri mango (MIS) and copra (PSS) procurement for Tamil Nadu farmers
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (approval by Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan)

- **What it is:** The Centre cleared procurement of **96,879 MT of Totapuri mangoes** under the **Market Intervention Scheme (MIS)** at a **Market Intervention Price of ₹1,545.41/quintal**, and **87,226 MT of copra** (87,000 MT milling + 226 MT ball copra) under the **Price Support Scheme (PSS)** at MSP, with an MSP outlay of over **₹1,049 crore**.
- **Why it matters:** A textbook example of India's **price-stabilisation toolkit** for farmers when market prices crash — crucial for the exam because it distinguishes **MIS (perishable horticultural/agri commodities NOT under MSP)** from **PSS (notified MSP crops — oilseeds, pulses, copra)**.
- **Key facts:** MIS is a **centrally sponsored** scheme triggered on a **State's request**, with loss (if any) shared **50:50** between Centre and State (75:25 for NE States); implemented via **NAFED / NCCF**.
- **Destination notebook:** GS3 (Agriculture — MSP, procurement, price policy).

## 5. Parliament update: Nuclear Energy Mission for *Viksit Bharat* — the roadmap to 100 GW by 2047
**Issuing body:** Department of Atomic Energy (PIB / Parliament reply, 22 July 2026)

- **What it is:** In a written Parliament reply, the Government detailed progress on the **Nuclear Energy Mission for Viksit Bharat**, announced in **Union Budget 2025-26**, targeting **100 GW of nuclear power by 2047**.
- **Why it matters:** Nuclear power is central to India's **net-zero-by-2070** and energy-security strategy. Parliament has enacted the **SHANTI Act, 2025**, which (with amendments to the Atomic Energy Act and the Civil Liability for Nuclear Damage Act) opens the sector to **private participation** — a major structural reform.
- **Key facts:** Mission targets **at least five indigenous Small Modular Reactors (SMRs)** operational by **2033**, backed by **₹20,000 crore**; capacity to rise to about **22,480 MW by 2031-32** from projects under construction. New reactors planned at brownfield/greenfield sites and to **replace retiring coal units**.
- **Destination notebook:** GS3 (Science & Technology / Energy & Economy).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the 16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting (July 2026):
1. It was hosted by India in Chandigarh under India's BRICS Chairship 2026.
2. BRICS agreed to establish a dedicated working group for Traditional, Complementary and Integrative Medicine.
3. BRICS currently has exactly five member countries.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to India's agricultural price-support instruments, consider the following:
1. The Price Support Scheme (PSS) is used to procure notified MSP crops such as oilseeds, pulses and copra.
2. The Market Intervention Scheme (MIS) is implemented on the request of a State/UT for perishable and horticultural commodities generally not covered under MSP.
3. Under MIS, procurement agencies such as NAFED/NCCF are typically engaged.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** With reference to the "Nuclear Energy Mission for Viksit Bharat," consider the following statements:
1. It aims to achieve 100 GW of installed nuclear power capacity by 2047.
2. It envisages the development of indigenous Small Modular Reactors (SMRs).
3. It was announced in the Union Budget 2025-26.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** India hosted the 16th BRICS Health Ministers' Meeting in Chandigarh (22–24 July 2026) under its 2026 Chairship, and members agreed to set up a dedicated TCIM working group. Statement 3 is wrong — BRICS has expanded to 11 members.

**Q2 — (d).** All three are correct. PSS covers notified MSP commodities (incl. copra); MIS is a State-triggered scheme for perishable/horticultural produce outside MSP; both are operationalised through agencies like NAFED and NCCF.

**Q3 — (d).** All three are correct. The Mission, announced in Budget 2025-26, targets 100 GW nuclear by 2047 and at least five indigenous SMRs (by 2033).
