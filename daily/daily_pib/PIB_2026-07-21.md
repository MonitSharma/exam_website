## 1. President Murmu's Europe tour: historic firsts in Moldova and North Macedonia

**Issuing body:** President's Secretariat / Ministry of External Affairs

President Droupadi Murmu is on a three-nation State Visit to **Moldova, North Macedonia and Romania (19–25 July 2026)** — the first-ever visit by an Indian President to each of these three countries. Her Chisinau leg concluded on 20 July; she arrived in **Skopje on 21 July** for a two-day visit (21–22 July).

**Key outcomes (Moldova, 20 July):**

- Agreement to expand cooperation in **trade, investment, supply chains and connectivity**, plus agriculture, healthcare, renewable energy, **Digital Public Infrastructure, AI and innovation**.
- India to **double ITEC training slots** for Moldovan professionals, including specialised AI and emerging-technology courses; a seat offered in the forthcoming course for foreign diplomats at the **Sushma Swaraj Institute of Foreign Service** on AI and cyber diplomacy.
- Moldova to join the **Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (CDRI)** and the **Global Biofuels Alliance (GBA)**; ongoing cooperation under the **International Solar Alliance (ISA)**.
- A new bilateral **higher-education cooperation agreement** to be signed.

**North Macedonia (21–22 July):** talks with President Gordana Siljanovska-Davkova, meetings with PM Hristijan Mickoski and the President of the Assembly, an **address to the national Assembly**, and the **India–North Macedonia Business Forum**.

**Why it matters:** A deliberate widening of India's diplomatic footprint in **Central and Eastern Europe / the Western Balkans** — a region where India has historically had thin high-level engagement but which matters for EU-market access, connectivity corridors and plurilateral coalition-building (ISA/CDRI/GBA as instruments of Indian "coalition diplomacy").

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (India and its bilateral relations; groupings involving India).

## 2. Kharif sowing down 6.04% year-on-year as on 17 July; pulses acreage worst hit

**Issuing ministry:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

The Ministry's weekly kharif area-coverage release (data as on **17.07.2026**) shows the area sown to kharif crops at **658.19 lakh hectares**, down **42.28 lakh ha (6.04%)** from **700.47 lakh ha** in the corresponding period last year. The same day, the Ministry's **weekly review meeting** took stock of fertiliser and seed availability and the **monsoon and El Niño situation**.

**Key figures:**

| Crop / group | 2026 (lakh ha) | 2025 (lakh ha) | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| **All kharif crops** | 658.19 | 700.47 | −6.04% |
| **Rice** | 166.41 | 167.83 | −0.84% |
| **Pulses (total)** | 69.23 | 81.52 | −15.08% |
| Arhar/tur | 24.80 | 30.17 | −17.78% |
| Moongbean | — | — | −11.02% |
| Urdbean | — | — | −5.92% |

Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan has publicly held that the **acreage gap can still be bridged since sowing continues till around 15 August**.

**Why it matters:** Pulses are India's chronic import-dependence point; a 15% acreage fall this early feeds directly into food-inflation and import-bill risk. The item also links the **El Niño–monsoon–sowing–inflation** chain that GS3 keeps returning to.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Agriculture (cropping patterns, MSP/food security) and Economy (food inflation).

## 3. Five new lichen species of the genus *Allographa* described from the Western Ghats

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Science & Technology — Department of Science & Technology (DST); work by **MACS–Agharkar Research Institute (ARI), Pune**, an autonomous DST institute

A study presenting an **updated molecular phylogeny of the genus *Allographa*** has described **five new species** alongside five known species from the Western Ghats: ***A. persistinspersa, A. semicarbonisata, A. keralensis, A. nayakae*** and ***A. paracarbonisata***. Species boundaries and evolutionary relationships were resolved using the molecular markers **mtSSU, nrLSU and RPB2**. The work was supported by **ANRF (formerly SERB), DST**.

**Static + why it matters:**

- **Lichens** are a symbiotic association of a fungus (mycobiont) with an alga and/or cyanobacterium (photobiont) — classic **bio-indicators of air quality** and of undisturbed forest.
- *Allographa* belongs to family **Graphidaceae** (order Graphidales) — "script lichens", crustose forms typical of humid tropical forests.
- This builds on the earlier ARI description of ***Allographa effusosoredica***, the first Indian *Allographa* species confirmed by DNA sequencing (the 53rd from India, 22nd from the Western Ghats).
- Reinforces the **Western Ghats as a global biodiversity hotspot** and the shift in Indian taxonomy from purely morphological to **integrative molecular taxonomy**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment & Biodiversity; Science & Technology (indigenous research capacity).

## 4. AYUSH integration into national health systems: standardised terminologies and digital health

**Issuing ministry:** Ministry of AYUSH

Among today's PIB releases is a cluster of Parliament replies from the Ministry of AYUSH on **integration of AYUSH in national health systems**, **promotion of AYUSH-based medical tourism**, **promotion of medicinal plants under the NMPB**, **digital initiatives**, and **market access and quality-assurance mechanisms for AYUSH products**.

The substantive thrust: in July 2026 the Ministry ran a **five-day workshop on the WHO–ICHI framework** (International Classification of Health Interventions) for **Ayurveda, Siddha and Unani (ASU)** systems, to finalise a layered hierarchy of **National Health Intervention Codes (NHIC)**. Secretary (AYUSH) **Vaidya Rajesh Kotecha** framed the objective as making AYUSH interventions codable to modern health-informatics standards so they sit inside digital health ecosystems and **Universal Health Coverage** frameworks.

**Supporting policy context:** Union Budget 2026 measures include new **All India Institutes of Ayurveda (AIIAs)**, upgradation of AYUSH pharmacies and drug-testing laboratories, integration of AYUSH wellness centres into regional **Medical Value Tourism (MVT)** hubs, and training of caregivers in yoga and wellness skills.

**Why it matters:** Standardised terminology is the unglamorous precondition for insurance coverage, evidence generation and global acceptance of traditional medicine — the same logic as WHO's earlier **ICD-11 Traditional Medicine chapter**. Also links to the **WHO Global Traditional Medicine Centre, Jamnagar**.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (health, government policies and interventions); GS3 — Science & Technology.

## 5. Market access for PM Vishwakarma artisans at airports

**Issuing ministries:** Ministry of MSME with Ministry of Civil Aviation / Airports Authority of India (AAI)

A PIB release today covers **empowerment of PM Vishwakarma (PMV) artisans through better market access at airports**. The delivery vehicle is AAI's **AVSAR scheme — "Airport as Venue for Skilled Artisans of the Region"** — under which AAI allots space at its airports to Self-Help Groups and artisans to display and sell region-specific handmade products.

**Scheme recap — PM Vishwakarma:**

- **Ministry:** Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME); launched 17 September 2023.
- **Objective:** support **traditional artisans and craftspeople** working with hands and tools in 18 identified trades.
- **Key features:** free skill training with stipend; **modern toolkit incentive**; **collateral-free credit up to ₹3 lakh** (₹1 lakh + ₹2 lakh tranches) at concessional interest; incentive for **digital transactions**; **marketing and branding support**.
- **Recent additions:** over **2,500 artisans trained in AI tools** by the MSME Ministry; capacity-building programmes on branding and marketing (e.g. at Kashmir Government Polytechnic, Srinagar); the **ODOP/OSOP-linked** outreach including Divyangjan Vishwakarma artisans.

**Why it matters:** A textbook case of the **"last-mile market access"** problem — credit and skilling alone don't lift artisan incomes without a demand-side channel. Airports offer high-footfall, high-willingness-to-pay retail without the artisan bearing rent.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (inclusive growth, MSME); GS2 — Government schemes for vulnerable sections.

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** *Allographa* is a genus of crustose "script lichens" in the family Graphidaceae (order Graphidales); lichens are a mycobiont–photobiont symbiosis; five new species were described from the Western Ghats by MACS–ARI, Pune under DST.

**Q2 — (c) Both 1 and 2.** Total kharif area was 658.19 lakh ha against 700.47 lakh ha a year earlier (−6.04%); pulses fell 15.08% (69.23 from 81.52 lakh ha) against rice's −0.84% (166.41 from 167.83 lakh ha).

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are outcomes of the 20 July 2026 Chisinau leg — the first visit by an Indian President to Moldova, Moldova's accession to CDRI and GBA, and doubling of ITEC slots including AI courses.
