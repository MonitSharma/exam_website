## 1. Mission "Golden Spice" launched to build a global value chain for Meghalaya's Lakadong turmeric

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Development of North Eastern Region (MDoNER), with the Government of Meghalaya.

Union Minister Jyotiraditya M. Scindia and Meghalaya CM Conrad K. Sangma launched the ₹175.45-crore **"Mission Golden Spice — Integrated Lakadong Turmeric Value Chain Enhancement Project."** It is a convergence-led, five-year roadmap (2025–2030) to transform the GI-tagged **Lakadong turmeric** — prized for its exceptionally high curcumin content (~7–12%) — into a premium global brand. It matters as a model for how GI monetisation and value-chain investment can lift a niche North-East horticultural crop into export markets while being overwhelmingly women-led.

**Key facts:** Two phases — (1) Value-Chain Strengthening, (2) Expansion & Scale-up. Currently ~2,600 ha yielding ~10,000 MT/year, grown mainly in West & East Jaintia Hills. Target: nearly triple cultivation to **7,000 ha**, benefiting ~**17,500 farmers (99% women)** and creating ~3,000 jobs. Focus areas: modern processing, cold-chain, cutting post-harvest losses, branding, and export linkages.

## 2. India launches its first postdoctoral fellowship in Space Medicine & Bioastronautics (ISRO–SCTIMST)

**Ministry / body:** Department of Space — ISRO's **Human Space Flight Centre (HSFC)** with the **Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology (SCTIMST)**, Thiruvananthapuram (an Institute of National Importance).

HSFC and SCTIMST signed an Implementing Agreement to jointly pursue research and training in **Bioastronautics** — the study of how spaceflight conditions (microgravity, cosmic radiation, isolation) affect the human body — and launched **India's first two-year postdoctoral fellowship** in the field, starting the July 2026 academic session. It matters because human-rated missions like **Gaganyaan** and the planned **Bharatiya Antariksh Station** require indigenous biomedical expertise to keep astronauts safe; India has so far relied heavily on foreign space-medicine know-how.

**Key facts:** Two fellowships advertised each year; open to candidates with PG medical degrees, super-speciality qualifications, or relevant PhDs. Fellows train and research across both institutions. Complements ISRO's recent first Integrated Air Drop Test for Gaganyaan.

*Destination notebook:* **GS3 — Science & Technology** (space, human spaceflight, indigenisation).

## 3. Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026 — statutory protection for 'Vande Mataram'

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Home Affairs; introduced in the Rajya Sabha (Monsoon Session, from 20 July 2026).

Home Minister Amit Shah introduced the **Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Bill, 2026**, which seeks to extend to the National Song **'Vande Mataram'** the same statutory protection currently enjoyed by the National Anthem **'Jana Gana Mana'**. It matters as a live Polity/legislation topic: it amends the parent **Act of 1971** (which protects the National Flag, Constitution and National Anthem) and drew significant opposition debate on the floor.

**Key facts:** Amends the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971. Makes intentional insult, disruption or obstruction of the singing of Vande Mataram a punishable offence carrying **up to three years' imprisonment**. 'Vande Mataram' has long been recognised as the National Song but lacked statutory protection until now.

*Destination notebook:* **GS2 — Polity & Governance** (national symbols, related laws).

## 5. Ministry of AYUSH opens "Prakriti Café" — first workplace wellness initiative

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of AYUSH (MoS Independent Charge Prataprao Jadhav), at Ayush Bhawan, New Delhi.

The Ministry inaugurated **Prakriti Café**, its first-of-its-kind in-house café built around the principles of **Ayush Ahaar** — wholesome, balanced diets rooted in India's traditional knowledge systems. It matters as a small but exam-relevant marker of the government's push on **preventive healthcare** through yoga, naturopathy and dietetics, aligning food choices with public-health goals.

**Key facts:** Menu features millet- and jaggery-based, plant-forward dishes (millet dhokla, little-millet upma, makhana kheer, kosambari) and herbal beverages (tulsi-ginger tea). Positioned as an "affordable healthy meals" model for offices, extending the Ayush Ahaar concept into everyday routines.
