## 1. Ministry of Earth Sciences: **MoES–AcSIR MoU — all 10 MoES institutes become AcSIR Associated Research Institutes for PhD programmes** (signed 21 Aug 2026; announced ~13 Oct 2026)
- **What it is:** A partnership giving MoES research institutes a common route to run research-based PhD programmes through the Academy of Scientific and Innovative Research (AcSIR). Selected scholars research at a host MoES institute under MoES scientists recognised as AcSIR faculty; **AcSIR awards the degree** and the host institute is named on the certificate. Coordinators are appointed at institute and ministry levels.
- **Why it matters:** Builds a doctoral pipeline for weather/climate, ocean, polar, seismology and coastal sciences — supports Deep Ocean Mission, Mission Mausam and climate-science capacity.
- **Key facts:** 10 MoES institutes; participating in the latest PhD enrolment cycle; areas include weather & climate, oceans, polar regions, earthquakes, coastal processes, marine robotics and AI in earth sciences. (Seat numbers/funding not stated.)
- **Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech / GS2 Education (higher-education & research capacity).

## 2. Ministry of Coal (Coal Controller Organisation): **SUVIKALP — data-driven mine repurposing tool wins SKOCH Gold Award 2026** (award presented 26 Sep 2026; highlighted 21–22 Oct)
- **What it is:** An in-house tool of the Coal Controller Organisation (CCO) that evaluates and ranks post-mining reuse options for closed/abandoned mines against technical, environmental, social and economic criteria. Includes **PRISM (Post-Mining Repurposing Implementation Strategy Model)** with project concepts and case studies; CCO also ran 12 "SUVIKALP Samvaad" webinars.
- **Why it matters:** Supports just transition and sustainable mine closure — converting mined-out land into solar, eco-parks, water bodies or agriculture instead of leaving it degraded.
- **Key facts:** Award category: "Digital Operational Efficiency – Data Driven Decision Making"; CCO functions under the Ministry of Coal.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment / Economy (mining, just transition).

## 3. DoPT / Karmayogi Bharat: **MoU with OpenAI to bring OpenAI Academy AI-training content to the iGOT Karmayogi platform** (announced 21 Oct 2026)
- **What it is:** Karmayogi Bharat (government-owned not-for-profit company under Mission Karmayogi) will integrate approved OpenAI Academy learning resources into **iGOT Karmayogi** so civil servants can build practical, responsible AI skills for administration and service delivery.
- **Why it matters:** Capacity building for AI-enabled governance (links to IndiaAI Mission, Mission Karmayogi/NPCSCB); also raises debate on data sovereignty, vendor dependence and neutrality in public training.
- **Key facts:** Over **1.7 crore** government employees (Centre, States, UTs) registered on iGOT Karmayogi (figure quoted by OpenAI India). Duration, financial terms and number of courses not disclosed.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (civil-service reforms) / GS3 Sci-Tech (AI).

## 4. Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers (Dept. of Chemicals & Petrochemicals): **India Chem 2026 (14th edition), Mumbai, 22–24 Oct** *(single secondary source for details)*
- **What it is:** FICCI-partnered flagship chemicals-industry event at NESCO, Mumbai; theme **"Advantage Bharat: An Emerging Global Chemicals Hub"**; inaugurated by Union Minister J.P. Nadda. FICCI–EY knowledge paper released; Youth Innovation Challenge launched; Japan is Partner Country; seven States showcased industrial hubs.

- **Key facts:** Sector targets quoted: USD 300 bn by 2030 and USD 1 trn by 2040; 200+ exhibitors, 50+ global CEOs, 100+ countries represented.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (industry, manufacturing, supply chains).

## 5. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: **Ladakh's first Government Medical College (Leh) approved for operationalisation from 2027–28** (approval 19 Oct 2026)
- **What it is:** MoHFW approved operationalisation of the Leh medical college with a **one-time relaxation** allowing preparatory recruitment before 80% of physical infrastructure is complete, after a request from the Lt Governor (13 Jul 2026).
- **Why it matters:** Addresses health-workforce and access gaps in a remote high-altitude UT; supports "a medical college in every district" and NMC norms.
- **Key facts:** 103 sanctioned regular posts (staggered recruitment); academic block and hostel in two new Leh buildings; clinical training at the 200-bed Sonam Norboo Memorial (SNM) Hospital; NMC Act 2019 framework; NEET-UG entry. (Seat number not stated.)
- **Notebook:** GS2 Social Sector (Health), regional development.

## Scheme-in-focus: **BHAVYA Rasayan (Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna) — as reported at India Chem 2026**
- **Ministry:** Chemicals & Fertilizers (Dept. of Chemicals & Petrochemicals).
- **Objective:** Build manufacturing hubs linked to chemical parks to deepen the domestic chemicals value chain.
- **Features (as reported; full guidelines not verified):** hub-and-park linkage; industrial-cluster focus; State participation (Maharashtra, Gujarat, Odisha, MP, Bihar, AP, UP showcased hubs); aligned to 2030/2040 sector targets.
- **Beneficiaries:** chemical manufacturers, MSME downstream users, States hosting chemical parks.
- **Significance:** import substitution and supply-chain resilience. *Treat details with caution until the scheme document is checked.*

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following regarding the MoES–AcSIR partnership announced in October 2026:
1. All ten research institutes of the Ministry of Earth Sciences are recognised as AcSIR Associated Research Institutes.
2. Scholars research at the host MoES institute, but the PhD degree is awarded by AcSIR.
3. The MoU is limited to atmospheric sciences and excludes ocean and polar research.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** "SUVIKALP", which won the SKOCH Gold Award 2026, is
(a) a Ministry of Power tool for pumped storage planning
(b) a Coal Controller Organisation tool that ranks post-mining reuse options for closed or abandoned mines
(c) a Ministry of Mines portal for auctioning critical minerals blocks
(d) a Ministry of Environment framework for compensatory afforestation

**Q3.** The iGOT Karmayogi platform, in the news for an MoU with OpenAI, is associated with
(a) Mission Karmayogi for civil-service capacity building, administered through Karmayogi Bharat
(b) the PM Vishwakarma scheme for artisan skilling
(c) the Skill India Digital Hub for school students
(d) the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme

## Answers
1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are as reported; statement 3 is wrong, as the areas include weather, climate, oceans, polar regions, earthquakes and coastal processes.
2. **(b)** — SUVIKALP is the CCO's (Ministry of Coal) mine-repurposing tool; PRISM is its implementation model.
3. **(a)** — iGOT Karmayogi is the learning platform of Mission Karmayogi; Karmayogi Bharat facilitates it.
