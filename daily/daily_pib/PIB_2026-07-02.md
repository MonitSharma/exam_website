# Daily PIB Briefing — 2 July 2026

## 1. 16th India-Japan Annual Summit — PM Modi holds talks with Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi in New Delhi

**Ministry/Body:** Prime Minister's Office / Ministry of External Affairs

Japanese PM Sanae Takaichi is on a three-day official visit to India for the 16th India-Japan Annual Summit, the first with her as PM. Talks at Hyderabad House covered economic security, resilient supply chains, emerging technology, defence and maritime security, and the Indo-Pacific. This matters for GS2 IR as it reflects the deepening of the India-Japan "Special Strategic and Global Partnership" amid China-centred regional recalibration.

**Key facts:** Japanese firms committed over $12.5 billion in investment through roughly 120 agreements involving 150+ companies; both sides agreed to convene a Japan-India Foreign and Defence Ministerial (2+2) Meeting before end-2026; the joint statement welcomed PM Takaichi's "Free and Open Indo-Pacific" (FOIP) vision and reaffirmed cooperation on the Global Biofuel Alliance and semiconductor/critical minerals supply chains.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations

## 2. VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 comes into force nationally — national launch held from Andhra Pradesh

**Ministry/Body:** Ministry of Rural Development

The Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) [VB-G RAM G] Act, 2025 came into force across the country on 1 July 2026, and Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan formally launched the mission on 2 July from Mukkavaripalle (Tirupati district, Andhra Pradesh) with CM Chandrababu Naidu and Deputy CM Pawan Kalyan. It raises the statutory rural employment guarantee from 100 to 125 days per eligible household — the most significant overhaul of the rural employment-guarantee framework since MGNREGA, relevant for GS2 Governance and GS3 Economy (rural livelihoods).

**Key facts:** Interim allocation of ₹95,692.31 crore released to States/UTs for a smooth transition; existing e-KYC-verified job cards remain valid until new Gramin Rozgar Guarantee Cards are issued; 29 States/UTs have made budgetary provisions and 24 have notified their state schemes; launch event included distribution of Rozgar Guarantee Cards and rollout of a new implementation software platform.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance; GS3 — Economy (Rural Development)

### Scheme-in-focus: VB-G RAM G (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission, Gramin)
- **Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development
- **Objective:** Statutorily guarantee enhanced wage employment to rural households, strengthen livelihood security and create durable rural assets in pursuit of Viksit Bharat @2047.
- **Key features:** (1) Raises the guarantee from 100 to 125 days of wage employment per eligible rural household; (2) places Gram Panchayats at the centre of planning, with focus on natural resource management, water conservation and rural infrastructure; (3) convergence with flagship rural programmes and technology-enabled, transparent implementation via a new digital platform; (4) women's empowerment through Self Help Group convergence.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Rural households nationwide currently covered under the erstwhile employment-guarantee scheme; rollout backed by ₹95,692.31 crore interim central allocation.
- **Significance:** Marks a legislative upgrade of India's flagship rural jobs guarantee, with implications for rural wages, panchayat-led asset creation and social security architecture.

## 3. NRAA/ICAR-NBPGR wild rice conservation project — Assam site notified as Biodiversity Heritage Site

**Ministry/Body:** National Rainfed Area Authority (NRAA), Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

The "In-situ Conservation and Management of Wild Rice (Oryza rufipogon) in Sonitpur District of Assam" project, run since 2022 by ICAR–National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (ICAR-NBPGR) with the Assam State Biodiversity Board, has achieved a key milestone: its Borjuli site has been notified as a Biodiversity Heritage Site by the National Biodiversity Authority. Relevant for GS3 Environment/Agriculture — wild crop relatives are a genetic reservoir for climate-resilient, high-yielding rice breeding.

**Key facts:** Project active since 2022 in Sonitpur district, Assam; focus species is Oryza rufipogon (common wild rice), a progenitor/relative of cultivated rice; NRAA CEO Chandra Shekhar Kumar was briefed on the milestone and called for replicating such conservation for other crop wild relatives nationally.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment/Agriculture (Biodiversity & food security)

## 4. NHRC takes suo motu cognizance of Muzaffarnagar bonded labour case

**Ministry/Body:** National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

NHRC has taken suo motu cognizance of reports that 12 bonded labourers, including minors, were beaten and forced to work without adequate food or wages for up to 18 months at a disposable-paper-plate factory in Muzaffarnagar, Uttar Pradesh. The workers, from UP, Haryana, Bihar, Uttarakhand and one from Nepal, were rescued after a police-administration raid; another former worker reportedly died after torture. Relevant for GS2 Social Justice and GS3 Internal Security (labour trafficking, bonded labour law enforcement).

**Key facts:** NHRC also separately took suo motu cognizance of a Kolkata under-construction warehouse collapse that killed at least five workers and injured twenty; both actions reflect NHRC's continuing scrutiny of labour safety and bonded-labour law enforcement (Bonded Labour System (Abolition) Act, 1976).

**Notebook:** GS2 — Social Justice; GS3 — Internal Security

## 5. Insurance surety bonds extended to coal-block security requirements

**Ministry/Body:** Ministry of Coal

The Coal Ministry has notified acceptance of insurance surety bonds as security for MDR (Mine Developer-cum-Operator/allocated) coal blocks, extending a broader government push — begun with a 2024 Department of Financial Services circular and applied to power-sector procurement in April 2026 — to replace bank guarantees with cheaper, non-collateralised surety bonds across public contracts. Relevant for GS3 Economy (ease of doing business, financial-sector reform in infrastructure financing).

**Key facts:** Surety bonds typically cost 1-3% of contract value in premium versus 8-10% effective cost for bank guarantees (including blocked margins) and do not consume a company's bank credit limits, freeing capital for coal-block developers and operators.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Infrastructure financing/ease of doing business)
