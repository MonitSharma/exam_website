# Daily PIB Briefing — 14 October 2026

*Covers PIB / government releases of 12–13 Oct 2026. Ministries rotated away from Agriculture, Health and Defence-heavy coverage of the last fortnight where possible. Items marked "single-source" were corroborated by secondary reports citing PIB, not by the PIB page text itself.*

## 1. Ministry of Home Affairs: **First BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting (HMM) held in New Delhi — "Delhi Declaration" adopted** (13 Oct)
**What it is:** India hosted the first-ever Home Ministers' Meeting of BIMSTEC, with delegations from all seven members (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand). Home Minister Amit Shah announced it as a step towards a "safer and more secure region". Reports say Bangladesh's Home Minister skipped it and sent a bureaucrat-led delegation.
**Why it matters:** SAARC has been dormant since 2016; BIMSTEC is becoming India's main sub-regional platform for security cooperation (terrorism, organised crime, drug and human trafficking, cyber threats, illicit financial flows).
**Key facts (single-source summary of the declaration):**
- Delhi Declaration calls for regular future HMMs on a rotating-host basis; follow-up through the BIMSTEC Joint Working Group on Counter-Terrorism and Transnational Crime (JWG-CTTC).
- Condemns terrorism "in all its forms", including cross-border terrorism; backs implementation of UNSC sanctions regimes.
- Pushes full implementation of the BIMSTEC Convention on Counter-Terrorism (in force 2021) and the MLA Convention (signed 2022); early finalisation of extradition, transfer-of-sentenced-persons and trafficking conventions.
- Addresses trafficking into online "scam centres" (forced criminality) with a victim-centred approach and an SoP for rescue, repatriation and reintegration; mentions the BIMSTEC Drug Offences Monitoring Desk (BDOMD).
**Notebook:** GS2 International Relations (Neighbourhood / regional groupings); GS3 Internal Security.

## 2. Ministry of Culture: **HAL–National Culture Fund–ASI Memorandum of Agreement for conservation of Hampi, Taj Mahal and Bekal Fort** (13 Oct, Kartavya Bhawan-02, New Delhi)
**What it is:** A tripartite MoA under which Hindustan Aeronautics Ltd (a defence PSU) gives in-principle CSR-style funding of **₹23.60 crore over 2026-27 to 2028-29** for conservation, preservation and visitor amenities at **Hampi (Karnataka), Taj Mahal (Agra, UP) and Bekal Fort (Kerala)**. ASI provides technical guidance and supervises execution. Signed in the presence of Culture Secretary Vivek Aggarwal.
**Why it matters:** Continues the shift to public-private/corporate participation in monument conservation, routed through the **National Culture Fund (set up 1996 under the Ministry of Culture)**, which channels donations with tax benefits.
**Notebook:** GS1 Art & Culture (heritage conservation); GS2 Governance (PPP / CSR in culture).

## 3. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare: **Khet Bachao Abhiyan — Rabi-season nationwide soil-health campaign, 22 Oct – 30 Nov 2026** (announced at the PMDDKY one-year review; single-source)
**What it is:** A nationwide campaign promoting balanced fertiliser use (N:P:K per soil and crop need) and curbing excess urea; the ministry has reported lakhs of farmers joining the campaign so far. *(UPDATE — the campaign itself began earlier in 2026; new is the dated Rabi-season run.)*
**Why it matters:** Urea is over-applied vs the benchmark N:P:K ratio of 4:2:1 because its farm-gate price is statutorily fixed, while P&K fertilisers are under the Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS, since April 2010). Linked tools: Soil Health Card (2015) and PM-PRANAM (2023; States get 50% of subsidy saved as grant).
**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture (inputs, subsidies, soil health).

## 4. Ministry of Defence (Indian Navy): **INS Samarthak — first of four indigenous Multi-Purpose Vessels (MPVs) built by L&T, to be commissioned at Naval Base Karwar** (release 13 Oct; commissioning reported for 16 Oct)
**What it is:** First of four MPVs from private yard Larsen & Toubro under Make in India. Roles: maritime surveillance and patrol, low-intensity maritime operations, mothership for unmanned/autonomous systems, target deployment and trials, HADR, limited hospital-ship conversion, salvage. Indigenous sensors, weapons and electronic systems.
**Why it matters:** Private-sector warship building and Navy's push for indigenous support platforms; Karwar is a key western-seaboard base (Project Seabird).
**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security / Defence production.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the first BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting held in New Delhi in October 2026, consider the statements:
1. It adopted the Delhi Declaration, calling for regular future meetings.
2. Follow-up on counter-terrorism and transnational crime is routed through a Joint Working Group.
3. Pakistan and Afghanistan participated as dialogue partners.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The National Culture Fund, recently in news for an agreement with HAL and ASI for conserving Hampi, Taj Mahal and Bekal Fort, was set up in:
(a) 1972 under the AMASR Act (b) 1996 under the Ministry of Culture (c) 2005 under the Companies Act (d) 2014 under NITI Aayog

**Q3.** Consider the following about fertiliser policy in India:
1. Urea's farm-gate price is statutorily fixed by the Government.
2. P&K fertilisers are covered under the Nutrient Based Subsidy scheme.
3. PM-PRANAM gives States a share of the fertiliser subsidy they save as a grant.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; BIMSTEC members are the seven littoral/adjacent South and Southeast Asian states — Pakistan and Afghanistan are not part of BIMSTEC.
2. **(b)** — NCF was set up in 1996 under the Ministry of Culture to channel public/private contributions to heritage conservation.
3. **(d)** — All correct: urea price is fixed; P&K are under NBS (since April 2010); PM-PRANAM (2023) passes 50% of savings to States as grant.
