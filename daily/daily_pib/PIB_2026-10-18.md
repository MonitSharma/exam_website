## 1. Department of Pharmaceuticals (NPPA), Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers: **Trade-margin cap of 30% of MRP on non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs** (NPPA approval in principle 8 Oct; reported 8–17 Oct)

**What it is:** The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (NPPA) has approved in principle a "Trade Margin Rationalisation" for non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs, capping the trade margin (the retailer/stockist/distributor mark-up between the price at which the manufacturer supplies and the MRP) at 30% of MRP. It invokes Para 19 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order (DPCO), 2013 — the extraordinary-circumstances, public-interest power — and follows the 2019 trade-margin rationalisation precedent (cardiac stents/knee implants/select cancer drugs earlier).

**Why it matters:** Out-of-pocket spending on cancer care is a major cause of impoverishment; the move targets excessive mark-ups (a Supreme Court-scrutinised case involved a drug with an MRP of ₹27,000 supplied to retailers at ₹2,700) and "mis-selling" driven by high margins. Covers branded and generic, domestic and imported, patented and non-patented drugs.

**Key facts / flags:**
- Legal basis: Para 19, DPCO 2013 (under the Essential Commodities Act, 1955). Scheduled drugs (NLEM-listed; 63 anti-cancer drugs in NLEM 2022) are already ceiling-price controlled — this targets the *non-scheduled* ones.
- MRP reductions of roughly 20–70% are estimated (savings ~₹2,500 crore/yr per one trade report).
- **Reported numbers differ:** a 17 Oct exam-prep summary says 137 drugs with prices applying from 9 Nov 2026; The Week (8 Oct) says 110 drugs (35 patented); Medical Dialogues says the drug list was still being finalised by a DGHS expert committee. Treat the exact count/effective date as unconfirmed until the NPPA notification.
- **Destination:** GS2 Governance/Health; GS3 Economy (price regulation).

## 2. Ministry of External Affairs: **India rolls over ₹3,000 crore currency swap for the Maldives** (MEA, 16 Oct; effective 23 Oct 2026)

**What it is:** India will roll over the ₹30 billion (₹3,000 crore) rupee-window swap with the Maldives from 23 Oct 2026, at the Maldives' request — the first rollover. MEA spokesperson: it "will add to the financial stability of Maldives".

**Why it matters:** The Maldives faces high debt-distress risk (IMF) and falling reserves (US$532.55 mn in Sept 2026, down 37.5% y/y). The swap is a key tool of India's "Neighbourhood First" economic diplomacy and of rupee internationalisation.

**Key facts:**
- Framework: **SAARC Currency Swap Framework 2024–27**, operated by the RBI. RBI–Maldives Monetary Authority agreement signed 7 Oct 2024; Maldives eligible for **US$400 mn (USD/euro window) + ₹30 bn (rupee window)**. Framework valid until 18 June 2027.
- A swap = drawing foreign exchange/currency from India's central bank against local currency, repayable later; it eases short-term BoP stress without conditionality like an IMF programme.
- **Destination:** GS2 International Relations (India–Maldives, neighbourhood); GS3 Economy (BoP, currency swaps).

## 3. Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs: **PMAY-U 2.0 — 10th CSMC meeting sanctions 1.93 lakh more houses** (16 Oct, New Delhi)

**What it is:** The Central Sanctioning and Monitoring Committee (chaired by Satendra Singh, Secretary, Urban Development) sanctioned over 1.93 lakh houses across 11 States/UTs (UP 73,182; Bihar 52,866; Telangana 18,120; West Bengal 16,559; Gujarat 14,290 …) under the BLC and AHP verticals.

**Key figures:** Total sanctioned under PMAY-U 2.0: **20.80 lakh**; ~97% (20.16 lakh) allotted to women singly/jointly; SC 3.61 lakh, ST 1.06 lakh, OBC 9.14 lakh, senior citizens 1.81 lakh. PMAY-U + 2.0 together: 1.27 crore sanctioned, 1.01 crore completed.
- **Destination:** GS2 Governance (welfare schemes, urban); GS1 Society (urbanisation, women's empowerment).

### Scheme-in-focus: **PMAY-Urban 2.0**
- **Ministry:** MoHUA; launched September 2024.
- **Objective:** Housing for 1 crore additional urban poor and middle-class families.
- **Key features:** Central assistance up to ₹2.50 lakh per family; four verticals — Beneficiary-Led Construction (BLC), Affordable Housing in Partnership (AHP), Affordable Rental Housing (ARH), Interest Subsidy Scheme (ISS); eligibility for EWS/LIG/MIG households (income up to ₹9 lakh); women ownership/co-ownership prioritised.
- **Significance:** Pushes the "Housing for All" mandate into the second phase, with a strong women-ownership and inclusion angle.

## 4. Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways: **PIB backgrounder — making the 111 National Waterways navigable; cargo hits 218 MMT in 2025-26** (16 Oct) *(UPDATE — NW-2/Bogibeel cargo covered 5 Oct; new here are the network-wide figures)*

**What it is:** The ministry says cargo on inland waterways rose from 29 MMT (2014-15) to **218 MMT (2025-26)** — CAGR 20.13% — surpassing the Maritime India Vision 2030 target of 200 MMT. Declaring a waterway is not enough: hydrographic surveys, Aids to Navigation and digital platforms decide viability.
**Key facts:** 111 National Waterways, 20,187 km, across 23 States and 4 UTs (confirmed by AIR). *Single-source for the rest (exam-prep summary of PIB):* only 32 NWs operational; thalweg surveys on 22 NWs; Electronic Navigational Charts for 18 NWs (only NW-1 before 2014); riverine lighthouses at four NW-2 sites (Assam); Jalvahak scheme reimburses up to 35% of operating cost on select routes. Legal base: IWAI Act 1985, National Waterways Act 2016, Inland Vessels Act 2021.
**Critical view:** Low share of national cargo, seasonality, dredging cost, ecology (Gangetic dolphin).
- **Destination:** GS3 Infrastructure/Economy.

## 5. Ministry of Science & Technology (DST): **Neyveli, Tiruchirappalli Badlands and Atacama identified as Martian analogue sites** (16 Oct)

**What it is:** University of Kerala geologists (team led by Dr Sajin Kumar K.S., DST-FIST programme) used a laser Raman spectrometer to identify hydrous sulphates — **melanterite** (FeSO₄·7H₂O) at Neyveli, Tamil Nadu, and **gypsum** at the Tiruchirappalli Badlands and Chile's Atacama Desert. Published in two papers in the *Journal of Geophysical Research: Planets*.
**Why it matters:** Terrestrial analogues help interpret Mars data (rover/orbiter findings) and astrobiology; melanterite indicates short-lived acidic water, gypsum can preserve biosignatures. FIST = DST's Fund for Improvement of S&T Infrastructure in universities/institutions.
- **Destination:** GS3 Science & Tech.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the recent trade-margin rationalisation for anti-cancer drugs, consider the following statements:
1. It is based on Para 19 of the Drugs (Prices Control) Order, 2013.
2. The trade margin is proposed to be capped at 30% of MRP for non-scheduled anti-cancer drugs.
3. The National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority functions under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** India's recent rollover of the ₹3,000 crore currency swap for the Maldives operates under which of the following?
(a) BIMSTEC Currency Swap Arrangement
(b) SAARC Currency Swap Framework 2024–27
(c) IMF Flexible Credit Line
(d) Local Currency Settlement System of the RBI

**Q3.** Consider the following statements about PMAY-Urban 2.0:
1. It targets assistance for one crore additional urban families.
2. Central assistance of up to ₹2.50 lakh per family is provided.
3. Women ownership or co-ownership of houses is prioritised.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

1. **(a)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct. NPPA functions under the Department of Pharmaceuticals, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers, not the Health Ministry.
2. **(b)** — The swap is under the SAARC Currency Swap Framework 2024–27 (RBI), with a ₹30 bn rupee window and a US$400 mn USD/euro window; it is not a BIMSTEC, IMF or LCS arrangement.
3. **(d)** — All three are correct; PMAY-U 2.0 (Sept 2024) targets 1 crore families with up to ₹2.50 lakh assistance and prioritises women's ownership.
