## 1. Government moves to curb the sugar price rise ahead of the festive season

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution (Department of Food & Public Distribution)

Retail sugar prices climbed sharply through late July and August, and the Centre has responded with a package of stock-limit, enforcement and supply-side measures under its essential-commodities price-management toolkit. This is a textbook example of how the government uses **stockholding limits + import calibration + advancing the crushing season** together, rather than any single instrument.

**Key facts and figures**

- Sugar price rose from **₹48.18/kg on 20 July 2026 to ₹55.70/kg on 20 August 2026**; average retail price was up about **13% year-on-year** in mid-August.
- **Stock limit of 400 tonnes on sugar dealers** across the country, in force **1 August – 30 November 2026**.
- From **1 September 2026**, **bulk consumers** (those using more than 10 MT of sugar a month) may not hold stocks exceeding **15 days** of consumption — halved from the earlier 30-day norm. The bulk-consumer cap runs **1 September – 30 November 2026**.
- **Joint Central–State teams** are physically verifying sugar stocks at mills to check hoarding and artificial scarcity; mills have been asked to furnish sale details.
- **Duty-free import of 10 LMT of raw sugar** permitted as a precautionary supply cushion.
- States and mills advised to **begin crushing from 15 October 2026**, which is expected to lift October production from the usual **3–4 LMT to more than 10 LMT**.

**Why it matters (UPSC angle):** Links directly to the Essential Commodities Act, 1955 machinery, buffer/stock-limit policy, the sugar–ethanol diversion trade-off, and food inflation management. Compare with the wheat and pulses stock limits of recent years.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Food inflation, Buffer stocks & Essential Commodities); Agriculture (Sugar sector, crushing season).

## 2. Health Ministry restricts cough-and-cold FDCs containing Chlorpheniramine Maleate + Phenylephrine Hydrochloride for children below four years

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (on the recommendation of the Drugs Technical Advisory Board and an expert committee; notified through CDSCO)

The Centre has restricted the **manufacture, sale and distribution** of all **Fixed Dose Combinations (FDCs)** containing **Chlorpheniramine Maleate and Phenylephrine Hydrochloride** for use in **children below four years of age**. This is one of the most widely used paediatric cough-syrup combinations in India, so the regulatory reach is very wide.

**Key facts**

- Notification issued under **Section 26A of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940**, which empowers the Central Government to prohibit or restrict a drug in the **public interest**.
- Based on the recommendation of an **expert committee** and the **Drugs Technical Advisory Board (DTAB)** — the statutory technical advisory body under the Act.
- Manufacturers must carry a prominent warning — *"Fixed Dose Combination shall not be used in children below four years of age"* — on the **label, package insert and promotional literature**.
- Effective from the date of publication in the **Official Gazette**.

**Why it matters (UPSC angle):** Ties together drug regulation architecture (CDSCO, DCGI, DTAB, Drugs Consultative Committee), the FDC rationality debate and earlier FDC bans, and the recurring question of paediatric drug safety and OTC medicine oversight.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 — Governance (Regulatory bodies, Health governance); GS3 — Science & Technology (Pharmaceutical regulation).

## 3. Cochin Shipyard delivers 'Mangrol', the third Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft, to the Indian Navy

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Defence / Cochin Shipyard Limited (CSL), a Defence PSU

CSL handed over **'Mangrol'**, the **third of eight** indigenously designed and built **Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW-SWC)**, to the Indian Navy on **21 August 2026**. These craft are purpose-built for a capability gap — hunting submarines in **shallow littoral waters**, where larger corvettes and destroyers are less effective.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Indigenous content approximately 80%** — a marker of the Aatmanirbharta push in naval shipbuilding.
- **Dimensions:** about **78 m** length, **11.36 m** beam, draught about **2.7 m**; displacement about **896 tonnes**.
- **Performance:** maximum speed about **25 knots**; endurance about **1,800 nautical miles**.
- **Propulsion:** diesel engine–**waterjet** propulsion.
- **Armament/sensors:** lightweight torpedoes, indigenous ASW rockets and advanced **shallow-water sonars**.
- **Secondary roles:** underwater surveillance, Search & Rescue, Low Intensity Maritime Operations, and **mine-laying**.

**Why it matters (UPSC angle):** Feeds the indigenisation-of-defence-production storyline (positive indigenisation lists, DPSU shipbuilding, Make-in-India in defence) and the maritime security discussion around littoral/shallow-water ASW in the Indian Ocean Region.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security & Defence (Indigenisation, Defence production, Maritime security).

## 4. Empowered Committee reviews proposals to scale up women-led rural enterprises under DAY-NRLM

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Rural Development (Empowered Committee chaired by the Rural Development Secretary)

Meeting on **22 August 2026**, the Empowered Committee took up proposals under the **Non-Farm Livelihoods (NFL) component of DAY-NRLM**, with the explicit aim of helping small SHG enterprises **graduate into higher-order, growth-oriented businesses** and feeding the national target of **6 crore Lakhpati Didis**.

**Key facts and figures**

- The Committee also examined **expansion of the Start-up Village Entrepreneurship Programme (SVEP)** into **West Bengal and Goa**.
- Anchoring scheme: **DAY-NRLM** (Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission), Ministry of Rural Development.

**Why it matters (UPSC angle):** A concrete illustration of the SHG → enterprise → credit-linkage ladder, and of academic-institution incubation being pulled into rural livelihoods policy. Strong Mains material on women's economic empowerment and the rural non-farm economy.

## 5. Scheme-in-focus: Employees' Enrolment Campaign (EEC), 2026 — EPFO's amnesty-style window closes 31 October

**Ministry / body:** Ministry of Labour & Employment / Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO). EPFO field offices are actively pushing establishments to use the window; the campaign was notified vide **Notification No. G.S.R. 525(E)**.

**Objective:** Bring workers who were **left out of EPF coverage between 1 April 2009 and 31 March 2026** into the statutory social-security net, by giving employers a time-bound, low-penalty route to regularise past non-compliance.

**Key features**

1. **Window:** operational **1 July 2026 – 31 October 2026** (a four-month window). Applies to all establishments covered or coverable under the **EPF & MP Act, 1952 / Code on Social Security, 2020**.
2. **Employee's share waived** where it was never deducted from wages during the left-out period. The employer deposits only the **employer's share**, applicable interest (Sec. 7Q / Sec. 127 of the Code) and administrative charges.
3. **Damages capped at a lump-sum ₹100 per defaulting establishment** across all three schemes — including for establishments already facing quasi-judicial proceedings.
4. **Fully online process** — UAN generation through **Face Authentication on the UMANG app**, declaration through the **EEC-2026 module** on the EPFO Employer Portal, ECR linked to a TRRN, and final declaration authenticated by **DSC or eSign**.

**Beneficiaries / coverage:** Employees **alive and actively working** in the establishment on the date of declaration (exited employees are excluded, and no suo-motu action will be taken against the establishment for them). Employers registering afresh or declaring under EEC-2026 can additionally claim benefits under the **Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY)**, subject to its conditions.

**Significance:** A formalisation instrument as much as a social-security one — it nudges informal-in-form employment inside covered establishments into the formal EPF fold, and pairs an amnesty with a digital-compliance rail.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Social security, Formalisation of the workforce); GS2 — Governance (Labour codes, EPFO).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the measures announced in August 2026 to moderate sugar prices, consider the following statements:

1. A stock limit of 400 tonnes has been imposed on sugar dealers across the country.
2. Bulk consumers have been permitted to hold sugar stocks of up to 15 days of consumption, reduced from an earlier limit of 30 days.
3. The Government permitted duty-free import of raw sugar as a precautionary supply-side measure.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** 'Mangrol', in the news recently, is:

(a) A nuclear-powered ballistic missile submarine commissioned into the Indian Navy
(b) An Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft delivered to the Indian Navy by Cochin Shipyard Limited
(c) An offshore patrol vessel delivered to the Indian Coast Guard by Goa Shipyard Limited
(d) An indigenous survey vessel of the National Institute of Ocean Technology

---

**Q3.** With reference to the recent restriction on Fixed Dose Combinations (FDCs) containing Chlorpheniramine Maleate and Phenylephrine Hydrochloride, consider the following statements:

1. The restriction applies to the use of such FDCs in children below four years of age.
2. The notification was issued under Section 26A of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
3. The measure followed the recommendation of the Drugs Technical Advisory Board.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct. The 400-tonne dealer stock limit runs 1 August–30 November 2026; the bulk-consumer holding cap was halved from 30 days to 15 days with effect from 1 September 2026; and duty-free import of 10 LMT of raw sugar was permitted as a precaution.

**Q2 — (b).** 'Mangrol' is the third of eight indigenously designed Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW-SWC), delivered by Cochin Shipyard Limited to the Indian Navy on 21 August 2026, with about 80% indigenous content.

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** The Health Ministry restricted the manufacture, sale and distribution of these FDCs for children below four years, acting under Section 26A of the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940, on the recommendation of an expert committee and the Drugs Technical Advisory Board (DTAB).
