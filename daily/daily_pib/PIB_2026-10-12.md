*Source: PIB releases dated 12 Oct 2026 (pib.gov.in). Ministries rotated away from the last few days (Finance, Education, Telecom, MSME, Commerce-trade, Ports, Rural Development, Health-TB, Statistics). No CA file for 12 Oct existed at run time; items avoid the 11 Oct CA set.*

## 1. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: **Centre urges States/UTs to ensure prominent display of healthcare service rates at clinical establishments** (12 Oct)

- **What:** Union Health Secretary wrote to the Chief Secretaries of all States/UTs asking them to ensure that rates for services and facilities at clinical establishments are displayed at conspicuous, easily accessible places; to review compliance; to issue advisories; and to encourage display of **package rates for international patients on Medical/Ayush visas**.
- **Why it matters:** Price transparency lets patients estimate costs and reduces billing disputes — a patient-rights and federalism question (health is a **State subject**, so enforcement rests with States/UTs).
- **Key facts:** Legal basis — **Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010** and **Clinical Establishments (Central Government) Rules, 2012**. Where the Act applies, rates for each service/facility must be displayed in **local language and English**; elsewhere State/UT frameworks govern.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance / Health; GS2 Federalism.

## 2. PIB Backgrounder: **Five Years of PM GatiShakti — Transforming India's Infrastructure Planning** (12 Oct) *(UPDATE — ITLA was covered 6 Oct; new here are the five-year stocktake figures)*

- **What:** GIS-based national infrastructure planning platform launched **13 Oct 2021**; now covers **58 Central Ministries/Departments and all 36 States/UTs**.
- **Why it matters:** Integrated, data-driven planning to cut logistics cost, land/clearance delays and ecological impact.
- **Key facts:** Six principles (integrated development, multimodal infrastructure, last-mile connectivity, expedited land acquisition, minimised clearances, reduced ecological impact); **seven engines** — roads, railways, ports, waterways, airports, mass transport, logistics. **23,493+ data layers**; **396 projects** evaluated by the **Network Planning Group** (~₹18.66 lakh crore, as of 11 Aug 2026); 256 sanctioned, 198 under implementation; **46 government systems on 12 Ministries linked via 142 APIs through ULIP**; 2.4 lakh+ officers trained via Gati Shakti Vishwavidyalaya. Three-tier governance: **EGoS** (chaired by Cabinet Secretary) → **NPG** → **TSU**. Featured: Pune–Bengaluru Expressway (699 km), Ladakh–Haryana Green Energy Corridor (13 GW evacuation, 480 km Pang–Kaithal HVDC line).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Infrastructure / Economy; GS2 Governance.

## 3. Ministry of Defence (Indian Army) with Dept. of Food & Public Distribution: **"Bharat Rice" launched for troops; Army–FCI MoU** (12 Oct, New Delhi)

- **What:** Launched in presence of Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh and Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution Minister Pralhad Joshi. Rice is delivered directly to Army Supply Depots (incl. Ladakh and the North East), milled to defence specifications, **supplied through NAFED in FY 2026-27**, in **10 kg and 30 kg bags**. A parallel **five-year Army–FCI MoU** lets the Army use FCI warehouses, labour and material-handling equipment during operational contingencies (extendable by mutual consent); release links it to lessons from **Operation Sindoor** and the PM GatiShakti vision.
- **Why it matters:** Civil–military logistics integration and food-supply resilience in forward areas. (Release does not state a price or total quantity.)
- **Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security / Logistics; GS3 Agriculture (food management).

## 4. Ministry of Science & Technology (DST — Technology Development Board): **TDB signs agreement with Peptris for ₹86 crore RDI support for AI-driven drug discovery** (12 Oct)

- **What:** TDB-DST and Peptris (AI drug-discovery company) signed an agreement providing ₹86 crore of **Research, Development & Innovation (RDI)** support to speed up AI-led discovery of next-generation therapeutics.
- **Why it matters:** State support for AI × biopharma; TDB is the statutory body that funds commercialisation of indigenous technology. (Funding scheme and therapeutic focus are not stated in the retrievable PIB text — not assumed.)
- **Notebook:** GS3 Science & Tech / Biotechnology.

## Scheme-in-focus: **PM GatiShakti National Master Plan (PMGS-NMP)**

- **Ministry:** Commerce & Industry (DPIIT — Logistics Division as secretariat of the NPG/EGoS framework).
- **Objective:** Integrated multimodal infrastructure planning on a single GIS platform.
- **Key features:** (1) geospatial data layers from ministries/States; (2) NPG appraisal of projects (ITLA now to appraise ₹500 crore+ projects and prepare a 10+ year National Transport Master Plan); (3) ULIP/API integration of logistics systems; (4) Query-Based Analytics opened to the private sector (Oct 2025).
- **Coverage:** 58 Ministries/Departments, 36 States/UTs.
- **Significance:** Reduces cost/time overruns and logistics cost; supports the National Logistics Policy.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the display of rates by clinical establishments in India, consider the statements:
1. The Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010 requires covered establishments to display rates for each service and facility.
2. Health is a subject in the Union List, so States have no role in enforcement.
3. Under the Central Government Rules, 2012, rates must be displayed in the local language and English.

Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Consider the following about the PM GatiShakti National Master Plan:
1. It was launched in October 2021 as a GIS-based platform for integrated infrastructure planning.
2. The Network Planning Group appraises projects, while the Empowered Group of Secretaries is chaired by the Cabinet Secretary.
3. Its seven engines include roads, railways, ports, waterways, airports, mass transport and logistics.

Which of the above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The "Bharat Rice" launch for troops on 12 October 2026 was accompanied by an MoU between the Indian Army and which organisation, enabling use of its warehouses and labour during contingencies?
(a) NAFED (b) Food Corporation of India (c) Central Warehousing Corporation (d) NCCF

## Answers

1. **(b)** — Statement 1 and 3 are correct (Act 2010; Rules 2012 require local language + English). Statement 2 is wrong: health is a State subject, so enforcement rests with States/UTs.
2. **(d)** — All three are correct as per the PIB backgrounder.
3. **(b)** — The Army signed a five-year MoU with FCI; NAFED is the supplier of the rice in FY 2026-27, not the MoU partner.
