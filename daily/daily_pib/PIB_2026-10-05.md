## 1. Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways (IWAI / NW-2): **Bogibeel–Dhaka methanol cargo — first commercial international cargo from Dibrugarh since Independence** (late Sep–early Oct)

**What and why it matters.** Two vessels carrying methanol from Assam Petro-Chemicals Ltd (Namrup) are moving from the new Bogibeel inland water terminal at Dibrugarh down the Brahmaputra (National Waterway-2) to Bangladesh. It revives the Brahmaputra as an international trade route and is the first commercial run of the Bogibeel terminal. This ties into the Indo-Bangladesh Protocol on Inland Water Transit and Trade, North-East connectivity and the "Act East" push.

**Key facts.**
- Cargo: 1,080 tonnes of methanol in 54 containers (27 per vessel, about 20 tonnes each; about 540 tonnes per vessel).
- Route: about 783 km, about 7 days, to Dhaka via the Indo-Bangladesh Protocol route. (One aggregator names Pangaon as the terminal; PIB text was not retrieved, so treat it as unconfirmed.)
- The two vessels are Assam government Inland Water Transport Department craft, leased and repaired at Pandu Ghat, Guwahati.
- Terminal: customs office, jetty and warehouse in place; lighthouse and Regional Centre of Excellence under construction. IWAI sees future exports of petrochemicals, polymers, coal, tea and agri-produce.
- NW-2 is the Brahmaputra, Sadiya to Dhubri (891 km), declared a National Waterway in 1988.

*Dates differ across sources (loading reported 26–27 Sep, aggregator flags 4 Oct), so the exact sailing date is unconfirmed.*

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (infrastructure/logistics); GS2 IR (Bangladesh, Act East); GS1 Geography (Brahmaputra).

## 2. Ministry of Defence (Defence Accounts Department): **Three digital finance initiatives launched on the DAD's 279th Annual Day** (1 Oct, DRDO Bhawan, New Delhi)

**What and why it matters.** Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh launched three reforms aimed at paperless defence payments and real-time fund tracking. Defence is the largest civil-ministry budget line, so this speaks directly to governance and public financial management.

**Key facts.**
- **SAKSHAM–IAFIS integration:** links DAD's bill-processing system with the Indian Army's financial platform, so budget, sanction and bill data move digitally and fund and bill status are visible in real time.
- **Treasury Single Account (TSA) via the PRISM platform,** connected to RBI's e-Kuber, for fund releases to autonomous bodies under MoD, with better visibility of expenditure.
- **Revamped National Compilation System (NCS):** the central repository of defence expenditure with granular reporting for planning.
- Minister's stated goal: "any time review" of the balance budget instead of only annual reviews.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (e-governance, PFM); GS3 Internal Security/Defence reforms.

## 3. Department of Pharmaceuticals (Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers): **PRIP scheme opens a "New Discovery Track" — up to ₹50 crore per project** (announced 30 Sep–1 Oct)

### Scheme-in-focus: **PRIP — Promotion of Research and Innovation in Pharma MedTech**
- **Ministry:** Chemicals & Fertilizers (Department of Pharmaceuticals). Total outlay about ₹5,000 crore.
- **Objective:** Move India from generics towards innovation-led drug and medtech R&D by bridging funding gaps.
- **Key features:** (i) Early Stage track: up to ₹5 crore (TRL 1–3 to 5). (ii) Later Stage track: up to ₹100 crore at 35% of project cost (TRL 4–6). (iii) **New Discovery Track:** up to ₹50 crore per company/project/portfolio for new chemical and new biological entities at TRL 1–3, with at least 25% co-funding by institutional investors; carries projects up to TRL 6. (iv) Second application call announced.
- **Beneficiaries:** Start-ups and MSMEs (Discovery Track); companies and academia under other tracks.
- **Coverage so far:** the first call approved 41 projects, about ₹1,600 crore of assistance.
- **Significance:** Targets the earliest, riskiest stage of drug discovery and crowds in private capital. It complements PLI for pharma and bulk-drug parks and supports Viksit Bharat self-reliance in medicines.

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech/Economy; GS2 Health.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Bogibeel–Dhaka methanol cargo, consider the statements:
1. The cargo moves along National Waterway-2 on the Brahmaputra.
2. It is the first commercial international cargo movement from Dibrugarh since Independence.
3. NW-2 runs from Sadiya to Haldia.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the initiatives launched by the Defence Accounts Department on its 279th Annual Day, which of the following is correct?
(a) The Treasury Single Account is operationalised through the PRISM platform and linked with RBI's e-Kuber.
(b) SAKSHAM has been integrated with the Navy's financial platform.
(c) The National Compilation System is a new platform for defence procurement tenders.
(d) All three initiatives replace the Controller General of Defence Accounts.

**Q3.** Under the PRIP scheme's New Discovery Track, assistance of up to ₹50 crore is meant for:
(a) Generic drug manufacturers at TRL 7–9
(b) Start-ups and MSMEs working on new chemical or biological entities at TRL 1–3, with at least 25% institutional-investor co-funding
(c) Bulk drug parks seeking common infrastructure
(d) State drug regulators for lab upgradation

## Answers
1. **(b)** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. NW-2 is Sadiya to Dhubri (891 km), not Sadiya to Haldia; NW-1 is Haldia–Prayagraj.
2. **(a)** The TSA runs via PRISM and connects to RBI's e-Kuber. SAKSHAM was linked with the Army's IAFIS; NCS is an expenditure repository; no body was replaced.
3. **(b)** The Discovery Track targets early-stage (TRL 1–3) NCE/NBE research by start-ups and MSMEs, with at least 25% institutional co-funding.
