## 1. MoRTH proposes a regulatory framework for Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication in Indian vehicles

**Issuing ministry:** Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (MoRTH) | **Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Technology / Infrastructure — Intelligent Transportation Systems)

MoRTH has issued a **draft notification amending the Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989** for the phased introduction of V2V communication systems. V2V lets nearby vehicles exchange real-time speed, position, direction and acceleration data, giving drivers warnings **beyond the direct line of sight** — something onboard sensors and ADAS alone cannot do. This is India's first regulatory step towards connected mobility, and it sits at the intersection of road safety (SDG 3.6) and the digital-infrastructure story.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Frequency band:** **5.875–5.925 GHz** (the 5.9 GHz ITS/V2X range), **exempted from licensing** by the Department of Telecommunications via **G.S.R. 466(E) dated 10 June 2026**.
- **Standard:** **AIS-230**, covering factory-installed On-Board Units using **Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X)** technology.
- **Timeline:** vehicles of categories **L, M and N** made on or after **1 Oct 2027** must comply with AIS-230 *if* fitted with V2V; vehicles made on or after **1 Oct 2028** **must be fitted** with AIS-230-compliant V2V.
- AIS-230 prescribes radio performance, receiver sensitivity, **GNSS/positioning**, EMI/EMC, **cybersecurity** and road-safety application requirements.
- **Safety use cases (phased):** Emergency Brake Alert, Forward Collision Warning, Wrong-way Driving, Emergency Vehicle Alert.
- Recommended by MoRTH's **Task Force on Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS)**; cleared by the **CMVR–Technical Standing Committee** at its **56th meeting on 7 May 2026**. Comments invited for **30 days**.

**Why it matters:** classic "technology + regulation" question material — remember the band (5.9 GHz), the standard (AIS-230), the technology (C-V2X) and the parent rules (CMVR 1989, framed under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988).

## 2. Two-wheeled road ambulances to get a legal category for the first time

**Issuing ministry:** Ministry of Road Transport & Highways | **Notebook:** GS2 (Governance — health service delivery); GS3 (Infrastructure)

A second MoRTH draft notification of the same day proposes amending the CMVR, 1989 to create a **distinct regulatory category for two-wheeled road ambulances**. At present these vehicles are unregulated — there are no national norms for their construction, functional safety, patient-handling systems, type approval, registration or fitness inspection. The rationale is **last-mile emergency access** in rural, remote and hilly terrain where conventional ambulances cannot reach quickly.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Standard adopted:** **AIS-209 (Part 1):2026** — *Construction and Functional Requirements for Life Support Two-Wheeled Road Ambulances*.
- **Vehicle category:** **L2**; compliance for vehicles manufactured on or after **1 October 2027**; emergency top lights also to conform from the same date.
- **Area of operation:** determined by the **respective State Governments** at registration — a federal/state-discretion hook.
- **Fitness:** treated as **transport vehicles**; certificate of fitness renewable **every two years**.
- **Inspection checks:** mounting of the patient-conveyance unit, tyres and rims, warning lights, stretcher and its locking mechanism, patient-restraint systems, loading/unloading mechanism, fire extinguisher validity.
- AIS-209 also covers vehicle stability and braking, parking brake and gradeability, rear visibility, coupling integrity and protection of the patient from environmental exposure. Comments invited for **30 days**.

**Why it matters:** a neat, examinable example of the State using **standard-setting** rather than expenditure to improve emergency health access — usable in Mains answers on last-mile health delivery and on regulatory gaps.

## 3. Scheme-in-focus: PM-VBRY completes one year — 72 lakh first-time employees formalised

**Issuing body:** PIB Research explainer (1 August 2026), Ministry of Labour & Employment / EPFO | **Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — employment, formalisation, social security)

> **What's new since this briefing last carried PM-VBRY (18 June 2026):** the scheme has now **completed one year** (1 August 2026), and PIB has published **first-year outcome data** — 72 lakh first-time employees, ~30% women beneficiaries, 15 lakh+ incentive recipients.

**Scheme template**

- **Name:** Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rojgar Yojana (PM-VBRY)
- **Ministry:** Labour & Employment; implemented through a **fully digital, EPFO-linked platform**
- **Objective:** encourage **first-time formal employment**, incentivise employers to create additional formal jobs, and expand social security coverage — converting the demographic dividend into formal-sector jobs for **Viksit Bharat 2047**
- **Key features:**
  1. **Period and outlay:** announced August 2025; implemented **1 Aug 2025 – 31 Jul 2027** (two years); outlay **₹99,446 crore**.
  2. **Part A (employees):** first-time employees earning **up to ₹1,00,000/month** get a **one-time incentive equal to one month's EPF wage, capped at ₹15,000**, paid in **two instalments** — after **6 months** of service and after **12 months** of continuous service plus completion of a **financial literacy programme**. A portion is parked in a designated savings instrument, withdrawable later.
  3. **Part B (employers):** up to **₹3,000 per month for two years** per eligible additional employee retained at least **6 months**; for **manufacturing**, extended to the **third and fourth years**.
  4. **Delivery:** Aadhaar-based authentication, **UAN** with **Face Authentication on UMANG**, ECR filings, and **DBT** to Aadhaar-linked accounts; a PM-VBRY portal for real-time monitoring; grassroots outreach by **Cross-Functional Teams** of EPFO, ESIC and the Chief Labour Commissioner.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** target of **over 3.5 crore** persons, including **1.92 crore first-time employees**.
- **First-year outcomes:** **72 lakh+** first-time employees joined the formal workforce; **~30% of beneficiaries are women**; **15 lakh+** have already received employment-linked incentives.
- **Significance:** complements the **National Manufacturing Mission**; links wage subsidy directly to EPFO registration, so the fiscal incentive itself becomes the formalisation instrument.

## 4. Six DDU-GKY youth invited as Special Guests for the 80th Independence Day

**Issuing ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development | **Notebook:** GS2 (Governance — welfare schemes); GS3 (Economy — skilling and rural livelihoods)

Six beneficiaries of the **Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)** — from **West Bengal, Chhattisgarh, Madhya Pradesh, Odisha, Mizoram and Nagaland** — have been invited as **Special Guests** for India's **80th Independence Day** celebrations in New Delhi on **15 August 2026**. They will attend the Red Fort ceremony and the President's **'At Home'** reception at Rashtrapati Bhavan; invitations were sent through India Post.

**Scheme context (worth memorising)**

- DDU-GKY is the **placement-linked skill development** component of the **Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission (DAY-NRLM)**, Ministry of **Rural Development**.
- It targets **rural poor youth**, providing industry-relevant training, **nationally recognised certification** and placement support.
- Delivery is through **State Rural Livelihood Missions (SRLMs)** and **Project Implementing Agencies (PIAs)**.

**Why it matters:** a low-cost but high-visibility example of **recognition as a governance tool** — useful as an illustration in Mains answers on demand-driven skilling and on making welfare delivery aspirational rather than merely transactional.

## 5. Commonwealth Games 2026, Glasgow: India finishes 4th with 39 medals; women win 8 of 13 golds

**Issuing bodies:** Ministry of Women and Child Development; Prime Minister's Office | **Notebook:** GS1 (Society — gender and sport); GS2 (Governance — sports policy)

India closed its **Glasgow Commonwealth Games 2026** campaign on 2 August in **fourth place** with **39 medals — 13 gold, 17 silver, 9 bronze**. On 3 August, Union WCD Minister **Annpurna Devi** highlighted that **Nari Shakti powered 8 of India's 13 golds**, across **boxing, weightlifting, judo and para athletics**, with **five Indian women boxers** taking gold. The PM separately issued a release expressing pride in the contingent's performance.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Host city:** Glasgow, Scotland (2026 edition). **Next edition: Ahmedabad, India.**
- **India:** 4th on the table; **13 G / 17 S / 9 B = 39**.
- **Women's share:** **8 of 13 golds**; disciplines — boxing, weightlifting, judo, para athletics.

**Why it matters:** pairs directly with the **revamped Khelo India Scheme and enhanced ANSF (₹36,441 crore)** approved by Cabinet on 1 August 2026 — the outcome data and the funding decision make a strong linked Mains illustration on sports governance and female participation.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the recent draft notification on **Vehicle-to-Vehicle (V2V) communication systems**, consider the following statements:

1. The draft notification proposes amendments to the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988.
2. The standard AIS-230 covers factory-installed On-Board Units using Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything (C-V2X) technology.
3. The 5.875–5.925 GHz frequency band identified for V2V has been exempted from licensing requirements by the Department of Telecommunications.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** With reference to the **Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rojgar Yojana (PM-VBRY)**, consider the following statements:

1. It is being implemented over a two-year period from 1 August 2025 to 31 July 2027 with an outlay of ₹99,446 crore.
2. Under Part A, the incentive to first-time employees is paid in a single instalment at the time of joining.
3. Under Part B, employers receive up to ₹3,000 per month for two years for every eligible additional employee retained for at least six months.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** Which one of the following statements about the **Deen Dayal Upadhyaya Grameen Kaushalya Yojana (DDU-GKY)** is correct?

(a) It is a self-employment credit-linked subsidy scheme of the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.
(b) It is the placement-linked skill development component of the Deendayal Antyodaya Yojana – National Rural Livelihoods Mission under the Ministry of Rural Development.
(c) It is an urban livelihoods and street-vendor support programme under the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs.
(d) It is a residential training programme exclusively for tribal youth under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** Statements 2 and 3 are correct. The draft notification amends the **Central Motor Vehicles Rules, 1989** (framed under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988), not the Act itself, so statement 1 is wrong; AIS-230 covers **C-V2X** On-Board Units, and DoT exempted the **5.875–5.925 GHz** band from licensing via G.S.R. 466(E) of 10 June 2026.

**Q2 — (c).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: the Part A incentive (one month's EPF wage, capped at **₹15,000**) is paid in **two instalments** — after six months of service and after twelve months of continuous service plus a financial literacy programme.

**Q3 — (b).** DDU-GKY is the **placement-linked skilling** component of **DAY-NRLM** under the **Ministry of Rural Development**, delivered through State Rural Livelihood Missions and Project Implementing Agencies.
