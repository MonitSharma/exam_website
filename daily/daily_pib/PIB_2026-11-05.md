## 1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: **Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada 2026 (1–15 Nov) — 302 projects, Jan-HIT portal, National FRA Digital Platform** — GS2 Governance / GS1 Society
*(UPDATE — Adi Karmayogi microsite and PM-JANMAN were covered earlier; new is the fortnight-long national programme and the two digital platforms.)*

- **What:** Fortnight honouring Bhagwan Birsa Munda's birth anniversary, ending on **Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas (15 Nov)** — a day designated in 2021.
- **Projects:** 302 in all — **228 inaugurations + 74 foundation stones**; includes a Tribal Research Institute building (Itanagar) and the Ropuiliani Tribal Freedom Fighter Museum (Kelsih, Mizoram).
- **Digital platforms:** (i) **Jan-HIT portal** with ICMR — tribe-disaggregated health data across 20 states; (ii) **National FRA Digital Platform** — single system for forest-rights claims, from Gram Sabha filing to digitally signed titles.
- **Camps/volunteers:** Health–nutrition–scheme camps for PVTGs at PM-JANMAN centres (15 Oct–15 Nov); scholarship camps in 73 districts; ~1.54 lakh Adi Karmayogi volunteers across 76,963 villages being trained.
- **Why it matters:** Tribe-wise health data and digitised FRA titling address two chronic gaps — evidence for tribal health policy, and delays/rejections under the Forest Rights Act, 2006.
- *Verification: DD News and Organiser reports of the ministry's plan; figures consistent across both. Not read on pib.gov.in.*

## 2. Ministry of Civil Aviation: **Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam (Rules), 2026 notified — replaces Aircraft Rules, 1937** — GS3 Economy/Infrastructure, GS2 Governance
- **Notified:** 31 Oct 2026; **in force 1 Jan 2027**. Framed under the **Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024** (which replaced the Aircraft Act, 1934).
- **Scope:** Aircraft registration, design/manufacture, airworthiness certification, maintenance, flight operations, licensing of pilots/AMEs/ATCs (training, experience, medical standards), flight-crew duty limits, aerodrome licensing and safety.
- **Consumer angle:** Airlines must disclose the **total ticket price** (base fare, taxes, fees, cancellation charges, refund policy). **DGCA monitors fares; in extraordinary circumstances the Centre may issue fare directives in the public interest.**
- **Why it matters:** Completes the replacement of colonial-era aviation law with a modern, safety-and-consumer-oriented rulebook ahead of India's fleet and airport expansion.
- *Verification: Chronicle India, Telangana Today and other news reports of the notification; not read on pib.gov.in.*

## 3. Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying (DAHD): **Centre of Excellence for Siri cattle, Sikkim; North District Milk Union launched under White Revolution 2.0** (2–3 Nov 2026) — GS3 Agriculture
- **Announced** by Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan (Lalan) Singh during a Sikkim visit.
- **CoE** for conserving and genetically improving the indigenous **Siri** cattle breed; the minister asked for a work plan to introduce **IVF** to multiply superior germplasm.
- **NDMU (North District Cooperative Milk Producers' Union Ltd)** launched digitally at Mangan; NDDB asked to plan plant modernisation, network expansion and a "Sikkim Milk" brand.
- **Also:** Siri Cattle Conservation Programme launched; foundation stone of a **Siri Bull Mother Farm** at Chuchachen (Pakyong); pig-semen/liquid-semen AI protocol released.
- **Why it matters:** Conservation of indigenous breeds + cooperative strengthening in the NE; fits the "White Revolution 2.0" push to expand dairy cooperative coverage.
- *Verification: DD India report of 2 Nov; no figures (herd size/funding) given in source.*

## 4. Ministry of Earth Sciences (National Centre for Seismology): **Seismological observatory inaugurated at Kishtwar, J&K** (~2 Nov 2026) — GS1 Geography / GS3 Disaster Management
- Inaugurated by MoS (Independent Charge) **Dr Jitendra Singh**; reported as the **183rd** NCS observatory, taking J&K to **4** observatories. Further expansion (more observatories for J&K and Ladakh; Doppler weather radars in 2027) was flagged.
- **Why it matters:** Himalayan seismic belt monitoring and early assessment; Kishtwar is also a flash-flood/cloudburst-prone district (2025 Chasoti tragedy), so denser sensing supports hazard response.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about the Bharatiya Vayuyan Niyam, 2026:
1. It replaces the Aircraft Rules, 1937 and comes into force on 1 January 2027.
2. It is framed under the Bharatiya Vayuyan Adhiniyam, 2024.
3. It empowers the Central Government to fix airfares permanently for all routes.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the Janjatiya Gaurav Pakhwada 2026, which one of the following is correct?
(a) It is organised by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
(b) It culminates on Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas, 15 November.
(c) The Jan-HIT portal provides state-level health data without tribe-wise disaggregation.
(d) The National FRA Digital Platform is meant to replace Gram Sabhas in forest-rights recognition.

**Q3.** The "Siri" breed, for which a Centre of Excellence has been announced, is an indigenous cattle breed of which State?
(a) Arunachal Pradesh (b) Sikkim (c) Meghalaya (d) Nagaland

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: DGCA monitors fares and the Centre may issue directives only in extraordinary circumstances in public interest.
**Q2 — (b).** The Pakhwada is a Tribal Affairs programme (1–15 Nov) ending on Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas. Jan-HIT is tribe-disaggregated; the FRA platform digitises the process from Gram Sabha filing to titles, not replacing Gram Sabhas.
**Q3 — (b).** Siri is the indigenous cattle of Sikkim; DAHD announced a CoE there on 2 Nov 2026.
