# Daily PIB Briefing — 29 October 2026

*Scope: PIB / government releases of 27–28 Oct 2026. Ministries rotated away from Finance/Cabinet items already covered on 28 Oct (CMPS, PM-VGSY, Khelo India scheme, DA hike). Where only a secondary summary of a PIB release could be read, it is flagged.*

## 1. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **India's National Statement at CBD COP-17, Yerevan (27 Oct 2026)** — GS3 Environment
Union Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav delivered India's statement at the High-Level Segment of the 17th Conference of Parties to the Convention on Biological Diversity. India pressed for "sufficient and predictable" finance, technology transfer and capacity-building for implementing the Kunming–Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework (KMGBF), backed a multilateral benefit-sharing mechanism including the **Cali Fund** for digital sequence information (DSI), and favoured a science-based, precautionary approach to synthetic biology and biosafety.
- Figures cited: updated NBSAP with 23 National Biodiversity Targets and 142 indicators; 1,134 Protected Areas; 103 Ramsar sites; 57 Biodiversity Heritage Sites; 18 Biosphere Reserves (13 in UNESCO's MAB network); International Big Cat Alliance covers seven big-cat species.
- Programmes showcased: Mission LiFE, Ek Ped Maa Ke Naam, MISHTI, Amrit Dharohar, Soil Health Card.
- *Update note:* Ramsar count of 103 was first covered on 16 Oct; new here is the COP-17 negotiating position. (One secondary report also lists publications released at COP-17, e.g. I-OECM Guidelines 2026; not verified, so omitted.)

## 2. Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation: **Quick Estimates of IIP, September 2026** (28 Oct) — GS3 Economy
Base 2022-23=100. Overall IIP growth **6.7% y/y** (index 124.3 vs 116.5 in Sept 2025), down from 8.0% in August. Manufacturing +7.6% (17 of 23 industry groups positive); Electricity & Gas +11.2%; Mining & Quarrying **-6.2%**. Estimates are quick estimates, subject to revision. Useful for the "industrial growth/indicators" theme; note the new base-year series.

## 3. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment: **Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY) — PIB backgrounder (27 Oct)** — GS2 Governance/Social Justice *(secondary summary of PIB backgrounder)*
Umbrella scheme for senior citizens launched 2021, subsuming the National Action Plan for Senior Citizens (NAPSrC). Latest release: ₹77.98 crore to 755 projects for the quarter ending 30 Sept 2026. Elderly (60+): 10.38 crore in Census 2011, projected ~23 crore by 2036.
- Components: IPSrC (grants to NGOs; senior citizen homes, continuous care homes, mobile medicare units), SAPSrC (States/UTs), Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana (assistive devices via ALIMCO), Elderline 14567 helpline, SAGE (elder-care startups, equity up to ₹1 crore), SCOPE job portal, PM-SPECIAL caregiver training, SHATAYU caregiver app.
- Legal frame: Maintenance and Welfare of Parents and Senior Citizens Act, 2007; National Policy on Older Persons, 1999.

## 5. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: **Tobacco-Free Youth Campaign 4.0 launched (60 days)** (reported 28 Oct) — GS2 Governance/Health *(single secondary source; confirm on pib.gov.in)*
Time-bound campaign targeting educational institutions and villages; earlier editions reportedly led to 5.8 lakh+ institutions and 73,000+ villages being declared tobacco-free. Link with COTPA 2003 and the National Tobacco Control Programme for static revision.

### Scheme-in-focus: Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY)
- **Ministry:** Social Justice & Empowerment. **Objective:** dignity, health, care and productive engagement of senior citizens.
- **Key features:** grants to NGOs/States for homes and day-care; free assistive devices (RVY); Elderline 14567; startups and jobs via SAGE/SCOPE; caregiver training (PM-SPECIAL).
- **Beneficiaries:** senior citizens (60+), with device support for BPL/≤₹15,000 monthly income.
- **Significance:** demographic ageing response (~23 crore elderly by 2036), links to Art. 41 DPSP and Maintenance of Parents Act 2007.

## 3 Prelims MCQs
**1.** With reference to India's national statement at CBD COP-17 (Yerevan, October 2026), consider the following statements:
1. India supported a multilateral benefit-sharing mechanism, including the Cali Fund, for digital sequence information on genetic resources.
2. India favoured a science-based, risk-proportionate, precautionary approach to synthetic biology and biosafety.
3. The Nagoya Protocol is the instrument under the CBD that deals with the biosafety of living modified organisms.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 and 3 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**2.** With reference to the Quick Estimates of IIP for September 2026, consider the following statements:
1. The estimates are released by MoSPI on the base year 2022-23=100.
2. Overall IIP growth was 6.7 per cent y/y, with Manufacturing (7.6 per cent) growing faster than the overall index.
3. Mining and Quarrying recorded positive growth, while Electricity contracted.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**3.** With reference to the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY), consider the following statements:
1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment and subsumed the National Action Plan for Senior Citizens.
2. Under its Rashtriya Vayoshri Yojana component, assistive devices go to senior citizens who are BPL or have monthly income up to ₹15,000.
3. It is administered by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 2 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1 and 2 only

## Answers
1. **(c)** — Statements 1 and 2 reflect India's stated positions; biosafety of LMOs is under the Cartagena Protocol, while the Nagoya Protocol covers access and benefit-sharing.
2. **(b)** — IIP is MoSPI's (base 2022-23); growth 6.7%, manufacturing 7.6%. Mining fell 6.2% and Electricity & Gas rose 11.2%, so 3 is reversed.
3. **(d)** — AVYAY is a MoSJE scheme subsuming NAPSrC; RVY is income-limited. It is not under the Ministry of WCD.
