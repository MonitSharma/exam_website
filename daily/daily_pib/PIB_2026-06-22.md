## 1. APEDA flags off India's first-ever export of dried whole egg powder — Odisha to Austria

**Issuing body:** APEDA (Agricultural & Processed Food Products Export Development Authority), Ministry of Commerce & Industry — consignment flagged off ~20 June 2026
**Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — agri-exports, food processing, value-addition) · GS3 (Agriculture — animal husbandry / poultry value chain)

APEDA facilitated India's **first-ever commercial export of dried whole egg powder**, a **22.6 metric-tonne** consignment shipped from **Balangir, Odisha** to **Austria** — marking the entry of Indian value-added poultry products into the strict European Union market. The exporter, **OVO Farm Pvt. Ltd.**, set up a modern egg-processing facility in Balangir in 2025 that converts fresh liquid eggs into shelf-stable powder via **spray-drying**. Why it matters: it signals a shift from exporting raw farm produce to high-value processed goods, and demonstrates that Indian processed animal products can clear demanding EU food-safety norms — a classic GS3 example of agri-value-addition and export diversification.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **Volume:** 22.6 MT; **destination:** Austria (EU market); **origin:** Balangir, Odisha — historically a migration-prone district now emerging as a poultry-processing hub.
- **Process:** industrial **spray-drying** of pasteurised liquid eggs — extends shelf life, eases logistics, ensures microbial safety; end-uses in bakeries, confectionery, cloud kitchens and pharma formulations.
- **Certifications cleared:** **FSSC 22000 (Version 6)**, Export Inspection Council (EIC) clearances, and Halal/Kosher approvals.
- **APEDA's role:** statutory body under the Ministry of Commerce & Industry (set up under the **APEDA Act, 1985**); extended financial assistance for cold-chain logistics, lab modernisation and food-traceability upgrades.

## 2. Ministry of Panchayati Raj rolls out "Nirbhay Chetna" — world's largest drive to sensitise *men* in local governance

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Panchayati Raj — 3-day national Training-of-Trainers (ToT) in New Delhi, 17–19 June 2026; module launched
**Notebook:** GS2 (Governance — Panchayati Raj Institutions, welfare delivery) · GS1 (Society — gender, positive masculinity)

The Ministry of Panchayati Raj launched the **Nirbhay Chetna** training module to sensitise an estimated **17.5 lakh male elected representatives** of rural local bodies on women's safety, gender equality and "positive masculinity" — billed as the **world's largest gender-sensitisation drive focused on men in local governance**. It is one pillar of the umbrella **Nirbhay Raho** programme (launched 11 March 2026), funded through the central **Nirbhaya Fund**. Why it matters: it operationalises Panchayati Raj devolution for social outcomes and engages male leaders — not just women — as agents of gender-responsive grassroots governance.

> **Scheme-in-focus — Nirbhay Raho (umbrella) and its three pillars**
> - **Ministry:** Panchayati Raj; **funding:** central **Nirbhaya Fund**; **knowledge partner:** *Transform Rural India* (developed the Nirbhay Chetna module).
> - **Three complementary pillars:** (i) **Nirbhay Netri** — training and legal awareness for ~**14.5 lakh elected women representatives**; (ii) **Nirbhay Chetna** — sensitising ~**17.5 lakh male elected representatives**; (iii) **Nirbhay Drishti** — safety infrastructure (CCTV cameras at key village locations).
> - **Delivery model:** a cascading cadre of **28,500 Master Trainers** at State / district / block levels; pilot batch of ~40 master trainers drawn from **Assam, Chhattisgarh, Himachal Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha and Uttarakhand**.
> - **Significance:** ties women's safety to the **Viksit Bharat @2047** vision; rare scheme design that targets men to shift social norms at the panchayat level.

## 3. India elected to FATF Vice-Presidency for the first time — Vivek Aggarwal to take charge

**Issuing body:** Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Plenary, Paris (17–19 June 2026); welcomed by Government of India
**Notebook:** GS2 (IR — global standard-setting bodies) · GS3 (Economy / Internal Security — anti-money-laundering & counter-terror financing)

At its Paris Plenary, the **FATF elected India's Vivek Aggarwal as Vice-President** for a one-year term (**July 2026 – June 2027**) — the **first time India holds the FATF Vice-Presidency**. He succeeds the UK's **Giles Thomson**, who moves up to the FATF Presidency. Why it matters: it gives India a leadership voice in shaping global anti-money-laundering (AML) and counter-terror-financing (CFT) standards, leverage India has long sought against terror-financing networks in its neighbourhood.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **About the official:** Vivek Aggarwal — **1994-batch IAS** (Madhya Pradesh cadre), currently **Secretary, Ministry of Culture**; previously **Director, Financial Intelligence Unit-India (FIU-IND)** and Additional Secretary, Department of Revenue, where he led Indian delegations to FATF.
- **About FATF:** an **inter-governmental body** (NOT a UN agency), established at the **1989 G7 Summit in Paris**; sets global standards to combat **money laundering, terror financing and proliferation financing**; maintains the "grey list" and "black list".
- **Secretariat:** housed at the **OECD headquarters, Paris**; India has been a **full member since 2010**.

## 4. IITM–ARIES sign 50-year MoU for a high-altitude climate station at Devasthal under "Bharat Climate Observation Network"

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Earth Sciences — MoU between IITM (Pune) and ARIES (Nainital), signed 18 June 2026 (PIB PRID 2275048)
**Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Tech — climate-research infrastructure) · GS3 (Environment — Himalayan climate monitoring)

The **Indian Institute of Tropical Meteorology (IITM), Pune** and the **Aryabhatta Research Institute of Observational Sciences (ARIES), Nainital** signed a **50-year-plus MoU** to set up a long-term **climate observation station at Devasthal, Uttarakhand**, under the **Bharat Climate Observation Network (BCON)** and the **Mission Mausam** initiative. The high-altitude, relatively pristine site will track **greenhouse gases, soil moisture and other climate variables** to sharpen India's climate research and projections over the Himalayan region. Why it matters: baseline atmospheric data from the Himalayas feeds better weather/climate models — directly tied to Mission Mausam's goal of making India "weather-ready and climate-smart".

**Key facts/figures:**
- **Signatories:** Dr. A. Suryachandra Rao (Director, IITM) and Dr. Manish Kumar Naja (Director, ARIES); MoU spans **over 50 years**.
- **Parent bodies:** IITM is an autonomous institute under the **Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES)**; ARIES is an autonomous institute under the **Department of Science & Technology (DST)**.
- **What it measures:** greenhouse gases, transport of climate-relevant pollutants, soil moisture and baseline atmospheric characteristics in the Himalayan zone.
- **Frameworks:** **BCON** (national climate-observation network) and **Mission Mausam** (₹2,000-crore MoES programme approved in 2024 to upgrade observation, modelling and forecasting).

## 5. World Day to Combat Desertification & Drought observed across 813 WDC–PMKSY 2.0 project areas

**Issuing body:** Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development — observance on 17 June 2026 (PIB PRID 2274283)
**Notebook:** GS3 (Environment — land degradation, desertification, watershed management)

India observed the **World Day to Combat Desertification & Drought** (17 June) across **813 project areas** under the **Watershed Development Component of PMKSY 2.0 (WDC–PMKSY 2.0)**. The **2026 global theme — "Rangelands: Recognize. Respect. Restore."** — coincides with the **International Year of Rangelands and Pastoralists**, spotlighting degraded grazing lands and pastoral communities. Why it matters: India is a party to the **UN Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD)** and has pledged to restore **26 million hectares** of degraded land by 2030 — watershed development is a core delivery vehicle.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **Theme 2026:** *"Rangelands: Recognize. Respect. Restore."*; **host country** of the global observance: **Kenya**; the day is observed annually on **17 June** (anniversary of the UNCCD's adoption in 1994).
- **Delivery scheme:** **WDC–PMKSY 2.0** — under the **Department of Land Resources, Ministry of Rural Development** — develops rain-fed/degraded lands through soil & moisture conservation, water harvesting and afforestation.
- **Coverage:** activities held across **813 project areas** nationwide.
- **India's commitments:** UNCCD party since 1996; **Bonn Challenge** pledge to restore **26 million hectares** of degraded land by 2030; relevant flagship programmes include the **Green India Mission** and the **Aravalli Green Wall** project.
