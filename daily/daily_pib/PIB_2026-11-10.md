## 1. Cabinet / Ministry of Defence: **"Second Tour of Duty" — Agniveers to serve two more years under Agnipath** (Cabinet decision, 4 Nov 2026; announced by Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh) — GS3 Internal Security / GS2 Governance

**What & why:** The Cabinet approved a two-year extension for Agniveers who are not absorbed into the regular cadre after the original four-year tenure. Earlier, only ~25% were eligible for regular enrolment on merit and the remaining ~75% exited after four years; the revised arrangement lets this group serve up to six years in total and then apply for regular enrolment under objective selection criteria. It addresses concerns over retention of trained manpower as the first batch nears the end of its tenure.

**Key facts:**
- Agnipath scheme launched 14 June 2022 (four-year engagement).
- The CDS clarified that the original four-year structure is unchanged and the scheme is not discontinued — the extension is an added option.
- Pathway for any willing Agniveer to become a regular soldier after four or six years, subject to criteria.
- *(Pay-scale/Seva Nidhi figures for year 5–6 appear in only one aggregator; not reproduced here.)*

**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (defence manpower reform); GS2 Governance.

## 2. Ministry of Mines: **9th tranche of Critical Mineral Block e-auction launched, with 3rd tranche of Exploration Licence (EL) auction** (5 Nov 2026; launched by Union Minister G. Kishan Reddy) — GS3 Economy / Sci-Tech / Mining

**What & why:** 20 critical and strategic mineral blocks are on offer, supporting the National Critical Mineral Mission and reducing import dependence for minerals used in batteries, electronics and clean energy. (Complements the 4 Nov Cabinet capex scheme for critical-mineral processing — that was downstream; this is upstream block allocation.)

**Key facts:**
- 20 blocks: Chhattisgarh 5; Rajasthan, Telangana, West Bengal 3 each; Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra 2 each; Bihar, Andhra Pradesh 1 each. Minerals include graphite, vanadium, gallium, titanium, tungsten, scandium.
- Across the first eight tranches, 100 unique blocks were offered and 64 successfully auctioned.
- EL auction (29 critical and deep-seated minerals): 11 EL blocks auctioned so far in two tranches.
- Mineral (Auction) Third Amendment Rules, 2026 (notified 16 Oct): minimum Technically Qualified Bidders for the second round cut from three to two for Part D minerals; Letters of Intent now issued automatically via the Unified Mining Portal.
- Critical mineral blocks: two-stage ascending forward auction (highest revenue-share bid wins); EL blocks: descending reverse auction (lowest revenue share wins). Conducted on MSTC e-auction platform. Bid deadline 7 Jan 2027.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy / Sci-Tech (resources, critical minerals).

## 3. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment: **Vayo Samman Rashtriya Abhiyan — national intergenerational bonding campaign** (launched 4 Nov 2026, New Delhi, by Dr Virendra Kumar) — GS2 Governance / Social Justice *(UPDATE — AVYAY scheme covered on 29 Oct; new is this campaign)*

**What & why:** A campaign under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY) to strengthen solidarity between generations and recognise the experience and contribution of senior citizens, reaching every village and mohalla through Panchayats, local bodies and educational institutions. Frames senior welfare as a shared social commitment, not only a state responsibility. A volunteer initiative, **Vayo SAATHI** (open to citizens 18+), was reported alongside it *(single secondary source)*.

**Notebook:** GS2 Social Justice (vulnerable sections, elderly); GS1 Society (ageing, family).

## 4. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **India's priorities for UNFCCC COP31 (Antalya, Türkiye, 9–20 Nov 2026)** (briefing by Minister Bhupender Yadav, early Nov) — GS3 Environment / GS2 IR *(UPDATE — CBD COP-17 covered on 29–30 Oct; this is the climate track)*

**What & why:** India's negotiating stance shapes its position among developing countries ahead of the first COP held in Türkiye.

**Key facts:**
- **Climate finance:** push to launch the Climate Finance Work Programme; developed countries must meet **Article 9.1** (Paris Agreement) obligations; finance should be "predictable, largely public and concessional" and not add to debt.
- **Mitigation / Global Stocktake:** should remain Party-driven, facilitative, non-punitive; NDCs are nationally determined; no new top-down targets.
- **Adaptation:** supports voluntary indicators under the Global Goal on Adaptation, not as conditions for finance.
- **Just Transition Work Programme** to continue; technology transfer is a developed-country obligation; carbon-market participation voluntary.
- Coalitions: G-77 & China, LMDC, BASIC. Side events: LeadIT 3.0 (with Sweden), International Big Cat Alliance, coastal resilience.
- Related: at **UNCCD COP17 (Ulaanbaatar)** India, as Chair of the Asia-Pacific Regional Group, reiterated its pledge to restore **26+ million hectares** of degraded land by 2030.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (climate negotiations); GS2 IR.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the revised Agnipath arrangement approved by the Union Cabinet in November 2026, consider the statements:
1. Agniveers not absorbed into the regular cadre can serve two additional years.
2. The original four-year engagement under the scheme has been discontinued.
3. After the extended period, willing Agniveers can apply for regular enrolment under objective criteria.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** In the auction of critical and strategic mineral blocks conducted by the Ministry of Mines, which of the following is correct?
(a) Blocks are awarded through a two-stage descending reverse auction to the lowest revenue-share bidder
(b) Critical mineral blocks use a two-stage ascending forward auction won by the highest revenue-share bid, while Exploration Licence blocks use a descending reverse auction won by the lowest revenue-share bid
(c) Only public sector undertakings may bid for critical mineral blocks
(d) Exploration Licences are awarded by lottery

**Q3.** With reference to India's stance at UNFCCC COP31, consider the following statements:
1. India seeks fulfilment of developed countries' obligations under Article 9.1 of the Paris Agreement.
2. India favours binding top-down mitigation targets for all Parties.
3. India wants the Just Transition Work Programme to continue.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 3 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — Statement 2 is wrong: the CDS clarified the four-year structure is unchanged; the extension is an added option for non-absorbed Agniveers.
2. **(b)** — Critical mineral blocks: two-stage ascending forward auction (highest revenue share); EL blocks: descending reverse auction (lowest revenue share); run on the MSTC platform.
3. **(b)** — India wants Article 9.1 finance obligations met and the Just Transition Work Programme continued, but opposes new top-down mitigation targets (stocktake should be Party-driven, non-punitive).
