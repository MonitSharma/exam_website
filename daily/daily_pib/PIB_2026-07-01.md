# Daily PIB Briefing — July 1, 2026

## 1. NITI Aayog–Ministry of Tourism release "Unlocking Growth in Tourism and Hospitality Sector"
**Ministry:** NITI Aayog / Ministry of Tourism | National Workshop, New Delhi, June 30, 2026

A joint NITI Aayog–Tourism Ministry report diagnoses India's tourism bottleneck as regulatory and institutional, not a lack of demand, and proposes a reform roadmap under the Viksit Bharat 2047 vision. It matters because tourism already contributes ₹15.73 lakh crore (~5.22% of GDP) and 84.6 million jobs, yet India's Visa Openness Index score (38.14) trails the global average (40).

Key facts: domestic tourist visits hit 2.9 billion in 2024 (vs 2.3 billion pre-pandemic peak); proposals include a 90-day multiple-entry Visa-on-Arrival for select nationalities, a single liquor/health-trade licence for hotels, de-linking star classification from regulatory approvals, and a proposed Tourist Refund Scheme (TRS) for GST on foreign-tourist purchases.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (tourism sector, ease of doing business) / GS2 Governance (Centre–State coordination)

## 2. Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) & APAAR cross 26.30 crore verified student IDs
**Ministry:** Ministry of Education / University Grants Commission (UGC)

ABC, UGC's digital platform for storing, transferring, and redeeming academic credits, anchors India's shift to a flexible, lifelong-learning system under NEP 2020. It matters as the backbone of "One Nation, One Student ID" and India's education Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), alongside DigiLocker, SAMARTH, and SWAYAM.

Key facts: 26.30 crore verified APAAR IDs generated (as of June 2026); credits valid up to 7 years, non-reusable once redeemed; 153 universities offer Multiple Entry & Exit; up to 40% of credits redeemable via SWAYAM (388 universities); UGC set a June 30, 2026 deadline for all HEIs to upload credit data to the ABC portal; the "Bharat Praman Chain" (Digital India Corporation's sovereign blockchain) issues tamper-resistant digital credentials.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (education policy, DPI) / GS3 Science & Tech (blockchain credentialing)

## 3. Kisan Sarathi crosses 2.95 crore registered farmers
**Ministry:** Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) / Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare

Kisan Sarathi, India's largest integrated digital agro-advisory platform (launched 2021, run via IASRI and Digital India Corporation), delivers multilingual, two-way farmer-expert advisory using an Interactive Information Dissemination System (IIDS). It matters as a scaled model of agri-tech convergence for last-mile advisory delivery.

Key facts: 2.95 crore registered farmers (as of June 25, 2026), including 56.16 lakh women; live across 37 States/UTs, 768 districts, 6.63 lakh villages; supports 13 regional languages; hosts information on 610 government schemes (102 Central); has handled 19.21 lakh farmer queries and issued 21,900+ advisories.

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture (digital agri-advisory, farmer welfare)

## 4. Rashtriya Gramin Vikas Sammelan 2026 — VB-GRAM-G rollout, SHE-LEAPS, Lakhpati Didi Dashboard
**Ministry:** Ministry of Rural Development | New Delhi, June 28–29, 2026 (rollout from July 1)

A two-day conference under the theme "Gramodaya Se Rashtrodaya" brought Rural Development Ministers from 29 States/UTs together to chart the 'Viksit Gram–Viksit Bharat' roadmap. It matters because it launches the Viksit Bharat–GRAM-G framework nationwide from July 1, 2026, restructuring MGNREGS-linked rural employment delivery, alongside new women-livelihood digital tools.

Key facts: SHE-LEAPS (Self Help Entrepreneur – Livelihoods and Enterprise Application for Prosperity and Sustainability) launched to support the raised target of 6 crore 'Lakhpati Didis' (up from 3 crore), with a ₹10 lakh crore bank-linkage roadmap over five years; Lakhpati Didi Dashboard launched for real-time tracking; SARAS Shakti collection launched to boost rural women-led enterprises; a National Study Report on "Low Participation in Gram Sabha Across States and UTs" was also released.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (Panchayati Raj, rural service delivery) / GS3 Economy (rural livelihoods, women's SHGs)

## 5. Rajnath Singh releases Delegation of Financial Powers to DRDO 2026 (DFP-2026)
**Ministry:** Ministry of Defence (DRDO) | June 29, 2026

DFP-2026 substantially expands financial and functional powers delegated at various levels within Defence R&D, aimed at faster execution of strategic R&D projects and quicker induction of indigenous systems into the Armed Forces. It matters as a governance reform meant to cut procedural delays in translating DRDO research into fielded defence technology, reinforcing Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence.

Key facts: includes dedicated financial provisions for trials and pre-project R&D, and clearer segregation of grants-in-aid; released alongside a parallel Delegation of Financial Powers to Defence Services 2026 (DFPDS-2026) to speed up defence procurement approvals.

**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security/Science & Tech (defence R&D, indigenisation) / GS2 Governance (financial delegation reform)

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the NITI Aayog–Ministry of Tourism report "Unlocking Growth in Tourism and Hospitality Sector" (2026):
1. It recommends de-linking hotel star classification from licensing and finance approvals.
2. It proposes a 90-day multiple-entry Visa-on-Arrival for select nationalities.
3. Tourism is classified as a Union subject under the Indian Constitution.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) and APAAR, consider the following statements:
1. ABC is regulated by the University Grants Commission.
2. Academic credits stored in ABC can be reused indefinitely once redeemed.
3. APAAR authentication relies on Aadhaar-linked DigiLocker credentials.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The "SHE-LEAPS" platform, launched at the Rashtriya Gramin Vikas Sammelan 2026, is associated with which of the following?

(a) Tracking Self-Help Group women entrepreneurs under the 'Lakhpati Didi' mission
(b) Providing digital agro-advisory services to farmers
(c) Issuing tamper-resistant academic credentials via blockchain
(d) Delegating financial powers for defence R&D projects

---

## Answers

**Q1. Answer: (a)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Tourism is a *State* subject (not Union), with the Centre coordinating through centrally sponsored schemes; statement 3 is incorrect.

**Q2. Answer: (b)** — Statements 1 and 3 are correct. ABC credits are valid for a maximum of 7 years and cannot be reused once redeemed, making statement 2 incorrect.

**Q3. Answer: (a)** — SHE-LEAPS (Self Help Entrepreneur – Livelihoods and Enterprise Application for Prosperity and Sustainability) is a digital platform to track and scale income growth of women-led SHG enterprises toward the 6-crore 'Lakhpati Didi' target.
