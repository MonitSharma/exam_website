## 1. NITI Aayog (Science & Technology Division): **India S&T Scoreboard launched, 3 Nov 2026, New Delhi** — GS3 Sci-Tech / GS2 Governance *(UPDATE — the 4 Nov briefing carried only the headline)*
**What & why it matters:** An interactive data platform that maps India's science and innovation ecosystem across States/UTs, so states can benchmark, spot gaps and prioritise interventions (cooperative federalism applied to R&D). Launched by NITI Aayog; DST Secretary also spoke.
**Key facts:** Indicators cover research institutions, publications and funding (central, state, private), patents (filings, strength, inter-state collaboration), start-ups incl. deep-tech, State S&T&I policies, Atal Tinkering Labs, and 11 priority research areas (clean energy, AI, semiconductors, critical minerals, agriculture, etc.). Features: national overview, state profiles, side-by-side comparison of any two States/UTs. Cited context: India 38th in Global Innovation Index (81st in 2015); 3rd in scientific publications; 6th in patent filings; resident share of patent filings 25.5% → 56%. Periodic updates planned. Separately, NITI Aayog and TEXMiN signed a Joint Statement of Intent on critical and strategic minerals (research, technical studies, policy support, capacity building) *(aggregator-sourced)*.

## 2. Ministry of Culture: **MoU with Google Arts & Culture to digitise India's cultural heritage (5 Nov 2026)** — GS1 Art & Culture / GS3 Sci-Tech
**What & why it matters:** Signed virtually (Culture Secretary Vivek Aggarwal; Google Arts & Culture's Amit Sood). Covers ASI-protected heritage, national museum collections, manuscripts documented under *Gyan Bharatam*, and crafts, performing arts and living traditions. A central focus is the planned *Yuge Yugeen Bharat* Museum in the North and South Blocks, New Delhi. Number of artefacts and MoU duration were not stated in the report.
**Why exam-relevant:** digital preservation of heritage, public–private partnership, soft power.

## 3. Ministry of New & Renewable Energy: **Bharat Renewable Energy (BRE) Summit & Expo 2026, Bharat Mandapam, 2–5 Nov** — GS3 Economy/Energy/Environment
**What & why it matters:** Inaugurated by the PM on 2 Nov; co-located with the 4th International Conference on Green Hydrogen and the **9th Session of the International Solar Alliance (ISA) Assembly**. Government estimates ~$220 billion is needed to reach the 500 GW non-fossil capacity target; financing enablers discussed: bankable PPAs, deeper bond markets, InvITs, blended finance.
**Key facts:** 135 countries, 75,000+ visitors, 400+ exhibitors; SECI–Power2X MoU on green molecules (first tranche indicating ~3 lakh tonnes/yr green methanol); ISA announcements: proposed $200 million Africa Solar Facility, ambition of one million solar pumps globally, up to 500 MW solar for small island developing states by 2030, SUNRISE circularity network expanded to 35 countries; 10 MoUs and 37 reports/platforms released (e.g., *Ease of Doing Solar*, National Hydrogen Survey). MNRE says renewables supplied over a quarter of India's electricity consumption last FY. *(single news source for figures)*

## 4. Ministry of Electronics & IT (Digital India BHASHINI Division) with SEBI: **MoU for investor education and services in regional languages (3 Nov 2026)** — GS2 Governance / GS3 Economy *(UPDATE — earlier headline-only)*
**What & why it matters:** BHASHINI's language tech (ASR, TTS, machine translation, OCR) will deliver SEBI's investor education and services in Indian languages; securities-market terminology will be added to BHASHINI models. Advances financial inclusion and language accessibility. *(aggregator-sourced)*

## 5. Ministry of Defence (DRDO): **Pralay quasi-ballistic missile test from ITR Chandipur, Odisha (3 Nov 2026)** — GS3 Internal Security/Defence Tech *(aggregator-sourced)*
**What & why it matters:** Surface-to-surface, solid-propellant, road-mobile missile; reported two simulated targets engaged at ~500 km. Reported specs: range 150–500 km, payload 350–1,000 kg. Strengthens conventional deterrence/aatmanirbharta in defence.

### Scheme-in-focus: India S&T Scoreboard (NITI Aayog)
- **Objective:** evidence-based, state-level benchmarking of science, technology and innovation.
- **Key features:** national + state dashboards; two-state comparison; indicators on institutions, publications, patents, start-ups, policies, ATLs; 11 priority research areas.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about the India S&T Scoreboard launched in November 2026:
1. It was launched by NITI Aayog and maps science and innovation performance across States and Union Territories.
2. It allows comparison of any two States/UTs.
3. It is published by the Ministry of Education as part of NIRF.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The Ministry of Culture recently signed an MoU with Google Arts & Culture. Which of the following is NOT within its stated scope?
(a) Heritage protected by the Archaeological Survey of India
(b) Manuscripts documented under Gyan Bharatam
(c) Planned Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum in the North and South Blocks
(d) Digitisation of Indian Parliament's legislative records

**Q3.** The 9th Session of the International Solar Alliance Assembly was held alongside which event in November 2026?
(a) Global Renewable Energy Investors Meet, Gandhinagar
(b) Bharat Renewable Energy (BRE) Summit & Expo, New Delhi
(c) World Hydropower Congress, Mumbai
(d) COP31 side events, Antalya

## Answers
1. **(b)** — The Scoreboard is a NITI Aayog (S&T Division) platform enabling state comparison; it is not a Ministry of Education/NIRF product.
2. **(d)** — The MoU covers ASI heritage, museum collections, Gyan Bharatam manuscripts, crafts/performing arts and the Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum; parliamentary records are not mentioned.
3. **(b)** — MNRE's BRE Summit & Expo (2–5 Nov, Bharat Mandapam) co-hosted the ISA's 9th Assembly session and the 4th International Conference on Green Hydrogen.
