## 1. PM rolls out farm-sector package in West Bengal — National Mission on Natural Farming + PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana + PMFBY

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare / PMO (Paschimbanga Divas event, Tarakeswar, Hooghly — 20 June 2026)
**Notebook:** GS3 (Agriculture — sustainable farming, crop insurance, productivity) · GS2 (Governance — scheme delivery)

On West Bengal Foundation Day the PM launched/extended a cluster of farm schemes in the State — the **National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)**, the **Pradhan Mantri Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (PMDDKY)**, and the **Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)**. Why it matters: it brings three of the Centre's flagship agriculture instruments — chemical-free farming, a districts-based productivity mission, and crop insurance — into a State that had stayed outside several of them, making it a clean prelims + GS3 anchor on India's current agriculture architecture.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **NMNF in WB:** 346 natural-farming clusters over ~17,300 hectares; Bio-Input Resource Centres (BRCs) and **Krishi Sakhis** to drive adoption.
- **PMFBY in WB (2026–27):** to cover nearly **50 lakh farmers** over ~**14 lakh hectares**, insured value ~**₹28,140 crore**.
- PMDDKY targets **100 low-productivity districts** (productivity, low cropping intensity, low credit) — convergence of **36 schemes across 11 departments**.

> **Scheme-in-focus — National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)**
> - **Ministry:** Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare; **standalone Centrally Sponsored Scheme** approved **25 November 2024**, total outlay **₹2,481 crore** (to 2025–26).
> - **Objective:** promote chemical-free, traditional farming using on-farm biomass / livestock-based inputs; reduce input costs and improve soil health.
> - **Key features:** (i) **15,000 NF clusters** & **1 crore farmers** / 7.5 lakh ha targeted; (ii) ~**10,000 Bio-Input Resource Centres (BRCs)**; (iii) cadre of **Krishi Sakhis** + Model Demonstration Farms; (iv) a simple **NMNF certification & branding** system for natural-farm produce.
> - **Significance:** institutionalises natural farming (earlier under BPKP/Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana) as a national mission; links to soil health, climate resilience and lower import dependence on agro-chemicals.

## 2. AgriStack rolled out in West Bengal under the Digital Agriculture Mission

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare (Digital Agriculture Mission) — launched in WB on 20 June 2026
**Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Tech — Digital Public Infrastructure for agriculture; Economy)

Alongside the farm package, **AgriStack** — the farmer-centric Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) — was launched in West Bengal. It creates a verified **Farmer ID** and registries so that benefits like fertiliser, **Kisan Credit Card (KCC)**, Direct Benefit Transfer, MSP procurement and crop insurance can be delivered seamlessly and without leakage. Why it matters: AgriStack is the agriculture analogue of Aadhaar/UPI-style DPI and is a recurring Sci-Tech/governance theme.

**Key facts/figures:**
- AgriStack is one of **three foundational pillars** of the **Digital Agriculture Mission** (approved Sept 2024, outlay ~**₹2,817 crore**) — the others being the **Krishi Decision Support System (Krishi-DSS)** and **Soil Profile Mapping**.
- AgriStack's three core registries: **Farmers' Registry**, **Geo-referenced Village (land) Maps**, and **Crop Sown Registry** (via Digital Crop Survey).
- The **Farmer ID** is a unique digital identity linked to land records, enabling targeted, paperless delivery of agri-services.

## 3. Fisheries infrastructure: Fraserganj fishing harbour modernised; new modern fish market at Sainthia

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying (Department of Fisheries), West Bengal projects — 20 June 2026
**Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — blue economy, allied agriculture; infrastructure)

The PM inaugurated the **modernised, capacity-expanded Fishing Harbour at Fraserganj** (South 24 Parganas) and a newly built **Modern Fish Market at Sainthia** (Birbhum). Why it matters: West Bengal is a leading **inland fish** producer, and harbour/market modernisation feeds the "blue economy" and post-harvest-loss-reduction story under PMMSY — a frequent GS3 hook.

**Key facts/figures:**
- Likely funded under the **Pradhan Mantri Matsya Sampada Yojana (PMMSY)** — launched **2020**, outlay **₹20,050 crore**, the largest-ever investment in fisheries; aims to boost fish production, exports and fishers' incomes.
- India is the **world's 2nd-largest fish producer** and 2nd-largest in **aquaculture**; fisheries is a **Sunrise Sector** and a major source of farm-export earnings (notably frozen shrimp).
- Fishing harbours and landing centres reduce post-harvest losses and improve price realisation for fishers.

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Railways, West Bengal projects (~₹590 crore) — 20 June 2026
**Notebook:** GS3 (Infrastructure — rail connectivity, logistics)

The PM dedicated the **Sankrail–Santragachi Link Line** (Howrah district) and laid foundation stones for a **300-bed New Divisional Railway Hospital at Howrah** and a **Road Over Bridge (ROB)** between Haur and Radhamohanpur (Purba Medinipur). **Key facts/figures:**

- Indian Railways runs **Asia's largest** and the **world's 4th-largest** rail network; broad-gauge network is near **100% electrified**, supporting the **Net-Zero carbon emitter by 2030** goal.

## 5. Ministry of Home Affairs — 12-year internal-security review: Left-Wing Extremism districts cut from 126 to 12

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA), 2014–2026 review
**Notebook:** GS3 (Internal Security — Left-Wing Extremism, security forces, development) · GS2 (Governance)

As part of the government's running 12-year-review series, MHA highlighted the sharp contraction of **Left-Wing Extremism (LWE/Naxalism)**. Why it matters: high-yield internal-security data points plus the policy template (security + development, the **SAMADHAN** doctrine) that examiners favour. The review also recalls **PRAHAAR** (23 Feb 2026), India's first comprehensive **National Counter-Terrorism Policy and Strategy**.

**Key facts/figures:**
- **Most LWE-affected districts:** down from **126 (2014) to 12**; LWE-affected area cut from **>18,000 sq km to ~4,200 sq km**.
- **597 fortified police stations** built (vs 66 pre-2014); **408 new CAPF camps**; 68 night-landing helipads.
- Anti-LWE strategy combines security (CAPF deployment, fortified stations) with development saturation (roads, telecom, banking, **Aspirational Districts**) — the **SAMADHAN** doctrine; MHA is the nodal ministry for internal security and CAPFs.

## Answers

**Q1 — (d).** All three are correct. NMNF (approved 25 Nov 2024; outlay ₹2,481 crore) is a **standalone Centrally Sponsored Scheme** of the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare; it promotes **chemical-free, biomass/livestock-input-based** farming; and **Bio-Input Resource Centres (BRCs)** plus Krishi Sakhis are core delivery features.

**Q2 — (b).** Statements 2 and 3 are correct. AgriStack is implemented by the **Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare** (not MeitY), so statement 1 is wrong. Its **Farmer ID** is a unique digital identity tied to **land records**, and its core registries include the **Geo-referenced Village Maps and Crop Sown Registry** (besides the Farmers' Registry). AgriStack is one of three pillars of the Digital Agriculture Mission (with Krishi-DSS and Soil Profile Mapping).

**Q3 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Most LWE-affected districts fell from **126 (2014) to 12**, and **MHA** is the nodal ministry for internal security and the **CAPFs**. Statement 3 is wrong — **PRAHAAR** (23 Feb 2026) is the **Ministry of Home Affairs'** National Counter-Terrorism Policy and Strategy, not a Ministry of Defence military doctrine.
