## 1. ISRO hot-tests semi-cryogenic engine at 175-tonne thrust — a step to the SC120 stage for LVM3

**Issuing body:** ISRO / Department of Space (test at ISRO Propulsion Complex, Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu)

ISRO successfully conducted a hot test of the **Power Head Test Article (PHTA)** of its indigenous **semi-cryogenic engine** at a **175-tonne (≈88% of designed) thrust level** — the 8th in the test series, after earlier runs at 94 tonne (47%) and 120 tonne (60%). The PHTA includes all engine systems *except* the thrust chamber, and demonstrated stable operation of the main turbopumps delivering 400 bar and 500 bar outlet pressures. The test gives ISRO confidence to move to steady-state demonstration at the full **200-tonne (100%) thrust**.

**Why it matters (GS3 – Sci-Tech):** The engine (the **2000 kN-class SE2000**) powers the new **Semi-Cryogenic Propulsion Stage (SC120)**, which will **replace the existing L110 liquid core stage of the LVM3** launch vehicle. Semi-cryogenic engines burn **refined kerosene (isrosene) with liquid oxygen** — cheaper, denser, more eco-friendly and higher-thrust than the current hydrazine-based core — boosting LVM3 payload capacity for future heavy-lift, Gaganyaan-class and lunar missions.

## 2. ASPIRE crosses 100 incubators — 109 Livelihood Business Incubators across 27 States/UTs

**Issuing ministry:** Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME)

The Ministry of MSME highlighted that **109 Livelihood Business Incubators (LBIs)** now stand approved across **27 States/UTs** under the **ASPIRE** scheme, having trained **1.23+ lakh beneficiaries** and helped establish **1,200+ micro-enterprises**. The LBI network offers training, mentoring, technology support and market linkages to move people from skilling to enterprise creation in sectors such as food processing, honey, bamboo, mushroom cultivation, spice processing, handicrafts and coir.

**Why it matters (GS3 Economy / GS2 Governance):** ASPIRE targets rural and agro-based entrepreneurship and job creation, with strong social inclusion — since FY 2022-23 it has reached **28,500+ women, 8,700+ SC, 9,600+ ST and 17,600+ OBC** beneficiaries.

### Scheme-in-focus: ASPIRE

- **Full form / Ministry:** *A Scheme for Promotion of Innovation, Rural Industries and Entrepreneurship*; Ministry of MSME, launched **2015** (guidelines revised 2018 and 2023).
- **Objective:** Promote innovation, rural & agro-industry, and entrepreneurship to generate employment and reduce migration.
- **Key features:** Network of **Livelihood Business Incubators (LBIs)** and Technology Business Incubators; hand-holding from skill to enterprise; focus on measurable livelihood outcomes (2023 guidelines).
- **Significance:** Strengthens the rural MSME pipeline and complements "vocal for local" by formalising micro-enterprises in non-metro India.

## 3. NITI Aayog releases the 8th *Trade Watch Quarterly* (Q4 FY26) — spotlight on pharma

**Issuing body:** NITI Aayog

NITI Aayog released the **eighth edition of the Trade Watch Quarterly**, covering **Q4 FY 2025-26 (Jan–Mar 2026)**. India's **total merchandise + services trade grew 5.4% YoY to USD 1.84 trillion**, with **exports up 4.2%** and **imports up 6.5%**. **Services exports rose 9.0%**, sustaining a services surplus; India remained the **8th-largest services exporter in 2025**, with services exports clocking a **10.3% CAGR (2015–2025)**, above the global average.

**Why it matters (GS3 Economy / Reports & Indices):** The thematic focus is the **pharmaceutical sector** — the global pharma + API market is ~**USD 1.3 trillion (2025)**, and India's **pharma + API exports reached USD 35.8 billion**, underscoring India's "pharmacy of the world" position even as it pushes to climb the value chain.

## 4. IAS e-Civil List 2026 launched — technology-driven mapping of officers

**Issuing ministry:** Department of Personnel & Training (DoPT), Ministry of Personnel, PG & Pensions (Dr. Jitendra Singh, MoS)

Dr. Jitendra Singh launched the **IAS e-Civil List 2026** at **Kartavya Bhawan, New Delhi** — the **71st edition** of the Civil List and the **6th** to be released as a searchable e-book/PDF. It carries each officer's batch, cadre, present posting, educational qualifications, pay level and date of superannuation, accessible on the DoPT website.

**Why it matters (GS2 Governance):** Digital, real-time talent mapping aids administrative planning, talent identification and deployment. As on **1 January 2026**, **5,755 IAS officers were in position against a sanctioned cadre strength of 7,026** across **25 cadres** — a reminder of the persistent shortfall in the all-India services.

## 5. PMRC Scheme 2026 — drawing global Indian research talent home

**Issuing ministry:** Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education

The Ministry of Education opened applications for the **Prime Minister Research Chair (PMRC) Scheme 2026** (window **1 June – 15 July 2026**), to attract accomplished **Indian-origin researchers, scientists and professionals** (including Indian nationals abroad, OCI cardholders and PIOs) to premier government higher-education institutions, national labs and research centres.

**Why it matters (GS2 – Education / GS3 – Sci-Tech):** Engagement is offered under three tiers — **Young Research Fellows, Senior Research Fellows and Research Chairs** — with grants up to **₹4 crore, ₹6.5 crore and ₹14 crore** respectively, supporting work across **13 national-priority areas** (AI, quantum computing, semiconductors, cybersecurity, healthcare, biotechnology, climate change, advanced materials, defence, communications, manufacturing, agriculture, blue economy, atomic energy). It targets reverse brain-drain and strengthens India's R&D-to-innovation pipeline.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the **semi-cryogenic engine** recently hot-tested by ISRO, consider the following statements:
1. It uses refined kerosene with liquid oxygen as propellants.
2. The SC120 stage powered by it is intended to replace the L110 core stage of the LVM3.
3. The hot test was conducted at the ISRO Propulsion Complex, Mahendragiri.

How many of the above statements are correct?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about the **Trade Watch Quarterly**, in news recently:
1. It is published by NITI Aayog.
2. As per its latest edition, India was the world's largest services exporter in 2025.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 2 only
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Q3.** The **ASPIRE** scheme, seen in the news for crossing 100 Livelihood Business Incubators, is implemented by the Ministry of:
(a) Rural Development
(b) Skill Development and Entrepreneurship
(c) Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises
(d) Agriculture and Farmers Welfare

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (c) All three.** Semi-cryogenic engines burn refined kerosene (isrosene) + liquid oxygen; the SC120 stage (SE2000 engine) will replace the LVM3's L110 core; the PHTA was hot-tested at ISRO Propulsion Complex, Mahendragiri (Tamil Nadu).

**Q2 — (a) 1 only.** The Trade Watch Quarterly is published by NITI Aayog. India was the **8th-largest** services exporter in 2025, not the largest — so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q3 — (c) Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises.** ASPIRE (A Scheme for Promotion of Innovation, Rural Industries and Entrepreneurship), launched in 2015, is a Ministry of MSME scheme; its 109 LBIs now span 27 States/UTs.
