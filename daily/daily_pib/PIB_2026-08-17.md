## 1. Container Manufacturing Assistance Scheme (CMAS) — ₹10,000 crore to end India's dependence on imported shipping containers

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways (MoPSW); announced in Union Budget 2026-27, now formally pushed by the Ministry.

**What it is.** India moves the overwhelming bulk of its containerised trade in boxes it does not make — global container manufacturing is concentrated in China, which leaves Indian exporters exposed to box shortages and freight-rate spikes. CMAS is a ₹10,000 crore, five-year production-support scheme to build a domestic container manufacturing ecosystem.

**Why it matters (GS3 Economy / Infrastructure).** This is the "trade-resilience" leg of the maritime push that already includes the Maritime Development Fund, shipbuilding clusters and the proposed **Bharat Container Shipping Line (BCSL)**. Containers are a classic supply-chain chokepoint: a shortage does not just raise costs, it strands cargo. Expect questions linking CMAS to *atmanirbharta* in logistics and to the Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047.

**Key facts**

- Outlay: **₹10,000 crore over five years**; announced in Union Budget 2026-27.
- Support for setting up **new** container manufacturing units and expanding existing ones.
- Near-term capacity target: about **7.5 lakh TEUs** of annual domestic manufacturing capacity.
- Ten-year ambition of the Ministry: **1 million TEUs per year** of Indian manufacturing.
- Employment estimate: roughly **3,000 direct** and **over 50,000 indirect** jobs.
- Related vehicle: **Bharat Container Shipping Line (BCSL)**, moved in February 2026 with SCI, CONCOR and major port authorities as stakeholders.

**Scheme-in-focus: Container Manufacturing Assistance Scheme (CMAS)**

| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Ministry | Ports, Shipping and Waterways |
| Objective | Create a globally competitive domestic container manufacturing base; cut import dependence for containerised trade |
| Key features | (i) ₹10,000 crore over 5 years; (ii) capital/financial assistance for greenfield and brownfield container plants; (iii) capacity ramp to ~7.5 lakh TEUs annually, aiming at 1 million TEUs/year in a decade; (iv) institutional support tied to port and logistics infrastructure |
| Beneficiaries | Container manufacturers, exporters and EXIM logistics operators, port-adjacent industrial clusters, ancillary steel and fabrication MSMEs |
| Significance | Removes a single-country supply chokepoint in India's export logistics; complements Maritime Development Fund, shipbuilding clusters and BCSL |

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Infrastructure / Manufacturing / Logistics).

## 2. MoD signs a 30-month lease for two MQ-9B Sea Guardian HALE drones for the Indian Navy

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Defence, Department of Defence (Acquisition Wing) — contract with **General Atomics Aeronautical Systems, Inc. (GA-ASI)**, USA.

**What it is.** The Ministry of Defence signed a contract on **17 August 2026** at Kartavya Bhawan-2, New Delhi, to **lease two MQ-9B Sea Guardian** High-Altitude Long-Endurance (HALE) Remotely Piloted Aircraft Systems (RPAS) for the Indian Navy for **30 months**, at a reported value of about **₹1,943 crore**. The signing was in the presence of the Additional Secretary & Director General (Acquisition), Shri A. Anbarasu.

**Why it matters (GS3 Internal Security / GS2 IR).** Leasing — rather than only buying — is now an accepted route under India's defence procurement framework, and it lets the Navy hold surveillance capability *now* while the larger 31-aircraft MQ-9B deal signed with the US in 2024 is executed. The stated purpose is **Maritime Domain Awareness**: persistent ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance) over the Indian Ocean Region, where Chinese research and naval vessel deployments are the standing concern.

**Key facts**

- **2 MQ-9B Sea Guardian** HALE RPAS; **30-month lease**; vendor **GA-ASI**.
- Contract value about **₹1,943 crore**; signed **17 August 2026**, New Delhi.
- Role: persistent ISR and **Maritime Domain Awareness** across the Indian Ocean Region.
- Background: the Navy has operated leased MQ-9Bs since 2020; separately, India signed a government-to-government deal in 2024 for **31 MQ-9B** aircraft (SeaGuardian for the Navy, SkyGuardian for the Army and Air Force).
- MQ-9B is the maritime/"B" variant of the Predator family — high altitude, long endurance, satellite-controlled.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security (defence acquisition, maritime surveillance); cross-tag GS2 — IR (India–US defence ties).

## 3. PIB Backgrounder — 'Bharat Taxi': the cooperative answer to app-based aggregators

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Cooperation (PIB Research Unit backgrounder issued 17 August 2026).

**What it is.** Bharat Taxi is India's **first cooperative-sector ride-hailing platform**, registered under the **Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002** and incorporated on **6 June 2025**. It was launched by the Union Home and Cooperation Minister on **5 February 2026** at Vigyan Bhavan, New Delhi. Drivers — called **"Sarathis"** — are members-cum-owners rather than gig workers on a commission platform.

**Why it matters (GS3 Economy / GS2 Governance).** This is the sharpest live test of "Sahkar se Samriddhi": can the cooperative form compete in a platform economy? The design targets the two grievances of aggregator drivers — commissions and surge-driven opacity — by running a **zero-commission, no-surge** model with profits distributed to driver-members. For Mains, it is a ready example of cooperatives as an instrument of social security and of ownership-based (not just wage-based) inclusion.

**Key facts**

- Registered under the **MSCS Act, 2002**; incorporated **6 June 2025**; launched **5 February 2026**.
- **Zero-commission and surge-free pricing**; profits distributed directly to driver-members.
- Drivers are **Sarathis**; social-security package includes health insurance, accident insurance and retirement savings.
- **~8.27 lakh Sarathis registered** as of 28 July 2026; Delhi-NCR has the largest pool.
- Rolled out in Delhi and Gujarat, then **Maharashtra**.
- Promoted by **eight major cooperatives**: NCDC, Amul, IFFCO, KRIBHCO, NAFED, NDDB, NCEL and NABARD.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (cooperatives, gig/platform economy); cross-tag GS2 — Governance.

## 4. Home Ministry: ISI-backed 'Shahzad Bhatti network' dismantled — over 200 operatives arrested across 14 states

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Home Affairs (statement by the Union Home Minister, 17 August 2026).

**What it is.** The Government announced that coordinated operations across **14 states** ahead of the 80th Independence Day dismantled the **ISI-backed terror network run by Pakistani national Shahzad Bhatti**, with **over 200 operatives arrested** and planned attacks pre-empted.

**Why it matters (GS3 Internal Security).** The Bhatti case illustrates the current shape of the threat: a **handler operating remotely through social media and encrypted messaging**, radicalising and tasking locally recruited, previously unknown individuals — the "online handler + local module" model, rather than infiltration. Since 2025, **nine modules** traced to Bhatti had been busted (about four by the Uttar Pradesh ATS and five by the Delhi Police Special Cell). Note the counter-terror architecture in the answer: NIA, MAC/Subsidiary MACs, state ATSs, UAPA.

**Key facts**

- **200+ arrests**, operations in **14 states**, timed ahead of Independence Day 2026.
- **Nine modules** linked to Bhatti busted since 2025 (UP ATS and Delhi Police Special Cell).
- Recoveries reported include IEDs, **grenades bearing Pakistan Ordnance Factory markings**, pistols and live cartridges.
- Reported targets: temples, police stations, hospitals and high-profile individuals.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security (cross-border terrorism, online radicalisation, centre–state coordination).

## 5. DoPPW holds the 9th National Anubhav Awards and 60th Pre-Retirement Counselling Workshop; 'Pension Mitra' handbook released

**Ministry/body:** Department of Pension and Pensioners' Welfare (DoPPW), Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances and Pensions — Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi, 17 August 2026.

**What it is.** The **9th National Anubhav Awards** were conferred on **15 awardees** — retiring/retired central government employees who documented their contribution to nation-building. The same event hosted the **60th Pre-Retirement Counselling (PRC) Workshop** for Union government employees retiring in the NCR, and saw the release of the **Handbook on Pension Mitra** and the **guidelines for nationwide Digital Life Certificate (DLC) Campaign 5**.

**Why it matters (GS2 Governance).** Anubhav (started 2015) is an institutional-memory instrument: it converts the tacit knowledge of retiring bureaucrats into a written, citable record — relevant to any question on administrative reform, knowledge management or civil-services capacity building (compare Mission Karmayogi). The **DLC campaign** is the face-authentication-based life certificate push that removes the annual physical visit to a bank for pensioners — a strong "ease of living"/digital-governance example.

**Key facts**

- **9th National Anubhav Awards**; **15 awardees**; contributions cited include Passport Seva Kendra work, repeal of obsolete rules, technology adoption in administration and accounting, and energy conservation.
- **60th PRC Workshop** — for NCR-posted retiring Union government employees, on timely settlement of retirement benefits.
- Releases: **Handbook on Pension Mitra**; **DLC Campaign 5 guidelines**.
- **17 pension-disbursing banks** participated in an accompanying exhibition.
- Anubhav was instituted in **2015** as a platform for retiring central government employees to record their experience; awards have been conferred since 2016.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (civil services, e-governance, welfare delivery for pensioners).

## 6. 'Sahkar se Samriddhi' in Rajasthan: ₹6,453 crore of works launched at Alwar, new Saras dairy plant and four new district cooperative banks

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Cooperation / Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying (Union Home and Cooperation Minister at Alwar, Rajasthan, 16 August 2026), with the Rajasthan Government.

**What it is.** At Alwar, the Union Home and Cooperation Minister laid foundation stones and performed bhoomi pujan for **954 works costing about ₹6,453 crore** across **17 districts** of Rajasthan, including the foundation of a **new dairy plant at the Alwar Saras Dairy complex**, and announced **four new district central cooperative banks** and four new Saras dairy plants.

**Why it matters (GS3 Economy / Agriculture).** Dairy cooperatives are the strongest Indian example of producer-owned value addition — the Anand pattern (village society → district union → state federation). Pairing new **milk processing capacity** with new **district central cooperative banks** is the credit-plus-market model the Ministry of Cooperation is scaling through the "Sahkar se Samriddhi" agenda (along with 2 lakh new multipurpose PACS, computerisation of PACS, and NCEL/NCOL as the export and organic arms).

**Key facts**

- **954 works, about ₹6,453 crore**, spanning **17 districts** of Rajasthan.
- Foundation stone for a new dairy plant at **Alwar Saras Dairy**; four Saras dairy plants worth about **₹274 crore** becoming operational at **Bhilwara, Jaipur, Pali and Rajsamand**.
- Four new **district central cooperative banks** at **Phalodi, Balotra, Didwana-Kuchaman and Pratapgarh**.
- DBT transfers to welfare-scheme beneficiaries done remotely at the event; reported farmer support of about **₹667 crore to 66.74 lakh farmers** during the Rajasthan visit.
- Umbrella framing: **"Sahkar se Samriddhi"** (Ministry of Cooperation, created 2021).

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy / Agriculture (cooperatives, dairy value chain, rural credit).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to 'Bharat Taxi', consider the following statements:

1. It is registered under the Multi-State Cooperative Societies Act, 2002.
2. It operates on a zero-commission model with no surge pricing, and profits are distributed to driver-members.
3. It is promoted by cooperative institutions including NCDC, IFFCO, NAFED and NABARD.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The MQ-9B Sea Guardian, recently in the news, is best described as:

- (a) A ship-launched supersonic anti-ship cruise missile jointly developed with Russia
- (b) A High-Altitude Long-Endurance Remotely Piloted Aircraft System leased for maritime surveillance
- (c) An indigenous autonomous underwater vehicle developed by DRDO for mine detection
- (d) A satellite of the ocean-observation series launched by ISRO for maritime domain awareness

---

**Q3.** Consider the following statements regarding the Container Manufacturing Assistance Scheme (CMAS):

1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
2. It has an outlay of ₹10,000 crore and aims to expand India's domestic container manufacturing capacity.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 only
- (b) 2 only
- (c) Both 1 and 2
- (d) Neither 1 nor 2

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct. Bharat Taxi is registered under the MSCS Act, 2002 (incorporated 6 June 2025, launched 5 February 2026), runs a zero-commission and surge-free model with profits going to the driver-members ("Sarathis"), and is promoted by eight cooperatives — NCDC, Amul, IFFCO, KRIBHCO, NAFED, NDDB, NCEL and NABARD.

**Q2 — (b).** The MQ-9B Sea Guardian is a High-Altitude Long-Endurance (HALE) Remotely Piloted Aircraft System from General Atomics; the Ministry of Defence signed a contract on 17 August 2026 to lease two of them for the Indian Navy for 30 months (about ₹1,943 crore) to strengthen Maritime Domain Awareness.

**Q3 — (b) 2 only.** Statement 1 is wrong — CMAS is driven by the **Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways**, not Commerce & Industry. Statement 2 is correct: a ₹10,000 crore, five-year scheme targeting roughly 7.5 lakh TEUs of annual domestic capacity, rising towards 1 million TEUs a year over a decade.
