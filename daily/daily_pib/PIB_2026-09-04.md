## 1. PIB Backgrounder (Ministry of New & Renewable Energy): **Geothermal Energy — India's first geothermal wells and the National Policy on Geothermal Energy**

The PIB Research Unit released a full backgrounder on **geothermal energy** on 4 September 2026, pegged to India's first field-scale geothermal activity. Geothermal is a **round-the-clock, weather-independent, low-carbon** source — unlike solar and wind — which makes it strategically different from the rest of India's renewable portfolio.

**Why it matters:** This is the cleanest single-source explainer for a topic UPSC has been circling for years (Prelims has asked about hot springs, Puga Valley and geothermal provinces; Mains asks about firm/baseload renewables and the Net Zero 2070 pathway). It also links physical geography (Earth's interior, plate tectonics, radioactive decay) directly to an energy-policy question.

**Key facts**

- **National Policy on Geothermal Energy** notified in **September 2025**; **MNRE is the nodal ministry** for implementation.
- **July 2026:** the **ONGC Energy Centre** commissioned India's **first two geothermal wells** at **Puga Valley, Ladakh** — over **14,000 ft** above sea level, wells **1,000 m deep**, meant for reservoir evaluation.
- These pave the way for India's **first 1 MW demonstration geothermal power project**.
- **Geological Survey of India** has mapped **381 hot springs** and identified **10 geothermal provinces**; theoretical prospect **~10.6 GW**, with **42 sites** flagged as promising.
- The 10 provinces: **Himalayan, Naga-Lusai, Andaman & Nicobar, Son–Narmada–Tapi (SONATA), West Coast, Cambay Graben, Aravalli, Mahanadi, Godavari, South Indian Cratonic.**
- Heat source: **radioactive decay of uranium, thorium and potassium** plus residual heat of planetary formation; mantle–core boundary ~**3,700 °C**, core **5,000–6,000 °C**.
- Enabling tech: **Enhanced Geothermal Systems (EGS)** and **Advanced Geothermal Systems (AGS)**; **abandoned oil and gas wells can be repurposed** for geothermal.
- Live government-supported projects: **Ankleshwar** (retrofitting unproductive oil/gas wells), **Gandhinagar** (solar–geothermal hybrid; three wells, fluid at 70–80 °C, 50–90 kW demonstrated), **Tawang** (five sites under study), **Hyderabad** (geothermal cooling for air-conditioning), plus a shallow-geothermal borehole heat exchanger pilot. **Five MNRE R&D projects** sanctioned July–August 2025.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment & Energy (renewables, Net Zero 2070); GS1 — Geography (Earth's interior, geothermal provinces, plate tectonics).

## 2. Ministry of Defence: **Raksha Mantri releases 'RAKSHA'** — India's first 10-year defence diplomacy roadmap

On **3 September 2026** in New Delhi, Raksha Mantri **Rajnath Singh** released **'RAKSHA'**, a comprehensive document on **defence diplomacy** setting out India's strategic roadmap and vision for **global defence partnerships over the next 10 years**.

**Why it matters:** Defence diplomacy has until now been an implicit practice — exercises, ToT deals, ship visits — without a published framework. A stated 10-year roadmap is exactly the kind of document that anchors a GS2 (India and its neighbourhood / bilateral groupings) or GS3 (defence, indigenisation) answer. Note the framing: national security objectives explicitly fused with **proactive global engagement**, not just deterrence.

**The four pillars**

1. **Strategic partnerships** — deepening bilateral and multilateral security cooperation.
2. **Capacity building** — joint exercises, training programmes, sharing defence best practices with friendly foreign countries.
3. **Indigenous exports** — positioning India as a **credible global defence manufacturing hub** by promoting Made-in-India platforms.
4. **Maritime security** — consolidating India's role as **net security provider and first responder in the Indian Ocean Region**.

**Key facts**

- Released in the presence of **Chief of the Army Staff General Dhiraj Seth**, Defence Secretary **Rajesh Kumar Singh**, Secretary (Defence Production) **Sanjeev Kumar**, Secretary (East) MEA **Rudrendra Tandon**, Vice Chiefs of the Air Force and Navy, and the **Chief of Integrated Defence Staff to the Chairman COSC, Air Marshal Tejinder Singh**.
- Released the same day as the **India–Belgium** bilateral defence talks and Joint Statement, where both sides called for **joint development and joint production** in the defence industrial sector.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (defence diplomacy, bilateral agreements); GS3 — Internal Security / Defence (indigenisation, defence exports, IOR maritime security).

## 3. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: **National workshop on 'SUMAN Roadmap 2030 — Accelerating the Continuum of Care'**

MoHFW held a **two-day national workshop** at **Lucknow on 2–3 September 2026** to translate the **SUMAN Roadmap 2030** from vision into implementation, with the stated goal of driving **preventable maternal and newborn deaths to zero**.

**Why it matters:** SUMAN (*Surakshit Matritva Aashwasan*) is India's assured-maternity-care initiative under the National Health Mission. The 2030 roadmap is the sharpened, geographically targeted successor phase — and its design (a **life-cycle / continuum-of-care approach** concentrated on the worst-performing districts) is a textbook example of moving from universal to differentiated targeting in health governance.

**Key facts**

- Chaired by **Aradhana Patnaik**, Additional Secretary, MoHFW and **Mission Director, NHM**; inaugural session joined by **Uttar Pradesh Deputy Chief Minister Brajesh Pathak**.
- **Targets 13 States and 130 districts** with high maternal and newborn mortality for focused strategies.
- Adopts a **continuum-of-care / life-cycle approach**: pre-pregnancy care → antenatal → intrapartum → postnatal, with special focus on **socially, economically, physically or environmentally disadvantaged populations** and hard-to-reach areas.
- Launched at the workshop: **guidelines on IEC material for Birth Waiting Homes (BWH)** and a SUMAN Roadmap 2030 video.
- **200+ participants** — State/UT Mission Directors, technical experts, academic and professional bodies, development partners.
- Notable policy suggestion from the dais: **registration of newly married couples** as a tool to prevent unintended pregnancies and enable planned, healthy pregnancies.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance & Social Justice (health, issues relating to development and management of health services); GS1 — Society (women's health).

## 4. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: **Review of PM-JANMAN and DA-JGUA; 'Adi Darpan' released, ITDA roadmap issued**

On **3 September 2026**, Tribal Affairs Secretary **Ranjana Chopra** chaired a component-wise review of **PM-JANMAN** and **Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan (DA-JGUA)**, focused on fund utilisation, Utilisation Certificates and removing implementation bottlenecks — and announced a set of new instruments.

**Why it matters:** Tribal development questions in Mains almost always turn on **delivery machinery**, not scheme intent. This release hands you the machinery: ITDA operational guidelines, an evidence compendium, a DPR competition, and the Article 275(1) grant pipeline — all in one place.

**Key announcements**

- **ITDA/ITDP roadmap and operational guidelines** shared with States — a structured framework for planning, operating and monitoring **Integrated Tribal Development Agencies/Projects**, to strengthen decentralised planning and Centre–State convergence at district and block level.
- **'Adi Darpan' released** — a compendium of scheme-wise progress plus ground realities of tribal communities; intended as a quick-reference for evidence-based planning and transparency.
- **District Agriculture DPR Challenge winners announced** — **Wayanad and Palakkad (Kerala), Dima Hasao (Assam), Palghar (Maharashtra)** — for high-quality, implementable DPRs on agriculture and allied livelihoods (horticulture, water management, value-chain development) financed through DA-JGUA.
- **Rani Durgavati Tribal Women Conference** — an upcoming **National Conference on Tribal Women Entrepreneurs** on market linkage, credit access, skilling and mentorship.
- Special attention to **Utilisation Certificates and expenditure commitments** under DA-JGUA, PM-JANMAN and **Article 275(1)** grants.

### Scheme-in-focus: **PM-JANMAN and DA-JGUA**

| | **PM-JANMAN** | **DA-JGUA** |
|---|---|---|
| Full name | Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan | Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan |
| Ministry | Tribal Affairs (with line ministries) | Tribal Affairs (with line ministries) |
| Target group | **Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs)** | Tribal-majority villages and aspirational tribal blocks more broadly |
| Approach | Saturation of basic services in PVTG habitations | Saturation + livelihoods across tribal villages |
| Delivery unit | Habitation | Village / block, routed increasingly through **ITDAs** |

**Constitutional hook:** **Article 275(1)** — grants-in-aid from the Consolidated Fund of India to States for promoting the welfare of Scheduled Tribes and administration of Scheduled Areas.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (welfare schemes for vulnerable sections, mechanisms and performance); GS1 — Society (tribal communities).

## 5. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment: **NAMASTE Cards issued to waste pickers in Delhi's Najafgarh Zone** *(UPDATE)*

**What's new since 23 August:** the NAMASTE scheme appeared in this briefing on 23 August via the *Mission Zero* sanitation-fatalities workshop. Today's development is different and concrete — the **first issuance of NAMASTE Cards to waste pickers** (as distinct from sewer/septic tank workers) in an MCD zone, bundled with Ayushman card distribution.

On **4 September 2026**, MoS for Social Justice and Empowerment **Ramdas Athawale** inaugurated the issuance of **NAMASTE Cards** to waste pickers of the **MCD Najafgarh Zone** at the MCD Community Hall, Matiala, New Delhi. **Ayushman Cards** were distributed at the same event, extending health coverage to this workforce. **More than 300 waste pickers** participated.

**Why it matters:** The NAMASTE Card is the instrument of **formal recognition** — the step that converts an informal, invisible worker into an identified beneficiary who can be routed into health cover, social security and skilling. That identification-first sequencing is the substantive answer point, not the event itself.

**Key facts**

- **NAMASTE** = **National Action for Mechanised Sanitation Ecosystem**, a **Central Sector Scheme of the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment**.
- Coverage: sanitation workers **including sewer and septic tank workers and waste pickers**.
- Focus areas: occupational safety, **formal recognition** of workers, healthcare and social security access, skill development, and **mechanisation of hazardous sanitation work**.
- Stated aim: **eliminating hazardous manual cleaning practices** across the country.
- Parallel development the same week: **first batch of occupational safety training under NAMASTE launched for Durgapur Municipal Corporation sanitation workers, West Bengal.**

**Notebook:** GS2 — Social Justice (welfare of vulnerable sections, manual scavenging); GS1 — Society (caste, dignity of labour).

## 6. Ministry of Women & Child Development: **NCPCR's Pan-India Rescue and Rehabilitation Campaign frees over 3,800 children from child labour**

The **National Commission for Protection of Child Rights (NCPCR)** ran a special **"Pan-India Rescue and Rehabilitation" Campaign from 12 June to 31 August 2026**, coordinating with State/UT governments, District Magistrates, Labour Departments and police to intensify identification, rescue and rehabilitation of children in child labour and those vulnerable to trafficking.

**Why it matters:** A clean, quotable figure for any answer on child rights enforcement — and a good illustration of a **statutory commission acting as a coordinating and monitoring body** rather than a direct implementer. NCPCR is a statutory body under the **Commissions for Protection of Child Rights (CPCR) Act, 2005**.

**Key facts**

- **Over 3,800 children rescued**; **over 575 FIRs registered** during the campaign.
- Campaign window: **12 June – 31 August 2026**.
- Method: communications to authorities for targeted operations at **child labour and trafficking hotspots**; regular State/UT data returns on rescues, FIRs, rehabilitation, restoration, **educational mainstreaming** and compensation; district-administration review meetings on ATRs; social-media outreach.
- NCPCR is a **statutory body under the Ministry of Women and Child Development**.

**Notebook:** GS1 — Society (child labour, vulnerable sections); GS2 — Governance (statutory bodies, CPCR Act 2005).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**1.** With reference to **geothermal energy in India**, consider the following statements:

1. The Ministry of Earth Sciences is the nodal ministry for implementing the National Policy on Geothermal Energy.
2. India's first geothermal wells have been commissioned in the Puga Valley of Ladakh.
3. The Geological Survey of India has identified ten geothermal provinces in the country.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**2.** The document **'RAKSHA'**, released in September 2026, is best described as:

(a) A Ministry of External Affairs white paper on India's neighbourhood-first policy
(b) A defence diplomacy document setting out India's strategic roadmap for global defence partnerships over the next ten years
(c) A tri-service joint doctrine on the creation of integrated theatre commands
(d) A scheme for transfer of DRDO-developed technologies to the private defence industry

**3.** Consider the following statements regarding tribal development programmes:

1. Both PM-JANMAN and Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan (DA-JGUA) are anchored in the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.
2. PM-JANMAN specifically targets Particularly Vulnerable Tribal Groups (PVTGs).
3. Article 275(1) of the Constitution provides for grants-in-aid to States for promoting the welfare of Scheduled Tribes.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**1. (b)** — Statement 1 is **incorrect**: the **Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE)** is the nodal ministry for the **National Policy on Geothermal Energy**, notified in September 2025; the Ministry of Earth Sciences is not. Statement 2 is correct — the **ONGC Energy Centre** commissioned India's **first two geothermal wells** (1,000 m deep) at **Puga Valley, Ladakh**, at over 14,000 ft, in July 2026, paving the way for a **1 MW demonstration project**. Statement 3 is correct — the **GSI** has mapped **381 hot springs** and identified **10 geothermal provinces** (Himalayan, Naga-Lusai, Andaman & Nicobar, SONATA, West Coast, Cambay Graben, Aravalli, Mahanadi, Godavari, South Indian Cratonic), with a theoretical prospect of ~**10.6 GW**.

**2. (b)** — **'RAKSHA'** was released by Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh on **3 September 2026** as a **defence diplomacy document** outlining India's **10-year strategic roadmap** for global defence partnerships. Its four pillars are **strategic partnerships, capacity building, indigenous exports, and maritime security** (India as net security provider and first responder in the Indian Ocean Region). Option (d) describes the separate August 2026 approval for **ToT of DRDO missile systems** to Indian industry.

**3. (d)** — All three are correct. **PM-JANMAN** (Pradhan Mantri Janjati Adivasi Nyaya Maha Abhiyan) and **DA-JGUA** (Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan) are both anchored in the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs**, reviewed component-wise by the Ministry on 3 September 2026. PM-JANMAN is specifically directed at **PVTGs**. **Article 275(1)** provides grants-in-aid from the Consolidated Fund of India to States for **Scheduled Tribe welfare and administration of Scheduled Areas** — the review explicitly covered Utilisation Certificates under Article 275(1) alongside the two schemes.
