## 1. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare: push for **expansion of natural farming**, farmer incomes and sustainable farming systems

Union Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and Rural Development Minister **Shivraj Singh Chouhan** said on 1 September that **holistic, multi-dimensional efforts** are needed to expand natural farming, raise farmers' incomes and promote sustainable agricultural systems. The thrust: move the sector beyond production growth to **farmer prosperity** — through natural farming, integrated farming systems, judicious fertiliser use, crop diversification and better branding/marketing of farm produce.

**Why it matters:** Natural farming is the government's chosen route to cut input costs, restore soil health and reduce import dependence on chemical fertilisers — the "cost-side" answer to the farm-income problem, as opposed to MSP/price-support (the "price-side" answer). Expect this as a GS3 agriculture question on sustainability vs. yield trade-offs.

### Scheme-in-focus: **National Mission on Natural Farming (NMNF)**

|---|---|
| **Ministry** | Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (standalone **Centrally Sponsored Scheme**) |
| **Approved** | Union Cabinet, **25 November 2024**; formally launched thereafter |
| **Objective** | Promote chemical-free, agro-ecological farming in mission mode — soil health, biodiversity, climate resilience and safe food |

| **Coverage target** | **1 crore farmers**, **7.5 lakh hectares** under natural farming |
| **Predecessor** | **Bharatiya Prakritik Krishi Paddhati (BPKP)**, a sub-mission of **Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana (PKVY)** |

*Note: the NMNF figures above are scheme background (Cabinet approval documents), not fresh numbers from today's release.*

**Notebook:** GS3 — Agriculture (cropping patterns, sustainable agriculture, farm incomes).

## 2. Ministry of Railways: **₹170 crore approved for Kavach 4.0 on the remaining 712 Rkm of Moradabad Division**

Indian Railways has sanctioned **₹170 crore** to provide **Kavach Version 4.0** on the balance **712 route kilometres (Rkm)** of **Moradabad Division, Northern Railway** — completing the division's Automatic Train Protection cover.

**Key facts on Kavach:**

- **Indigenously developed Automatic Train Protection (ATP)** system (RDSO with Indian industry partners); a "Make in India" safety product.
- Functions: prevents **SPAD (Signal Passing at Danger)**, **automatically applies brakes** when the loco pilot fails to, enforces **speed control** in critical/restricted sections, and reduces collision risk.
- **Deployment status:** Kavach 4.0 commissioned on **2,633 Rkm as on 31 July 2026** — **1,423 Rkm on the Delhi–Mumbai corridor** and **1,210 Rkm on the Delhi–Howrah corridor**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Infrastructure (Railways) and Science & Technology (indigenous technology).

## 3. Ministry of Commerce & Industry: **DGFT enables automated issuance of Free Sale and Commerce Certificates (FSCC)**

The **Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)** has moved the **Free Sale and Commerce Certificate** to **automatic, system-based issuance** on the DGFT portal, removing mandatory manual scrutiny for eligible applications.

**Key facts:**

- Effected through **DGFT Trade Notice No. 24/2026-2027, dated 31 August 2026**.
- **What is an FSCC?** A certificate issued by DGFT under the **Foreign Trade Policy**, certifying that a product is **freely sold in India** — required by several importing countries. It covers products **not regulated under the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940** (drugs/cosmetics are certified by CDSCO instead).
- **Earlier process:** online application → routed to the concerned **Regional Authority** for manual verification and approval → "substantial processing time".
- **Now:** eligible applications are auto-issued, with **risk-based oversight** — the system can still flag auto-approved applications to the Regional Authority for post-facto review.

**Why it matters:** A textbook example of **trust-based, risk-management governance** replacing pre-approval scrutiny — the same design philosophy as faceless assessment and self-certification. Useful GS2 governance illustration and GS3 example of export facilitation / Ease of Doing Business.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (external sector, trade facilitation); GS2 — Governance (e-governance, regulatory reform).

## 4. Ministry of MSME: **India and Denmark deepen cooperation on MSME development, innovation and intellectual property**

The **4th meeting of the India–Denmark Joint Working Group** on MSMEs was held in **Copenhagen**, with the **Ministry of MSME** and the **Danish Patent and Trademark Office (DKPTO)** as the lead counterparts.

**Key facts:**

- Areas covered: **technology adoption, innovation, intellectual property, technology commercialisation, entrepreneurship and cluster-based MSME development**.
- The Indian delegation engaged with **Aarhus University's Innovation "Kitchen" and Agro Food Park** and the wider **Nordic startup ecosystem** — studying startup mentoring, fundraising and industry–academia linkages; India shared its **cluster-based MSME development** experience.
- **Framework:** the **India–Denmark Green Strategic Partnership (2020)**, which covers trade, investment, innovation and SME cooperation.
- **Bilateral trade (2025):** goods **USD 2.05 billion**; services **USD 4.25 billion**.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (bilateral partnerships); GS3 — Economy (MSMEs, IPR).

## 5. Ministry of Communications (DoT): **caution against IMEI tampering and misuse of telecommunication identifiers**

The **Department of Telecommunications** has issued a public advisory warning against **altering mobile IMEI numbers** or using devices with **tampered/unauthorised telecommunication identifiers**.

**Key facts:**

- **Legal basis:** the **Telecommunications Act, 2023**. **Section 42(3)(c)** prohibits tampering with telecommunication identifiers; **Section 42(3)(e)** bars obtaining SIM cards or other identifiers through **fraud, cheating or impersonation**.
- **Penalty:** up to **3 years imprisonment**, fine up to **₹50 lakh**, or both.
- The advisory also warns against apps/websites used to **alter Calling Line Identity (CLI)** — the basis of most spoofed-call and "digital arrest" frauds.
- **IMEI** (International Mobile Equipment Identity) can be verified on the **Sanchar Saathi** portal/app, which also supports blocking of lost/stolen handsets.

**Why it matters:** Ties directly to the Telecommunications Act, 2023 (a high-probability Prelims Act) and to cyber-fraud/internal-security questions on telecom-enabled crime.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security (cyber security, telecom fraud); GS2 — Polity (new legislation).

## 6. Department of Consumer Affairs: **sugar stock limit for dealers halved to 2,000 quintals from 15 September** *(UPDATE)*

**What's new since the 22 August briefing:** the earlier stock-limit regime has been **tightened by 50%** — the dealer limit falls from **4,000 to 2,000 quintals** for the festive window.

**Key facts:**

- Effective **15 September to 30 November 2026** (the festive-demand period).
- Dealers may not hold more than **2,000 quintals** at any time or at any location, and may not retain sugar for **more than 30 days** from the date of receipt.
- **Exception:** the **4,000-quintal** limit is retained for **Kolkata and its extended metropolitan area**, citing that region's supply pattern.
- Backed by **intensified monitoring and physical verification** of stocks at mills, dealers and traders.

**Why it matters:** A live application of the **Essential Commodities Act, 1955** stock-limit route to curb hoarding and speculative trading — the standard supply-side price-management toolkit alongside buffer releases (compare the ongoing onion buffer/"Kanda Express" operation).

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (food management, price stabilisation, inflation control).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to **'Kavach'**, recently seen in the news, consider the following statements:

1. It is an indigenously developed Automatic Train Protection (ATP) system.
2. It can automatically apply brakes to prevent Signal Passing at Danger (SPAD).
3. It is deployed by the Ministry of Road Transport and Highways on national highways.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** The **'Free Sale and Commerce Certificate'**, whose issuance was recently automated, is issued by which of the following?

(a) Bureau of Indian Standards (BIS)
(b) Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO)
(c) Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT)
(d) Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI)

---

**Q3.** Tampering with **telecommunication identifiers** such as the IMEI number of a mobile handset is an offence under which one of the following?

(a) Information Technology Act, 2000
(b) Indian Telegraph Act, 1885
(c) Telecommunications Act, 2023
(d) Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Kavach is an indigenously developed **Automatic Train Protection** system for **Indian Railways** (Ministry of Railways, developed with RDSO), preventing SPAD, applying automatic braking and enforcing speed limits. Statement 3 is wrong — it has nothing to do with highways. Kavach 4.0 stood commissioned on **2,633 Rkm as on 31 July 2026**.

**Q2 — (c) DGFT.** The Free Sale and Commerce Certificate is issued by the **Directorate General of Foreign Trade** under the Foreign Trade Policy, for products **not covered by the Drugs & Cosmetics Act, 1940**. DGFT Trade Notice No. 24/2026-2027 (31 August 2026) enabled automatic, system-based issuance with risk-based post-issue oversight.

**Q3 — (c) Telecommunications Act, 2023.** **Section 42(3)(c)** prohibits tampering with telecommunication identifiers and **Section 42(3)(e)** bars obtaining SIMs/identifiers by fraud or impersonation; punishment is up to **3 years' imprisonment and/or a fine up to ₹50 lakh**. IMEI details can be verified on the **Sanchar Saathi** platform.
