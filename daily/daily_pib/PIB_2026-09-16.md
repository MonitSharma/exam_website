## 1. Cabinet / Ministry of Labour & Employment: **EPFO wage ceiling for mandatory coverage raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month**

**What it is.** The Union Cabinet, chaired by the Prime Minister, approved the Ministry of Labour & Employment's proposal to raise the **wage ceiling for mandatory coverage under the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO)** from **₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month**. More than **51 lakh additional employees** are expected to come under mandatory EPFO coverage.

**Why it matters.** This is the single biggest social-security expansion decision of the year and the **first revision since September 2014**. It directly addresses a structural gap: a fresh employee joining at a wage above ₹15,000 is not automatically covered, and therefore falls outside statutory provident fund, pension and insurance protection. Raising the threshold pulls the entire ₹15,000–₹25,000 wage band into the statutory net — a formalisation measure as much as a welfare one. For GS2 (social-sector schemes, vulnerable sections) and GS3 (employment, formalisation), this is prime material.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Old ceiling:** ₹15,000/month, fixed in **September 2014**; unchanged 2004–2014 before that.
- **New ceiling:** ₹25,000/month. **Additional coverage:** 51 lakh+ employees.
- Benefits extended: **EPF** (provident fund), **EPS** (Employees' Pension Scheme), **EDLI** (Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme).
- **Fiscal cost:** annual Government outgo estimated at about **₹11,339 crore**, against existing annual budgetary support of about **₹10,250 crore**; **five-year cost approximately ₹56,696 crore**.
- Recommended by the **Expenditure Finance Committee** on **16 June 2026**, after inter-ministerial consultations.
- **EPFO scale today:** around **7.98 crore contributing members**, about **7.68 lakh contributing establishments**; EPS pays pension to around **82 lakh pensioners**.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (social security architecture) + GS3 Economy (employment, formalisation).

## 2. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **India's Net Zero Portal and NAPCC Dashboard launched on World Ozone Day 2026**

**What it is.** MoS for Environment, Forest and Climate Change **Shri Kirti Vardhan Singh** launched two digital platforms at the **World Ozone Day 2026** observance in **Mumbai**: the **Net Zero Portal** (voluntary Net Zero registration and disclosure) and the **NAPCC Dashboard** (consolidated monitoring of the National Missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change).

**Why it matters.** India's climate architecture has long been criticised for weak, scattered monitoring. The NAPCC Dashboard is an attempt to put all National Missions on one indicator platform for inter-ministerial review — useful for answers on "institutional gaps in India's climate governance". The Net Zero Portal, meanwhile, creates a **public repository of voluntary corporate climate commitments**, which sits alongside (not inside) the compliance-based Carbon Credit Trading Scheme.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Net Zero Portal** — `netzeroindia.moef.gov.in`. Entities voluntarily register, disclose GHG emissions, declare Net Zero targets and file **annual progress updates**.
- **Scope 1 and Scope 2** emissions reporting is **encouraged**; **Scope 3 remains voluntary**.
- Entities can outline pathways covering clean energy transition, energy efficiency, technological interventions and **carbon removals**; those declaring targets get an **acknowledgement from MoEFCC**.
- **NAPCC Dashboard** — `napccindia.moef.gov.in`. Consolidates mission-wise progress and indicators; intended to link domestic climate action to **India's international climate commitments (NDCs)**.
- Occasion: **World Ozone Day**, observed on **16 September**, marking the **Montreal Protocol (1987)**.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (climate governance, NAPCC, net-zero).

## 3. Ministry of MSME: **PM Vishwakarma completes three years — 30 lakh artisans registered; registration target achieved**

**What it is.** The Ministry of MSME announced that the **PM Vishwakarma Scheme**, launched on **17 September 2023**, has completed three years with **30 lakh beneficiaries registered** after the prescribed three-stage verification — **achieving the scheme's registration target**. The third anniversary is being celebrated at the **Dhono Dhanyo Auditorium, Kolkata, on 17 September 2026**.

**Why it matters.** PM Vishwakarma is the flagship intervention for the **informal, tool-based artisan economy** — a segment historically outside both MUDRA-type credit and formal skilling. Hitting the full registration target in three years makes it a usable example for questions on **traditional livelihoods, informal-sector credit and skill formalisation**. Note also the West Bengal angle: the State onboarded only in **May 2026**, a good illustration of the Centre–State implementation lag in centrally sponsored schemes.

### Scheme-in-focus: **PM Vishwakarma**

- **Ministry:** Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises. **Launched:** 17 September 2023.
- **Objective:** end-to-end support to traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in **18 identified trades**, strengthening traditional skills and entrepreneurial capability.
- **Key features (4):** (i) **recognition** through PM Vishwakarma certificate and ID card; (ii) **skill upgradation** (basic and advanced training) plus **toolkit incentive**; (iii) **collateral-free credit** up to ₹1 lakh (first tranche) at a **concessional 5% interest rate**; (iv) **incentives for digital transactions** and **marketing support**.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** **30 lakh registered**; **~24.50 lakh** given Basic Skill Training by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship; **6.18 lakh** given loans worth **₹5,297 crore**; **18.15 lakh modern toolkits** delivered to doorsteps through **India Post**.
- **West Bengal:** onboarded **May 2026**; **3,000 beneficiaries** registered so far.
- **Delivery partners:** DC-MSME, KVIC, NSIC, SLBC, India Post, Common Service Centres / Village Level Entrepreneurs, Gram Panchayats and ULBs.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (MSME, informal sector, skill development).

## 4. Ministry of Jal Shakti: **'Swachh Seva Aankalan' launched — Gram Panchayat-level digital self-assessment for Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen)**

**What it is.** The **Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation** launched **Swachh Seva Aankalan (SSA)**, an **annual, Gram Panchayat-level digital self-assessment framework** under **SBM (Grameen) 2.0** for assessing rural sanitation service delivery. Union Jal Shakti Minister **Shri C R Patil** launched it on **15 September 2026** in a virtual meeting with States/UTs, attended by MoS **Shri V. Somanna**.

**Why it matters.** This is a **monitoring instrument, not a new scheme** — and that is exactly why it is exam-relevant. It shifts rural sanitation assessment from top-down inspection to **self-assessment validated by the Gram Sabha**, explicitly invoking the **73rd Constitutional Amendment Act**. It is a clean example for GS2 answers on **local-government empowerment, social audit and accountability in scheme delivery**.

### Scheme-in-focus: **Swachh Seva Aankalan (SSA)**

- **Ministry/Department:** Jal Shakti — Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation, under **SBM(G) 2.0**.
- **Objective:** annual assessment and monitoring of rural sanitation service delivery at the Gram Panchayat level.
- **Key features (4):** (i) **digital self-assessment** by **ODF Plus (Model)** declared Gram Panchayats along with their **Village Water & Sanitation Committees (VWSC)** and local communities; (ii) parameters covered — **ODF sustainability, visual cleanliness, and Solid and Liquid Waste Management (SLWM)**, plus **functionality and O&M status** of sanitation and community SLWM assets; (iii) findings **discussed and validated by the Gram Sabha**; (iv) validated results flow to District and State/UT authorities for review in **DWSM / SWSM** meetings, which then prepare improvement plans.
- **Institutional chain:** Gram Panchayat → Gram Sabha → VWSC → District (DWSM) → State/UT (SWSM).
- **Significance:** operationalises the spirit of the **73rd Amendment** by making the Gram Sabha the validating authority for sanitation performance.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (local government, transparency and accountability, social audit).

## 5. NITI Aayog: **Second edition of the India Electric Mobility Index (IEMI) released; 29 of 36 States/UTs now have notified EV policies**

**What it is.** NITI Aayog released the **second edition of the India Electric Mobility Index (IEMI)** at its **Workshop on State EV Policies**. Member-Secretary **Shri Rajiv Gauba** described electric mobility as **"an economic, environmental, and strategic imperative"** for Viksit Bharat 2047.

**Why it matters.** The IEMI is a **State-ranking index** — a category UPSC consistently mines for prelims. Equally important is the framing: Gauba linked EV adoption directly to **energy security**, citing India's ~89% crude import dependence and invoking the **West Asia crisis** as a possible inflection point analogous to the 1970s oil shocks. That argument is directly usable in GS3 answers on energy security and the energy transition.

**Key facts and figures**

- **State coverage:** **29 of India's 36 States and UTs** have now notified EV policies.
- **IEMI scores:** highest State score rose from **77 to 84**; **median score from 36 to 40** over the past year.
- **Import dependence:** India imports nearly **89% of its crude oil** requirement.
- **Automotive sector:** ~**7.1% of GDP**; supports nearly **1.9 crore jobs**.
- **Government support:** over **₹92,000 crore** across **FAME**, **PM E-DRIVE**, **PM e-Bus Sewa**, and two PLI schemes — **Automobiles & Auto Components** and **Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC) battery storage**.
- **Global benchmark (2025):** electric cars were ~**one-tenth** of new car sales in the US, **more than a quarter** in the EU, **over half** in China.
- Charging ecosystem: newly launched **Unified Bharat e-Charge app** to improve accessibility of charging infrastructure.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy / Environment (energy security, EV transition) + Reports & Indices.

## 6. Ministry of Finance (CBIC): **Ten years of India's Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) Programme — "AEO@10"**

**What it is.** The **Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC)** marked a decade of India's **Authorised Economic Operator (AEO) Programme** with an event titled **"AEO@10: Accelerating Growth, Enhancing Trust, Optimizing Trade"** in New Delhi. CBIC Chairman **Shri Vivek Chaturvedi** set out the roadmap for the next decade.

**Why it matters.** AEO is India's principal **trade-facilitation and trusted-trader** instrument, aligned with the **World Customs Organisation's SAFE Framework of Standards**. The shift it represents — from a **transaction-centric** customs administration to a **risk-based, trust-oriented** one — is a standard GS3 Economy theme (ease of doing business, trade facilitation, WTO TFA obligations).

**Key facts and figures**

- **Framework:** aligned with the **WCO SAFE Framework of Standards**; current AEO framework introduced through **Circular No. 33/2016-Customs**.
- **Five "big ideas" for the next decade** announced by the CBIC Chairman: technology-driven facilitation; **radical inclusivity for MSMEs**; seamless inter-agency coordination; global leadership through expanded **Mutual Recognition Arrangements (MRAs)**; synergy across indirect tax verticals.
- **Eligible Manufacturer Importer (EMI) Scheme** — flagged in the Union Finance Minister's Budget speech — positioned as a stepping stone rewarding early compliance and creating a pathway to higher AEO tiers. Documentation under EMI rationalised via **Circular No. 39/2026-Customs (3 September 2026)**, cutting uploads from **ten documents to three**, effective **15 September 2026**.
- Process reforms under consideration, in line with the **National Trade Facilitation Action Plan**: unified SOP across zones, applicant checklist, **single-deficiency approach**, time-bound processing, clarified financial-solvency position, dedicated **Client Relationship Managers** and trained validators.
- Publication released: **"A Decade of AEO in India: Celebrating Trusted Trade Partnerships"**; a structured stakeholder **feedback response form** launched on the AEO portal.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (trade facilitation, customs reform, WCO/WTO linkages).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the Union Cabinet's decision of September 2026 on the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), consider the following statements:

1. The wage ceiling for mandatory coverage under EPFO has been raised from ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month.
2. The wage ceiling being revised had last been enhanced in September 2014.
3. The revision extends coverage under the Employees' Pension Scheme and the Employees' Deposit Linked Insurance Scheme in addition to the provident fund.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** Consider the following statements regarding the PM Vishwakarma Scheme:

1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.
2. It provides credit support of up to ₹1 lakh at a concessional interest rate of 5% per annum.
3. It covers traditional artisans and craftspeople engaged in 18 identified trades.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 2 and 3 only
- (c) 1 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** 'Swachh Seva Aankalan', recently seen in the news, is:

- (a) A ranking of urban local bodies on solid waste management under the Swachh Survekshan framework
- (b) An annual Gram Panchayat-level digital self-assessment framework for rural sanitation service delivery under Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen)
- (c) A certification scheme for sanitation workers under the NAMASTE scheme
- (d) A third-party audit of faecal sludge treatment plants conducted by the Central Pollution Control Board

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct. The Cabinet raised the EPFO mandatory-coverage wage ceiling from **₹15,000 to ₹25,000 per month**, bringing in **51 lakh+ additional employees**. The ceiling was last enhanced in **September 2014** (it had stayed unchanged from 2004 to 2014). The revision expands access to **EPF, EPS and EDLI** benefits. Annual Government outgo is estimated at about **₹11,339 crore**, with a five-year cost of about ₹56,696 crore.

**Q2 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** Statement 1 is the trap: PM Vishwakarma is implemented by the **Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises**, not the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship — though MSDE does deliver the **Basic Skill Training** component (~24.50 lakh trained). Statement 2 is correct: **6.18 lakh** beneficiaries have received loans of up to ₹1 lakh at **5% per annum**, totalling ₹5,297 crore. Statement 3 is correct: the scheme covers **18 identified trades** and was launched on **17 September 2023**; 30 lakh beneficiaries are now registered.

**Q3 — (b).** Swachh Seva Aankalan is an **annual, Gram Panchayat-level digital self-assessment framework** launched by the **Department of Drinking Water and Sanitation** under **SBM (Grameen) 2.0**. **ODF Plus (Model)** Gram Panchayats, with their **VWSCs**, self-assess ODF sustainability, visual cleanliness and SLWM; findings are **validated by the Gram Sabha** and reviewed in **DWSM/SWSM** meetings. Option (a) describes Swachh Survekshan (urban), and option (c) misstates NAMASTE, which is a Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment scheme for sanitation workers.
