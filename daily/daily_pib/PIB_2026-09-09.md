## 1. Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (Ministry of Railways): **Three multitracking projects worth ₹10,783 crore approved — about 656 km added to the network**

**What it is.** The CCEA, chaired by the Prime Minister, approved three Railway capacity-augmentation projects on 9 September 2026 at a total estimated cost of **₹10,783 crore**, targeted for completion by **2029-30**:

- **Kharagpur–Jharsuguda (Bagdehi) 4th line**
- **Katni–Pendra Road 4th line**
- **Bilaspur (Uslapur)–Pendra Road 3rd line**

**Why it matters.** These are the loaded coal/cement/iron-and-steel corridors of the eastern-central mineral belt. Line capacity here is the binding constraint on freight throughput, so the approval is a direct read on the logistics-cost reduction agenda.

**Key facts and figures.**

- Covers **14 districts** across **West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh**.
- Adds roughly **656 km** to the existing Indian Railways network.
- Improves connectivity for about **4,790 villages** with a population of nearly **56 lakh**.
- Additional freight capacity of about **27 million tonnes per annum (MTPA)**.
- Estimated savings: **~12 crore litres of oil imports** and **~62 crore kg of CO₂** avoided — equivalent to planting about **2.48 crore trees**.
- Prepared under the **PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan**, whose stated purpose is multimodal connectivity and logistics efficiency through integrated planning and stakeholder consultation.
- Improves rail access to Taratarini Temple, Bandhavgarh Tiger Reserve, Amarkantak, Achanakmar Tiger Reserve, Malhar archaeological site and Ratanpur Mahamaya Temple, among others.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Infrastructure, Railways, Logistics)

## 2. Ministry of Earth Sciences: **ORV *Sagar Manthan* launched — India's first advanced ocean research vessel built domestically**

**What it is.** Yard 3041, officially named **ORV *Sagar Manthan***, was launched on **9 September 2026** at **Rishi Bankim Shipyard, Kolkata**. It was designed, engineered and built by the defence PSU **Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE)** for the **National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research (NCPOR)** under the Ministry of Earth Sciences. Union Minister **Dr. Jitendra Singh** launched it virtually; Mrs. Manju Singh performed the naming.

**Why it matters.** It marks the policy shift from *acquiring* research platforms abroad to *building* them at home. It also underpins India's ambitions in deep-sea critical minerals and in ocean-climate science — the data-generating platform that any claim to a role in "ocean geopolitics" ultimately rests on.

**Key facts and figures.**

- Built at a cost of about **₹840 crore** under **Vertical-4 (Deep Ocean Survey and Exploration)** of the **Deep Ocean Mission**.
- All-weather, multidisciplinary platform designed for extreme marine environments **including the Southern Ocean**; projected service life of **30 years**.
- Launched within **two years of contract signing** and just **five months after keel-laying**.
- Will progressively replace the **43-year-old ORV *Sagar Kanya***, which was **acquired from Germany in 1983**.
- Equipment: dynamic positioning, **multibeam bathymetry**, multichannel seismic system, sub-bottom profiling, weather radar, drop keels, and deployment facilities for **ROVs and AUVs**.
- Research scope: multidisciplinary exploration along the **mid-ocean ridge of the Indian Ocean** to identify **polymetallic hydrothermal sulphide mineralisation**, establish environmental baselines, collect upper-air data and conduct CTD profiling.
- MoES Secretary **Dr. T. Srinivasa Kumar** noted India is now among the few countries able to design and build such platforms; the Ministry plans more oceanographic vessels and a **polar research vessel**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (Ocean science, Blue Economy); GS1 links to oceanography

## 3. Ministry of Finance (FIU-IND): **Notices to 15 Virtual Digital Asset Service Providers under Section 13 of PMLA; URLs and apps ordered blocked**

**What it is.** The Director of the **Financial Intelligence Unit–India (FIU-IND)** issued non-compliance notices on 9 September 2026 to **fifteen Virtual Digital Asset Service Providers (VDA SPs)** under **Section 13 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002**. Entities named include **WhiteBIT, ChangeNOW, SimpleSwap, FixedFloat, DigiFinex, BloFin, Bitunix, LATOKEN, WOO X, XT.com, Toobit, Weex, Rezorex, Pionex and Guardarian**.

**Why it matters.** This is the recurring template of India's crypto regulation: not a ban, but AML/CFT obligations enforced on an **activity basis** — offshore entities serving Indian users are covered even without a physical presence in India. The parallel use of intermediary rules to block access is the enforcement teeth.

**Key facts and figures.**

- Acting as **Nodal Officer**, the FIU-IND Director also issued notices under **Section 79(3)(b) of the Information Technology Act, 2000** and **Rule 3(1)(d) of the IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2025**, to remove the apps/URLs from public access in India.
- **VDA SPs were brought under the AML/CFT framework of PMLA in March 2023.**
- Covered activities: exchange between VDAs and fiat currencies, transfer of VDAs, safekeeping or administration of VDAs, and control over instruments enabling VDAs.
- Obligations include **registration with FIU-IND as a Reporting Entity**, plus reporting and record-keeping duties. These are **activity-based and do not depend on physical presence in India**.
- The release repeats the standing public caution: **crypto products and NFTs are unregulated**, can be highly risky, and regulatory remedies may not be available for losses.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Financial regulation) and Internal Security (money laundering); GS2 links to regulatory bodies

## 4. Ministry of Labour & Employment: **National Building and Other Construction Workers (BOCW) Conference, Mumbai, 11–12 September 2026**

**What it is.** The Ministry, with the Government of Maharashtra, will hold a **National BOCW Conference** in Mumbai on **11–12 September 2026**, chaired by Union Minister **Dr. Mansukh Mandaviya**, with MoS **Ms. Shobha Karandlaje**. Participants: State/UT Labour Ministers and senior officials, and **Chairpersons and Secretaries of BOCW Welfare Boards**.

**Why it matters.** Construction is among the largest employers in India and the archetype of informal-sector welfare delivery. With the **new Labour Codes now in force**, the conference is the stocktaking forum for whether cess-funded welfare boards actually reach registered workers — the classic gap between statutory entitlement and delivery.

**Key facts and figures.**

- Agenda: statutory responsibilities, **worker registration**, availability of welfare services, **monitoring and utilisation of welfare funds**, convergence, and technology-driven service delivery.
- Reviews progress on three initiatives launched at the **National Labour Conference (New Delhi, November 2025)**:
  1. **Digital Labour Chowk**
  2. **Labour Chowk-cum-Facilitation Centres**
  3. **Online BOCW Cess Collection Portal**
- Also covers **international labour mobility pathways**, skilling and employment opportunities, and **labour statistics**.
- Explicit aim: sharing innovative practices across State BOCW Welfare Boards for replication.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance / Social Justice (welfare schemes for vulnerable sections); GS3 — Economy (informal labour)

## 5. Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship: **India to compete in 11 new skills for the first time at WorldSkills Shanghai 2026**

**What it is.** India will field its largest-ever contingent at the **48th WorldSkills Competition**, to be held in **Shanghai, China from 22 to 27 September 2026**. Team Skill India will compete in **63 of 64 skills** with a contingent of about **70 competitors**.

**Why it matters.** WorldSkills performance is one of the few externally benchmarked measures of a country's vocational training ecosystem. India's entry into aviation maintenance, drones and optoelectronics signals that the skilling system is being pushed beyond traditional trades.

**Key facts and figures.**

- **Eleven new skills** for India: Dental Prosthetics, Digital Interactive Media Design, Intelligent Security Technology, Landscape Gardening, Optoelectronic Technology, Retail Sales, Unmanned Aerial Systems, Industrial Mechanics, Software Testing, Heavy Vehicle Technology and **Aircraft Maintenance**.
- Led by **MSDE**, with the **National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC)** coordinating industry partners, training institutes and experts.
- Trajectory: **29th in 2015 → 13th in 2024**; **8th in Asia at WorldSkills Asia 2025**; **five medals at the Taipei Capital Cup 2026**.
- Preparation partners include GTTC Karnataka, NAMTECH Gandhinagar, Bennett University, Croma, Titan–Tanishq, Shoppers Stop, Reliance Trends, SANY Heavy Industry India and Prakhar Software Solutions.
- MSDE Secretary **Ms. Debashree Mukherjee** framed the expansion as movement toward "future-ready and globally relevant" capabilities.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (Education/Skilling); GS3 — Economy (employment, human capital)

## 6. Ministry of Cooperation: **CRCS–Sahara Refund Portal restored; ₹9,267 crore refunded to 41.56 lakh depositors**

**What it is.** The Ministry of Cooperation announced on **8 September 2026** that the **CRCS–Sahara Refund Portal** has been restored and processing of depositor claims has resumed. The refund mechanism operates under the **Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies (CRCS)** pursuant to the **Supreme Court order dated 29 March 2023 in W.P. No. 191/2022**.

**Why it matters.** A rare, concrete case study of depositor protection in the **multi-state cooperative** space — useful for GS2 questions on the Ministry of Cooperation's mandate and for GS3 on financial-sector consumer protection. Note the constitutional angle: cooperation is a State subject, but *multi-state* cooperative societies fall under the Centre via CRCS.

**Key facts and figures.**

- **₹9,267 crore** refunded directly into **Aadhaar-seeded bank accounts** of **41,56,711 depositors** so far.
- About **20 lakh applications** remain pending; verification has restarted.
- Portal launched on **18 July 2023**; process is **digital, paperless and verification-based**, using **Aadhaar e-KYC** matched against society-supplied data.
- Covers four Sahara group societies: **Sahara Credit Cooperative Society Ltd (Lucknow)**, **Saharayn Universal Multipurpose Society Ltd (Bhopal)**, **Hamara India Credit Cooperative Society Ltd (Kolkata)** and **Stars Multipurpose Cooperative Society Ltd (Hyderabad)**.
- Operations had been disrupted from **November 2025** after premises were sealed in matters unrelated to the societies, compounded by IT infrastructure, AMC and administrative bottlenecks; CRCS coordinated with Uttar Pradesh authorities to resolve them.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (Ministry of Cooperation, statutory regulators); GS3 — Economy (financial inclusion, depositor protection)

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to **ORV *Sagar Manthan***, consider the following statements:

1. It has been built by Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers for the National Centre for Polar and Ocean Research under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.
2. It has been constructed under Vertical-4 (Deep Ocean Survey and Exploration) of the Deep Ocean Mission.
3. It is India's first nuclear-powered ocean research vessel.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** With reference to the regulation of **Virtual Digital Asset Service Providers (VDA SPs)** in India, consider the following statements:

1. The Financial Intelligence Unit–India (FIU-IND) functions under the Ministry of Finance.
2. VDA service providers were brought within the anti-money laundering framework of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, 2002 in March 2023.
3. Obligations under the PMLA apply only to those VDA service providers that have a physical presence in India.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** With reference to the three railway multitracking projects approved by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs in September 2026, consider the following statements:

1. They cover districts in West Bengal, Jharkhand, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh and Chhattisgarh.
2. They have been prepared under the PM Gati Shakti National Master Plan.
3. They will add about 656 km to the existing network of Indian Railways.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** ORV *Sagar Manthan* was designed and built by GRSE (a defence PSU) for NCPOR under MoES, at about ₹840 crore under Vertical-4 of the Deep Ocean Mission. Statement 3 is wrong — it is a conventionally powered all-weather research vessel, not nuclear-powered; it will replace the 43-year-old ORV *Sagar Kanya*, acquired from Germany in 1983.

**Q2 — (b) 1 and 2 only.** FIU-IND is under the Ministry of Finance and issued Section 13 PMLA notices to 15 VDA SPs. VDA SPs were brought under the PMLA AML/CFT framework in March 2023. Statement 3 is wrong — the obligations are **activity-based** and apply to offshore as well as onshore entities serving Indian users, irrespective of physical presence.

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct. The three projects (Kharagpur–Jharsuguda 4th line, Katni–Pendra Road 4th line, Bilaspur–Pendra Road 3rd line) cost ₹10,783 crore, span 14 districts across those five States, are framed under PM Gati Shakti NMP, and add roughly 656 km, giving about 27 MTPA of extra freight capacity.
