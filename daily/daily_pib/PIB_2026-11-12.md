## 1. Union Cabinet (Dept of Fertilizers): **Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) rates for Rabi 2026-27 approved — ~₹62,370 crore** (11 Nov 2026) — GS3 Agriculture / Economy
- **What:** The Cabinet approved NBS rates on Phosphatic & Potassic (P&K) fertilisers for the Rabi season, applicable **1 Oct 2026 – 31 Mar 2027**. Briefed by Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw.
- **Why it matters:** Budgetary requirement of about **₹62,370.02 crore** is roughly **₹24,418 crore (~64%) higher** than Rabi 2025-26 (₹37,952.29 crore) and well above Kharif 2026 (~₹41,534 crore), reflecting high global prices of DAP, MOP, sulphur and inputs. The Government says farmers will not bear extra burden and domestic manufacturing will rise.
- **Key facts:** NBS in force since **1 April 2010**; **28 P&K grades** subsidised; urea is outside NBS (statutory MRP, separate subsidy).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture (inputs, subsidies), Economy (fiscal burden of subsidies).
- *Ministry note: sources did not name the ministry; NBS is administered by the Department of Fertilizers, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers (static knowledge).*

## 2. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare: **First BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting, Colombo** (10–11 Nov 2026) — GS2 International Relations / Health
- **What:** Union Minister J.P. Nadda represented India. Members adopted a **Joint Ministerial Statement** and the **Revised BIMSTEC Plan of Action on Public Health (2026–2030)** covering surveillance, pandemic preparedness, digital health, traditional medicine, One Health and health workforce.
- **Key facts:** India to host the **BIMSTEC Centre of Excellence in Traditional Medicine at ITRA, Jamnagar** and the next Task Force on Traditional Medicine meeting; a **BIMSTEC Digital Health Framework** (telemedicine, interoperable records, AI diagnostics) to be explored; ministers to meet **every two years**, Thailand to host the second in **2028**. Meeting follows the 6th BIMSTEC Summit (Bangkok, April 2025). India–Sri Lanka: MoU for **100 ambulances** (~US$5.28 m grant) and support for **50 health kiosks** under the post-Cyclone Ditwah rehabilitation package.
- **Members:** Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (neighbourhood-first, BIMSTEC), GS2 Health.

## 3. Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (NCAHP): **Draft rules on provisional registration of allied & healthcare professionals** (released ~9–11 Nov 2026; comments reportedly till 4 Dec — one source) — GS2 Governance / Health
- **What:** The National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions has issued draft provisions to bring practitioners **without recognised qualifications** into the regulated system.
- **Key features:** (1) **Provisional registration** with the State Allied & Healthcare Council (NCAHP directly where no council exists), valid **3 years, extendable by 1**; (2) **bridge courses** of 6–12 months with supervised clinical training; (3) a **qualifying exam** to obtain full registration within three years; (4) covers trades across **10 broad categories** (physiotherapy, optometry, lab technology, psychology, dietetics etc.). Draft only — not final.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (regulation of health workforce), GS2 Health (NCAHP Act, 2021).

## 4. Ministry of Women & Child Development (NCPCR): **Draft child-safety guidelines for social media platforms** (reported 12 Nov 2026; *single source — MediaNama; verify*) — GS2 Governance / GS3 Sci-Tech (cyber)
- **What:** NCPCR is drafting guidelines, to be put to public consultation and finalised in **early 2027**, and has asked platforms (Meta, Google/YouTube, X, Snapchat, Reddit, Roblox, Discord) to self-report against a "Child Safety Implementation Matrix" of **10 requirement areas**.
- **Key ideas:** safety by design; age assurance beyond self-declaration; private-by-default minor accounts, location sharing off; no behavioural-ad profiling of minors; limits on infinite scroll/autoplay/streaks and a night-time quiet mode; automated CSAM and grooming detection (incl. Indian languages); single-step child reporting linked to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. Measures are exploratory and may not be binding.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Child rights/Governance; GS3 Cyber security.

### Scheme-in-focus: Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) Scheme
- **Ministry:** Department of Fertilizers, Ministry of Chemicals & Fertilizers.
- **Objective:** Ensure balanced use of nutrients (N, P, K, S) and availability of P&K fertilisers at affordable prices while rationalising subsidy.
- **Key features:** (1) Fixed per-kg subsidy on nutrients, reset each season (Kharif/Rabi); (2) in force since 1 April 2010; (3) covers 28 P&K grades (DAP, NPKS complexes, MOP etc.); (4) manufacturers/importers set MRPs, with Government monitoring; urea stays under separate MRP regime.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Farmers nationwide, indirectly; fertiliser companies are paid the subsidy.
- **Significance:** Large fiscal commitment (₹62,370 crore this Rabi) and import dependence (DAP, potash, sulphur) link it to food security, fiscal deficit and balanced-fertilisation (soil health) debates.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**1.** With reference to the Nutrient Based Subsidy (NBS) scheme, consider the statements:
1. It has been in force since 1 April 2010.
2. Urea is also covered under the NBS scheme.
3. The Rabi 2026-27 rates approved by the Cabinet apply from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**2.** With reference to the first BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting (Colombo, November 2026), consider the statements:
1. Members adopted a Revised BIMSTEC Plan of Action on Public Health (2026–2030).
2. India will host the second Health Ministers' Meeting in 2028.
3. India will host the BIMSTEC Centre of Excellence in Traditional Medicine at ITRA, Jamnagar.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 2 and 3 only

**3.** Consider the draft provisions recently issued by the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions (NCAHP):
1. NCAHP is a statutory body constituted under the National Commission for Allied and Healthcare Professions Act, 2021.
2. Practitioners without recognised qualifications may get provisional registration valid for three years, extendable by one year.
3. Practitioners completing a bridge course get full registration without any qualifying examination.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — NBS dates from 1 April 2010 and the Rabi 2026-27 rates run 1 Oct 2026–31 Mar 2027; urea is *not* under NBS (statement 2 wrong).
2. **(c)** — The Revised Plan of Action (2026–2030) was adopted and India will host the Traditional Medicine CoE at ITRA Jamnagar; Thailand, not India, hosts the second meeting in 2028.
3. **(b)** — NCAHP was set up under the 2021 Act and the draft provides 3(+1)-year provisional registration; full registration requires a qualifying exam after the bridge course, so statement 3 is wrong.
