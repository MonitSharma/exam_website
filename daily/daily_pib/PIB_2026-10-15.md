## 1. Ministry of Rural Development: **Cabinet extends the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) for 2026-27 to 2030-31 and renames it "Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana"** (Cabinet, 14 Oct)
- **What:** Union Cabinet approved continuation of NSAP for five years with an outlay of **₹53,428 crore**, 100% centrally funded so benefits are uniform nationwide. About **3.20 crore beneficiaries** across all 36 States/UTs.
- **Why it matters:** NSAP (launched 15 Aug 1995) operationalises **Article 41 (DPSP)** — right to public assistance in old age, sickness, disablement. The revision widens disability coverage.
- **Key facts:** Disability-pension eligibility threshold cut from **80% to 40%** (aligned with RPwD Act, 2016); minimum age condition removed (eligibility from birth); reported assistance ₹375/month (up to 79 yrs) and ₹625/month (80+) *(amounts single-source)*. Old-age, widow and family-benefit components continue unchanged. Revised guidelines due within three months.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (welfare schemes, vulnerable sections); Polity (DPSP).

### Scheme-in-focus: NSAP → Pradhan Mantri Samajik Sahayata Yojana
- **Ministry:** Rural Development (Centrally Sponsored; fully Central funding per release).
- **Objective:** Social pension/assistance to poor households (BPL) — old age, widowhood, disability, death of breadwinner.
- **Components (new names):** IGNOAPS → PM Vridhavastha Pension Yojana; IGNWPS → PM Vidhawa Pension Yojana; IGNDPS → PM Divyangjan Pension Yojana; National Family Benefit Scheme → PM Parivar Labh Yojana.
- **Coverage:** ~3.20 crore beneficiaries, all States/UTs.
- **Significance:** Rights-based disability inclusion; Article 41 in practice; scheme rebranding trend (cf. VB-G RAM G replacing MGNREGA).

## 2. Ministry of Fisheries, Animal Husbandry & Dairying: **India approves accession to the Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement (SIOFA)** (14 Oct)
- **What:** India moves from **Cooperating Non-Contracting Party (CNCP, since 2022)** to full participant in SIOFA, a regional fisheries management treaty for non-highly-migratory high-seas stocks in the Southern Indian Ocean.
- **Why it matters:** Indian-flagged vessels can be authorised to fish SIOFA stocks (squid, toothfish, alfonsino); India gets a vote in the Scientific and Compliance Committees and can join the High Seas Boarding & Inspection scheme. Linked to the Guidelines for High Sea Fisheries (Dec 2025) and Blue Economy goals.
- **Key facts:** SIOFA signed in Rome 7 Jul 2006, in force 2012; covers fish, molluscs, crustaceans and other non-highly-migratory species. Tuna (highly migratory) is handled by IOTC, of which India is a member.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy/Environment (Blue Economy, fisheries); GS2 IR (global commons, UNCLOS framework).

## 3. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment (via NBCFDC): **BHARAT TULIP — market-access programme for traditional artisans in news** (reported 13–15 Oct, Mysuru event)
- **What:** Traditional Artisans' Upliftment Livelihood Programme, launched 5 Nov 2024, implemented by the National Backward Classes Finance & Development Corporation (NBCFDC).
- **Features:** Tie-ups with e-commerce platforms; heritage collections (Virasat Living – home décor, Ratna Rekha – jewellery, Resham Sutra – textiles, Leather Legacy – leather); participation in fairs/exhibitions (Shilp Samagam melas, buyer-seller meets); quality checks and skilling; 1,500+ associated artisans (NBCFDC site).
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (vulnerable sections); GS1 Society/Culture (handicrafts, livelihoods).

## 4. Ministry of Defence / MP-IDSA: **First ASEAN–India Defence Think Tank Forum, New Delhi** (14–15 Oct)
- **What:** Inaugural forum hosted by Manohar Parrikar Institute for Defence Studies and Analyses (MP-IDSA).
- **Themes (five sessions):** strategic stability; maritime cooperation vs grey-zone coercion; maritime HADR and climate risks; defence tech (counter-drone, AI, start-ups); defence industry co-production and trade. Linked to Act East, SAGAR/MAHASAGAR.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (ASEAN, Indo-Pacific); GS3 Internal/External Security.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the National Social Assistance Programme (NSAP) as continued by the Cabinet in October 2026, consider:
1. It is rooted in Article 41 of the Constitution.
2. The disability pension eligibility threshold has been lowered from 80% to 40%.
3. It is a State-funded scheme with no Central assistance.

Which of the above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** India's accession to the Southern Indian Ocean Fisheries Agreement (SIOFA) is significant because:
(a) It regulates fishing in India's territorial waters
(b) It upgrades India from Cooperating Non-Contracting Party status and enables fishing in high-seas SIOFA areas
(c) It governs tuna and other highly migratory species
(d) It was concluded under the Convention on Biological Diversity

**Q3.** BHARAT TULIP, recently in news, is an initiative of which Ministry and implemented through which agency?
(a) Ministry of Textiles – Handicrafts Development Commissioner
(b) Ministry of MSME – NSIC
(c) Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment – NBCFDC
(d) Ministry of Tribal Affairs – TRIFED

## Answers
1. **(b)** — NSAP flows from Article 41 (DPSP); disability threshold cut to 40%; funding is 100% Central, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — India held CNCP status since 2022; SIOFA covers non-highly-migratory stocks (tuna falls under IOTC) on the high seas, not territorial waters.
3. **(c)** — Launched Nov 2024 under MoSJE and implemented by NBCFDC.
