## 1. Digital India completes 11 years — pivot from connectivity to frontier technology
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) · 11th-anniversary review (programme launched 1 July 2015)
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (digital economy, DPI) · GS2 Governance (e-governance)

**What it is / why it matters:** As the Digital India programme nears its 11th anniversary (1 July), MeitY has put out a decadal review framing a shift from a *digital-access* mission to a *frontier-technology* mission anchored in AI and semiconductors, aligned with Viksit Bharat@2047. It is the single most exam-relevant "Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI)" consolidation of the year.

**Key facts & figures:**
- Built on the **JAM Trinity** — Jan Dhan, Aadhaar, Mobile — to plug welfare leakages via Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT). Cumulative DBT: **₹51 lakh crore+** to 176 crore beneficiaries.
- **UPI** (completing 10 years in 2026) now handles ~81% of India's digital payments and ~**49% of global real-time payment volume**; live in 8–9 countries (UAE, Singapore, France, Mauritius, Sri Lanka, etc.). Operated by **NPCI**, not the RBI directly.
- **Aadhaar:** 144+ crore enrolments; statutory basis is the Aadhaar Act, 2016, upheld in *Puttaswamy-II (2018)* via a three-fold proportionality test; *Puttaswamy-I (2017)* made privacy a fundamental right under Article 21.
- **DigiLocker** 70.69 cr users; **UMANG** 2,572 services; **BharatNet** connected ~97% of Gram Panchayats; **5G** in 99.9% of districts.
- **Frontier pivot:** India Semiconductor Mission 2.0 — ₹1.64 lakh crore approved across 12 projects; India hosted the **India AI Impact Summit (Feb 2026)** as the first Global South host.

## 2. 19th National Statistics Day observed — "75 Years of the National Sample Survey"
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI) · 29 June 2026, Dr Ambedkar International Centre, New Delhi
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Economy (statistical system, planning) · GS2 Governance (evidence-based policy)

**What it is / why it matters:** Statistics Day is observed every 29 June on the birth anniversary of **Prof. Prasanta Chandra Mahalanobis**, founder of the Indian Statistical Institute (ISI, Kolkata) and architect of India's **Second Five Year Plan**. The 2026 theme marks **75 years of the National Sample Survey (NSS)** — the backbone of India's household-survey data (consumption, employment, health) used for poverty lines, CPI weights and policy design.

**Key facts & figures:**
- NSS was established in **1950**; the NSSO now sits within the **National Statistical Office (NSO)** under MoSPI.
- The apex body advising on statistics is the **National Statistical Commission** (Chairman: Prof. Rajeeva Laxman Karandikar); MoSPI Secretary: Dr Saurabh Garg.
- The **Prof. C.R. Rao National Award in Statistics** was conferred; a **commemorative coin and "My Stamp"** marked NSS@75.
- MoSPI launched the **GoIStat** mobile app for easy public access to official statistics, and felicitated Data Visualization Hackathon winners.
- *UPSC angle:* Mahalanobis is also remembered for the **Mahalanobis distance** and the heavy-industry-led growth model of the 1956 Plan.

## 3. MSME Day 2026 — "Udyami Bharat": digital portals, NSIC upgrade, PM Vishwakarma at 3 years
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Micro, Small & Medium Enterprises · International MSME Day (27 June); presided by Vice-President Shri C.P. Radhakrishnan
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Economy (industry, MSMEs, employment)

**What it is / why it matters:** On UN-designated International MSME Day, the Ministry rolled out a digital-governance bundle for the sector that contributes ~30% of GDP and the bulk of non-farm jobs. The headline institutional change: the **National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC)** was upgraded from a **Schedule 'B' to a Schedule 'A' CPSE**.

**Key facts & figures:**
- New/upgraded platforms: **PMEGP 2.0, SAMADHAAN 2.0** (delayed-payments grievance redress), **MSME Global Mart 2.0, MSME Testing Portal, PMS Portal, and MSME Idea Hackathon 6.0**.
- **PM Vishwakarma Scheme** marked **3 years** (launched Sept 2023) — a coffee-table book of artisan success stories was released.
- A book on the **Self-Reliant India (SRI) Fund** highlighted equity support for growth, jobs and women's entrepreneurship.
- KVIC launched eco-friendly product lines (Tukun, Rangtaal, Bela, Vanya, Umang).

**Scheme snapshot — Self-Reliant India (SRI) Fund**
- *Ministry:* MSME. *Objective:* provide **equity/growth capital** to viable MSMEs to help them scale and list, easing their chronic dependence on debt.
- *Key features:* mother-fund/daughter-fund (fund-of-funds) structure with a corpus of **₹50,000 crore** (₹10,000 cr Government + ₹40,000 cr leveraged from private equity/VC); invests in growth-stage MSMEs.
- *Beneficiaries:* formal MSMEs with growth potential, with an emphasis on women-led enterprises.
- *Significance:* shifts MSME financing from collateral-based lending toward **equity infusion**, encouraging formalisation and larger firm sizes.

## 5. 16th Conference of the Central Council of Health & Family Welfare (CCHFW)
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare · 29 June 2026, Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi; chaired by Union Health Minister Shri J.P. Nadda
**Destination notebook:** GS2 Polity & Governance (cooperative federalism, Article 263) · health policy

**What it is / why it matters:** A high-value **polity + governance** item. The CCHFW is the apex advisory body in the health sector, constituted under **Article 263** of the Constitution — the same provision that empowers the President to set up inter-State coordination councils (e.g., the Inter-State Council). It is therefore created by **Presidential order, not by statute**, making it a textbook example of a body for promoting Centre–State cooperation.

**Key facts & figures:**
- Brought together State/UT Health Ministers, MPs, officials and experts to strengthen Centre–State collaboration on health (a State/Concurrent-list subject).
- Thematic sessions: **National Health Mission (NHM) — SDG goals & priorities; Food & Drug reforms; Allied Health Services.**
- Minister unveiled **Operational Guidelines on National Ambulance Services** at the conference.
- *Prelims hook:* Article 263 bodies are advisory and inter-governmental — contrast with the GST Council (Art. 279A, a constitutional body with binding recommendations-style mandate) and the (now-defunct) Planning Commission/NITI Aayog (executive bodies).

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to **Statistics Day** in India, consider the following statements:
1. It is observed on 29 June to mark the birth anniversary of Prof. P.C. Mahalanobis.
2. Prof. Mahalanobis founded the Indian Statistical Institute and is regarded as the architect of India's Second Five Year Plan.
3. The 2026 theme commemorated 75 years of the National Sample Survey (NSS).

How many of the statements given above are correct?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q2.** With reference to the **Central Council of Health and Family Welfare (CCHFW)**, consider the following statements:
1. It is constituted under Article 263 of the Constitution of India.
2. It is a statutory body established by an Act of Parliament.
3. It functions as an apex advisory body to promote Centre–State cooperation in the health sector.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** Consider the following statements regarding the **Digital India** programme and its ecosystem:
1. It was launched in 2015 by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
2. The "JAM trinity" underpinning it refers to Jan Dhan, Aadhaar and Mobile.
3. The Unified Payments Interface (UPI) is owned and operated directly by the Reserve Bank of India.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (c) All three.** Statistics Day is on 29 June (Mahalanobis's birth anniversary); he founded the ISI (Kolkata) and shaped the Second Five Year Plan; the 2026 observance marked NSS@75.

**Q2 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** The CCHFW is set up under **Article 263** by **Presidential order**, not by statute — so statement 2 is wrong. It is an apex advisory body fostering cooperative federalism in health.

**Q3 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Digital India was launched in 2015 under MeitY and rests on the JAM trinity. UPI is operated by the **National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI)** — an umbrella organisation promoted by the RBI and IBA — not directly by the RBI; statement 3 is incorrect.
