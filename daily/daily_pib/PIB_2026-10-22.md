## 1. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment: **PM-AJAY — Adarsh Gram, grants-in-aid and hostel progress** (PIB-based report, 17 Oct 2026)

**What/why:** A progress statement on the Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana, the main Scheduled Caste development scheme. It matters for GS2 (welfare schemes for vulnerable sections) and as a recurring Prelims scheme.

**Key facts/figures (as reported):**
- 18,093 villages declared Adarsh Grams; the same report cites 47,200+ villages covered (about 47.79 lakh people). The report does not reconcile the two figures.
- 46,944 development works completed; 27,032 Village Development Plans prepared.
- About ₹1,730 crore released to States as grants-in-aid (2021-22 to 2025-26), supporting over 2.5 lakh individual and group beneficiaries in skilling and livelihoods.
- 785 student hostels completed (Sept 2026); ₹61.46 crore allocated for 2026-27 for two hostels (264 students).

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance — Welfare schemes / Social Justice.

### Scheme-in-focus: **PM-AJAY**
- **Ministry:** Social Justice & Empowerment. **Objective:** Reduce poverty among SCs, improve socio-economic indicators, and build SC-majority villages' infrastructure.
- **Key features:** (1) Merges three earlier schemes: PMAGY, SCA to SCSP and BJRCY (2021-22); (2) three components: Adarsh Gram, grants-in-aid, hostels; (3) reported as 100% centrally funded; (4) mobile app and portal for real-time monitoring.
- **Beneficiaries:** SC persons and SC-majority villages (population above 50%).
- **Significance:** Convergence of infrastructure, livelihood and education in one scheme; the Adarsh Gram count versus "villages covered" gap shows why outcome data needs care.

## 2. Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (with Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare): **7th AgriVoltaics World Conference, Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi (21–23 Oct 2026)**

**What/why:** India hosts the global agri-PV conference, hosted by MNRE and the International Climate Initiative (IKI); the conference theme is "Powering the Global South". Union Minister of Agriculture Shivraj Singh Chouhan inaugurated it (per PIB listing, 21 Oct). Agrivoltaics co-locates solar PV with crop cultivation, linking energy security, farm incomes and land-use efficiency. PM Modi shared an article by Pralhad Joshi and Shivraj Singh Chouhan on agrivoltaics and farmers' incomes (21 Oct).

**Key facts:** 7th edition; 21–23 Oct; Bharat Mandapam. Media describe it as the first edition in the Global South — *not confirmed on the organiser's page*. In September 2026 the MNRE minister spoke of farmers becoming "Urjadata" (energy providers) through agrivoltaics.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy/Environment — Renewable energy, Agriculture.

## 3. Ministry of Tribal Affairs with Karmayogi Bharat (DoPT): **MoU to launch an "Adi Karmayogi" microsite on iGOT Karmayogi** (13 Oct, New Delhi) *(single source)*

**What/why:** Extends the iGOT capacity-building platform to Adi Karmayogi Volunteers (AKVs) and Adi Sewa Kendras (ASKs), the grassroots delivery network of the Adi Karmayogi Abhiyan.

**Key facts/figures (as reported):**
- Registrations: 60,507 ASKs (target 76,000); 1,22,072 AKVs (target 1,54,000).
- Mobile-friendly modules on PM-JANMAN, DAJGUA, EMRS and Forest Rights Act, with assessments and certificates; plus a 3-day in-person programme through State ATIs and Tribal Welfare Departments.
- Rollout: priority States (Maharashtra, Odisha, Goa, Tripura) by 31 Oct 2026; nationwide by 31 Dec 2026.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance — Tribal welfare, capacity building (Mission Karmayogi).

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about the Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana (PM-AJAY):
1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment.
2. It combines the earlier Pradhan Mantri Adarsh Gram Yojana, Special Central Assistance to Scheduled Castes Sub-Plan and Babu Jagjivan Ram Chhatrawas Yojana.
3. It is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme with a 60:40 Centre–State funding pattern for all States.
Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 1 and 2 only  (c) 2 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the MoU between the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and Karmayogi Bharat to launch an 'Adi Karmayogi' microsite on the iGOT platform, consider:
1. The microsite offers mobile-friendly training modules on schemes such as PM-JANMAN and the Forest Rights Act to Adi Karmayogi Volunteers.
2. Karmayogi Bharat is the special purpose vehicle that operates the iGOT Karmayogi platform under Mission Karmayogi.
Which of the statements is/are correct?
(a) 1 only  (b) 2 only  (c) Both 1 and 2  (d) Neither 1 nor 2

**Q3.** The 7th AgriVoltaics World Conference (Bharat Mandapam, 21–23 Oct 2026) is hosted by MNRE with the International Climate Initiative. 'Agrivoltaics' refers to:
(a) Floating solar panels over reservoirs and canals
(b) Co-location of solar PV generation and crop cultivation on the same land
(c) Biogas digesters powering agricultural pumps
(d) Conversion of crop residue into electricity in thermal plants

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — PM-AJAY (MoSJE) merged PMAGY, SCA to SCSP and BJRCY; it is reported as 100% centrally funded, so statement 3 is wrong.
2. **(c)** — The 13 Oct 2026 MoU adds PM-JANMAN, DAJGUA, EMRS and FRA modules for AKVs; Karmayogi Bharat is the non-profit SPV running iGOT under Mission Karmayogi.
3. **(b)** — Agrivoltaics combines solar power generation and agriculture on the same land.
