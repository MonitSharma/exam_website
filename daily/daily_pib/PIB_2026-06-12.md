## 1. Cabinet clears Rs 4,703 cr — Amaravati office/housing + Ahmedabad Metro Phase 2(A)
**Issuing body:** Union Cabinet / CCEA (implementing agency: MoHUA via CPWD) → **GS3 (Economy/Infrastructure)**

The Cabinet approved three projects worth ~Rs 4,703 crore. For **Amaravati** (AP's greenfield capital): a Central Government General Pool Office Accommodation (CGGPOA) at **Rs 1,299 cr** (23.25 lakh sq ft, ~8,000 officials) and a residential complex at **Rs 1,235 cr** (1,504 units). Separately, **Ahmedabad Metro Phase 2(A)** — a 6.03 km Koteshwar Road–SVP International Airport corridor at **Rs 2,169 cr** (4 elevated + 1 underground station), positioned as a transit link for the 2030 Commonwealth Games.

**Why it matters:** Tests the federal capital-building push in Amaravati and urban-transit expansion. Remember: implemented by **MoHUA/CPWD**; Amaravati = greenfield capital of Andhra Pradesh.

## 2. PMSMA completes 10 years — "A Decade of Care"
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare (MoHFW) → **GS2 (Governance / Social sector)**

Health Minister J.P. Nadda launched nationwide celebrations for a decade of the **Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA)**, releasing a **Rs 75 commemorative coin** and a **Rs 5 postal stamp**. Awareness/outreach activities run across all States/UTs from 9 June.

**Scheme template:**
- **Name/Ministry:** PMSMA — MoHFW. Launched **9 June 2016**.
- **Objective:** Free, quality, comprehensive antenatal care (ANC) to all pregnant women.
- **Key features:** Fixed-day care on the **9th of every month**; 9 assured free ANC services; voluntary participation of private practitioners; focus on 2nd/3rd trimester.
- **Beneficiaries:** Pregnant women (esp. high-risk pregnancy identification).
- **Significance:** Tackles maternal mortality (MMR); one of India's flagship maternal-health interventions.

## 3. Textile PLI Round-3 — 96 companies, Rs 12,822 cr investment
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Textiles → **GS3 (Economy)**

The Ministry cleared **22 new applicants** under Round-3 of the **PLI Scheme for Textiles** (Rs 2,339 cr investment, 36,217 jobs). Cumulatively, **96 companies** are now selected with **Rs 12,822.67 cr** committed investment and Rs 58,294 cr projected turnover.

**Scheme template:**
- **Name/Ministry:** PLI Scheme for Textiles — Ministry of Textiles. Launched **2021**.
- **Objective:** Boost domestic manufacturing of high-value man-made and technical textiles.
- **Focus segments:** **MMF apparel, MMF fabrics, technical textiles** (NOT cotton/natural fibre).
- **Significance:** Supports Aatmanirbhar Bharat; textiles = India's 2nd-largest employer after agriculture.

## 4. MoD signs Rs 449 cr deal for 20 ECGNSS Jammers (Indian Navy)
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Defence → **GS3 (Internal Security / Sci-Tech)**

MoD signed a **Rs 449 crore** contract with **Accord Software & Systems Pvt Ltd (ASSPL), Bengaluru** for **20 Enhanced Capability GNSS (ECGNSS) Jammers** for the Indian Navy, under the **Buy (Indian-IDDM)** category with minimum **75% indigenous content**.

**Why it matters / facts to remember:** ECGNSS jammers degrade an adversary's satellite-navigation (GNSS) acquisition/tracking via spoofing and deceptive jamming — boosting the Navy's **Electronic Warfare (EW)** capability. Key concept: **IDDM = Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured** — the top-priority category under the Defence Acquisition Procedure.

## 5. CGSMFI-2.0 extended to 31 Aug 2026; NBFC-MFI loan cap raised to Rs 1,000 cr
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Finance (admin. by NCGTC) → **GS3 (Economy)**

The Centre extended the **Credit Guarantee Scheme for Microfinance Institutions-2.0 (CGSMFI-2.0)** until **31 August 2026** (or until Rs 20,000 cr guarantees are issued), and raised the loan cap for large NBFC-MFIs from **Rs 300 cr to Rs 1,000 cr** (capped at 20% of AUM).

**Facts to remember:** Launched 20 March 2026; administered by **NCGTC** under Ministry of Finance. Guarantee cover on default: **80% small / 75% medium / 70% large** MFIs. Aim: ease the microfinance credit squeeze and channel credit to small borrowers.

## 6. BSNL–IIT Kanpur MoU on next-gen telecom (D2M, indigenous 4G)
**Issuing body:** BSNL (Ministry of Communications) + IIT Kanpur → **GS3 (Sci-Tech)**

BSNL and IIT Kanpur signed an MoU to develop future telecom tech — **Direct-to-Mobile (D2M)** broadcasting, indigenous 4G, and advanced spectrum research.

**Why it matters:** Supports digital self-reliance (Aatmanirbhar Bharat). **D2M** lets mobile devices receive TV/radio broadcasts **without an active data connection** — useful for emergency alerts and mass content delivery. BSNL is a Ministry of Communications PSU.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme for Textiles, which saw 96 companies approved under Round-3 in June 2026, consider the following statements:
1. It is administered by the Ministry of Textiles.
2. Its focus segments are Man-Made Fibre (MMF) apparel, MMF fabrics and technical textiles.
3. The scheme exclusively incentivises cotton and other natural-fibre products.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The 'Buy (Indian-IDDM)' category, frequently seen in news on defence procurement (such as the June 2026 Rs 449 crore contract for ECGNSS Jammers for the Indian Navy), refers to the acquisition of defence equipment that is:
(a) Imported in fully-built form from foreign Original Equipment Manufacturers
(b) Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured
(c) Imported and merely assembled in India under a licence agreement
(d) Procured jointly through an Inter-Departmental Defence Manufacturing pool

**Q3.** Consider the following statements about the Pradhan Mantri Surakshit Matritva Abhiyan (PMSMA), which completed 10 years in June 2026:
1. It assures free, comprehensive antenatal care to pregnant women on the 9th day of every month.
2. It is implemented by the Ministry of Women and Child Development.
3. It was launched in 2016.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. The scheme (launched 2021, run by the Ministry of Textiles) targets MMF apparel, MMF fabrics and technical textiles. Statement 3 is wrong — it deliberately avoids natural fibres like cotton.

**Q2 — (b).** IDDM = Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured, the highest-priority category under the Defence Acquisition Procedure. The ECGNSS Jammers deal carries ≥75% indigenous content.

**Q3 — (b).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct — PMSMA (launched 9 June 2016) gives fixed-day (9th of every month) free ANC. Statement 2 is wrong — it is run by the **MoHFW**, not WCD.
