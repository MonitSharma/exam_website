# Daily PIB Briefing — 9 July 2026

## 1. India–Indonesia heritage diplomacy — ASI begins conservation of the Prambanan Temple Complex, Yogyakarta

**Ministry:** Ministry of Culture (Archaeological Survey of India) | **Date:** 8 July 2026

During PM Modi's visit to Indonesia, he and President Prabowo Subianto jointly unveiled a plaque marking the start of the Archaeological Survey of India's (ASI) conservation and restoration project at the Prambanan Temple Complex, Yogyakarta — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and the largest temple complex in Indonesia, built in the 9th century and dedicated to the Hindu Trimurti (Brahma, Vishnu, Shiva). The project operationalises an understanding reached during President Prabowo's 2025 State Visit to India and builds on ASI's earlier documentation work at the Borobudur Temple Compounds (a Buddhist UNESCO site), extending India's heritage-diplomacy footprint under the Act East Policy.

**Key facts:** Prambanan — 9th century, dedicated to Trimurti, tallest (Shiva) shrine ~47 m; ASI functions under Ministry of Culture; this is ASI's second major Southeast Asian conservation engagement after Borobudur.

**Notebook:** GS1 (Culture, shared civilisational heritage) / GS2 (India's neighbourhood, cultural diplomacy)

## 2. DRDO flight-tests the Pinaka Long Range Guided Rocket (LRGR) at Chandipur

**Ministry:** Ministry of Defence (DRDO) | **Date:** 8 July 2026

DRDO successfully flight-tested the Pinaka Long Range Guided Rocket (LRGR) at the Integrated Test Range (ITR), Chandipur, Odisha, for a user-defined minimum range of 60 km. The rocket executed all planned in-flight manoeuvres and hit the target with precision along its predicted trajectory. Significantly, it was fired from the existing in-service Pinaka launcher — without any dedicated new launcher — demonstrating that different-range Pinaka variants can be operated from a single platform, a major logistics advantage for the Army. Defence Minister Rajnath Singh called it a "major milestone" in indigenous long-range guided-rocket capability.

**Key facts:** Lead design agency — Armament Research and Development Establishment (ARDE); supporting labs — HEMRL, DRDL, RCI; trial coordinated by ITR and PXE; Pinaka MBRL has been in Army service since the 1999 Kargil War.

**Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Technology, indigenisation, defence production, internal/external security)

## 3. Mizoram University's Natural History Museum notified as India's 21st Designated Repository under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002

**Ministry:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change | **Notified:** 19 June 2026 (announced 7 July 2026)

On the recommendation of the National Biodiversity Authority, the Central Government notified the Natural History Museum (NHM) at Mizoram University, Aizawl, as a Designated Repository under Section 39 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 — India's 21st such repository. The NHM, established in 2022, will maintain voucher specimens of select flora (pteridophytes, macrofungi) and fauna (reptiles, amphibians, fishes, moths, beetles, butterflies). Its location within the Indo-Burma Biodiversity Hotspot gives it special taxonomic value, complementing existing repositories like the Botanical Survey of India and Zoological Survey of India, and advancing National Biodiversity Target 4 of India's NBSAP (2024–2030).

**Key facts:** Legal basis — Section 39, Biological Diversity Act, 2002; total Designated Repositories in India — 21; host institution — Mizoram University (a Central University).

**Notebook:** GS3 (Environment, biodiversity conservation, species/genetic-resource repositories)

## 5. MoHFW conducts National Training of Trainers under the Integrated Training Strategy for Ayushman Arogya Mandirs

**Ministry:** Ministry of Health & Family Welfare | **Date:** 8 July 2026

The Union Health Ministry held a two-day National Training of Trainers at the National Health Systems Resource Centre (NHSRC), New Delhi, covering all five frontline cadres delivering primary healthcare at Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (AAMs) — Medical Officers, Staff Nurses, Community Health Officers, ANMs and ASHAs. This operationalises the Integrated Training Strategy (ITS), first unveiled by Union Health Minister J.P. Nadda at the National Summit on Best Practices in Chandigarh (30 April 2026), which shifts from programme-specific silos to a patient-centric, competency-based training model. Over 110 National and Master Trainers took part; rollout to over 100 Regional Training Institutes begins from August 2026.

**Key facts:** Five cadres covered; 110+ trainers trained; nationwide rollout via 100+ Regional Training Institutes from August 2026.

**Notebook:** GS2 (Governance — health-system delivery and human-resource capacity building)

## Answers

**Q1. (b) 1 and 3 only.** The LRGR was fired from the existing in-service Pinaka launcher — no dedicated new launcher was built — so statement 2 is incorrect. ARDE is confirmed as the lead design agency.

**Q2. (b) Biological Diversity Act, 2002.** The NHM, Mizoram University was notified as India's 21st Designated Repository under Section 39 of the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, on the National Biodiversity Authority's recommendation.

**Q3. (a) 1 and 2 only.** Prambanan is indeed a UNESCO site dedicated to the Trimurti. Statement 3 is incorrect — ASI has previously undertaken extensive documentation work at the Borobudur Temple Compounds in Indonesia, so Prambanan is not its first heritage engagement there.
