## 1. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare: **Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses — one year on** (PIB backgrounder, 11 Oct 2026)

**What it is / why it matters:** The Mission (Cabinet-approved 1 Oct 2025, launched 11 Oct 2025) aims to end India's chronic pulses import dependence by 2030-31 through area expansion (esp. rice-fallows), certified seed, assured procurement and processing capacity. The one-year backgrounder reports production, area and yield gains in 2025-26 and a sharp fall in imports.

**Key facts / figures:**
- Outlay ₹11,440 crore, 2025-26 to 2030-31 (six years). Focus crops: **tur, urad, masoor**. Cluster approach in **489 districts**; add ~35 lakh ha, aiming for 310 lakh ha area.
- **100% procurement** of tur, urad and masoor at MSP by **NAFED and NCCF**, 2025-26 to 2028-29; 1,000 pulse processing units (dal mills); 87.5 lakh free seed kits and 126 lakh quintals certified seed over six years.
- 2025-26 (3rd Advance Estimates): production 274.09 lakh tonnes (from 256.83); area 286.46 lakh ha; yield 957 kg/ha (from 926); procurement up ~70.7% to 31.41 lakh tonnes.
- Trade: imports fell 31.52% in value (₹46,427.60 cr → ₹31,793.59 cr); exports rose 13.4% to ~US$969.5 million.
- 2030-31 targets: ~350 lakh tonnes production, 1,130 kg/ha yield. MSP 2026-27: tur ₹8,450, urad ₹8,200, masoor ₹7,000.
- Delivery gaps: 528 of 1,000 dal mills allocated so far; only 9.25 lakh seed kits distributed in Rabi 2025-26.

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture (pulses, MSP, food security, import substitution).

## 2. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare: **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (PMDDKY) completes year one** (PIB backgrounder, 10 Oct; anniversary 11 Oct 2026) *(UPDATE — scheme/State rollout was mentioned earlier in the CA; new here are the year-one results and monitoring architecture)*

**What it is / why it matters:** An aspirational-district-style model for agriculture: 100 low-performing districts get a convergence-driven District Action Plan. The year-one data show average scores rising sharply — a template for outcome-based, data-driven governance.

**Key facts / figures:**
- ₹24,000 crore a year for six years from 2025-26 (Cabinet 16 July 2025; launched 11 Oct 2025); ~1.7 crore farmers; **100 districts**, at least one per State.
- Converges **36 Central schemes of 11 Ministries/Departments** plus State schemes and private sector. Selection: low productivity, low cropping intensity, low credit.
- Six objectives: productivity, crop diversification, post-harvest storage, irrigation, credit, governance/service delivery.
- Governance: **District Samiti** (chaired by DM/Collector) prepares the District Action Plan; **State Samiti** (Chief Secretary) reviews; **National Executive Committee** (Union Agriculture Minister); National Monitoring Committee (Secretary, DA&FW).
- Monitoring: **121 indicators** (74 output, 47 outcome), monthly district ranking; dashboard on the NITI Aayog portal. Average score 18.85 (Apr 2026) → 46.89 (Aug 2026).
- Top districts reported: Nagaur (Rajasthan), Gaya (Bihar), Umaria (Madhya Pradesh). DPE has listed adopting these districts as CSR for CPSEs in FY 2026-27 and 2027-28.

**Scheme in focus — PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana:** *Ministry:* Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare. *Objective:* raise productivity and incomes in 100 lagging agricultural districts. *Features:* (i) convergence of 36 schemes; (ii) District Action Plans; (iii) 121-indicator monthly monitoring; (iv) CSR/private participation. *Beneficiaries:* ~1.7 crore farmers. *Significance:* outcome-based convergence governance; blueprint for district-level agri planning.

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture; GS2 Governance (convergence, aspirational-district model).

## 3. Ministry of Home Affairs: **Central Armed Police Forces (General Administration) Act, 2026 brought into force on 8 Oct 2026** *(UPDATE — Act's challenge covered earlier; new is the commencement notification)*

**What it is / why it matters:** MHA notified under Section 1(2) that the Act takes effect from 8 October 2026. It governs cadre/administration of CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP and SSB and preserves IPS deputation in senior posts — widely seen as overriding the Supreme Court's 2025 ruling in *Sanjay Prakash v. Union of India*, which held CAPF officers entitled to Group A promotions (IPS deputation to be curtailed). Petitions challenging the Act are pending before a bench of Justices Manoj Misra and Ujjal Bhuyan; contempt petitions are also being heard.

**Key facts:** at least 50% of IG posts and at least 67% of ADG posts to IPS deputationists; all Special DG and DG posts to IPS on deputation. *No court ruling reported yet.*

**Notebook:** GS2 Polity/Governance (legislative override of judgments, separation of powers); GS3 Internal Security (CAPF cadre).

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Mission for Aatmanirbharta in Pulses, consider:
1. It has an outlay of ₹11,440 crore for 2025-26 to 2030-31.
2. NAFED and NCCF are to procure tur, urad and masoor at MSP without a quantity cap for four years.
3. Its focus crops include chana and moong.
Which are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Regarding the PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana, consider:
1. It covers 100 districts and converges 36 Central schemes of 11 Ministries/Departments.
2. The District Action Plan is prepared by a District Samiti chaired by the District Collector.
3. The National Executive Committee is chaired by the Chief Secretary of a State.
Which are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** Consider the Central Armed Police Forces (General Administration) Act, 2026:
1. It came into force on 8 October 2026 through an MHA notification.
2. It provides at least 67% of ADG posts to IPS officers on deputation.
3. It applies to Assam Rifles and the Special Protection Group.
Which are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 2 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

1. **(a)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct (100% procurement of tur, urad, masoor via NAFED/NCCF, 2025-26 to 2028-29). Focus crops are tur, urad and masoor, not chana/moong.
2. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct. The National Executive Committee is chaired by the Union Agriculture Minister; the State Samiti is chaired by the Chief Secretary.
3. **(c)** — The Act took effect 8 Oct 2026 and reserves at least 67% of ADG posts for IPS deputation. It covers CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP and SSB — not Assam Rifles or SPG.
