## 1. PM Modi begins three-nation visit — Indonesia, Australia and New Zealand (6–11 July)

**Issuing body:** Ministry of External Affairs / PMO | **Date:** Departure 6 July 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations

PM Modi departed on a six-day, three-nation tour: **Indonesia (6–8 July)** at President Prabowo Subianto's invitation — his fourth visit and first bilateral trip since India–Indonesia ties were elevated to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership in 2018; **Australia (8–10 July)**, travelling to Melbourne at PM Anthony Albanese's invitation; and **New Zealand (10–11 July)**, a state visit to Auckland at PM Christopher Luxon's invitation — the **first state visit by an Indian PM to New Zealand in four decades**. It matters as a single trip touching Southeast Asia, the Indo-Pacific and Oceania, reinforcing India's Act East and Indo-Pacific partnerships simultaneously.

**Key facts:** In Jakarta, PM will address the Indian diaspora and visit the Prambanan Temple (UNESCO site) at Yogyakarta; the New Zealand leg will review trade, commerce and defence ties, which have deepened over the last two years.

## 2. India hosts BRICS Heads of Anti-Drug Agencies Meeting — Guwahati (6–7 July)

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Home Affairs / Narcotics Control Bureau, under India's BRICS Chairship | **Date:** 6–7 July 2026, Guwahati (Assam) | **Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security · GS2 — International Relations (BRICS)

As BRICS chair, India is hosting heads of anti-narcotics agencies and senior officials from member states to deepen operational cooperation against drug trafficking. Six thematic sessions cover synthetic drugs and precursor-chemical diversion, digital tools for real-time interdiction, darknet-enabled trafficking, New Psychoactive Substances (NPS), and demand reduction. It matters for GS3 as a live case of India's counter-narcotics diplomacy and institutional cooperation architecture (parallel to domestic efforts like MANAS, covered in an earlier briefing).

**Key facts:** Three priority areas — synthetic drugs/precursor diversion, intelligence-sharing and operational coordination, and capacity-building; outputs expected include joint training programmes and expert exchanges among BRICS drug-control agencies.

## 3. India's High-Speed Rail Future — standardised template for expansion beyond Mumbai–Ahmedabad

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Railways / National High-Speed Rail Corporation Ltd. (NHSRCL) | **Date:** PIB Backgrounder, 3 July 2026 | **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy/Infrastructure

With the **Mumbai–Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail (MAHSR)** corridor — India's first bullet train project — nearing completion, PIB Research detailed how its engineering template (foundations tailored to local soil, but common standards for piers, viaducts, tracks, stations, OHE and signalling) will be replicated across **seven identified future corridors** spanning nearly 4,000 km. It matters as India's clearest infrastructure case study of standardisation-driven cost/time efficiency and indigenous manufacturing under Make in India.

**Key facts:** MAHSR — 508 km, 12 stations, Mumbai–Ahmedabad in ~1 hr 58 min, design speed 350 kmph (operational 320 kmph), first section (Surat–Vapi) targeted August 2027; uses Japanese Shinkansen technology and **J-Slab ballastless track — a first for India**; seven future corridors (e.g., Delhi–Varanasi, Chennai–Bengaluru, Mumbai–Pune) projected to draw ~₹16 lakh crore investment; ICF-BEML developing indigenous 280 kmph train sets.

## 4. World's oldest scientifically-dated banyan tree confirmed in Munger, Bihar

**Issuing body:** Department of Science & Technology — Birbal Sahni Institute of Palaeosciences (BSIP), Lucknow | **Date:** reported early July 2026 | **Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology / Environment

Researchers at BSIP used **Accelerator Mass Spectrometry (AMS) radiocarbon dating** — calibrated against the IntCal20 curve via OxCal software — on alpha-cellulose extracted from a Munger banyan tree (*Ficus benghalensis*), confirming it as roughly **700 years old**, the oldest banyan anywhere to be dated by a rigorous scientific protocol rather than folklore or local record. It matters as a rare application of precision dating science to living heritage trees, relevant to both Science & Tech methodology and heritage/conservation policy.

**Key facts:** Study led by Dr. Trina Bose with Dr. Mayank Shekhar and Dr. Akhilesh K. Yadava; samples taken from a secondary trunk and an ancient primary branch; establishes a reusable protocol for dating other candidate "ancient" banyans across India.

## 5. LokOS — national digital backbone for Self-Help Groups crosses major scale milestones

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Rural Development (DAY-NRLM) | **Date:** reported early July 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (DPI) · GS3 — Economy (financial inclusion)

LokOS ("Lok" = people, "OS" = operating system) is the Ministry's end-to-end digitisation platform for Self-Help Groups (SHGs) and their federations — records, savings, lending and convergence — and is the parent platform on which **SHE-LEAPS** (covered in the 5 July briefing) runs. Fresh national numbers released this week show its scale: one of the largest digital community-institution platforms globally. *(Not a repeat of 5 July's SHE-LEAPS item — that covered the women-entrepreneurship sub-platform; this is the master SHG digitisation backbone with its own dataset.)*

**Key facts:** Coverage — 34 States/UTs, 762 districts, 7,241 blocks, 2.57 lakh Gram Panchayats, 5.92 lakh villages; institutions digitised — 34,314 Cluster-Level Federations, 5.62 lakh Village Organisations, **94.16 lakh SHGs**, **10.03 crore SHG members**, each with Aadhaar- and bank-linked digital IDs; captures ~₹2 lakh crore/year in SHG transactions, alongside a ₹9,718.41 crore Revolving Fund and ₹64,607.66 crore Community Investment Fund.

## Answers

**Q1. (b) 1 and 3 only** — Statements 1 and 3 are correct: the visit covers Indonesia, Australia and New Zealand, and follows the 2018 elevation of India–Indonesia ties to a Comprehensive Strategic Partnership. Statement 2 is incorrect — it is the first *state* visit by an Indian PM to New Zealand in four decades, not the first-ever visit.

**Q2. (b) Guwahati; Ministry of Home Affairs / Narcotics Control Bureau** — India, as BRICS chair, hosted the meeting in Guwahati, Assam, on 6–7 July 2026, led by home affairs/narcotics-control agencies to deepen counter-narcotics cooperation among BRICS states.

**Q3. (c) All three** — All statements are correct: MAHSR introduces J-Slab ballastless track (a first for India); its design speed (350 kmph) exceeds Vande Bharat's design speed (180 kmph), which is well above operational speeds too; and the first operational section is planned between Surat and Vapi.
