# Daily PIB Briefing — 16 October 2026

*Focus: Environment (MoEFCC), Finance (CCEA/DIPAM), Commerce & Industry, and PM's Ladakh projects (Defence/BRO, Power, Culture, Jal Shakti). Items not repeated from the 2–15 Oct briefings or the 15 Oct CA note.*

## 1. Ministry of Environment, Forest & Climate Change: **India's Ramsar sites rise to 103 — Sukhna Wetland (Chandigarh) and Sauj Jheel (Mainpuri, UP) added** (announced 15 Oct)

**What / why it matters:** The Environment Minister announced two new Wetlands of International Importance under the Ramsar Convention, taking India's tally from 101 to 103. It is a standard Prelims "tally + location" item and links to wetland conservation, the Central Asian Flyway and the Wetlands (Conservation and Management) Rules.

**Key facts:**
- **Sukhna Wetland (Chandigarh)** — Ramsar Site No. 2602; man-made reservoir at the Shivalik foothills, created in 1958 by damming the seasonal Sukhna Choe; wintering/stopover site on the Central Asian Flyway. Species cited: Common Pochard (VU), Pallas's Fish Eagle (EN), Black Pond Turtle (EN). First Ramsar site of Chandigarh.
- **Sauj Jheel (Mainpuri, Uttar Pradesh)** — Ramsar Site No. 2603; perennial shallow floodplain lake in the Ganga basin, an Important Bird and Biodiversity Area; hosts 30,000+ waterbirds; habitat for Sarus Crane and Greater Spotted Eagle (both VU).
- Mainpuri becomes the only district with two Ramsar sites (other: Saman Bird Sanctuary, 2019). UP's tally reported as 14.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (wetlands, biodiversity conservation, international conventions).

## 2. Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (Ministry of Finance — DIPAM): **Strategic disinvestment of 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank to a Fairfax Financial Holdings entity** (CCEA, 14 Oct)

**What / why it matters:** The CCEA approved the sale of a 60.72% stake (30.48% GoI + 30.24% LIC) in IDBI Bank to 10955230 Canada Inc., a Fairfax Financial Holdings company, for about ₹52,878 crore. The government calls it the largest FDI in India's financial sector. It is the culmination of the long-running strategic sale (RBI categorised IDBI as a private-sector bank in 2019 after LIC took control in 2018; PCA restrictions applied May 2017–Mar 2021).

**Key facts:**
- Consideration ≈ ₹52,877–52,878 crore; DIPAM Secretary said completion is expected before the end of FY 2026-27.
- Completion subject to regulatory approvals; buyer must make an **open offer to minority shareholders**; Fairfax's existing stake in CSB Bank must be merged into IDBI or exited within two years (per one report).
- Residual public holding: one source says GoI ~15% and LIC ~19% (≈34% together) — *single-source, verify against PIB text.*

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (disinvestment, banking sector reforms, FDI, PCA framework).

## 3. Ministry of Commerce & Industry: **National Strategic Trade Command Centre (NSTCC) launched at Vanijya Bhawan** (15 Oct)

**What / why it matters:** A data-analytics platform to monitor, analyse and govern India's trade ecosystem, giving a consolidated view of trade performance, export-promotion schemes, regulatory approvals, application processing and operational indicators. It supports evidence-based policy and trade facilitation.

**Key facts:**
- Launched by Commerce & Industry Minister Piyush Goyal.
- Includes a **SCOMET** (Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies) dashboard; Minister asked for regular (even weekly) Inter-Ministerial Committee meetings to clear pending SCOMET applications.
- Analysis at national, regional-authority (DGFT), scheme, product, destination-market and application level; role-based access; three focus areas — trade trends, application pendency, timely issue resolution.
- Same day: Commerce data put April–September 2026-27 exports at **US$476.20 billion**; September merchandise exports up 23.04% y/y to US$44.40 bn (secondary source, verify).

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (external sector, trade facilitation, e-governance); GS2 Governance.

## 4. Prime Minister's Office: **Five projects worth ~₹3,350 crore inaugurated in Ladakh** (15 Oct, via video conferencing)

**What / why it matters:** Connectivity, power and heritage projects in a strategic border region; the road adds an alternative corridor to Ladakh.

**Key facts:**
- **Leh airport (Kushok Bakula Rimpochee) new terminal** — ₹640 crore, 23,800 sq m, capacity 35 lakh passengers/yr.
- **Nimmu–Padum–Darcha Road** — 298 km, built by BRO; links Ladakh to Himachal Pradesh via Zanskar Valley; supplements Srinagar–Leh and Manali–Leh highways.
- **Hanu Small Hydro Project (9 MW)** and **66 kV double-circuit Dah–Hanu–Khaltsi transmission line** — grid reliability/power evacuation.
- **India's first Petroglyph (rock painting) Conservation Park**, Sindhu Ghat, Leh — heritage conservation.
- **Project Him Sarovar** — 40 water bodies in 40 villages (reports differ on whether counted among the five or as a sixth item; cost breakdown not given).

**Notebook:** GS3 Infrastructure/Internal Security (border infrastructure); GS1 Art & Culture (rock art); GS3 Energy.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about the two wetlands recently added to India's Ramsar list:
1. Sukhna Wetland in Chandigarh is a man-made reservoir created by damming a seasonal stream.
2. Sauj Jheel is located in Mainpuri district of Uttar Pradesh.
3. With these additions, India has 103 Ramsar sites.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the recently approved strategic disinvestment of IDBI Bank, consider the following statements:
1. The approval was given by the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs.
2. Both the Government of India and LIC are selling part of their shareholding.
3. The transaction is described by the government as the largest FDI in India's financial sector.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The "National Strategic Trade Command Centre" recently launched by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry includes a dashboard on which of the following?
(a) GI Tag registrations (b) SCOMET items (c) Special Economic Zones' land allotment (d) Anti-dumping duties

## Answers
1. **(d)** — All three are correct: Sukhna was created in 1958 by damming the Sukhna Choe; Sauj Jheel is near Kishni, Mainpuri; tally is now 103.
2. **(d)** — CCEA approved; 30.48% (GoI) + 30.24% (LIC) = 60.72% stake; the government called it the largest FDI in the financial sector.
3. **(b)** — The NSTCC has a SCOMET (Special Chemicals, Organisms, Materials, Equipment and Technologies) dashboard.
