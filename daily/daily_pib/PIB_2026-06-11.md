## 1. Cabinet approves "Mission for Cotton Productivity"
**Issuing body:** Union Cabinet → Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare + Ministry of Textiles | **Date:** 10 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS3 — Agriculture

A new five-year mission to make India self-sufficient in cotton and competitive in global textile markets by 2030-31, by closing the gap between India's low cotton yields and global benchmarks. It matters because cotton supports ~32 lakh farmers and is the backbone of India's textile economy, yet productivity has stagnated. Key facts to remember: outlay **₹5,659.22 crore** (2026-27 to 2030-31); raise lint productivity from **~440 kg/ha to 755 kg/ha** by 2031; target **498 lakh bales** (170 kg lint each); initial focus on **140 districts across 14 States**; involves 10 ICAR institutes, AICRP-Cotton centres and ~2,000 ginning/processing units; aligned with the **5F vision (Farm–Fibre–Factory–Fashion–Foreign)**.

**Scheme template:**
- **Name:** Mission for Cotton Productivity
- **Ministry:** Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (with Textiles)
- **Objective:** Self-sufficiency in cotton + global textile competitiveness by 2030-31
- **Key features:** ₹5,659 cr / 5-yr outlay; lint productivity 440→755 kg/ha; tech transfer via ICAR & SAUs in 140 districts; diversify natural-fibre base (flax, ramie, sisal, bamboo, banana)
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** ~32 lakh cotton farmers; 14 major cotton States
- **Significance:** Reduces import dependence on long-staple cotton; supports the 5F textile value chain

## 2. Power distribution turnaround & DigiLocker–DISCOM integration
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Power | **Date:** 11 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS2 Governance / GS3 — Economy (Infrastructure)

PIB highlighted the financial recovery of power DISCOMs and the deepening of digital governance in the sector. Why it matters: DISCOM losses had long been the weakest link in India's power chain; the turnaround and smart-metering push under RDSS are central to energy and fiscal reform. Key facts: DISCOMs posted a **positive PAT of ₹2,701 crore in FY25** (turnaround from a heavy loss the previous year); **AT&C (Aggregate Technical & Commercial) losses fell from 22.62% (FY14) to ~15.04% (FY25)**; **68 DISCOMs/power departments across 35 States/UTs** onboarded on DigiLocker. This sits under the **Revamped Distribution Sector Scheme (RDSS)** — outlay **₹3,03,758 crore** (FY22–FY26), target AT&C losses of **12–15%** pan-India via prepaid smart metering and infrastructure upgrades.

## 3. Historic breakthrough at the Zojila Tunnel
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Road Transport & Highways / NHIDCL | **Date:** 9 June 2026 (reported via PIB) | **Notebook:** GS3 Infrastructure / GS2 — Internal Security

The Zojila Tunnel achieved its **"breakthrough"** — excavation from both ends met — completing over 13 km of mountain tunnelling. Significance: it will give the first **all-weather road link between the Kashmir Valley and Ladakh**, which is currently cut off 5–6 months a year by snow at Zojila Pass; strategically vital for Army logistics to the LAC post-Galwan. Key facts: **~13.15 km bi-directional tunnel** on **NH-1**, between **Baltal and Minamarg**; billed as **Asia's longest bi-directional road tunnel**; project cost **₹6,809 crore**; executed by **MEIL under NHIDCL** (not BRO); revised completion **February 2028**; cuts Sonamarg–Minamarg travel from ~2 hrs to ~30 min. *Note: "breakthrough" = excavation done, not full commissioning.*

## 4. Infrastructure review — "Instrument for Nation Building" (Viksit Bharat 2047)
**Issuing body:** Government of India (multiple ministries via PIB) | **Date:** 10 June 2026 | **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy & Infrastructure

A consolidated government review positioning infrastructure as the foundation of Viksit Bharat 2047 — a goldmine of prelims data points. Worth memorising the headline figures: public **CapEx ₹12.2 lakh crore (FY27)**, up from ₹2 lakh crore (FY15); **railway broad-gauge electrification 99.6%** (May 2026); **installed power capacity 532.74 GW** (March 2026) with **50% non-fossil capacity achieved in June 2025 — 5 years ahead of the Paris NDC**; **metro network 1,155 km** (world's 3rd largest); **Jal Jeevan Mission rural tap-water coverage 81.94%** (extended to 2028); **World Bank LPI rank 38** (2023, from 54 in 2014); **National Highways 1,46,566 km** (March 2026). Key institutional anchors: **PM GatiShakti** (58 ministries, 3,200+ data layers) and the **National Logistics Policy, 2022**.

## 5. PMRC Scheme 2026 — applications opened
**Issuing body:** Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education | **Date:** applications opened 1 June 2026 (PIB reiterated early June) | **Notebook:** GS2 Governance / GS3 — Sci-Tech

The **Prime Minister Research Chair (PMRC) Scheme 2026** aims to draw leading **Indian-origin researchers from abroad** back to premier Indian institutions to lead cutting-edge research — a brain-gain / reverse-migration initiative. Why it matters: links higher-education reform (NEP 2020) with India's deep-tech and innovation goals.

**Scheme template:**
- **Name:** Prime Minister Research Chair (PMRC) Scheme 2026
- **Ministry:** Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education
- **Objective:** Attract accomplished Indian-origin researchers/scientists abroad to undertake research in Indian institutions
- **Key features:** Three categories — **Young Research Fellows, Senior Research Fellows, Research Chairs**; 13 priority sectors (AI, semiconductors, cybersecurity, healthcare, climate tech); host = NIRF Top-100 (Overall/Engineering) or Top-50 (Research) institutions & national labs under **DST/DBT/ICMR/CSIR**
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Indian Nationals abroad, OCI cardholders, PIOs
- **Significance:** Reverse brain-drain; strengthens India's R&D and deep-tech base

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the "Mission for Cotton Productivity" approved by the Union Cabinet in June 2026, consider the following statements:
1. It is to be jointly implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and the Ministry of Textiles.
2. It targets raising cotton lint productivity to 755 kg per hectare by 2030-31.
3. It has a total outlay of over ₹5,000 crore for the period 2026-27 to 2030-31.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about the Zojila Tunnel, which achieved its breakthrough in June 2026:
1. It is being constructed on National Highway-1 between Baltal and Minamarg.
2. It will provide all-weather connectivity between the Kashmir Valley and Ladakh.
3. It is being executed by the Border Roads Organisation (BRO).

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The Prime Minister Research Chair (PMRC) Scheme 2026, recently in the news, is an initiative of which one of the following?
(a) Ministry of Science and Technology
(b) Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education
(c) NITI Aayog
(d) Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All correct. Outlay ₹5,659.22 cr (2026-27 to 2030-31); jointly run by Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and Textiles; lint productivity target 440→755 kg/ha by 2031; aligned with the 5F vision.

**Q2 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** The ~13.15 km tunnel on NH-1 (Baltal–Minamarg) gives all-weather Srinagar–Ladakh connectivity. Statement 3 is wrong — the executing agency is MEIL under NHIDCL, not the BRO.

**Q3 — (b) Department of Higher Education, Ministry of Education.** PMRC 2026 attracts Indian-origin researchers abroad to NIRF top-ranked institutions and DST/DBT/ICMR/CSIR labs across 13 priority sectors.
