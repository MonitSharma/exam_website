## 1. Revised WPI (base 2022-23) & India's new Producer Price Index (PPI) launched
**Issuing body:** Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT — Ministry of Commerce & Industry · **Notebook: GS3 Economy**

India's wholesale-price measurement got its biggest overhaul in over a decade. On 15 June 2026 the revised **WPI** series was released with the base year moved from **2011-12 → 2022-23**, and a brand-new **Producer Price Index (PPI)** was debuted. Why it matters: PPI is a globally standard inflation gauge (it tracks prices received by producers, including services, which WPI ignores); its introduction modernises India's price statistics and will eventually replace WPI.

Key facts to remember:
- WPI basket items expanded from **697 → 957**; electricity category now includes **solar, wind and nuclear**.
- **PPI has three indices:** Output PPI, Input PPI, Service PPI. Service PPI covers seven sectors (Banking, Insurance, Railways, Telecom, etc.).
- WPI and PPI will **run in parallel for 5 years**, after which WPI is discontinued.

## 2. India–France Innovation Roadmap 2030 adopted (PM Modi's France visit)
**Issuing body:** Prime Minister's Office / MEA · **Notebook: GS2 IR** (cross-tag GS3 Sci-Tech)

During the first leg of PM Modi's France visit on **14 June 2026**, the two countries adopted the **India–France Innovation Roadmap 2030** along with **13 outcomes** spanning AI, startups, space, education, semiconductors, agritech, medtech, renewable energy and defence. A **Joint India–France AI Working Group** will be set up to advance cooperation on AI and its global governance.

Key facts to remember:
- Builds on the **17 February 2026** decision to elevate ties to a **"Special Global Strategic Partnership"** and launch the **India–France Year of Innovation 2026**.
- Showcased alongside **Bharat Innovates 2026** at Nice (Palais des Expositions): 120 startups, 20+ Institutes of Excellence across 13 critical-tech domains.

## 3. Census 2027 — Phase-I (Houselisting) completed in Maharashtra
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Home Affairs (Office of RGI) · **Notebook: GS2 Governance/Polity** (cross-tag GS1 Society)

Maharashtra completed the **Houselisting & Housing Census (Phase-I)** of Census 2027, conducted **16 May – 14 June 2026**. This is significant because **Census 2027 is India's first fully digital census**, using a mobile app for field data, a citizen Self-Enumeration facility, and real-time monitoring.

Key facts to remember:
- Self-enumeration window in Maharashtra: 1–15 May 2026 (optional).
- ~**2.64 lakh** enumerators/supervisors deployed statewide.
- Monitoring via the **CMMS (Census Monitoring & Management System)** portal.
- Census 2027 reference dates: **1 October 2026** for snow-bound/Himalayan areas; **1 March 2027** for the rest of the country (Phase-II population enumeration).

## 4. WT-MARUT — India's first Wind Turbine Supply Chain Management Portal
**Issuing body:** Ministry of New & Renewable Energy (MNRE) · **Notebook: GS3 Environment/Energy**

At the **Global Wind Day 2026** conference in **Goa** (theme: *"Wind Energy: From Ambition to Acceleration"*) on 15 June 2026, Union Minister **Pralhad Joshi** launched **WT-MARUT**, India's first dedicated wind-turbine supply chain management portal, to strengthen domestic manufacturing and exports.

Key facts to remember:
- Minister urged industry to raise the wind-sector **localisation target to 85% by 2030**.
- Supported by industry bodies IWTMA, WIPPA and IWPA.

*Quick initiative snapshot:* **Name** – WT-MARUT Portal · **Ministry** – MNRE · **Objective** – map & strengthen the domestic wind-turbine supply chain, boost local manufacturing/exports · **Key features** – online supply-chain database, localisation tracking, industry coordination · **Significance** – supports India's clean-energy/Viksit Bharat goals and Atmanirbharta in wind manufacturing.

## 5. National Sports Board — Search-cum-Selection Committee constituted
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports · **Notebook: GS2 Polity/Governance**

The Centre constituted the **Search-cum-Selection Committee** under the **National Sports Governance Act, 2025** to recommend the leadership of the new **National Sports Board (NSB)** — operationalising the Act's reform of sports administration.

Key facts to remember:
- Committee is **chaired by the Cabinet Secretary**; includes Secretary (Sports), a sports-administration expert and two national sports-award recipients.
- It recommends a panel for the **Chairperson and members of the NSB**.
- The Act also establishes a **National Sports Tribunal** for speedy resolution of sports disputes, and seeks to bring National Sports Federations under a transparent governance framework.
