## 1. Ministry of External Affairs / PMO: **India–Poland — official visit of Polish PM Donald Tusk; trade-doubling target by 2030** (22 Oct 2026, New Delhi)

**What & why it matters:** Talks between PM Modi and PM Tusk upgraded the agenda across trade, defence, technology and security. Poland is India's key economic partner in Central Europe and a gateway to EU markets and the defence-industrial supply chain; the outcomes signal diversification of India's Europe outreach beyond Western Europe.

**Key facts (from the PMO outcomes list):**
- Target of **doubling bilateral trade by 2030**.
- **Poland joins the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (CDRI)** — India-led global coalition launched at the UN Climate Action Summit, 2019.
- A **Defence Industry Dialogue** to be established; a **dialogue on counter-terrorism** agreed.
- Agreed to expand cooperation in AI, cybersecurity, education, culture, sports and tourism; deeper ties in defence, space and nuclear energy, with Polish firms invited to co-develop/co-produce; an **India–Poland StartUp Bridge** and youth tech hackathons; an **Indian Studies Chair in Kraków**.
- The outcomes list records no signed MoUs or monetary values.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (India–Europe; bilateral groupings).

## 2. Ministry of Law & Justice (23rd Law Commission of India): **National Conference on Co-operative Federalism and Law Reforms, New Delhi** (inaugurated by Vice-President C.P. Radhakrishnan; ~22 Oct 2026)

**What & why it matters:** The Law Commission convened State Law Commissions, jurists, former judges, Bar and academia to strengthen coordination between the Centre and States on law reform while respecting State autonomy — relevant to the Concurrent List, uniform vs. regional laws, and the Commission's advisory role.

**Key facts:** Organised by the 23rd Law Commission; Union Law & Justice Minister Arjun Ram Meghwal present; themes — law reforms, co-operative federalism, constitutional governance, institutional frameworks for administration of justice. The Law Commission is a **non-statutory, executive-constituted advisory body** (constituted for fixed terms by the Government via gazette notification). The exact conference date was inferred from a 21 Oct pre-announcement ("tomorrow") and the VP Secretariat's 22 Oct release (secondary source for details).

**Notebook:** GS2 — Polity/Governance (federalism; judiciary & law reform).

## 3. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment: **Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA) — outreach crosses ~35 crore people** (UPDATE — earlier figures were 34.54 crore as of 14 Sep 2026)

**What & why it matters:** MoSJE reports that the campaign's reach has grown to roughly **35 crore** people, with a stated aim to eliminate the drug trade by 2029-end (secondary source for the latest figure; 14 Sep figures are from the Ministry via All India Radio).

**Key facts:**
- As of 14 Sep 2026: **34.54 crore** citizens reached, ~**13.70 crore youth**, ~**10.64 crore women**, **1.67 lakh+ Nasha Mukti Mitras**, **31.40 lakh+** outreach activities; a **National Mass Pledge** targeted 10 crore+ participants around the 6th anniversary.
- Launched **15 Aug 2020** in the most vulnerable districts; implemented under the umbrella **National Action Plan for Drug Demand Reduction (NAPDDR)**; national de-addiction helpline **14446**.
- Critique: "reach" measures awareness, not outcomes; treatment capacity and the NDPS Act's treatment of personal use remain debated.

**Scheme-in-focus — Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan (NMBA)**
- **Ministry:** Social Justice & Empowerment (with Health, Home, Education, NCB and others).
- **Objective:** Demand reduction and awareness; community-based prevention and treatment linkage.
- **Key features:** (1) Awareness in educational institutions and campuses; (2) Community outreach through volunteers (Nasha Mukti Mitras); (3) Focus on treatment facilities and counselling via Integrated Rehabilitation Centres for Addicts; (4) Helpline 14446 and mass pledges.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Pan-India, targeting youth, women, students and vulnerable communities.
- **Significance:** Public-health approach to drugs; links to SDG 3, internal security (narco-terror) and the NDPS framework.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Social Justice / GS3 — Internal Security.

## 4. Ministry of Education (Dept. of Higher Education): **Kashi Tamil Sangamam 5.0 — registration portal opened; edition on 28 Nov–12 Dec 2026** (portal launched 22 Oct)

**What & why it matters:** A civilisational-linkage programme under **Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat (EBSB)** connecting Tamil Nadu with Kashi; this edition's theme is **"Thirukkural Payilvom – Thirukkural Abhyas Karen"** (introducing the Thirukkural to learners nationally).

**Key facts:**
- Organisers: Department of Higher Education with **IIT Madras, BHU, the Central Institute of Classical Tamil (CICT)** and the Uttar Pradesh Government.
- ~**1,400 participants from Tamil Nadu** in seven groups of ~200 (students, teachers, writers/media, agriculture & allied, professionals & artisans, women, spiritual leaders); eight-day tours covering **Varanasi, Prayagraj and Ayodhya**.
- Registration at kashitamil.iitm.ac.in until **13 Nov 2026**. First edition: 2022.
- Static link: **Tamil was the first language declared Classical (2004)**; 11 languages now hold the status (five added Oct 2024).

**Notebook:** GS1 — Art & Culture / Society (cultural integration); GS2 — Education.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the India–Poland official visit of October 2026, consider the following:
1. Poland joined the Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure (CDRI).
2. The two sides set a target of doubling bilateral trade by 2030.
3. Both sides signed a Free Trade Agreement.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the Law Commission of India, consider the following statements:
1. It is a constitutional body established under Article 124.
2. It is constituted by the Government of India for a fixed tenure and functions in an advisory capacity.
3. Its recommendations are binding on Parliament.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** Kashi Tamil Sangamam, whose fifth edition's registrations opened in October 2026, is organised under which broader initiative?
(a) Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat
(b) Swachh Bharat Mission
(c) Smart Cities Mission
(d) Mission Karmayogi

## Answers
1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are in the PMO outcomes list; no FTA was signed (no signed agreements were listed).
2. **(b)** — The Law Commission is a non-statutory, non-constitutional advisory body constituted by executive notification; its reports are not binding.
3. **(a)** — Kashi Tamil Sangamam is implemented under Ek Bharat Shreshtha Bharat by the Ministry of Education.
