## 1. National Framework and Operational Guidelines for strengthening ITDAs and ITDPs released

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Tribal Affairs, jointly with NITI Aayog (released by Union Minister Shri Jual Oram and Dr. R. Balasubramaniam, Member, NITI Aayog)

**What it is:** Two companion documents — a *National Framework* and *Operational Guidelines* — to reform the functioning of **Integrated Tribal Development Agencies (ITDAs)** and **Integrated Tribal Development Projects (ITDPs)**, the field-level institutions that actually deliver tribal development schemes at the last mile. This is the first systematic national attempt to standardise and rank these bodies.

**Why it matters:** ITDAs/ITDPs are a legacy of the **Tribal Sub-Plan (TSP) approach introduced in the Fifth Five Year Plan (1974–79)**, but have functioned unevenly across States with no common performance yardstick. The framework shifts them from a uniform template to **context-specific, accountable institutions**, and positions them as the vehicle for *saturation* of entitlements — the same delivery logic the government applies to Aspirational Districts.

**Key facts and figures:**

- Exercise begun in **April 2026**; covered **214 ITDAs/ITDPs across 17 States and UTs**.
- Three design principles built into the reform architecture: **institutional ranking**, **real-time data-driven monitoring**, and **convergence** across departments.
- Framing: evidence-based administrative intervention; ITDAs as vehicles for **saturation of entitlements at the last mile**.
- Static anchor for prelims: ITDAs operate in **Scheduled Areas** under the Fifth Schedule; the constitutional backing for special tribal-area administration is **Articles 244, 275(1)** and the Fifth/Sixth Schedules.

**Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (welfare schemes for vulnerable sections); GS1 — Society (tribal issues).

## 2. DRI dismantles a syndicate misusing SAFTA concessions in areca nut imports

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Finance — **Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI)**, Department of Revenue

**What it is:** A month-long, intelligence-led DRI operation broke a network that imported **South-East Asian areca nut (supari)** into India while **falsely declaring it as Bangladeshi origin** to claim concessional duty under the **South Asian Free Trade Area (SAFTA)**.

**Why it matters:** This is the cleanest recent illustration of **rules-of-origin abuse** — the single biggest enforcement weakness in preferential trade agreements. Because Bangladesh is an **LDC** under SAFTA, it enjoys deeper tariff concessions; the syndicate exploited exactly that differential. Areca nut is also a politically sensitive crop (Karnataka, Kerala, Assam) protected by a **Minimum Import Price**, so the fraud has a domestic-farmer angle as well as a revenue one.

**Key facts and figures:**

- Estimated revenue loss to the exchequer: **more than ₹2,500 crore**.
- **Nine persons arrested**.
- *Modus operandi:* consignments sourced from **Indonesia, Thailand, Malaysia** and other South-East Asian countries, routed through an **Export Processing Zone (EPZ) in Bangladesh**, re-stuffed into different containers and bags, then shipped to India as Bangladeshi-origin goods with **fraudulently obtained Certificates of Origin**.
- Static anchor: SAFTA was signed at the **12th SAARC Summit, Islamabad (2004)** and came into force on **1 January 2006**; India's domestic guardrail is the **CAROTAR, 2020** rules (Customs — Administration of Rules of Origin under Trade Agreements).

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (external sector, trade agreements) and Internal Security (economic offences); GS2 — IR (SAARC/SAFTA).

## 3. NIELIT launches 'CYBER KUSHTI 2026' — a hackathon that scores judgment, not detection speed

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Electronics & IT — **NIELIT** (National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology), with the **ISAC Foundation** under the **National Security Database (NSD)**; **CERT-In** as knowledge partner

**What it is:** A national cybersecurity-and-AI hackathon with an unusual scoring premise. Instead of a conventional Capture-The-Flag or vulnerability-hunting format, every team receives the **same deliberately imperfect Security Assessment Package** and is judged on how well it **triages and prioritises** the findings.

**Why it matters:** As AI security tooling floods analysts with automated findings, the scarce skill is separating **genuine threats from false positives** — this is the first Indian government competition explicitly designed around that. It is a concrete data point for the "AI changes the nature of cyber work" argument in GS3 essays and answers.

**Key facts and figures:**

- Official **parallel technical track of the 3rd National Conference on Cyber Security, Digital Forensics and Intelligence (NCCDFI 2026)**.
- Assessment package contains **AI-generated findings, source code, application logs, architecture documents, static analysis reports, dependency scans and secrets scans**.
- **Registration opened 15 August 2026 and runs to 10 September 2026**.
- Static anchor: **CERT-In** is the national nodal agency for cyber incident response under **Section 70B of the IT Act, 2000**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology / Internal Security (cybersecurity, awareness and capacity building).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding Integrated Tribal Development Agencies (ITDAs) and Integrated Tribal Development Projects (ITDPs):

1. They originated as the institutional arm of the Tribal Sub-Plan approach adopted during the Fifth Five Year Plan.
2. The National Framework released in August 2026 introduces institutional ranking and real-time monitoring for these bodies.
3. The framework was released jointly by the Ministry of Tribal Affairs and the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** With reference to the South Asian Free Trade Area (SAFTA), consider the following statements:

1. It was signed at the 12th SAARC Summit and entered into force in January 2006.
2. It provides for deeper tariff concessions for member countries designated as Least Developed Countries.
3. India's domestic mechanism to verify origin claims under such trade agreements is the CAROTAR Rules, 2020.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** 'CYBER KUSHTI 2026', seen in the news recently, is:

(a) A joint India–Japan cyber defence exercise conducted by the National Security Council Secretariat
(b) A national cybersecurity and AI hackathon launched by NIELIT in which teams are assessed on prioritising findings in a common security assessment package
(c) A CERT-In advisory framework mandating incident reporting by critical information infrastructure within six hours
(d) A MeitY scheme providing financial incentives for domestic manufacturing of hardware security modules

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.**
ITDAs/ITDPs are the field institutions created under the Tribal Sub-Plan approach of the Fifth Five Year Plan (1974–79), so statement 1 is correct. The 2026 framework does build in institutional ranking, real-time data-driven monitoring and convergence, so statement 2 is correct. Statement 3 is wrong — it was released jointly by the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs and NITI Aayog**, not the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

**Q2 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.**
SAFTA was signed at the 12th SAARC Summit (Islamabad, 2004) and came into force on 1 January 2006. It gives LDC members — Bangladesh, Nepal, Bhutan, Maldives (at the time of signing), Afghanistan — a longer tariff-liberalisation timeline and deeper concessions, which is precisely the differential the areca-nut syndicate exploited. CAROTAR 2020 is India's rules-of-origin verification mechanism for FTA imports.

**Q3 — (b).**
CYBER KUSHTI 2026 is NIELIT's national cybersecurity and AI hackathon, run as the parallel technical track of NCCDFI 2026 with ISAC Foundation and CERT-In as knowledge partner. Its distinguishing feature is that all teams get the same deliberately imperfect assessment package and are scored on judgment and prioritisation rather than detection speed.
