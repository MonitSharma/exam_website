## 1. Ministry of Science & Technology (DBT / BIRAC): **India's first commercial-scale bio-based biodegradable plastic facility launched under BioE3**

A 100 TPA pilot-scale R&D facility for **Polylactic Acid (PLA) and co-polymers** was launched at Kumbhi, Uttar Pradesh, inside the complex of **Balrampur Chini Mills Limited (BCML)**. It converts sugar-based feedstock into compostable bioplastic, moving India from imported PLA towards indigenous biomanufacturing. This is the first commercial translation of the **BioE3 Policy** framework into a working plant.

**Why it matters:** PLA is the main drop-in substitute for single-use fossil plastics. Domestic PLA capacity links three policy goals at once — plastic-waste reduction under the EPR regime, "waste to wealth" use of sugar-industry by-products, and reduced import dependence in advanced materials.

**Key facts**
- Launched **24 September 2026**; supported by a **₹75 crore BIRAC grant**.
- Capacity: **100 tonnes per annum**, pilot/R&D scale.
- **BioE3** = *Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment* — the policy approved in 2024 to build high-performance biomanufacturing.
- India's **bioeconomy has grown from about US$10 billion (2014) to nearly US$195 billion**.
- Launched by **Dr. Jitendra Singh** (MoS, Science & Technology and Earth Sciences); present were Dr. Rajesh S. Gokhale (Secretary, DBT and Chairman, BIRAC) and Dr. Dhananjay K. Tiwary (MD, BIRAC).

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (biotechnology, biomanufacturing); cross-tag GS3 Environment (plastic waste).

## 2. Ministry of Defence (DRDO): **First high-value deep-tech agreement under the TDF Scheme — an indigenous 20 millikelvin dilution refrigerator**

DRDO signed its first high-value deep-technology agreement under the **Technology Development Fund (TDF)** with **Zero mK India Private Limited**, a Rajasthan-based start-up, to develop an indigenous **20 mK dilution refrigerator**. **Solid State Physics Laboratory (SSPL)**, a DRDO lab, provides technical mentorship and monitoring.

**Why it matters:** A dilution refrigerator is not a quantum computer but is the *enabling infrastructure* for superconducting qubit architectures — it holds hardware at a few thousandths of a degree above absolute zero so fragile quantum states survive long enough to compute. India currently imports these systems, which is a strategic vulnerability when the end use is sensitive defence and cryptographic research. This is a direct industrial feed into the **National Quantum Mission**.

**Key facts**
- Agreement signed **24 September 2026**.
- **TDF corpus: ₹500 crore**; the scheme funds Indian industry, especially MSMEs and start-ups, to develop defence technologies, with DRDO as the implementing agency.
- Target: **20 millikelvin (0.02 K)** operating temperature.
- **Rajnath Singh** (Defence Minister) said the partnership would set a precedent for future DRDO–industry collaboration; Defence Secretary **Rajesh Kumar Singh** also spoke.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (quantum technology, indigenisation); cross-tag GS3 Internal Security (defence R&D ecosystem).

## 3. Ministry of Mines: **PIB backgrounder on PMKKKY — District Mineral Foundation spending crosses ₹1.09 lakh crore sanctioned**

PIB issued a consolidated backgrounder on the **Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY)**, the framework that channels mining revenue back into mining-affected districts through **District Mineral Foundations (DMFs)**.

**Why it matters:** DMFs are one of the largest untied development pools available to district administrations in India, and the standard exam angle is their statutory basis and the 70% high-priority earmark. The backgrounder also flags the shift from "how much was spent" to outcome-based convergence with the Aspirational District Programme.

**Key facts**
- **Sanctioned: ₹1,09,938 crore across 4,70,020 projects** (as of July 2026).
- **Completed: ₹49,973 crore / 2,92,156 projects**; **ongoing: ₹30,512 crore / 78,809 projects**.
- DMFs now exist in **656 districts across 23 States**.
- **January 2024 guideline revision** shifted PMKKKY towards targeted, transparent, outcome-oriented development; **July 2025** launched the **Aspirational DMF Programme**.

### Scheme-in-focus: **Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY)**
- **Ministry:** Ministry of Mines. **Launched:** 17 September 2015.
- **Objective:** Use DMF funds to mitigate the adverse effects of mining on people and the environment in mining-affected areas, and to create durable livelihood assets there.
- **Key features:** (i) implemented through **DMFs — statutory trusts under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957**, funded by a levy on royalty paid by mine lease holders; (ii) **at least 70% of funds** must go to **high-priority sectors** (drinking water, environment, health, education, welfare of women and children, skill development, sanitation, the aged and differently abled), the rest to other priority sectors (physical infrastructure, irrigation, energy, watershed); (iii) 2024 guidelines mandate outcome orientation and convergence with Central/State schemes; (iv) **Aspirational DMF Programme** aligns DMF spending with Aspirational District Programme indicators.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** communities in **656 districts, 23 States**.
- **Significance:** converts a natural-resource rent into a decentralised, district-level development fund — a live example of fiscal federalism plus the "polluter pays"/benefit-sharing principle in India's mineral governance.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 — Governance (centrally sponsored frameworks, district-level fund devolution); cross-tag GS3 Economy (mineral sector).

## 4. Ministry of Steel: **Steel demand stays robust — finished steel consumption at 70.5 MT in April–August 2026, up 7.4%**

The Ministry of Steel released the sector's performance data for the first five months of FY 2026-27. Consumption growth is comfortably ahead of production growth, and both imports and exports rose sharply.

**Why it matters:** Steel consumption is the standard proxy for construction and capital-formation momentum, so a 7.4% rise signals infrastructure-led demand holding up. The bigger exam-relevant tension is in the trade numbers — imports grew 29.5% with China, South Korea and Japan supplying about three-quarters of them, which is the backdrop to India's safeguard-duty and quality-control-order debates.

**Key facts (April–August 2026)**
- **Finished steel consumption: 70.5 MT (+7.4% y-o-y)**.
- **Crude steel production: 71 MT (+2.8%)**; **finished steel production: 68.3 MT (+4.1%)**.
- Hot metal **40.4 MT (+2.9%)**; sponge iron **25.4 MT (+1.9%)** — India is the world's largest sponge-iron producer.
- **Imports: 3.49 MT (+29.5%), ₹36,194 crore** — from **China 31.8%, South Korea 29.8%, Japan 14.2%**.
- **Exports: 2.99 MT (+34.1%), ₹23,647 crore** — to **Vietnam 13.9%, UAE 13%, Italy 9.7%**.
- India remains a **net importer** of finished steel in this period.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Economy (infrastructure, industrial performance, trade).

## 5. Ministry of Culture: **BRICS Theatre Festival 2026 announced — nine countries in New Delhi, 12–14 October**

The Ministry of Culture will host the **BRICS Theatre Festival 2026** in New Delhi from **12 to 14 October 2026**, with the **National School of Drama (NSD) as nodal agency**. Theme: **"Shared Stories, Shared Futures."**

**Why it matters:** This is the cultural-diplomacy limb of **India's BRICS Presidency 2026** — the deliverable flowing from the **Bhopal Declaration** adopted at the BRICS Culture Ministers' Meeting in August 2026 and welcomed in the BRICS Leaders' Declaration. For GS2 it is a clean example of soft power institutionalised through a multilateral grouping; for GS1 it is an instance of state patronage of performing arts.

**Key facts**
- **Dates: 12–14 October 2026**; venues include the **National School of Drama, Kamani Auditorium, LTG Auditorium and Meghdoot Theatre**, New Delhi.
- **Nine BRICS member and partner countries**: India, Russia, South Africa, Iran, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Kazakhstan, Ethiopia.
- **Over 100 theatre practitioners, scholars and cultural representatives**; India presents two productions, Russia sends two theatre groups.
- Mandate covers **classical, contemporary and experimental** theatre plus workshops on performance traditions and stagecraft.
- **Vivek Aggarwal**, Secretary, Ministry of Culture, called it "an important cultural engagement under India's BRICS Presidency."

**Destination notebook:** GS1 — Art & Culture (performing arts, institutions); cross-tag GS2 IR (BRICS, cultural diplomacy).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the **BioE3 Policy** and the facility recently launched under it at Kumbhi, Uttar Pradesh, consider the following statements:

1. The facility produces Polylactic Acid (PLA) and its co-polymers from sugar-based feedstock.
2. "BioE3" stands for Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment.
3. The grant support was extended by the Biotechnology Industry Research Assistance Council (BIRAC).

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** With reference to **District Mineral Foundations (DMFs)** and the Pradhan Mantri Khanij Kshetra Kalyan Yojana (PMKKKY), consider the following statements:

1. DMFs are statutory trusts established under the Mines and Minerals (Development and Regulation) Act, 1957.
2. Under PMKKKY guidelines, at least 70% of DMF funds are to be used for high-priority sectors such as drinking water supply, health and education.
3. PMKKKY is administered by the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** The **Technology Development Fund (TDF) Scheme**, recently in the news for financing an indigenous 20 millikelvin dilution refrigerator, is implemented by:

(a) The Department of Science and Technology, Ministry of Science and Technology
(b) The Defence Research and Development Organisation, Ministry of Defence
(c) The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology
(d) NITI Aayog, through the Atal Innovation Mission

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct. The 100 TPA pilot facility at Balrampur Chini Mills' Kumbhi complex makes PLA and co-polymers from sugar-based feedstock, backed by a ₹75 crore BIRAC grant; BioE3 stands for Biotechnology for Economy, Environment and Employment.

**Q2 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** DMFs are statutory trusts under the MMDR Act, 1957, and PMKKKY earmarks at least 70% of funds for high-priority sectors. Statement 3 is wrong — PMKKKY is administered by the **Ministry of Mines**, not the Ministry of Panchayati Raj.

**Q3 — (b) The Defence Research and Development Organisation, Ministry of Defence.** TDF is a DRDO-implemented scheme (₹500 crore corpus) that funds Indian industry, especially MSMEs and start-ups, to develop defence technologies; the dilution refrigerator project is with Zero mK India Pvt. Ltd., mentored by DRDO's Solid State Physics Laboratory.
