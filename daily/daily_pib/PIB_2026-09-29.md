## 1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs: **MoU with IGNCA to preserve, document and promote tribal cultural heritage** (28 Sep)

**What it is:** The Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA) and the **Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA)** signed an MoU in New Delhi. Secretary (MoTA) **Ranjana Chopra** attended, and IGNCA was represented by Head of Department **K. Anil Kumar**. The MoU links MoTA's network of **Tribal Research Institutes (TRIs)** with IGNCA's academic, archival and curatorial expertise.

**Why it matters:** Tribal languages, oral traditions and art forms are disappearing quickly. The MoU sets up a formal structure for documenting and digitising them, covering both tangible and intangible heritage, which links to the Eighth Schedule, endangered-languages and cultural-rights debates.

**Key facts:**
- Scope: identify and document tribal arts, music, instruments, crafts, cuisines and **languages**. It gives priority to **endangered tribal languages** and indigenous art forms.
- Outputs: dedicated galleries, **digital museums** and archives, exhibitions, publications and youth awareness camps in educational institutions.
- It also documents oral traditions, folklore and **traditional ecological knowledge**, and showcases tribal contributions to the **freedom struggle**.
- IGNCA is an autonomous body under the **Ministry of Culture**.

**Notebook:** GS1 – Art & Culture (tribal heritage); GS2 – Welfare of vulnerable sections (STs).

## 2. Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment: **PM-AJAY stocktake — 47,243 SC-majority villages covered; 18,093 declared Adarsh Grams** (factsheet, 28 Sep)

**What it is:** A government factsheet reviews the **Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana**. The scheme merged three older schemes into a single umbrella for the socio-economic development of Scheduled Castes. The figures are as of **8 September 2026**.

**Why it matters:** The scheme works at two levels. It saturates SC-majority villages with infrastructure and also pays for individual or group livelihood assets. The newly released outcome data is useful for GS2 answers on the welfare of vulnerable sections.

### Scheme-in-focus: **PM-AJAY (Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana)**
- **Ministry:** Social Justice & Empowerment (Department of Social Justice & Empowerment).
- **Objective:** Reduce poverty among SCs through livelihood and skill support, and improve socio-economic indicators in SC-majority villages.
- **Key features:**
  - Launched in **2021–22**. It merged **PM Adarsh Gram Yojana (PMAGY)**, **Special Central Assistance to the Scheduled Castes Sub-Plan (SCA to SCSP)** and the **Babu Jagjivan Ram Chhatrawas Yojana (BJRCY)**.
  - Three components: **Adarsh Gram** (village development), **Grants-in-aid** for district/state projects (skilling, income-generating assets, infrastructure), and **hostels** for SC students.
  - Village eligibility: **≥40% SC population** and a total population of at least **500**. Individual grants-in-aid go to families with income up to **₹2.5 lakh a year**.
  - A **PM-AJAY mobile app and portal** handle real-time workflow and milestone-linked fund release.
- **Coverage/figures (as of 8 Sep 2026):** **47,243 villages** covered, **18,093** declared Adarsh Grams, **27,032** Village Development Plans prepared, **46,944** works completed. **₹1,730+ crore** released to States (FY22–FY26), and **2.5 lakh+** individual and group beneficiaries received skilling or livelihood support. **785 hostels** completed.
- **Significance:** Consolidates fragmented SC schemes, uses the village as the unit of saturation, and ties fund release to outcomes.

**Notebook:** GS2 – Governance / Welfare schemes for vulnerable sections (SCs).

## 3. Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways: **Foundation stone laid for India's first port-based e-methanol plant at Kandla** (26 Sep)

**What it is:** A **₹2,300-crore** plant at **Deendayal Port (Kandla), Gujarat**. It is being developed by the **Deendayal Port Authority (DPA)** and **Assam Petro-Chemicals Ltd (APCL)** and will make **150 tonnes per day** of e-methanol from renewable power, water and biogenic CO₂.

**Why it matters:** Methanol is one of the main candidate fuels for decarbonising shipping. Producing it at the port makes Kandla a green **bunkering** point on the Asia–Europe route. This links to India's green-shipping agenda and the **green hydrogen hub** at Kandla.

**Key facts:**
- Funding split **76:24 (DPA:APCL)**. DPA provides equity (~₹567 crore), 75 acres of land, desalinated water and green hydrogen.
- **Phase I:** 50 TPD by **January 2027** (~₹1,200 crore). **Phase II:** another 100 TPD by **March 2027** (~₹1,100 crore).
- Target production cost is about **US$750/tonne**, against a reported global figure of about US$1,300/tonne. The project is expected to create 3,500+ direct and indirect jobs.
- Kandla is one of India's designated **Green Hydrogen Hubs**.

**Notebook:** GS3 – Economy (Infrastructure: ports) / Environment (clean fuels, maritime decarbonisation).

## 4. Ministry of Defence (Indian Air Force): **Exercise TARANG SHAKTI 2026 — operational phase begins at Jodhpur** (26 Sep–12 Oct)

**What it is:** This is the **second edition** of the IAF-hosted multinational air exercise. The first was held in **2024**. The opening ceremony took place on **26 September** at **Air Force Station Jodhpur**, and flying operations began on **27 September**. Theme: **"Power in Partnership"**.

**Why it matters:** India is using a flagship multilateral air exercise to build interoperability with Quad, European and Gulf partners and with its neighbours. The exercise also supports defence diplomacy and showcases indigenous platforms.

**Key facts:**
- Participating air forces (as reported by ANI/DD): **Australia, USA, UAE, France, Germany, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh**. Several other countries are attending as **observers**. *(One summary site reports 8 participants and 32 observers; confirm on PIB before memorising.)*
- Aircraft include **F-35**, **Rafale**, **F-16**, **A400M**, **MRTT**, **C-130/C-130J** and **KC-30A** tankers.
- Alongside the flying: seminars, industry tours, and cultural and yoga events.

**Notebook:** GS3 – Internal Security / Defence; GS2 – IR (defence diplomacy).

## 5. Ministry of Road Transport & Highways (PIB backgrounder): **Green Highways — 3.61 crore saplings; new NHAI plantation-survival benchmarks tied to project completion**

**What it is:** A PIB backgrounder on the **Green Highways (Plantation, Transplantation, Beautification & Maintenance) Policy, 2015**. It comes with new **NHAI guidelines (September 2026)** that link plantation performance to contractual milestones.

**Why it matters:** It moves the focus from counting saplings to checking whether they survive. The measure is tied to payments and completion certificates, which is a useful example of outcome-based environmental compliance in infrastructure.

**Key facts:**
- The policy reserves **1% of civil project cost** for plantation and maintenance.
- **3.61 crore+ saplings** planted along about **1.32 lakh km** of NHs (as of July 2026). The NH network grew from **91,287 km (2014)** to **1,46,572 km (March 2026)**.
- New NHAI norms: **≥80%** of the plantation right-of-way must be covered, and **≥90% sapling survival** is needed for a **provisional completion certificate**. The same survival benchmark applies through the O&M period.
- Innovations: **Miyawaki** dense plantations, and **"Trees for Bees"** pollinator corridors of native flowering species along the Delhi–Amritsar–Katra Expressway. Highways also use recycled materials such as fly ash, C&D waste, plastic waste and steel slag.

**Notebook:** GS3 – Environment (afforestation, sustainable infrastructure) / Economy (infrastructure).

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** Pradhan Mantri Anusuchit Jaati Abhyuday Yojana (PM-AJAY) was formed by merging which of the following schemes?
1. Pradhan Mantri Adarsh Gram Yojana
2. Special Central Assistance to Scheduled Castes Sub-Plan
3. Babu Jagjivan Ram Chhatrawas Yojana
4. Post-Matric Scholarship for Scheduled Castes

Select the correct answer using the code below:
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1, 2 and 3 only
(c) 2, 3 and 4 only
(d) 1, 2, 3 and 4

**Q2.** With reference to the e-methanol plant recently started at Deendayal Port, Kandla, consider the following statements:
1. It will produce methanol from renewable electricity, water and biogenic carbon dioxide.
2. It is being developed entirely by a foreign private shipping company under a concession.
3. One use of the fuel is bunkering of ships as part of maritime decarbonisation.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** "Tarang Shakti", recently in the news, is:
(a) a bilateral naval exercise between India and France
(b) a multinational air exercise hosted by the Indian Air Force
(c) an indigenous hypersonic missile test programme of DRDO
(d) a joint Army–Air Force disaster-relief drill with Bangladesh

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — PM-AJAY (launched 2021–22) merged PMAGY, SCA to SCSP and BJRCY. The Post-Matric Scholarship for SCs is a separate scheme.
2. **(b)** — The plant uses renewable power, water and biogenic CO₂ to supply green marine fuel for bunkering. It is a public-sector venture of the Deendayal Port Authority (76%) and Assam Petro-Chemicals Ltd (24%).
3. **(b)** — Tarang Shakti is the IAF-hosted multinational air exercise. The 2026 edition, its second, is being held at Jodhpur from 26 Sep to 12 Oct.
