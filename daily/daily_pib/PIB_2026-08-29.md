## 1. Ministry of Tribal Affairs holds the first **National Conclave on Tribal Languages & Museums**; **Mysuru Declaration** adopted

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Tribal Affairs (MoTA), with the Karnataka State Tribal Research Institute (KSTRI) and the Central Institute of Indian Languages (CIIL), Mysuru.

A three-day national conclave at Mysuru brought together tribal research institutes, linguists and museum curators to do two things at once — revive endangered tribal languages and turn Tribal Freedom Fighter Museums (TFFMs) into working knowledge centres rather than static galleries. It matters because language loss and the thinness of tribal historiography are the two structural gaps in India's tribal-welfare architecture that money alone does not close.

**Key facts**

- Inaugurated by MoS **Durgadas Uikey**; held **24–26 August 2026** at Mysuru, Karnataka.
- Adopted the **Mysuru Declaration** on documentation, preservation and revitalisation of tribal languages.
- Launched the **upgraded Adi Vaani platform** and the **"Jago" AI conversational assistant**; released the **Jenukuruba primer** and other tribal-language learning resources.
- **MoU signed between MoTA and CIIL** for tribal-language documentation and promotion.
- Context on Adi Vaani: India's first AI-powered translator for tribal languages, built by a consortium led by **IIT Delhi** with BITS Pilani, IIIT Hyderabad and IIIT Naya Raipur; beta covered **Santali, Bhili, Mundari and Gondi**, with Kui and Garo in development.
- TFFMs use AI-curated archival content, projection storytelling and immersive AV modules across **seven museums**.

**Notebook:** GS1 Society (tribal communities, language & culture) + GS2 Governance (welfare of STs).

## 2. **'Sagar Samvad'** — National Shipping Board's inaugural dialogue sets a five-point roadmap and a **100-vessel** target

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways (MoPSW); National Shipping Board (NSB).

India carries the overwhelming bulk of its own EXIM cargo on foreign-flag ships, and the foreign-exchange leakage from that is now being framed as a national-security-adjacent economic problem. The inaugural Sagar Samvad is the first structured attempt to put a number and a mechanism on fixing it.

**Key facts**

- Chaired by Union Minister **Sarbananda Sonowal**; held in New Delhi; theme — *"Charting the Roadmap Towards Maritime India Vision 2030 and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047."*
- Headline target: **add 100 vessels** to India's merchant fleet over the next five years; India's flag currently carries about **1,600 vessels**.
- The stated problem being solved: an estimated **USD 75 billion foreign freight bill**.
- **Five-point roadmap:** fiscal reforms, assured cargo support, competitive financing, regulatory simplification, improved Ease of Doing Business.
- **MAKV 2047 ambition:** place India among the world's **top five ship-owning nations**.
- The **NSB** is a statutory advisory body on shipping policy, tonnage, seafarer welfare and port-linked development — first constituted in **1959** under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, and now continued under the **Merchant Shipping Act, 2025**, which repealed the 1958 Act (and the Coasting Vessels Act, 1838). Its rules were overhauled in 2026.
- The official **NSB website** was launched at the event.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (infrastructure, transport, external sector).

## 3. **Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission launches India's first seven Nitrosamine Impurity Reference Standards**

**Ministry/body:** Indian Pharmacopoeia Commission (IPC), an autonomous institution under the Ministry of Health & Family Welfare.

Nitrosamines are genotoxic, probably-carcinogenic contaminants that can creep into drugs during manufacture, storage or packaging — the class of impurity behind global recalls of ranitidine and some sartans. Until now Indian labs largely imported the reference standards needed to measure them; this closes an import dependency in the quality-control chain.

**Key facts**

- Launched at the interactive meeting **"IP 2026: Perspectives, Suggestions & Way Forward"** at the **Central Drugs Laboratory (CDL)** and Central Cosmetics Laboratory, **Kolkata** — IPC's first such engagement with CDL Kolkata.
- The **seven standards**: NDMA, NDEA, NEIPA, NDBA, NMPA, NDIPA and NMBA.
- Relevant to formulations for **hypertension, gastric disorders, diabetes and tuberculosis**, among others.
- Purpose: accurate detection and quantification, analytical method validation, regulatory compliance, and **harmonisation with international standards**.
- IPC is the body that publishes the **Indian Pharmacopoeia**, the official book of drug standards under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (health regulation) + GS3 Science & Technology.

## 4. Ministry of Mines launches the **Critical Minerals Innovation Hackathon 2026 (CMiH 2026)**

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Mines; organised by the **Jawaharlal Nehru Aluminium Research Development and Design Centre (JNARDDC), Nagpur**, an autonomous body under the Ministry.

India's critical-minerals problem is not only about mining rights abroad — it is also about not knowing precisely where domestic lithium and rare-earth-bearing pegmatites sit, and about tracking who owns the processing technology. CMiH 2026 crowdsources exactly those two gaps.

**Key facts**

- Launched by Union Minister of Coal and Mines **G. Kishan Reddy**; supports the **National Critical Mineral Mission (NCMM)**.
- **Problem statement 1:** Mineral Prospectivity Mapping from open-source data — a prospectivity heat map for **lithium and Rare Earth Element (REE) pegmatite** zones, validated against known occurrences.
- **Problem statement 2:** a **Smart Technology & Patent Tracker** — a web-based R&D and patent tracking system for critical minerals.
- **Idea submission deadline: 20 September 2026**; winners announced at **India Mining Week 2026 (15–17 November 2026)**.
- Prizes per problem statement: **₹1.50 lakh (first)** and **₹1 lakh (second)**, plus mentorship.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (mineral resources, resource security) + Science & Technology.

## 5. **DRDO successfully tests the Military Combat Parachute System (MCPS) at 22,000 feet in Ladakh**

**Ministry/body:** Ministry of Defence — DRDO; Aerial Delivery Research & Development Establishment (ADRDE), Agra, with the Defence Bioengineering and Electromedical Laboratory (DEBEL), Bengaluru.

High-altitude airborne insertion in the Ladakh theatre has always been constrained by imported parachute and life-support kit. A validated indigenous system at extreme altitude directly expands special-operations reach along the northern borders.

**Key facts**

- Trial held on **26 August 2026** at the **Nyoma-Mudh Drop Zone**, Leh district, Ladakh — one of the world's highest drop zones, near the LAC in south-eastern Ladakh (the IAF recently commissioned a fighter-capable airbase at Nyoma).
- Jump from **22,000 ft AMSL**, canopy deployed at **20,000 ft**, landing at **13,700 ft**, in temperatures around **−15 °C**.
- Test jumpers: **Wg Cdr Rahul Jha** (Chief Test Jumper), **MWO R J Singh**, **MWO Vivek Tiwari**.
- All sub-systems indigenous — **para computer, oxygen system, specialised jumpsuits**.
- Features: lower descent rate, improved steering, **NavIC** compatibility; supports both **HALO** (High Altitude Low Opening) and **HAHO** (High Altitude High Opening) profiles.

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (indigenisation) + Internal/External Security.

## 6. Vice-President launches **'Gnani Artha'**, a sovereign AI stack

**Ministry/body:** Vice-President's Secretariat (developer: Gnani.ai); reads onto MeitY's IndiaAI/sovereign-AI agenda.

"Sovereign AI" has moved from slogan to specification — the argument being that data residency, compute cost and Indian-language performance are three distinct constraints, and a stack has to solve all three. Useful as a concrete example when writing about India's AI self-reliance.

**Key facts**

- Launched by Vice-President **C. P. Radhakrishnan** on **28 August 2026** at Upa-Rashtrapati Bhavan, New Delhi.
- Two components: **Evon 3.3**, a large language model, and **Plexus**, an agentic AI platform.
- **Evon v3.3** — a **30-billion-parameter open-weight** model with roughly **3.5 billion parameters active per token** (a mixture-of-experts design), trained natively across **11 Indian languages**; reported to consume about **40% fewer tokens** on Indian-language workloads than comparable models.
- Framed as addressing three problems: **data sovereignty, cost of AI at scale, and Indian-language capability**.

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (AI, digital sovereignty) + GS2 (technology governance).

## Scheme-in-focus: **Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS)**

*(Notified 21 August 2026 by MeitY — not covered in earlier briefings, and it is the direct successor to a scheme UPSC has repeatedly touched.)*

| Field | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| **Name** | Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS) |
| **Ministry** | Ministry of Electronics & Information Technology (MeitY) |
| **Outlay** | **₹62,500 crore** |
| **Tenure** | Five years, **FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31** |

**Objective.** To sustain the scale India built under the PLI for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing (**PLI-LSEM**, whose tenure ended **31 March 2026**), while shifting the emphasis from assembly volume to **Domestic Value Addition (DVA)**, component depth and Indian-owned brands.

**Key features**

1. Incentive on eligible sales at **differentiated rates of 2.25% to 5%**.
2. An **additional incentive of up to 1.5%** linked to **domestic sourcing** of key components and sub-assemblies.
3. A dedicated track for **Indian mobile phone brands** — reported at **5% incentive**, plus an additional **3%** for domestic design and R&D.
4. Eligibility thresholds keyed to turnover (reported at **₹10,000 crore in FY26** for the large-scale segment), covering electronics contract manufacturers.

**Beneficiaries/coverage.** Large-scale manufacturers and contract manufacturers; Indian-owned brands; component and sub-assembly suppliers pulled into the domestic supply chain.

**Significance.** PLI-LSEM made India an assembly and export hub; MPMS is the deliberate second step towards **technological sovereignty** — component ecosystems, Indian IP and design ownership — and is the scheme to cite whenever a question asks how India moves up the electronics value chain.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the **National Shipping Board (NSB)**, consider the following statements:

1. It is a statutory advisory body that advises the Government on shipping policy, tonnage and seafarer welfare.
2. It was first constituted under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, which has since been repealed and replaced by the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025.

1. It is an AI-powered translation platform for tribal languages developed under the Ministry of Tribal Affairs.
2. Its beta version supported Santali, Bhili, Mundari and Gondi.
3. Its upgraded version was launched at the National Conclave on Tribal Languages & Museums held at Mysuru.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only

## Answers

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** The NSB advises on shipping policy, tonnage, seafarer welfare and port-linked maritime development; it was first constituted in 1959 under the Merchant Shipping Act, 1958, and continues under the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025, which repealed the 1958 Act. Statement 3 is wrong — it functions under the **Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways**.

**Q2 — (b).** CMiH 2026 was launched by the Union Minister of Coal and Mines and is organised by **JNARDDC, Nagpur**, an autonomous body under the **Ministry of Mines**, in support of the **National Critical Mineral Mission**; its two problem statements are mineral prospectivity mapping for lithium/REE pegmatites and a smart technology-and-patent tracker.

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct. Adi Vaani is MoTA's AI translator for tribal languages, built by a consortium led by IIT Delhi; the beta covered Santali, Bhili, Mundari and Gondi (Kui and Garo under development); the upgraded platform, along with the "Jago" AI conversational assistant, was launched at the Mysuru conclave.
