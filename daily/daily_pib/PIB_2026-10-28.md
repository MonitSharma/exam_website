## 1. Cabinet: Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (CMPS) — Rs 10,000 crore
**Ministry:** Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways (inferred; announced in Union Budget 2026-27) | **Notebook:** GS3 Economy / Infrastructure & Industry

**What & why:** A long-duration support scheme (up to FY 2039-40) to build a domestic shipping-container manufacturing base. Global production is highly concentrated (about 95% in one country, per the Minister), so the scheme targets import dependence and supply-chain resilience, and aims to make India an export hub.

**Key facts**
- Outlay Rs 10,000 crore; runs up to FY 2039-40.
- Target: raise annual capacity nearly 10x, to 7.5 lakh TEUs.
- Components: Interest Subvention Assistance (3% p.a. on eligible term loans for greenfield/brownfield units; Rs 859 crore); Operational Expenditure Assistance for containers built to global standards (Rs 8,891 crore); testing & certification support incl. common facilities (Rs 150 crore). *(Reported component figures sum to Rs 9,900 crore; one outlet also mentions land-leasing support — check PIB text for the exact split.)*
- Jobs: ~3,000 direct, 50,000+ indirect (53,000+).

### Scheme-in-focus: Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme
- **Objective:** Competitive domestic container industry; cut import dependence.
- **Features:** 3% interest subvention; opex assistance to bridge cost gap; common testing/certification; Budget 2026-27 announcement.
- **Beneficiaries:** Eligible container manufacturers (greenfield & brownfield), ancillary industry (corner castings, specialised steel, wooden frames, paints).
- **Significance:** Maritime self-reliance, logistics cost, Atmanirbhar Bharat; links to Maritime Amrit Kaal themes.

## 2. Cabinet: Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM-VGSY) — Rs 7,395.55 crore
**Ministry:** Ministry of Panchayati Raj (inferred) | **Notebook:** GS2 Governance / Polity (Local Government)

**What & why:** A Centrally Sponsored Scheme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 (co-terminus with the Sixteenth Finance Commission period) to make Panchayats self-reliant, accountable, high-performing institutions. Extends to all States/UTs including traditional local bodies in non-Part IX areas.

**Key facts**
- Outlay Rs 7,395.55 crore: Centre Rs 4,869.48 crore, States Rs 2,526.07 crore.
- Covers ~2.55 lakh Panchayats/traditional bodies; capacity building for ~32 lakh elected representatives and functionaries.
- Annual targets: ~2.55 lakh Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDPs) on eGramSwaraj; ~2.55 lakh online audit plans on AuditOnline; 1 crore+ citizens per awareness exercise.
- Digital: common platform on e-Panchayat systems; PANCHAM AI platform (17 languages) reported.
- No permanent posts; need-based contractual staff. States submit annual action plans, appraised by a Central Empowered Committee.
- Context: 16th FC recommends ~Rs 4.35 lakh crore grants for rural local bodies (2026-31).

### Scheme-in-focus: PM-VGSY
- **Objective:** Self-reliant, accountable Panchayats in line with Viksit Bharat.
- **Features:** Evidence-based GPDPs; awareness & capacity building; online audit, accounting, Own Source Revenue mobilisation; coverage of non-Part IX areas.
- **Beneficiaries:** ~2.55 lakh local bodies; ~32 lakh representatives/functionaries.
- **Significance:** Operationalises 73rd Amendment / Part IX, Finance Commission grant utilisation; fiscal decentralisation. *(Relationship to the earlier RGSA is not stated in sources read.)*

## 3. Cabinet: Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme — Rs 1,856.67 crore
**Ministry:** Ministry of Youth Affairs & Sports (inferred), with MSDE for skilling | **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Manufacturing) / GS2 Sports policy

**What & why:** 2026-27 to 2033-34 scheme to make India a global hub for quality sports goods, cutting import dependence and raising domestic value addition.

**Key facts**
- Components: Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones (SGMZs) built on the BHAVYA scheme and NICDC industrial parks; Anchor Investment Support and Turnover-Linked Support (about Rs 1,130 crore combined); cluster upgradation for MSMEs; International Sports Federation certification support (Rs 105 crore); Centres of Excellence and R&D linked to Anusandhan National Research Foundation; skilling with MSDE; branding via Export Promotion Mission.
- Aligned with Khelo Bharat Niti 2025, Make in India. No numerical output/job targets stated in sources read.
- Sports-goods clusters such as Jalandhar and Ludhiana flagged as beneficiaries (Tribune).

## 4. Cabinet: Dearness Allowance / Dearness Relief raised by 3 percentage points
**Ministry:** Ministry of Finance (Dept. of Expenditure) | **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Public Finance) | *Verified on PIB release, 28 Oct 2026*

- DA/DR rises from 60% to 63% of Basic Pay/Pension, effective 1 July 2026.
- Beneficiaries: ~48.54 lakh Central Government employees, ~68.71 lakh pensioners.
- Annual exchequer impact: Rs 10,016.10 crore (DA + DR combined).
- Rationale: compensate for price rise under the accepted formula based on 7th CPC recommendations.

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme approved by the Union Cabinet in October 2026, consider the following statements:
1. It aims to raise India's annual container manufacturing capacity to about 7.5 lakh TEUs.
2. It provides a 3% per annum interest subvention on eligible term loans.
3. It is a short-term scheme to be completed within five years.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (PM-VGSY):
1. It is a Centrally Sponsored Scheme running from 2026-27 to 2030-31.
2. It extends to rural local bodies in areas not covered by Part IX of the Constitution.
3. It creates permanent posts at State, district and block levels.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The Union Cabinet's October 2026 approval raises Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees to:
(a) 58% of Basic Pay, effective 1 January 2026
(b) 60% of Basic Pay, effective 1 July 2026
(c) 63% of Basic Pay, effective 1 July 2026
(d) 65% of Basic Pay, effective 1 October 2026

---

## Answers
1. **(a)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; the scheme runs up to FY 2039-40, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — It is a CSS (2026-27 to 2030-31) covering non-Part IX bodies; no permanent posts are created, only need-based contractual staff.
3. **(c)** — DA/DR rose 3 percentage points from 60% to 63%, effective 1 July 2026 (about Rs 10,016.10 crore a year).
