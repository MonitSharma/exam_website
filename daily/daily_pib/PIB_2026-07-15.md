## 1. Department of Posts + TRAI to run India's largest village-level telecom network performance survey

**Issuing body:** **Ministry of Communications** — Department of Posts (DoP) and the **Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI)** · **MoU signed:** 10 July 2026 (PIB PRID 2283414) · **Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (evidence-based policy, last-mile delivery, DPI) · GS3 — Science & Technology (telecom, digital divide)

DoP and TRAI signed an MoU for a **year-long, app-based survey of mobile network performance across more than 5.68 lakh villages** — India's largest such rural exercise. It will generate field-level data to map connectivity gaps and support evidence-based telecom policy. It matters as a model of **institutional convergence**: the postal network becomes a data-collection backbone for telecom regulation, and it directly feeds the "digitally inclusive India" goal.

**Key facts/figures:** The survey covers the four Telecom Service Providers — **Bharti Airtel, Reliance Jio, Vodafone Idea and BSNL**. Field work is done by **Gramin Dak Sevaks (GDSs)** during routine mail delivery, using a purpose-built **Android app developed by TRAI**, leveraging DoP's network of **1.40 lakh+ rural Branch Post Offices**. TRAI provides the app, technical support, training and centralised monitoring; DoP provides on-ground reach. (Note: TRAI is a statutory regulator set up under the **TRAI Act, 1997**.)

## 2. MeitY launches NIDAR 2.0 — indigenous autonomous-drone innovation challenge on the VEGA processor

**Issuing body:** **Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY)** with the **Drone Federation India (DFI)**, under the **SwaYaan** initiative · **Date:** launched 13 July 2026, Electronics Niketan, New Delhi (problem statements/rulebook released by MeitY Secretary S. Krishnan) · **Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (indigenous drones, semiconductors, Atmanirbhar Bharat)

MeitY launched the **second edition of the National Innovation Challenge for Drone Application and Research (NIDAR 2.0)** for 2026-27. Its stated shift is "from flying drones to building the drone's brain" — i.e., from *operating* drones to *developing their core autonomy* using home-grown hardware. A clean GS3 case study linking the drone ecosystem, indigenous chips and skilling.

**Key facts/figures:** Two tracks — (a) **Drone Innovation**: student teams build **fully autonomous swarm drones** for disaster zones (locating survivors, delivering supplies without external communications) and **GPS-denied drones** for confined indoor industrial inspection; and (b) **Component Innovation**: indigenous **flight controllers and autopilots built around the VEGA processor** (India's home-grown RISC-based processor family, developed by C-DAC) using locally developed components. Edition 1 drew **3,448 students from 22 states, 4 UTs and 109 cities**; 93 teams reached the finale and 24 won prizes worth **₹40 lakh**.

## 3. Lakhanpur (J&K) among first towns approved for a "Street Food Hub" under PM SVANidhi

**Issuing body:** **Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs (MoHUA)** (announced 11 July 2026 by MoS Dr Jitendra Singh) · **Notebook:** GS2 — Governance (urban livelihoods, street-vendor welfare) · GS3 — Economy (informal sector, tourism-led local economy)

**Lakhanpur, in Kathua district**, was selected among the first towns to develop a **Street Food Hub** under the PM SVANidhi scheme. Strategically placed at the J&K gateway from Pathankot and on the route to Shri Mata Vaishno Devi, the hub is meant to formalise street-vending infrastructure, promote **Dogra cuisine** and boost transit tourism.

**Scheme-in-focus — PM SVANidhi (PM Street Vendor's AtmaNirbhar Nidhi):**
- **Ministry:** Housing & Urban Affairs. A micro-credit scheme (launched June 2020) giving street vendors collateral-free working-capital loans with an interest subsidy and incentives for digital transactions.
- **This add-on ("Street Food Hubs"):** MoHUA proposes **up to 50 Street Food Hubs** nationwide; **each approved project gets ₹4 crore**, released in three instalments (**30% – 50% – 20%**, the last after completion).
- **Lakhanpur specifics:** spread over **1,754.25 sq m** across two clusters (near the Old Bus Stand and the Sales Tax Office).
- **Significance:** moves PM SVANidhi from pure credit toward **physical vending infrastructure**, linking informal-sector welfare with tourism and clean-city goals.

## 4. WIPO's World Intangible Investment Highlights 2026 — India the fastest-growing among major economies

**Issuing body:** **World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)** with Luiss Business School; highlighted for India by **Commerce & Industry Minister Piyush Goyal** · **Date:** reported 9–14 July 2026 · **Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (innovation, IPR, knowledge economy) · Reports & Indices

The 2026 edition (third) reports that **India recorded the fastest growth in intangible investment among the world's 15 largest economies**. "Intangible" assets include software, R&D, intellectual property, data and organisational capital — increasingly the real drivers of modern growth. It reinforces India's positioning as an innovation- and services-led economy.

**Key facts/figures:** India's intangible investment reached about **US$78.2 billion (2023)**, growing **7.9%** — the fastest among the top 15 economies — and exceeding several EU economies (Denmark, Czechia, Finland). **Software and databases made up ~45%** of India's intangible investment, the **highest share of any economy** in the study. Globally, **intangible investment crossed US$10 trillion for the first time**, growing **more than three times faster than tangible investment**; the largest absolute investors remained the **United States, Japan and Germany**.

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the village-level telecom network performance survey recently in the news:
1. It is a joint initiative of the Department of Posts and the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI).
2. The field survey is carried out by Gramin Dak Sevaks using an app developed by TRAI.
3. TRAI is a statutory body established under an Act of Parliament.

How many of the statements given above are correct?
(a) Only one
(b) Only two
(c) All three
(d) None

**Q2.** With reference to **NIDAR 2.0**, recently launched, consider the following:
1. It is anchored by the Ministry of Electronics & IT under the SwaYaan initiative.
2. Its Component Innovation track promotes indigenous flight controllers built around the VEGA processor.
3. It is a scheme to procure combat drones for the armed forces.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The **"Street Food Hub"** component recently in the news is associated with which one of the following schemes, and which ministry implements it?
(a) PM SVANidhi — Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs
(b) PM FME — Ministry of Food Processing Industries
(c) PM-KISAN — Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
(d) Deen Dayal Antyodaya Yojana — Ministry of Rural Development

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (c) All three.** The DoP–TRAI MoU (10 July 2026) launches an app-based survey across 5.68 lakh villages; GDSs collect data on a TRAI-built Android app; TRAI is a statutory regulator under the **TRAI Act, 1997**. All three statements are correct.

**Q2 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** NIDAR 2.0 is run by **MeitY** with the Drone Federation India under **SwaYaan**, and its Component Innovation track centres on indigenous flight controllers/autopilots built on the **VEGA processor**. Statement 3 is wrong — it is a **student innovation challenge** (autonomous swarm/GPS-denied drones), **not** a military procurement programme.

**Q3 — (a) PM SVANidhi — Ministry of Housing & Urban Affairs.** The Street Food Hub (₹4 crore per hub, up to 50 nationwide) is an infrastructure add-on under **PM SVANidhi**, implemented by **MoHUA**; Lakhanpur (Kathua, J&K) is among the first approved towns.
