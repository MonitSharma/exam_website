## 1. The "Defence Decade" — 12-Year Review of India's Defence Transformation (2014–2026)
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Defence (MoD)
**Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Tech / Defence; Internal Security) · GS2 (IR — defence diplomacy)

A comprehensive government review documents India's defence transformation across four pillars — Capability (R&D, innovation), Capacity (production, exports, corridors), Operational Excellence (indigenous platforms), and Credibility (defence diplomacy). The headline story is the shift from heavy import dependence (65–70% of equipment once imported) toward self-reliance under Atmanirbhar Bharat. High prelims-value because it bundles together schemes, procedures and figures that repeatedly appear in the exam.

**Key facts/figures:**
- Defence budget FY27: **₹7.85 lakh crore** (≈3× the ₹2.53 lakh crore of FY14); capital outlay FY27 ₹2.19 lakh crore.
- Indigenous defence production FY26: **₹1.78 lakh crore**; private sector share at an all-time high of **24%**.
- Record defence **exports FY26: ₹38,424 crore** to 80+ countries; target **₹50,000 crore by 2029**.
- **DAP 2020** replaced DPP 2016; top procurement priority = **Buy (Indian-IDDM)** — Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured.
- **iDEX** (run by Defence Innovation Organisation); sub-scheme **ADITI** (₹750 cr) for critical & emerging tech; **TDF** grants up to ₹50 cr/project.
- **Positive Indigenisation Lists** issued since 2020 (500+ items); Defence is a Union subject; **DAC** (chaired by Raksha Mantri) is the apex acquisition body.
- Milestones recalled: Mission Shakti (ASAT, 27 Mar 2019), Mission Divyastra (Agni-5 MIRV, 11 Mar 2024), Operation Sindoor validation of Akash/BrahMos/anti-drone systems.

## 2. PM Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY) — ₹2,400 crore Incentive Disbursal
**Issuing body:** PMO / Ministry of Labour & Employment (implemented via EPFO)
**Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — employment & formalisation)

The PM was scheduled to disburse incentives of around **₹2,400 crore** under PM-VBRY on **19 June 2026** at a nationwide employment festival (address from Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi). The scheme pushes formal-sector job creation by rewarding both first-time workers and employers, and is a strong "scheme + data" item for Mains and Prelims.

> **Scheme template**
> - **Name:** Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY)
> - **Ministry:** Labour & Employment; operations & IT-monitoring by **EPFO**
> - **Objective:** Boost formal employment generation, employability and social security
> - **Key features:** (i) Effective **1 August 2025**; (ii) outlay **₹99,446 crore**; (iii) **Part A** — first-time EPFO-registered employees get incentive **up to ₹15,000**; (iv) **Part B** — incentives to employers for additional hiring (extended support for manufacturing)
> - **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Targets creation of **3.5+ crore jobs over two years**
> - **Significance:** Links wage-subsidy to EPFO formalisation; nudges workers and firms into the formal economy

## 3. Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification — 3rd Investor Roadshow, Mumbai
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Coal
**Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — Energy security; Environment — clean coal)

The Ministry of Coal held the **third roadshow** on Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification on **18 June 2026 in Mumbai** (after New Delhi and Hyderabad) to attract investors, technology providers and financiers to its gasification scheme. Chief Guest: Union Coal & Mines Minister **G. Kishan Reddy**; Special Guest: Maharashtra CM **Devendra Fadnavis**; MoS Coal **Satish Chandra Dubey**.

> **Scheme template**
> - **Name:** Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects
> - **Ministry:** Coal
> - **Objective:** Incentivise gasification to produce **syngas** and downstream products (methanol, ammonia, etc.), cutting import dependence on natural gas/methanol/ammonia
> - **Key features:** (i) Expanded financial outlay **₹37,500 crore** (approved May 2026; earlier 2024 scheme was ₹8,500 crore, structured in 3 categories — PSUs, private/PSU, demonstration projects); (ii) targets gasification of **~75 million tonnes** of coal/lignite; (iii) grants linked to capex
> - **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Coal PSUs, private players, technology demonstrators
> - **Significance:** Clean coal utilisation, energy security and Atmanirbhar Bharat in critical feedstocks

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the **Pradhan Mantri Viksit Bharat Rozgar Yojana (PM-VBRY)**, consider the following statements:
1. It is implemented by the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship.
2. Under Part A, first-time employees registered with the EPFO can receive an incentive of up to ₹15,000.
3. The EPFO carries out the IT-based monitoring of the scheme.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** Consider the following statements regarding the **Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects**:
1. The scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Coal.
2. It targets the gasification of about 75 million tonnes of coal/lignite.
3. Its primary objective is to export syngas to neighbouring countries.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** With reference to India's defence acquisition and innovation framework, consider the following statements:
1. The Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020 replaced the Defence Procurement Procedure (DPP) 2016.
2. 'Buy (Indian-IDDM)' is the most preferred category of capital acquisition under DAP 2020.
3. ADITI is a sub-scheme under iDEX for critical and emerging technologies.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only  (b) 2 and 3 only  (c) 1 and 3 only  (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** Statements 2 and 3 are correct. PM-VBRY (effective 1 Aug 2025; outlay ₹99,446 cr; target 3.5+ crore jobs) gives first-time EPFO-registered employees up to ₹15,000 (Part A), and EPFO handles IT-based monitoring. Statement 1 is wrong — it is run by the **Ministry of Labour & Employment**, not Skill Development & Entrepreneurship.

**Q2 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. The Ministry of Coal runs the scheme (₹37,500 cr expanded outlay, ~75 MT gasification target). Statement 3 is wrong — the aim is to produce syngas/downstream products (methanol, ammonia) for **domestic use to cut imports**, not to export syngas.

**Q3 — (d).** All three are correct. DAP 2020 replaced DPP 2016; 'Buy (Indian-IDDM)' is its highest priority category; and ADITI (₹750 cr) is an iDEX sub-scheme (run by the Defence Innovation Organisation) for critical & emerging technologies.
