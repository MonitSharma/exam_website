## 1. Department of Financial Services (Ministry of Finance): **Financial Inclusion Saturation Campaign, 21 Oct – 31 Dec 2026** (statement 19 Oct)
- **What:** A nationwide last-mile drive covering about **2.70 lakh Gram Panchayats and Urban Local Bodies** to saturate financial-inclusion schemes.
- **Activities:** bank camps to enrol people in **PMJJBY, PMSBY and Atal Pension Yojana**; open **PMJDY** accounts; re-KYC; C-KYC IDs; nomination updates for accounts and lockers; Kisan Credit Card applications (to saturate PM-Kisan beneficiaries) and PMFBY enrolment ahead of Rabi; awareness on digital frauds and unclaimed assets under **"Aapki Poonji, Aapka Adhikar"**.
- **Why it matters:** moves from account-opening to *insurance, pension and credit* coverage; links with SLBC machinery. Monitored by state administrations, banks and the Department of Finance.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (financial inclusion); GS2 Governance (delivery).

## 2. Ministry of Power / CEA: **Pumped Storage Projects (PSPs) — PIB backgrounder on India's storage push** (19 Oct)
- **What:** PSPs pump water to an upper reservoir when power is cheap/surplus and release it at peak demand; long-duration storage (typically 6–20 hours) that supports frequency regulation, black-start capability and absorbs renewable curtailment. Types: open-loop (natural water bodies, e.g. Tehri) vs closed-loop (isolated reservoirs, e.g. Kundah).
- **Key figures:** per the Ministry of Power (Rajya Sabha reply, July 2026) **11 PSPs (15,870 MW) are under construction** and 6 more (8,140 MW) are CEA-concurred; commissioning phased 2026–2032. The backgrounder (reported via secondary summaries) puts operational capacity at ~7.43 GW and identified potential at ~345 GW across 320 sites *(these two figures single-sourced; verify on PIB)*.
- **Enablers:** 2023 PSP development guidelines; MoEFCC B2-category appraisal; CEA DPR timeline cut from 90 to 50 days; inter-state clearance dispensed with; **100% ISTS charge waiver for 25 years** (contracts awarded by 30 June 2028); tariff-based competitive bidding guidelines (2025).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy/Infrastructure (energy, renewable integration).

## 3. Ministry of Skill Development & Entrepreneurship: **PM-SETU — Rs 1,194.92 crore approved for five ITI clusters** (6th National Steering Committee, 16 Oct) *(UPDATE — PM-SETU was mentioned in earlier briefings; new are these approvals)*
- Clusters: **Kataka (Odisha)**, **Pune** and **Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar (Maharashtra)**, **Jammu** and **Srinagar (J&K)** — 25 ITIs in all (1 hub + 4 spokes per cluster), each anchored by an industry partner. Chaired by MSDE Secretary Debashree Mukherjee.
- Cumulative sanctioned investment under PM-SETU: **Rs 3,687 crore**. Training of Trainers has covered 3,744 trainers via NSTIs. Clusters are encouraged to seek ISO certification and include a WorldSkills-aligned trade.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Education/Skills; GS3 Employment.

### Scheme-in-focus: PM-SETU (Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs)
- **Ministry:** MSDE.
- **Objective:** modernise ITIs to industry-led, demand-driven skilling.
- **Features:** hub-and-spoke cluster model; industry partner leads each cluster and co-invests; trainer upskilling through NSTIs; alignment to WorldSkills/ISO standards.
- **Coverage:** ITI trainees; so far five clusters in this tranche add to Rs 3,687 crore sanctioned.
- **Significance:** addresses the skill–industry mismatch and regional skewness of vocational capacity.

## 4. Ministry of Commerce & Industry: **India–New Zealand Free Trade Agreement enters into force, 20 Oct 2026** *(UPDATE — negotiations/signing covered earlier; new is entry into force)*
- Signed 27 April 2026. Indian exports get duty-free access to New Zealand from day one; sensitive Indian items (dairy, sugar, edible oils, certain meat/agri goods) are excluded from India's concessions; apples, kiwifruit and Manuka honey get limited access via tariff-rate quotas/seasonal windows.
- NZ commits to facilitate **USD 20 billion** investment into India; mobility: 5,000 temporary employment entry visas, 1,000 working-holiday visas a year, and post-study work rights (up to 3 years STEM; up to 4 years doctoral).
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR; GS3 Economy (trade).

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Financial Inclusion Saturation Campaign (21 Oct – 31 Dec 2026), consider:
1. It covers about 2.70 lakh Gram Panchayats and Urban Local Bodies.
2. Bank camps enrol people in PMJJBY, PMSBY and APY and also handle re-KYC and nomination updates.
3. Its awareness component includes the 'Aapki Poonji, Aapka Adhikar' initiative.
Which are correct?
(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** Which statement about pumped storage projects in India is NOT correct?
(a) They pump water uphill in low-demand periods and release it at peak demand.
(b) A closed-loop PSP uses isolated reservoirs without continuous natural inflow.
(c) Eligible PSPs get a 100% ISTS charge waiver for 25 years if contracts are awarded by 30 June 2028.
(d) Inter-state clearance from the CEA is mandatory for every PSP irrespective of type or cost.

**Q3.** PM-SETU, in the news, is a scheme of which Ministry, and what is its approach for upgrading ITIs?
(a) Ministry of Education; stand-alone upgrade of engineering colleges
(b) Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship; hub-and-spoke ITI clusters with industry partners
(c) Ministry of Labour and Employment; closure of low-performing ITIs
(d) Ministry of MSME; new industrial parks around ITIs

---

## Answers
1. **(d)** — All three are correct (campaign covers ~2.70 lakh GPs/ULBs; PMJJBY/PMSBY/APY enrolment, re-KYC and nominations; 'Aapki Poonji, Aapka Adhikar').
2. **(d)** — Inter-state clearance is no longer required for PSPs, and off-stream closed-loop PSPs are exempt from mandatory CEA concurrence.
3. **(b)** — MSDE; 5 clusters / 25 ITIs approved (Rs 1,194.92 crore), each hub-and-spoke with an industry partner.
