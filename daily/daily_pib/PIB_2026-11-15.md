# Daily PIB Briefing — 15 November 2026

*Sources: PIB and government releases, with media reports where a PIB page was not reachable (marked). Ministries rotated away from Tribal Affairs, Defence, Pharma and MEA, which dominated the 14 Nov briefing.*

## 1. DARPG, Ministry of Personnel, Public Grievances & Pensions: **Web-portal launched for Prime Minister's Awards for Excellence in Public Administration 2026** (13 Nov 2026) — GS2 Governance

**What & why it matters:** The portal (pmawards.gov.in) opens registration for the 2026 awards. It was launched by video conference and attended by over 2,000 officers from about 750 locations, including Principal Secretaries and District Collectors. The awards reward district-level delivery of priority schemes, so they work as a tool of competitive and cooperative federalism.

**Key facts:**
- 16 awards in 3 categories: Holistic Development of Districts under 12 priority sector programmes (5 awards); Aspirational Blocks Programme (5 awards, covering 513 blocks in 331 districts); Innovations by central ministries, states/UTs and districts (6 awards).
- Period considered: 1 April 2024 to 30 November 2026. Each award carries a trophy, a scroll and ₹20 lakh, to be used for a project or to fill resource gaps in public welfare.
- Nine new priority schemes added: SBM (Urban) and AMRUT, Gyan Bharatam, PM Matsya Sampada Yojana, TB Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan, Crop Diversification, PM SHRI, SBM (Grameen), National Mission on Natural Farming, and IGOT capacity building. Continuing from 2025: Har Ghar Jal, PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, Saksham Anganwadi and Poshan 2.0.
- Ministries must submit their parameters to DARPG by 17 Nov 2026.

## 2. Ministry of Electronics & IT: **Semicon 2.0 application portal launched; 12 more ECMS projects approved** (5–6 Nov 2026; PIB-based coverage) — GS3 Economy / Sci-Tech *(not covered before: the 30 Oct briefing carried only the earlier ECMS tranche)*

**What & why it matters:** Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw launched a web portal for applications under the Semicon India Programme (Semicon 2.0, India Semiconductor Mission 2.0). It covers the machines and materials, new fabs and ATMP/OSAT pillars. He also announced 12 more approvals under the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS), which aims to move India from downstream assembly to a domestic components value chain.

**Key facts:**
- The 12 new ECMS projects carry proposed investment of ₹7,503 crore, expected production of ₹39,045 crore and about 7,127 direct jobs (PTI report).
- Cumulative ECMS: 118 applications across 16 states and 34 products; investment ₹77,051 crore; projected production ₹5.73 lakh crore; about 81,755 direct jobs (PTI). Nearly half of the approved units are MSMEs (Drishti, citing PIB).
- Semicon 2.0 outlay is reported as ₹1,27,500 crore, with six pillars: chip design, machines and materials, fabs, ATMP/OSAT, R&D and talent (reports; Cabinet approval reported as July 2026, which I could not verify on PIB).
- Components covered include multi-layer PCBs, relays, heat sinks, specialty ceramics, copper foil, battery materials and precision tooling.

## 3. Ministry of Commerce & Industry (ITPO): **45th India International Trade Fair (IITF) 2026, Bharat Mandapam** (inaugurated 13 Nov by Piyush Goyal; 14–27 Nov) — GS3 Economy / Trade

**What & why it matters:** India's largest annual trade fair, organised by the India Trade Promotion Organisation (ITPO). It showcases state economies, ODOP products and start-ups, and links to export promotion and "Vocal for Local".

**Key facts:** Theme "Vikas aur Virasat". Business (B2B) days 14–18 Nov; public days 19–27 Nov. Over 3,500 exhibitors in 17 halls. Partner States: Bihar, Maharashtra, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh. Focus States: Assam, Delhi, Jharkhand and West Bengal. 32 States/UTs and 51 central bodies participate, plus 11 country pavilions and about 600 start-ups at the Hall of Inspiration (single secondary source).

## 4. Department of Science & Technology / Indian Institute of Astrophysics: **COSMOS-Mysuru Planetarium inaugurated by the PM** (13 Nov 2026) — GS3 Science & Technology

**What & why it matters:** Billed as India's first LED-dome planetarium, built by the Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA) with the University of Mysore at the foot of Chamundi Hill. It is a tool for science popularisation and scientific temper.

**Key facts:** Tilted 15 m dome, over 6,000 LED panels, 8K visuals (about 42 megapixels) and no projector; seats 168; shows in Kannada, English and Hindi. In his speech the PM cited the ₹1 lakh crore Research, Development and Innovation (RDI) Scheme, the Anusandhan National Research Foundation, the "One Nation One Subscription" scheme for research journals, and over 10,000 Atal Tinkering Labs (single secondary source).

### Scheme-in-focus: Semicon India Programme 2.0 and the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS)
- **Ministry:** MeitY (India Semiconductor Mission). **Objective:** build an end-to-end domestic semiconductor and electronics-components ecosystem and cut import dependence.
- **Key features:** (1) Semicon 2.0 pillars on equipment and materials, fabs and ATMP/OSAT, alongside design, R&D and talent; (2) ECMS incentivises components and sub-assemblies (PCBs, relays, ceramics, battery materials) and capital goods; (3) a single online application portal to speed up approvals; (4) a large MSME share among approved units.
- **Coverage:** 118 ECMS projects in 16 states (reported). **Significance:** supply-chain resilience, Atmanirbhar Bharat, jobs and the "India as an electronics hub" strategy; link to PLI reviews (see the CA briefing of 14 Nov for the PLI assessment).

## Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** Consider the following statements about the Prime Minister's Awards for Excellence in Public Administration 2026:
1. The awards portal was launched by the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances.
2. The awards are given in 16 slots across three categories, including Holistic Development of Districts and Aspirational Blocks.
3. Each award carries a cash incentive of ₹1 crore payable to the awardee individually.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** With reference to the Electronics Components Manufacturing Scheme (ECMS) and Semicon 2.0, consider the following statements:
1. ECMS is implemented by the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology.
2. ECMS aims to shift India from downstream assembly towards a domestic components value chain.
3. A Semicon 2.0 application portal has been launched for pillars such as machines and materials, fabs and ATMP/OSAT.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3.** The COSMOS-Mysuru Planetarium, recently inaugurated, is described as India's first LED-dome planetarium. Consider the following statements:

1. It uses an LED dome rather than conventional projectors.
2. It was built by the Indian Institute of Astrophysics with the University of Mysore.
3. It is operated by the Indian Space Research Organisation.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only
(b) 1 and 2 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct: DARPG launched the portal, and there are 16 awards across three categories. Statement 3 is wrong: each award carries a trophy, scroll and ₹20 lakh, to be used for a project or public-welfare gaps, not ₹1 crore to an individual.

**Q2 — (d).** All three are correct: ECMS is a MeitY scheme meant to build a domestic components value chain, and the Semicon 2.0 portal covers the machines and materials, fabs and ATMP/OSAT pillars.

**Q3 — (b).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: the planetarium was built by the IIA (an autonomous institute under the Department of Science & Technology) with the University of Mysore, not operated by ISRO.
