## 1. **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana completes 12 years** — 59 crore+ accounts, ₹3.17 lakh crore in deposits

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Finance, Department of Financial Services (PIB, 27 August 2026)

PMJDY, launched on **28 August 2014** as the National Mission for Financial Inclusion, has completed twelve years. The Finance Ministry's anniversary release positions the scheme as the base layer of the JAM (Jan Dhan–Aadhaar–Mobile) trinity on which Direct Benefit Transfer now rides — which is exactly the framing UPSC prefers over the raw account count.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Over 59 crore** PMJDY accounts opened; total deposits **₹3.17 lakh crore**.
- **~78%** of accounts opened in **rural or semi-urban** areas; **~56%** held by **women**.
- **Average deposit per account ₹5,356** (as on 19 August 2026) — a **3.4x rise** over twelve years. This "deposit deepening" is the government's answer to the old criticism that Jan Dhan accounts were zero-balance shells.
- **41.29 crore RuPay debit cards** issued to PMJDY holders, carrying **accident insurance cover of up to ₹2 lakh** (₹1 lakh for cards issued before 28 August 2018).

**Why it matters:** Financial inclusion is a recurring GS3 theme — link PMJDY to DBT leakage reduction, the ₹10,000 overdraft facility, PMJJBY/PMSBY/APY micro-insurance stacking on top of it, and the persistent problem of dormant/inoperative accounts.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Inclusive Growth, Financial Inclusion) · GS2 — Governance (DBT)

## 2. **India presents science-based snow leopard conservation at the Moscow Roundtable** — IBCA's first big outing with Russia

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (PIB, 27 August 2026)

The **Russian–Indian Roundtable Meeting on Snow Leopard Study and Conservation** was held on **26 August 2026** at the **A.N. Severtsov Institute of Ecology and Evolution, Russian Academy of Sciences, Moscow**. The meeting was convened under the **Partnership Agreement between Russia's Interregional Association 'Irbis' and the International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA)**, signed in **December 2025**.

**Key facts**

- An **MoEFCC** delegation, with officials of the **Wildlife Institute of India (WII)**, presented India's advances in **population estimation, radio telemetry and genetic (scat-based) analysis** of snow leopards.
- Participants included Russia's **Ministry of Natural Resources and Environment**, the **Severtsov Institute**, **Moscow Zoo** and **Rosprirodnadzor**.
- Static anchors worth carrying: snow leopard is **IUCN 'Vulnerable'**, **CITES Appendix I**, **Schedule I** of the Wild Life (Protection) Act; India's **SPAI (Status of Snow Leopards in India) 2024** exercise put the population at **718**; India is one of **12 range countries** under the **GSLEP Programme** (Bishkek Declaration, 2013); **Project Snow Leopard** dates to 2009. IBCA, headquartered in India, covers **seven big cats** — tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar and puma.

**Why it matters:** This is IBCA converting from an announcement into operational bilateral science diplomacy — a clean example of India using a conservation platform for soft-power outreach, and a ready-made Mains illustration of "technology in wildlife monitoring."

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment (Conservation, Biodiversity) · GS2 — IR (India–Russia, Institutions)

## 4. **Government begins calibrated release of onion buffer stocks; 'Kanda Express' rolls from Nashik**

**Issuing body:** Department of Consumer Affairs, Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution (PIB, 26 August 2026)

Ahead of the festive season, the Centre has started a **calibrated and targeted release** of onions from the buffer maintained under the **Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF)**, stating that availability is adequate and the intervention is meant to moderate seasonal price pressure rather than signal scarcity.

**Key facts and figures**

- **Onion production 2025-26 estimated at 307.37 lakh tonnes**, marginally below **307.67 lakh tonnes** in 2024-25.
- **Rabi onion procurement target for PSF in 2026-27: 2 lakh tonnes**; about **1.21 lakh tonnes** procured so far.
- A **hybrid rail-plus-road** dispatch model is being used. The first **'Kanda Express'** rake left **Nashik for Delhi-NCR**; road consignments are moving to **Chennai, Kolkata, Ernakulam, Guwahati, Varanasi, Lucknow, Patna, Chandigarh, Jammu and Amritsar**.
- **All-India average retail price of onion: ₹37.87/kg** as on 26 August 2026.

**Why it matters:** A textbook case of **buffer-stock-based price stabilisation** for a non-PDS commodity. Note the distinction from FCI's foodgrain buffer: the PSF (created in 2014-15, shifted to the Department of Consumer Affairs in 2016) targets **TOP crops — tomato, onion, potato — and pulses**, and works through market releases, not statutory stocking norms.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Food Management, Buffer Stocks, Inflation) · GS3 — Agriculture (Marketing)

## 5. **7th Session of the India–Morocco Joint Commission concludes** — broader trade basket, fertilizers, renewables, pharma

**Issuing body:** Ministry of Commerce and Industry (PIB, 26 August 2026)

**Shri Jitin Prasada**, Minister of State for Commerce & Industry and Electronics & IT, visited Morocco on **24–25 August 2026** and co-chaired the **7th Session of the India–Morocco Joint Commission** with Morocco's Secretary of State to the Minister of Industry and Trade, **Omar Hejira**.

**Key facts**

- Both sides agreed to work on **expanding market access, diversifying the trade basket and boosting two-way investment**.
- Priority sectors identified: **fertilizers, renewable energy and pharmaceuticals**.
- **Bilateral merchandise trade stood at roughly USD 4 billion in 2025.** India mainly exports refined petroleum, pharmaceuticals, vehicles, synthetic textiles, food items and spices; it imports **phosphates and phosphatic fertilisers** — Morocco holds the world's largest phosphate rock reserves, which makes this a **fertiliser-security** relationship, not merely a trade one.
- A **Cultural Exchange Programme for 2026–2030** covering artists, festivals, heritage conservation and museum/archive linkages was on the agenda.

**Why it matters:** Connect to India's fertiliser import dependence (urea, DAP, phosphates), diversification of critical-input sources, and India's Africa outreach beyond the eastern seaboard.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (India–Africa, Bilateral) · GS3 — Economy (Fertiliser Security)

## 6. **Centre monitors the Nepal flash floods; PM speaks to Nepal's Prime Minister**

**Issuing body:** Prime Minister's Office and the Government of India (PIB, 26 August 2026)

Following the **flash floods of 26 August 2026** in Nepal's **Rasuwa and Nuwakot districts** — along the **Bhotekoshi/upper Trishuli** river system — the Government of India stated it was keeping a **close watch** on the situation, and the **Prime Minister spoke to the Prime Minister of Nepal**.

**Key points**

- The Bhotekoshi–Trishuli system drains into the **Narayani/Gandak**, which enters India in **Bihar** — hence the downstream flood-alert relevance for Bihar's north-Gangetic districts.
- Institutional handles: **India–Nepal Joint Committee on Inundation and Flood Management**, the **Kosi and Gandak barrage arrangements**, the **Central Water Commission's** flood forecasting network, and **NDRF/NDMA** response mechanisms.
- Broader theme: **Glacial Lake Outburst Floods (GLOFs)** and earthquake-triggered avalanche-debris flows in the fragile Higher Himalaya — a recurring Mains angle after Chamoli (2021) and South Lhonak/Sikkim (2023).

**Why it matters:** Two GS papers at once — neighbourhood-first diplomacy and transboundary river cooperation (GS2), and Himalayan disaster risk plus early-warning systems (GS3).

**Notebook:** GS3 — Disaster Management · GS2 — IR (India–Nepal, Transboundary Rivers)

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1.** With reference to the **Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY)**, consider the following statements:

1. It was launched in 2014 as the National Mission for Financial Inclusion and is implemented by the Department of Financial Services.
2. Accounts opened under the scheme are Basic Savings Bank Deposit Accounts that require no minimum balance.
3. The RuPay debit card issued under the scheme carries an in-built accident insurance cover.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only
(c) 1 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q2.** Consider the following statements about the **International Big Cat Alliance (IBCA)** and snow leopard conservation:

1. The IBCA is headquartered in India and covers seven big cat species, including the snow leopard and the puma.
2. The snow leopard is currently listed as 'Endangered' on the IUCN Red List.
3. India is one of the range countries participating in the Global Snow Leopard and Ecosystem Protection (GSLEP) Programme.

Which of the statements given above are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 1 and 3 only
(c) 2 and 3 only
(d) 1, 2 and 3

---

**Q3.** The **Price Stabilisation Fund (PSF)**, in the news recently in connection with the release of onion buffer stocks, is best described as:

(a) A fund under the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare that pays farmers the difference between the Minimum Support Price and the market price
(b) A fund under the Department of Consumer Affairs used to build and release buffer stocks of commodities such as onion, potato and pulses to moderate price volatility
(c) A statutory reserve maintained by the Food Corporation of India for foodgrains under the National Food Security Act
(d) A market-intervention corpus operated by NAFED exclusively for oilseeds procurement

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** PMJDY was launched on 28 August 2014 as the National Mission for Financial Inclusion under the Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance (statement 1 correct). Accounts are zero-minimum-balance BSBDAs (statement 2 correct). The free RuPay debit card carries accident insurance cover — up to ₹2 lakh for cards issued on or after 28 August 2018 (statement 3 correct).

**Q2 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** IBCA is headquartered in India and covers seven big cats — tiger, lion, leopard, snow leopard, cheetah, jaguar and puma (statement 1 correct). The snow leopard is listed as **'Vulnerable'**, not 'Endangered', on the IUCN Red List — it was downlisted from Endangered in 2017 (statement 2 incorrect). India is one of the 12 snow leopard range countries under the GSLEP Programme, launched with the Bishkek Declaration of 2013 (statement 3 correct).

**Q3 — (b).** The PSF was created in 2014-15 and shifted to the **Department of Consumer Affairs** in 2016. It funds procurement, storage and calibrated market release of buffer stocks of TOP crops (tomato, onion, potato) and pulses to smooth price volatility for consumers. Option (a) describes a price-deficiency-payment model (e.g. Bhavantar-type schemes); (c) confuses PSF with FCI's foodgrain buffer norms; (d) misstates NAFED's role, which is that of an implementing agency, not the fund itself.
