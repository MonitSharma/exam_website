## 1. Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare: **India creates 11 crore Farmer IDs — five months ahead of deadline** (announced 24 Oct 2026) *(UPDATE — Farmer Registry/Agri-Stack mentioned in earlier briefings; new is the target being met)*

**What / why it matters:** Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan announced that the target of 11 crore Farmer IDs under the **Digital Agriculture Mission** was reached by the night of 23 Oct 2026. Each farmer gets a unique, verified digital identity linked to land records, so schemes, credit and relief can be delivered without repeated document checks. This is the "farmer registry" layer of India's agricultural digital public infrastructure (DPI).

**Key facts**
- Digital Agriculture Mission: approved by the Union Cabinet on **2 Sep 2024**; builds DPI incl. a verified farmer database.
- Target reached about **five months ahead** of the deadline (ANI: 31 March 2027; one other report says Dec 2027 — treat the date as unsettled).
- Over **6 crore farmers** have already received benefits through Farmer-ID-based delivery.
- Intended uses: faster KCC/credit, crop insurance (PMFBY) and disaster relief, MSP procurement with direct bank transfers, fewer duplicate/fraudulent claims.
- States/UTs are working to include **tenant farmers/actual cultivators**; Minister called it "cooperative federalism".

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture / GS2 Governance (e-governance, DPI).

## 2. Ministry of Earth Sciences (IITM): **AATAL — integrated air-pollution analysis lab inaugurated in Delhi** (24 Oct 2026)

**What / why it matters:** MoS Dr Jitendra Singh inaugurated the *Integrated Atmospheric Chemistry and Aerosol Toxicity Analysis Laboratory (AATAL)* at IITM's Delhi branch, ahead of the winter smog season. It aims to identify pollution sources in near real time and estimate each source's relative share, and to study aerosol toxicity.

**Key facts**
- Set up under **Mission Mausam** at a cost of about **₹80 crore**.
- Sources covered: vehicles, industry, biomass/crop-residue burning, construction and road dust, and transported pollution.
- Works with IITM's **AIRWISE** system (forecasts + observations + source apportionment).
- Evidence meant to support the **Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)** and **GRAP** planning.
- "World's first of its kind" is the Minister's claim — not independently verified.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment / Science & Tech.

## 3. Ministry of Rural Development: **Upgraded NMMS app launched for VB-G RAM G** (23 Oct 2026, New Delhi) *(UPDATE — VB-G RAM G covered earlier; new is the app upgrade and progress data)*

**What / why it matters:** Union Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched an upgraded **National Mobile Monitoring System (NMMS)** app to curb fake attendance and wage claims under the rural employment scheme, built with technical support from **IIT Madras** and other agencies.

**Key facts**
- New: AI-based face verification with **liveness detection**; **offline attendance** uploaded when connectivity returns; anti-cloning/anti-tampering and device-security checks; **exception mechanism** for elderly, disabled or device-problem cases; AI photo **de-duplication**, with wage lists/Fund Transfer Orders held until flagged entries are verified.
- Builds on face authentication introduced in March 2026.
- Progress since 1 July 2026: ~**46 crore person-days**; **3.65 crore workers** from 2.89 crore households; 31.23 lakh worksites across 2.41 lakh+ gram panchayats; ~40 crore person-days via face authentication; **nine States** at 100% face-authenticated attendance.
- Allocation 2026-27: **₹95,692 crore** (Centre); with States' share over ₹1.51 lakh crore.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance / Welfare schemes; GS3 Economy (rural employment).

### Scheme-in-focus: **VB-G RAM G (Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin))**
- **Ministry:** Rural Development. **Implemented from:** 1 July 2026 (national rollout began from Andhra Pradesh).
- **Objective:** statutory rural wage-employment and livelihood mission under the Viksit Bharat vision.
- **Key features:** wages paid directly to bank accounts via muster rolls; attendance via NMMS with face authentication; Centre–State cost-sharing (Centre ₹95,692 cr in 2026-27); transparency tools (AI de-duplication, FTO holds).
- **Coverage:** rural households nationwide (3.65 crore workers so far this year).
- **Significance:** technology-led social audit/anti-leakage model; test of the accountability–inclusion balance (exception mechanism for those failing biometrics).
- *Not confirmed in sources read: guaranteed number of workdays and the legal relationship to MGNREGA — verify before citing.*

## 4. Ministry of Steel: **India remains a net importer of finished steel in H1 FY2026-27; consumption outpaces output** (data released early Oct; highlighted 23 Oct)

**What / why it matters:** India is the world's second-largest crude steel producer, yet domestic demand is growing faster than output, and imports have risen — relevant to the National Steel Policy, safeguard duties and import dependence.

**Key facts (April–September 2026)**
- Crude steel: ~**85.1 Mt** (+2.9%); finished steel production ~81.8 Mt (+3.7%).
- Finished-steel **consumption ~84.9 Mt (+7.5%; PIB headline cites ~7.6%)**.
- **Imports ~4.14 Mt (+23.8%)**; **exports ~3.55 Mt (+26.2%)** — net importer by volume (~0.59 Mt).
- September 2026 alone: consumption +8%, crude steel 14.1 Mt (+3.4%).

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (industry, infrastructure).

## 5. Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways: **Sagarmala Finance Corporation (SMFCL) — ₹5,107 crore disbursed; ₹60,205 crore target by FY2031** (23 Oct 2026) *(UPDATE — Sagarmala covered earlier; new is the SMFCL stocktake)*

**What / why it matters:** SMFCL is the state-owned **maritime-dedicated NBFC** (became an NBFC in June 2025) mobilising long-term finance for port-led development, shipbuilding and coastal connectivity — part of the maritime financing ecosystem.

**Key facts**
- Sanctions **₹11,563 crore**; disbursed **₹5,107 crore** (as of 30 Sep 2026).
- Target: **₹60,205 crore** cumulative disbursements by end-FY2030-31, dependent on equity support under RBI norms.
- Early loans: ₹150 cr to Dredging Corporation of India; ₹110 cr to Goa Shipyard.
- Maritime fund history: ₹4,300 cr → ₹8,000 cr (FY2025-26) → aim of ₹10,000 cr.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (infrastructure, financing) / Ports & Shipping.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1.** With reference to the Farmer ID milestone announced in October 2026, consider the following statements:
1. Farmer IDs are being created under the Digital Agriculture Mission, which was approved by the Union Cabinet in September 2024.
2. The IDs aim to reduce repeated document verification for access to credit, insurance and relief.
3. The initiative is implemented by the Ministry of Rural Development.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2.** The Integrated Atmospheric Chemistry and Aerosol Toxicity Analysis Laboratory (AATAL), recently in the news, is:
(a) an ISRO facility for satellite-based aerosol retrieval
(b) a facility at IITM, Delhi, set up under Mission Mausam to identify pollution sources and study aerosol toxicity
(c) a CPCB laboratory for testing vehicular emission norms
(d) a CSIR facility for testing industrial effluents

**Q3.** The upgraded National Mobile Monitoring System (NMMS) app launched in October 2026 for VB-G RAM G includes which of the following?
1. AI-based face verification with liveness detection
2. Offline attendance upload
3. An exception mechanism for workers whose face authentication fails for genuine reasons

Select the correct answer using the code below:
(a) 1 only (b) 1 and 2 only (c) 2 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

---

## Answers

1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; the Digital Agriculture Mission is under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers' Welfare, so 3 is wrong.
2. **(b)** — AATAL is at IITM (Ministry of Earth Sciences), Delhi, under Mission Mausam (~₹80 crore), for source identification and aerosol toxicity; it supports CAQM/GRAP planning.
3. **(d)** — All three features were announced for the upgraded NMMS app (developed with IIT Madras support).
