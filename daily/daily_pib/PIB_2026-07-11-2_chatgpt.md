# Daily PIB Briefing — 2026-07-11

## 1. Centre of Excellence on Human-Wildlife Conflict launched at WII-SACON, Coimbatore

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change  
**Source:** [PIB release, 10 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283188)  
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Environment; GS2 Governance

The Union Environment Minister inaugurated a Centre of Excellence on Human-Wildlife Conflict at Coimbatore and launched the National Human-Wildlife Conflict Portal. This is UPSC-relevant because it links biodiversity conservation with livelihood protection, technology-enabled governance and centre-state coordination in forest administration.

**Key facts/figures:**
- The CoE is to act as a national hub for research, innovation, policy support, capacity building and best-practice dissemination on human-wildlife conflict.
- The National Human-Wildlife Conflict Portal is designed for data management, knowledge sharing and decision support.
- The first edition of *Current Status of Human-Wildlife Conflict in India: An Overview* was also released.
- Workshop sessions focused on human-elephant conflict, human-big cat conflict, and technology/innovation for mitigation.

## 2. CPSEs must settle all MSME invoices through TReDS

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises  
**Source:** [PIB release, 10 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283195&lang=1&reg=3)  
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Economy; GS2 Governance

The Ministry of MSME has notified mandatory use of the Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS) by all operating Central Public Sector Enterprises for settlement of transactions with MSME suppliers. It matters for UPSC because delayed payments, working-capital access and public procurement discipline are recurring MSME-policy themes.

**Key facts/figures:**
- The notification is dated 30 June 2026 and gives effect to a Union Budget 2026-27 commitment.
- CPSEs must route MSME goods/services invoices through RBI-authorised TReDS platforms and disclose invoice routing/settlement details as specified by RBI.
- TReDS is an RBI-regulated electronic platform, operational since 2017, for financing and discounting MSME trade receivables.
- Five platforms are operational: RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart, C2treds and DTX.
- Invoice discounting on TReDS rose from Rs 40,000 crore in FY 2021-22 to Rs 3.47 lakh crore in FY 2025-26.

**Policy update-in-focus: TReDS settlement mandate**
- **Objective:** Ensure quicker, collateral-free working capital access for MSME suppliers to CPSEs.
- **Key features:** Mandatory CPSE invoice settlement through TReDS; RBI-authorised platforms; statutory-auditor certification of TReDS registration/compliance; competitive bidding by banks/NBFCs to discount invoices.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** MSME suppliers to operating CPSEs; MSME ecosystem of over 8.70 crore registered enterprises employing more than 38 crore persons.
- **Significance:** Converts approved public-sector invoices into financeable assets and sets a payment-discipline benchmark for large buyers.

## 3. India-Australia CSIR-TKDL agreement to protect traditional knowledge

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Science & Technology / CSIR, with IP Australia  
**Source:** [PIB release, 10 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283119&lang=1&reg=48)  
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology; GS1 Culture; GS2 International Relations

CSIR and IP Australia signed an agreement giving IP Australia access to India's Traditional Knowledge Digital Library during the 3rd India-Australia Annual Summit in Melbourne. The development is important for IPR, biopiracy prevention, traditional knowledge protection and India-Australia science cooperation.

**Key facts/figures:**
- TKDL was established in 2001 by CSIR and the Ministry of AYUSH.
- It documents more than 5.2 lakh formulations and practices from Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa and Yoga.
- Content is translated into English, German, French, Japanese and Spanish for use by patent examiners.
- With IP Australia, 18 patent offices now have TKDL access under non-disclosure agreements.
- TKDL prior-art evidence has contributed to more than 375 patent applications being revoked, rejected, amended, withdrawn or abandoned.

## 4. Drugs Rules amended for high alcohol-containing formulations

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Health and Family Welfare  
**Source:** [PIB release, 10 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283196&lang=1&reg=3)  
**Destination notebook:** GS2 Governance/Health regulation; GS3 Science & Technology

The Government amended the Drugs Rules, 1945 to regulate high alcohol-containing medicinal formulations more tightly. This matters for public health governance because products earlier exempted from licensing could contain very high ethyl alcohol levels and be diverted for intoxication.

**Key facts/figures:**
- Formulations containing more than 12% v/v ethyl alcohol in quantities exceeding 30 mL are no longer covered by the Schedule K exemption.
- Such products now require licences under the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.
- They have been shifted to Schedule H1, requiring sale against prescription by a registered medical practitioner and stricter record-keeping.
- Some exempt preparations, including tinctures and aromatic preparations, could contain ethyl alcohol up to 80-90% v/v.

**Regulatory update-in-focus: high alcohol-containing formulations**
- **Objective:** Prevent misuse/diversion while preserving legitimate therapeutic availability.
- **Key features:** Removal of Schedule K exemption; licensing requirement; Schedule H1 inclusion; prescription-only sale and stricter records.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Patients, pharmacists, state drug regulators and the regulated pharmaceutical supply chain.
- **Significance:** Strengthens rational-use regulation and closes a public-health loophole in medicinal alcohol products.

## 5. Department of Posts and TRAI to conduct village-level telecom network survey

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Communications; Department of Posts and TRAI  
**Source:** [PIB release, 10 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283414&lang=1&reg=3)  
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Infrastructure/Science & Technology; GS2 Governance

The Department of Posts and TRAI signed an MoU for a nationwide app-based survey of mobile network performance across rural India. It is relevant for digital inclusion, regulatory capacity and evidence-based infrastructure planning.

**Key facts/figures:**
- The survey will assess mobile network performance of Bharti Airtel, Reliance Jio, Vodafone Idea and BSNL across more than 5.68 lakh villages.
- More than 1.40 lakh rural Branch Post Offices will be leveraged.
- Gramin Dak Sevaks will conduct field survey operations during routine delivery using a TRAI-developed Android app.
- TRAI will provide the survey application, technical support, training and centralised monitoring.
- The partnership is for one year and aims to identify connectivity gaps and support evidence-based telecom decisions.

## 6. Bureau of Port Security review: coastal and port security architecture

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Home Affairs, with Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways  
**Source:** [PIB release, 10 July 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2283439&lang=1&reg=48)  
**Destination notebook:** GS3 Internal Security; GS3 Economy/Infrastructure

The Union Home Minister and Ports, Shipping and Waterways Minister reviewed progress on establishing the Bureau of Port Security. This is UPSC-relevant for coastal security, critical infrastructure protection and maritime governance after the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025.

**Key facts/figures:**
- The Bureau of Port Security is being established as a statutory body under Section 13 of the Merchant Shipping Act, 2025.
- It will be headed by a Director General and function under the Ministry of Ports, Shipping and Waterways.
- It will regulate and inspect security of ships and port facilities, with a dedicated division for port IT/cybersecurity.
- Directions included creating a database of port security personnel, container-scanning facilities at covered ports and CISF trial runs at major ports including Visakhapatnam Port, JNPT and Mundra Port.
- MHA also directed stronger security at fishing harbours and fish landing centres, and wider promotion of ISRO's Nabhmitra app among fishermen.

## 3 Prelims MCQs

**Q1. With reference to the Centre of Excellence on Human-Wildlife Conflict and the National Human-Wildlife Conflict Portal, consider the following statements:**

1. The Centre of Excellence was inaugurated at WII-SACON, Coimbatore.
2. The portal is intended to support data management, knowledge sharing and decision support for conflict mitigation.
3. The first edition of *Current Status of Human-Wildlife Conflict in India: An Overview* was released alongside the initiative.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 and 2 only  
(b) 2 and 3 only  
(c) 1 and 3 only  
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2. With reference to the Trade Receivables Discounting System (TReDS), consider the following statements:**

1. It is an RBI-regulated electronic platform for financing and discounting MSME trade receivables.
2. The new mandate requires all operating CPSEs to route settlement of MSME invoices through RBI-authorised TReDS platforms.
3. TReDS financing is intended to give MSME suppliers collateral-free access to working capital against approved invoices.

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

(a) 1 only  
(b) 1 and 2 only  
(c) 2 and 3 only  
(d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q3. The July 2026 amendment to the Drugs Rules, 1945 relating to high alcohol-containing formulations provides that:**

(a) all formulations containing any ethyl alcohol are banned from sale in India.  
(b) formulations with more than 12% v/v ethyl alcohol in quantities exceeding 30 mL are removed from Schedule K exemption and brought under Schedule H1.  
(c) alcohol-containing medicinal formulations are shifted from the Drugs and Cosmetics Act to the Food Safety and Standards Act.  
(d) only imported high alcohol-containing formulations require prescription-based sale.

## Answers

**Q1. (d)** — The MoEFCC release states all three: CoE at WII-SACON, a conflict portal for data/knowledge/decision support, and release of the overview publication.

**Q2. (d)** — TReDS is RBI-regulated, the CPSE mandate applies to MSME invoice settlement, and the platform enables collateral-free invoice discounting.

**Q3. (b)** — The amendment removes Schedule K exemption for formulations with more than 12% v/v ethyl alcohol in quantities above 30 mL and places them under Schedule H1.
