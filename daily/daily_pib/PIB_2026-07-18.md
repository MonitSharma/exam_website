## 1. Vikram-1 lifts off — India's first private orbital rocket launch succeeds

**Issuing ministry/body:** Prime Minister's Office / Department of Space (Skyroot Aerospace, under IN-SPACe authorisation)

Skyroot Aerospace's Vikram-1 lifted off from Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota on 18 July and injected its payload into a ~450 km low-Earth orbit, making it India's first privately developed orbital-class rocket to reach orbit. The PM congratulated the Skyroot team, and PIB issued a backgrounder on the launch. It validates the 2020 space-sector reforms (IN-SPACe as regulator-promoter of private players) and marks India's transition from ISRO-only orbital access.

**Key facts:** Mission named "Mission Aagaman"; ~22 m tall; payload capacity up to ~350 kg to LEO; three solid-fuel stages plus a liquid orbital adjustment module with a 3D-printed engine; Skyroot (founded 2018, Hyderabad) is India's first space-sector startup to cross a $1 billion valuation. Named after Vikram Sarabhai.

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (space sector reforms, IN-SPACe, private space economy)

## 2. "One Nation, One Time": White Rabbit-based IST distribution network inaugurated in Bengaluru

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution (Legal Metrology Division), with CSIR-NPL, ISRO, BSNL and SEBI

Union Minister Pralhad Joshi inaugurated India's first 'White Rabbit Technology'-based Indian Standard Time (IST) Distribution Demonstration Network at the Regional Reference Standards Laboratory (RRSL), Jakkur, Bengaluru (16 July). White Rabbit is an Ethernet-based protocol (originally from CERN) enabling sub-nanosecond time synchronisation, allowing IST to be disseminated over wide-area digital networks without relying on foreign GPS-based time sources — a "time sovereignty" and cyber-resilience measure.

**Key facts:** Implements the 'One Nation, One Time' vision; Legal Metrology Division is the nodal agency; critical users include telecom, power grids, banking/stock markets (SEBI) and digital payments, where precise timestamping is mandatory.

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (also GS2 Governance — standards/regulation)

### Initiative-in-focus: One Nation, One Time
- **Ministry:** Consumer Affairs (Legal Metrology) with CSIR-NPL and Department of Consumer Affairs
- **Objective:** A single, legally enforceable, high-precision IST across all digital systems in India
- **Key features:** (1) Regional Reference Standards Laboratories disseminate IST; (2) White Rabbit/precision time protocol networks replace GPS-derived time; (3) draft Legal Metrology (Indian Standard Time) Rules mandate IST use; (4) partnership of NPL (timekeeper), ISRO, BSNL, SEBI
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** Telecom, BFSI, power, strategic sectors nationwide
- **Significance:** Digital sovereignty, cybersecurity, accurate transaction timestamping

## 3. Land records meet lending: DoLR explores DPI convergence with RBI Innovation Hub

**Issuing ministry/body:** Department of Land Resources (DoLR), Ministry of Rural Development

DoLR Secretary Narendra Bhooshan met the RBI Innovation Hub (RBIH) leadership to explore converging the Digital Public Infrastructure of land governance (digitised land records under DILRMP) with digital lending via the Unified Lending Interface (ULI). The aim: secure, standardised API access to authenticated land records so Kisan Credit Card and farm loans can be sanctioned digitally, cutting transaction costs and processing time for rural borrowers.

**Key facts:** Emphasis on common data standards, API interoperability and wider use of ULPIN ("Bhu-Aadhaar" — the 14-digit Unique Land Parcel Identification Number); builds on the Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme (DILRMP).

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance (DPI, e-governance) / GS3 Agriculture (rural credit)

## 4. ICAR turns 98 — 386 improved crop varieties in one year, 94% climate-resilient

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)

At ICAR's 98th Foundation Day (16 July, New Delhi), Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan announced that ICAR developed 386 improved varieties across 44 crops in the past year — 94% climate-resilient and 29 biofortified. He also released 43 new varieties, 17 technologies and 14 publications, including saline/alkaline-tolerant rice, export-oriented mango production technology and India's first indigenous African Swine Fever vaccine.

**Key facts:** ICAR founded 1929; other releases included a Digital Swine Disease Atlas and a low-cost cassava harvester; framing tied to "Viksit Krishi for Viksit Bharat 2047."

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture (climate-resilient agriculture, biofortification)

## 5. INS Malvan — second Mahe-class ASW Shallow Water Craft to be commissioned on 22 July

**Issuing ministry/body:** Ministry of Defence (Indian Navy)

The Navy announced that Malvan, the second Mahe-class Anti-Submarine Warfare Shallow Water Craft (ASW-SWC), will be commissioned on 22 July; its crest was unveiled on 18 July. Built by Cochin Shipyard Ltd with over 80% indigenous content, the class is designed to detect and destroy submarines in shallow coastal waters and to protect harbours — filling a gap that larger frigates (like the recently commissioned INS Mahendragiri, covered 14 July) cannot address close to shore.

**Key facts:** Commissioning at Kochi, presided over by Chief of the Air Staff Air Chief Marshal A.P. Singh; roles include coastal surveillance, harbour defence and ASW in shallow waters; Mahe-class named after coastal towns.

**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security / Defence (coastal security, Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence)

## 6. Core sector gets a new lens: revised Index of Core Industries with base year 2022-23

**Issuing ministry/body:** Office of Economic Adviser, DPIIT, Ministry of Commerce & Industry

The OEA announced (17 July) that the revised Index of Core Industries (ICI) series with base year 2022-23 will be released on 20 July, replacing the 2011-12 series. Iron ore becomes the ninth core industry; steel will use gross (not net) production for IIP consistency; coal is simplified to raw coal to remove double counting. This follows the base-year modernisation drive across official statistics (new IIP and CPI series covered earlier).

**Key facts:** Weights derived pro-rata from the IIP (2022-23) series of MoSPI; first release will carry provisional ICI for June 2026 plus a 38-month back series (April 2023–May 2026). Existing eight core industries: coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilizers, steel, cement, electricity.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (index numbers, industrial statistics)

## 3 Prelims MCQs (based on today's PIB items)

**Q1. With reference to the Vikram-1 launch vehicle, recently in news, consider the following statements:**
1. It is India's first privately developed orbital-class rocket to place a payload in orbit.
2. It uses liquid fuel in all its stages.
3. It was launched from the Satish Dhawan Space Centre, Sriharikota.

Which of the statements given above are correct?
- (a) 1 and 2 only
- (b) 1 and 3 only
- (c) 2 and 3 only
- (d) 1, 2 and 3

**Q2. The "White Rabbit Technology", recently inaugurated for a demonstration network in Bengaluru, is best described as:**
- (a) A quantum-encryption protocol for securing UPI transactions
- (b) An Ethernet-based protocol for sub-nanosecond synchronisation and dissemination of Indian Standard Time
- (c) A satellite constellation for GPS-independent navigation
- (d) An AI platform for real-time monitoring of food supply chains

**Q3. Which of the following has been added as the ninth core industry in the revised Index of Core Industries (base year 2022-23)?**
- (a) Aluminium
- (b) Copper
- (c) Iron ore
- (d) Automobiles

---

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: Vikram-1 uses three solid-fuel stages plus a liquid-fuelled orbital adjustment module — not all-liquid propulsion.

**Q2 — (b).** White Rabbit (developed at CERN) enables ultra-precise, Ethernet-based time synchronisation; India is using it to distribute IST without dependence on foreign GPS time under 'One Nation, One Time'.

**Q3 — (c).** Iron ore joins the eight existing core industries (coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilizers, steel, cement, electricity) in the 2022-23 base-year ICI series.
