## BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. India–Oman CEPA enters into force (IR)
**Headline:** India's Comprehensive Economic Partnership Agreement (CEPA) with Oman came into force on **1 June 2026**, deepening the Gulf footprint amid the Strait of Hormuz crisis.

**Exam angle:** Highest-priority IR item. Tests FTA terminology (CEPA vs CECA vs FTA), India's Gulf/West Asia strategy, and energy/maritime security. The Hormuz disruption makes Oman a *map-and-case-study* item: most of Oman's coast faces the Arabian Sea/Gulf of Oman, *outside* the Strait, so its ports double as an alternative corridor.

**Memorize:**
- India's **2nd FTA with a Gulf (GCC) nation**, after the **UAE CEPA (2022)**. Signed during PM Modi's **Muscat visit, Dec 2025**.
- Duty-free access for **99.38% of India's exports to Oman by value** (covers 98.08% of Oman's tariff lines); up from just **15.33%** under the earlier MFN regime.
- Key Oman ports outside Hormuz: **Duqm and Salalah** (Arabian Sea). ~Two-thirds of India's crude/LNG transits Hormuz, so this matters for energy security.

**Notebook:** GS2 IR (+ GS3 Economy, external sector).
**Static anchor:** *Mrunal Economy — Pillar 3 (BoP / Foreign Trade / FTAs).* Add Oman to India's operational FTA list (UAE 2022, Oman 2026); cross-link to your West Asia/Gulf notes and the Strait of Hormuz map.

### 2. DRDO–IAF flight-test RudraM-II missile (Sci-Tech / Defence)
**Headline:** DRDO and the IAF successfully flight-tested the indigenous **RudraM-II air-to-surface missile** on **2 June 2026** from a Su-30 MKI.

**Exam angle:** Strong Prelims fact-MCQ on India's missile arsenal and *Aatmanirbhar Bharat* in defence. Watch the "anti-radiation / stand-off strike" descriptor — easy to confuse RudraM-I/II with surface-to-air or ballistic systems.

**Memorize:**
- **Air-to-surface** missile for **stand-off precision strike** (targets radars, C2 nodes, air-defence assets — i.e., SEAD/anti-radiation role).
- Launched from **Su-30 MKI**; flight data tracked by **Integrated Test Range (ITR), Chandipur (Odisha)**.
- Indigenous; part of **Aatmanirbhar Bharat** defence push (successor to RudraM-I / NGARM).

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Defence) (+ GS3 Internal Security cross-link).
**Static anchor:** *Vision IAS / Shankar Sci-Tech — India's Missiles page.* Add RudraM-II under "air-to-surface / anti-radiation"; tabulate against Agni, Prithvi, BrahMos, Astra, Akash, Nag to keep launch-platform vs range vs role straight.

**Exam angle:** Classic monetary-policy MCQ territory (corridor instruments, MPC composition, stance vocabulary). The "pause" is justified by **West Asia energy shock / Hormuz disruption** pushing inflation toward ~5% — links macro to IR.

### C. Mains-Style Question

**Q (GS2 / GS3).** "The India–Oman CEPA, operationalised amid disruptions in the Strait of Hormuz, reflects the growing convergence of trade, energy security and strategic autonomy in India's Gulf policy." **Examine.** *(15 marks, 250 words)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Set context — Oman CEPA in force (1 June 2026), against Hormuz disruption and India's deep Gulf stakes (energy imports, ~9 million diaspora, trade, remittances).
- **Trade convergence:** 2nd Gulf FTA after UAE CEPA; ~99% duty-free coverage; export diversification and supply-chain de-risking; complements IMEC/I2U2 architecture.
- **Energy & maritime security:** Oman's Duqm/Salalah lie *outside* Hormuz → alternative corridor; cushions the chokepoint risk through which most of India's crude/LNG flows.
- **Strategic autonomy:** Balancing across Gulf partners; defence/logistics access at Duqm; rupee-trade and investment potential — Gulf policy as multi-alignment, not dependence.
- **Conclusion:** A positive-sum partnership; deepen via investment, energy storage, and security cooperation while diversifying chokepoint exposure.

## ANSWERS

**Q1 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** Oman is India's *second* GCC FTA (UAE CEPA, 2022, was first), so (1) is wrong. Duqm/Salalah are on the Arabian Sea outside Hormuz (2 correct); duty-free coverage is 99.38% by value (3 correct).

**Q2 — (b) An air-to-surface missile launched from the Su-30 MKI.** RudraM-II is DRDO's indigenous air-to-surface (anti-radiation/stand-off) missile, tested from a Su-30 MKI; data tracked by ITR Chandipur.

**Q3 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Repo held at 5.25% (1 correct) and stance kept neutral (2 correct). The MPC is chaired by the **RBI Governor**, not the Finance Minister, so (3) is wrong.

**Q4 — (c) 7.8%.** NSO provisional estimate for Q4 (Jan–Mar) FY26; full-year FY26 revised up to 7.7%.

**Q5 — (b) 2 only.** WED 2026 was hosted by **Azerbaijan (Baku)**, not India, so (1) is wrong. India's tally of 58 tiger reserves and 100 Ramsar sites (2) is correct.

**Q6 — (c) Both 1 and 2.** The Montreux Record flags Ramsar sites facing adverse ecological change (1 correct). India's two Montreux Record sites are Keoladeo NP (Rajasthan) and Loktak Lake (Manipur) (2 correct).

**Q7 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** MSF sits above repo (ceiling) and SDF below it (floor) — both correct. A repo *hike* is **contractionary**, not expansionary, so (3) is wrong.
