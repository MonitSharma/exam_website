# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 8 November 2026

*Window: 6–8 Nov 2026. Items cleared against the last 14 briefings and question sets (Step 0). Air-quality is re-featured only for the new Supreme Court development (suo motu hearing, 6 Nov); NCAP basics were covered on 30 Oct and are not repeated.*

## Briefing

### 1. SC clarifies the OBC creamy-layer ruling: CSE 2025 service allocation must follow it (6 Nov) — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** In *Union of India v. Rohith Nathan* (11 Mar 2026) the SC held that a parent's salary alone cannot decide OBC creamy-layer status; the rank/category of the post must be weighed (the 1993 DoPT OM excluded salary from the income test, while a 2004 clarification counted PSU/private salaries, producing unequal treatment). The Centre sought a clarification on temporal effect; a Bench of Justices P.S. Narasimha and R. Mahadevan ruled on 6 Nov. Links Art. 14, 15(4), 16(4) and the *Indra Sawhney* (1992) creamy-layer doctrine.
**Memorise:** Grant of the benefit does not reopen concluded selections or seniority; post-11 Mar 2026 selections follow the 1993 OM as read in the judgment; CSE 2025 allocation (not yet finalised; 958 candidates recommended) is governed by it; the Court noted DoPT's assurance of supernumerary posts and refused to fix a timeline for post-equivalence (Centre had asked for two years). Current income limit for non-government parents: ₹8 lakh.
**Notebook:** GS2 Polity/Governance (Reservation, Judiciary).
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth — *Fundamental Rights* (Art. 15, 16) and *Backward Classes Commission*; Shankar IAS — Reservation. *Nudge:* add a "Rohith Nathan (2026)" line under Indra Sawhney and cross-link to the 1993 OM/2004 clarification.

### 2. India and EU conclude negotiations on India's association with Horizon Europe (7 Nov) — International Relations / Science & Tech
**Exam angle:** Association is the closest form of cooperation the EU offers non-EU countries: Indian researchers and firms can get direct funding and lead consortia (chiefly Pillar II, "Global Challenges and European Industrial Competitiveness"). It builds on the 2001 India–EU S&T agreement (renewed to 2030) and arrives ahead of an expected India–EU FTA signing in mid-December.
**Memorise:** Programme budget ~€93.5 bn (2021–27); association expected from January 2027 once the agreement is signed and procedures are completed; associated countries contribute financially and participate on equal terms.
**Notebook:** GS2 IR (India–EU); GS3 Sci-Tech (research ecosystem).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal/Shankar IAS — India–EU relations; Science & Tech policy (ANRF, STI policy). *Nudge:* add "Horizon Europe association" to the India–EU timeline next to the FTA.

### 3. Record EV retail in October 2026: ~3.55 lakh units, up ~45% (FADA, 6 Nov) — Economy
**Exam angle:** EV adoption as a lever for the oil-import bill and emissions goals; segment mix (two- and three-wheelers) matters more than car headline numbers. Good Mains data point for "transport decarbonisation".
**Memorise:** ~3,54,625 EVs vs ~2,44,764 in Oct 2025; penetration ~11.5% (vs ~6%); e-2W 2,24,064 (+48.5%, ~9.7% share); e-PV 37,049 (+76.5%); e-3W 88,570 (~63% of the 3W segment). *Caveat: segment figures sum slightly below the stated total (reporting gap).*
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy / Infrastructure (Transport); Environment.
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh/Shankar IAS — Infrastructure: transport; PM E-DRIVE / PLI auto. *Nudge:* update the EV data box with Oct 2026 penetration.

### 4. SC suo motu on Delhi-NCR air: stop-gap curbs "old, tried and failed" (6 Nov) — Environment
*New since 30 Oct:* a CJI Surya Kant-led Bench (with Justices Joymalya Bagchi and V. Mohana) asked CAQM for a scientific strategy beyond temporary vehicle/construction halts.
**Exam angle:** Judicial scrutiny of CAQM (a statutory body under the CAQM Act, 2021) and GRAP's emergency-response design versus long-term source control.
**Memorise:** Court sought a report on enforcement of its orders; will seek expert opinion on hybrid/closed physical classes (no order yet); ASG said PUC certificates are geotagged and fuel is refused without a valid one; farm fires fell this year, notably in Haryana; next hearing 16 Nov.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (Pollution); GS2 Governance.
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS — Environmental Pollution; Laxmikanth — Art. 21/48A, NGT & Judiciary. *Nudge:* add "emergency vs structural" framing to the air-pollution notes.

### 5. India's 18 Biosphere Reserves: 13 in UNESCO's World Network (PIB backgrounder, 2 Nov; Intl Day 3 Nov) — Environment / Geography (Places in News)
**Exam angle:** Zonation (core, buffer, transition), the MAB Programme and which Indian reserves are in the WNBR are classic Prelims mapping facts.
**Memorise:** 18 notified BRs, 13 in WNBR; not in WNBR: Manas, Dibru-Saikhowa, Dehang-Dibang, Great Rann of Kutch, Seshachalam; first designated Nilgiri (1986); latest WNBR addition Cold Desert, Himachal (2025); largest Great Rann of Kutch (12,454 sq km), smallest Dibru-Saikhowa (765 sq km). MAB launched 1971; global network 797 reserves in 145 countries. Funding via Biodiversity Conservation Scheme (60:40; 90:10 for NE/Himalayan states).
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment; GS1 Geography (mapping).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS — Protected Area Network; NCERT Class 12 Geography/Biology. *Nudge:* mark the 5 non-WNBR reserves on your map.

### B3. Mains question
**Q8.** *Critically examine* how the Supreme Court's 2026 rulings on the OBC creamy layer reshape the balance between social justice and administrative certainty. (15 marks, 250 words)
**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. Intro: Indra Sawhney (1992) mandated creamy-layer exclusion; the 1993 OM excluded salary income but the 2004 clarification counted PSU/private salaries, producing unequal treatment.
2. Rohith Nathan (11 Mar 2026): parental salary alone is not decisive; post category matters — arguments of equality under Arts. 14, 15, 16 (curing "hostile discrimination").
3. 6 Nov clarification: no reopening of concluded selections; CSE 2025 allocation follows the ruling; supernumerary posts; no timeline for post-equivalence — administrative certainty versus substantive equality.
4. Way forward: a uniform, rule-based post-equivalence mechanism, periodic revision of income limits, transparency; conclude with constitutional morality.

## Answers
- **Q1 — (b).** Statements 1 and 2 reflect the 6 Nov order; the Court declined to set any timeline for post-equivalence (the Centre had asked for two years), so 3 is wrong.
- **Q2 — (c).** Talks concluded 7 Nov; ~€93.5 bn (2021–27); association expected from Jan 2027. Associated countries contribute financially, so 3 is wrong.
- **Q3 — (b).** e-2W (2,24,064) is the largest segment; e-3W (88,570) outsold e-PV (37,049), so 4 is wrong.
- **Q4 — (c).** The Court called the curbs "old, tried and failed" and CAQM is statutory (CAQM Act, 2021); no order for hybrid classes was passed (it will seek expert opinion).
- **Q5 — (d).** 18 notified, 13 in WNBR; Cold Desert (2025) is the latest addition; Manas is among those not in WNBR.
- **Q6 — (a).** *Indra Sawhney* upheld 27% OBC reservation, required creamy-layer exclusion, set the 50% ceiling as a general rule and disallowed reservation in promotions (later changed by the 77th Amendment).
- **Q7 — (c).** Core: strictly protected; buffer: research, education, regulated ecotourism; transition: settlements and sustainable activities.
