## Part A — Briefing

### 1. Cyclone Arnab makes landfall on the Andhra Pradesh–Yanam coast (28 Oct) — Geography / Disaster Management
- **Exam angle:** Bay of Bengal cyclones peak in the post-monsoon season (Oct–Dec) and the east coast bears most of the damage. Evacuation counts and shelter numbers show how the NDMA/SDMA model works. The tie-in is IMD's cyclone classification and naming under the WMO/ESCAP Panel.
- **Key facts:** Crossed the AP–Yanam coast near Narsapur on 28 Oct with sustained winds of 125–135 kmph, gusting to 150 kmph (the "very severe cyclonic storm" band). More than 84,000 people were evacuated to about 290 shelters; 3 deaths reported (press figures). Flagged for verification: "Arnab" is, to my knowledge, the Bangladesh-proposed name in the WMO/ESCAP list.
- **Notebook:** GS1 Geography (and GS3 Disaster Management).
- **Static anchor:** Savindra Singh / NCERT Class XI *Fundamentals of Physical Geography*, "Tropical Cyclones" chapter. Add this cyclone as a case study (landfall site, wind band, evacuation numbers) and cross-link it to the NDMA cyclone guidelines.

### 2. Kuno: a 19-month-old cheetah dies of suspected shotgun injuries; poaching probe ordered — Environment
- **Exam angle:** This is the first reported cheetah death at Kuno from suspected shooting (as opposed to disease or intra-species injury), so it bears on human–wildlife conflict and on how well Project Cheetah is run outside the park. An SIT has been formed, and the NTCA and WCCB sent a team.
- **Key facts:** Five pellets were recovered from the body (27 Oct). The animal is reported to be a cub of the female Nirva, found near Morena/Sheopur. The cheetah was declared extinct in India in 1952. Project Cheetah began at Kuno on 17 Sep 2022 with Namibian cheetahs, followed by South African cheetahs in Feb 2023. Reports differ on the animal's sex and on its ID (KNG22), so don't memorize either.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment (conservation).
- **Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, "Species in News" and the Wildlife Protection Act 1972 (NTCA is a statutory body). Add a line under Project Cheetah: "first suspected poaching mortality, Oct 2026".

### 3. Cabinet approves PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana, ₹7,395.55 crore for 2026-27 to 2030-31 — Polity & Governance (Schemes)
- **Exam angle:** A Centrally Sponsored Scheme for Panchayati Raj capacity-building and Gram Panchayat planning (the earlier flagship was the Rashtriya Gram Swaraj Abhiyan, RGSA, restructured in 2022 — treat the successor link as likely, not confirmed). Mains line: deepening of the 73rd Amendment through *functions, funds and functionaries*.
- **Key facts:** Centre ₹4,869.48 cr and States ₹2,526.07 cr. About 2.55 lakh Gram Panchayat plans a year. About 32 lakh elected representatives to benefit. PANCHAM platform in 17 languages (these numbers come from a single aggregator, so flag them).
- **Notebook:** GS2 Polity/Governance.
- **Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, "Panchayati Raj" chapter (Part IX, Arts. 243–243O; Eleventh Schedule). Update the scheme list and cross-link it to GPDP / People's Plan Campaign.

### 4. India and Mexico sign Terms of Reference to launch Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA) talks — International Relations / Economy
- **Exam angle:** India is seeking a limited-scope PTA after Mexico raised tariffs sharply (reported at up to 50%) on goods from countries with no FTA with it. A PTA covers only a negotiated list of tariff lines, which makes it narrower than an FTA, CECA or CEPA. Sectors cited: autos, pharma, electronics, critical minerals.
- **Key facts:** Signing reported on 27-28 Oct (earlier press said "likely"; confirm via MEA/Commerce Ministry when you can). Mexico belongs to USMCA. India–EFTA TEPA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland) came into force on 1 Oct 2025, with a USD 100 bn FDI target over 15 years.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (Latin America) and GS3 Economy (external sector).
- **Static anchor:** Mrunal, "Trade agreements: PTA vs FTA vs CEPA". Add Mexico to the "India's Latin America engagement" map notes (with Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Peru).

### 5. IIP grows 6.7% in September 2026 (revised base 2022-23); mining contracts — Economy
- **Exam angle:** IIP is a lead indicator of the industrial cycle. The revised base year (2022-23) and the use-based split show capital-goods and intermediate-goods demand.
- **Key facts:** Overall 6.7% y/y (August: 8.0%; July: 6.7%). Manufacturing 7.6%, electricity 11.2%, mining −6.2%. Capital goods 10.4%, intermediate goods 12.2%. Only the August and July figures were confirmed against a second source, so treat the September breakdown as single-source.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy.
- **Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh, "Industry and Infrastructure" / "Indices". Update the IIP row with base 2022-23 and the Sept 2026 print. Cross-link to the Core-industries note from 23 Oct.

### B3. Mains question

**Q.** *Critically examine* how far strengthening Gram Panchayats through centrally sponsored capacity-building schemes can fulfil the vision of the 73rd Constitutional Amendment. (15 marks, 250 words)

**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. *Intro:* Part IX and the Eleventh Schedule give Panchayats 29 subjects, yet devolution varies widely across States. Cite the new scheme (₹7,395.55 cr, 2026-27 to 2030-31).
2. *Case for:* training for about 32 lakh representatives, better GPDP quality (about 2.55 lakh plans a year), multilingual digital platforms (PANCHAM), and convergence with local goals.
3. *Limits:* devolution of the three Fs (functions, funds, functionaries) is a State subject, so own-source revenue stays weak. Capacity is uneven, and sarpanch-pati proxy rule persists. Centrally sponsored design can crowd out local priorities.
4. *Way forward:* State Finance Commissions with timely action-taken reports, activity mapping, empowered Gram Sabhas, social audit, and untied funds. Conclude with a link to Mahatma Gandhi's *Gram Swaraj*.

## Part C — Answers

1. **(b)** Statements 1 and 2 are correct; the 125–135 kmph band sits within IMD's "very severe cyclonic storm" category (118–166 kmph). Statement 3 is wrong: names come from agreed WMO/ESCAP panel lists contributed by member countries, and IMD assigns the next name on the list.
2. **(b)** Project Cheetah began at Kuno in 2022, using African cheetahs from Namibia and South Africa. Cheetahs were declared extinct in India in 1952, not 1972.
3. **(b)** PTAs cover a negotiated tariff-line list. A PTA is narrower than a CEPA, not broader. TEPA is with EFTA (Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, Switzerland), all non-EU.
4. **(d)** All three are correct: base 2022-23, manufacturing has the largest weight, and use-based groups include capital and intermediate goods.
5. **(b)** The Eleventh Schedule has 29 subjects for Panchayats (Art. 243G). The Twelfth Schedule has the 18 municipal functions.
6. **(b)** Increasing intensity: Depression, Deep Depression, Cyclonic Storm, Severe Cyclonic Storm, then Very Severe, Extremely Severe and Super Cyclonic Storm.

*Verification notes: Cyclone, cheetah and PMVGSY details come from press reports plus an aggregator. The India–Mexico signing and the IIP September breakdown rest on a single aggregator source each. The Mains PYQ year is unconfirmed.*
