### 4. ISRO's NVS-03 slated for ~30 Oct on GSLV: the push to restore NavIC — Science & Technology
- **Exam angle:** NavIC's reliability crisis (ageing rubidium clocks, NVS-02 failure) makes NVS-03 strategic: defence, aviation, Aadhaar-linked and fleet use. Classic UPSC space-tech terrain (NavIC was a Prelims 2018 question).
- **Facts to memorise:** Third satellite of the **second-generation NVS series**; launch vehicle **GSLV** from **Sriharikota** (date reported as 30 Oct; vehicle reported variously as GSLV Mk II/GSLV-F18, so verify on the ISRO notice). **11** NavIC satellites launched so far, **8** functional; only **3** serving navigation, **4** needed for a 3D fix. **NVS-02 (29 Jan 2025)** failed to reach intended orbit. **IRNSS-1F** lost its atomic clock after its 10-year design life. Features: indigenous rubidium clocks, **L1, L5 and S-band**, coverage up to **~1,500 km** beyond India, **SPS** (civil) and **RS** (restricted).
- **Notebook:** GS3 – Science & Technology (Space).
- **Static anchor:** Shankar/Spectrum – Space technology: navigation (GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, NavIC); orbits. *Nudge:* update your NavIC card with the NVS series and the 3 GEO + 4 IGSO original design.

### 5. State Legislative Councils back in focus: elections to 28 seats in UP, Bihar, Maharashtra and Karnataka (23 Oct) — Polity & Governance
- **Exam angle:** Bicameralism at State level: Art. 169 creation/abolition, composition, and weak powers versus Rajya Sabha. UPSC asked Legislative Councils in **Mains 2021 (GS2)**.
- **Facts to memorise:** Elections on **23 Oct 2026** for **28 seats**. **Six** States have Councils: **UP, Bihar, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh**. Members' term **6 years**, **one-third retire every 2 years**, minimum age **30**. Parliament creates/abolishes a Council by **simple law** on the Assembly's special-majority resolution (Art. 169); not an Art. 368 amendment. Andhra Pradesh abolished its Council in **1985**, revived **2007**; Tamil Nadu abolished it in **1986**. Council delays an ordinary Bill by up to **3 months** (first time) and **1 month** (second); Money Bill: must return within **14 days**. Strength is at most **one-third of Assembly, minimum 40**.
- **Notebook:** GS2 – Polity (State legislature).
- **Static anchor:** Laxmikanth – State Legislature (Arts. 168–177); Shankar IAS – Bicameralism. *Nudge:* add a Rajya Sabha vs Legislative Council comparison table.

### B3. Mains Question (GS2 · 15 marks · 250 words)
**Critically examine** why reform of the UN Security Council has remained elusive despite broad support, and suggest what strategy India should adopt to advance its claim.

*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. **Intro:** UNSC composition frozen since 1965 expansion (non-permanent seats 6 → 10); India calls it a "legitimacy deficit"; IGN talks since 2009 have produced no text.
2. **Why stalled:** Charter amendment needs two-thirds GA plus all P5 ratification (Art. 108); P5 reluctance over veto; UfC (Italy, Pakistan etc.) opposes new permanent seats; divided blocs (G4 vs African Union's Ezulwini demand for veto-bearing seats); no text-based negotiation.
3. **India's case:** population, economy, peacekeeping contributions, eight terms, democratic credentials; push for 2028–29 seat to retain visibility.
4. **Way forward:** insist on text-based IGN, coalition-building with Africa and the Global South, interim "longer-term/renewable seats" as a stepping stone, reform through UNGA revitalisation and G4-L.69 convergence.

## PART C — ANSWERS

1. **(b)** — India seeks both categories; Art. 108 needs two-thirds including all P5. Statement 3 wrong: India has served eight times (latest 2021–22).
2. **(a)** — PLFS is run by MoSPI/NSO (not Labour Bureau); monthly CWS estimates since 2025; platform-worker stratum proposed.
3. **(a)** — Agreed at COP27, operationalised at COP28; World Bank is interim trustee. Statement 3 wrong: the Fund is designed to provide grants and concessional finance, not only loans.
4. **(b)** — NVS-03 is a second-generation satellite; 4 satellites needed for a 3D fix. NavIC covers India plus about 1,500 km beyond.
5. **(b)** — Ordinary law under Art. 169; size limits as stated. A Council must return a Money Bill within 14 days (recommendations only), not delay it for three months.
6. **(a)** — Ten non-permanent members, two-year terms, no immediate re-election.
7. **(a)** — Original design: 3 GEO + 4 IGSO (inclined geosynchronous) satellites.
