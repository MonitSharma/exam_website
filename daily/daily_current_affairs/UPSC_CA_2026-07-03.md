# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 2026-07-03

## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 replaces MGNREGA — guaranteed employment rises to 125 days
**Headline:** From 1 July 2026, the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025 (VB-G RAM G) came into force nationwide, replacing the two-decade-old Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (MGNREGA), and raising the annual guaranteed wage-employment entitlement from 100 to 125 days per rural household.
- **Exam angle:** Tests the DPSP/right-to-work lineage (Article 41) and a genuine fiscal-federalism shift — MGNREGA's 100% Central wage funding gives way to Centre-State cost-sharing — a fresh angle on a well-worn PYQ theme (MGNREGA eligibility/objectives, see Part B).
- **Key facts to memorise:** Guarantee raised from 100 to 125 days/household/FY; new funding pattern — 90:10 (Centre:State) for NE/Himalayan States and UTs with legislature, 60:40 for other States/UTs with legislature, 100% Central for UTs without legislature (vs earlier 100% Central wage funding); Sections 6(1)/6(2) allow a statutory pause of up to 60 days during sowing/harvesting; Sections 4(5)/4(6) introduce "normative allocation" (capped, supply-driven) replacing the demand-driven Labour Budget; ₹300/day floor wage notified, 21 States/UTs raised to this level; interim allocation of ₹95,600 crore to States/UTs; launched at a national event in Andhra Pradesh; rationale cited FY2024-25 misappropriation of ₹193.67 crore and only 7.61% of households completing the full 100 days under MGNREGA.
- **Notebook:** GS2 (Polity & Governance — DPSP Article 41, rights-based legislation, cooperative/fiscal federalism) with a GS3 (Economy — rural employment) cross-tag.
- **Static anchor:** *Laxmikanth — "Directive Principles of State Policy"* (Art 41, right to work) + *Ramesh Singh/Mrunal — "Rural Employment/MGNREGA"* chapter. **Nudge:** replace your MGNREGA note with VB-G RAM G's 125-day entitlement, cost-sharing ratios, and four-domain infrastructure stack (water security, rural infra, livelihood infra, extreme-weather mitigation); retain MGNREGA only as a "repealed scheme" comparison row.

### 2. India Semiconductor Mission 2.0 gets ₹1.25 lakh crore outlay clearance
**Headline:** The Expenditure Finance Committee cleared an outlay of about ₹1.25 lakh crore (~$13 billion) for India Semiconductor Mission (ISM) 2.0 — up from ISM 1.0's ₹76,000 crore — clearing the way for Union Cabinet consideration, reported 3 July 2026.
- **Exam angle:** High-yield Science & Technology–Economy crossover on chip self-reliance; tests ISM's institutional design and value-chain vocabulary (fabrication, packaging, compound semiconductors) — a natural "which of the following is/are true" Prelims setup.
- **Key facts to memorise:** ISM 2.0 outlay ≈ ₹1.25 lakh crore (~$13.21 bn) vs ISM 1.0's ₹76,000 crore (~$8 bn); announced in Union Budget 2026-27; covers the full value chain — chip design, fabrication, packaging, compound semiconductors, and raw materials (gases, ingots); current pipeline under ISM 1.0 = 12 approved manufacturing projects worth ~₹1.64 lakh crore (1 semiconductor fab, 2 compound-semiconductor fabs, 9 packaging units); CG Semi's commercial production was inaugurated on 4 July 2026 — among the first under the mission.
- **Notebook:** GS3 (Science & Technology — indigenous technology/electronics manufacturing) with a GS3 (Economy — industrial policy) cross-tag.

### 5. MANAS: fresh June-2026 data on India's National Narcotics Helpline
**Headline:** Fresh June-2026 usage data released via PIB show MANAS (Madak Padarth Nishedh Asoochna Kendra) — the National Narcotics Helpline launched by the Union Home Minister in 2024 — has logged nearly 10 lakh connection attempts and escalated 16,200+ actionable leads to NCB/state anti-narcotics units, reported 3 July 2026.
- **Exam angle:** An internal-security + digital-governance crossover on India's anti-narcotics architecture; tests the acts/institutions ladder (NDPS 1985, PITNDPS 1988, NCORD, NIA's 2020 narco-terrorism mandate) that Prelims regularly probes.
- **Key facts to memorise:** MANAS launched 18 July 2024 by the Union Home Minister under the Narcotics Control Bureau (NCB), Ministry of Home Affairs, with Digital India Corporation as technology partner; accessible via helpline 1933, web portal, UMANG app; counselling calls are transferred to the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment's Helpline 14446. June-2026 data: ~10 lakh connection attempts since launch, 2,65,673 drug-related inputs logged, 16,200+ leads escalated to 30 NCB Zonal Units/36 State-UT Anti-Narcotics Task Forces, 47,500+ calls connected to counselling. Legal base: NDPS Act, 1985 (offences/penalties) and PITNDPS Act, 1988 (preventive detention); NCORD mechanism (4-tier: Apex-Executive-State-District) formed 2016, restructured 2019; NIA empowered under the NDPS Act in 2020 to investigate narco-terrorism cases.
- **Notebook:** GS3 (Internal Security — narco-terrorism, anti-trafficking architecture) with a GS2 (Governance — digital public service delivery) cross-tag.

### Mains-Style Question

**Q.** "The transition from MGNREGA to the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 marks a shift from a rights-based, demand-driven employment guarantee to a budget-capped, supply-driven model." Critically examine this statement with reference to its implications for cooperative fiscal federalism and rural livelihood security. *(GS Paper 2/3, 250 words, 15 marks)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. **Introduction:** State the core shift — entitlement rises to 125 days (from 100), but 100% Central wage funding is replaced by Centre-State cost-sharing (90:10/60:40), and the demand-driven Labour Budget is replaced by a capped "normative allocation."
2. **Case for "budget-capped, supply-driven":** Sections 4(5)/4(6) place expenditure beyond the normative allocation on States; the open-ended legal guarantee weakens; fiscally weaker/high-demand States bear disproportionate new wage-cost liability, straining cooperative federalism.
3. **Counter-view — efficiency gains:** Higher 125-day entitlement, ₹300 floor wage, and tech-driven accountability (AI fraud detection, GPS monitoring, biannual social audits) directly target MGNREGA's documented failures (₹193.67 crore misappropriation in FY2024-25; only 7.61% of households completing 100 days).
4. **Way forward/conclusion:** Recommend a transparent, objective normative-allocation formula, dedicated safety-net funding for fiscally weak States, and outcome-based monitoring (actual days delivered, not just budgetary compliance) to preserve the Article 41 "right to work" spirit under the new framework.
