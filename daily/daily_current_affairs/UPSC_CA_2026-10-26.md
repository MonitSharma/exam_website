## Briefing

### 1. 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum opens in New Delhi (26–27 Oct) — International Relations
**Exam angle:** India hosts the legislators' track of BRICS at Parliament House, chaired by Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla. Theme: "Fostering Parliaments for people-centric development"; AI and digital technology feature across four plenary sessions. It is a parliamentary-diplomacy platform that supports summit decisions, not a decision-making body.
**Memorize:** 1st forum Moscow, June 2015; 11th Brasília, June 2025 (zero tolerance on terrorism, Pahalgam condemned). Members: Brazil, Russia, India, China, South Africa, Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE, Indonesia.
**Notebook:** GS2 – International Relations.

### 2. Indian shipbuilding: order book growing, global share still tiny — Economy
**Exam angle:** BIMCO data show a record global order book of 203.2 million CGT, up 13% in a year and above the 2008 peak (195.4 m CGT). India holds ~1.1 m CGT (0.5%), ranking 12th, but deliveries due 2026–30 are nearly double those of 2021–25 (verify).
**Memorize:** China ~64%, South Korea ~19%, Japan ~6% of the order book; China won 74% of tonnage contracted last year. Targets: Maritime India Vision 2030 (top 10) and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 (top 5). ₹69,725-crore 2025 package with Maritime Development Fund. CGT = compensated gross tonnage.
**Notebook:** GS3 – Economy (Infrastructure / Manufacturing).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal "Infrastructure – Ports & Shipping"; Sagarmala. Update: add the share data and the Amrit Kaal targets; cross-link to Make in India / Atmanirbhar defence shipbuilding.

### 3. AgriStack crosses 11 crore Farmer IDs (23 Oct), ahead of the 31 Mar 2027 deadline — Agriculture
**Exam angle:** The Farmer Registry is one of AgriStack's three foundational registries, under the Digital Agriculture Mission. Farmer IDs link Aadhaar and land records. Mains angle: exclusion of tenant farmers, sharecroppers and women without land titles; land-record errors; privacy under the DPDP Act.
**Memorize:** Digital Agriculture Mission – approved Sept 2024, ~₹2,817 crore; components: AgriStack, Krishi Decision Support System, Soil Profile Maps. AgriStack registries: Farmers' Registry, Geo-referenced Village Maps, Crop Sown Registry. Agriculture Census 2015–16: ~14.6 crore operational holdings.
**Notebook:** GS3 – Agriculture (Technology / Governance).
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh – Agriculture; Mrunal "Agri-marketing & digitisation". Update: add the 11 crore milestone; cross-link to DPI (Aadhaar, Digilocker) and land-records modernisation (DILRMP).

### 4. Free bus travel and women's mobility: Time Use Survey comparison — Society
**Exam angle:** A comparison of MoSPI's Time Use Surveys (2019 vs 2024) finds women's trip-making rose 6.6 percentage points in the four free-bus States against 3.7 for men. Women travelling rose 38.7% → 45.3% there; care-related trips nearly tripled (0.9% → 2.6%); women in any paid work rose 30.5% → 34.4% (verify – single source).
**Memorize:** Free-bus States in the 2019–24 window: Punjab and Tamil Nadu (2021), Karnataka and Telangana (2023); Delhi has had it since 2019; Andhra Pradesh from Aug 2025 (after the survey). Mains hook: freebies vs gender-responsive public transport; fiscal burden on State transport corporations.
**Notebook:** GS1 – Society (Women); GS2 – Welfare schemes.
**Static anchor:** Spectrum/NCERT – Women's empowerment; Laxmikanth – Art. 15(3). Update: add this data point to the women's LFPR/mobility notes; cross-link to PLFS (covered 25 Oct) and Time Use Survey.

### 5. Personal-data leaks: vehicle-owner data sold cheaply, SBI breach — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** Security firm Cyderes reported that vehicle-owner data (licence, challan, FASTag, registration) was being sold via apps, sites and a Telegram channel for as little as ₹5. MoRTH asked CERT-In for a forensic audit of VAHAN, SARATHI, e-Challan and PUCC and sought MeitY's review of its data-sharing policy. An IFF RTI found 25,314 credentials issued to private entities running 1.708 crore queries over 33 months. SBI separately reported ~3.75 lakh records on the dark web, of which 3,897 held customers' personal information (verify).
**Memorize:** DPDP Act 2023 – breach-notification provisions (Sec 8(6), Rule 7) come into force 13 May 2027 per the source; penalties up to ₹250 crore (security failure) and ₹200 crore (non-notification). CERT-In 2022 Directions: report incidents within 6 hours. The 2019 bulk data-sharing policy was withdrawn in 2020.
**Notebook:** GS2 – Governance (e-governance, data protection); GS3 – Internal security (cyber).
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth – Art. 21 and privacy (Puttaswamy 2017); Shankar IAS – Cyber Security. Update: add the DPDP commencement timeline; cross-link to the eMudhra and CERT-In AI-agent advisory items already in notes.

### Mains question
**Critically examine whether fare-free bus travel for women is an effective instrument for improving women's mobility and workforce participation in India. (15 marks, 250 words)**
Model-answer skeleton:
1. Intro: Time Use Survey 2019 vs 2024 – women's trips up 6.6 pp in free-bus States vs 3.7 for men; link with Art. 15(3).
2. Gains: saves household income, care and paid-work trips rise, safety and public-transport ridership increase.
3. Limits: fiscal stress on State transport undertakings, bus supply and last-mile gaps, no effect on norms/safety; free passes may crowd out service quality.
4. Way forward: pair with fleet expansion, safety audits, targeted vs universal subsidy, direct transfers of subsidy to the corporations, integrated gender-responsive urban transport.

## Answers
- **Q1 – (a).** 1st forum Moscow 2015; Om Birla chairs the New Delhi forum. Statement 3 is wrong: the forum is a non-binding consultative platform.
- **Q2 – (b).** China ~64% + Korea ~19% + Japan ~6% ≈ 89%; India has ~0.5%, so statement 1 is wrong. CGT weights tonnage by work content.
- **Q3 – (a).** Digital Agriculture Mission was approved in Sept 2024 (₹2,817 crore), not 2019.
- **Q4 – (b).** The Time Use Survey is conducted by the NSO under MoSPI.
- **Q5 – (a).** VAHAN/SARATHI are registration/licence databases; CERT-In requires reporting within 6 hours, not 24; DPDP breach-notification provisions take effect 13 May 2027 per the source.
- **Q6 – (b).** Art. 15(3) is an exception to the non-discrimination clause, enabling special provisions for women and children.
- **Q7 – (d).** A nine-judge Constitution Bench delivered the unanimous 2017 verdict.
