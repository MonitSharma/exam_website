## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. ISRO hot-tests its 200-tonne Semi-Cryogenic Engine (SE-2000) at 175 t thrust — Science & Technology
**Headline:** On 24 June 2026, ISRO conducted a successful hot test of the indigenous **Semi-Cryogenic Engine (SE-2000)** power head at the ISRO Propulsion Complex (IPRC), Mahendragiri (Tamil Nadu), reaching **175 tonne (~88%) thrust** — the 8th test of the Power-Head Test Article (PHTA) programme.

**Exam angle:** A clean "process" milestone that lets examiners test the *cryogenic vs semi-cryogenic* distinction and India's launch-vehicle roadmap. The engine will power the new **SC120 stage** that replaces the current L110 (Vikas-engine) liquid core stage of the **LVM3**, raising payload to GTO; first LVM3 flight with the semi-cryo stage is targeted for **Q1 2027**.

**Memorize:** SE-2000 = **2,000 kN (2 MN) / 200-tonne class**; propellants = **Liquid Oxygen (LOX) + refined kerosene ("Isrosene")** — non-toxic, cheaper, denser than cryogenic LH₂. Contrast: **cryogenic** engines (CE-7.5/CE-20) use **Liquid Hydrogen + LOX**. ISRO Chairman: **V. Narayanan**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (Space).
**Static anchor:** Sci-Tech notes → "ISRO Launch Vehicles" (PSLV / GSLV Mk-II / LVM3 stage configuration). *Add a row for the SC120 semi-cryogenic stage and cross-link the cryogenic-stage entry with a one-line propellant comparison.*

### 2. EAM Jaishankar's Mongolia + South Korea visit; reviews India-funded Mongol Refinery — International Relations
**Headline:** External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar visited **Mongolia (22–23 June)** and the **Republic of Korea (24–25 June)**. In Mongolia he reviewed the **Mongol Oil Refinery** at Sainshand (Dornogobi province) — Mongolia's **first-ever oil refinery**, built with a **US$1.7 billion Indian Line of Credit (LoC)**.

**Exam angle:** "India's development partnership" + "third/spiritual neighbour" diplomacy. South Korea leg advanced the **India–ROK Special Strategic Partnership** (shipbuilding, defence, trade, tech).

**Memorize:** Refinery at **Sainshand**; **520-km crude pipeline** under construction; LoC route = **EXIM Bank of India**. India–Mongolia ties = "spiritual partnership" (Buddhism). India–ROK = "**Special Strategic Partnership**" (since 2015).

## PART C — ANSWERS & EXPLANATIONS

**Q1 — (b).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. SE-2000 burns **LOX + refined kerosene (Isrosene)** and will power the **SC120** stage replacing the LVM3's L110. Statement 3 is wrong — burning **liquid hydrogen** is the feature of a *cryogenic* engine, not a semi-cryogenic one.

**Q2 — (b).** The Mongol Refinery at Sainshand is being financed by a **~US$1.7 billion Line of Credit via the EXIM Bank of India** — India's flagship development-partnership project in Mongolia.

**Q3 — (b).** NHCX (under the National Health Authority) is a **digital exchange that standardises and speeds up health-insurance claims** between hospitals and insurers; it does not replace PM-JAY.

**Q4 — (b).** Corporate Mitra trains certified **para-professionals to help MSMEs with regulatory/business compliance** (partners: IIT Madras, SWAYAM Plus, ICAI, ICSI, ICMAI).

**Q5 — (c).** Both correct — June 2026 saw a **~46% deficit (among the driest Junes in ~146 years)**, with **Maharashtra (~85%) and Gujarat (~84%)** among the worst-hit major states.

**Q6 — (b).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Cryogenic = **LH₂ + LOX**; **GSLV Mk-II and LVM3 both use an indigenous cryogenic upper stage**. Statement 2 is wrong — the **PSLV has no cryogenic stage** (solid + liquid/Vikas stages only).

**Q7 — (a).** 1, 2 and 3 strengthen the SW monsoon (northward ITCZ, Tibetan heating + Tropical Easterly Jet, strong Mascarene High driving the Somali Jet). **El Niño (4) weakens/suppresses** the monsoon, so it is excluded.
