### Mains Question (GS3 — Security & Science-Tech)

**Q.** India's recent validation of a multi-layered Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) capability marks a significant leap in its strategic deterrence. *Examine* the strategic significance of an indigenous BMD shield for India, along with the challenges it poses. *(250 words)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Define BMD — a layered system to detect and intercept incoming ballistic missiles across endo- and exo-atmospheric phases; note the Phase-II AD-1/AD-2 validation and India joining the small club (US, Russia, China, Israel).
- **Strategic significance:** Strengthens credible minimum deterrence and second-strike survivability; insurance against decapitation strikes on cities/leadership; reduces vulnerability in a two-front setting; advances Atmanirbharta in defence with technology spin-offs.
- **Challenges:** Cost-exchange ratio is unfavourable (decoys, MIRVs, saturation, hypersonics defeat shields cheaply); risk of offence–defence arms race and erosion of strategic stability with neighbours; partial geographic coverage; demands robust early-warning, C2 and NCA integration; does nothing against sub-conventional/terror threats.
- **Way forward / conclusion:** Pair BMD with arms-control diplomacy and confidence-building; integrate space-based early warning (ISRO synergy); calibrate deployment to preserve deterrence stability rather than provoke a spiral.

## ANSWERS

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** France hosts the 2026 G7 at Évian; India attends as an invitee/partner, not a member. Statement 3 is wrong — the G7 has *seven* member countries; the EU participates as a "non-enumerated member," not as one of the seven.

**Q2 — (a) Phase-II Ballistic Missile Defence programme.** AD-1 (and the upcoming AD-2) are long-range interceptors under DRDO's Phase-II BMD, tested 10–11 June 2026 at Chandipur.

**Q3 — (c) Both 1 and 2.** May 2026 CPI of 3.93% is below the 6% upper tolerance band; FIT mandates 4% ± 2% (i.e., 2%–6%).

**Q4 — (c) 64% of the population.** India reported social protection coverage of 64.3% (~94 crore people), up from 19% in 2015 — 2nd highest globally by beneficiary count.

**Q5 — (b) An invasive alien plant species that degrades native habitats.** Lantana camara out-competes native grasses, depletes herbivore prey, and intensifies human–tiger conflict.

**Q6 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** KMGBF was adopted at CBD COP15 (Montreal, Dec 2022) and sets the "30×30" target. It is *not* legally binding — progress is tracked via national biodiversity plans/reports.

**Q7 — (a) 1 only.** The MPC has six members with the RBI Governor as ex-officio Chairperson. The inflation target is set by the *Government of India in consultation with the RBI* (every five years), not by the RBI alone — so statement 2 is wrong.
