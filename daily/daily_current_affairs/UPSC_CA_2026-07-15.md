### 1. Supreme Court sets up four dedicated "oldest cases" benches from 15 July — Polity & Governance (Judiciary)

**Headline:** From **15 July 2026**, the Supreme Court has constituted **four special Division Benches** to *exclusively* hear its **oldest pending civil and criminal appeals** — an administrative first that CJI **Surya Kant** called "probably a first" for the Court.

**Exam angle:** A clean, high-yield hook for the perennial GS2 theme of **judicial pendency and case-management reform**. It illustrates the **CJI's role as "master of the roster"** (allocation of cases/benches is the CJI's administrative prerogative) and gives a concrete, quotable reform to cite in any answer on delayed justice.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- Two Division Benches (headed by **Justices P.K. Mishra** and **S.V.N. Bhatti**) take the **oldest civil appeals**; two (headed by **Justices Manoj Misra** and **Ujjal Bhuyan**) take the **oldest criminal appeals** — on non-miscellaneous days (Tue–Thu).
- Each bench initially picks up **~200 of the oldest cases** for continuous hearing; listing is strictly by seniority (e.g., oldest civil appeal from 2005 → serial no. 1).
- The Supreme Court's total pendency is **~86,000+ cases**; the initiative's success will decide whether future CJIs continue the model.

### 4. RBI rationalises the bank-board governance framework; effective 1 October 2026 — Economy

**Headline:** On **14 July 2026**, the **RBI** issued **final amendment directions** streamlining what commercial-bank **boards** must take up — freeing board time for **strategy and risk governance** instead of routine operational items. Effective **1 October 2026**.

**Exam angle:** A crisp banking-governance item (distinct from monetary policy). It tests the RBI's role as the **banking regulator** and the theme of **corporate governance in banks** after years of NPA/fraud episodes.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- The board must give **oversight of risk management, strategy, related-party exposures and corporate-governance conformity**; must explicitly **list matters reserved for board approval** and periodically review delegated powers.
- The **Chairperson** gets primary responsibility for **setting the board agenda**.
- Crucially, governance provisions earlier applied to **Public Sector Banks (PSBs)** are now **extended to Private Sector Banks (PVBs)** with modifications.
- Legal basis: **Banking Regulation Act, 1949** (RBI's supervisory powers over bank management/boards).

**Destination notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Banking & financial-sector regulation).

### Mains-Style Question

**Q8 (GS2, 15 marks, ~250 words):** *"Judicial pendency in India is as much a problem of case management as of judicial vacancies."* **Examine** this statement in the light of recent Supreme Court measures to prioritise its oldest cases.

*Model-answer skeleton:*
- **Frame the scale:** cite pendency (~5+ crore cases across courts; ~86,000+ in the SC) and the twin drivers — vacancies/low judge-population ratio **and** procedural/case-management inefficiencies (adjournments, no differentiated case-flow).
- **Case-management side:** SC's July 2026 "oldest-cases" special benches, continuous hearing, seniority-based listing; NJDG, e-Courts Phase III, ADR/Lok Adalats, mediation (Mediation Act, 2023), differentiated case-flow management.
- **Vacancy/capacity side:** collegium delays, sanctioned-vs-working strength, need for All India Judicial Service debate, subordinate-judiciary infrastructure.
- **Balanced conclusion:** durable reform needs **both** — administrative innovation (rosters, tech, ADR) *and* structural fixes (filling vacancies, court infrastructure) — to make "justice delayed is justice denied" a genuinely addressable problem.

## PART C — ANSWERS

**Q1 — (b).** The benches (two civil, two criminal) exclusively take up the Court's *oldest* pending appeals, ~200 each, by seniority.

**Q2 — (c).** SHANTI = *Securing Holistic Advancement through Norms, Trust and Integrity*, the theme of India's UNSC 2028–29 candidature launched at UN HQ.

**Q3 — (b).** Statements 2 and 3 are correct. Statement 1 is wrong — PSB governance provisions are **extended to private banks** too; the Chairperson sets the agenda; effective **1 Oct 2026**.

**Q4 — (c).** It is a **Ministry of Corporate Affairs** scheme (Budget 2026–27), delivered via SWAYAM Plus with ICAI/ICSI/ICMAI and IIT Madras.

**Q5 — (b).** TSDSI is India's **national telecom Standards Development Organisation**; it is neither a manufacturer nor a regulator (TRAI remains the regulator).

**Q6 — (b).** Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong — a **retiring member is *not* eligible for immediate re-election** (Article 23, UN Charter). Terms are two years; election by UNGA needs a two-thirds majority.

**Q7 — (b).** "Master of the roster" is the **CJI's administrative prerogative** to constitute benches and allocate cases — reaffirmed by the SC itself (e.g., 2018 rulings).

**Q8 — Mains:** directive **"Examine"** → weigh both case-management and vacancy dimensions; use the July 2026 special-benches reform as the live illustration; conclude that both administrative and structural measures are needed.
