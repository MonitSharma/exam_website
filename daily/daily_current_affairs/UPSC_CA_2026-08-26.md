### 1. Adi Vaani goes full-scale — India's first AI translator for tribal languages — Society

**Headline.** At the **National Conclave on Tribal Languages & Museums** in **Mysuru, Karnataka** (three-day conclave, opened **24 August 2026**), the **Ministry of Tribal Affairs** launched the **full-fledged Adi Vaani platform** along with its **iOS application**, added **five new languages**, unveiled the Adi Vaani logo, launched the **Jago AI assistant**, and signed an **MoU with the Central Institute of Indian Languages (CIIL), Mysuru** for tribal-language documentation and corpus development.

**Exam angle.**
- This is the cleanest available case study of **AI applied to low-resource languages as a public good** — it sits simultaneously in GS1 (Indian society, tribal communities, intangible heritage), GS2 (governance, service delivery to marginalised groups) and GS3 (AI/emerging tech). A single Mains answer on "technology and inclusion" can now be anchored to a named, dated Indian programme instead of a generic claim.
- The prelims trap is treating Adi Vaani as *merely a translation app*. Its stated objectives are wider: **documentation, digitisation, language learning, and improved access to public services** for tribal speakers.
- Ties directly to the constitutional fact that **Santali is the only tribal language in the Eighth Schedule** — the rest of India's tribal languages have no such statutory shelter, which is precisely the gap Adi Vaani targets.

**Facts to memorise.**
- **Nodal ministry:** Ministry of Tribal Affairs. **Venue:** Mysuru, Karnataka. **Partner signed with:** CIIL (Ministry of Education, established **1969**, headquartered at **Mysuru**).
- **Developing consortium:** led by **IIT Delhi**, with **BITS Pilani, IIIT Hyderabad and IIIT Naya Raipur**, working with **Tribal Research Institutes (TRIs)**.
- **Underlying AI models:** **NLLB (No Language Left Behind)** and **IndicTrans2** — both built for low-resource languages.
- **Functions:** speech-to-text, text-to-speech, **speech-to-speech**, **OCR**, bilingual dictionaries, digital repositories.
- **Beta-stage languages:** **Santali, Bhili, Mundari, Gondi.** (Santali and Mundari = **Munda branch, Austroasiatic**; Bhili = **Indo-Aryan**; Gondi = **Dravidian**. Three different families — a favourite UPSC pairing trap.)
- Conclave also covered strengthening **Tribal Freedom Fighter Museums (TFFM)**.

**Notebook:** GS1 — Society (Tribal communities & cultural heritage); cross-tag GS3 — Sci-Tech (AI).

**STATIC ANCHOR:** *Laxmikanth* — **Chapter on Official Language (Articles 343–351) and the Eighth Schedule**. → Add a margin note: "**Santali = only tribal language in the Eighth Schedule**, added by the **92nd Amendment Act, 2003** along with **Bodo, Dogri, Maithili** (22 languages total). Cross-link to Article 350A (mother-tongue instruction at primary stage) and Article 350B (Special Officer for Linguistic Minorities) → *Adi Vaani is the technological arm of the same objective*." ### 2. Modi to Uzbekistan on 28 August, then the SCO's 25th-anniversary summit at Bishkek — International Relations

**Exam angle.**

### 3. SEBI puts a number on IT resilience — the ITRI framework for market infrastructure institutions — Economy

**Headline.** On **24 August 2026**, **SEBI** issued a circular introducing an **IT Resilience Index (ITRI)** — a **system-driven composite index on a 100-point scale** measuring the health and resilience of the critical IT systems of **Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs)**: **stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories**.

**Exam angle.**
- India's securities market is now regulated on the premise that **technology failure is systemic risk**, not an operational nuisance. That framing — *cyber/IT resilience as financial stability* — is the quotable Mains line for GS3 (Economy) and GS3 (Internal Security, cyber).
- The design detail worth remembering is that the computation is **system-driven, objective and non-discretionary** — SEBI is deliberately removing regulatory judgement from the scoring, in the same spirit as rule-based fiscal and monetary frameworks.
- Prelims distinction: **ITRI is not a general cybersecurity index for all financial institutions.** It applies **only to the critical IT systems of MIIs**.

**Facts to memorise.**
- **Nine parameters and weights:** **Availability 20%**, **Security 20%**, Integrity 10%, Governance 10%, Reliability & Monitoring 10%, Business Continuity 10%, Modularity & Flexibility 10%, **Scalability 5%**, Other (incl. incident handling) 5%. *(Availability and Security are jointly 40% — the two highest.)*
- **Frequency:** computed **half-yearly**, within **60 days** of the end of each half-year.
- **Reporting line:** a comparative analysis of **two consecutive half-years**, plus corrective actions taken/proposed, goes to the MII's **Standing Committee on Technology (SCOT)** and its **governing board**.
- **First computation:** for the half-year ended **31 March 2027**.
- **Operationalisation deadline: 28 February 2027** — including an **Early Warning System (EWS)** and **real-time monitoring of service delivery**.
- **MIIs = stock exchanges + clearing corporations + depositories.** (India's depositories: **NSDL and CDSL**.)

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Capital markets & regulation); cross-tag GS3 — Internal Security (cybersecurity).

**STATIC ANCHOR:** *Mrunal — Pillar 4 (Financial Intermediaries / SEBI)*. → Update the SEBI page with a small box: "**MIIs = exchanges, clearing corps, depositories.** SEBI's tech-oversight stack now reads: Cyber Security & Cyber Resilience Framework → CSCRF → Cyber Suraksha & Incident Reporting portals (Aug 2026) → **ITRI (Aug 2026, quantified, half-yearly, 100-point)**." Cross-link to the **Financial Stability and Development Council (FSDC)** entry and to **CERT-In** under the IT Act, 2000.

### 4. ICMR–NIN: lead and amyloid-β together wreck the cell's disposal system — Science & Technology

**Headline.** Researchers at the **ICMR–National Institute of Nutrition (NIN), Hyderabad**, reported in the ***Journal of Applied Toxicology*** that **environmental lead exposure significantly worsens neuronal damage in the presence of amyloid-β peptides** — the peptides associated with Alzheimer's pathology. The work, on **human neuronal cells in the laboratory**, was led by **Dr Suresh Challa**, Scientist-G and Head of Cell Biology at ICMR–NIN.

**Exam angle.**
- This is a rare item that pays in **three** places: Sci-Tech (cell biology mechanism), Environment (heavy-metal pollution), and Social Justice (lead exposure is a poverty- and childhood-linked burden). Use it as the concrete example whenever an answer needs "environmental determinants of non-communicable disease".
- The **honest caveat is itself examinable**: this is an **in-vitro study**. It does **not** establish that lead causes Alzheimer's disease in humans. UPSC likes statements that overstate a finding.
- The mechanism to hold on to: both lead **and** amyloid-β individually impair **lysosomal function**; **combined exposure is substantially worse** — reduced cell survival, disturbed **lysosomal acidity (pH)**, structural damage and **membrane leakage**. In plain terms: the cell's waste-disposal and recycling unit stops working, so damaged proteins accumulate.

**Facts to memorise.**
- **Lysosome** = membrane-bound organelle containing acid hydrolases; the cell's **waste-disposal and recycling** compartment; requires an **acidic interior (~pH 4.5–5)** to function.
- **Molecular players named:** **TFEB** (master transcriptional regulator of lysosome biogenesis/autophagy), **TRPML1** (lysosomal calcium channel), **LAMP1 and LAMP2** (lysosome-associated membrane proteins), **Cathepsin B** (lysosomal protease).
- **Lead (Pb):** atomic number **82**; principal ore **galena (PbS)**; typically occurs with **zinc, silver and copper**; **no known beneficial biological role**.
- **Uses:** lead-acid batteries, ammunition, **radiation shielding**, some pigments/solders.
- **Health effects:** damages the **nervous, renal and cardiovascular** systems; **children are the most vulnerable** (interferes with neurobehavioural development); there is **no known safe blood-lead level**.
- **Institutional fact:** ICMR–**NIN, Hyderabad**, is the ICMR institute that also publishes India's **Dietary Guidelines** and **RDA/EAR** values.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (Biotechnology/health); cross-tag GS3 — Environment (Pollution) and GS2 — Health.

**STATIC ANCHOR:** *Shankar IAS Environment — chapter on Environmental Pollution → sub-topic "Heavy Metals / Health effects of pollutants"*. → Add a **heavy-metals table** (Lead–galena–neuro/renal; Mercury–cinnabar–Minamata; Cadmium–Itai-itai; Arsenic–groundwater, Bengal basin) and cross-link to the **UPSC 2020 PYQ on coal ash containing arsenic, lead and mercury** (see PYQ Mirror). For the cell-biology half, cross-link to **NCERT Class XI Biology, Ch. 8 "Cell: The Unit of Life"** — lysosome as "suicidal bag", acid hydrolases.

### 5. INS Nipun to commission on 31 August — the second Nistar-class diving support vessel — Internal Security

**Headline.** Announced **25 August 2026**: the **Indian Navy will commission *Nipun***, the **second Nistar-class Diving Support Vessel (DSV)**, on **31 August 2026 at Naval Dockyard, Mumbai**. Indigenously designed and built by **Hindustan Shipyard Limited (HSL), Visakhapatnam**, the ship was **delivered to the Navy on 30 July 2026** after outfitting and trials.

**Exam angle.**
- The examinable idea is not the ship but the **capability**: a DSV is the **mother ship for a Deep Submergence Rescue Vehicle (DSRV)**. India operates a submarine fleet; until *Nistar* was commissioned in 2025, the Navy relied on **commercially leased vessels** for this role. Two purpose-built DSVs close a genuine strategic gap.
- Second angle: **defence indigenisation through a struggling PSU**. HSL received this project on a **nomination basis** partly to shore up its finances — a good, specific example for a Mains answer on defence-industrial base policy that avoids the usual generic "Make in India" filler.
- Prelims trap: *Nistar* (first of class) was commissioned into the **Eastern Naval Command at Visakhapatnam on 18 July 2025**; ***Nipun* commissions at Mumbai** on 31 August 2026.

**Facts to memorise.**
- **Builder:** Hindustan Shipyard Limited, **Visakhapatnam**. **Contract signed 20 September 2018**, ₹**2,392.94 crore** for two ships. **Both launched together on 22 September 2022.**
- **Indigenous content ~80%**; **steel wholly supplied by SAIL**.
- **Displacement ~9,350 tonnes**; **length 118.4 m**; **beam 22.8 m**; **endurance 60 days** without replenishment.
- **Key fit:** **Dynamic Positioning System (DP-II)**; six-person **Decompression Chamber**; **Submersible Decompression Chamber (diving bell)** deployed through a **moon pool**; **Self-Propelled Hyperbaric Lifeboat (SPHL)**; two **Observation-class Remotely Operated Vehicles (OROVs)**; a **DSRV**; **side-scan sonar**; **15-tonne subsea crane**; **helipad**.
- **Roles:** submarine rescue, deep-sea/saturation diving, search and rescue, sustained patrolling, **non-combatant evacuation operations (NEO)**, helicopter operations.
- **Lineage:** the original **INS Nistar** was acquired from the **Soviet Navy in 1969** and served until **1989**. *Nistar* (2025) carries the nickname **"Guardian of the Deep"** and took part in **Exercise Pacific Reach 2025** at Singapore.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security (Defence indigenisation & naval capability).

**STATIC ANCHOR:** *Defence/Internal Security notes — "Indian Navy: ships, commands and indigenisation"*. → Add **Nistar-class (Nistar A16 → ENC; Nipun → Mumbai)** to your ship-name table alongside **INS Dhruv** (missile-tracking, also HSL) and the **INS Vikrant / Nilgiri / Kalvari** entries, and add a column for **shipyard** (HSL Vizag / MDL Mumbai / GRSE Kolkata / CSL Kochi / GSL Goa) — UPSC pairs ship names with builders. Cross-link to the **three Naval Commands** (Western–Mumbai, Eastern–Visakhapatnam, Southern–Kochi) and the **tri-service Andaman & Nicobar Command, Port Blair**.

### C. Mains-Style Question

**GS2 — International Relations (250 words, 15 marks)**

> *"India's Central Asia policy has been rich in civilisational rhetoric and poor in physical connectivity."* **Critically examine** this statement in the light of India's engagement with the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation and its bilateral outreach to the region.

**Model-answer skeleton (4 points):**

1. **Set up the paradox.** India enjoys deep historical goodwill and has institutionalised engagement — full SCO membership since 2017, the India–Central Asia Summit (first held January 2022) and the India–Central Asia Dialogue, plus exercises such as *Dustlik* with Uzbekistan and *Khanjar* with Kyrgyzstan. Yet India's trade with the region remains a small fraction of China's. Rhetoric outruns throughput.

2. **Name the binding constraint honestly.** India has **no direct land access**: Pakistan blocks the overland route and Afghanistan remains unstable. The two workarounds — **Chabahar** and the **INSTC** — are both hostage to the geopolitics around Iran, which leaves India's one hard-built connectivity node underused. This, not a lack of political attention, is the primary limiter.

3. **Acknowledge the region's changed agency (this is what earns marks for "critically").** Central Asian states now practise their own multi-vector diplomacy — regular regional summits, settled Fergana Valley borders, growing Gulf and Turkish engagement, a returning US commercial interest in critical minerals and energy corridors, and a Russia diminished relative to China. They are not looking for another patron.

4. **Conclude with a realistic, sectoral agenda.** India cannot outspend China on infrastructure, outreach Russia geographically, or outbid Europe financially. The defensible strategy is a **modest, sustained, sectoral** one — pharmaceuticals, IT and digital public infrastructure, education and healthcare, capacity building, and **critical minerals** — with the SCO used mainly as a counter-terrorism and convening platform rather than as an economic vehicle. Close on the line that India's Central Asia policy must convert goodwill into *cargo*.

## PART C — ANSWERS & EXPLANATIONS

**Q1 — (b) 1 and 3 only.**
Statement 2 is the error. The consortium is **led by IIT Delhi** (with BITS Pilani, IIIT Hyderabad and IIIT Naya Raipur). CIIL, Mysuru, is a **partner that signed an MoU** for documentation and corpus development — not the developer. Statements 1 and 3 are correct.

**Q2 — (b) 1 and 3 only.**
Statement 2 is the error: the **Secretariat is in Beijing**, but the **Regional Anti-Terrorist Structure (RATS) is in Tashkent**. Statements 1 and 3 are correct — the SCO was founded in **2001** out of the **Shanghai Five (1996)**, and India and Pakistan became full members at **Astana in 2017**.

**Q3 — (b).**
The ITRI is a **SEBI** framework, introduced by circular on 24 August 2026, applying to **Market Infrastructure Institutions** — stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories — scored out of **100** across nine parameters. It is not a general index for all banks, all States, or data centres.

**Q4 — (b).**
The ICMR–NIN study found that lead and amyloid-β each impair **lysosomal function**, and that combined exposure disturbs **lysosomal acidity**, damages lysosomal structure, causes **membrane leakage** and reduces cell survival. Proteins implicated included TFEB, TRPML1, LAMP1, LAMP2 and Cathepsin B.

**Q5 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.**
All three are correct. *Nipun* is the **second Nistar-class DSV**, built by **HSL Visakhapatnam**; the class is designed as a **mother ship for a DSRV**; and the fit includes **DP-II dynamic positioning** and a **Self-Propelled Hyperbaric Lifeboat**, alongside decompression chambers, a diving bell launched through a moon pool, OROVs and a 15-tonne subsea crane.

**Q6 — (a) 1 and 2 only.**
Statement 3 is the error: **Eighth Schedule inclusion and Classical Language status are entirely separate**. Classical Language status is conferred by the **Government of India on the recommendation of a Linguistic Experts Committee** under the Sahitya Akademi, and several Eighth Schedule languages are not classical (while the two categories overlap only partly). Statements 1 and 2 are correct — **Santali** is the only Scheduled Tribe language in the Eighth Schedule, added along with **Bodo, Dogri and Maithili** by the **92nd Amendment Act, 2003**, taking the total to **22**.

**Q7 — (d) All four.**
All pairs are correct. **Lead** — ore **galena (PbS)**, no safe blood level, impairs neurobehavioural development in children. **Mercury** — **Minamata disease**, from methylmercury discharged into Minamata Bay, Japan. **Cadmium** — **Itai-itai disease**, from cadmium-contaminated rice and water in the Jinzu river basin, Japan. **Arsenic** — endemic groundwater contamination across the **Bengal basin** (West Bengal and Bangladesh).
