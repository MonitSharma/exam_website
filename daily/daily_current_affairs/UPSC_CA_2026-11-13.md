## Part A — Briefing

### 1. Retail inflation hits 6.09% in October 2026, breaching RBI's upper tolerance band — Economy
**Exam angle:** CPI (new series, base 2024=100) rose to 6.09% from 5.54% in September, the first breach of the 6% ceiling in about two years and the highest on the new series. Food inflation (CFPI) was 8.94% (7.74% in September), driven by onion (+116%), garlic, ginger and lemon; potato and cauliflower prices fell. This tests the flexible inflation-targeting (FIT) framework: target 4% ±2% retained for 1 Apr 2026–31 Mar 2031. A breach alone does not trigger the formal failure clause, which needs three consecutive quarters outside the band.
**Memorise:** CPI Oct 2026 6.09%; rural 6.63%, urban 5.41%; CFPI 8.94%; band 2–6%; failure = 3 consecutive quarters → RBI report to the Government.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Inflation & Monetary Policy).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal/Ramesh Singh "Inflation & Monetary Policy" pillar. *Nudge:* add the Oct-2026 data point and the 2026–31 target period to your FIT note; cross-link to MPC composition.

### 2. SC: cultivation alone does not make one a "forest dweller" under FRA (A. John Kennedy v. State of Tamil Nadu, 6 Nov) — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** A bench of Justices Vikram Nath and Sandeep Mehta held that people who cultivate forest land but only reside temporarily in the forest, or live outside its boundary, cannot qualify as Forest Dwelling STs (s.2(c)) or Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (s.2(o)) on cultivation alone; planned commercial-crop cultivation is prima facie not a recognised right. The Court set up a Special Committee headed by former CJI B.R. Gavai to scrutinise FRA claim orders, and directed the State to constitute the remaining Gram Sabhas in 30 days. Good for Mains: balancing tribal rights, forest conservation and administrative delay.
**Memorise:** 2026 INSC 1204; FRA 2006; ss.2(c), 2(o); Special Committee under ex-CJI Gavai; 30 days for Gram Sabhas, then 5 months for adjudication.
**Notebook:** GS2 Polity/Governance (also GS1 Society, GS3 Environment).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS Environment "Forest Rights Act"; Laxmikanth "Scheduled and Tribal Areas". *Nudge:* add this judgment under FRA case law and cross-link to the Gram Sabha's role.

### 3. India rejects US court order on serving NSA Doval and ex-RAW chief in Pannun civil suit — International Relations
**Exam angle:** On 9 Nov, Judge Katherine Polk Failla (SDNY) allowed the plaintiff to pursue alternative service on NSA Ajit Doval and former RAW chief Samant Goel through Delhi courts under Article 19 of the Hague Service Convention; the service packet is due by 30 Nov. MEA (10 Nov) rejected "fabricated and malicious attacks" and said service requests must go through the Ministry of Law and Justice. The order does not mean anyone has been served or establishes liability. Raises sovereign immunity, extraterritorial jurisdiction and India–US trust issues.
**Memorise:** SDNY, 9 Nov 2026; Hague Service Convention Art. 19; deadline 30 Nov; Nikhil Gupta pleaded guilty in Feb 2026 (does not establish the civil allegations).
**Notebook:** GS2 International Relations.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal/Vision "India–US relations" pillar. *Nudge:* add a "legal-diplomatic friction" sub-heading with the sovereign-immunity argument.

### 4. TDB commits ₹64.05 crore from RDI Fund for Astrome's AI-enabled ELINT satellite constellation (12 Nov) — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** The Technology Development Board (DST) will fund Astrome Technologies (Bengaluru) through Optionally Convertible Debt, against a total project cost of ₹128.11 crore, to build a "sovereign space-based intelligence system": ~50-kg microsatellites in LEO with an ELINT payload, AI-based ground processing and inter-satellite links. Links the RDI Fund (private-sector deep-tech financing), space-sector reforms and defence ISR self-reliance. Know TRL-4 → TRL-9.
**Memorise:** ₹64.05 cr of ₹128.11 cr; OCD; TRL-4→9; ~50 kg microsatellites; ELINT = non-communication signals (e.g. radar).
**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Space) and Internal Security.
**Static anchor:** Spectrum/Vision "Space Technology"; Mrunal "Space sector reforms". *Nudge:* add ELINT vs COMINT vs SIGINT definitions and the RDI Fund line.

### 5. India prohibits export of lithium-ion battery "black mass" (DGFT, 10 Nov) — Environment
**Exam angle:** DGFT moved black mass (ITC-HS 85491400) from "Free" to "Prohibited" with immediate effect, to keep lithium, cobalt, nickel and manganese feedstock at home. Recycling capacity above 60,000 tonnes a year is under-utilised for want of feedstock. Complements the National Critical Mineral Mission and the ₹1,500-crore recycling incentive scheme (Sept 2025); follows the 6 Nov news on solar/wind recycling guidelines — a circular-economy and resource-security cluster.
**Memorise:** Notification 10 Nov 2026; HS 85491400; Li, Co, Ni, Mn; DGFT is under the Commerce Ministry.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment / Economy (Critical Minerals).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS "Waste Management"; Mrunal "Critical Minerals". *Nudge:* add a "Battery recycling" box and cross-link to the Battery Waste Management framework.

### B3. Mains question
**Critically examine the Supreme Court's interpretation of "forest dweller" under the Forest Rights Act, 2006, and discuss its implications for tribal rights and forest governance. (150 words, 10 marks)**

*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. **Context:** A. John Kennedy (6 Nov 2026): cultivation without residence in or traditional dependence on the forest is not enough under ss.2(c)/2(o).
2. **Merits:** Prevents dilution of the Act and claims by encroachers or commercial cultivators; reinforces the residence and dependence test and the 75-year, pre-13 Dec 2005 requirement for OTFDs.
3. **Concerns:** Seasonal and migratory users, shifting cultivators and pastoralists may be excluded; delays and Gram Sabha non-functionality (the Court blamed the State) risk wrongful rejections.
4. **Way forward:** Functional Gram Sabhas and Forest Rights Committees, a timely Special Committee review, digitised claim tracking, and convergence between the Tribal Affairs and Environment Ministries.

## Part C — Answers

1. **(b)** — S1 correct (MoSPI CPI series base 2024=100; headline 6.09% vs 5.54% in September; food inflation 8.94%). S2 correct (three consecutive quarters rule under the RBI Act, 1934). S3 wrong: the target (4% with +/-2% band, 1 Apr 2026-31 Mar 2031) is notified by the Central Government in consultation with the RBI.
2. **(a)** — S1 and S2 reflect the ruling (Special Committee under former CJI B.R. Gavai). S3 is wrong: the Court directed the State to constitute remaining Gram Sabhas within 30 days and complete adjudication within five months after that; under FRA the Gram Sabha is the first-level authority for claims.
3. **(c)** — S1 and S2 are correct (order of 9 Nov; proposed service packet due 30 Nov; MEA briefing 10 Nov rejected the 'fabricated and malicious attacks'). S3 is wrong: the order does not mean anyone has been served and establishes no allegation or liability.
4. **(d)** — S1 correct (ELINT = signals intelligence from non-communication emitters like radars). S2 correct (total project cost Rs 128.11 crore; ~50-kg microsatellites in LEO with AI-based ground processing). S3 wrong: TRL-9 means the actual system is proven in operational environment; TRL-4 is lab validation.
5. **(d)** — S1 and S2 correct (DGFT notification of 10 Nov 2026, effective immediately, aimed at feeding domestic recyclers; recycling capacity >60,000 tonnes/yr is under-utilised). S3 wrong: DGFT is an attached office of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.
6. **(c)** — The Gram Sabha receives claims, constitutes the Forest Rights Committee for verification and passes the resolution, which then goes to the Sub-Divisional Level Committee and District Level Committee for approval. The Ministry of Tribal Affairs is the nodal ministry.
7. **(b)** — Under the RBI Act, 1934 (amended 2016), the MPC has six members: the Governor (chair), the Deputy Governor in charge of monetary policy, one RBI officer, and three external members appointed by the Centre. Decisions are by majority; the Governor has a casting vote. The inflation target is notified by the Government in consultation with the RBI.
