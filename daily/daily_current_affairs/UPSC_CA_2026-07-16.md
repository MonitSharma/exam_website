# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 16 July 2026

## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. India–UK CETA and the Double Contribution Convention enter into force (15 July 2026) — International Relations
**NEW since the June coverage of CETA diplomacy: the pact is now legally operational — entry into force on 15 July 2026, with the DCC's social-security exemption extended from 3 to 5 years.**
- **Exam angle:** Signed 24 July 2025, in force a year later after ratification. Billed by the Commerce Ministry as the "gold standard" of India's FTAs — India's most comprehensive trade pact with a major developed economy. Prelims loves the tariff architecture; Mains loves "FTA 2.0 strategy" and services/mobility gains.
- **Key facts:** UK eliminates duties on **~99% of Indian tariff lines** from day one (textiles 12%, leather/footwear 16%, marine 21.5%, engineering 18%, processed food up to 70% → zero). India removes/reduces duties on **90% of tariff lines covering ~92% of UK imports** (64.1% of lines immediately); sensitive lines excluded. **Double Contribution Convention (DCC):** Indian professionals on temporary UK assignments exempt from UK social-security contributions for up to **5 years** (improved from 3 at signing).
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR + GS3 Economy (external sector).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Mrunal Pillar 3 (Balance of Payments & FTAs) — update the India-FTA table with entry-into-force date; cross-link to ECTA (Australia) and TEPA (EFTA) for the "FTAs with developed economies" comparison.

### 2. CCEA approves NIPU-2026 — nine new gas-based urea plants, 10 million tonnes capacity — Economy
- **Exam angle:** The National Investment Policy for Urea-2026 replaces the **New Investment Policy (NIP)-2012** (expired 2019) and targets atmanirbharta in India's most-consumed fertilizer by cutting import dependence.
- **Key facts:** ~**9 gas-based plants**, each ~**12.7 lakh MT/yr**, totalling ~**1 crore MT (10 MT)** of new capacity; revised financial framework estimated to save **>₹250 crore per plant** vs NIP-2012. Feedstock: natural gas (ammonia route).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Agriculture inputs & subsidies).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Mrunal Pillar 4 (Agriculture — fertilizer subsidy) — add NIPU-2026 beside the urea MRP-control vs NBS (P&K) distinction; note urea is outside NBS.

### 3. Draft National Health Research Policy, 2026 released for public feedback — Polity & Governance (Health)
- **Exam angle:** India's first unified framework for ALL health research — biomedical, clinical, public health, digital health, AI — replacing the **National Health Research Policy, 2011**; a roadmap to 2047 with ~6x research spending. Feedback open till 27 July.
- **Key facts:** Three-tier governance — **National Health Research Stewardship Committee** (strategic oversight) → **Department of Health Research (nodal)** → **ICMR** (scientific/technical guidance). Proposes a **National Research Integrity Office (NRIO)**, impact-based (not paper-count) evaluation of scientists, simplified multicentre ethics approvals. Priorities: TB, AMR, cancer, NCDs, mental health, anaemia, maternal/neonatal mortality.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (Health).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** GS2 Health notes (National Health Policy 2017 / Ayushman Bharat section) — add NHRP-2026 as the research-side counterpart; cross-link ICMR-DHR institutional structure.

### 4. Maharashtra becomes the first state to give women farmers independent legal recognition — Society
- **Exam angle:** The **Maharashtra Women Farmers Empowerment Act, 2026** (passed unanimously by both Houses) decouples "farmer" status from land ownership — directly answering the "feminisation of agriculture without land titles" critique. Superb GS1 Society/GS3 Agriculture case study.
- **Key facts:** Issues a **'Women Farmer Certificate'** (single-window access to subsidies, credit, insurance, markets); expands "farmer" to include **tenant farmers, sharecroppers, agricultural labourers and seasonal migrant women workers**; creates a **State Women Farmers Fund** and a three-tier framework (governing council → state monitoring committee → empowerment cell) with district/taluka support officers.
- **Notebook:** GS1 Society (women) + GS3 Agriculture.
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** NCERT Indian Society (women & work) + Mrunal Pillar 4 — pin to "feminisation of agriculture" (≈13.9% female operational holders, Agriculture Census) and cross-link the 2014 GS1 PYQ below.

### 5. Indian Grey Hornbill re-established in Gir, 60+ years after local disappearance — Environment
- **Exam angle:** A rare successful **bird reintroduction** by the Gujarat Forest Department — species-recovery template alongside Cheetah (Kuno) and gharial programmes; classic "species in news" Prelims fodder.
- **Key facts:** **Ocyceros birostris**, arboreal, largely endemic to the Indian subcontinent; family Bucerotidae; IUCN **Least Concern**; unique nesting — female seals herself inside a tree cavity. Gir = only wild population of the **Asiatic lion**; Kathiawar-Gir dry deciduous forests.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment (conservation).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Shankar IAS (Protected Areas — Gujarat; hornbills of India list) — add this reintroduction under Gir NP; cross-link Great/Rufous-necked Hornbill (Schedule I, NE India) for contrast.

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS1 — Society / GS3 — Agriculture, 250 words, 15 marks)

**"Legal recognition of women as farmers, delinked from land ownership, is the missing first step in addressing the feminisation of agriculture in India." Discuss in the light of the Maharashtra Women Farmers Empowerment Act, 2026.**

*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. **Context:** Feminisation of agriculture (male out-migration; women ~63% of the agricultural workforce by some estimates but only ~13.9% of operational holdings) — yet "farmer" status, credit, insurance and compensation flow through land titles.
2. **What the Act does:** Women Farmer Certificate as single-window entitlement; definition widened to tenants, sharecroppers, labourers, migrant workers; State Women Farmers Fund; three-tier institutional machinery.
3. **Why it matters / strengths:** de-links entitlement from patriarchal land inheritance; administrative recognition enables scheme convergence (PM-KISAN-type transfers, KCC credit, crop insurance).
4. **Limitations / way forward:** certificates ≠ land rights (inheritance under personal laws still the core barrier); funding and district-level capacity; need land-title reform (e.g., joint pattas), replication via a model central law.
