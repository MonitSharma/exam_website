# PART A — BRIEFING

### 1. Japan asks the UN to delete "enemy state" clauses from the Charter — and China and Russia say no — International Relations
**Exam angle:** In her UNGA-81 address, Japan's PM Sanae Takaichi called the Charter's wartime "enemy state" language obsolete. It links three UPSC staples: UN Charter architecture, P5 veto over amendments, and UNSC reform (India and Japan are G4 partners). Fresh hook: Japan–China friction over Taiwan; both Beijing and Moscow object.
**Memorise:** Articles **53, 77, 107** = "enemy state" clauses (Axis states of WWII; allowed action against them without Security Council authorisation). UNGA declared them obsolete in **1995 (Res 50/52)**; the **2005 World Summit Outcome** recorded the intent to delete them, but no formal amendment has happened. Amendment route = **Art. 108** (two-thirds of GA + ratification by two-thirds of members including **all five P5**). *Mains line:* "A Charter frozen in 1945 cannot serve a 2026 order — yet only the P5 can unfreeze it."
**Notebook:** GS2 – IR (UN & reforms).
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth-style IR shelf → *UN system / Charter (Chs. VII, XVIII)*; Shankar/Mrunal IR pillar "UNSC reform". *Nudge:* add a cross-link from "UNSC reform / G4" to Art. 108 and to this enemy-state clause note.

### 2. TDB puts ₹200 crore behind Agnikul's fully reusable Agnibaan — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** Reusability is the next space-economy frontier (post-IN-SPACe, private launchers). Note the *instrument*: funding via the **Optionally Convertible Debentures**, drawn from the **RDI (Research Development & Innovation) fund**, to lift the technology from **TRL-4 to TRL-8**. A clean way to remember what TRL means.
**Memorise:** Signed by Technology Development Board (statutory body under DST) with Agnikul Cosmos (Chennai). Targets: full-system reuse (not just first stage), lightweight upper stage, **semi-cryogenic** liquid propulsion with deep throttling and restart, controlled descent and recovery. Agnikul earlier built the single-piece 3D-printed engine ("Agnilet"). Benefits stressed: lower cost per launch, high cadence, less space debris. Reported alongside: GalaxEye ≈ ₹63.84 crore.
**Notebook:** GS3 – Sci-Tech (Space).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum/Mrunal *Space technology* → launch vehicles (expendable vs reusable), cryogenic vs semi-cryogenic; IN-SPACe/NSIL cross-link. *Nudge:* add a "TRL 1–9" mini-table and the RDI scheme to your S&T innovation-financing page.

### 3. Four "mega ports" notified for the first time — Economy (Infrastructure/Logistics)
**Exam angle:** A new tier in India's port hierarchy created under the **Indian Ports Act, 2025**. Expect questions on Sagarmala/maritime vision, competitive private ports, and Union vs State ports (Entry 27, Union List).

### 4. Karnataka rejects the Western Ghats ESA draft again — and asks for a year to do ground surveys — Environment
**Exam angle:** *Not a repeat of the 20 Sept ESZ story — that was about Eco-Sensitive Zones around protected areas; this is the Western Ghats ESA under the Gadgil–Kasturirangan track.* New sub-development: Karnataka's Assembly unanimously rejected the latest draft (**seventh draft ESA notification, 27 July 2026**, as reported) and wants a plot-level physical survey. Classic conservation-vs-livelihood, federalism (land is a State subject) and remote-sensing-vs-ground-truth case.
**Memorise:** Reported Karnataka portion: **~20,668 sq km, 1,449 villages, 10 districts**; the overall draft is reported as **~56,825 sq km across six states** (figures vary slightly across sources — verify before quoting). Background: **Gadgil (WGEEP, 2011)** = whole Ghats ESA in three zones; **Kasturirangan HLWG (2013)** = ~**37%** of the Ghats as ESA. ESA drafts are issued under the **Environment (Protection) Act, 1986**.
**Notebook:** GS3 – Environment (Conservation, Western Ghats); GS1 – Geography.
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS *Environment → Conservation efforts in India*; NCERT/Spectrum *Western Ghats*. *Nudge:* add a Gadgil vs Kasturirangan comparison row and cross-link to "UNESCO WHS: Western Ghats (2012)".

**Mains question (GS3, 150 words, 10 marks)**
**Critically analyse** why the demarcation of Ecologically Sensitive Areas in the Western Ghats has remained unresolved for over a decade, and suggest a way forward that balances conservation with livelihoods.
*Model-answer skeleton:*
- **Context:** Gadgil (2011: whole Ghats, 3 zones) vs Kasturirangan (2013: ~37%, cultural vs natural landscape); repeated draft notifications since 2014.
- **Why stalled:** satellite-based boundaries; whole-village inclusion; forest-rights and land-title claims; State opposition (land is a State subject); fear of curbs on mining, quarrying, roads and plantation expansion.
- **Why it still matters:** landslides, floods, biodiversity hotspot status, water security for peninsular rivers.
- **Way forward:** ground-truthed plot-level surveys with local bodies (gram sabha consultation, FRA compliance), graded regulation over blanket bans, payment for ecosystem services/compensation, time-bound consultation with a fixed notification deadline.

# PART C — ANSWERS

1. **(b)** 1 and 2 correct. Statement 3 is wrong: the 2005 outcome only expressed the *intent* to delete the clauses; no Charter amendment has happened. (Art. 108 route.)
2. **(c)** All three correct per the PIB release: statutory body under DST; OCD via RDI fund; TRL-4 → TRL-8.
3. **(c)** Notified by the Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways under Section 73 of the Indian Ports Act, 2025. The list is Deendayal, JNPA, Paradip and Mundra; Mundra is privately operated, so (d) is wrong.
4. **(a)** 1 and 2 correct. Draft ESA notifications come under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, not the WPA 1972.
5. **(c)** 1 and 2 correct. Only the CEC has the Supreme-Court-judge removal protection; other ECs are removed on the CEC's recommendation (Art. 324(5)).
6. **(b)** 1 and 2 correct; non-major ports are administered mainly by States through their maritime boards, so 3 is wrong.
7. **(b)** 1 and 2 correct: inscribed 2012, 39 sites in four states. The Dang forests are in Gujarat and are not part of the property.
