# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 7 November 2026

*Items are drawn from news of 3–6 Nov 2026 (plus one pre-COP31 report). None repeats a story from the last 14 days; Pralay, UPI MDR and the Emissions Gap appear here only for genuinely new developments. Subjects: Polity, Internal Security, Economy, Environment, IR.*

## Part A — Briefing

### 1. MHA amends the Immigration and Foreigners (Exemption) Order: visa now mandatory for six undocumented minority communities (notified 4 Nov, reported 5–6 Nov) — Polity & Governance
- **Exam angle:** Members of six communities from Afghanistan, Bangladesh and Pakistan who entered India by 31 Dec 2024 without valid (or with expired) documents, citing religious persecution, must now get an "appropriate visa" to keep staying. They stay exempt from needing a passport or visa to *enter*. The same Order also tightens border documentation with Nepal and Bhutan. Good for Mains on citizenship, refugee policy and Article 14/21 debates.
- **Memorize:** Amends the Immigration and Foreigners (Exemption) Order, 2025, under Section 33 of the Immigration and Foreigners Act, 2025. Communities: Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis, Christians. Indians, Nepalese and Bhutanese moving between India, Nepal and Bhutan must show an ID listed in the "Visa Manual"; passports remain needed for travel to third countries. Registered Tibetans (entered 1959–30 May 2003) stay exempt but must get a registration certificate. Registered Sri Lankan Tamils who came by 9 Jan 2015 stay exempt.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Polity (Citizenship) / GS3 Internal Security.
- **Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, *Citizenship* chapter (Articles 5–11, Citizenship Act 1955, CAA 2019 with its 31 Dec 2014 cut-off). Add a box: "2025 Act + 2026 amendment: passport exemption → visa regime". Cross-link to Article 14 and the Free Movement Regime on the India–Myanmar border.

### 2. DRDO flight-tests Pralay at ~500 km range (3 Nov) — Internal Security
- **Exam angle:** Pralay is a surface-to-surface quasi-ballistic missile, a tactical-strike system for the battlefield range band. The new fact is the test: two simulated targets engaged at about 500 km. It shows induction readiness, with a salvo test already done in Dec 2025.
- **Memorize:** Test on 3 Nov 2026 from Launch Complex-3, ITR Chandipur, Odisha. Developed by Research Centre Imarat (Hyderabad) with BDL and BEL as industry partners. Solid propellant, mobile launcher, multiple warhead options; reported range 150–500 km. Earlier tests: Dec 2021, 28–29 Jul 2025, Dec 2025 (two-missile salvo). Reported that 2,500+ villagers were temporarily moved. Performance figures are "as reported", not officially confirmed.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (defence tech).
- **Static anchor:** Shankar IAS / Spectrum, *Indian missile programme*. Add Pralay to the table (type, range, agency) next to Prithvi, Prahaar and Agni; cross-link to the MTCR range limits (India is a member since 2016).

### 3. UPI MDR: NPCI defends the revised framework before the Supreme Court — Economy
- **Exam angle:** *Only the new development here.* In its counter-affidavit NPCI says MDR is needed to sustain UPI's cost (infrastructure, security, fraud prevention), that merchants cannot pass it to customers, that it is a commercial fee and not a tax, and that NPCI is not "State" under Article 12. The Court had earlier (28 Sep) declined a stay and issued notice to the Centre, RBI and NPCI. The framework's start (originally 15 Oct) has been postponed.
- **Memorize:** Finance Ministry notification of 14 Sep 2026: zero charge on RuPay debit cards and UPI up to ₹2,000. P2P is free at any amount. For eligible P2M payments above ₹2,000 the MDR is capped at 0.4% (max ₹300); flat ₹5 for essential/thin-margin sectors. Merchants with monthly inward credits up to ₹1 lakh are exempt. P2P is 37% of UPI volume and 70% of value; about 4% of P2M transactions attract MDR. NPCI relies on Sections 20–21 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007; a small-merchant fund would take 5% of each entity's MDR share.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (payments, fintech).
- **Static anchor:** Mrunal, *Banking → Payment systems* (PSS Act 2007, RBI as the designated authority, NPCI as a not-for-profit Section 8 company). Update the "Zero-MDR 2020 → 2026 reversal" note in your UPI notes.

### 4. UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2026, with COP31 (Antalya, 9–20 Nov) two days away — Environment
- **Exam angle:** The report (released around 22 Oct per coverage) says the chance of holding 1.5°C with no or limited overshoot has been lost; the aim is now to limit overshoot and return below 1.5°C by 2100. It matters for COP31 prep and Mains on NDC adequacy, with India shown as a relative positive.
- **Memorize:** Global GHG emissions 58.4 GtCO2e in 2025 (−0.1 Gt on 2024). Over the decade 63 countries cut emissions and 124 raised them. Cuts needed vs 2025: 18% by 2030, 45% by 2035, 69% by 2040. Full NDC delivery gives only 5–8% by 2030 and 11–15% by 2035. India: 3rd largest emitter in absolute terms, per capita below half the global average, emissions fell in 2025, and it is one of six G20 members likely to meet unconditional 2030 targets (others: China, Indonesia, Russia, South Africa, Türkiye).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment (climate change, reports).
- **Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, *Climate Change → UNFCCC, Paris Agreement, NDCs*. Add the "Emissions Gap Report" to your Reports table (publisher: UNEP; annual, ahead of COP) and link to India's NDC and the 2070 net-zero pledge.

### 5. Indians in the Russian military: MEA says release and return is a priority (3 Nov briefing) — International Relations
- **Exam angle:** *New angle, not a repeat of the G20 Atlanta item.* Jaishankar met Lavrov on the G20 foreign ministers' sidelines in Atlanta (Ukraine, Gulf, multilateral issues), and MEA says the case of Indians in Russia's army is being taken up "at various levels". Tests India's balancing of strategic partnership with consular duty.
- **Memorize:** Reports: about 259 Indians recruited; 139 discharged and brought back; talks on a further 69. MEA has no data on those who took Russian citizenship (its records cover Indian citizens only). The Atlanta G20 meeting had no joint communiqué; the US issued a chairman's statement.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (India–Russia; Indian diaspora and consular affairs).
- **Static anchor:** Mrunal/Shankar, *India–Russia relations* (Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership, 2010 upgrade). Add a "Frictions" row: nationals in the war, oil, defence supply chains.

### B3. Mains question
**Critically examine how the findings of the UNEP Emissions Gap Report 2026 sharpen the debate on climate equity, and assess whether India's trajectory as reported strengthens its negotiating position at COP31. (250 words, 15 marks)**

Model-answer skeleton:
1. Intro: the 1.5°C no-overshoot pathway is declared lost; required cuts (18% by 2030, 45% by 2035, 69% by 2040 vs 2025) versus NDC delivery of only 5–8% by 2030.
2. Equity: historic responsibility and CBDR-RC; 124 countries raised emissions over the decade, but the report puts India's per capita emissions below half the global average; finance and technology gaps for developing countries.
3. India's position: emissions fell in 2025 and India is among six G20 members on track for unconditional 2030 targets; this gives credibility, but absolute emissions are still the third highest and power, transport and industry keep growing.
4. Way forward: ramp up renewables, storage and critical-minerals processing; push for climate finance and loss-and-damage delivery at COP31; weigh the "35 by 2035" electrification-style targets against energy security.

## Part C — Answers
1. **(c)** The Order is made under Section 33 of the 2025 Act and now requires the six communities (Hindus, Sikhs, Buddhists, Jains, Parsis, Christians) to obtain an appropriate visa for continued stay. Statement 3 is wrong: Indians travelling to Nepal/Bhutan must show one of the identity documents listed in the Visa Manual, not a passport.
2. **(b)** Pralay was tested on 3 Nov 2026 from Launch Complex-3, ITR Chandipur; it is a solid-fuelled quasi-ballistic missile (RCI Hyderabad lead; BDL and BEL partners) with a reported 150-500 km range. It uses mobile launchers, so statement 3 is wrong.
3. **(c)** NPCI's counter-affidavit says merchants cannot recover MDR from customers and that it is a not-for-profit company, not 'State' under Article 12. P2P transfers remain free irrespective of amount; MDR applies only to certain person-to-merchant payments above ₹2,000 (capped at 0.4%, max ₹300). The Court earlier declined to stay the framework.
4. **(b)** The report (as summarised in coverage) says the 1.5°C no/limited-overshoot pathway is lost and that India is one of six G20 members likely to meet unconditional 2030 targets (with China, Indonesia, Russia, South Africa, Türkiye). India's per capita emissions are less than half the global average, so statement 4 is wrong.
5. **(a)** The meeting took place on the Atlanta sidelines and the Indians-in-Russian-army issue was raised (259 reportedly recruited, 139 returned, talks on 69 more). The G20 meeting ended without a joint communiqué; the US issued a chairman's statement instead.
6. **(a)** The CAA, 2019 fixes 31 December 2014 as the cut-off and reduces the residence requirement for these groups from 11 years to 5 years. The later 31 December 2024 date is only a cut-off in the 2025 exemption order for staying without a passport.
7. **(b)** Article 4 of the Paris Agreement requires all Parties to prepare, communicate and maintain successive NDCs, communicated every five years. NDCs are nationally determined, not enforceable quotas, and are not approved by the IPCC.
