# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 2026-10-31

*Prelims 2027 · Physics optional · RBI Gr B / SSC CGL backups. Five items, five subjects; nothing repeated from 17–30 Oct except one flagged follow-up (NVS-03).*

## Briefing

### 1. NVS-03 launched on GSLV-F18: NavIC restored to a four-satellite minimum — Science & Technology
**Follow-up (the 25 Oct brief previewed the launch):** the launch is now done. GSLV-F18 lifted off at 23:51 IST on 30 Oct 2026 from the Second Launch Pad, Sriharikota, and put NVS-03 into a Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit about 18 minutes later.
**Exam angle:** NVS-03 is the third second-generation NavIC satellite. It adds a new L1 signal to the existing L5 and S-band signals, which makes NavIC usable in mass-market devices that rely on L1. It also carries an indigenous atomic clock, since clock failures degraded the earlier satellites.
**Memorize:**
- Mass is about 2,232 kg (some reports say 2,250 kg).
- This is reportedly the 20th GSLV flight.
- Reports say it brings NavIC back to a minimum operational set of four satellites.
- The original constellation is 7 satellites: 3 geostationary and 4 inclined geosynchronous.

*Verification note:* the restoration claim and the exact mass come from secondary reports; check ISRO's mission page.
**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Space).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS / Mrunal Sci-Tech, Space chapter (navigation systems: GPS, GLONASS, Galileo, BeiDou, NavIC). Add a table comparing orbits (GEO / GSO / MEO) and frequency bands (L1, L5, S).

### 2. Fiscal deficit at 42.1% of the FY27 target in April–September — Economy
**Exam angle:** the first-half deficit is already wider than last year's. The deficit is Rs 7.14 lakh crore, against about Rs 5.7 lakh crore in April–September 2025-26. This matters for the government's glide path to a lower deficit and for government borrowing and bond yields (RBI Grade B angle).
**Memorize:**
- FY27 target is Rs 16.96 lakh crore, or 4.3% of GDP.
- The 42.1% figure is the share of the Budget Estimate used by end-September.
- Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − total receipts excluding borrowings.
- Primary deficit = fiscal deficit − interest payments.

*Verification note:* the figures come from UNI/Reuters-based reports. The sources did not name the Controller General of Accounts, which compiles the monthly accounts.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy.
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh, Indian Economy, Fiscal Policy / Budgeting chapter; Mrunal Economy, Budget and Deficits pillar. Cross-link to the FRBM Act and escape clauses.

### 3. India–Brazil: MEA calls ties "truly strategic", with a USD 30 bn trade target — International Relations
**Exam angle:** at Brazil's National Day event in New Delhi (30 Oct), Secretary (East) Rudrendra Tandon said cooperation is deepening in critical minerals and rare earths, pharma, digital partnership, defence, space and AI. The two countries will soon mark 80 years of diplomatic relations.
**Memorize:**
- Target is USD 30 bn of bilateral trade by 2030; trade is expected to touch about USD 20 bn soon.
- India is Brazil's fifth-largest trading partner.
- Talks to expand the India–MERCOSUR trade agreement have reportedly made concrete progress.
- Overlapping groupings are BRICS, IBSA and G20.

**Notebook:** GS2 IR.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal IR / Vajiram IR notes, Latin America and groupings chapter. Add MERCOSUR (members, India–MERCOSUR PTA in force since 2009) and IBSA (Brasilia Declaration, 2003) to the groupings table.

### 4. Supreme Court sends the Reliance–Government KG basin gas dispute to mediation — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** the case is a long arbitration-versus-court contest. In 2018 an international tribunal, by a 2–1 majority, rejected the Government's USD 1.55 bn claim that Reliance, BP and Niko drew gas from deposits they had no right to exploit, and awarded the companies USD 8.3 m. The Delhi High Court (14 Feb 2025) set aside a single-judge ruling that had upheld the award. The Supreme Court, hearing the appeals, is reported by LiveLaw's 30 Oct blog to have referred the dispute to mediation.
**Memorize:**
- Section 34 of the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996 provides for setting aside an award.
- Mediation Act, 2023 institutionalises mediation.
- Courts encourage settlement but retain jurisdiction.

*Verification note:* the mediation reference rests on a live blog with possibly garbled text; other reports say the bench had earlier declined a stay of the hearing. Treat the final order as to be confirmed.
**Notebook:** GS2 Polity / Governance (judiciary, ADR).
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, Supreme Court chapter, plus the Arbitration Act and Mediation Act 2023 note. Cross-link to Article 136 (special leave).

### 5. UAE oil spill from a damaged tanker widens; beaches shut in three emirates — Environment
**Exam angle:** Copernicus Sentinel imagery (24 Oct) showed the slick widening toward the coast of Umm Al Quwain. Ras Al Khaimah closed all its beaches on 25 Oct, Umm Al Quwain closed its beaches and coastal areas, and Sharjah's Al Hamriyah beach was closed. Experts attribute the spill to the tanker Gem No. 2, which authorities have not officially named. Shipping sources link the damage to an earlier projectile strike, in a wider regional conflict. This is a case study in marine pollution, remote sensing for monitoring, and the vulnerability of shipping lanes.
**Memorize:**
- MARPOL Annex I covers oil pollution.
- The International Maritime Organization (IMO) administers MARPOL and the OPRC Convention.
- Copernicus is the EU's Earth observation programme; Sentinel-2 is optical.
- India has a National Oil Spill Disaster Contingency Plan led by the Coast Guard.

*Verification note:* the spill volume is not stated in the Reuters report; the cause is not officially confirmed.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment.
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS Environment, Environmental Pollution and International Conventions chapters (MARPOL, OPRC). Add the Persian Gulf spill to the oil-spill case-study list next to the Mumbai (2010) and Ennore (2017) incidents.

### Part B3 — Mains question
**Examine how the restoration of NavIC's operational strength through NVS-03 advances India's strategic autonomy, and the challenges that remain. (15 marks, 250 words)**

Model-answer skeleton:
1. **Intro:** NavIC (Navigation with Indian Constellation) is India's regional navigation system. NVS-03 restores its minimum operational set and adds the L1 signal, which improves interoperability and device support.
2. **Strategic autonomy:** removes dependence on GPS for defence, disaster management, fisheries and aviation; reduces vulnerability to denial of service during a conflict.
3. **Challenges:** earlier satellites suffered atomic-clock failures; chipset and smartphone adoption is limited; the system is regional, not global; there is no redundancy beyond the minimum of four satellites.
4. **Way forward:** launch the remaining NVS satellites on schedule; mandate NavIC support in government and critical-infrastructure devices; build an indigenous clock and chip ecosystem; integrate with GAGAN.

## Answers
1. **(c)** — 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: the GSLV injects the satellite into a Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit (about 170 x 37,000 km); the satellite's own motor then raises it to its operational geosynchronous orbit.
2. **(b)** — 1 and 2 are correct (Rs 7.14 lakh crore of a Rs 16.96 lakh crore target = 42.1%; target 4.3% of GDP). Statement 3 is wrong: the same period of 2025-26 saw about Rs 5.7 lakh crore, so the deficit is wider this year.
3. **(d)** — 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: India is Brazil's fifth-largest trading partner. Trade is expected to touch about USD 20 billion soon.
4. **(a)** — 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: on 14 Feb 2025 the Delhi High Court division bench set aside the single-judge ruling that had upheld the award; the companies are appealing that order in the Supreme Court. (Mediation reference as reported by LiveLaw's 30 Oct blog.)
5. **(c)** — 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: Copernicus is the European Union's Earth observation programme, implemented with ESA and others; NASA does not operate it.
6. **(b)** — Primary deficit = fiscal deficit - interest payments; it shows borrowing needed for expenditure other than interest. Fiscal deficit itself = total expenditure - total receipts excluding borrowings; option (d) is not primary deficit.
7. **(d)** — MARPOL Annex I covers oil. Annex II: noxious liquid substances in bulk; III: harmful substances in packaged form; IV: sewage; V: garbage; VI: air pollution.
