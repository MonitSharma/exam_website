# PART A — BRIEFING

### 1. September GST: gross ₹2.04 lakh crore (+14.7%), and imports, not domestic demand, are carrying the growth — Economy
**Exam angle:** The headline is strong, but the composition is the exam point. Import-linked GST is growing about 2.5 times as fast as domestic GST, which is a ready Mains line on the quality of tax buoyancy. It also gives you a data set to quote for GS3 on revenue mobilisation.
**Memorise:** Released **1 Oct 2026** (provisional). **Gross GST ₹2.04 lakh crore** (+**14.7%** from ₹1.77 lakh crore in Sept 2025). **Net GST ₹1.77 lakh crore** (+**18.1%**). **Domestic ₹1.38 lakh crore** (+10.1%); **import-linked ₹65,525 crore** (+25.9%). **Refunds ₹27,001 crore** (−3%). **April–Sept FY27 gross: ₹12.46 lakh crore** (+11.6%); over the half-year, import GST is up **27.1%** against **6.1%** for domestic. State-wise: **Maharashtra ₹29,986 crore** (+15%) leads, **Karnataka ₹13,884 crore** (+16%), **Assam +88%** is the fastest riser. *Mains line:* "A tax base that grows on imports is only as durable as the import bill."
**Notebook:** GS3 – Economy (Public finance; Taxation).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal Economy, *Taxation → GST (structure, IGST, GST Council, compensation)*; Laxmikanth, *Art. 246A, Art. 279A*. *Nudge:* add a "gross vs net vs domestic vs import" row for Sept 2026 to your monthly GST tracker, and cross-link to your note on the Union–State fiscal federalism angle.

### 3. ISRO lines up seven launches by March 2027, and the second spaceport at Kulasekarapattinam is due in 2026-27 — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** The launch pipeline ties together the Gaganyaan programme, the small-launcher segment and India's launch-site geography. The Kulasekarapattinam spaceport is a likely "Places in News" and static-science hybrid. The SSLV hand-over to a private manufacturer is a good example of the Indian Space Policy 2023 in action.
**Memorise:** ISRO Chairman **V. Narayanan** said **seven launches** are planned by **March 2027** using **PSLV, GSLV, LVM3 and SSLV**; missions include the first **uncrewed Gaganyaan** flight plus Earth-observation, communication and navigation satellites. He said **two satellites are ready** and **five to six missions** are in final integration. The second spaceport at **Kulasekarapattinam (Tamil Nadu)** is expected to become operational in **2026-27** for **SSLV** launches. Background: the **SSLV technology** was transferred to **HAL**, which won manufacturing rights after a competition; **IN-SPACe** authorises private space activity and **NSIL** handles commercial deals. *(One report's vehicle-wise counts did not add up to seven, so only the total and vehicle list are used here.)*
**Notebook:** GS3 – Science & Technology (Space).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum / Shankar IAS, *Space technology → launch vehicles (PSLV, GSLV, LVM3, SSLV) and launch sites*. *Nudge:* add a "Sriharikota (AP) vs Kulasekarapattinam (TN)" row to your launch-site table, with SSLV against polar and geosynchronous orbits, and cross-link to your IN-SPACe / Space Policy 2023 note.

### 4. CAQM rewrites GRAP before winter: diesel generators and bus curbs move into Stage I, and construction oversight widens — Environment
**Exam angle:** The shift is from reacting to a smog episode to front-loading curbs. It tests your grasp of GRAP as a *graded, AQI-triggered* tool run by a statutory commission, which is the core of the Delhi air-pollution case study for Mains.
**Memorise:** Approved by the **Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM)** on **28 Sept 2026**. **Diesel generator-set** and **inter-city bus** restrictions moved from **Stage II to Stage I**. **Stage III** limits on **BS-III petrol and BS-IV diesel** vehicles now extend to **Sonipat** and border areas of **Ghaziabad, Faridabad and Gautam Buddha Nagar**. Directions **11–18** extend construction oversight beyond municipal limits (controlled areas, industrial areas, highway corridors); projects of **500 sq m and above** must register on the state pollution board portal and install monitoring cameras. The **Citizen Charter** grows from **22 to 34 actions**. Separately, Delhi announced a **construction ban from 1 Nov**. CAQM is a statutory body under the **CAQM Act, 2021**. Standard GRAP stages by AQI: **I Poor (201–300), II Very Poor (301–400), III Severe (401–450), IV Severe+ (>450)**.
**Notebook:** GS3 – Environment (Pollution; institutions).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, *Environmental Pollution → Air pollution (NCAP, AQI, GRAP, CAQM)*. *Nudge:* add "GRAP revised 28 Sept 2026: pre-emptive Stage I" to your Delhi-NCR air-quality case study, with a cross-link to today's firecracker case (item 5).

### 5. Supreme Court: no total firecracker ban, no 24-hour bursting — time-limited use is the middle path — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** A clean case of the Court balancing fundamental rights against each other: religious and cultural practice (Art. 25) on one side, and health and a clean environment (Art. 21) on the other. It is a strong Mains example of judicial balancing and of the Court as environmental regulator.
**Memorise:** ***Arjun Gopal v. Union of India*** (W.P.(C) 728/2015), bench of **Justices M.M. Sundresh and Augustine George Masih**. The Court said it would not agree to a **total ban** and would not allow **unrestricted use**, and signalled **specified bursting hours** and limits on cracker types during festivals. It approved manufacture of **new-generation joint (series) crackers** subject to conditions (shell size and quantity limits, intervals between bursts) based on **CSIR-NEERI** specifications. **Barium** content in **Generation-2 green crackers** is being tested in **Delhi, Bhopal, Kolkata and Chennai**; the decision is **deferred to 15 Oct 2026**. Delhi-NCR restrictions run in the separate **M.C. Mehta** proceedings. Constitutional hooks: **Art. 21** (health, clean environment), **Art. 25(1)** (subject to public order, morality, health).
**Notebook:** GS2 – Polity (Judiciary; Fundamental Rights); GS3 – Environment.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, *Fundamental Rights → Art. 21 and Art. 25*. *Nudge:* add "No total ban; time-bound use; barium test decision 15 Oct 2026" under your Art. 21 environmental-rights case list, and cross-link to the GRAP item above.

### Mains question (GS3, 150 words)
**Critically examine** whether the Graded Response Action Plan (GRAP) is an adequate response to the recurring winter air pollution in Delhi-NCR.
*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. **Intro:** GRAP is a statutory-commission-run, AQI-triggered emergency protocol (CAQM Act 2021); the Sept 2026 revision moves some curbs (DG sets, buses) into Stage I and widens construction oversight.
2. **Strengths:** graded, predictable triggers; a single commission across Delhi, Haryana, UP and Rajasthan; pre-emptive Stage I; wider geography (Sonipat, border districts).
3. **Limitations:** it manages peaks, not causes (stubble burning, transport, industry, power); enforcement gaps (closure orders issued then units resumed after compliance); compliance burden falls on the poor (construction workers); firecracker and festival season tensions (SC: neither total ban nor free use).
4. **Way forward:** shift to year-round source control (NCAP, EV and public transport, cleaner industrial fuel), airshed-level planning, real-time source apportionment, and incentives with enforcement.

# PART C — ANSWERS

- **Q1 – (b).** Gross ₹2.04 lakh crore (+14.7%); import GST +25.9% against domestic +10.1%. Statement 3 is wrong: refunds fell 3% to ₹27,001 crore.
- **Q2 – (a).** The US holds the 2026 presidency; the AU joined as a full member in 2023. The G20 has no permanent secretariat (it relies on the rotating presidency and troika).
- **Q3 – (c).** Kulasekarapattinam in Tamil Nadu is planned for SSLV launches; Sriharikota (Andhra Pradesh) is the main spaceport.
- **Q4 – (d).** CAQM approved the revision on 28 Sept 2026 and moved DG-set curbs to Stage I. Statement 3 is wrong: GRAP is implemented under CAQM direction (CAQM Act, 2021), not issued by the CPCB.
- **Q5 – (b).** Statements 1 and 2 match the bench's remarks and order. Statement 3 is wrong: the barium decision on Gen-2 green crackers is deferred to 15 Oct 2026.
- **Q6 – (d).** Art. 25(1) reads "subject to public order, morality and health and to the other provisions of this Part".
- **Q7 – (c).** All three statements are correct: Art. 279A (101st Amendment, 2016), the Union Finance Minister as Chairperson, and the three-fourths weighted-vote rule (Centre one-third, States two-thirds).
