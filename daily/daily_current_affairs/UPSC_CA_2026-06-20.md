## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. Bangladesh revives the Padma Barrage as the 1996 Ganga Waters Treaty heads to its 2026 expiry
**Subject: International Relations / Geography**

- **Exam angle:** Classic transboundary-river flashpoint. The 30-year Ganga Waters Treaty (signed 12 Dec 1996) lapses in **December 2026**, exactly as Dhaka's interim government has revived a domestic **Padma Barrage** project. Tests your grasp of Farakka, India's "Neighbourhood First" policy, and federalism (a state — West Bengal — can stall river diplomacy, as with Teesta).
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - Treaty signed **12 Dec 1996** (Deve Gowda ↔ Sheikh Hasina); **30-year** term; shares **dry-season (Jan–May)** flows of the Ganga at the **Farakka Barrage** in **West Bengal** across fifteen 10-day cycles.
  - In Bangladesh the Ganga is called the **Padma**. The proposed Padma Barrage's Phase 1 includes a ~**2.1-km** structure with channel restoration of the Madhumati and Hisna–Mathabhanga rivers; targeted completion **2031**.
  - Dhaka's demand in renewal talks: account for **upstream withdrawals** (UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttarakhand) before Farakka, not just the Farakka gauge.
- **Notebook:** GS2 (IR — India & neighbourhood) + GS1 (Geography — drainage).
- **Static anchor:** *NCERT Class XI 'India: Physical Environment'* — **Drainage System (Ganga & distributaries; Farakka/Bhagirathi–Hooghly)** + your IR "India–Bangladesh" page. *Nudge:* add a cross-link card comparing **Ganga Treaty vs Indus Waters Treaty vs the unresolved Teesta**.

### 2. Iran's enriched-uranium "downblending" back on the negotiating table
**Subject: Science & Technology**

- **Exam angle:** High-yield Sci-Tech + IR crossover (post the 2025 West Asia conflict). Lets examiners test the **nuclear fuel cycle** — enrichment levels, what "weapons-grade" means, and the chemistry of **downblending**. Directly relevant to your Physics optional.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - **Natural uranium** ≈ **0.7% U-235**; reactor-grade **LEU ≈ 3–5%**; **20%** is the conventional HEU threshold; **~90%** U-235 = **weapons-grade**.
  - Iran's stockpile of **60%-enriched** uranium was reported by the **IAEA** at ~**440.9 kg** (Sept 2025 report). Proposals on the table: downblend the 60% material to **20%** or even **~3.67%** (the JCPOA 2015 cap), under **IAEA** supervision.
  - **Downblending** = mixing highly enriched uranium with **natural/depleted uranium** to *reduce* the U-235 fraction (irreversible if done on-site). Enrichment itself is done by spinning **UF₆** gas in **centrifuges**.
- **Notebook:** GS3 (Sci-Tech — nuclear) + GS2 (IR — IAEA/NPT).
- **Static anchor:** Your **Sci-Tech "Nuclear energy: fuel cycle & enrichment"** note + IR "NPT / IAEA / JCPOA" card. *Nudge:* link to **India's three-stage nuclear programme** and India's **non-NPT** status.

### 3. Nipah virus resurfaces in Kerala (Kozhikode)
**Subject: Society / Public Health**

- **Exam angle:** Recurring zoonotic spillover — a reliable Sci-Tech/health hook. Tests reservoir host, transmission, "One Health," and why Kerala is repeatedly hit (forest-edge living, fruit-bat habitat loss, dense health surveillance that *detects* more).
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - Nipah is a **zoonotic** *Henipavirus* (family **Paramyxoviridae**); natural reservoir = **fruit bats of genus *Pteropus*** ("flying foxes").
  - Causes fever, encephalitis (brain inflammation) and respiratory illness; **high case-fatality (~40–75%)**; **no licensed vaccine** — a **WHO priority pathogen**. First identified **1998–99 (Malaysia, Sungai Nipah village)**; Kerala's first outbreak was **2018 (Kozhikode)**, with recurrences in 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025.
  - 2026 case: a Kozhikode resident critical on ventilator; epidemiology pointed to exposure while **cleaning a bat-infested building**.
- **Notebook:** GS2 (Health/Governance) + GS3 (Sci-Tech — biotech/diseases).
- **Static anchor:** **Sci-Tech "Emerging & zoonotic diseases"** note. *Nudge:* add the **One Health** concept + **NIV Pune / BSL-4** containment and India's mobile BSL-3 lab.

### 4. Korea district's "5% Model" — community-led groundwater recharge wins national attention
**Subject: Environment / Governance**

- **Exam angle:** A ready-made **Mains case study** on participatory water governance and *jal sanchay*. Pairs neatly with groundwater schemes and the over-exploitation problem; quotable in Environment, governance and women-in-development answers.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - In **Korea district, Chhattisgarh**, farmers dedicate **5% of their farmland** to **soak pits/trenches** that recharge groundwater.
  - Scale jumped from **~2,000 farmers (2025) to ~30,000 (2026)**; **~2.8 million m³** recharged; a historic **5.41 m** rise in groundwater level. Praised in **Mann Ki Baat**; Jal Shakti Ministry floated it as a national template.
  - Women led as **"Neer Nayikas"**; youth volunteers as **"Jal Doots."**
- **Notebook:** GS3 (Environment — water conservation) + GS2 (Governance — community participation).
- **Static anchor:** **Shankar IAS Environment — "Water resources / conservation"** + your schemes page. *Nudge:* cross-link **Atal Bhujal Yojana** (Central Sector, World-Bank-aided, launched 1 Apr 2020) and **CGWB's "over-exploited" block** categorisation.

### 5. Retail inflation (CPI) climbs to 3.93% in May 2026
**Subject: Economy**

- **Exam angle:** Fresh, examinable data point. Tests the CPI machinery — who compiles it, the **new 2024=100 base**, the RBI's **flexible inflation-targeting** anchor, and CPI vs WPI/CFPI.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - **CPI (Combined) inflation = 3.93%** (provisional) for **May 2026** — up from **3.48%** in April; the **fifth straight monthly rise**. Rural 4.25%, Urban 3.53%.
  - **Food (CFPI)** inflation = **4.78%**. Compiled by the **National Statistical Office (NSO)** under **MoSPI**, now on **base year 2024=100**.
  - RBI's target: **4% CPI, band ±2% (2–6%)** — May's print sits **comfortably within the band**. (For context, the RBI's June 2026 MPC held the **repo at 5.25%**.)
- **Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — inflation & monetary policy).
- **Static anchor:** **Mrunal Economy — Inflation pillar (CPI/WPI, base year, monetary policy)**. *Nudge:* attach the **inflation-targeting** card (Urjit Patel Committee → 4±2%, FY2016 amendment to RBI Act) and note the **base-year shift to 2024=100**.

### Mains-Style Question (GS2 — IR)

**Q.** *"India's water-sharing arrangements with its neighbours are increasingly strained by climate variability and shifting political equations."* In this light, **examine** the challenges before India in renewing the 1996 Ganga Waters Treaty with Bangladesh. **(15 marks, 250 words)**

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro / context:** The 1996 Treaty (30-year, dry-season sharing at Farakka) expires **Dec 2026**; renewal coincides with Dhaka reviving the **Padma Barrage** and a political transition in Bangladesh — raising the stakes of "hydro-diplomacy."
- **Challenges (body):** (i) **Climate stress** — shrinking lean-season flows make the fixed Farakka formula brittle; (ii) **Bangladesh's demand** to count *upstream* Indian withdrawals (UP, Bihar, Jharkhand, Uttarakhand), not just the Farakka gauge; (iii) **Federal friction** — a riparian state (West Bengal) can veto, as the **Teesta** stalemate shows; (iv) **Trust deficit** post-2024 political churn in Dhaka and third-party (China) interest in basin projects; (v) absence of a **basin-wide, data-sharing** framework.
- **Way forward:** Shift from a single-point to a **basin-level, climate-resilient** approach; institutionalise **real-time data sharing** via the Joint Rivers Commission; bring **states on board** early; anchor it in **Neighbourhood First** and SDG-6.
- **Conclusion:** Equitable, climate-adjusted water-sharing is a cornerstone of regional stability and India's credibility as an upper riparian.

## PART C — ANSWERS & EXPLANATIONS

**Q1 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** The 1996 Treaty covers only the **Ganga at Farakka**; the **Teesta** is a separate, still-unresolved dispute. 30-year term → expires Dec 2026. *(St. 3 wrong.)*

**Q2 — (c) 1 and 2 only.** Natural U ≈ 0.7% U-235 (<1%); ~90% = weapons-grade. **Downblending *lowers*** the U-235 fraction by mixing with depleted/natural uranium. *(St. 3 wrong.)*

**Q3 — (d) 1 and 2 only.** *Pteropus* fruit bats are the reservoir; Nipah causes encephalitis/respiratory illness. There is **no licensed vaccine** — a WHO priority pathogen. *(St. 3 wrong.)*

**Q4 — (c).** The "5% Model" is **Korea district, Chhattisgarh**: farmers set aside 5% of farmland for recharge pits; lifted groundwater by 5.41 m and scaled to ~30,000 farmers.

**Q5 — (b) 1 and 2 only.** CPI is compiled by **NSO/MoSPI**; RBI targets **4% ±2%**. May 2026 CPI was **3.93%**, **within** the band, not above the 6% upper limit. *(St. 3 wrong.)*

**Q6 — (b) 1 and 2 only.** Farakka is on the **Ganga in West Bengal**; its feeder canal feeds the **Bhagirathi–Hooghly** (to flush the Kolkata–Haldia port), **not** the Brahmaputra. *(St. 3 wrong.)*

**Q7 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** Stage 1 = **PHWR + natural uranium**; Stage 3 = **thorium**; programme conceived by **Homi Bhabha**. All correct.
