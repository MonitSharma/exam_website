## Briefing

### 1. RBI hikes repo rate 25 bps to 5.50% — first hike since Feb 2023; stance turns "calibrated tightening" — Economy
**Exam angle:** After holding 5.25% for four reviews (125 bps of cuts in 2025), the MPC voted unanimously for a hike. The Governor said inflation and its outlook "are not benign" as last year, and near-term cuts are off the table. Drivers cited: food and fuel inflation with second-round risks, a deficient monsoon (2026 closed at 87% of LPA), crude near US$100/bbl and elevated global yields. A classic supply-shock vs. demand-management debate for Mains.
**Memorize:** Repo 5.50%; SDF 5.25%; MSF and Bank Rate 5.75%; CPI FY27 5.2% (Q2 4.9, Q3 6.0, Q4 5.7); real GDP FY27 7.1%; inflation target 4% ±2%.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Monetary Policy, Inflation).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal Economy pillar "Monetary policy and inflation targeting" / Ramesh Singh, Money & Banking chapter. *Update:* add the 2026 hike cycle to the policy-rate timeline and cross-link to the 87% LPA monsoon note (Geography).

### 2. Nobel Prize in Physics 2026: Francis Halzen, for building IceCube and opening neutrino astronomy — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** Announced 6 Oct and reported as a sole award. IceCube is a cubic-kilometre detector in Antarctic ice at the South Pole that records faint light from charged particles made when neutrinos interact. Its results mark the birth of "multi-messenger astrophysics", the same family of ideas behind India's planned INO. Directly useful for the Physics optional (particle physics, astrophysics).
**Memorize:** Halzen first proposed the project in 1988; IceCube completed c. 2011; first high-energy extraterrestrial neutrinos 2013; blazar source evidence 2017–18; Milky Way neutrinos 2023. Neutrinos: neutral, nearly massless, weakly interacting.
**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Physics/Space) + Physics optional.
**Static anchor:** Spectrum / NCERT Class 12 Physics (Nuclei) and Shankar IAS "Basic Science: particles". *Update:* add IceCube next to INO (Bodi West Hills, Theni, Tamil Nadu) as a comparison card.

### 3. Jaishankar's Africa tour: Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia — science and agriculture MoUs in Accra — International Relations
**Exam angle:** The Ghana leg (6 Oct) covered trade, defence, technology and multilateral engagement, and produced two MoUs: Zoological Survey of India with the University of Ghana, and ICAR with CSIR-Crops Research Institute. It follows PM Modi's July 2025 Ghana visit (first bilateral PM visit in about three decades) and positions India for the AU/ECOWAS outreach.
**Memorize:** Tour = Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia (Ethiopia hosts the African Union HQ); MoUs = ZSI–Univ. of Ghana, ICAR–CSIR-CRI. Ghana's President Mahama is slated to take the AU chair in 2027 (per one source; verify).
**Notebook:** GS2 International Relations (India–Africa).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS / Mrunal IR pillar "India and Africa" (India-Africa Forum Summit, AU's G20 membership 2023). *Update:* add Ghana row to the India-Africa partnership table.

### 4. Cabinet clears Integrated Transport & Logistics Authority (ITLA) and a ₹10,000 crore SME Growth Fund — Economy (Infrastructure)
**Exam angle:** ITLA is an apex body to prepare a long-term transport master plan across eight domains (roads, railways, ports and shipping, civil aviation, inland waterways, coastal shipping, urban mobility, logistics), appraise and monitor projects of ₹500 crore and above, and run a data repository (reports mention GSTN, FASTag, Vahan data). The SME Growth Fund is a ₹10,000 crore equity fund for growth-stage SMEs, aimed at the "missing middle" between startups and bank credit. Reports describe it as an AIF; its manager and structure were not confirmed.
**Memorize:** ITLA threshold ₹500 crore; 8 transport domains; SME fund ₹10,000 crore (direct equity). Fits PM Gati Shakti and the National Logistics Policy 2022 (logistics cost target to be benchmarked, not memorised).
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Infrastructure, MSMEs).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal Economy pillar "Infrastructure and Logistics". *Update:* link ITLA with PM Gati Shakti and NLP 2022 in one flow chart.

### 5. Gujarat and Assam Uniform Civil Code laws receive Presidential assent — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** After Uttarakhand, two more states have UCC laws cleared at the Presidential level. Gujarat's law covers marriage registration and monogamy, equal inheritance for sons and daughters, intestate succession, status of surrogacy/adoption children and live-in relationships, and excludes Scheduled Tribes. Key analytical issue: Art 44 is a non-justiciable DPSP, and personal law sits on the Concurrent List (Entry 5), so a state law conflicting with a central law needs Presidential assent under Art 254(2). Reports on assent dates conflict (one cites 17 Sep for Gujarat and 29 Sep for Assam; another is dated 2 Oct), so treat exact dates as unverified.
**Memorize:** Art 44 (DPSP); Concurrent List Entry 5; Art 254(2); ST exclusion tied to Part XXI / Sixth Schedule protections.
**Notebook:** GS2 Polity.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth — DPSPs and Centre-State Legislative Relations. *Update:* add a "State UCCs" row (Goa, Uttarakhand, Gujarat, Assam) and cross-link to the Art 200/201/254 note of 27 Sep.

### C. Mains Question
**"Critically examine the RBI's shift to a 'calibrated tightening' stance in the face of supply-side inflation. Is monetary policy the right tool?"** (15 marks, 250 words)
Model-answer skeleton:
1. Context: 25 bps hike to 5.50%; FY27 CPI projected 5.2% (peaking about 6.0% in Q3); stance reset from neutral.
2. Case for: anchor expectations, prevent second-round effects from food and fuel, defend the 4% ±2% mandate, guard against imported inflation from US$100 oil and high global yields.
3. Case against / limits: food and fuel shocks are supply-driven and respond better to buffer stocks, duty cuts and import management; a hike risks growth when GDP is projected at 7.1% and slowing through H2.
4. Way forward: policy mix with fiscal and supply measures, data-dependent guidance, and clear communication.

## Answers
1. **(c)** Repo 5.50% and FY27 GDP 7.1% are right; SDF is 5.25%, not 5.50%.
2. **(a)** IceCube is at the South Pole and senses Cherenkov light; neutrinos are electrically neutral.
3. **(b)** Tour = Ghana, Côte d'Ivoire, Ethiopia; MoUs were ZSI–University of Ghana and ICAR–CSIR-CRI, not defence.
4. **(b)** ITLA appraises and monitors projects of ₹500 crore and above and prepares the long-term plan; it is not constitutional and replaces nothing.
5. **(d)** Art 44 is a DPSP; Gujarat's law excludes STs; Uttarakhand implemented first.
6. **(a)** RBI Act s.45ZB (2016 amendment): 6 members, Governor's casting vote; target notified by the Centre with the RBI.
7. **(b)** INO is proposed at Bodi West Hills, Theni, Tamil Nadu.
