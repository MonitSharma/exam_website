## BRIEFING

### 1. Russia offers India the Su-57; full tech transfer + joint production
**Headline:** At SPIEF (St. Petersburg International Economic Forum), Putin renewed Russia's pitch for India to co-develop and co-produce the **Su-57 fifth-generation stealth fighter**, reportedly with *full technology transfer including source-code access* and HAL–Sukhoi joint production.

**Exam angle:** Highest-value IR + defence-indigenisation item. Tests the India–Russia defence relationship, the "strategic autonomy / multi-alignment" frame, and the gap left by India's stalled 5th-gen programmes. Source-code access is the hook that distinguishes this from the Rafale deal (where it stays restricted) — a classic MCQ discriminator and a ready Mains example on "self-reliance vs. import dependence."

**Memorize:**
- Su-57 = 5th-generation, single-seat, twin-engine stealth fighter (Sukhoi).
- India–Russia **FGFA** (Fifth Generation Fighter Aircraft) programme launched **2007**; India **withdrew 2018** (cost, limited tech transfer, stealth/engine concerns).
- India's indigenous **AMCA** (Advanced Medium Combat Aircraft) unlikely operational before **~2035** → near-term capability gap.
- Strategic backdrop: Pakistan reportedly eyeing China's **J-35** stealth fighter.

**Notebook:** GS2 IR (+ cross-tag GS3 Internal Security / Defence).
**Static anchor:** Vision/Mrunal **India–Russia bilateral** notes → add a "5th-gen fighter" row to the defence-deals table (alongside S-400, BrahMos, AK-203, leased nuclear sub). *Cross-link to your AMCA / DRDO page so import-vs-indigenous shows up in one place.*

### 2. RBI June 2026 policy: repo held at 5.25%, growth cut, inflation raised
**Headline:** The MPC (Governor **Sanjay Malhotra**) kept the **repo rate unchanged at 5.25%** for a second straight meeting, retained a **neutral** stance, **cut FY GDP growth to 6.6%** (from 6.9%) and **raised CPI inflation to 5.1%** (from 4.6%) — prompting talk of emerging **stagflation** risk.

**Exam angle:** A data point tied straight to the GS3 monetary-policy syllabus. The combination — slower growth + higher inflation — is the textbook setup for a "stagflation" conceptual MCQ, and the figures are exactly the kind UPSC pairs in match-the-following.

**Memorize:**
- Repo **5.25%**, stance **neutral**; GDP forecast **6.6%**; CPI **5.1%**.
- Repo rate = rate at which RBI lends to banks against G-secs (LAF). MPC = 6 members (3 RBI + 3 govt-appointed); meets bi-monthly; inflation target **4% ±2%** under Flexible Inflation Targeting (since 2016).
- Drivers cited: elevated energy prices, global supply-chain constraints.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal **Pillar 1A (Money & Banking / Monetary Policy)** or Ramesh Singh "Money & Banking" → update your **repo-rate timeline** and MPC stance log. *Add a one-line note distinguishing "neutral vs. accommodative vs. withdrawal of accommodation" stances.*

### 3. State of India's Environment 2026: 7 of 9 planetary boundaries breached; tigers behaving differently
**Headline:** The **SOE 2026** report (Centre for Science and Environment / *Down To Earth*) warns that **seven of nine planetary boundaries** are now breached — **ocean acidification the latest** — and links habitat loss + invasive **Lantana camara** to rising **human–tiger conflict**.

**Exam angle:** Report + concept + diagram, all in one. "Planetary boundaries" is a tested framework; the count (7/9) is a likely factual MCQ. The Lantana → prey depletion → cattle predation → conflict chain is a tidy Mains case study on conservation's "second-order" problems.

**Memorize:**
- **9 planetary boundaries** (Rockström framework); **7 breached** per SOE 2026: climate change, biosphere integrity, land-system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flows (N & P), novel entities, **ocean acidification** (the new 7th).
- **Lantana camara** = invasive alien shrub, now on ~**50%** of India's forest/scrubland; suppresses native grasses → less prey.
- India holds the **world's largest wild tiger population ≈ 3,682** (2022 All-India Tiger Estimation).

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment.
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS **Environment** → "Invasive Alien Species" and "Biodiversity" topics; plus the **Project Tiger / NTCA** page. *Cross-link Lantana under both "invasive species" and "tiger conservation challenges."*

### 5. Supreme Court strength raised 34 → 38 by Ordinance
**Headline:** The **Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Ordinance, 2026** (promulgated 16 May 2026 under **Article 123**) raised the sanctioned strength to **38** (CJI + 37), up from 34 (CJI + 33) — to cut pendency and convene Constitution Benches more often. Filling of seats has begun; commentators flag debate over using an ordinance for the judiciary.

**Exam angle:** Landmark Polity item with crisp MCQ hooks: who fixes SC strength (Parliament by law, **Art. 124(1)**), the ordinance route (**Art. 123**), and the number itself. Mains angle: separation of powers / judicial independence vs. executive ordinance-making.

**Memorize:**
- SC sanctioned strength: **34 → 38** (incl. CJI); judges other than CJI **33 → 37**.
- **Art. 124(1):** SC strength = "such number of Judges as Parliament may by law prescribe." Promulgated via **Art. 123** (President's ordinance power when Parliament not in session).
- Original 1950 strength: 8 (CJI + 7); raised over decades to 34 (2019) and now 38 (2026).

**Notebook:** GS2 Polity.
**Static anchor:** **Laxmikanth → "Supreme Court"** chapter; update the strength table and add a cross-link to the **"Ordinance (Art. 123 / 213)"** topic. *Note: strength is statutory, not constitutional — so an ordinance can change it.*

### Mains Question

**Q (GS3, 250 words):** *"India's pursuit of a fifth-generation fighter capability sits at the intersection of strategic autonomy, defence indigenisation, and a near-term capability gap." Critically examine in light of the recent Russian offer to co-produce the Su-57.*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. **Frame the gap:** AMCA not operational before ~2035; regional pressure (China's J-20, Pakistan's prospective J-35) → near-term 5th-gen vacuum.
2. **Case for the offer:** Full tech transfer + source-code access aids indigenisation, jobs, and HAL's learning curve; deepens a time-tested defence partnership.
3. **Case against / risks:** FGFA experience (exited 2018) — cost, stealth/engine doubts; CAATSA-type sanctions exposure; opportunity cost vs. funding AMCA; over-dependence on one supplier dilutes strategic autonomy.
4. **Balanced way forward:** Tie any deal to genuine ToR and ecosystem-building; protect AMCA timelines; diversify; let it complement, not replace, indigenous capability. Conclude on "strategic autonomy through capability, not just procurement."

## ANSWERS

**Q1 — (a).** The Su-57 is Russia's fifth-generation stealth fighter (Sukhoi); the offer is for joint production with tech transfer. (S-400 is the air-defence system; BrahMos the cruise missile.)

**Q2 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** Repo held at 5.25% (correct) and inflation raised to 5.1% (correct); GDP was *cut* to 6.6%, not raised — so statement 2 is wrong.

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2, 3 and 4.** All four are among the nine planetary boundaries (the others: land-system change, freshwater change, biogeochemical flows, novel entities, atmospheric aerosol loading). SOE 2026 flags ocean acidification as the latest breached.

**Q4 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** NDB was set up by BRICS, is HQ'd in Shanghai, and uses one-country-one-vote with no veto.

**Q5 — (c) 38 judges including the CJI.** The 2026 Ordinance raised total strength from 34 to 38 (CJI + 37). Option (d) is wrong because excluding the CJI the number is 37, not 31.

**Q6 — (c) 3 only.** Statement 1 is wrong — the Constitution leaves the number to Parliament by law (Art. 124(1)), it does not fix a maximum. Statement 2 is imprecise/wrong as framed — the President promulgates an *ordinance* under Art. 123 only when Parliament is not in session and it must be a legislative act later ratified by Parliament; the President does not independently "set" SC strength. Statement 3 is correct — the 1950 strength was CJI + 7.

**Q7 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** Project Tiger launched 1973; NTCA notifies tiger reserves (statutory body under the WLPA, 2006 amendment); *Lantana camara* is an invasive alien species.
