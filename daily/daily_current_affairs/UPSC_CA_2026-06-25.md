## BRIEFING

### 1. FCRA (Regulation) Amendment Rules, 2026 notified — tighter leash on foreign-funded NGOs
**Subject / Notebook:** Polity & Governance — GS2 (also a GS3 internal-security cross-link).

**Exam angle:** A live example of executive rule-making under a parent Act, the State–civil society interface, and the "national sovereignty vs. associational freedom (Art. 19(1)(c))" tension. Classic GS2 governance + GS3 (foreign funding as an internal-security lever). The 2020 amendment + this 2026 tightening are a ready Mains case study on regulation of NGOs.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- Notified by the **Ministry of Home Affairs** on **22 June 2026** as the *Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Rules, 2026* (rules under the **FCRA, 2010**; original Act **1976**).
- NGOs must now pick activities/areas of operation from a **fixed list under five categories — social, economic, educational, cultural, religious**.
- **Proselytisation explicitly barred** under the religious sub-categories; NGOs must **disclose social-media accounts** and **ultimate donors** in intermediary funding.
- Bodies with **foreign nationals (other than persons of Indian origin) in key positions are generally ineligible** for registration/prior permission.
- **Renewal threshold:** must have spent **≥ ₹10 lakh** of foreign contribution on declared activities over the previous two FYs; **₹300 fee** per extra state/purpose.

**Static anchor:** *Laxmikanth* — "Non-Governmental Organisations / Pressure groups"; pair with 2nd ARC governance notes. **Nudge:** add an FCRA timeline box (1976 → 2010 → 2020 → 2026) and cross-link to GS3 "money laundering / foreign funding & internal security."

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **SEBI** passed a **109-page interim ex-parte order on 3 June 2026** (WTM **K.C. Varshney**) against **Rajesh Exports** (Bengaluru-based, a major gold refiner) and promoter **Rajesh Mehta**.

- **ED conducted searches** on the company around **23 June 2026** under PMLA/FEMA angles.

**Static anchor:** *Mrunal Economy* Pillar 1C (financial markets) → SEBI: establishment (**1988**; statutory under **SEBI Act, 1992**), functions, powers. **Nudge:** add a one-liner case-card under "SEBI – enforcement powers (ex-parte interim orders)."

### 3. Green Hydrogen Certification Portal of India (GHCI) launched
**Subject / Notebook:** Environment / Energy transition — GS3 (Sci-Tech cross-link).

**Exam angle:** Operationalises a flagship mission → high-probability Prelims fact + Mains "energy transition / decarbonising hard-to-abate sectors." The portal is the *compliance/verification* layer that makes "green" hydrogen auditable.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- Launched **18 June 2026** by **MNRE** (Minister **Pralhad Joshi**) under the **National Green Hydrogen Mission (NGHM)**.
- Portal **verifies that hydrogen is made from renewable energy** and meets the emission standard; **India's norm: "green" only if < 2 kg CO₂-equivalent per kg of H₂**.
- NGHM (launched **Jan 2023**): outlay **₹19,744 crore**; target **5 MMT/yr green-H₂ by 2030**, ~**125 GW** added RE capacity.
- **₹100 crore** earmarked for green-H₂ start-ups; first **9 start-ups** cleared (₹22 crore); supply contracts of **30,000 MTPA** to IOCL/BPCL/HPCL/NRL.

**Static anchor:** *Shankar IAS Environment* — "Climate change → Renewable energy / Hydrogen economy." **Nudge:** add the **< 2 kg CO₂e/kg** threshold and 5-MMT-by-2030 target to your NGHM flashcard.

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS2)

**Q.** *"The Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Amendment Rules, 2026 tighten the State's oversight of foreign-funded civil-society organisations."* **Critically examine** the implications of such regulation for civil society and national security. **(15 marks, 250 words)**

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Define FCRA's purpose (regulate foreign funds so they don't harm sovereignty/internal security); note the 2026 Rules (five-category activity-mapping, donor & social-media disclosure, foreign-national bar, ₹10 lakh spend threshold).
- **Case *for* tighter regulation:** Prevents misuse/round-tripping and foreign influence on internal affairs; transparency & accountability of donor money; consistency with FATF/anti-money-laundering norms; protects national security (Art. 19 reasonable restrictions).
- **Case *against* / concerns:** Compliance burden may shrink genuine NGO space (health, education, relief); chilling effect on dissent and associational freedom (Art. 19(1)(c)); reduced foreign funding hits service delivery where State capacity is thin; risk of arbitrary, opaque application.
- **Way forward / conclusion:** Calibrated regulation — clear, time-bound, appealable procedures; distinguish security-risk cases from routine welfare NGOs; independent review; balance accountability with a vibrant civil society (2nd ARC spirit).

## ANSWERS

**Q1 — (c) All three.** All three provisions appear in the FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026 notified by the MHA on 22 June 2026.

**Q2 — (b) SEBI.** SEBI's interim ex-parte order (3 June 2026) targeted Rajesh Exports over unverifiable revenues (~₹15.15 lakh crore, FY21–FY25).

**Q3 — (b) MNRE — National Green Hydrogen Mission.** The GHCI portal (launched 18 June 2026) provides certification under the NGHM.

**Q4 — (c) Both 1 and 2.** The June 2026 Bulletin projected FY27 real GDP at 6.6% and warned a weak SW monsoon could weigh on growth and inflation.

**Q5 — (b).** The Forum (30 June–1 July 2026) promotes legal/ethical migration by connecting Indian workers with vetted foreign employers (focus: Denmark, Germany, Italy, Japan, Russia).

**Q6 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** The < 2 kg CO₂e/kg "green" threshold and the 5-MMT-by-2030 target are correct; the Mission is implemented by **MNRE**, not the Petroleum Ministry — so statement 3 is wrong.

**Q7 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Paddy and cotton are kharif (sown with the SW monsoon); mustard/rapeseed is a **rabi** crop.
