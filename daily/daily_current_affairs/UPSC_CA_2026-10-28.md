## Briefing

### 1. Cabinet clears ₹10,000 crore Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme — Economy
**Exam angle:** India depends heavily on imported shipping containers; the scheme links manufacturing policy (Make in India) to maritime logistics and the Sagarmala/Maritime Amrit Kaal agenda. Mains hook: import substitution, supply-chain resilience and freight-cost competitiveness.
**Key facts:** Approved by the Union Cabinet on 28 Oct 2026 · outlay ₹10,000 crore, up to FY 2039–40 · target ~7.5 lakh TEU/year (about 10x current capacity) · >53,000 jobs expected · components reported: interest subvention, testing and certification support, land-leasing support, opex assistance. *Also cleared the same day:* 3% DA/DR hike from 1 Jul 2026; Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme (₹1,856 crore, 2026–27 to 2033–34); PM Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana (₹7,395 crore, 2026–27 to 2030–31, centrally sponsored, ~2.78 lakh rural local bodies). *Flag:* the earlier August proposal was described as a five-year scheme; the approved version runs to FY 2039–40 (media reports differ slightly on structure, so check the PIB release).
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Manufacturing / Infrastructure — Ports).
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh, Infrastructure chapter (Ports, Sagarmala) + Mrunal, Industry/Trade pillar. *Nudge:* add a "container manufacturing" bullet under Make in India sectors and cross-link to the Vadhavan port note.

### 2. DAC clears ₹2.12 lakh crore of proposals (26 Oct); Army's Land Warfare Doctrine 2026 released (23 Oct) — Internal Security
**Exam angle:** AoN is only the first step in the capital acquisition chain; the ~82% Indian-industry share and the counter-drone/kamikaze-drone focus reflect lessons from Operation Sindoor. The new doctrine replaces the December 2018 edition and stresses AI, drones, autonomy and multi-domain operations.
**Key facts:** DAC chaired by Defence Minister · AoN ≈ ₹2.12 lakh crore · ~82% from Indian industry · Army items: High Mobility Vehicles, Self-Propelled Tracked Gun System, VSHORADS, platform-based counter-UAS, long-range kamikaze drones, surveillance UAVs, Light Multi-Role Helicopters · Navy: Active Offboard Decoy System · IAF: Special Utility Helicopters, Mobile X-Band Doppler Weather Radar · Doctrine released 23 Oct at the Army Commanders' Conference; detailed provisions are not public.
**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (Defence).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, Internal Security — defence procurement. *Nudge:* add the DAP 2020 category order and the AoN→RFP→trials→contract chain; cross-link to the Operation Sindoor note.

### 3. Ashoke Sen becomes the first Indian physicist to receive the Oskar Klein Medal — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** Relevant to Physics optional (string theory, dualities, black-hole entropy) and to "Indian scientists in news" in Prelims.
**Key facts:** Received 22 Oct 2026 at Stockholm; awarded by Stockholm University with the Nobel Committee for Physics · lecture: "Gravity from Strings and Strings from Gravity" · Sen: ICTS-Infosys Madhava Chair Professor, ICTS Bengaluru · work: S-duality, black hole entropy, string field theory · earlier: Breakthrough Prize (2012), Dirac Medal (2014).
**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Achievements of Indians) + Physics optional (quantum gravity / string theory).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum, Science & Tech — Indian scientists; Physics optional notes on string theory. *Nudge:* add a one-line "S-duality = strong↔weak coupling equivalence" memory hook.

### 4. Cheer pheasant: Himachal's conservation breeding shows survival rising from 11% to 100% — Environment
**Exam angle:** A case study in ex-situ conservation with soft release and radio/camera-trap monitoring; the species is Schedule I (WPA 1972) and IUCN Vulnerable. Useful Mains example for "successful species recovery".
**Key facts:** Survival of released birds 11.11% (2019–20) → 100% (2024–25) · Khadiyun Breeding Centre, Chail (Solan) has released 77 birds · 12 birds soft-released near Koti, Shimla (Wildlife Week, Oct 2026) · no release in 2025–26 (consolidation year) · threats: grassland fires, grazing, hunting.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (Biodiversity — Conservation).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, Environment — ex-situ conservation and species in news. *Nudge:* add cheer pheasant to the "Himalayan galliformes" list (Western Tragopan, Monal) with status tags.

### 5. Supreme Court (27 Oct) asks Union to revisit acid-sale guidelines, flags rising attacks as policy failure — Society
**Exam angle:** Bench remarks reported from the 27 Oct court round-up; no final holding yet (the matter has been pending in a PIL since earlier this year, when the Court asked the Centre about curbing or banning retail sale). Links gender-based violence, State-subject regulation and enforcement gaps.
**Key facts:** Laxmi v. UoI (2013): curbs on over-the-counter sale, ID and register; minimum compensation ₹3 lakh · Model Poisons Possession and Sale Rules, 2013 under the Poisons Act, 1919 (States to frame own rules) · BNS s.124 (min 10 years to life) · NCRB: 207 reported cases in 2023, 703 pending, only 16 convictions that year.
**Notebook:** GS1 Society (Women) / GS2 Governance.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, Ch. on Fundamental Rights (Art. 21) + Shankar IAS, Women issues. *Nudge:* add the Laxmi case to the Art. 21 judgments table.

### A. Current-affairs MCQs
**Q1.** With reference to the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme approved by the Union Cabinet on 28 October 2026, consider the following statements:

- (b) 2 and 3 only

- (d) 1, 2 and 3

### C. Mains question
**Q8.** Critically examine how far the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme can reduce India's import dependence and strengthen its maritime logistics ecosystem. (15 marks, 250 words)

**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. Intro: scheme basics (₹10,000 crore, to FY 2039–40, ~7.5 lakh TEU, >53,000 jobs) and the rationale of import substitution.
2. Strengths: lower freight and supply-chain risk, jobs and MSME ancillaries, synergy with Sagarmala, Bharat Container Shipping Line and new ports.
3. Challenges: steel and component costs, scale and cost gap versus China, long incentive horizon, execution and the need for demand assurance.
4. Way forward: link to port capacity, performance-linked support, cluster development near ports, skilling; conclude with a balanced assessment.

## Answers

1. **(a)**: Statements 1 and 2 are right; a TEU is based on a 20-foot container.
2. **(b)**: AoN is in-principle and ~82% is Indian-sourced; the DAC is chaired by the Defence Minister, not the CDS.
3. **(d)**: Sen is at ICTS Bengaluru, not TIFR.
4. **(c)**: Schedule I and IUCN Vulnerable are right; it is a western-Himalayan bird, not a Western Ghats endemic.
5. **(c)**: Laxmi (2013) and BNS s.124 are right; the Model Rules are not directly binding on States, which must frame their own.
6. **(b)**: DAP 2020 gives top priority to Buy (Indian-IDDM).
7. **(d)**: Near Threatened < Vulnerable < Endangered < Critically Endangered.
