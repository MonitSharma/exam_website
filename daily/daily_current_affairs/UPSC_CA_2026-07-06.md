### C. Mains-Style Question (GS2 — Polity/Governance & Judiciary, 250 words, 15 marks)

**Directive: Critically examine.**
"The Supreme Court's 2026 ruling against AI-generated 'hallucinated' judicial precedents underscores the risks of unregulated generative-AI use in India's justice-delivery system. Critically examine the challenges this poses to the sanctity of adjudication and suggest a regulatory framework governing AI use by courts and legal practitioners."

**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. **Challenges:** erosion of the sanctity/reliability of precedent-based reasoning; verification burden on already-overloaded courts; due-process risk (Article 21 — fair trial) when fake material influences outcomes; accountability gap — who is liable, the advocate, the AI tool, or the drafting associate?
2. **Underlying causes:** absence of a statutory AI-use policy for courts/lawyers; over-reliance on generative tools without cross-checking against SCC/manual citation databases; uneven digital/AI literacy among the Bar and even some Benches.
3. **Way forward:** BCI-mandated mandatory verification protocols and disclosure ("AI-assisted drafting" flags) in pleadings; an AI-citation-validation layer integrated into e-Courts/NJDG; graded misconduct sanctions for repeat unverified citation; judicial training via the National Judicial Academy on AI tool limitations.
4. **Conclusion:** balance the efficiency gains of AI-enabled legal research (e-Courts Phase III, faster case management) against the non-negotiable requirement that adjudication rest only on verified, real law — technology must remain a tool, not a source, of legal reasoning.

## Answers

**Q1. (d)** — All three are correct: Indonesia was the first leg (Modi's 4th visit there); CSP dates to 2018; the tour continues to New Zealand, Modi's first visit there in ~40 years (since Rajiv Gandhi, 1986).

**Q2. (d)** — All three are correct: Kulasekharapatnam is in Thoothukudi, Tamil Nadu; its southward trajectory avoids the Sriharikota dog-leg manoeuvre around Sri Lanka; DRDO is building a roadmap to use the site for early-warning/satellite tech.

**Q3. (b)** — Seven of nine planetary boundaries have been breached as of the 2026 report, with ocean acidification the newest addition; global forest cover (59%) is well below, not above, the 75% safe threshold, and only 2 of India's 23 CBD biodiversity targets are on track.

**Q4. (d)** — All three are correct per RBI's revised CME framework effective 1 July 2026.

**Q5. (d)** — All three are correct: the Court held even an "iota" of hallucinated material voids a decision, called unverified AI citation a professional-misconduct issue for advocates, and directed the BCI to frame norms.

**Q6. (a)** — Johan Rockström led the Stockholm Resilience Centre team (with co-author Paul Crutzen among others) that introduced the Planetary Boundaries framework in a 2009 *Nature* paper.

**Q7. (b)** — Tier 1 capital is a bank's core, most loss-absorbing capital (equity + disclosed reserves), distinct from the supplementary Tier 2 capital (revaluation reserves, subordinated debt, etc.).
