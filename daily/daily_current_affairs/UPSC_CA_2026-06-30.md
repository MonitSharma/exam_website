## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. RBI finalises a Digital-Fraud Compensation Framework for bank customers — **Economy (Banking / Consumer Protection)**

**Headline:** The RBI notified the *(Commercial Banks – Responsible Business Conduct) Third Amendment Directions, 2026* (issued 24 June 2026), creating a structured compensation mechanism for victims of fraudulent electronic banking transactions (EBTs); rollout deferred six months to **1 January 2027**.

**Exam angle:** A clean "regulatory + consumer-rights" story — builds on the RBI's 2017 *limited-liability* circular for unauthorised electronic transactions, and links to cyber-fraud governance (I4C, helpline 1930). Tests the candidate's grasp of RBI's developmental/consumer-protection role beyond monetary policy.

**Memorise:**
- Compensation = **85% of net loss OR ₹25,000, whichever is lower**, for losses **up to ₹50,000**; a **once-in-a-lifetime** benefit.
- Victim must report to **both** the bank **and** the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal / **Helpline 1930 within 5 calendar days**.
- **Burden of proving customer liability lies on the bank**; bank must respond within **45 days (domestic) / 60 days (cross-border)**.
- Cost-sharing: RBI bears the bulk (≈65% for small losses); customer's bank + beneficiary bank ≈10% each.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Banking & financial-sector regulation); cross-tag GS2 (consumer protection / cyber-governance).
**Static anchor:** *Mrunal → Banking pillar (BC-Banking / customer protection).* Add a cross-link from the 2017 "**zero/limited liability** for unauthorised electronic transactions" note to this 2026 framework, and tag I4C + Helpline 1930.

### 3. **Millisecond pulsar PSR J0125−5854** found by the MWA's SMART survey — **Science & Technology (Radio Astronomy)**

**Headline:** Astronomers reported (arXiv, 17 June 2026; accepted in *ApJ Letters*) the discovery of **PSR J0125−5854**, a ~24.6-ms **millisecond pulsar** in a wide binary — the **first MSP found by the Murchison Widefield Array (MWA)** and the first pulsar from the SMART survey's "deep-pass" search.

**Exam angle:** Pulsars/neutron stars are recurring Prelims Sci-Tech fodder (cf. the 2019 black-hole/gravitational-waves question). **India relevance:** India is a **full member of the SKA Observatory (SKAO)**; the MWA is a key **SKA-Low pathfinder**, and India's **upgraded GMRT (uGMRT, NCRA-TIFR, Pune)** plus the **Indian Pulsar Timing Array (InPTA)** are central to global pulsar-timing and nanohertz gravitational-wave hunts.

**Memorise:**
- **Pulsar** = rapidly rotating, highly magnetised **neutron star** emitting beamed radio pulses; **millisecond pulsars** (spin periods ≈1–10s of ms) act as ultra-precise **cosmic clocks**.
- **MWA** = low-frequency (**80–300 MHz**) array in **Western Australia**; **SMART** = Southern-sky MWA Rapid Two-metre survey. **SKA** is being built in **Australia (SKA-Low) + South Africa (SKA-Mid)**.

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Astronomy & space science).
**Static anchor:** *Sci-Tech notes → "Telescopes & astronomy."* Add SKA-Low/MWA and **uGMRT + InPTA**; cross-link to **AstroSat**, LIGO-India and gravitational waves.

### 5. Cabinet doubles its **NIIF** commitment with an extra **₹30,000 crore** — **Economy (Infrastructure Financing)**

**Headline:** The Union Cabinet on **29 June 2026** approved an additional government investment commitment of **₹30,000 crore** to the **National Investment and Infrastructure Fund (NIIF)**, taking the Centre's total commitment to **₹60,000 crore**; the money seeds **NIIF Infrastructure Fund II** (target corpus ₹30,000 crore).

**Exam angle:** Core "**infrastructure financing**" topic — NIIF is India's quasi-sovereign-wealth/anchor infrastructure-investment platform. Lets you weave in the **National Infrastructure Pipeline, PM Gati Shakti, FAME/PM E-DRIVE** and the role of patient long-term capital in crowding-in private/institutional investment.

**Memorise:**
- NIIF set up in **2015**; **Government of India holds 49%** (rest with marquee global/domestic institutional investors) — so it is **not** a pure sovereign wealth fund.
- Managed by **NIIF Ltd**; funds include the **Master Fund, Fund of Funds, Strategic Opportunities Fund** and a **Private Markets Fund**. New tranche targets **transport, energy, digital infrastructure and e-mobility**.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Infrastructure & investment).
**Static anchor:** *Mrunal → Infrastructure-financing pillar.* Add NIIF's structure (49% GoI) and Fund-II beside the **National Infrastructure Pipeline / National Monetisation Pipeline / PM Gati Shakti**; cross-link "sovereign wealth funds vs NIIF."

### Mains-Style Question (GS3 — Internal Security / Sci-Tech)

**Q.** "Self-reliance in defence depends as much on reforming *how* research is funded as on the technology itself." In the light of recent measures such as the delegation of enhanced financial powers to the DRDO, **examine** how procedural and financing reforms can accelerate India's defence indigenisation. *(250 words)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Frame Aatmanirbharta in defence (high import dependence historically; ~65% of needs once imported) and note DFP-2026 as a financing/procedural reform.
- **Why reform funding matters:** Long approval chains delay trials, testing and pre-project R&D; delegated powers cut lead-time and align with the innovation cycle.
- **Ecosystem levers:** Pair with iDEX, Technology Development Fund, positive-indigenisation lists, DRDO patent free-access, and industry/MSME/start-up/academia partnerships.
- **Caveats / way forward:** Accountability and audit safeguards, avoiding cost/quality trade-offs, exports (e.g., BrahMos), and sustained capital outlay; conclude that process reform multiplies the payoff from technological capability.

## PART C — ANSWERS & EXPLANATIONS

**Q1 — (b) Only two.** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong: implementation was **deferred to 1 January 2027** (from the original 1 July 2026), not immediate.

**Q2 — (a).** DFP-2026 is the **Delegation of Financial Powers to DRDO 2026**, released by the Defence Minister on 29 June 2026 to fast-track strategic R&D.

**Q3 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** Millisecond pulsars are fast-spinning neutron stars used as precise clocks; the MWA is a low-frequency (80–300 MHz) array in Western Australia and an SKA-Low pathfinder that found PSR J0125−5854.

**Q4 — (b).** The analysis reported a steep rise in anxiety disorders (~123%, 1990–2023) with the burden peaking among adolescents (15–19 years).

**Q5 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** Statement 1 is wrong: NIIF (est. 2015) is **not fully GoI-owned** — the Centre holds **49%**, with the rest from institutional investors; the new ₹30,000 cr seeds Infrastructure Fund II, and NIIF Ltd manages the Master Fund, Fund of Funds, etc.

**Q6 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** AstroSat (2015) is India's first multi-wavelength space observatory; uGMRT is near Pune (Khodad); India is an SKAO member (telescopes in Australia + South Africa).

**Q7 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** The Mental Healthcare Act, 2017 decriminalised attempted suicide (presumption of severe stress), guarantees the right to access government mental healthcare, and allows advance directives.
