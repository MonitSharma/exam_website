## Briefing

### 1. India releases I-OECM Guidelines 2026 at CBD COP17, Yerevan (28 Oct) — Environment
**Exam angle:** India's environment ministry (MoEFCC), with the National Biodiversity Authority and UNDP, launched guidelines for designating "Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures" at a side event of the CBD's 17th Conference of the Parties. OECMs let conservation outside national parks and sanctuaries (for example community-managed sites) count towards the global 30x30 goal. For Prelims this is a definitional and treaty-linkage topic. For Mains it ties to community-led conservation and the limits of fortress conservation.
**Memorize:**
- OECM = a geographically defined area outside protected areas, governed and managed to deliver positive, long-term biodiversity outcomes (CBD definition).
- KMGBF Target 3 (adopted at COP15, Montreal, Dec 2022): effectively conserve at least 30% of land, inland waters, coastal and marine areas by 2030, via protected areas and OECMs.
- A 2022 MoEFCC–NBA–UNDP publication on OECM criteria sorted sites into 14 categories. The 2026 guidelines build on it. Three other publications were released at the same event, including India's fifth national report under the Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety.
- Not verified: the nomination and designation procedure in the 2026 guidelines. Read the PIB release before quoting any process detail.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (Biodiversity).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS *Environment* — Biodiversity conservation / Protected Area network. Update: add a "beyond protected areas" box (OECM, I-OECM 2026) and cross-link to the Biological Diversity Act, 2002 and sacred groves/community reserves.

### 2. Forex reserves fall for a seventh straight week to $719.75 bn — Economy
**Exam angle:** RBI data for the week ended 23 Oct 2026 show reserves down $1.27 bn. Gold's dollar value fell about $2.65 bn as the metal dropped about 4%, while foreign currency assets rose $1.36 bn. Reserves are about $66 bn below the 4 Sept record of $785.71 bn. Reported drivers include RBI dollar sales to smooth rupee volatility, a dollar facility for three state-run oil marketing companies, and repayment obligations from the June–Sept foreign-currency deposit inflows. A good Mains case on valuation effect vs intervention.
**Memorize:**
- Components (23 Oct): FCA $593.21 bn; gold $103.15 bn; SDRs $18.61 bn; IMF reserve position $4.79 bn.
- Record: $785.71 bn on 4 Sept 2026. Weekly data are released by RBI on Fridays.
- Valuation effect: gold, euro, pound and yen moves change the dollar value of reserves without any RBI transaction.

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (External sector).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal *Balance of Payments & Forex* pillar / Ramesh Singh, external sector chapter. Update: add "reserves ≠ only intervention: valuation effect" and cross-link to Impossible Trinity and the RBI forward book.

### 3. Centre notifies the National Social Security Board for unorganised, gig and platform workers (28–30 Oct) — Society
**Exam angle:** The Board is created under the Code on Social Security, 2020 and is chaired by the Union Labour Minister. It brings State and Central governments, aggregators, employers, civil society and worker unions to one table. It recommends schemes, monitors Central welfare schemes, advises on administering the Code and reviews State-level record-keeping. It must meet at least three times a year. This is the institutional follow-through for gig-worker coverage that the PLFS stratum story (25 Oct) only measured.
**Memorize:**
- Code on Social Security, 2020 defines "gig worker", "platform worker" and "aggregator". Under the Code, aggregators contribute 1–2% of annual turnover, capped at 5% of what they pay gig and platform workers (Code text).
- Reported notification: S.O. 5884(E), 28 Oct 2026 (one aggregator source; the three-year term also comes from that source only, so treat it as unverified).
- Registration route for gig workers: e-Shram portal.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance / GS1 Society (Labour).
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, *Social Justice* and Vision/Shankar notes on Labour Codes. Update: add the Board to the "institutions under the Code" list and cross-link to e-Shram and the Rajasthan/Karnataka gig-worker laws.

### 4. DRDO's indigenous GaN and SiC chips for radar and electronic warfare — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** A Ministry of Defence report says DRDO has demonstrated Gallium Nitride (GaN) devices for high-frequency military use. Its Solid State Physics Laboratory (SSPL) has developed processes to grow 4-inch Silicon Carbide (SiC) wafers, which serve as the substrate for GaN HEMTs and X-band MMICs. This matters for strategic autonomy in semiconductors, and the Physics-optional link is the wide band-gap argument.
**Memorize:**
- GaN HEMTs up to 150 W; X-band MMICs up to 40 W; a 3.5 × 3 mm chip delivers up to 30 W (as reported).
- Limited GaN-on-SiC MMIC production capability at GAETEC, Hyderabad.
- iDEX contract with Agnit Semiconductors for GaN wireless transmitters.
- Uses: radar, electronic warfare, communications, ISR, unmanned systems; civil spin-offs in 5G and satcom.

**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (Defence tech).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum / Shankar IAS *Science & Tech — Electronics and Semiconductors*; also the Physics optional notes on band theory. Update: add a GaN/SiC vs Si comparison table (band gap ≈3.4 / 3.3 / 1.12 eV) and cross-link to the India Semiconductor Mission.

### Part B3 — Mains question
**Q8. Critically examine how the recognition of Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures (OECMs) can help India meet its 30x30 commitment while protecting community rights. (150 words, 10 marks)**

Model-answer skeleton:
1. **Context:** KMGBF Target 3 asks for 30% of land and sea to be effectively conserved by 2030. India's protected-area network covers only a small share of land (roughly 5%; check the latest figure), so area outside it must count. I-OECM Guidelines 2026 give a designation route.
2. **Benefits:** Counts community-conserved sites (sacred groves, community reserves, Biodiversity Heritage Sites) without costly land acquisition. Improves connectivity between protected areas. Builds on Biodiversity Management Committees and People's Biodiversity Registers.
3. **Risks:** Possible "paper" targets if sites whose main purpose is not conservation are counted. Weak monitoring of outcomes. Rights risk if designation overrides Forest Rights Act, 2006 community rights or happens without free, prior and informed consent.
4. **Way forward:** Outcome-based criteria and independent monitoring; designation only with Gram Sabha/BMC consent; dovetail with FRA community forest resource rights; fund stewardship through benefit-sharing and the National Biodiversity Authority.

## Answers

**Q1. (b)** — 2 and 3 are correct. Statement 1 is wrong: an OECM is an area OUTSIDE formal protected areas that is governed and managed to deliver positive, long-term biodiversity outcomes. Target 3 (30x30) can be met through protected areas and OECMs. MoEFCC launched the I-OECM Guidelines 2026 on 28 Oct 2026 at a COP17 side event, with the National Biodiversity Authority and UNDP.

**Q2. (a)** — 1 and 2 are correct. Gold reserves are valued at market prices, so a ~4% drop in gold cut their dollar value (about $105.8 bn to $103.15 bn) in the week. Statement 3 is wrong: foreign currency assets (~$593.2 bn) are by far the LARGEST component; gold, SDRs (~$18.6 bn) and the IMF reserve position (~$4.8 bn) are smaller.

**Q3. (c)** — 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: the Board is for unorganised workers AND gig and platform workers. It recommends schemes, monitors Central welfare schemes, advises on administering the Code and reviews State-level record-keeping; it must meet at least three times a year. Its notification was reported on 28-30 Oct 2026.

**Q4. (d)** — 1 and 2 are correct. Per the Ministry of Defence report, SSPL grows 4-inch SiC wafers that serve as the base for GaN HEMTs and X-band MMICs. Statement 3 is wrong: GaN (~3.4 eV) and SiC (~3.3 eV for 4H-SiC) are WIDE band-gap materials, versus ~1.12 eV for silicon, which is why they handle higher voltage, temperature and frequency.

**Q5. (c)** — 1 and 2 are correct. The visit was announced after a Modi-Carney call in mid-October, and both leaders aimed to finish trade talks by the G20 Leaders' Summit (Miami, 14-15 Dec). Statement 3 is wrong: the Quad comprises India, the US, Japan and Australia; Canada is a member of the G7 and the Five Eyes.

**Q6. (a)** — 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: Biodiversity Management Committees are set up by LOCAL bodies (panchayats, municipalities) at the local level; State Biodiversity Boards operate at the State level and the NBA at the national level. BMCs also help record People's Biodiversity Registers and have a role in access and benefit sharing.

**Q7. (a)** — A wide band gap (~3.3-3.4 eV against ~1.12 eV for Si) gives a higher critical breakdown field, low intrinsic carrier density at high temperature and high electron saturation velocity. This lets devices be smaller, hotter-running and faster at high power. Options (b) and (c) state the opposite of the physics; (d) is incorrect, since SiC and GaN wafers are small and expensive.

**Q8.** Mains question: no key; use the skeleton in B3.
