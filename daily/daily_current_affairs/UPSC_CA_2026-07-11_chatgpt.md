## Briefing

### 1. High-seas fisheries LoA programme launched at Bhubaneswar — Environment / Blue Economy

**Headline:** The Vice-President launched the national programme for issuing **Letters of Authorisation (LoAs)** for **sustainable high-seas fishing** on 9 July 2026, with LoAs handed to ten FPPOs/fisher groups and the Odisha Deep Sea Fishing Mission document also launched.

**Exam angle:** This is a clean blue-economy + ocean-governance item: Indian-flagged vessels can enter areas beyond India's EEZ only through a regulated, traceable, conservation-linked framework. Link it to **UNCLOS**, high seas, RFMOs, tuna resources, island territories and SDG-14.

**Key facts to memorize:**
- LoA is **vessel-specific, non-transferable** and integrated with the **ReALCraft** fishing-vessel registration portal.
- It flows from the **Guidelines for Sustainable Harnessing of Fisheries in the High Seas by Indian-Flagged Fishing Vessels, 2025**.
- LoA vessels must follow RFMO rules: catch limits, gear rules, bycatch mitigation, FAD management and voyage reporting.
- A&N Islands and Lakshadweep together account for nearly **49% of India's EEZ area**; deeper waters hold high-value tuna and tuna-like resources.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 Environment / Agriculture / Blue Economy.

**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS Environment "**Marine Ecosystem + UNCLOS/BBNJ**" and GS3 Agriculture "**Fisheries**" notes. *Update: add LoA = permit for Indian-flagged high-seas fishing; cross-link EEZ (200 nm), high seas, RFMOs and SDG-14.*

### 2. CPSEs must route all MSME invoice settlements through TReDS — Economy

**Headline:** The MSME Ministry mandated that **all CPSE purchases from MSMEs** be settled through **RBI-authorised TReDS platforms**, giving MSME suppliers faster, collateral-free invoice discounting options.

**Exam angle:** High-yield GS3 economy item on delayed MSME payments, working-capital stress and formal digital credit infrastructure. It also connects Budget 2026-27's MSME payment push to RBI-regulated receivables financing.

**Key facts to memorize:**
- **TReDS** = Trade Receivables Discounting System; an **RBI-regulated electronic platform**, operational since **2017**.
- Financing is **collateral-free** and **without recourse to the seller**; banks/NBFCs bid competitively to discount approved invoices.
- Five platforms are operational: **RXIL, M1xchange, Invoicemart, C2treds and DTX**.
- Invoice discounting rose from **₹40,000 crore in FY2021-22** to **₹3.47 lakh crore in FY2025-26**.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 Economy (MSMEs, financial inclusion, formal credit).

**Static anchor:** Mrunal "**Banking/Financial Markets + MSME**" pillar. *Update: add TReDS as invoice-discounting infrastructure; cross-link factoring, working capital, MSMED Act 2006 and CPSE procurement.*

### 3. Gaganyaan IMAT-05 qualifies main parachute under maximum load conditions — Science & Technology

**Headline:** ISRO successfully completed **IMAT-05**, the fifth Integrated Main Parachute Airdrop Test for Gaganyaan, qualifying the main parachute system under the maximum expected load conditions for the first uncrewed Gaganyaan G1 mission.

**Exam angle:** This is a genuinely new sub-development after the earlier SOLVE motor item: not another vehicle test, but a **crew-module recovery safety** milestone. It helps revise human spaceflight architecture: crew module, service module, re-entry, deceleration and recovery.

**Key facts to memorize:**
- A dummy mass with simulated single main parachute assembly was dropped from **2.5 km** using an **IAF IL-76** aircraft.
- Sequence: extractor parachute → drogue parachute to stabilize oscillations → main parachute to reach safe terminal speed.
- Joint effort of **ISRO, DRDO, Indian Air Force and Indian Army**.
- IMAT-05 builds confidence for **Gaganyaan G1**, the first uncrewed mission.

**Destination notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (Space technology).

**Static anchor:** S&T "**ISRO launch vehicles and human spaceflight**" table. *Update: add Gaganyaan recovery stack: drogue/main parachutes, crew-module re-entry and sea recovery; cross-link TV-D1 and abort/crew-safety tests.*

### 4. IP Australia gets access to India's CSIR-TKDL — International Relations / IPR

**Headline:** During the 3rd India-Australia Annual Summit in Melbourne, **CSIR and IP Australia** signed an access agreement for India's **Traditional Knowledge Digital Library (TKDL)**.

**Exam angle:** Use this as a focused new outcome from the Australia summit, not a repeat of the broader tour. It is a strong IR + Science/Tech + Art & Culture bridge: India exports an institutional model to prevent **biopiracy** and erroneous patents on traditional knowledge.

**Key facts to memorize:**
- **TKDL** was established in **2001** by CSIR with the Ministry of AYUSH.
- It contains over **5.2 lakh** formulations/practices from Ayurveda, Unani, Siddha, Sowa Rigpa and Yoga.
- It is translated into **five languages**: English, German, French, Japanese and Spanish.
- With IP Australia, **18 patent offices** now have access under NDAs; TKDL evidence has helped over **375** patent applications be revoked, rejected, amended, withdrawn or abandoned.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 International Relations; GS3 Science & Technology/IPR; GS1 Art & Culture.

**Static anchor:** Spectrum/Art & Culture "**Traditional Knowledge**" + GS3 "**IPR/TRIPS/Patents Act**". *Update: add TKDL = defensive prior-art database; cross-link neem/turmeric/basmati biopiracy examples and the 2024 WIPO genetic resources/TK treaty.*

### 5. Drugs Rules amended for high-alcohol medicinal formulations — Polity & Governance / Health

**Headline:** The Health Ministry amended the **Drugs Rules, 1945** so formulations containing **more than 12% v/v ethyl alcohol** in quantities exceeding **30 mL** no longer get the Schedule K licensing exemption; they move to **Schedule H1**.

**Exam angle:** A precise Prelims trap: this is not prohibition policy, but drug regulation under the Drugs and Cosmetics framework. It links public health, rational drug use, prescription control and Centre-state regulatory concerns over misuse of high-alcohol tinctures/aromatic preparations.

**Key facts to memorize:**
- Earlier Schedule K exemptions covered some preparations such as tinctures of cardamom/ginger; some contained **80-90% v/v** ethyl alcohol.
- New threshold: **>12% v/v ethyl alcohol** and **>30 mL** quantity.
- Such formulations now require licences under the **Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940**.
- **Schedule H1** means prescription-only sale plus stricter record-keeping.

**Destination notebook:** GS2 Governance / Health; GS3 Science & Technology (pharma regulation).

**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth/Governance "**Health regulation**" + pharma notes. *Update: add Schedule K exemption vs Schedule H1 prescription-control distinction; cross-link Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and DTAB/CDSCO basics.*

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS3 — Economy / Environment, 250 words, 15 marks)

**Q.** **Examine** how a regulated high-seas fisheries framework can advance India's blue economy while addressing ecological and governance risks.

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Context:** India has launched vessel-specific LoAs for Indian-flagged high-seas fishing; deeper waters beyond the usual 40-50 nautical mile fishing zone hold high-value tuna/tuna-like resources.
- **Economic gains:** Higher fisher incomes, FFPO/cooperative participation, marine exports, island-territory potential, diversification away from crowded near-shore fisheries.
- **Governance design:** ReALCraft-linked registration, non-transferable LoAs, voyage reporting, RFMO compliance, traceability and online service delivery.
- **Ecological safeguards:** Catch limits, gear rules, bycatch mitigation, FAD management, alignment with UNCLOS/SDG-14 and conservation of areas beyond national jurisdiction.
- **Caveats/way forward:** Avoid overcapacity, strengthen monitoring-control-surveillance, onboard small fishers equitably, invest in cold chains and data, and keep conservation rules credible.

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** The LoA is a mandatory, vessel-specific and non-transferable authorisation for Indian-flagged fishing vessels operating in the high seas, linked to ReALCraft and RFMO compliance.

**Q2 — (d).** TReDS is RBI-regulated; CPSE-MSME invoice settlements must now be routed through it; financing is collateral-free and without recourse to the MSME seller. All three statements are correct.

**Q3 — (b).** IMAT-05 qualified the main parachute system under maximum expected load conditions for the first uncrewed Gaganyaan G1 mission.

**Q4 — (b).** IP Australia gained access to India's CSIR-TKDL, a prior-art database built to prevent erroneous patents on documented Indian traditional knowledge.

**Q5 — (b).** Formulations containing more than 12% v/v ethyl alcohol in quantities exceeding 30 mL lose the Schedule K exemption and are included under Schedule H1.

**Q6 — (c).** All three are correct: territorial sea up to 12 nm, EEZ up to 200 nm, and high seas are beyond national jurisdiction.

**Q7 — (a).** TReDS converts approved receivables into faster working capital for MSMEs through competitive discounting by financiers such as banks and NBFCs.
