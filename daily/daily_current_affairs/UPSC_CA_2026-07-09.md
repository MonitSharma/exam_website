## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. India hosts the 13th AITIGA Joint Committee to advance the ASEAN–India trade-pact review — International Relations
**Exam angle:** The ASEAN–India Trade in Goods Agreement (AITIGA) is being reviewed for the *first time* since it took effect, to make it "more trade-facilitative and balanced" — a live Act East + trade-deficit story that maps onto both GS2 (IR) and GS3 (external sector). India runs a large goods trade deficit with ASEAN, which is the political driver behind the review.
**Key facts to memorize:**
- 13th AITIGA Joint Committee met at **Vanijya Bhawan, New Delhi (6–10 July 2026)**, in hybrid mode; the JC session was on **7 July**.
- Co-chaired by India (Additional Secretary, Dept. of Commerce, **Nitin Kumar Yadav**) and **Malaysia** (Deputy Secretary-General, Trade).
- AITIGA was **signed 2009, in force 2010**; all **10 ASEAN members** are parties. The review is targeted for conclusion in 2026.
- **India–ASEAN bilateral trade ≈ USD 128 billion in 2025–26.** Three of the eight sub-committees met — Customs & Trade Facilitation, National Treatment & Market Access, and **Rules of Origin** (the key deficit-plugging chapter).
**Notebook:** GS2 (IR) + GS3 (Economy/External Sector).
**Static anchor:** Vision/Mrunal *India–ASEAN & FTAs* note and **Mrunal Pillar 4 (BoP/Foreign Trade)** — add a cross-link: "AITIGA review ↔ why India stayed out of RCEP (2019) ↔ Rules of Origin misuse." Update your Act East Policy page with the 2026 review milestone.

### 2. PM-SETU rolled out nationwide; ₹1,237.58 crore cleared for ITI clusters — Polity & Governance (Skilling)
**Exam angle:** A flagship Centrally Sponsored **skilling** scheme that restructures Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) into "state-of-the-art" hubs — high-yield for GS2 (governance/CSS design) and GS3 (employability/human capital), and a clean scheme-fact for Prelims.
**Key facts to memorize:**
- **PM-SETU = Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs.**
- Cabinet cleared **nationwide rollout across all 200 identified ITI clusters**; approved **Strategic Investment Plans (SIPs) worth ₹1,237.58 crore** for clusters in **Odisha, Gujarat and Telangana**.
- Total scheme outlay **₹60,000 crore over 5 years** — Centre ₹30,000 cr, States ₹20,000 cr, **Industry ₹10,000 cr** (note the tri-partite financing); ~**1,000 ITIs** targeted; first Budget allocation ₹6,140.50 cr (2026-27). Industry anchors include Jindal, ArcelorMittal, Apollo MedSkills.
**Notebook:** GS2 (Governance/Schemes) + GS3 (Skilling/Employment).
**Static anchor:** **Mrunal Pillar 6 (HRD → Skilling)** and Skill India Mission notes — cross-link PM-SETU ↔ **DGT, NCVET, NEP-2020 vocationalisation, hub-and-spoke ITI model**.

### 3. India becomes the world's largest hub for Retail & FMCG Global Capability Centres — Reports & Indices / Economy
**Exam angle:** A McKinsey–FICCI report data-point that captures India's services-led, "office to the world" story (GCCs are captive offshore centres of MNCs). Good for a Prelims data MCQ and a Mains services-sector line.
**Key facts to memorize:**
- Per **McKinsey & Company–FICCI** report (8 July 2026): India is now the **world's largest Retail & FMCG GCC hub** with **~180 centres** and **over 2.72 lakh (272,000) professionals**.
- India's retail-GCC ecosystem is **~34% larger than the next five global peer markets combined**.
- Concentrated in Bengaluru, Mumbai, NCR, Pune, Hyderabad; spilling into tier-II/III (e.g., Mangaluru). Anchors: Lowe's, Tesco, Walmart Global Tech, Target, H&M, L'Oréal, AB InBev.
**Notebook:** GS3 (Economy — Services/Employment) + Reports & Indices.
**Static anchor:** **Mrunal Pillar 4A (Services/Invisibles in BoP)** and Economic Survey services chapter — cross-link GCC ↔ **IT-ITeS exports, SEZ/DESH Bill, "premiumisation" of India's services trade surplus**.

### 4. 'Sirsa Kinnow' gets a GI tag — Haryana's first fruit to be geo-tagged — Agriculture / IPR
**Exam angle:** A textbook Prelims **Geographical Indication** item (UPSC loves GI questions) with a neat Indo-Israel agri-diplomacy hook. Doubles as a "Places in News" (Sirsa, Haryana) and IPR fact.
**Key facts to memorize:**
- **Sirsa Kinnow** (a citrus/mandarin-orange hybrid) is **Haryana's first fruit to receive a GI tag** (granted 17 June 2026, widely reported early July).
- Grown on **~13,106 hectares** in Sirsa, producing **~1.82 lakh metric tonnes** annually; quality credited to the **Indo-Israel Centre of Excellence, Mangiana**.
- GI is a **collective/community right** for goods of a specific geographical origin — protects against imitation, lifts market value and export potential.
**Notebook:** GS3 (Agriculture) + GS1/Art&Culture (IPR/GI overlap).
**Static anchor:** **Geographical Indications of Goods (Registration & Protection) Act, 1999** page and your running GI-tags list — cross-link GI ↔ **TRIPS/WTO, GI Registry (Chennai), 10-year renewable registration**.

### 5. Defence Acquisition Council clears ~₹52,000 crore of acquisitions, air-defence heavy — Internal Security
**Exam angle:** The **DAC → Acceptance of Necessity (AoN)** is the first stage of India's capital-procurement pipeline; the air-defence and anti-drone tilt reflects post-2025 lessons. High-yield for GS3 (defence indigenisation, Atmanirbhar Bharat).
**Key facts to memorize:**
- On **3 July 2026**, the DAC (chaired by **Defence Minister Rajnath Singh**) accorded **AoN worth ~₹52,000 crore**.
- Army systems include the **AKASH TARANG** anti-UAV electronic-warfare system, **MPATGM** (Man-Portable Anti-Tank Guided Missile), **MRSAM**, **V-SHORADS** (Very Short-Range Air Defence), an Active Protection System for tanks, and a jet-based kamikaze drone — a clear **air-defence + counter-drone** thrust with an indigenous focus.
- **AoN = "Acceptance of Necessity"** — the *first* approval stage under the Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020, not a contract signed.
**Notebook:** GS3 (Internal Security / Defence Indigenisation).
**Static anchor:** **Defence Acquisition Procedure (DAP) 2020** — procurement categories with **Buy (Indian–IDDM)** as top priority; cross-link DAC ↔ **Atmanirbhar Bharat, positive indigenisation lists, DRDO/DPSUs**.

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS2 — IR / GS3 — Economy, 250 words, 15 marks)

**Q.** "The ongoing review of the ASEAN–India Trade in Goods Agreement (AITIGA) is as much about correcting a lopsided trade balance as about deepening India's Act East Policy." **Discuss.**

*Model-answer skeleton:*
- **Intro:** AITIGA (in force 2010) is the economic anchor of the Act East Policy; India–ASEAN trade ≈ USD 128 bn (2025-26) but with a persistent goods deficit — hence the first-ever review (13th Joint Committee, New Delhi, July 2026).
- **Why review (trade balance):** rising import surge, **Rules of Origin** misuse (third-country goods routed via ASEAN), non-tariff barriers, and limited market access for Indian goods/services.
- **Strategic dimension (Act East):** ASEAN centrality in the Indo-Pacific, supply-chain diversification ("China+1"), connectivity (IMT Highway), and countering economic over-dependence on China.
- **Way forward:** conclude the review with tighter RoO, digital/customs facilitation, services & investment chapters; balance protection of domestic MSMEs/agriculture with deeper integration. Conclude that a rebalanced AITIGA converts Act East from strategic rhetoric into economic substance.

## PART C — ANSWERS & EXPLANATIONS

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** AITIGA was signed 2009, entered into force 1 January 2010, and is under its first review; India hosted the 13th Joint Committee in New Delhi (July 2026); all 10 ASEAN states are parties.

**Q2 — (a) Upgradation of ITIs.** PM-SETU = Pradhan Mantri Skilling and Employability Transformation through Upgraded ITIs; the July 2026 approval covered 200 ITI clusters and ₹1,237.58 cr of Strategic Investment Plans.

**Q3 — (b) Retail and FMCG.** The McKinsey–FICCI report placed India as the largest Retail & FMCG GCC hub — ~180 centres, ~2.72 lakh professionals, ~34% larger than the next five peer markets combined.

**Q4 — (b) Citrus (mandarin-orange hybrid).** Kinnow is a high-yield citrus (mandarin) hybrid; Sirsa Kinnow is Haryana's first GI-tagged fruit (Indo-Israel Centre of Excellence, Mangiana).

**Q5 — (c) First-stage in-principle approval.** An AoN from the DAC is the initial "in-principle" clearance under DAP 2020; a contract, trials and production come later. The DAC is chaired by the Defence Minister.

**Q6 — (c) 2 and 3 only.** GI is a **collective** right of producers of a region (not an individual) — so (1) is wrong; registration is for 10 years, renewable (2 correct); the GI Registry is at Chennai (3 correct).

**Q7 — (c) Buy (Indian – IDDM).** Buy (Indian – Indigenously Designed, Developed and Manufactured) is the topmost priority category under DAP 2020, ahead of Buy (Indian), Buy & Make (Indian), etc.
