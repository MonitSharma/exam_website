## Briefing

### 1. First BIMSTEC Home Ministers' Meeting in New Delhi adopts the "Delhi Declaration" — International Relations
**Exam angle:** India's "Neighbourhood First" and Act East converge in BIMSTEC, and the new Home Ministers' track gives the grouping a security pillar (India is lead on security). It also tests the SAARC-vs-BIMSTEC comparison and Myanmar-conflict constraints.
**Key facts:** Held 13 Oct 2026, chaired by Amit Shah; seven members (Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand). Mechanism mandated at the 4th Summit (Kathmandu, 2018), welcomed at the 6th (Bangkok, Apr 2025). Declaration: condemns cross-border terrorism, backs UNSC 1267 sanctions, targets scam centres/"cyber slavery" and trafficking (victim-centred SOP), drug-offences monitoring desk, early finalisation of conventions on extradition and trafficking. Bangladesh sent a senior-secretary-led delegation, not its Home Minister. BIMSTEC: Bangkok Declaration 1997; Secretariat Dhaka; Charter in force 2024.
**Notebook:** GS2 IR (regional groupings) / GS3 Internal Security.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal IR pillar — BIMSTEC; Shankar/Laxmikanth not needed. *Nudge:* add a BIMSTEC "security pillar" box (JWG-CTTC, 2021 counter-terror convention) and cross-link to the SAARC comparison table.

### 2. PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana completes one year: Gaya, Nagaur and Umaria top the district ranking — Agriculture
**Exam angle:** Aspirational-district logic applied to agriculture; convergence-based governance monitored by a dashboard. Good for both Prelims facts and a Mains "cooperative federalism / convergence" argument.
**Key facts:** Launched 11 Oct 2025 (Cabinet approval July 2025); 100 districts chosen on low productivity, low cropping intensity and low credit; outlay ₹24,000 crore per year for six years; converges 36 Central schemes of 11 ministries; District Dhan-Dhaanya Samiti under the Collector; 121 indicators tracked with monthly rankings. Year-one ranks: Gaya (Bihar) 1st, Nagaur (Rajasthan) 2nd, Umaria (MP) 3rd; 18 more districts recognised across six objectives. Related: Khet Bachao Abhiyan (balanced fertiliser use) runs 22 Oct–30 Nov 2026.
**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture.
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh, Indian Economy — Agriculture chapter; Aspirational Districts Programme (NITI Aayog, 2018). *Nudge:* add a PMDDKY vs ADP vs Aspirational Blocks comparison row.

### 3. Delhi Police de-notifies Jantar Mantar as a protest site; Ramlila, Burari and CBD grounds named — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** Where, not whether, one may protest. Tests Art. 19(1)(b) and 19(3), the "public order" ground, and the proportionality line from earlier Supreme Court cases. The order follows protests over the Election Commission, and challenges are being reported.
**Key facts:** Standing Order of the Delhi Commissioner of Police dated 11 Oct 2026, citing a Union Home Ministry committee on alternate venues; new venues Ramlila Ground, Burari Ground and CBD Ground (East Delhi); a revised SOP "will be issued separately". Reports (secondary) say the Delhi HC has issued notice on the wider 10-day advance-notice rule (returnable 17 Nov). Static: Art. 19(1)(b) is for citizens, peaceful and unarmed; *Ram Manohar Lohia* (1966) – law and order / public order / security of State; *MKSS v. UoI* (2018) – protest rights balanced against residents' rights.
**Notebook:** GS2 Polity.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, Ch. 'Fundamental Rights' (Art. 19). *Nudge:* add a "public order concentric circles" diagram and cross-link to the Sept firecracker/SC items.

### 4. PFAS "forever chemicals" in Indian water: studies flag contamination, India has no drinking-water limit — Environment
**Exam angle:** An emerging-pollutant explainer: persistent, bioaccumulative, linked to Stockholm Convention listings. Prelims favours convention-listing and IARC-class facts; Mains favours the regulatory-gap argument.
**Key facts:** Chennai study (IIT-Madras, 2024): treated water carried 5–103% more of eight target PFAS than raw water; Visakhapatnam study (2026): 38 of 77 PFAS detected. IARC: PFOA Group 1, PFOS Group 2B. Stockholm Convention: PFOS (2009), PFOA (2019), PFHxS (2022). US EPA limit 4 ng/L for PFOA and PFOS (2024); the Indian standard IS 10500:2012 has no PFAS limit. (Figures from an aggregator summary of the studies; verify against the papers before citing.)
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (pollution).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, Environment — Pollution and International Conventions (Stockholm/POPs). *Nudge:* add PFAS to the POP list with listing years.

### 5. ICMR to set up its first High Altitude Medicine and Public Health Research Centre at Keylong — Science & Technology (Health)
**Exam angle:** Health research infrastructure for Himalayan and Armed Forces populations; ties to the high-altitude physiology and climate-sensitive disease themes.
**Key facts:** At Keylong (HQ of Lahaul and Spiti, Himachal Pradesh); foundation stone scheduled for 12 Oct 2026 with Union Health Minister JP Nadda (one source reports it as laid); built by ICMR with the Armed Forces Medical Services and the HP government; research areas include high-altitude physiology, mountain medicine, climate-sensitive diseases, maternal and child health, telemedicine and drone-based health logistics. ICMR: founded 1911 as the Indian Research Fund Association, renamed 1949.
**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Health).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum / health notes — Biomedical research institutions. *Nudge:* add ICMR centres to the "institutions" table.

### C. Mains question
**"The right to protest is integral to democracy, yet public order is a legitimate State concern. Critically examine how far designating protest venues reconciles the two." (15 marks, 250 words)**
Skeleton: (1) Context: Art. 19(1)(b) and 19(3); Jantar Mantar de-notification and the 11 Oct Standing Order. (2) Case for designated sites: traffic and residents' rights (*MKSS*, 2018), crowd management, predictable policing. (3) Case against: distance from seats of power dilutes expression, advance-notice rules and blanket orders invite arbitrariness, Section 163 BNSS misuse; proportionality test. (4) Way forward: transparent SOP, objective criteria for denial, judicial review, nearby accessible sites with amenities.

## Answers
1. **(c)** — All seven members attended (Bangladesh at senior-secretary level); Secretariat in Dhaka; BIMSTEC was formed by the 1997 Bangkok Declaration, not Delhi.
2. **(b)** — Criteria and Collector-led Samiti are correct; the outlay is ₹24,000 crore per year over six years, not one-time.
3. **(d)** — All three are reported in the Standing Order (a detailed SOP is yet to follow).
4. **(a)** — IARC: PFOA Group 1; Stockholm lists PFOS (2009), PFOA (2019), PFHxS (2022); IS 10500:2012 has no PFAS limit.
5. **(b)** — Keylong is the headquarters of Lahaul and Spiti, Himachal Pradesh.
6. **(c)** — Art. 19(1)(b) is for citizens, peaceful and unarmed; Art. 19(3) permits restrictions for sovereignty and integrity or public order; strike is not a fundamental right.
7. **(b)** — The programme is anchored by NITI Aayog, not the Rural Development Ministry.
