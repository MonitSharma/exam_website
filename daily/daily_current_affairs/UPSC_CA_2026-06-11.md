### 2. India's net FDI collapses even as gross FDI hits record high (2025–26)
**Exam angle (GS3 Economy):** The "FDI paradox" — a high-value data point straight off the external-sector syllabus. Headline gross inflows are at a record, but **net FDI** (what India actually retains) shrank sharply because of record **repatriation/disinvestment** by foreign investors plus surging **outward FDI** by Indian firms.
**Memorize:** Gross FDI 2025–26 ≈ **$94.5 bn** (record, ~+17% YoY). **Net FDI** fell to a low single-digit figure (~$7.6 bn full-year range per RBI). **Repatriation ≈ $53.6 bn** — highest in at least 3 years. Net FDI = Gross inflows − repatriation − outward FDI. FDI is booked in the **Financial/Capital account** of BoP (not current account).
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal Pillar **3 (BoP / External Sector)**. *Update the FDI vs FPI page; cross-link "repatriation" and "outward FDI" as drivers of the net-vs-gross gap.*

### Mains-Style Question (GS3 — Economy)

> **Q.** India's gross FDI inflows are at record highs even as net FDI dwindles to a fraction of that figure. **Critically examine** the factors driving this divergence and its implications for India's external sector. *(150 words / 10 marks)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Define net FDI = gross inflows − repatriation/disinvestment − outward FDI; flag the 2025–26 paradox (record gross ≈ $94.5 bn, but net low single digits; repatriation ≈ $53.6 bn).
- **Why the gap (critical):** (i) Record **repatriation** — maturing investments, profit-booking, exits via IPOs/secondary sales; (ii) surging **outward FDI** by Indian firms (globalising, supply-chain diversification); (iii) a sign of a **maturing market** with easy exits — not purely negative.
- **Implications:** Pressure on the **capital account** and rupee; lower retained capital, tech transfer and job creation; but easy exits can also **attract** future investors.
- **Way forward:** Stable & predictable policy, ease of doing business, dispute resolution, deepening manufacturing (PLI) to convert gross interest into retained, productive capital.
- **Conclusion:** Track **net**, not just headline gross; the divergence is a quality-of-capital signal, not just a quantity one.

## PART C — ANSWERS

**Q1 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** India is an **invitee**, not a member (St.1 wrong). France hosts the 2026 summit at Évian (St.2 right); the EU participates in G7 summits (St.3 right).

**Q2 — (b).** The FCNR(B) swap and the twin USD–INR swap facilities are **RBI** tools to attract forex inflows and support external financing (ECB/OFCB).

**Q3 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Record gross inflows (right) and a sharp net fall from repatriation + outward FDI (right) are correct; FDI is recorded under the **Financial/Capital account**, not the current account (St.3 wrong).

**Q4 — (c) ICIMOD.** The Kathmandu-based International Centre for Integrated Mountain Development issued the HKH Monsoon Outlook 2026 on 11 Jun 2026.

**Q5 — (b).** The Zojila Tunnel links **Sonmarg (Kashmir Valley)** with the **Dras/Minamarg (Ladakh)** side across Zoji La on NH-1.

**Q6 — (b) Only two.** Zoji La (J&K/Ladakh) ✓ and Nathu La (Sikkim) ✓ are correct; **Bomdi La is in Arunachal Pradesh**, not Himachal Pradesh ✗.

**Q7 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** Remittances (private transfers) and trade in goods & services are **current account** items; **FDI is a capital/financial account** item.
