## PART A — BRIEFING

### 1. India–Myanmar hold maiden tri-services HADR exercise "Rahat Mitra" in Chennai (5–6 Nov) — International Relations
**Exam angle:** First bilateral tri-services humanitarian-assistance and disaster-relief drill with Myanmar, coordinated by the Indian Navy; officials tied it to "Neighbourhood First" and the MAHASAGAR vision. HADR is a soft-power tool and an Indian Ocean Region (IOR) "first responder" credential (cf. Operation Brahma, Myanmar quake 2025). Mains line: *interoperability and trust are built in peacetime drills, not during the crisis.*
**Memorize:** 2 days; 300+ personnel (Army, Navy, IAF); theme "Together for Humanity, Stronger through Partnership"; Army showed dewatering and bridge-laying.
**Notebook:** GS2 IR (Neighbourhood; Myanmar).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal/Spectrum – India's neighbourhood policy & IOR. Add a cross-link: Myanmar → Act East, Kaladan project, BIMSTEC (see 3 Nov BIMSTEC maritime exercise).

### 2. Global Innovation Index 2026: India 38th of 139 economies — Reports & Indices
**Exam angle:** India tops the lower-middle-income group and Central & Southern Asia (Kazakhstan 73rd, Bhutan 74th). Output rank (25th) well above input rank (49th) — an "innovation overperformer". Leads in ICT services exports. Theme: science-powered entrepreneurship / deep-science startups. Mains hook: input–output gap shows weak R&D spending and institutions.
**Memorize:** Published by WIPO with the Portulans Institute; 139 economies; top 5 Switzerland, Sweden, US, Republic of Korea, Singapore. Release listed as 29 Sep 2026 (flagged: date from the WIPO page's citation metadata).
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy / Sci-Tech (innovation ecosystem).
**Static anchor:** Reports & Indices sheet (publisher → report → India rank). Add GII 2026 row; cross-link to Anusandhan National Research Foundation and RDI Scheme.

### 3. RBI's FLA Census 2025-26 (provisional, 6 Nov): inward FDI-to-ODI ratio falls to 4.2x — Economy
**Exam angle:** India's outward investment is growing faster than inward stock — a sign of Indian firms going global but also of tepid foreign inflows. Source-country concentration persists.
**Memorize:** Reference date end-March 2026. Inward FDI stock ₹70.71 lakh crore (+8.5%); ODI ₹16.71 lakh crore (+~40.8%); ratio 4.2x (from 5.5x). Top sources: US 16.5%, Mauritius 14.5%, Singapore 13.7%, UK 10.8%. Manufacturing 50.1% of FDI equity, services ~45%. 43,123 reporting entities, ~97% unlisted. Top ODI destinations: Singapore, UAE, US.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (External sector).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal/Shankar IAS – FDI vs FPI, FEMA, BoP. Add the FLA census to the "RBI surveys/publications" list; cross-link the DTAA/Mauritius route story.

### 4. INCOIS marine-heatwave bulletins and Indian Ocean warming — Environment / Geography
**Exam angle:** INCOIS issues Marine Heatwave (MHW) Watch/Alert/Warning bulletins; a 9 Nov current-affairs digest reports an alert over the Arabian Sea, Bay of Bengal and Indian Ocean with SST up to ~1°C above normal (flagged: secondary source; I could not open the INCOIS bulletin for this week). Impacts: coral bleaching, shifts in tuna migration, lower productivity, fisher livelihoods.
**Memorize:** MHW = SST above the 90th percentile of local climatology for ≥5 consecutive days. INCOIS grades: Watch (up to 0.5°C above threshold), Alert (0.5–1°C), Warning (>1°C). INCOIS is under MoES, Hyderabad.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment / GS1 Geography (oceanography).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum/NCERT – ocean temperature, El Niño/IOD; Shankar IAS – coral reefs. Cross-link to the Tropical Cyclone Arnab note (29 Oct) and El Niño.

### 5. Vayo Samman Rashtriya Abhiyan launched (4 Nov) — Society / Schemes
**Exam angle:** A national intergenerational-bonding campaign under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY), launched by Union Minister Virendra Kumar (Social Justice & Empowerment). Aims to reach every village/mohalla via Panchayats, local bodies and educational institutions. Context: 60+ population projected to rise from ~10 crore (2011) to ~23 crore (2036) — ageing is a Mains staple.
**Memorize:** AVYAY umbrella; 5C model (Connect, Converse, Co-create, Contribute, Continue) and "Vayo SAATHI" volunteer initiative are from a secondary digest (flagged, not on the PIB-style page I fetched).
**Notebook:** GS1 Society / GS2 Social Justice.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth/Ramesh Singh – welfare of vulnerable sections; MWPSC Act 2007, Article 41. Cross-link to Census 2027 and demographic dividend notes.

### B3. Mains question
**"India's demographic transition towards an ageing population demands a shift from welfare to a rights-and-inclusion approach. Discuss." (15 marks, 250 words)**
Skeleton: (i) Intro: 60+ population ~10 crore (2011) → ~23 crore (2036); feminisation and rural ageing. (ii) Existing framework: Article 41, MWPSC Act 2007, NPSrC, AVYAY, Vayo Samman Abhiyan. (iii) Gaps: pension coverage, geriatric healthcare, loneliness, enforcement of the 2007 Act. (iv) Way forward: universal social pension, community care, intergenerational programmes, silver economy; conclude with dignity as a constitutional value (Article 21).

## PART C — ANSWERS
1. (a) Statements 1 and 2 are reported facts; the drill is bilateral, not ADMM-Plus.
2. (a) 38th of 139 and lead among lower-middle-income economies; GII is by WIPO/Portulans.
3. (b) Manufacturing (50.1%) is the largest share, not services (~45%).
4. (a) Standard SST-percentile, 5-day definition; INCOIS grades Watch/Alert/Warning.
5. (a) It sits under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, not Health.
6. (b) MAHASAGAR frames cooperative maritime security, growth and HADR.
7. (b) 10% threshold under FEMA norms; FPI is SEBI-regulated and more volatile.
