## Briefing

### 1. 'Ethanol ATMs' — Centre readies a policy to sell ethanol as a household/commercial cooking fuel — Science & Technology (Energy)

**Exam angle:** The Ministry of Petroleum & Natural Gas is drafting a policy (target timeline ~September 2026) to dispense ethanol in sealed canisters from kiosks ("ethanol ATMs") at fuel retail outlets, paired with purpose-built ethanol stoves. The aim is to absorb India's surplus ethanol and cut dependence on imported LPG — a fresh downstream use for the ethanol programme beyond petrol blending. This is a Sci-Tech + energy-security + import-substitution story, distinct from the E20 blending milestone (which is static now).

**Facts to memorise:** India's ethanol production capacity has crossed **20 billion litres/year**, projected to reach **~24 billion litres by March 2027**. Ethanol = alcohol biofuel from sugarcane/maize/biomass. LPG = propane + butane mix. First phase likely targets **commercial kitchens, restaurants, street-food vendors** (bulk users); ethanol is positioned as a *supplement* to LPG, not a full replacement.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (Energy) / Sci-Tech.
**Static anchor:** *Mrunal Economy — Energy & Biofuels pillar* / *Shankar IAS — Renewable energy*. Nudge: cross-link this to your "Ethanol Blending Programme (EBP), E20, National Policy on Biofuels 2018 (amended 2022)" note — add a new sub-head "Ethanol beyond petrol: cooking-fuel use, ethanol ATMs."

### 2. China conducts a live-fire naval drill inside Japan-claimed EEZ near Okinotorishima — International Relations

**Exam angle:** On 20 July 2026 a Chinese **Luyang III-class** guided-missile destroyer fired live rounds ~180 km southwest of **Okinotorishima**, inside waters Japan claims as its EEZ; a Russian frigate was also present. Japan lodged a diplomatic protest on 21 July and called it the first publicly detected foreign live-fire drill inside its claimed EEZ. The exam gold here is the **UNCLOS "island vs rock" debate** and the legality of military exercises in another state's EEZ — a recurring Prelims/Mains theme via UNCLOS.

**Facts to memorise:** An **EEZ extends up to 200 nautical miles** from the baseline (coastal state has sovereign rights over resources; others keep freedom of navigation/overflight). **Article 121 of UNCLOS** governs the "regime of islands" — distinguishing islands (generate EEZ + continental shelf) from **rocks that cannot sustain human habitation or economic life** (no EEZ/continental shelf). China argues Okinotorishima is a submerged coral atoll = a "rock"; Japan treats it as its **southernmost territory** capable of generating an EEZ. Okinotorishima lies in the **Philippine Sea**.

**Notebook:** GS2 — IR (also GS1 Geography "Places in News").
**Static anchor:** *Shankar/DD geography — Law of the Sea (UNCLOS maritime zones)*. Nudge: add Okinotorishima to your "Places in News (Pacific)" map card alongside Senkaku/Diaoyu and the nine-dash-line disputes.

### 3. Five new *Allographa* lichen species discovered in the Western Ghats — Environment

**Exam angle:** Scientists at the **MACS–Agharkar Research Institute (ARI), Pune** (autonomous body under the **Department of Science & Technology**) described five new lichen-forming fungi of genus *Allographa* from the Western Ghats — the first comprehensive molecular-phylogenetic study of the genus in India. Species-in-news + "lichens as bioindicators" + Western Ghats biodiversity is a high-frequency Environment combo.

**Facts to memorise:** The five species — *A. keralensis, A. nayakae, A. paraconsanguinea, A. persistinspersa, A. semicarbonisata* (*nayakae* honours lichenologist Dr. Sanjeeva Nayaka). **Lichen = symbiotic association of a fungus + a photosynthetic partner (alga/cyanobacterium)**; used as **bioindicators of air pollution** (sensitive to SO₂) and as **pioneers in ecological succession** on bare rock. Study used multi-locus DNA markers (**mtSSU, nrLSU, RPB2**). Western Ghats = **UNESCO World Heritage Site** and one of the world's biodiversity hotspots.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment & Biodiversity.
**Static anchor:** *Shankar IAS Environment — Biodiversity / Ecological Succession & Bioindicators*. Nudge: add these to your running "New species 2026" list and cross-link to the "lichens = pioneer species + pollution indicator" flashcard (PYQ 2014).

This operationalises the post-Agnipath rehabilitation promise — an Internal Security + governance angle.

Ex-Agniveers are **exempt from the Physical Standards Test, Physical Efficiency Test and written exam** for these posts. **Agnipath scheme (2022)**: four-year tenure in Army/Navy/Air Force; a share retained afterward. **CAPFs (under MHA) = CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB** (+ Assam Rifles, which does border + internal-security duty). No appointments made yet as the first Agniveer batch hadn't completed its tenure.

### C. Mains-Style Question

**Q.** "India's ethanol programme has evolved from a fuel-blending measure into a broader energy-security and rural-economy strategy." Examine this statement in the light of recent policy moves such as 'ethanol ATMs' for cooking fuel. *(GS-III, 250 words, 15 marks)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Frame ethanol as a renewable biofuel; note the shift from the Ethanol Blended Petrol (EBP) programme (E20 achieved) to newer downstream uses.
- **Energy security:** Cuts crude-oil and LPG imports; utilises >20 bn-litre surplus capacity (→24 bn by March 2027); diversifies the cooking-fuel basket via ethanol ATMs/stoves.
- **Rural economy & farmers:** Sugarcane/maize/grain and 2G lignocellulosic feedstock raise farm incomes; supports "Annadata to Urjadata."
- **Challenges/way forward:** Food-vs-fuel and water use of sugarcane, need for 2G/compressed-biogas balance, distribution & safety of ethanol canisters, price competitiveness vs subsidised LPG; conclude with a balanced, sustainability-first view.

## Answers

**Q1 — (a).** Ethanol ATMs (MoP&NG) dispense ethanol as cooking fuel; capacity has crossed 20 bn litres. Statement 3 is wrong — ethanol supplements, not replaces, LPG.
**Q2 — (b) Japan.** Okinotorishima is Japan's southernmost territory (Philippine Sea); China disputes its EEZ-generating status under UNCLOS Art. 121.
**Q3 — (a).** 50% CAPF Constable(GD)/Rifleman posts reserved for ex-Agniveers; exempt from written + physical tests. Statement 3 is wrong — CAPFs are under the **Ministry of Home Affairs**, not Defence.
**Q4 — (c) NITI Aayog.** IFI 2026 is NITI Aayog's first edition; Gujarat topped with 56.6.
**Q5 — (b) Lichen-forming fungi.** *Allographa* is a genus of lichen-forming fungi, found in the Western Ghats.
**Q6 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** All three are correct — symbiosis, pioneer succession, and SO₂ bioindication.
**Q7 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** EEZ = 200 nm; a "rock" gets no EEZ/continental shelf (Art. 121); freedom of navigation/overflight survives in the EEZ.
