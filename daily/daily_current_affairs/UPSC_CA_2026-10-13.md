## Briefing

### 1. Retail inflation hits a 23-month high of 5.54% in September — Economy
**Exam angle:** Food and fuel are driving a firmer price cycle just five days after the RBI's repo hike to 5.50%. It tests CPI composition, the 4% (±2) target band, and the monetary-policy link. Note the new-series versus back-series caveat in comparisons.
**Key facts:** CPI 5.54% (Aug revised 4.83%); food (CFPI) 7.74% vs 5.97%; onion inflation 108.09%; rural 6.08%, urban 4.88%; core 4.6%; Tamil Nadu highest state at 6.81%. RBI sees ~6% in Oct–Dec and 5.2% average for FY27; another hike is widely expected at the 2–4 Dec MPC.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy.
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh, Indian Economy — Inflation & Monetary Policy chapter. *Nudge:* add a "CPI vs WPI vs core" table and a Sept-2026 data row; cross-link to the MPC note.

### 2. IPCC meets in Addis Ababa, split over when the Seventh Assessment Report should land — Environment
**Exam angle:** Climate-science governance. Many European states want AR7 approved before the 2028 global stocktake; Saudi Arabia, China and India are among those seeking 2029 so developing countries get time to review. Deadlock was already visible at IPCC-64 (Bangkok, March 2026). Reports indicate India also withdrew its offer to host COP33 (2028) — single-source report, treat with caution.
**Key facts:** IPCC established 1988 (WMO + UNEP); HQ Geneva; chair Jim Skea; the US reportedly left the body earlier this year; the plenary was under way this week and the outcome was not yet reported at the time of writing.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (climate governance) / GS2 IR.
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS, Environment — Climate Change chapter (IPCC, UNFCCC, Paris stocktake). *Nudge:* add an "AR7 timeline dispute" box and cross-link to India's CBDR-RC stand.

### 3. Pakistan declares emergency in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and suspends the PTI-led government — International Relations (Neighbourhood)
**Exam angle:** Civil–military and federal–provincial tensions next door, with spillover on terrorism and the Afghanistan frontier. India-relevant for border security and the Pakistan policy debate; it also compares neatly with Article 356 and state emergencies at home.
**Key facts:** Announced 11 Oct 2026 on federal cabinet recommendation, approved by President Zardari; initial six months; Governor to run the province with an advisory council; CM Sohail Afridi reported arrested; PTI called it "a coup disguised as emergency". The trigger was PTI's planned Islamabad march demanding Imran Khan's release.
**Notebook:** GS2 IR (India and neighbours).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal IR pillar — Pakistan; Laxmikanth, Ch. 'President's Rule' for the Indian comparison. *Nudge:* add a one-line "KP = borders Afghanistan, Durand Line" map note.

### 4. TRISHNA: ISRO says the Indo-French thermal-infrared satellite is in the advanced stage of realisation — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** A bilateral space mission for water stress, crop health and heat mapping, and the third joint India–France satellite after Megha-Tropiques and SARAL.
**Key facts:** CNES supplies the thermal-infrared (TIR) payload; ISRO the VNIR-SWIR payload and bus; launch on PSLV expected in 12–16 months (Wikipedia lists a target of Oct 2027); orbit is sun-synchronous at ~761 km and land resolution ~57 m (secondary source; the revisit figure is inconsistent across sources).
**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Space).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum / ISRO notes — Earth-observation missions. *Nudge:* add TRISHNA to the India–France cooperation table.

### 5. Lakshadweep declared 100% literate under ULLAS — Society
**Exam angle:** The ULLAS (Nav Bharat Saaksharta Karyakram) model: "fully literate" means identified adult non-literates passing the FLNAT, not Census literacy.
**Key facts:** Declared 11 Oct 2026 at Kavaratti by Administrator Praful Patel; 1,113 adult non-literates identified in the initial survey; ULLAS targets those aged 15+, a centrally sponsored scheme. Census definition: age 7+, reads and writes with understanding.
**Notebook:** GS1 Society / GS2 Education.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, Ch. 'Education' (Art. 21A, 45); NCERT Class 12 Geography on population and literacy. *Nudge:* add a ULLAS vs Census literacy comparison row.

### C. Mains question
**"Rising food prices are testing the credibility of India's inflation-targeting framework. Critically examine the supply-side and monetary measures available." (15 marks, 250 words)**
Skeleton: (1) Context: CPI 5.54%, food 7.74%, onion spike, RBI hiking to 5.50%. (2) Why rate hikes are blunt against supply shocks; the case for looking through food prices versus the risk of de-anchoring expectations. (3) Supply-side tools: buffer stocks, calibrated export/stock limits, mandi and storage reform, the Pulses Mission, Operation Greens. (4) Way forward: better food-price forecasting, fuel-pass-through management, coordination between Centre and RBI; conclude with a balanced view.
