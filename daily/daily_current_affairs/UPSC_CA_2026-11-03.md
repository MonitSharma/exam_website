## Briefing

### 1. First BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise at Sri Vijaya Puram, Andaman & Nicobar (from 30 Oct 2026) — International Relations
**Exam angle:** The Andaman and Nicobar Command (ANC) is hosting the first-ever BIMSTEC maritime security exercise. It has a harbour phase for planning and exchanges, then a sea phase for interoperability. Aims: maritime domain awareness, coordinated responses to disaster relief, cross-border maritime crime and oil or hazardous-material spills. For the exam it shows BIMSTEC moving from trade talk to security practice, and India positioning the Andamans as its eastern gateway.
**Memorize:**
- BIMSTEC has seven members: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand. Secretariat: Dhaka. Bhutan and Nepal are landlocked but depend on the Bay of Bengal.
- Sri Vijaya Puram = renamed Port Blair.
- *Source conflict:* one report gives 30 Oct–2 Nov with ships from Bangladesh, Myanmar, Sri Lanka and Thailand and observers from Bhutan and Nepal; another gives 30 Oct–3 Nov and says all seven members took part. Quote only "first edition, hosted by ANC, from 30 Oct".
- The exercise was reportedly announced after the 6th BIMSTEC Summit (Bangkok, April 2025); not verified from a primary source.

**Notebook:** GS2 International Relations (Neighbourhood / Bay of Bengal).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS *International Relations* — BIMSTEC; Mrunal *India's neighbourhood* pillar. Update: add a "BIMSTEC security track" line (exercise 2026) and cross-link to SAGAR/MAHASAGAR, the Act East Policy and the Andaman and Nicobar Command.

### 2. Defence Ministry signs ₹53,866.50 crore contracts for eight Next Generation Corvettes (2 Nov) — Internal Security
**Exam angle:** Contracts were signed in New Delhi on 2 Nov 2026 in the presence of the Defence Secretary. Garden Reach Shipbuilders & Engineers (GRSE) will build five and Goa Shipyard Limited (GSL) three. The ships are Navy-designed and meant for trade-route protection, anti-surface and anti-submarine warfare, coastal defence and littoral operations. The Ministry also stresses indigenous content, private shipyards and MSMEs. The Ministry's estimate is about 72 lakh person-days of direct shipyard employment and 460 lakh in ancillary industry over ten years. A good Mains case on Atmanirbharta in defence and the shipbuilding ecosystem.
**Memorize:**
- Value ₹53,866.50 crore; GRSE 5 + GSL 3 = 8 ships; both builders are defence public sector undertakings.
- Role: corvette = small warship for coastal and littoral work (smaller than a frigate or destroyer).
- Delivery timeline: not stated in the report I read.

**Notebook:** GS3 Internal Security (Defence production).
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh / Mrunal *Defence production and procurement*. Update: add "DPSU-built Next Generation Corvettes (2026)" to the Navy modernisation list and cross-link to the 28 Oct shipbuilding/container-scheme notes and the Defence Acquisition Procedure.

### 3. Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 and the 9th ISA Assembly open in New Delhi (2–5 Nov) — Environment
**Exam angle:** MNRE is hosting the Summit at Bharat Mandapam, held alongside the 9th International Solar Alliance (ISA) Assembly and the 4th International Conference on Green Hydrogen. Theme: "Invest. Innovate. Inspire." The Ministry reports about 308 GW of installed non-fossil capacity against the 500 GW target for 2030, and hopes to attract about $220 billion over 2027–30. It is a good hook for India's NDC and for the shift from capacity to storage, financing and manufacturing.
**Memorize:**
- 130+ countries, 400+ exhibitors; Country Partners include Australia, Denmark, the EU, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden and the UK.
- India: 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030 (Panchamrit, COP26); roughly 308 GW installed (MNRE, reported).
- ISA: launched by India and France at COP21, Paris (2015); headquartered in Gurugram.
- Not verified: what the PM announced in his inaugural speech.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (Climate / Renewables).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS *Environment* — Climate change & India's commitments; Mrunal *Energy* pillar. Update: refresh the "installed non-fossil capacity" figure and cross-link to the National Green Hydrogen Mission and ISA.

### 4. Census 2027: enumerator training stresses the caste and Aadhaar questions — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle:** Trainers are preparing about 34 lakh enumerators for India's first digital census, run on a mobile app. Reports say the population-enumeration questionnaire adds 13 new questions (including Aadhaar, parents' birth details, bank accounts and digital literacy). Caste is recorded for all respondents. Non-SC/ST respondents write in their caste, and there is no separate OBC column. Officials have clarified that giving the Aadhaar number is optional and that Aadhaar is not proof of citizenship. This is the first all-caste count in a Census since 1931 (SECC 2011 was a separate exercise). Tie to welfare targeting, OBC sub-categorisation and the Mains debate on data versus social divisions.
**Memorize:**
- Census Act, 1948; conducted by the Registrar General and Census Commissioner under the MHA; "Census" is Entry 69 of the Union List.
- Two phases: houselisting, then population enumeration.
- Reported figures (The Hindu digest, 30 Oct): 34 lakh enumerators, 13 new questions, blocks of 750–800 people, 20–25 minutes per interview.
- *Source conflict:* one report says phase 2 starts 1 Dec in poll-bound states and Feb 2027 elsewhere; another gives 17 Aug for snow-bound areas and Feb 2027 for the rest. Do not memorize a date until the Registrar General's notification is checked.

**Notebook:** GS2 Governance / GS1 Society.
**Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, *Union List and Seventh Schedule*; Vision/Shankar notes on Census. Update: add a "Census 2027 firsts" box (digital, all-caste) and cross-link to SECC 2011 and delimitation.

### 5. SEBI lengthens KYC review cycle for eligible FPIs from 3 to 8 years, effective 1 Dec 2026 — Economy
**Exam angle:** A SEBI circular on "rationalisation of periodicity of KYC review for FPIs" stretches the review for eligible Foreign Portfolio Investors from 3 years to 8 years. Specified higher-risk FPIs move from annual to 2-yearly review. It is an ease-of-doing-business signal for foreign capital inflows at a time of falling forex reserves (2 Nov briefing).
**Memorize:**
- Effective 1 Dec 2026; low-risk Category I and regulated Category II FPIs from non-high-risk jurisdictions get 8-year reviews (reported).
- SEBI FPI Regulations, 2019 recognise only Category I and Category II FPIs.
- FPI = portfolio flows without control, unlike FDI (10% single-FPI cap on a company's paid-up equity; above that, it is treated as FDI).

**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Capital markets / External sector).
**Static anchor:** Mrunal *Capital Markets* and *BoP* pillars; Ramesh Singh, FDI vs FPI. Update: add this to the FPI regulatory timeline and cross-link to hot money and the Impossible Trinity.

### B3. Mains question
**Q8.** *Critically examine* the significance and challenges of enumerating caste for all communities in Census 2027. (15 marks, 250 words)
**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. Intro: Census 2027 is the first all-caste count since 1931, run digitally under the Census Act, 1948 by the Union (Entry 69).
2. Significance: evidence for reservation sub-categorisation (OBC commission debates), better welfare targeting, replacing outdated 1931 estimates and the SECC 2011 data-quality problems.
3. Challenges: write-in responses create classification and synonym problems; mandatory field and privacy/data-security concerns in an app-based census; risk of social polarisation and political mobilisation; no separate OBC column makes later analysis harder; enumerator training and the Aadhaar clarification show implementation friction.
4. Way forward: transparent coding of castes, independent expert review, strict data protection, public communication, and use of data for policy rather than politicisation.

## Answers
- **Q1 — (c).** 1 and 2 are correct. 3 is wrong: the BIMSTEC Secretariat is in Dhaka.
- **Q2 — (d).** 1 and 2 are correct. 3 is wrong: GRSE and GSL are defence public sector undertakings.
- **Q3 — (b).** 1 and 2 are correct. 3 is wrong: about 308 GW is installed against the 500 GW target for 2030 (MNRE, reported).
- **Q4 — (a).** 1 and 2 are correct (digital mode as reported). 3 is wrong: Aadhaar is optional and not proof of citizenship.
- **Q5 — (d).** All three are correct: effective 1 Dec 2026; 3 to 8 years for eligible FPIs; Category I and II only under the 2019 Regulations.
- **Q6 — (b).** The Ten Degree Channel (10°N) separates the Andamans from the Nicobars. Duncan Passage lies between South Andaman and Little Andaman; the Great Channel lies south of Great Nicobar, towards Sumatra.
- **Q7 — (d).** All three are correct: Entry 69 of the Union List, the Census Act, 1948, and the first synchronous Census in 1881 (the first, non-synchronous one was in 1872).
