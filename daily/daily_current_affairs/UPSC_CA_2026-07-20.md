# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 2026-07-20

## Briefing

### 1. Core Industries index rebased to 2022-23; iron ore becomes the 9th core industry — Economy / Reports & Indices
- **Exam angle:** A clean Prelims update for industrial statistics: the Office of Economic Adviser, DPIIT released the first revised ICI series with **base year 2022-23**, replacing 2011-12.
- **Key facts:** ICI now covers **9 industries** after adding **iron ore**; June 2026 ICI grew **5.0% YoY**, up from May's 3.2%. Weights to remember: Electricity **30.932**, Refinery Products **22.572**, Steel **17.584**, Crude Oil **7.430**, Coal **5.596**, Iron Ore **4.905**, Cement **4.410**, Natural Gas **3.841**, Fertilizers **2.731**.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy — industrial growth indicators.
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Mrunal Pillar 2/3 (National Income + IIP/industrial indices) — update the "8 core industries" table to "9 core industries" and cross-link IIP base-year revision.
- **Source:** [PIB, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2286615).

### 2. Draft Advocates (Amendment) Bill, 2026 opened for public consultation — Polity & Governance
- **Exam angle:** Legal profession regulation is a recurring UPSC theme because it links statutory bodies, judicial administration, legal education and access to justice.
- **Key facts:** The draft was prepared in consultation with the **Bar Council of India (BCI)**; BCI hosted it on **18 July 2026**; comments are invited until **31 July 2026, 3:00 PM**.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Polity/Governance — judiciary and statutory regulation.
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Laxmikanth (Judiciary + statutory bodies) — add a note under the Advocates Act, 1961: BCI regulates professional conduct and legal education; track final Bill provisions only after official text is enacted/introduced.
- **Source:** [PIB, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2286679).

### 3. India has 11 pumped-storage projects under construction, totalling 15,870 MW — Science & Technology / Environment
- **Exam angle:** PSPs are the long-duration storage backbone for high-renewables grids; UPSC already tested the concept in Prelims 2024.
- **Key facts:** **11 PSPs / 15,870 MW** are under construction; **6 PSPs / 8,140 MW** have CEA concurrence/appraisal but are yet to begin construction. CEA has reduced DPR concurrence timeline from **90 days to 50 days**; off-stream closed-loop PSPs get lighter clearance treatment in specified cases.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology + Environment — energy storage and grid stability.
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Shankar IAS climate/energy transition + Geography hydropower — add PSPs beside batteries, green hydrogen and grid balancing.
- **Source:** [PIB, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2286558).

### 4. NAKSHA pilot clarification: GIS mapping is not land acquisition — Geography / Governance
- **Exam angle:** Urban land records are a governance bottleneck: cadastral mapping, property-title certainty, town planning, dispute reduction and digital land administration.
- **Key facts:** **NAKSHA = National Geospatial Knowledge-based Land Survey of Urban Habitations**; it runs under **DILRMP** and maps urban land parcels through GIS. The Goa clarification says existing titles/boundaries remain protected; the survey is not land acquisition or a property-tax hike mechanism.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance + GS1 Geography — land records and urban planning.
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Governance notes on Digital India Land Records Modernization Programme — add NAKSHA as the urban GIS layer; cross-link cadastral maps and land-titling reforms.
- **Source:** [PIB, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2286590).

### 5. NMCM/Mera Gaon Meri Dharohar maps village-level cultural profiles, not artist registrations — Art & Culture / Society
- **Exam angle:** Useful for separating documentation, welfare and tourism claims. UPSC often turns such scheme details into statement traps.
- **Key facts:** The **National Mission on Cultural Mapping (NMCM)** documents village-level cultural heritage through **Mera Gaon Meri Dharohar (MGMD)**. It does **not** maintain category-wise registered artist data, does **not** register artists, and does **not** provide financial assistance, insurance or social-security support under NMCM.
- **Notebook:** GS1 Art & Culture + GS2 Governance.
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** NCERT Fine Arts + Culture ministry schemes — add MGMD under "documentation/digital cultural mapping", not under direct artist-welfare schemes.
- **Source:** [PIB, 20 Jul 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2286580).

### C. Mains-Style Question

**Q. Examine the role of pumped-storage hydropower in India's renewable-energy transition. What governance safeguards are needed while fast-tracking such projects? (GS3, 250 words)**

Model-answer skeleton:

1. **Why PSPs matter:** long-duration energy storage, peak-load support, frequency regulation, black-start capability and balancing variable solar/wind generation.
2. **Current hook:** 11 PSPs/15,870 MW under construction; 6 more/8,140 MW appraised; faster CEA DPR timelines show policy urgency.
3. **Risks:** forest diversion, slope instability, river-basin impacts, local displacement, cumulative hydrological impacts and weak public consultation.
4. **Way forward:** prefer off-stream closed-loop designs where feasible; basin-level cumulative impact assessments; transparent DPRs; benefit-sharing with local communities; grid-planning integration with storage procurement.

### D. PYQ Mirror

- **Pumped-storage hydropower:** UPSC Prelims **2024** asked the term "pumped-storage hydropower" in the context of **long-duration energy storage**. Today’s PSP status update is a direct continuation of that concept.
- **Legal profession / Bar Councils:** UPSC Prelims **2022** asked whether Bar Councils have power to lay down rules on legal education and recognition of law colleges. The Advocates amendment consultation is the live statutory-regulation hook.

## Answers

- **Q1 — (d).** All three are correct: the new ICI base is 2022-23, iron ore is the ninth core industry, and June 2026 growth was 5.0% YoY.
- **Q2 — (d).** All three are correct: the draft was prepared in consultation with BCI, hosted on 18 July, and comments are due by 31 July 2026.
- **Q3 — (d).** All three are correct: PSPs are long-duration storage assets and the July 2026 status note gives both the 15,870 MW construction figure and the faster DPR timeline.
- **Q4 — (d).** NAKSHA is an urban GIS land-parcel mapping exercise under DILRMP; the Goa clarification explicitly separated it from acquisition/title change.
- **Q5 — (a).** Only statement 1 is correct. NMCM/MGMD documents village profiles; it is not an artist-registration or welfare-disbursement scheme.
- **Q6 — (a).** The revised ICI covers nine sectors: the earlier eight plus iron ore.
- **Q7 — (a).** Statements 1 and 2 are correct. BCI is statutory under the Advocates Act; ECI does not supervise BCI elections under Article 324.
