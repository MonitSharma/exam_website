### 1. India–US Bilateral Trade Agreement: ministerial talks to finalise Phase-1 framework
**Subject: International Relations / Economy (GS2 IR, GS3 Economy)**

- Commerce & Industry Minister **Piyush Goyal** and **US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer** held two-day ministerial talks (around **20–21 June 2026**) to give final touches to the **first tranche of the India–US Bilateral Trade Agreement (BTA)**, following chief-negotiator-level talks held 2–4 June in Delhi.
- **Exam angle:** Classic IR-meets-economy theme — tests understanding of bilateral FTAs vs WTO multilateralism, "reciprocal/retaliatory" tariffs, and India's export exposure. The pressure point is timing: the **10% temporary US tariff** imposed on trading partners on **24 February 2026** (150-day window) **expires 24 July 2026**, so a framework deal is being raced to completion before a new US tariff regime kicks in.
- **Memorise:** BTA = bilateral (India–US), being negotiated in **phases/tranches**; current target is **Phase-1**; deadline pressure from the **24 July 2026** tariff expiry. Distinguish a BTA from a full FTA/CEPA.
- **Notebook:** GS2 (International Relations — India & developed world) + GS3 (Economy — external sector/trade).
- **Static anchor:** Mrunal **Pillar 4 (BoP, trade, FTAs)** + Vision IAS *India–US relations* note → add a cross-link card: "Reciprocal tariffs (2026) → BTA Phase-1; contrast with WTO MFN principle."

### 2. I4C launches two cyber-victim portals: Grievance Redressal Mechanism (GRM) + Money Restoration Module (MRM)
**Subject: Internal Security (GS3)**

- **Exam angle:** Internal Security is under-covered relative to its PYQ weight (cyber security is one of the two biggest GS3-IS clusters since 2013). This is a ready "governance + tech" example: institutional architecture for cybercrime, victim restitution, and inter-agency coordination.
- **Memorise:** I4C is under **MHA**; MRM uses a **three-tiered framework** letting victims **reclaim up to ₹50,000** from single/multiple accounts; the portals link **police–banks–National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP)**. (Pair-up trap: **CERT-In is under MeitY**, **NCIIPC under NTRO** protects critical information infrastructure — **I4C is MHA**.)
- **Notebook:** GS3 (Internal Security — cyber security; role of media/communication & basics of cyber security).
- **Static anchor:** Shankar IAS / Vision IAS **Internal Security → Cyber Security** chapter → add cross-link: "Cyber architecture map: I4C (MHA) · CERT-In (MeitY) · NCIIPC (NTRO) · NCRP · DPDP Act 2023."

### 5. ISRO + DAE developing lunar-night survival tech to extend lander life to 100–200 days
**Subject: Science & Technology — Space (GS3)**

- ISRO (Chairman **V. Narayanan**) said ISRO is working **with the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE)** on **artificial heating systems** — likely **Radioisotope Heater Units (RHUs)** — to let lunar landers survive the harsh **lunar night** and operate for **100–200 days**, versus the **~14-day** life of the **Chandrayaan-3 Vikram** lander.
- **Exam angle:** A vivid Sci-Tech + nuclear-tech crossover. The lunar night plunges to roughly **−180°C to −250°C** at the poles, with no solar power — a sunlight-independent heat source (radioactive decay) is the workaround. Feeds future missions.

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS3)

**Q.** "India is among the strongest improvers in global energy-transition readiness, yet structural constraints continue to weigh on its journey to a low-carbon energy system." **Examine.** (15 marks, 250 words)

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Define energy transition; cite ETI 2026 (India 70th, a top gainer; non-fossil share already crossing ~50% of installed capacity ahead of the 2030 NDC target).
- **Gains/strengths:** Record solar & wind additions and falling tariffs; policy push (PLI for solar/cells, green hydrogen mission, ISA leadership); improved transition-readiness on investment & infrastructure; rising equity and access.
- **Structural constraints:** Coal still dominant in generation; grid integration & storage (battery/pumped-hydro) gaps; financing costs and DISCOM health; critical-mineral (lithium, etc.) import dependence; land/transmission bottlenecks; just-transition concerns for coal-dependent regions.
- **Way forward:** Storage + grid modernisation, domestic solar-manufacturing depth, transmission build-out, green finance and carbon-market roll-out, just-transition framework. **Conclude:** Improving readiness must convert into deep decarbonisation to hit net-zero-2070.

## ANSWERS

**Q2 — (b).** GRM and MRM were launched by the **I4C under the MHA** to help cyber-fraud victims (MRM enables recovery of up to ₹50,000).

**Q5 — (b).** It is an initiative of the **Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution** (foodgrain storage).

**Q6 — (c) All three.** CERT-In→MeitY, NCIIPC→NTRO, I4C→MHA are all correctly matched.

**Q7 — (c) Both 1 and 2.** Chandrayaan-3 had Propulsion Module + Lander (Vikram) + Rover (Pragyan) and achieved the first soft landing near the lunar **south pole**.
