## Briefing

### 1. Rabi MSPs for 2027-28: wheat up only ₹25 to ₹2,610, while pulses and oilseeds get the bigger hikes — Agriculture
**Exam angle.** The CCEA (30 Sept 2026) approved MSPs for six rabi crops. The hike is lopsided: the cereal (wheat) gets a small raise and the oilseeds and pulses get large ones. That fits the policy push to cut edible-oil and pulse imports and shift area away from wheat. It is a good Mains hook on MSP design and cropping-pattern signals.
**Memorise.**
- Wheat ₹2,610 (+₹25; expected return ~106%, the highest).
- Barley ₹2,286 (+₹136).
- Gram ₹5,958 (+₹83).
- Masur ₹7,390 (+₹390).
- Rapeseed-mustard ₹6,613 (+₹413).
- Safflower ₹7,215 (+₹675, the largest absolute rise and the lowest return, ~50%).
- Policy basis: MSP is set at at least 1.5× the all-India weighted average cost of production (A2+FL).
- CACP recommends, CCEA decides.

**Notebook:** GS3 Agriculture.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal Agriculture pillar, "MSP, procurement & CACP"; Shankar IAS "Agriculture – Pricing". *Nudge:* add a one-line row to your MSP card: "Rabi 2027-28: wheat ₹2,610; safflower ₹7,215". Cross-link to the edible-oil duty cut (25 Sept) and the pulses self-reliance mission.

### 3. National Coastal Mission 2.0 guidelines released: ₹767 crore to 2030-31 — Environment
**Exam angle.** The Environment Ministry's mission (released 30 Sept 2026) is a central-sector scheme for 9 coastal States and 4 UTs. It covers shoreline and ecosystem restoration, risk assessment and livelihoods, and builds on the earlier National Coastal Management Programme and the CRZ framework. Link it to climate adaptation and the blue economy.

### 4. CAG-BHASHINI Anuvaad launched: AI translation for audit reports — Polity & Governance
**Exam angle.** The CAG (Article 148) and the Digital India BHASHINI Division launched an AI translator tailored to audit documents on 2 Oct 2026. It supports 36 Indian text languages, 23 voice languages and 35 international languages, keeps humans in the loop and stresses data sovereignty. It matters for transparency: CAG reports on Union accounts go to the President, who lays them before Parliament (Article 151). Regional-language access widens public scrutiny.
**Memorise.**
- Launched 2 Oct 2026.
- 36 + 23 + 35 languages.
- Human-in-the-loop design.
- Articles 148–151 cover the CAG.
- CAG is appointed by the President and removed only like a Supreme Court judge.

### C. Mains Question
**Q8 (GS3, 15 marks / 250 words).** *Critically examine* whether the present Minimum Support Price regime, with its cost-plus formula and uneven procurement, sends the right signals for crop diversification in India.

**Model-answer skeleton**
1. **Intro:** MSP is announced for 22 crops on a 1.5× A2+FL basis. The 2027-28 rabi hikes tilt towards oilseeds and pulses (safflower +₹675, mustard +₹413, masur +₹390), against wheat's +₹25.
2. **Where it works:** the signal is a price floor with a clear margin, aimed at cutting import dependence in edible oils and pulses. It is supported by the Mission on Edible Oils and the pulses mission.
3. **Where it fails:** effective procurement is concentrated in rice and wheat and in a few States, so a higher MSP on paper does not become a market price for pulses and oilseeds. The A2+FL basis ignores land rent and capital cost (C2). No legal guarantee and weak storage and market linkages remain.
4. **Way forward:** a Price Deficiency Payment model (as in Madhya Pradesh's Bhavantar, or PM-AASHA), expanded procurement and aggregation (FPOs, e-NAM), assured offtake for target crops and a gradual shift of input support away from water-intensive crops.

## Answers
1. **(b)** Wheat +₹25 to ₹2,610 and safflower +₹675 are correct. Statement 3 is wrong, because safflower (~50%) has the lowest expected return, not gram (~59%).
2. **(c)** The exercise was co-hosted by the Indian and Philippine Navies, with INS Sahyadri and INS Kulish participating. It is ASEAN-India, not trilateral.
3. **(d)** The outlay is ₹767 crore to 2030-31, for 9 States and 4 UTs. It is an Environment Ministry (MoEFCC) scheme, not Jal Shakti.
4. **(b)** An AI translation solution for audit documents, built on BHASHINI.
5. **(a)** Kaliningrad lies between Poland and Lithuania and hosts the Baltic Fleet HQ at Baltiysk. It has no Belarus border.
6. **(c)** CACP recommends and the CCEA decides, and there are 22 mandated crops plus FRP for sugarcane. MSP is not a legal entitlement under the NFSA.
7. **(d)** India is a dialogue partner and not a member. ASEAN's founders were Indonesia, Malaysia, the Philippines, Singapore and Thailand.
