## Part A — Briefing

### 1. WWF Living Planet Report 2026: monitored wildlife populations down 73% since 1970 — Environment
**Exam angle:** The 2026 edition, "The Science of Hope", reports the Living Planet Index (compiled with the Zoological Society of London) at an average 73% fall in monitored populations over 1970–2022. This is a change in average population size, not 73% of animals lost. Reports of this kind are standard prelims fodder (publisher, index, what the number means) and give Mains data on the biodiversity crisis, Kunming–Montreal GBF targets and Red List trends.
**Memorize:** 73% average decline, 1970–2022 (about 2.5% a year) | 35,803 populations of 5,790 species | roughly half of the tracked populations are stable or growing | biennial WWF report with the LPI from ZSL.
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (Biodiversity — reports/indices).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS *Biodiversity* chapter (IUCN Red List, threats) and the "Reports & Indices" tab. *Nudge:* add the LPR 2026 line with the "average, not absolute" caveat, and cross-link to the National Red List Roadmap in your Wildlife Week notes.

### 2. MoSPI's Environmental-Economic Accounts Strategy 2026–2030 — Economy
**Exam angle:** MoSPI has released the 2026–30 strategy, which builds on the 2022–26 one (material flow, ocean, energy, biodiversity and urban ecosystem accounts). It follows the UN SEEA-Central Framework and ties into the National Accounts Framework 2025. The idea is that natural capital should be visible in national accounts alongside GDP. It supports SDG 12 and SDG 15. *Reported domain counts differ by source, so do not memorize a number.*
**Memorize:** MoSPI (NSO) | period 2026–30 | UN SEEA-CF | natural-capital accounting is a supplement to, not a replacement of, GDP.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Inclusive growth / National income) and GS3 Environment.
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh *National Income* chapter (GDP, GNP, Green GDP). *Nudge:* add "SEEA / natural capital accounts" under the national income concepts and cross-link to the 2022–26 strategy.

### C. Mains Question
**Q8.** "Natural capital is the missing line item in India's national accounts." **Critically examine** this statement in the light of the Environmental-Economic Accounts Strategy 2026–30. (15 marks, 250 words)

*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. **Intro:** GDP counts output but not the depletion of forests, water, soil or minerals. Give MoSPI's 2026–30 strategy and SEEA-CF as the response.
2. **Case for the statement:** Degradation costs go uncounted, so growth looks better than it is. Examples: groundwater, forest cover, air pollution. Accounts would guide subsidy, mining and land policy.
3. **Limits and challenges:** Valuation methods are contested, data across States is uneven, accounts supplement GDP rather than replace it, and capacity at State level is thin.
4. **Way forward:** Institutionalise inter-departmental groups in States, publish accounts regularly, link them to budgeting and GBF/SDG indicators, and use independent audit. Conclude that accounts make trade-offs visible, not that they remove them.

## Part C — Answers
- **Q1: (d)** — The 73% figure is the average fall in the size of monitored populations (about 2.5% a year), not a count of animals lost, so statement 2 is wrong. The LPI is compiled with ZSL and the 2026 edition covers 35,803 populations of 5,790 species for 1970–2022.
- **Q2: (c)** — MoSPI issued the 2026–30 strategy, building on the 2022–26 one, and it follows SEEA-CF, a UN Statistical Commission standard. It supplements the national accounts with natural-capital accounts. It does not replace GDP with Green GDP.
- **Q3: (b)** — JOSH stands for Joint Squad for Health. It is launched by the Health Ministry with the Youth Affairs and Defence ministries under TB Mukt Bharat. Its first phase covers 55 districts in 11 States, and teams include Class-I officers, MY Bharat volunteers and NCC cadets. It supports, rather than replaces, existing TB programme strategies.
- **Q4: (a)** — FSS-1 INS Surya was launched by the Defence Minister and was designed and built by HSL. Fleet support ships replenish warships at sea and extend their reach and endurance. HSL is a Ministry of Defence PSU, so statement 3 is wrong.
- **Q5: (c)** — Optogenetics inserts genes for light-gated channels such as channelrhodopsin, found in green algae, into chosen cells, so that light can control their activity. Activation by ultrasound describes a different technique (sonogenetics), so statement 3 is wrong.
- **Q6: (a)** — The Red List runs Least Concern, Near Threatened, then the threatened categories Vulnerable, Endangered and Critically Endangered, before Extinct in the Wild and Extinct. Only the first sequence keeps this order.
- **Q7: (d)** — Green GDP deducts the estimated costs of environmental damage and depletion of natural capital from conventional GDP. Environmental-economic accounts such as SEEA provide the data needed to compute it.
- **Q8:** Mains question; see the skeleton above.
