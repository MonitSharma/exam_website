## PART A — BRIEFING

### 1. NITI Aayog–IIM Ahmedabad assessment: only 18.5% of PLI outlay disbursed so far — Economy / Reports
**Exam angle:** PLI is the flagship manufacturing-push tool (Atmanirbhar Bharat). The assessment shows a gap between investment/production outcomes and incentive payouts, and that MSME benefits are largely indirect (via supply chains of anchor firms). Good for GS3 "industrial policy" and "inclusive growth" answers.
**Key facts:** 14 sectors; outlay ~₹1.91 lakh crore; incentives disbursed ₹35,354 cr (18.5%) till March 2026; investment ₹2.4 trn (76% of target); production ₹22.66 trn (64%); jobs ~8.49 lakh (~28% of ~30.2 lakh target). White goods best MSME case (domestic value addition ~20% FY20 → 55% FY25). Report says PLI "never designed primarily" for MSMEs.
**Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Industry).
**Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh / Shankar IAS "Industrial Policy & Manufacturing" → update with this assessment; cross-link to *Make in India*, MSME definition, and Economic Survey chapter on manufacturing.

### 2. Global Carbon Budget 2026: global fossil CO₂ dips, India's set to rise 4.4% — Environment
**Exam angle:** Prelims-friendly report (who publishes, key numbers) and a Mains hook on India's "industry-led" emissions growth while the power sector flattens, linked to NDC and COP31 negotiations.
**Key facts:** 21st edition by the **Global Carbon Project** (journal *Earth System Science Data*). Fossil CO₂ projected −0.4% to 38.2 Gt in 2026; total incl. land use ~43.3 Gt; ~150 Gt CO₂ left for 1.5°C ("<4 years" at current rates); China −0.9%, US −1.3%, EU −1.5%, **India +4.4%**. CO₂ ~427.7 ppm. (CREA, as reported: India's H1 2026 CO₂ +3.7%, led by steel and cement.)
**Notebook:** GS3 Environment (Climate change).
**Static anchor:** Shankar IAS "Climate Change" chapter → add GCB to the "reports & institutions" table (GCP vs IPCC vs UNEP vs WMO); India's NDC (45% intensity cut by 2030 vs 2005; net zero 2070).

### 3. First BIMSTEC Health Ministers' Meeting, Colombo (11 Nov) — International Relations
**Exam angle:** BIMSTEC is India's preferred platform under "Neighbourhood First/Act East" while SAARC is dormant; functional cooperation (health, traditional medicine, digital) is a soft-power tool.
**Key facts:** Joint Ministerial Statement and revised BIMSTEC Plan of Action on Public Health (2026–30) adopted; **BIMSTEC Centre of Excellence in Traditional Medicine to be hosted at ITRA, Jamnagar (Gujarat)**; proposed BIMSTEC Digital Health Framework; AMR and pandemic preparedness listed as priorities; India–Sri Lanka MoU for 100 ambulances (grant ~USD 5.28 mn) and support for 50 health kiosks; next ministerial meeting in Thailand, 2028 (biennial). Seven members: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand.
**Notebook:** GS2 International Relations.
**Static anchor:** Mrunal "India & its neighbourhood / regional groupings" pillar → update the BIMSTEC sectoral-lead table; cross-link to Ayush diplomacy and Ayushman Bharat.

### 4. ISRO fires revised PSLV third stage (PS3) at Sriharikota (12 Nov) — Science & Technology
**Exam angle:** Return-to-flight diligence after third-stage anomalies on PSLV-C61/EOS-09 (May 2025) and PSLV-C62/EOS-N1 (Jan 2026). Tests understanding of launch-vehicle staging and reliability engineering.
**Key facts:** Ground static test at the Static Test Facility, SDSC-SHAR; changes include improved nozzle design margins, **redundant sealing**, strengthened interfaces, and revised raw-material acceptance, assembly and non-destructive testing; ISRO will review data and conduct further tests (no launch date announced). PS3 is a solid motor (reported max thrust ~240 kN).
**Notebook:** GS3 Science & Technology (Space).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum/Shankar IAS "Space Technology" → add PSLV stage table (PS1 solid, PS2 liquid, PS3 solid, PS4 liquid); cross-link to SSLV, NSIL and IN-SPACe.

### 5. Kurumba language and the Census 10,000-speaker threshold — Society
**Exam angle:** Linguistic diversity, PVTGs and Census methodology meet in one case: a language with no script and ~2,700 speakers risks vanishing and will not be tabulated separately.
**Key facts (as reported):** Kurumbas of Attappady (Palakkad, Kerala) — a PVTG of ~2,719 people; languages with <10,000 speakers are grouped under "others" in the Census; India has 75 PVTGs (Kerala has five); relevant: SPPEL (2013, CIIL Mysuru), PM-JANMAN (2023), Articles 29 and 350A, NEP 2020 mother-tongue instruction, UN Decade of Indigenous Languages (2022–32).
**Notebook:** GS1 Society (Tribes, Language) / GS2 (Welfare of vulnerable sections).
**Static anchor:** Spectrum "Indian Society — Tribal issues" and Laxmikanth "Scheduled and Tribal Areas" → add PVTG list/schemes; cross-link to the Census 2027 notes.

### B3. Mains question
**Q8.** "PLI schemes have succeeded in attracting investment but struggle on disbursal and inclusiveness." **Critically examine** this statement in the light of the recent NITI Aayog assessment and suggest reforms. (15 marks, 250 words)

**Model-answer skeleton:**
1. *Intro:* PLI (2020–) — 14 sectors, ~₹1.91 lakh cr outlay; aims: scale, exports, import substitution.
2. *Successes:* ₹2.4 trn investment (76% of target), ₹22.66 trn production (64%), ~8.5 lakh jobs; white goods value addition 20% → 55%.
3. *Shortcomings:* only 18.5% disbursed (outcome-linked, thresholds not met); jobs ~28% of target; benefits skewed to large firms; MSME gains indirect; weak data on MSME graduation; imported-component loopholes (e.g., telecom).
4. *Reforms:* sector-specific design and demand-side support; supplier-development/technology-absorption incentives; value-addition norms; standardised outcome data; tie to skilling and credit for MSMEs.
5. *Conclusion:* shift from "incentive-led" to "ecosystem-led" manufacturing.

## PART C — ANSWERS

1. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 3 match the assessment; MSME benefits are largely indirect, not primary.
2. **(b)** — Global Carbon Project publishes the GCB; fossil CO₂ projected −0.4% globally, India +4.4%.
3. **(b)** — Statements 1 and 2 are correct; Pakistan is not a BIMSTEC member (members: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand).
4. **(b)** — PSLV: PS1 solid, PS2 liquid (Vikas), PS3 solid, PS4 liquid; cryogenic stage is GSLV's.
5. **(b)** — Kurumbas are a PVTG and sub-10,000-speaker languages are grouped under "others"; Kerala has five PVTGs, so 3 is wrong.
6. **(c)** — Article 9 deals with finance (9.1: developed countries shall provide); Art. 4 = NDCs/mitigation, Art. 6 = cooperative approaches/markets, Art. 14 = global stocktake.
7. **(a)** — BIST-EC formed 1997 (Bangkok Declaration), renamed BIMSTEC; Secretariat in Dhaka; not SAARC's successor and no Maldives/Afghanistan.
