## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. RBI approves record ₹2.87 lakh crore surplus transfer to the Centre for FY26
**Subject:** Economy · **Notebook:** GS3 Economy

- **Exam angle:** A textbook high-yield Economy story — links the RBI's balance sheet, the Economic Capital Framework (ECF), non-tax revenue and fiscal federalism in one item. Prelims tests the *mechanism* (Section 47, ECF, CRB); Mains tests *central-bank autonomy vs. fiscalisation* and the fact that this windfall sits **outside the divisible pool**, giving States zero automatic share.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - FY26 surplus transfer = **₹2,86,588 crore (~₹2.87 lakh crore)** — the **highest ever**, ~7% above FY25's then-record ₹2.69 lakh crore. Decided at the RBI's **623rd Central Board meeting** (Governor **Sanjay Malhotra**).
  - Governed by the **Economic Capital Framework**, adopted on the **Bimal Jalan Committee (2018)** recommendations; surplus transfer enabled under **Section 47 of the RBI Act, 1934**.
  - **Contingent Risk Buffer (CRB)** maintained at **6.5%** of balance sheet (Jalan panel band: **5.5–6.5%**); ₹1.09 lakh crore moved to CRB for FY26.
  - RBI balance sheet expanded **20.6%** to **₹91.97 lakh crore** (March 2026); gross income up **26.42%**.
  - Classified as **non-tax revenue of the Union** → not shared with States via the Finance Commission divisible pool.
- **Static anchor:** *Mrunal Economy — Pillar 1A (Banking/Monetary policy) & Pillar 1B (Budget → Non-Tax Receipts)* / Ramesh Singh, *Indian Economy*, Banking chapter. **Nudge:** add a cross-link from your "RBI surplus" note to (a) the **Bimal Jalan ECF** table and (b) **Article 280 / divisible pool** so the fiscal-federalism angle is one click away.

### 2. National Statistical Commission (NSC) reconstituted — Dr. Saibal Chattopadhyay named Chairperson
**Subject:** Polity & Governance · **Notebook:** GS2 Polity/Governance

- **Exam angle:** Classic Prelims "body in news" — examiners love testing whether such bodies are **constitutional, statutory or executive**. NSC is a **non-statutory, non-constitutional advisory body** (set up by executive resolution), a frequent trap.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - **Chairperson:** Dr. **Saibal Chattopadhyay** (former Director, **IIM Calcutta**); approved by the **Appointments Committee of the Cabinet (ACC)**. Three members: Prof. Shubhabrata Das, Satyendra Bahadur Singh, Dr. Madhavan Mukund.
  - NSC created by **executive resolution (1 June 2005)** on **Rangarajan Commission** recommendations; **operational from 12 July 2006**. *Not* a statutory body.
  - Structure: **part-time Chairperson + four part-time Members**; **CEO of NITI Aayog** is an **ex-officio Member**; **Chief Statistician of India (CSI)** is the **Secretary**. Sits under **MoSPI**.
- **Static anchor:** *Laxmikanth, Indian Polity — "Non-Constitutional Bodies" chapter.* **Nudge:** add NSC beside NITI Aayog/National Human Rights bodies and tag it "executive resolution, NOT statutory"; cross-link to **Chief Statistician of India / NSO / MoSPI**.

### 3. India–Russia logistics pact (RELOS) operationalised
**Subject:** International Relations · **Notebook:** GS2 IR

- **Exam angle:** Adds to the family of India's "foundational/logistics" agreements (LEMOA-US, ACSA-Japan, etc.). Prelims can test parties, the **non-basing** character and validity; Mains can use it for India's **multi-alignment** and defence diplomacy with Russia amid the Ukraine-era pressures.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - **RELOS** = **Reciprocal Exchange of Logistics Agreement** (also RELOA), a **bilateral India–Russia** logistics-support pact.
  - **Signed:** Moscow, **18 February 2025**; **entered into force: January 2026**; **valid 5 years**.
  - Enables **reciprocal use of bases, ports, airfields** for refuelling, supplies, repairs, POL, medical aid — only during **mutually agreed** exercises, training, HADR or port calls.
  - **Administrative ceiling of 3,000 personnel** (not standing troops); **strict non-basing clause** — no permanent foreign bases.
- **Static anchor:** *India–Russia bilateral relations notes (Vision/Mrunal IR).* **Nudge:** add a one-row comparison table — **LEMOA (US, 2016) · ACSA (Japan) · RELOS (Russia, 2025/26)** — so logistics agreements are revisable at a glance.

### 4. NOAA confirms a new El Niño; 63% odds of a "super" event by winter 2026–27
**Subject:** Geography · **Notebook:** GS1 Geography (with GS3 Environment/agriculture spillover)

- **Exam angle:** Perennial high-yield Geography/climate topic with strong PYQ pedigree. India relevance = **monsoon suppression** and kharif risk; pair with **IOD** and **Walker circulation**.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - **NOAA** confirmed El Niño formation (declared ~**11 June 2026**); **63%** chance of a **"very strong"/super** event during **Nov 2026–Jan 2027**.
  - **Super El Niño** = Niño-region SST departure **exceeding +2°C**. Only **four** such years since 1950: **1972-73, 1982-83, 1997-98, 2015-16**.
  - Mechanism: weakening/reversal of **equatorial trade winds** → warm water drifts **eastward** to South American coast → self-reinforcing feedback.
  - India impact: **weakens the southwest monsoon**, erratic rainfall, drought risk; a **positive IOD** can *partly* offset it.
- **Static anchor:** *NCERT Class XI Geography (Physical) + G.C. Leong — atmospheric/ocean circulation; Shankar IAS Environment (climate).* **Nudge:** cross-link your ENSO note to **IOD**, **Walker Circulation** and the **Indian monsoon mechanism** page.

### 5. "BGP hijacking" in the news after Telegram access disruption
**Subject:** Science & Technology · **Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (Cyber Security)

- **Exam angle:** Cyber-security terms recur in Prelims (DDoS, DNS, phishing, zero-day). BGP hijacking is a clean "what is it" target and a Mains hook on critical-information-infrastructure resilience.
- **Key facts to memorise:**
  - **BGP = Border Gateway Protocol**, the internet's inter-network routing protocol; networks are organised as **Autonomous Systems (AS)**.
  - **BGP hijacking** = a rogue/misconfigured AS **falsely advertises IP prefixes** it doesn't own → traffic is **misrouted**.
  - Consequences: **blackholing → Denial of Service (DoS)**, **Man-in-the-Middle (MitM)** interception, and **cascading global propagation** within minutes.
  - Context: claimed during India's **temporary block on Telegram** (founder Pavel Durov's allegation).
- **Static anchor:** *Vision/Vajiram Sci-Tech — Cyber Security notes (internet protocols, DNS, DDoS).* **Nudge:** add BGP next to **DNS spoofing / DDoS / MitM**; cross-link **CERT-In** and the **IT Act, 2000**.

### Mains-style question (GS3 — Economy)

**Q.** "The Reserve Bank of India's record surplus transfers have made it an increasingly important source of the Union government's fiscal capacity." **Examine** the implications of this trend for central-bank autonomy and fiscal federalism. *(250 words, 15 marks)*

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** Note the FY26 record (₹2.87 lakh crore) and that under the ECF/Section 47 RBI Act, surplus above risk buffers flows to the Centre as non-tax revenue.
- **Body — for fiscal capacity:** Eases fiscal-deficit pressure without higher taxes/borrowing; predictable non-tax cushion; supports capex.
- **Body — autonomy risks:** Recurring large dividends risk "fiscalisation" of monetary policy; executive pressure on reserve-management and exchange-rate decisions; converting gold to liquid foreign paper alters the balance-sheet risk profile.
- **Body — fiscal federalism:** Being non-tax revenue, it bypasses the **Finance Commission divisible pool** → zero automatic devolution to States, while States stay bound by Article 293 borrowing limits → vertical imbalance.
- **Way forward / conclusion:** Conservative CRB within ECF, transparent reporting, and voluntary review of non-tax windfalls to protect both autonomy and cooperative federalism.

## PART C — ANSWERS

**Q1 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** ₹2.87 lakh crore is the highest-ever transfer (St.1 ✓); it is non-tax revenue retained by the Union and is **not** shared via the divisible pool (St.2 ✗); the ECF governs the transferable surplus (St.3 ✓).

**Q2 — (a) 1 only.** RELOS is a bilateral India–Russia logistics pact (St.1 ✓); it has an explicit **non-basing clause** barring permanent foreign bases (St.2 ✗).

**Q3 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** El Niño is the warming of the central/eastern equatorial Pacific (St.1 ✓); it **weakens**, not strengthens, the SW monsoon (St.2 ✗); a positive IOD can partly offset its effect (St.3 ✓).

**Q4 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** NSC was created by an **executive resolution (2005)**, not a statute (St.1 ✗); the CEO of NITI Aayog is an ex-officio member (St.2 ✓); the Chief Statistician of India is its Secretary (St.3 ✓).

**Q5 — (b)** Misdirection of internet traffic through false (BGP) network routing announcements.

**Q6 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** ECF rests on the **Bimal Jalan Committee** (St.1 ✓); CRB band is **5.5–6.5%** (St.2 ✓); surplus transfer is under **Section 47, RBI Act 1934** (St.3 ✓).

**Q7 — (b)** The Southern Oscillation is the see-saw of atmospheric pressure between the eastern and western tropical Pacific (measured by the SOI), the atmospheric component of ENSO.
