## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. India's Total Fertility Rate falls to 1.9 — fifth straight year below replacement
**Subject / Notebook:** Society (population & demographic transition) — GS1.

**Headline:** The **Sample Registration System (SRS) Statistical Report 2024**, released by the **Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner of India (ORGI)**, puts India's **Total Fertility Rate (TFR) at 1.9** in 2024 — below the **replacement level of 2.1** for the fifth consecutive year.

**Exam angle:** A textbook GS1 society item — India has firmly entered **below-replacement fertility**, shifting the policy conversation from *population control* to *ageing, pensions, healthcare and the closing window of the demographic dividend*. The state-wise spread is a ready Prelims/Mains data set on demographic divergence between north and south.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **TFR 1.9 (2024)**; replacement level = **2.1**; below replacement for the **5th year running**.
- Source = **SRS** (a large-scale demographic survey by the **ORGI**, the primary source for birth/death rate, IMR, TFR between censuses).
- Only **6 large states still above replacement**: **Bihar (2.9), Uttar Pradesh (2.6), Madhya Pradesh (2.4), Rajasthan (2.3), Jharkhand (2.2), Chhattisgarh (2.2)**.
- Lowest: **Delhi (1.2), Kerala & Tamil Nadu & West Bengal (1.3)**, then AP/Maharashtra/Punjab/J&K (~1.4).
- Implication: working-age share peaks (~2041 per Economic Survey 2018-19) then declines → future ageing pressure.

**Static anchor:** *NCERT — "India: People and Economy" (Population Composition / Human Development)* + *Mrunal/Vision Society notes on demographic dividend*. **Nudge:** add a TFR-by-state map and a "demographic dividend → demographic burden" timeline; cross-link to GS2 (old-age social security, pensions) and GS3 (labour force).

### 2. DRDO's indigenous 'Netra' AEW&C system gets Final Operational Clearance
**Subject / Notebook:** Science & Technology / Defence — GS3.

**Headline:** On **25 June 2026**, at the **Centre for Airborne Systems (CABS), Bengaluru**, DRDO handed the **Final Operational Clearance (FOC)** for the indigenous **Netra Mk-1 Airborne Early Warning & Control (AEW&C)** system to the **Indian Air Force** — capping a programme that began in **2003**.

**Exam angle:** High-yield Sci-Tech/Defence fact distinct from the recent missile items. AEW&C = a flying radar that gives **360°/long-range airspace surveillance, early threat detection and battle management**, a force-multiplier validated in recent operations. Good for "indigenisation / Aatmanirbhar Bharat in defence" Mains lines.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **FOC date 25 June 2026**; developed by **DRDO–CABS** (nodal agency); IAF operates **3 Netra Mk-1** aircraft.
- Platform: **Embraer ERJ-145**; based at **Bhisiana Air Force Station (near Bathinda, Punjab)** with **No. 200 Squadron**; first inducted **2015**.
- **FOC** certifies the aircraft is fully combat-ready/mission-capable (the stage after **Initial Operational Clearance, IOC**).
- Distinguish from the bigger **AWACS** ("Eye in the Sky", on IL-76) and the upcoming **Netra Mk-2 / AWACS-India**.

**Static anchor:** *Vision/Shankar Sci-Tech — "Defence technology / DRDO systems."* **Nudge:** tabulate **AEW&C (Netra, Embraer ERJ-145) vs AWACS (IL-76)**, and pin CABS as the DRDO lab for airborne surveillance.

### 3. Kashmir's Sufiyana Music proposed for UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list
**Subject / Notebook:** Art & Culture (music traditions) — GS1.

**Headline:** The J&K government has **forwarded a proposal to the Union Ministry of Culture** to nominate **Sufiyana Mausiqi** — the classical Sufi ensemble music of Kashmir — for **UNESCO's Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage (ICH) of Humanity**.

**Exam angle:** Perfect "Art & Culture in news" item — couples a *living musical tradition* with the *UNESCO ICH framework* (a recurring Prelims theme). Sufiyana is described as endangered, which is exactly the safeguarding rationale behind the 2003 Convention.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **Sufiyana Mausiqi:** Kashmir's classical/Sufi **ensemble (choral) music**, born of **14th-century Indo–Central Asian** exchange; performed by **5–7 musicians** led by a **santoor** player.
- The **Kashmiri santoor** (~**100 strings over 25 bridges**) is the principal instrument — note it differs from the Persian santoor.
- Process: State → **Union Ministry of Culture**; the **Sangeet Natak Akademi** is the **nodal agency** for India's ICH nominations to UNESCO.
- Framework: **UNESCO Convention for the Safeguarding of Intangible Cultural Heritage (2003)**; **India ratified in 2005**; India already has **15 elements** on the list (latest: **Garba of Gujarat, 2023**; *Kolkata's Durga Puja, 2021*).

**Static anchor:** *Nitin Singhania — "Music (Hindustani/folk traditions)" + "UNESCO & Heritage"* chapters. **Nudge:** add Sufiyana (santoor) to your folk/classical-music table and refresh the running list of India's UNESCO ICH inscriptions.

### 4. MoSPI to launch India's first Index of Services Production (ISP)
**Subject / Notebook:** Economy (national statistics / high-frequency indicators) — GS3.

**Headline:** The **Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation (MoSPI)** will release India's first **Index of Services Production (ISP)** — a **monthly** measure of real output of the **formal services sector** — with **trial indices published from 14 July 2026**.

**Exam angle:** Services are **over half of India's GVA/GDP** yet had **no high-frequency volume index** (unlike industry's IIP). The ISP fills that gap — a clean "data infrastructure / evidence-based policy" point and an easy Prelims fact pair (ISP ↔ IIP).

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **ISP** = monthly index of *real output (volume)* of the **formal services** sector; complements the **Index of Industrial Production (IIP)**.
- **Base year 2024-25**; trial indices for 2025-26 and April 2026 out **14 July 2026**; thereafter monthly with a **~60-day lag**.
- Initial coverage: **wholesale & retail trade, transport, banking, insurance, telecom, hotels, real estate, professional services, arts & entertainment**; **health & education added later**.
- Released by **MoSPI** (which also produces **IIP, CPI, GDP/GVA, ASI, PLFS**).

**Static anchor:** *Mrunal Economy — "National Income / Statistical system (MoSPI, NSO, IIP, CPI)."* **Nudge:** add ISP next to IIP in your "high-frequency indicators" card; note base year **2024-25** vs IIP's **2011-12**.

### 5. Tungabhadra Dam gets 33 new spillway gates — three-state water consensus
**Subject / Notebook:** Geography (peninsular rivers) + interstate water governance — GS1/GS2.

**Headline:** On **25 June 2026**, leaders dedicated **33 newly installed spillway gates** at the **Tungabhadra Dam (Hosapete/Hospet, Karnataka)** — completed within ~6 months after **Gate No. 19 was washed away in the August 2024 floods** — in a rare show of Karnataka–Andhra Pradesh–Telangana cooperation.

**Exam angle:** Doubles as **"Places in News" map practice** and a **federal water-sharing** hook. The replacement followed a **National Dam Safety Authority** recommendation → links to the **Dam Safety Act, 2021**. Good for a Mains line on cooperative federalism in interstate river management.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- Event attended by **Karnataka CM D.K. Shivakumar, Union Jal Shakti Minister C.R. Patil, AP CM Chandrababu Naidu, Telangana CM Revanth Reddy** (25 June 2026).
- **Tungabhadra River** = formed by confluence of the **Tunga + Bhadra** (both rising in the **Western Ghats**, Karnataka); it is a **major right-bank tributary of the Krishna**.
- **Tungabhadra Dam** at **Hosapete (Vijayanagara dist., Karnataka)**, near **Hampi**; serves **Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana**.
- Trigger: **NDSA** (under the **Dam Safety Act, 2021**) flagged ageing gates after the 2024 breach → all gates replaced.

### Mains-Style Question (GS1 — Society)

**Q.** *"With its Total Fertility Rate now below replacement level, India's demographic challenge is shifting from managing growth to managing transition."* **Examine** the implications of below-replacement fertility for India's economy and society, and suggest measures to harness the remaining demographic dividend. **(15 marks, 250 words)**

**Model-answer skeleton:**
- **Intro:** SRS 2024 → TFR 1.9 (< replacement 2.1), 5th straight year; India past the "population explosion" framing.
- **Implications:** (i) closing window of the **demographic dividend** (working-age share peaks ~2041); (ii) future **ageing** → pension, healthcare, old-age security burdens; (iii) **regional divergence** — high-TFR north (Bihar/UP) vs below-replacement south → labour migration, federal fiscal/political stress (delimitation, devolution); (iv) **gendered** care economy and shrinking workforce.
- **Measures:** invest in **education, skilling & employability** (turn the dividend real — *cf.* the GS1 2016 PYQ); raise **female labour-force participation**; build **pension/social-security** architecture (APY, EPFO, informal-sector cover); healthy-ageing & care infrastructure; data-driven, region-sensitive policy.
- **Conclusion:** The dividend is a **time-bound** opportunity — leverage it now through human-capital investment before the demographic burden sets in.

## PART C — ANSWERS

**Q1 — (c) All three.** SRS 2024 reports TFR 1.9 (below replacement); the SRS is an ORGI product; Bihar (2.9) and UP (2.6) remain above 2.1.

**Q2 — (b) AEW&C aircraft developed by DRDO.** Netra Mk-1 (DRDO–CABS, on the Embraer ERJ-145) received FOC on 25 June 2026; the IAF operates three.

**Q3 — (b) Classical Sufi ensemble music of Kashmir, led by the santoor.** Sufiyana Mausiqi is a 5–7-member Kashmiri ensemble tradition; the J&K government has forwarded it for a UNESCO ICH nomination via the Ministry of Culture.

**Q4 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** ISP is a monthly real-output index of the formal services sector, complements the IIP, and uses base year 2024-25.

**Q5 — (b) A tributary of the Krishna, formed by the confluence of the Tunga and Bhadra.** Both head-streams rise in the Western Ghats; the dam is at Hosapete (Karnataka). Mettur (Cauvery) and Sriram Sagar (Godavari) are different projects.

**Q6 — (c) Both 1 and 2.** India's replacement-level TFR is ~2.1; the demographic dividend arises when the working-age share exceeds the dependent share.

**Q7 — (a) 1, 2 and 3 only.** Bhima, Tungabhadra and Malaprabha are Krishna tributaries; the **Indravati** is a tributary of the **Godavari**.
