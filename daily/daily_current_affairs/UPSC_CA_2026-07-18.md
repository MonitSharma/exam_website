### 2. White Rabbit-based IST Distribution Demonstration Network inaugurated at Jakkur, Bengaluru — Science & Technology

**Headline:** India's first high-precision, Ethernet/fibre-based time-distribution facility using CERN's open-source **White Rabbit** technology was inaugurated at the Regional Reference Standards Laboratory (RRSL), Jakkur — disseminating Indian Standard Time with **sub-nanosecond** accuracy.

**Exam angle:** "One Nation, One Time" push — sovereign timekeeping (CSIR-NPL + ISRO atomic time) reduces dependence on foreign GPS, protecting critical infrastructure (power grids, stock exchanges, 5G/6G, quantum communication) from GPS spoofing/timing attacks. Led by the Legal Metrology Division (Ministry of Consumer Affairs) with NPL, ISRO, BSNL and SEBI.

**Memorize:** White Rabbit = CERN open-source tech; uses **IEEE 1588 Precision Time Protocol (PTP)** over Ethernet/optical fibre; <1 ns sync; scalable to thousands of devices across >1,000 km.

**Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech.
**Static anchor:** Sci-Tech notes — "Atomic clocks / NavIC" section (add White Rabbit alongside NavIC as the *timing* leg of sovereignty); NCERT Geography Class XI IST concept → cross-link.

### 3. Supreme Court clears India's first night safari at Kukrail Reserve Forest, Lucknow — Environment

**Headline:** The SC gave **conditional clearance** to UP's ~₹1,500-crore night safari + zoological park in Kukrail Reserve Forest — India's first (urban) night safari — mandating strict compliance with CEC, Central Zoo Authority and MoEFCC safeguards.

**Exam angle:** Eco-tourism vs forest conservation balance; judicial supervision of forest diversion via the **Central Empowered Committee** (CEC to inspect periodically; first report in 3 months). Project confined to **855.07 ha** of the ~5,000-ha reserve forest; ~71% of the project area kept as green cover; tree translocation preferred over felling.

**Memorize:** Kukrail = reserve forest in Lucknow, UP (also historically known for its gharial rehabilitation centre); India's *first* night safari; monitoring trio = CEC + CZA + MoEFCC.

**Notebook:** GS3 Environment.

### 4. MoPSW approves Porbandar greenfield shipbuilding cluster + Vadinar ship-repair facility — Economy (Infrastructure)

**Headline:** The Ministry of Ports, Shipping & Waterways gave in-principle approval under the **Shipbuilding Development Scheme** to a ~2,000-acre greenfield shipbuilding cluster at Kuchhadi, Porbandar (capacity **1.2–1.5 million GT/year** of large commercial vessels) and a **₹1,570-crore** ship-repair facility at Vadinar (Gulf of Kutch), to be developed by **Cochin Shipyard Ltd + Deendayal Port Authority**.

Ties into Maritime India Vision 2030 / Amrit Kaal maritime targets.

### 5. Maharashtra legislature to go paperless — adopts NeVA (National e-Vidhan Application) — Polity & Governance

**Headline:** Maharashtra decided (16–17 July) to implement **NeVA** across both Houses (Mumbai & Nagpur), targeting a fully paperless legislature in ~3 months; project cost ~₹80 crore on a **60:40 Centre–State** sharing formula.

**Exam angle:** "One Nation, One Application" for legislatures — NeVA is run by the **Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs** (not MeitY) under Digital India, hosted on NIC cloud; digitises questions, bills, committee reports and proceedings. Good Mains line: digital tools improving legislative efficiency and transparency (link to declining sitting days/scrutiny debate).

**Memorize:** NeVA = Ministry of Parliamentary Affairs + NIC; 60:40 central sponsorship (90:10 for NE/hill states); **Nagaland** was the first state to fully implement NeVA (2022); Maharashtra is one of the last big legislatures to join.

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS2 — International Relations, 250 words, 15 marks)

**"India's outreach to Central and Eastern Europe adds a necessary layer to its wider Europe policy." Discuss in the light of recent high-level engagements.**

Model-answer skeleton:
1. **Context:** President Murmu's first-ever visits to Moldova and North Macedonia and the first presidential visit to Romania in 3+ decades (July 2026) signal deliberate diversification beyond Paris–Berlin–Brussels.
2. **Why CEE matters:** gateway/transit corridor into the EU single market for Indian supply chains; diaspora and mobility partnerships; post-Ukraine-war reconstruction and defence-industrial openings; support base for India–EU FTA and multilateral candidatures.
3. **Instruments:** presidential/VVIP diplomacy, Lines of Credit, education–tech–agriculture MoUs, connectivity links (Black Sea–Danube access via Romania).
4. **Limitations & way forward:** thin trade volumes, Russia-sensitivity of some capitals, capacity of Indian missions — institutionalise an India–CEE summit format and business councils rather than episodic visits.
