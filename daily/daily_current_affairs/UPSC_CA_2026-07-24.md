## BRIEFING

### 1. India delivers its first indigenous 350-kg-thrust-class expendable turbojet engine — Science & Technology
**Headline:** On **22 July 2026**, Hyderabad-based **Azad Engineering** manufactured, assembled and delivered to DRDO India's **first indigenous Expendable Turbojet Engine (ETJE)** in the **350-kg thrust class**, designed by DRDO's **Gas Turbine Research Establishment (GTRE)**, Bengaluru.

**Exam angle (GS3 — Sci-Tech / Defence / Internal Security):** Jet-engine propulsion (the "hot core" — high-temperature turbine blades in nickel superalloys) is the last big gap in India's defence-manufacturing self-reliance; India still imports engines (e.g., GE F404/F414 for Tejas). An *expendable* engine (single-use, powers a missile/UAV that is not recovered) is cheaper and simpler than a reusable aero-engine, making it the logical first indigenous win. Classic Atmanirbhar Bharat + private-sector-in-defence case study.

**Facts to memorise:** 350-kg thrust class; designer = **GTRE** (a DRDO lab, Bengaluru; also develops the **Kaveri** engine); production partner = **Azad Engineering** (contract secured May 2024, sole industry/production agency); intended to power India's **medium-range anti-ship missile**, with spillover to **long-range UAVs and cruise missiles**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (indigenisation of defence).
**Static anchor:** *Mrunal / Shankar-type Sci-Tech notes → "Defence indigenisation & DRDO labs" page.* Add a cross-link: GTRE ↔ **Kaveri engine** ↔ engine imports (F404/F414) ↔ **DAP-2020 / Positive Indigenisation Lists**.

### 2. Ministry of Coal releases 'AAROH' — India's first national report on scientific mine closure — Environment
**Headline:** On **22 July 2026** in New Delhi, the **Ministry of Coal** released **AAROH — Annual Report on Mine Closure**, documenting the **scientific closure of 42 coal mines**; the same event saw an **India–Germany implementation agreement with GIZ** on mine-closure technical cooperation and the inauguration of **"Coal NEER"** water-supply plants.

**Exam angle (GS3 — Environment / Economy):** First-ever consolidated national record of **scientific mine closure** — the structured reclamation of exhausted mines (backfilling, re-vegetation, safe repurposing of land) instead of leaving abandoned voids. Connects the **Mine Closure Plan** requirement (mandatory since 2009, with an **Escrow account** funding closure) to a "just transition" theme — repurposing post-mining land for solar parks, eco-parks, fisheries, and community use. Strong Mains material on sustainable/responsible mining.

**Facts to memorise:** AAROH = **A**nnual **R**eport **O**n mine closure (Ministry of Coal); **42 mines** scientifically closed; India–Germany partner = **GIZ** (Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit); companion initiative = **Coal NEER** (drinking-water access in coal-bearing regions). Mine-closure funding runs through an **Escrow account** mechanism.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment (sustainable mining / land degradation & reclamation).
**Static anchor:** *Shankar IAS Environment → "Environmental Pollution / Degradation" + "Environmental Impact Assessment" topics.* Add a cross-link: **Mine Closure Plan → Escrow account → Just Transition → repurposing to solar/eco-parks**; tie to **coal-sector reforms** in Mrunal's Infrastructure/Energy pillar.

### 3. RBI July 2026 Bulletin: FDI recovery and "resilient" growth amid global uncertainty — Economy
**Headline:** The **RBI Bulletin (released 22 July 2026)** flagged a sharp **FDI recovery** — **net FDI in Apr–May FY27 rose to ~US$6.5 billion**, up from **~US$2.47 billion** a year earlier — while its "State of the Economy" article called domestic demand and the industrial/services mix **resilient** against global headwinds, with **core inflation (ex-precious metals) staying low**.

**Exam angle (GS3 — Economy):** High-yield conceptual hook — **FDI is a non-debt-creating capital inflow** (Balance of Payments: capital account). Distinguish **net vs gross FDI** (net = inward minus repatriation/outward FDI by Indian firms) and **FDI vs FPI/FII**. Note the RBI Bulletin is authored by staff (here under **Deputy Governor Poonam Gupta**) and its views are *not* official RBI policy — a factual trap.

**Facts to memorise:** Net FDI **Apr–May FY27 ≈ $6.5 bn** vs **$2.47 bn** year earlier; **core inflation low** (ex-precious metals); FDI = **non-debt-creating** flow on the **capital account** of the BoP; Bulletin views ≠ RBI's official stance.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (external sector / BoP).
**Static anchor:** *Mrunal Economy → "BoP, FDI–FPI, Capital Account" pillar (Pillar 3/External Sector).* Add a cross-link: **non-debt vs debt-creating flows**, **FDI routes (automatic vs government)**, and the **FDI ≠ FPI** distinction that UPSC repeatedly tests (see PYQ mirror below).

### 5. Puga Valley, Ladakh — India's first geothermal wells commissioned (Places in News) — Geography
**Headline:** **Lt. Governor V.K. Saxena commissioned India's first two geothermal wells** at **Puga Valley, Ladakh** on **17 July 2026** (well-2 drilled to 1,000 m by 8 July) — the base for India's **first 1-MW demonstration-scale geothermal power project**, executed by the **ONGC Energy Centre**.

**Exam angle (GS1 — Geography / GS3 — Energy):** Prime "Places in News" + non-conventional energy item. **Puga** lies in the **Changthang region** of south-eastern Ladakh, within the tectonically active **Indus Suture Zone** (why the geothermal gradient is steep — **135°C recorded at just 400 m**). Geothermal = **base-load renewable** (unlike intermittent solar/wind) — key for remote, high-altitude, grid-isolated Ladakh. Remember India's other geothermal provinces for the map question.

**Facts to memorise:** **Puga Valley → Changthang, SE Ladakh → Indus Suture Zone**; executor = **ONGC Energy Centre** (Iceland's **ÍSOR** as consultant); two wells ~**1,000 m** deep; **135°C at 400 m**; target = **1-MW pilot**. Other Indian geothermal sites: **Tattapani (Chhattisgarh), Manikaran (HP), Bakreshwar (WB)**; GSI estimates India's geothermal potential at **~10 GW**.

**Notebook:** GS1 — Geography (energy resources / places in news).
**Static anchor:** *NCERT (Geography) "Mineral & Energy Resources" + Shankar IAS "Renewable energy" note.* Add a cross-link: **geothermal provinces map (Puga/Tattapani/Manikaran) → Indus Suture Zone → base-load vs intermittent renewables**.

### C. Mains-Style Question

**Q8. (GS3 — 250 words, 15 marks):** "Indigenous propulsion is the last frontier of India's defence self-reliance." **Examine** the significance of recent milestones in indigenous engine development, and **suggest** measures to bridge the aero-engine technology gap.

*Model-answer skeleton:*
- **Intro:** Frame the propulsion gap — India imports engines (GE F404/F414 for Tejas) despite airframe/missile progress; the July 2026 350-kg-thrust expendable turbojet (GTRE + Azad Engineering) is the first indigenous win.
- **Significance:** Strategic autonomy (no import dependence/embargo risk); cost-effective expendable propulsion for missiles/UAVs; catalyses private-sector + superalloy/metallurgy ecosystem; spillovers to Kaveri and future fighter-engine programmes.
- **Challenges:** "Hot core" metallurgy (single-crystal turbine blades, thermal-barrier coatings); thrust-to-weight scaling; long, capital-heavy testing cycles; limited high-altitude test infrastructure.
- **Way forward:** Sustained R&D funding; DRDO–private–academia (IIT/IISc) consortia; co-development/ToT with trusted partners; iDEX/Technology Development Fund; positive indigenisation lists to guarantee demand.
- **Conclusion:** Expendable-engine success is a proof-of-concept; scaling to reusable fighter engines will make Atmanirbhar Bharat in defence complete.

**Q1 — (b).** AAROH ("Annual Report on Mine Closure," Ministry of Coal, 22 Jul 2026) is India's first national report on scientific closure of coal mines — 42 mines documented.

**Q2 — (b) [1 and 3 only].** Designed by GTRE (statement 1 ✓) and made by a private firm, Azad Engineering (statement 3 ✓); it is an *expendable* engine for a missile/UAV, **not** for manned fighters (statement 2 ✗).

**Q4 — (b).** The LFOF Consortium is led by **ICMR–NIN** (National Institute of Nutrition, Hyderabad), with FSSAI, WHO, UNICEF and others as partners.

**Q7 — (a).** FDI is a **non-debt-creating** capital flow (equity ownership, no repayment obligation) — unlike debt/portfolio flows. *(UPSC Prelims 2020.)*
