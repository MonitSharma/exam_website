## Part A — Briefing

### 1. India and Kuwait hold their first-ever Joint Defence Committee meeting — International Relations

**Headline:** The inaugural meeting of the India–Kuwait Joint Defence Committee (JDC) was held in New Delhi on 28 August 2026, operationalising the Defence Cooperation MoU signed during the Prime Minister's Kuwait visit in December 2024.

**Exam angle:** This is the "institution-building" half of India's Gulf policy, and it is the half UPSC actually tests — not the visit, but the *mechanism* the visit created. India now runs a defence-institutional architecture with almost every GCC state, and a first-ever JDC is exactly the kind of "which of these was set up in 2026" factoid Prelims likes. For Mains it feeds the argument that India's West Asia policy has shifted from an energy-and-diaspora relationship to a security-provider relationship in the Gulf.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- Meeting held **New Delhi, 28 August 2026**; **first** meeting of the India–Kuwait JDC.
- Co-chaired by **Joint Secretary Amitabh Prasad** (Ministry of Defence) and **Brigadier General Raed Alsakran**, leading a five-member Kuwaiti delegation.
- Indian side included the **three Services, Department of Defence Production, DRDO, Armed Forces Medical Services and MEA** — note the inclusion of *military medicine* and *defence industry/R&D* as agreed cooperation areas alongside training, exercises and staff talks.
- Flows from the **MoU on Defence Cooperation signed during PM Modi's visit to Kuwait, December 2024** — the first visit by an Indian PM to Kuwait in 43 years, during which ties were elevated to a **Strategic Partnership**.
- The Kuwaiti delegation visited **DPSU Bhawan, New Delhi** for talks with Indian defence industry, and laid a wreath at the **National War Memorial**.

**Notebook:** GS2 — International Relations (India and its neighbourhood / bilateral groupings involving India).

**Static anchor:** Your **India–West Asia / Gulf** note (Vision IAS or Mrunal IR module). Under "India–GCC", add a one-line row for Kuwait: *Strategic Partnership (Dec 2024) → Defence Cooperation MoU → first JDC (Aug 2026)*, and cross-link it to the India–GCC Foreign Ministers' mechanism and to your energy-security note (Kuwait as a top-tier crude supplier, ~1 million-strong Indian community).

### 2. Jan Dhan Yojana completes 12 years — 59.09 crore accounts, ₹3.17 lakh crore of deposits — Economy

**Headline:** PMJDY completed **12 years on 28 August 2026**; deposits have risen **12.8 times** over the period to **₹3,16,514 crore** across **59.09 crore** accounts.

**Exam angle:** PMJDY is the load-bearing wall of the JAM trinity and of every DBT answer you will ever write, so the anniversary is really an excuse to refresh a set of numbers that UPSC has used before and will use again. The examinable shift is qualitative: the story is no longer *account opening* but *account usage* — average balance, dormancy, and whether the accounts are now a credit-delivery rail rather than a savings rail.

**Key facts to memorise (as on 19 August 2026):**
- **59.09 crore** PMJDY accounts; up from **14.72 crore in 2015**.
- Aggregate deposits **₹3,16,514 crore** — a **12.8x** rise in 12 years.
- **Average deposit per account ₹5,356** — up **3.4 times** in 12 years (the more meaningful number than the account count).
- **41.29 crore RuPay debit cards** issued.
- **32.92 crore** female beneficiaries (~56% of accounts).
- **45.95 crore** accounts in rural/semi-urban areas vs **13.14 crore** in urban/metropolitan areas.
- Static spine to keep attached: launched **28 August 2014** under the **National Mission for Financial Inclusion** (Department of Financial Services, Ministry of Finance); **Basic Savings Bank Deposit Account (BSBDA)** with zero minimum balance; **overdraft up to ₹10,000** (age 18–65); **RuPay accident insurance ₹2 lakh** for cards issued on/after 28.08.2018 (₹1 lakh before); made **open-ended in 2018** with the target shifting from "every household" to **"every unbanked adult"**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Economy (inclusive growth, mobilisation of resources, banking).

### 3. PARIVARTAN picks up pace — Delhi-NCR's ₹9,585-crore fleet-renewal push — Environment

**Headline:** The Centre's **PARIVARTAN** scheme for replacing old commercial trucks and buses in Delhi-NCR has crossed **1,300 beneficiaries**, with **42 banks/NBFCs** and **15 auto OEMs** onboarded.

**Exam angle:** This is the first time India has attacked vehicular air pollution with a *financing* instrument rather than a *prohibitory* one — interest subvention, tax waiver and mandated OEM discounts instead of a court-ordered age cap. That contrast (GRAP = emergency curbs; National Vehicle Scrappage Policy = fitness-test-based deregistration; PARIVARTAN = subsidised replacement) is a ready-made three-column comparison for both Prelims matching and a Mains intro.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **PARIVARTAN** = *Programme for Accelerated Renewal and Incentivization of Vehicle Assets for Reducing Transport Air Pollution and Network Emissions*.
- **Administered by MoRTH**; **funded through the National Capital Region Planning Board (NCRPB)** under **MoHUA** — an unusual split worth remembering.
- Total outlay **₹9,585 crore**, of which **₹5,041 crore** is direct central budgetary support. Union Cabinet approval **3 June 2026**; MoHUA guidelines issued **16 July 2026**.
- Target: replace **over 2 lakh (≈2.07 lakh)** pre-BS-IV and BS-IV commercial trucks and buses in NCR with **BS-VI or electric** vehicles.
- Incentive stack: **100% motor-vehicle tax waiver for 10 years** + full registration-fee waiver (notified in **Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh**); **5% interest subvention** on fleet loans for 5 years; **minimum 8% OEM discount** on ex-showroom price; monthly fuel vouchers of **₹1,200–₹4,800** for BS-VI diesel/CNG; one-time EV incentive of **₹64,000–₹2.56 lakh**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Environment (environmental pollution and degradation) with a GS2 Governance edge (Centre–State–NCR coordination).

**Static anchor:** **Shankar IAS Environment — "Air Pollution" chapter.** Add PARIVARTAN as a fourth entry beside **NCAP (2019)**, **GRAP** and the **National Vehicle Scrappage Policy (2021)**, and draw an arrow to **CAQM** (statutory under the CAQM Act, 2021) so you can answer "who does what in the NCR airshed" in one line.

### 4. 'Gnani Artha' — a sovereign AI stack with a 30-billion-parameter Indic model — Science & Technology

**Headline:** The Vice-President launched **Gnani Artha** on 28 August 2026 — an indigenous sovereign AI stack pairing **Evon 3.3**, a 30-billion-parameter open-weights LLM, with **Plexus**, an agentic orchestration platform.

**Exam angle:** The examinable content here is architectural, not commercial. Evon 3.3 is a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE)** model — 30B total parameters but only ~3.5B activated per token — which is exactly the design trick that makes sovereign-scale AI affordable on Indian compute budgets. Pair that with the **Indic tokenizer** point (fewer tokens per word = lower inference cost for Indian languages) and you have a crisp, technically literate paragraph for any "can India build its own AI?" question.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- Launched by **Vice-President C. P. Radhakrishnan**, New Delhi, **28 August 2026**; built by **Gnani AI**, a Bengaluru voice/conversational-AI start-up.
- **Evon 3.3**: **30 billion parameters total**, **~3.5 billion active per token** (**Mixture-of-Experts** routing); **open weights under the Apache 2.0 licence**; trained natively across **11 Indian languages**.
- **Re-engineered Indic tokenizer** — up to **20% fewer tokens per word** than leading global foundation models, which directly cuts inference cost for Indian-language workloads.
- **On-premise / single-node deployment** — designed to run inside an organisation's own data centre, which is the actual meaning of "sovereign" here (data localisation and **DPDP Act, 2023** compliance).
- **Plexus**: agentic orchestration layer letting non-technical users build AI agents by natural-language prompt, with built-in audit logging.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Science & Technology (indigenisation of technology, IT & computers).

**Static anchor:** Your **Artificial Intelligence / IndiaAI Mission** note. Create a running table of Indian foundation models — **BharatGen, Sarvam, Evon 3.3** — with three columns: *parameters, licence, language coverage*. Cross-link to the **DPDP Act, 2023** (data localisation) and to the **India AI Impact Summit** entry so the AI page reads as one story rather than a list of launches.

### 5. India signs for the Javelin ATGM through the US Foreign Military Sales route — Internal Security

**Headline:** The Indian Army signed a **Letter of Offer and Acceptance (LOA)** on **28 August 2026** for the **FGM-148 Javelin** anti-tank guided missile system through the US **Foreign Military Sales (FMS)** process.

**Exam angle:** Two separable things to learn. First, the *route*: FMS is a government-to-government sale executed through the US DSCA, distinct from a Direct Commercial Sale — a recurring Prelims-style distinction. Second, the *capability*: Javelin is the textbook third-generation, fire-and-forget, top-attack ATGM, and it lets you draw the contrast with India's own **MPATGM** (DRDO) and the legacy **Milan-2T / Konkurs-M** that it is meant to supplement.

**Key facts to memorise:**
- **FGM-148 Javelin**, produced by the **Javelin Joint Venture** — **Raytheon (RTX) + Lockheed Martin**; in US service since **1996**.
- **Third-generation, fire-and-forget** man-portable ATGM using a **passive Imaging Infrared (IIR) seeker** — the operator can move immediately after launch ("shoot-and-scoot").
- **Top-attack profile**: climbs to roughly **150 m** and strikes the thinly armoured roof of a tank; a **direct-attack** mode is available for bunkers, buildings and low-flying helicopters.
- **Tandem-charge HEAT warhead** — precursor charge defeats **Explosive Reactive Armour (ERA)**; penetration of the order of **750–800 mm RHA**.
- **Soft launch** (missile ejected before main motor ignition) allows firing from inside rooms and bunkers; **reusable Command Launch Unit (CLU)** with day/thermal sights; range about **2.5 km**, extending to **4–4.75 km** in newer variants.
- The **US DSCA had cleared a possible sale valued at about US$45.7 million** when it notified the case; the LOA opens discussion on **co-production**.

**Notebook:** GS3 — Internal Security (defence technology, indigenisation) with a GS2 IR spillover (India–US defence partnership).

**Static anchor:** Your **Defence Procurement / Indigenisation** note. Add a two-line box distinguishing **FMS (govt-to-govt, via DSCA, LOA is the contract)** from a **Direct Commercial Sale**, and extend your **ATGM comparison table** — *Nag / MPATGM (DRDO), Milan-2T, Konkurs-M, Spike, Javelin* — with columns for generation, guidance and range. Cross-link to the **Positive Indigenisation Lists** entry so the "buy vs build" tension sits on one page.

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS3 — Environment, 250 words, 15 marks)

**Q.** Delhi-NCR's air-quality policy has moved from emergency response to asset replacement. **Critically examine** whether replacing the region's ageing commercial vehicle fleet can deliver durable air-quality gains in the absence of reforms in urban mobility and airshed-level governance.

**Model-answer skeleton (4 points):**

1. **Frame the shift.** Set out the three-instrument sequence: **GRAP** (episodic, curbs activity when AQI crosses thresholds), the **National Vehicle Scrappage Policy, 2021** (structural but voluntary, keyed to fitness tests), and now **PARIVARTAN** (₹9,585 crore, ₹5,041 crore central share, targeting ~2.07 lakh commercial vehicles with BS-VI/EV replacements). The direction of travel is from *restricting use* to *changing the stock*.

2. **Concede the case for asset replacement.** Heavy-duty diesel commercial vehicles are disproportionate contributors to PM2.5 and NOx relative to their share of the fleet; BS-VI cuts diesel NOx sharply against BS-IV, and the financing stack (5% interest subvention, 100% road-tax waiver, 8% OEM discount) addresses the real barrier, which is the small operator's balance sheet rather than his willingness. Cross-state notification in Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan and UP also treats the NCR as one market.

3. **State the limits.** (i) **Rebound effect** — cheaper, newer trucks may increase vehicle-kilometres travelled, eroding per-km gains. (ii) **Sectoral share** — vehicles are only one contributor; stubble burning, construction dust, industry and secondary aerosol formation are untouched by a fleet scheme. (iii) **Airshed vs boundary** — pollution respects the Indo-Gangetic airshed, not the NCR's administrative line, so a scheme bounded by NCR registration is bounded by the wrong geography. (iv) **Enforcement** — scrappage integrity depends on RVSFs and Automated Testing Stations; without them, "scrapped" vehicles migrate to non-NCR states rather than leaving the road.

4. **Way forward and verdict.** Fleet renewal is necessary but not sufficient: pair it with modal shift (rail/coastal freight, dedicated freight corridors, bus fleet electrification), with **CAQM** using its statutory powers for airshed-wide source apportionment, and with the **NCAP** monitoring network extended to non-attainment cities beyond the NCR. Conclude that PARIVARTAN buys a step-change in the emission factor, while only mobility and land-use reform can bend the activity level — and air quality is the product of the two.

### D. PYQ Mirror

**Theme 1 — Air pollution / AQI.** UPSC has tested the *composition* of the index rather than the policy: **Prelims 2016** asked, *"In the cities of our country, which among the following atmospheric gases are normally considered in calculating the value of Air Quality Index?"* — with carbon dioxide and methane offered as distractors; the answer was **carbon monoxide, nitrogen dioxide and sulphur dioxide only**. *Takeaway:* for PARIVARTAN, the examinable core is the **pollutant list and the BS-IV → BS-VI distinction**, not the ₹9,585 crore outlay. Learn the eight AQI pollutants (PM10, PM2.5, NO₂, SO₂, CO, O₃, NH₃, Pb) cold.

**Theme 2 — Financial inclusion.** **Mains 2016 (General Studies Paper III)** asked, *"Pradhan Mantri Jan Dhan Yojana (PMJDY) is necessary for bringing the unbanked to the institutional finance fold. Do you agree with this for the financial inclusion of the poorer sections of Indian society? Give arguments to justify your opinion."* *Takeaway:* UPSC frames PMJDY as a **debate**, not a datasheet — so the 2026 numbers matter only as evidence for or against usage-versus-access. Keep two counter-facts ready (dormancy rates; average balance of ₹5,356) alongside the headline totals.

## Part C — Answers

**Q1 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** The 28 August 2026 meeting in New Delhi was the inaugural JDC; it operationalises the Defence Cooperation MoU signed during the PM's December 2024 Kuwait visit; and the agreed areas expressly include military medicine and defence-industry/R&D cooperation alongside training, exercises and staff talks.

**Q2 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** 59.09 crore accounts with ₹3,16,514 crore in deposits; 32.92 crore of the account holders are women (over half); and the scheme was made open-ended in 2018 with the unit of targeting shifting from household to unbanked adult.

**Q3 — (a) 1 and 2 only.** Statement 3 is wrong: the road-tax and registration-fee waivers have been notified across **Delhi, Haryana, Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh**, since the scheme covers the NCR, not the NCT alone. MoRTH administers it while the NCRPB (under MoHUA) is the funding channel.

**Q4 — (b).** Evon 3.3 is the 30-billion-parameter, open-weights (Apache 2.0) Mixture-of-Experts large language model at the core of the 'Gnani Artha' sovereign AI stack, with roughly 3.5 billion parameters active per token.

**Q5 — (b) 2 and 3 only.** Statement 1 describes a second-generation, SACLOS wire-guided missile such as Milan or Konkurs. Javelin is third-generation and **fire-and-forget**, using a passive imaging-infrared seeker locked on before launch; it does have a top-attack profile and a tandem-charge warhead for ERA-protected targets.

**Q6 — (d) 1, 2 and 3.** CAQM is statutory under the Commission for Air Quality Management in National Capital Region and Adjoining Areas Act, 2021; GRAP escalates restrictions by AQI stage; and NCAP was launched by MoEF&CC in 2019.

**Q7 — (b) 1 and 3 only.** The GCC has six members — Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Kuwait, Qatar, Bahrain and Oman — with its Secretariat at Riyadh. **Iraq and Yemen are not members**, founding or otherwise, which makes statement 2 wrong.
