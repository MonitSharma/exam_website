# UPSC Daily CA Briefing — 17 July 2026

## PART A — BRIEFING (5 items)

### 1. Cabinet approves the Mobile Phone Manufacturing Scheme (MPMS) — ₹62,500 crore successor to PLI-LSEM — Economy

- **Exam angle:** The PLI for Large Scale Electronics Manufacturing (PLI-LSEM) ended 31 March 2026; MPMS (under MeitY) is its 5-year successor (FY2026-27 to FY2030-31). Classic Prelims fodder: outlay, incentive structure, targets. Mains: industrial policy shifting from flat production incentives to *value-addition-linked* incentives.
- **Key facts:** Outlay **₹62,500 crore**; sales-linked incentives **2.25%–5%**; extra **up to 1.5%** for higher domestic sourcing of components/sub-assemblies; extra **3%** for Indian brands investing in design/R&D. Targets: **₹39 lakh crore** cumulative production, **₹15 lakh crore** exports, ~**60,000** direct jobs.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Industrial policy / electronics manufacturing).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Mrunal Pillar 4 (Sectors of Economy — manufacturing & PLI). *Update your PLI table: mark PLI-LSEM "concluded 31-3-2026 → succeeded by MPMS" and cross-link Make in India / Semicon entries.*

### 2. US–Iran hostilities resume after the Islamabad MoU collapses — Strait of Hormuz back in play — International Relations

- **Exam angle:** The June 2026 interim peace deal (**Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding**) broke down within a month; strikes resumed near Bandar Abbas, Qeshm and Bushehr, and Iran's Hormuz blockade returned. India's stake: energy security, Chabahar/INSTC investments, diaspora. (Cross-ref: the 13 July item on China's helium ban — Hormuz also bottles up ~1/3 of global helium.)
- **Key facts:** Brent spiked **~15% to near $85/barrel**; ~**90% of India's LPG imports** transit Hormuz; India co-sponsored and voted for **UNSC Resolution 2817** condemning Iran's attacks on Jordan and GCC states — a notable break from its traditional abstention habit.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (West Asia) + GS3 Economy (energy security).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** World Geography map work (chokepoints: Hormuz, Bab-el-Mandeb, Malacca) + IR notes "India–West Asia". *Add a cross-link from your Chabahar/INSTC note to this escalation as a live risk case study.*

### 3. UN's first scientific assessment of AI — Preliminary Report of the Independent International Scientific Panel on AI — Science & Technology

- **Exam angle:** First UN-commissioned *scientific* (not political) assessment of AI, released 1 July and presented at the **Global Dialogue on AI Governance, Geneva (6–7 July 2026)**; it entered Indian news cycles this week. Ties neatly to India's stated UNSC priority of "responsible AI governance" (SHANTI, 15 July item).
- **Key facts:** **40-member** independent panel; co-chairs **Yoshua Bengio** (Turing Award) and **Maria Ressa** (Nobel Peace Prize). Core warning: AI capability growth is outpacing governance; no known technical guarantee that AI agents follow instructions consistently. Compute concentration: **USA ~75%**, China ~15%, rest of world ~10%.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech (AI) + GS2 IR (global governance institutions).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Sci-Tech notes, "AI governance" section (alongside GPAI, Bletchley/Seoul summits, EU AI Act). *Add this panel as the "IPCC-for-AI" analogy — a quotable Mains line.*

### 4. Two new solitary bee species discovered in Siang Valley, Arunachal Pradesh — Environment

- **Exam angle:** "Species in news" + Eastern Himalaya (Himalaya biodiversity hotspot) — UPSC loves newly described taxa with an ethnonym (species named after the **Adi** tribe). Also a pollinator-conservation hook for Mains.
- **Key facts:** **Elaphropoda triangulata** (triangular abdominal markings) and **Habropoda adi** (honours the Adi community); found by **ATREE** researchers on the Siang Expedition; published in the *European Journal of Taxonomy*; each known from a single male specimen. Globally only 13 *Elaphropoda* and 55 *Habropoda* species; both are solitary, ground-nesting pollinators (subfamily Anthophorinae).
- **Notebook:** GS3 Environment (biodiversity/conservation).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Shankar IAS — Biodiversity chapter (hotspots: Himalaya). *Add to your running "new species 2026" list; cross-link the Adi tribe (Siang belt) in your tribes-of-India note.*

### 5. UMANG portal data-exposure flaws — a Digital Public Infrastructure trust test — Polity & Governance

- **Exam angle:** Security researchers found vulnerabilities in UMANG exposing EPFO UANs, LPG booking details and Aadhaar numbers (some stored in **plain text**, contrary to Aadhaar Act, 2016 restrictions). MeitY patched APIs after reports to **MeitY and CERT-In**, but fixes were called inadequate. Mains: e-governance vs data protection; accountability of the state as *data fiduciary* under the DPDP Act, 2023.
- **Key facts:** UMANG aggregates **2,400+** Union/State services; the EPFO module logged **40 crore+ transactions in three months** — the platform's most-used service.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Governance (e-governance) + GS3 Internal Security (cyber).
- **STATIC ANCHOR:** Laxmikanth (e-Governance chapter) + your DPDP Act 2023 note. *Add UMANG as a case study under "DPI: successes vs safeguards" beside the CoWIN/Aadhaar debates.*

### C. Mains-Style Question (GS3 — Economy/Energy Security, 250 words, 15 marks)

**"Recurring instability in West Asia has turned maritime chokepoints into instruments of economic coercion." Examine the implications of the renewed Strait of Hormuz crisis for India's energy security, and suggest measures to insulate the Indian economy from such shocks.**

*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. **Exposure:** ~90% of India's LPG and a large share of crude/LNG imports transit Hormuz; Brent's ~15% spike to ~$85 shows imported-inflation and CAD risk; Chabahar/INSTC investments and Gulf diaspora add non-energy exposure.
2. **Implications:** costlier crude basket → fiscal pressure on fuel subsidies; freight/war-risk insurance surcharges; forced re-routing erodes the price advantage of West Asian grades.
3. **Measures:** diversify sourcing (US, West Africa, Russia within sanctions limits); expand Strategic Petroleum Reserves and commercial storage; accelerate ethanol/compressed biogas/renewables substitution; alternative corridors (IMEC, INSTC land legs); naval presence and convoy escorts (Op Sankalp-style); coordinated diplomacy via UNSC and non-Western middle powers.
4. **Conclusion:** energy security = supply security + price stability + route security; strategic autonomy demands all three.

- **Q2 — (b).** The Islamabad MoU was the June 2026 interim US–Iran peace agreement; it collapsed within a month, reigniting hostilities.

- **Q4 — (b).** Both are solitary ground-nesting bees found by ATREE in the Siang Valley; *H. adi* honours the Adi tribe.
