## PART A — BRIEFING

### 1. Cabinet (11 Nov) extends CGFMU (Mudra guarantee) and Stand-Up India to 2031 — Economy
- **Exam angle:** Credit guarantees de-risk lending to micro units and first-generation entrepreneurs without direct subsidy. Guarantee fee cuts and a higher loan ceiling signal a push on formal credit for the informal sector (GS3 inclusive growth; MSME finance).
- **Key facts:** CGFMU continued for 5 years to 31 Mar 2031; corpus ₹5,000 crore backing PMMY loans of ₹12.6 lakh crore; guarantee fee cut 1% → 0.75%; cover for PMMY loans up to ₹20 lakh; also ₹10,000 Jan Dhan overdrafts and ₹10–20 lakh SHG loans; run by NCGTC. PMMY so far: 60.36 crore loans, ₹43.34 lakh crore (Sep 2026). **Stand-Up India** (launched Apr 2016) extended to 31 Mar 2031: corpus ₹4,111 crore; loans above ₹10 lakh up to ₹1 crore for Women/SC/ST greenfield enterprises; guarantee up to 85%, fee 0.50%; target 2.5 lakh more entrepreneurs.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Economy (Inclusive growth / MSME).
- **Static anchor:** Ramesh Singh, Banking & Financial Inclusion; Mrunal Economy "Credit & Financial Inclusion". *Nudge:* add CGFMU–Stand-Up–CGTMSE as a comparison table (corpus, fee, ceiling).

### 2. CCEA approves ₹3,230 crore Yuge Yugeen Bharat Museum — Art & Culture
- **Exam angle:** India's largest national museum project, repurposing North and South Block (heritage-use of Central Vista-era buildings). Tests museums policy, the Culture Ministry's role, and Harappan artefacts. Reported controversy: Dancing Girl and Pashupati Seal removed from the National Museum for display in a mock gallery.
- **Key facts:** Approved 11 Nov 2026; phased over 2026-27 to 2030-31; announced May 2023; ~1.8 million objects identified; capacity up to 1 crore visitors/yr; first gallery "Adornment" in North Block in latter half of 2027; other galleries include Architecture & Cityscapes, Mathematics & Astronomy, Languages & Scripts, Faith & Spirituality, "Bharat Connects – Trade and Ideas".
- **Notebook:** GS1 Art & Culture.
- **Static anchor:** Nitin Singhania, Indus Valley art / Museums; NCERT Class 11 *An Introduction to Indian Art*. *Nudge:* cross-link Dancing Girl (lost-wax bronze) and Pashupati Seal to Harappan notes.

### 3. SC: grandson has no birthright in property father got on partition under Section 8, HSA — Polity / Society
- **Exam angle:** Clarifies the line between ancestral (coparcenary) and separate property post-1956; important for Hindu law, women's property rights and the 2005 amendment (Section 6).
- **Key facts:** *J. Rajesh (minor) v. M. Kumaran (dead) through LRs*, 2026 INSC 1208; bench of Justices A.G. Masih and Sunita Agarwal (reported 9 Nov). Section 8 held "self-contained"; property partitioned among Class I heirs became the father's separate property; son's son is not a Class I heir (*CWT v. Chander Sen*, 1986); Section 6 and TN Amendment s.29-A held inapplicable.
- **Notebook:** GS2 Polity / GS1 Society.
- **Static anchor:** Laxmikanth, Judiciary & Personal laws (Directive context); Shankar IAS Family law notes. *Nudge:* add a Section 6 vs Section 8 flowchart.

### 4. Nepal PM Balendra Shah's first bilateral visit to India (reported 22–24 Nov) — International Relations
- **Exam angle:** Neighbourhood First reset with Nepal's new government (in office since 27 Mar 2026, per press reports); connectivity, energy, flood-relief diplomacy.
- **Key facts:** MEA (10 Nov): "entire range" of ties to be discussed; dates still being worked out officially. Foreign Secretary Misri met PM Shah in Kathmandu (4–5 Nov): energy, water, trade, transit, aviation. Ayodhya–Janakpur rail service trial run held 5 Nov; Shah thanked India for Rasuwa–Bhotekoshi flood relief. No agreements signed yet; reports expect an assistance package and agreements during the visit.
- **Notebook:** GS2 IR (Neighbourhood).
- **Static anchor:** Shankar IAS / Vajiram IR notes: India–Nepal (1950 Treaty, Sugauli 1816, Kalapani). *Nudge:* add a 2026 timeline box.

### 5. IIT-Jodhpur's niobium-based Refractory Complex Lean Alloy (RCLA) — Science & Technology
- **Exam angle:** Indigenous advanced materials for hypersonic, rocket and re-entry systems: self-reliance in strategic technology (Atmanirbharta in defence/space).
- **Key facts:** Density ~7.6 g/cm³ (vs ~9–10 for C-103/WC3009); after 24 h at 1,000°C gained 43.48 mg/cm² vs 258 mg/cm² for C-103, no pesting; room-temperature yield strength 1.13 GPa; ~29% compressive strain; "Complex Lean Alloy" design approach; led by Dr Saurabh Nene; made and tested at IIT-Jodhpur without foreign help.
- **Notebook:** GS3 Sci-Tech.
- **Static anchor:** Spectrum / Sci-Tech notes: Materials (alloys, superalloys, refractory metals). *Nudge:* add "pesting" and refractory metals to the glossary.

### B3. Mains question (15 marks, 250 words)
**Discuss** how credit guarantee schemes such as CGFMU and Stand-Up India can widen formal credit to micro enterprises and marginalised entrepreneurs, and the risks they pose.

*Model-answer skeleton:*
1. Context: PMMY scale (60 crore loans, ₹43 lakh crore); CGFMU ₹5,000 cr corpus, fee cut to 0.75%, cover up to ₹20 lakh; Stand-Up India ₹4,111 cr for Women/SC/ST.
2. Mechanism: guarantee to lenders cuts collateral demand and NPA fear; reaches first-time and SHG borrowers.
3. Limits: moral hazard, guarantee-fund depletion, concentration in Shishu loans, weak credit-appraisal, low formal credit to women/SC/ST beyond headline numbers.
4. Way forward: risk-based fee, data-driven monitoring, handholding and market linkage, fiscal-cost accounting; conclude on inclusive growth (SDG 8/10).
