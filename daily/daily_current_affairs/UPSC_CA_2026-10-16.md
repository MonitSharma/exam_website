### 4. DRDO tests Quantum Key Distribution platform on a live network at PXE Chandipur — Science & Technology
- **Exam angle:** Quantum communication for defence. Eavesdropping disturbs quantum states, so it is detectable; keys then feed classical encryption. Pairs with the National Quantum Mission in a Mains answer on quantum technologies.

Developed under DRDO's MCC cluster; production partner Bengaluru-based Taqbit Labs (spelled "Tagbit" in some reports). ### B3. Mains question
**"Despite declining stunting and undernourishment, child wasting in India has risen. Critically examine the reasons and suggest policy measures." (Critically examine, 15 marks, 250 words)**
- Intro: GHI 2026 — rank 100/122, score 24.1; stunting 29.3% but wasting 19%.
- Reasons: acute vs chronic malnutrition; poor dietary diversity and IYCF practices; WASH and infection burden; gaps in anganwadi delivery and early detection; climate/food-price shocks.
- Measures: community management of acute malnutrition, wasting screening at anganwadis, convergence of Poshan 2.0, PDS/fortification and WASH; data-driven monitoring.
- Conclusion: shift from stunting-centric targets to a life-cycle, wasting-sensitive approach.
