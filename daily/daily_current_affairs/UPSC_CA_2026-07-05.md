### C. Mains-Style Question (GS2 — Polity & Governance, 250 words, 15 marks)

**"Despite constitutional backing under the 73rd Amendment, Gram Sabhas in India continue to function as weak, symbolic institutions rather than vibrant organs of grassroots democracy." Examine the reasons for low citizen participation in Gram Sabhas and suggest measures to strengthen them.**

Model-answer skeleton:
1. **Reasons (evidence-based):** livelihood/time constraints (55.5% per NITI Aayog's 2026 study), poor awareness/communication (16.2%), low institutional trust and Sarpanch-centric capture of power, weak quorum enforcement.
2. **Structural gap:** Article 243A leaves Gram Sabha's powers to state legislation — resulting in wide inter-state variation and incomplete devolution of the "3Fs" (funds, functions, functionaries).
3. **Consequences:** undermines participatory planning (Gram Panchayat Development Plan), social audits, and PESA-mandated rights in Schedule V areas.
4. **Way forward:** technology-enabled mobilisation (as NITI Aayog recommends), civic/voter education, mandatory recorded proceedings, aligning Gram Sabha meetings with MGNREGA/VB-G RAM G social audits, and incentivising attendance.
