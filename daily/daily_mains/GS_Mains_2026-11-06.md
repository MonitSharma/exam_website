## MODEL ANSWER (≈250 words)

**Introduction.** India–US ties, elevated to a "Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership" (2020), now run on two tracks: a robust defence–technology–Indo-Pacific track and a turbulent trade track. The February 2026 interim-trade *framework* remains unsigned, tariff authority has shifted legal footing (IEEPA struck down; a 10% Section 301 duty since July), and Russian-oil-linked penalties hang over India as a withdrawable waiver.

**Strengths of the partnership**
- **Strategic convergence:** Quad, foundational defence agreements (LEMOA, COMCASA, BECA), maritime domain awareness, and shared concern over China's assertiveness.
- **Economic complementarity:** the US is among India's largest export markets; technology, pharma and services link both economies.
- **Emerging sectors:** civil nuclear cooperation (SHANTI Act, per reports), critical minerals, defence co-production.

**Frictions**
- **Trade:** tariff unpredictability harms labour-intensive exporters (textiles, gems, shrimp); US asks on agriculture/dairy touch livelihoods and political sensitivities.
- **Energy and sanctions:** secondary-tariff threats over Russian crude test India's energy security and its right to diversify supply.
- **Mobility and visas:** concerns for Indian professionals and students.
- **Asymmetry:** India is asked to open markets and make purchase commitments, while US tariff rates remain legally contingent.

**Strategic-autonomy dimension.** Autonomy today means *multi-alignment* — engaging the US, Russia, EU, Gulf and the Global South on issue-specific terms — rather than Cold War non-alignment. Over-dependence on any one partner raises vulnerability.

**Way forward**
- Sign a *limited, legally durable* interim deal that ring-fences agriculture and dairy.
- Diversify exports via the India–EU and India–UK trade agreements and other FTAs.
- Calibrate energy sourcing on price, security and legal exposure — without public capitulation or defiance.
- Compartmentalise: protect the Quad and defence-technology track from trade disputes.

**Conclusion.** The partnership is too consequential for both sides to be hostage to tariff cycles. India's interest lies in *strategic partnership with strategic autonomy* — firm on core livelihoods, flexible on market access, and anchored by diversification.

### Value-additions (use 2–3)
- **Doctrine:** S. Jaishankar's *The India Way* — "multi-alignment" / issue-based convergence; contrast with Nehruvian non-alignment.
- **Institutional anchors:** 2+2 Ministerial Dialogue; foundational agreements — LEMOA (2016), COMCASA (2018), BECA (2020); Quad (revived 2017); "Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership" (2020).
- **Legal/contextual:** US Supreme Court's 20 Feb 2026 ruling on IEEPA tariffs; Section 301 vs Section 232 distinction; India's earlier 2025 episode of 50% US tariffs (25% base + 25% Russian-oil penalty) — reported figures, verify.
- **Framework:** "Hedging vs balancing vs bandwagoning" (IR theory) to show analytical depth.
