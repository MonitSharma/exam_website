## 1. DigiLocker integrates 68 electricity DISCOMs nationwide
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Electronics & IT (MeitY) / Ministry of Power · **Notebook: GS2 Governance (e-Governance) + GS3 Infrastructure (Digital Economy)**

DigiLocker, India's flagship digital document wallet under Digital India, has onboarded 68 electricity Distribution Companies (DISCOMs) and power departments. Consumers across 35 States/UTs can now pull issuer-authenticated digital electricity bills directly — usable as KYC/address proof. It deepens India's Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) by extending DigiLocker from identity documents into utility records.

**Key facts to remember:** DigiLocker launched 2015, under MeitY · documents legally at par with originals under Rule 9A, IT (Digital Locker) Rules, 2016 · 67.63 crore registered users and 950+ crore documents issued (March 2026) · 68 DISCOMs across 35 States/UTs · DPI pillars = Aadhaar, UPI, DigiLocker · DISCOMs regulated by SERCs under the Electricity Act, 2003 · reform vehicle = RDSS (outlay ₹3,03,758 crore; nodal agencies PFC & REC; AT&C-loss target 12–15%; losses fell 22.62% in FY14 → 15.04% in FY25).

## 2. National SC-ST Hub (NSSH) Scheme — flagship MSME push
**Issuing body:** Ministry of MSME (PIB) · **Notebook: GS2 Governance / Vulnerable Sections**

PIB highlighted the "transformational impact" of the NSSH Scheme in empowering Scheduled Caste and Scheduled Tribe entrepreneurs through credit, capital subsidy, market access and capacity building. Important as a ready answer-anchor for "schemes for vulnerable sections" and the public-procurement-as-empowerment theme.

**Quick scheme template:**
- **Name / Ministry:** National SC-ST Hub (NSSH) Scheme · Ministry of MSME · launched **2016**.
- **Objective:** Build entrepreneurship culture among SC/ST and help them meet the public-procurement mandate.
- **Key features:** (1) Implemented by **National Small Industries Corporation (NSIC)**; (2) Special Credit Linked Capital Subsidy — 25% subsidy up to ₹25 lakh on plant/machinery; (3) Single Point Registration Scheme — EMD exemption on tenders; (4) Special Marketing Assistance for trade fairs/vendor-development.
- **Beneficiaries/coverage:** SC/ST-owned Micro & Small Enterprises (MSEs) nationwide.
- **Significance:** Anchors the Public Procurement Policy target of sourcing **≥4% of annual purchases** from SC/ST-owned MSEs.

## 4. Higher ethanol-blended petrol (E22–E30) exempted from central excise duty
**Issuing body:** Ministry of Finance, Department of Revenue · **Notebook: GS3 Economy / Environment (Energy)**

The Finance Ministry exempted four higher ethanol-blend petrol grades — **E22, E25, E27 and E30** — from the entire central excise duty (plus Special Additional Excise Duty, Road & Infrastructure Cess and Agriculture Infrastructure & Development Cess). Aim: cut production costs for Oil Marketing Companies and incentivise the next phase of the ethanol-blending programme beyond E20.

**Key facts to remember:** Grades = E22/E25/E27/E30 (e.g., E30 = 30% ethanol + 70% petrol) · follow new BIS standard **IS 19850:2026** (effective 15 May 2026) · excise duty is an indirect tax on manufacture/production · India already achieved the **E20 (20%)** target; this signals the move toward higher blends, subject to vehicle-compatibility testing.

## 5. ISRO sanctions research project on rip currents for beach safety
**Issuing body:** ISRO (with Andhra University) · **Notebook: GS3 Science & Technology / GS1 Geography (Oceanography)**

ISRO has sanctioned a project to study rip currents along the Visakhapatnam coast and develop AI- and satellite-based tools for beach safety. Useful as both a prelims geography concept and an example of space-tech for disaster/public safety.

**Key facts to remember:** Rip currents = narrow, fast channels of water flowing **away from the shore** toward the sea · form when waves pile up water at the coast that escapes back through a narrow gap · study pairs ISRO satellite data with AI for hazard warning.

**Q1.** Consider the following statements regarding the National SC-ST Hub (NSSH) Scheme:

(a) 1 and 2 only
(b) 2 and 3 only

## Answers

**Q1 — (b).** NSSH is a Ministry of **MSME** scheme (launched 2016), not Social Justice — so statement 1 is wrong. It is implemented by NSIC and backs the ≥4% public-procurement target from SC/ST MSEs.

**Q2 — (b).** The **Ministry of Finance (Dept. of Revenue)**, not Petroleum, issued the notification — statement 2 is wrong. The exemption covers E22–E30 and E30 = 30% ethanol + 70% petrol, so 1 and 3 are correct.

**Q3 — (d).** All correct. DigiLocker (2015, MeitY) is a core DPI pillar with Aadhaar and UPI; its documents are legally equivalent to originals under Rule 9A, IT (Digital Locker) Rules, 2016.
