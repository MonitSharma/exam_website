## Added 2026-06-26

*First edition. Drawn from the week's daily current-affairs, PIB and editorial notes (19–25 June 2026), cross-checked against primary reporting, plus standing general knowledge for the thinker-quotes.*

### Governance & Polity

- **"Right to Walk" as a Fundamental Right (Supreme Court, 19 June 2026).** A two-judge bench (Justices P.S. Narasimha & A.S. Chandurkar) held that the right to walk on a safe, demarcated footpath flows from **Article 19(1)(d)** (freedom of movement) read with **Article 21**, and that pedestrians have **priority over motor vehicles**; it imposed a *positive duty* on municipal bodies to build/maintain footpaths. The case arose from the death of a 5-year-old hit by a tanker on a road with no footpath. *Use: governance/urban-mobility and "Article 21 as a bundle of positive rights" answers — the latest entry in the lineage Maneka Gandhi (1978) → Olga Tellis (1985) → Puttaswamy (2017). Also an essay line on dignity in everyday public space.*

- **DISHA 2.0 — "Access to Justice" as a constitutional promise (approved June 2026).** Restructured Central Sector scheme (Dept. of Justice; ₹255 crore, 2026–31) delivering free legal aid, tele-law and legal literacy, explicitly anchored in the **Preamble and Articles 14, 21 and 39A** and mapped to **UN SDG-16**; adds an AI "Nyaya Setu" chatbot for last-mile reach. *Use: governance answers on welfare delivery / justice for the poor, and how DPSP (Art 39A) is operationalised. Pairs with the Rawls quote below.*

- **FCRA Amendment Rules, 2026 — civil society vs. national security (MHA, 22 June 2026).** Tighter leash on foreign-funded NGOs: activities must be picked from five fixed categories, proselytisation barred, social-media handles and ultimate donors disclosed, ≥₹10 lakh spend threshold for renewal. *Use: the classic GS2 tension between associational freedom (Art 19(1)(c)) and sovereignty/internal security; a "regulation must be calibrated, time-bound and appealable" balance point.*

### Economy & Development

- **RBI's record surplus transfer — ₹2.87 lakh crore for FY26 (highest ever).** Governed by the **Economic Capital Framework (Bimal Jalan Committee, 2018)** and Section 47 of the RBI Act, 1934; it is **non-tax revenue of the Union and bypasses the Finance Commission divisible pool** (zero automatic share for States). *Use: fiscal-federalism and central-bank-autonomy answers; a concrete number for "fiscalisation of monetary policy" debates.*

- **The "11 million a year" demographic-dividend number (World Bank, 18 June 2026).** The World Bank approved a **$1.5 billion** Development Policy Financing operation framed around the **~11 million youth entering India's labour market every year for the next two decades**. *Use: the single most quotable figure for jobs/demographic-dividend essays — pair with the "window closes by ~2040s" framing and the formal-vs-informal job-quality caveat.*

- **India is the world's No. 1 ship-recycling nation (UNCTAD, 2025).** Market share rose to **35.4%** (from 30.1% in 2024); **2.99 million GT** recycled (+60% YoY); the **Maritime India Vision 2030** top-spot target met **five years early**; hub at **Alang, Gujarat**; enabled by the **Recycling of Ships Act, 2019** (gives effect to the IMO's Hong Kong Convention). *Use: a rare win-win where green compliance and economic competitiveness reinforce each other — circular economy / blue economy / Aatmanirbhar steel-inputs answers.*

### Environment & Sustainability

- **Korea district "5% Model", Chhattisgarh — community-led groundwater recharge.** Farmers set aside **5% of their land** for soak pits/trenches; in 2025 nearly **2.8 million m³** was recharged and groundwater rose a historic **5.41 metres**, with seasonal migration down ~**25%**. Women led as **"Neer Nayikas"** and youth as **"Jal Doots."** *Use: the model case study for participatory water governance, jal-sanchay and women-in-development; a vivid essay anecdote on bottom-up solutions.*

- **Western Ghats — Gadgil vs. Kasturirangan, the development-vs-ecology benchmark.** **Gadgil/WGEEP (2011)** proposed the entire ~1,29,037 sq km as an Eco-Sensitive Area; **Kasturirangan/HLWG (2013)** scaled it to ~37% (~60,000 sq km). The 2014 draft ESA notification is still unresolved after five revisions. *Use: the go-to example for cooperative-federalism friction and "conservation vs livelihood"; ESAs are notified under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986.*

- **"You can't manage what you don't measure" — India's industrial-emissions blind spot.** Per India's first Biennial Transparency Report, industry is **>20%** of national emissions, yet **>40%** sits in an undefined **"non-specific industries"** bucket outside the PAT scheme / Carbon Credit Trading Scheme. *Use: a data-governance argument for environment + economy answers; shows decarbonisation needs better classification, not just targets. Net-zero by 2070 vs Make-in-India framing.*

### Society & Social Justice

- **NFHS-6 (2023–24) — "access has outpaced outcomes."** Under-5 stunting fell from **35.5% (NFHS-5) to 29.3%**, and institutional births (~90%) and full immunisation (~87%) are near-universal — *yet only ~15% of children aged 6–23 months get an "adequate diet."* *Use: the defining society/health paradox of the year — medical access improved faster than nutrition because the binding constraints shifted to diet quality, sanitation and **maternal time poverty**. Argue for crèches "as social infrastructure" and the first-1,000-days window.*

- **"Nirbhay Raho" — engaging men, not just women, in gender safety (Panchayati Raj, 2026).** Its **Nirbhay Chetna** pillar aims to sensitise ~**17.5 lakh male elected representatives** on "positive masculinity" — billed as the world's largest drive to sensitise men in local governance; companion pillars train ~14.5 lakh women reps and add CCTV safety infrastructure. *Use: a fresh, design-smart example for gender-justice answers — shifting social norms by targeting men and using PRI devolution for social outcomes.*

### Science / Tech & its Ethics

- **India's commercial-space leap — reforms → outcomes.** NewSpace India Ltd (NSIL) revenue jumped roughly **10×, from ₹321.77 cr (FY22) to ₹3,246.09 cr (FY25)**; registered space start-ups crossed **400** (Feb 2026, up from one in 2014); the space economy is projected at **$40–45 billion** within a decade. Enablers: **IN-SPACe** (single-window), **Indian Space Policy 2023**, FDI liberalisation. *Use: the cleanest "deregulation unlocks private enterprise" case study; Sci-Tech + economy crossover.*

- **Fibre-optic drones — the jam-proof weapon and the ethics of asymmetric war.** Controlled through a physical fibre-optic spool rather than radiofrequency, they emit almost no RF and are **immune to jamming/spoofing** (seen in Russia–Ukraine, Israel–Hezbollah); the trade-off is limited range and a breakable cable. *Use: Sci-Tech/internal-security answers on electronic warfare and counter-drone doctrine; an essay hook on how cheap tech reshapes the ethics and economics of conflict.*

- **Green-hydrogen certification gets a hard number (GHCI portal, June 2026).** India's standard certifies hydrogen as "green" only if production emits **< 2 kg CO₂-equivalent per kg of H₂**; the National Green Hydrogen Mission targets **5 MMT/yr by 2030** (₹19,744 cr outlay). *Use: a precise, quotable threshold for energy-transition / hard-to-abate-sector answers — "green" must be auditable, not just branded.*

- **Drug "track-and-trace" and the AMR fight (MoHFW, June 2026).** QR-code authentication (Schedule H2, Drugs Rules 1945) extended to **vaccines, antimicrobials, anti-cancer and narcotic drugs** to curb spurious medicines and slow **Antimicrobial Resistance**. *Use: "pharmacy of the world" credibility, One Health, and regulation-as-trust answers.*

### International Relations

- **India takes the FATF Vice-Presidency for the first time (June 2026).** Vivek Aggarwal elected VP for July 2026–June 2027 — India's first since joining the **inter-governmental** Financial Action Task Force (est. 1989 G7 Paris; Secretariat at the OECD) in 2010. *Use: India in global standard-setting (AML/CFT), and leverage against terror-financing in the neighbourhood.*

- **India back on ITLOS — Bimal N. Patel elected judge (2026–2035).** Won 115 of 168 votes; ITLOS was set up under **UNCLOS (1982)**, sits in **Hamburg**, and has 21 elected judges. *Use: India's footprint in maritime/international law at a time of South China Sea, deep-sea-mining and delimitation disputes.*

- **MEA's "ethical migration" push — Human Resource Mobility Forum (June–July 2026).** Matches Indian job-seekers with vetted foreign employers (focus: Denmark, Germany, Italy, Japan, Russia) to curb illegal agents and trafficking. *Use: diaspora-as-foreign-policy-asset, "demographic dividend export," and migrant-worker protection answers.*

### Ethics & Philosophy (thinkers & quotes)

- **John Rawls (A Theory of Justice, 1971):** *"Justice is the first virtue of social institutions, as truth is of systems of thought… laws and institutions no matter how efficient and well-arranged must be reformed or abolished if they are unjust."* *Use: any justice/rights/governance essay; pairs perfectly with the DISHA 2.0 "access to justice" item.* (Verified — exact, Ch. I, §1.)

- **B.R. Ambedkar (closing speech to the Constituent Assembly, 25 Nov 1949):** *"However good a Constitution may be, it is sure to turn out bad because those who are called to work it, happen to be a bad lot. However bad a Constitution may be, it may turn out to be good if those who are called to work it, happen to be a good lot."* *Use: governance/ethics answers on the primacy of people and institutions over rules — implementation gap.* (Verified.)

- **B.R. Ambedkar (same 1949 speech):** *"Political democracy cannot last unless there lies at the base of it social democracy."* *Use: social-justice and equality essays; link to the NFHS-6 access-vs-outcome and FCRA civil-society items.* (Verified.)

- **Amartya Sen (Development as Freedom, 1999):** development is *"a process of expanding the real freedoms that people enjoy"* — freedom is both the end and the means of development, across five freedoms (political, economic, social opportunities, transparency, protective security). *Use: development-vs-growth, capability-approach and human-capital essays; deploy against any "GDP-only" framing of progress.* (Verified — central thesis/opening of the book.)

- **Kautilya (Arthashastra, Bk. 1):** *"In the happiness of his subjects lies the king's happiness; in their welfare his welfare."* *Use: ethics/governance answers on citizen-centric administration and public service as duty.* (Classical translation; widely cited.)

- **Brundtland Commission (Our Common Future, 1987)** — sustainable development is *"development that meets the needs of the present without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs."* *Use: the canonical definition for any environment/climate essay; pair with the industrial-emissions and green-hydrogen items above.* (Verified — exact.)

### Essay-ready quotes & anecdotes

- **The "two Indias" of NFHS-6.** "India learned to deliver babies safely before it learned to feed them well" — frame the gap between ~90% institutional births and ~15% adequately-fed toddlers. *Use: opening or pivot for a health/human-development essay; the access-≠-outcomes thesis in one image.*

- **The 5% that filled the wells.** Farmers in Korea district giving up 5% of their land lifted the water table 5.41 m and cut distress migration — proof that "development" can be authored from the bottom up, by "Neer Nayikas" and "Jal Doots," not only from Delhi. *Use: essays on participatory development, water security, or "small actions, large outcomes."*

- **On health as wealth.** *"It is health that is real wealth and not pieces of gold and silver."* — widely attributed to Mahatma Gandhi (popular attribution; exact original source uncertain, so cite as "attributed"). *Use: health/well-being essays alongside the NFHS-6 item — flag the attribution honestly if quoting.*
